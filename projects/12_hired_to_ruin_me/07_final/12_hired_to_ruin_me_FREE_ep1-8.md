# I Married the Woman Hired to Ruin Me — FREE (EP1-8)

## EP1 - S#1  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — dining room
Characters: Claire, Richard, Vanessa

△ Claire plates dinner for two at a long table, careful, almost hopeful. From the next room drifts a woman's easy laugh.

CLAIRE (V.O.): I still set his place every night. Three years, and I can't unlearn hoping.

△ Richard crosses through, cuffs open, Vanessa on his arm in Claire's silk robe.

RICHARD: We're going out. Don't wait up — I stopped eating here months ago.

VANESSA: You really still try for him. It's almost sweet.

△ The front door shuts. Claire stands alone over two cooling plates, then leans down and blows out the candle.

△ Her phone lights up — a text from Richard, sent an hour ago: "Booked you a trainer. Starts tomorrow. Do something about yourself."

CLAIRE (V.O.): The first thing he's handed me in years. An insult, with a start time.

△ Claire reads it twice, sets the phone face-down, hard, and doesn't cry.

## EP1 - S#2  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — home gym
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Claire waits in the gym, arms crossed. She's done her hair. Put on the good set.

CLAIRE (V.O.): I told myself I dressed up out of spite. And I'm already lying — she isn't even here yet.

△ The door opens. The woman who walks in is nothing like a trainer — short dark hair swept back, a sharp jaw, a leather jacket she has no intention of working out in. She takes Claire in, head to toe, slow, and grins.

EVE: Well. Nobody warned me the wife was the best-looking thing in this house.

△ She crosses the room easy, unhurried, and turns the deadbolt behind her like she owns the place.

CLAIRE: ...That door usually stays open.

EVE: Trust me, gorgeous — you'll want it shut. Arms down. Let me get a look at what your husband's been wasting.

△ Claire's arms come down before she's decided to obey.

CLAIRE (V.O.): Who talks to me like this? When did anyone last talk to me at all?

△ Eve steps in close and lays one warm palm flat on Claire's stomach, her thumb moving slow.

EVE: Right here. This is where three years of being ignored goes to sit. Breathe for me — let a little of it go.

△ Claire's breath snags. No one has stood this close to her in longer than she can remember.

CLAIRE: You're not a trainer.

EVE: No. I'm the best mistake your husband is ever going to make. He booked me for you, sweetheart. He just has no clue what for.

CLAIRE: ...I should go.

EVE: You curled your hair for a gym session. Women don't do that on their way to nowhere.

△ Claire's face burns. Her feet stay exactly where they are.

CLAIRE (V.O.): Leave. Pick up your keys and leave.

[END HOOK]
EVE: Tomorrow you'll swear this never happened. And tomorrow night you'll be lying on the cold half of that bed, wishing it would.

## EP2 - S#1  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — home gym · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Claire still hasn't moved. Eve circles behind her, close, and gathers her hair to one side, baring the back of her neck.

CLAIRE (V.O.): Move. Say something. Anything that isn't yes.

EVE: You want to know what I love about a woman who's been ignored this long? She's honest. Her body hasn't remembered how to lie yet.

△ Eve sets her mouth just below Claire's ear, not quite a kiss, and speaks against the skin.

EVE: Your husband's somewhere in this house right now. Say his name and I'll walk out that door.

CLAIRE: ...That's not fair.

EVE: Neither is a woman like you eating dinner alone every night. Turn around.

△ Claire turns to tell her to leave. Eve is closer than she expected, grinning like she already knows the ending.

CLAIRE: I'm not the kind of woman who—

EVE: You're exactly the kind. You just got married before anyone bothered to find out.

△ Eve tips Claire's chin up with one finger and waits there, letting her feel the pull.

CLAIRE (V.O.): Don't. Don't. Don't—

△ Claire closes the gap herself. The kiss is furious, years of nothing tearing loose at once. Her fists knot in Eve's jacket and pull.

△ Eve takes her time, then eases back, catching Claire's lower lip on the way out.

CLAIRE: That can't happen again.

[END HOOK]
EVE: Gorgeous, you kissed me. Tomorrow you'll do worse — and you'll call it my fault.

Hard Cut.

## EP3 - S#1  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — home gym
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Claire is on the mat before the session even starts, watching the door.

CLAIRE (V.O.): I got here twenty minutes early. I'm choosing not to think about what that means.

△ Eve strolls in with a slim black bag, in no hurry, already smiling.

EVE: Look at you. Early, dressed up, pretending you're not. My favorite kind of liar.

CLAIRE: Just show me a workout.

EVE: Oh, we'll work. Wrists — together, out in front.

CLAIRE: I'm not doing whatever that is.

△ Eve takes both her wrists in one hand, easy, and lifts them over Claire's head to the wall bar. Claire could pull free. She doesn't.

EVE: That's the part that gives you away. I left you every second to stop me. You spent them holding still for me.

△ Eve drags a single fingertip down the inside of Claire's raised arm, along her ribs, to her hip. Claire shivers the whole length of it.

CLAIRE (V.O.): He hired her. She's a stranger. I can't be doing this — I can't—

EVE: Stop arguing with yourself. I can hear it from here.

△ Eve sets her teeth, light, at the tendon of Claire's throat. Claire's spine comes clean off the wall.

CLAIRE: ...Oh my god.

EVE: We haven't even started. Keep those hands up. They come down, I stop — and you really don't want me to stop.

△ Eve lets go. Claire keeps her wrists pinned above her own head, chest heaving, and doesn't lower them.

△ Eve unzips the black bag, watching Claire's face as the first thing inside catches the light.

[END HOOK]
EVE: Good girl. Now let's see what your husband's money actually bought.

Hard Cut.

## EP4 - S#1  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — master bedroom
Characters: Claire

△ Claire lies awake in the huge bed, alone. She turns into the pillow, then onto her back, restless, thighs pressed together.

CLAIRE (V.O.): Three years I slept fine in this bed. One week of her and I can't lie still in it.

△ She reaches for her phone, opens Eve's number, thumb hovering. Types nothing. Closes it. Opens it again.

CLAIRE (V.O.): A married woman in her husband's house, aching for the woman he hired to ruin her. And I'm going to go down there anyway.

△ Claire pushes the covers back and gets up.

## EP4 - S#2  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — home gym · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Claire slips into the dark gym in a robe. Eve is on the bench like she'd been waiting all night, not surprised at all.

EVE: There she is. Right on time. My time — not yours.

CLAIRE: You didn't know I'd come.

EVE: Gorgeous, I knew before you did.

△ Claire crosses the room and stops a foot short, hands trembling at her sides.

CLAIRE: One time. To get you out of my head.

EVE: No. You came down here to get me further in. Say what you actually want, or go back upstairs and lie to the ceiling some more.

CLAIRE (V.O.): Say it. Just once, out loud, say what you came down here for.

△ Claire's composure breaks all at once. She grabs the front of Eve's shirt and pulls her up off the bench.

CLAIRE: I want you to not stop this time. Any of it.

EVE: There she is.

△ Eve pushes the robe off Claire's shoulders and walks her backward until the backs of her knees hit the weight bench and she sits.

△ Eve sinks down over her, one knee riding up between Claire's, and takes her jaw in hand.

[END HOOK]
EVE: Then take off everything he never once bothered to look at. Slow. I want to watch you decide to do it.

Hard Cut.

## EP5 - S#1  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — home gym · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Claire's hands go to the top button. She stops, watching Eve watch her.

CLAIRE: The lights are on.

EVE: They stay on. I've waited all week to see your face when it finally happens. I'm not missing it in the dark.

△ Claire works the buttons open, slow, and lets the top slip from her shoulders. Eve's gaze moves over her like a hand.

CLAIRE (V.O.): No one's looked at me like I'm worth looking at in years. She looks at me like she's starving.

EVE: Look at you. Three years wasted. Criminal.

△ Eve presses her back to the bench and pins both her wrists over her head with one hand.

CLAIRE: I've never done this — not with a — I don't know how this goes—

EVE: You don't have to know a thing, sweetheart. That's the whole point of me. Hold still and let me do what he never learned how to.

△ Eve kisses a slow line down the center of Claire's chest, her stomach, lower. Claire's back lifts clean off the bench.

CLAIRE: Eve— I can't— that's—

EVE: You can. And you're going to be loud about it. This whole empty house should hear what he's been throwing away.

△ Claire's free hand flies out and locks around the leg of the bench.

CLAIRE (V.O.): Oh god. Oh god, I'm—

[END HOOK]
△ Claire's whole body pulls taut, then breaks apart, and Eve doesn't stop.

Hard Cut.

## EP6 - S#1  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — master bedroom
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Late morning light. Claire lies wrecked and warm in the sheets. Eve is propped on an elbow, tracing lazy lines down her bare back.

CLAIRE (V.O.): This is where the guilt is supposed to show up. I keep checking for it. Nothing comes.

CLAIRE: You should have been gone hours ago.

EVE: Probably. But you make a very convincing case for staying, and you're not even trying to.

△ Claire rolls over to face her, studying her too closely.

CLAIRE: You always know the exact thing to say. Every time. Like you've done this a hundred times.

EVE: I have. That's the one thing I won't lie to you about.

△ The word hangs there. Claire's eyes narrow, a question surfacing behind them.

CLAIRE: Done what, exactly. What did my husband pay you to do to me?

EVE: ...Ask me that in an hour. Not now.

CLAIRE (V.O.): Ask her now. Whatever it is — ask her now, before you lose your nerve.

△ But Eve leans in, and Claire lets the question slide — one more hour — and pulls her down by the back of the neck.

[END HOOK]
CLAIRE: One hour. Then you tell me everything. All of it.

## EP7 - S#1  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — Richard's study
Characters: Claire, Richard

△ Claire opens the study door looking for Eve and stops cold. Richard sits at a wall of monitors. One screen frozen on the gym. Another on this bedroom.

CLAIRE: ...There are cameras in our bedroom.

RICHARD: In every room. There's a very good reason for it. Sit.

△ Claire doesn't sit. Richard drags a video window wide across the wall — Claire, arched off the bench under Eve — and leaves it hanging there.

CLAIRE (V.O.): That's me. That's me on his wall, and he was watching. He was always watching.

RICHARD: I hired her, Claire. To do exactly that. A wife caught on tape with another woman walks out of the divorce with nothing. You did all the hard work for me.

△ Claire stares at the frozen frame of herself, then slowly at him. Whatever he's waiting for — shame, tears — doesn't come.

CLAIRE: You paid a woman to seduce me. And you sat in that chair and watched every second of it.

RICHARD: I paid for evidence. I got a very thorough show. Frankly, I underestimated you.

CLAIRE (V.O.): He built a trap out of the one thing I was starving for. And he thinks that makes me the fool in this room.

[END HOOK]
CLAIRE: You watched all of it. Good. Then you already know I wasn't pretending.

Hard Cut.

## EP8 - S#1  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — home gym
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Eve is pacing the gym when Claire walks in — and for the first time, Claire is the one who turns the deadbolt.

CLAIRE (V.O.): A week ago that lock scared me. Tonight I'm the one throwing it.

EVE: You found out.

CLAIRE: That my husband bought you to bury me. That he watched from that chair. That you knew every camera in this house and put your hands on me anyway.

EVE: I deleted the ones that mattered. I was going to tell you tonight, I swear to—

CLAIRE: I don't want the apology.

△ Claire crosses the room and takes Eve's chin in her hand — the exact grip Eve used on her that first day — and tips it up. Eve lets her.

CLAIRE (V.O.): She taught me this one herself. Let's see how she likes the other side of it.

EVE: ...Then what do you want.

△ Claire steps in until there's no space left between them and speaks against Eve's mouth.

CLAIRE: He paid you to make me fall. So finish the job. Every camera in this room still running. Let my husband watch the wife he threw out come all the way back to life.

△ Eve's easy control finally cracks — she grabs Claire by the waist and hauls her in.

△ Claire glances up once at the red light in the corner, smiles, and pulls Eve down beneath it, her mouth already at Eve's throat.

[END HOOK]
CLAIRE: Give him a very thorough show.

Hard Cut.
