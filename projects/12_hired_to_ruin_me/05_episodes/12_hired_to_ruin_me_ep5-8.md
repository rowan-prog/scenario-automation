# I Married the Woman Hired to Ruin Me — EP5-8

## EP5 - S#1  DAY / EXT / Ashford estate — pool cabana · Continuous
Characters: Eve, Claire

△ Inside the shaded cabana, Eve pins Claire against the wooden post, one hand flat on the wood beside Claire's head.

EVE (low, rough): No camera reaches this corner. I checked. It's just you and me.

CLAIRE (breathless, chin up): Nothing's happening here. I'm his wife.

△ Eve drags one hand slow up Claire's bare arm to the side of Claire's throat.

EVE (silky, close): Then tell your skin that. You're already shaking.

CLAIRE (unsteady, holding on): That's the cold. It means nothing.

△ Claire's fingers twist into the front of Eve's shirt and pull Eve in flush.

EVE (rough, thrown): Your mouth says nothing. Your hands just pulled me flush against you.

△ Claire lifts her chin and looks straight into Eve's eyes.

CLAIRE (quiet, hungry): You're the first person who's looked at me in three years.

△ Eve's steady face cracks for half a second.

EVE (low, claiming): Then nobody else gets to look. Not him. Only me.

△ Eve kisses Claire, hard.

△ Claire's arms lock around Eve's neck and drag Eve down onto the low bench.

△ Claire's back arches up off the bench, pressing her whole body into Eve.

CLAIRE (wrecked, denying): I'm not— this doesn't mean anything. Don't read into it.

EVE (low, rough): You're arching up off the bench and telling me it's nothing.

△ Eve drags her open mouth down the side of Claire's throat.

△ Claire's chest heaves fast under Eve, her mouth falling open.

△ Claire's head tips back against the cushion, both hands fisting the front of Eve's shirt.

## EP5 - S#2  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — Richard's study · Continuous
Characters: Richard, Eve, Simone (voice)

△ Richard leans back at the monitor wall, watching a grid of live feeds, grinning.

RICHARD (gleeful): Look at her — panting after some gym girl. My own wife. Harmless.

△ Richard swivels his chair toward Eve, who stands in the doorway in her trainer jacket.

RICHARD (cold): Get me the bedroom on camera. Then the prenup's worth nothing and she walks out with nothing.

△ Eve's phone buzzes in her hand, the screen lit with one word: SIMONE.

SIMONE (voice, business-cold): You've gone quiet, Eve. You don't walk away from a signed contract. Deliver, or I do it the ugly way.

## EP5 - S#3  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — master bedroom · Continuous
Characters: Claire

△ Claire pauses at the mirror, blouse half-buttoned, and stares up at the vent grille above the glass.

CLAIRE (guarded): That wasn't there this morning.

△ Claire climbs onto the low bench and leans toward a pinpoint of glass catching the light inside the grille.

CLAIRE (wary, low): Something's tucked in the vent.

[END HOOK]
△ Claire's fingertip touches the tiny lens hidden behind the grille.

CLAIRE (guarded): What is that?

Hard Cut.

## EP6 - S#1  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — master bedroom · Continuous
Characters: Claire

△ Claire works the small lens free of the grille and holds it in her open palm — a camera the size of a shirt button, trailing a thin wire.

CLAIRE (shaken): It's a camera. In my bedroom.

△ Claire's hand closes around it and she turns slowly, looking at the closet, the bed, the doorway.

CLAIRE (breath going short): How long. How long has this been here.

△ Claire sits down hard on the edge of the bed, the camera clutched against her chest.

## EP6 - S#2  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — master bedroom · Continuous
Characters: Claire

△ Claire opens her hand and stares at the camera, her jaw tight.

CLAIRE (low, working it out): Richard put you here. To watch me. To catch me.

△ Claire's eyes move to the window that looks down on the home gym.

CLAIRE (quiet): The trainer showed up three days after he stopped touching me. Three days.

△ Claire shakes her head hard and grips the bed sheet.

CLAIRE (denying): No. Not her. She's the only real thing in this house.

△ Claire goes still, and her hand comes up to cover her mouth.

CLAIRE (going quiet, barely out): She always knew exactly where the cameras weren't.

## EP6 - S#3  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — hallway · Continuous
Characters: Eve, Claire

△ Eve comes around the corner in her trainer jacket and stops when she sees Claire standing in the hall, the camera raised in Claire's fist.

EVE (careful, mask slipping): Claire. Put that down. Let's talk somewhere else.

CLAIRE (level, cold): You knew where they were. Every corner you pulled me into. Every single time.

△ Eve takes one step forward, her hand half-raised.

EVE (low, thrown): It's not — let me explain. Please.

△ Claire backs away, her spine straightening, the camera still held up between them.

[END HOOK]
△ Claire looks Eve dead in the eyes, her face gone still and cold.

CLAIRE (cold): Who are you. Really.

Hard Cut.

## EP7 - S#1  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — hallway · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Eve's phone lights up in her jacket pocket, the screen glowing through the fabric.

EVE (fast, reaching for it): Claire, don't—

△ Claire snatches the phone before Eve can and turns the screen to face herself.

△ INSERT — the phone screen: RICHARD — "Half now, half when you get the bedroom footage. Don't let my wife stall you."

CLAIRE (breaking, raw): He paid you?! My own husband paid a stranger to seduce me and film it — so he could throw me out with nothing!

EVE (raw): Claire. Listen to me. That message is old, I—

CLAIRE (barely breathing): You're not a trainer. There was never a trainer.

## EP7 - S#2  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — hallway · Continuous
Characters: Eve, Claire

EVE (stepping in, rough): I can't deny the job. I won't. But somewhere in it I stopped acting. That part is real.

CLAIRE (shaking her head): Real. You filmed me for him. You picked the corners so he wouldn't see the good parts.

△ Eve reaches for Claire's wrist.

EVE (breaking, fierce): I deleted every frame that mattered! I lied to him for days — for you! Don't you dare put me in the same box as him.

△ Claire pulls her arm out of Eve's reach and backs against the wall.

CLAIRE (hollow): I never even said it out loud — and you already had all of me. He threw me away, then sent you to take the rest.

## EP7 - S#3  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — hallway · Continuous
Characters: Vanessa, Claire, Eve

△ Vanessa strolls in from the stairs holding a thick envelope, smiling.

VANESSA (sweet, cruel): Divorce filing, sweetheart. Richard already has everything he needs on tape. You leave with the clothes on your back.

△ Vanessa sets the envelope on the hall table and pats it twice.

VANESSA (bored): I already picked which of your rooms I'm taking first.

△ Claire looks down at the envelope, then up at Eve, her eyes wet, her jaw locked.

[END HOOK]
△ Claire lifts one hand toward the door without looking away from Eve.

CLAIRE (breaking): Get out. You got what he paid for.

Hard Cut.

## EP8 - S#1  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — Eve's quarters
Characters: Eve, Simone (voice)

△ Eve stands at the dark window with her phone to her ear, the printed contract crushed in her other hand.

SIMONE (voice, business-cold): The client wants the bedroom footage tonight, Eve. Send it, you're free and paid. This is not a negotiation.

EVE (low, done): There is no footage. I deleted all of it. Tell him the deal's dead.

SIMONE (voice, sharpening): You don't walk away from a signed contract. You know what happens to girls who try.

△ Eve tears the contract down the middle and lets the two halves drop to the floor.

EVE (flat, final): Then it happens. I'm out.

## EP8 - S#2  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — master bedroom · Continuous
Characters: Eve, Claire

△ Claire opens the bedroom door a hand's width, the chain still on, and looks at Eve through the gap.

CLAIRE (cold, quiet): I told you to get out.

EVE (steady, low): I'm not here to touch you. I'm here to hand you a way out. Give me one minute.

△ Eve holds up the tiny camera and a phone.

EVE (fast, level): These cameras are his. He's about to put you on the street with nothing. But the footage he begged me for, plus what I know about the money he's hiding — that buries him, not you.

△ Claire's hand stays on the chain, but she doesn't push the door shut.

CLAIRE (guarded): Why would you hand me a weapon against him.

EVE (rough): Because I stopped working for him three days ago.

## EP8 - S#3  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — master bedroom · Continuous
Characters: Eve, Claire

△ Eve leans in close to the gap in the door, one hand flat on the door frame.

EVE (low, raw, dead serious): Your husband's the one who paid me to seduce you. But guess what — I think I'm actually crazy about you.

△ Eve keeps her eyes locked on Claire through the narrow gap.

EVE: Wanna run a real love affair on that bastard's money?

[END HOOK]
△ Claire stares at Eve through the gap, the offer hanging in the air between them. Claire's lips part to answer.

Hard Cut.
