# I Married the Woman Hired to Ruin Me — EP21-26

## EP21 - S#1  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — Richard's study
Characters: Richard, Vanessa

△ Richard stands at the monitor wall, the screens still dark from the night before, cables torn loose at the wall behind them.

RICHARD (cold): Two months. Two months that trainer had gaps in her sessions, right where my cameras go blind.

△ Vanessa watches him from the doorway, one shoulder against the frame.

VANESSA (sweet, careful): So the girl's sloppy. Fire her and move on, Richard.

RICHARD (cold): Sloppy people don't know exactly where a lens can't reach. Somebody handed her a map of my house.

VANESSA (bored): You think your wife drew it? She cried into a place setting for three years.

RICHARD (low): My wife couldn't plan a dinner. Someone's using her — and using my trainer to do it.

## EP21 - S#2  DAY / EXT / Ashford estate — pool edge · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ At the pool's edge in the last light, Claire sits pressed close against Eve's side, both their feet in the water.

CLAIRE (quiet): His screens are black. I clawed one hidden account back tonight. For one night I'm not afraid in my own house.

EVE (low): Don't get comfortable. You cornered a man who owns this whole city. He doesn't lose quiet.

△ Claire turns and rests her forehead against the side of Eve's jaw.

CLAIRE (low, hungry): Then give me tonight, before he gets loud.

△ Eve tips Claire's chin up and kisses her slow and deep.

△ Claire fists a hand in the front of Eve's shirt and drags her closer along the tile.

## EP21 - S#3  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — Richard's study · Continuous
Characters: Richard

△ Richard drags the trainer's frozen face wide across a monitor that flickers back to life.

RICHARD (low): I hired you through Simone's little agency. Time Simone told me what her girl's really doing in my house.

△ Richard lifts his phone and thumbs down to the name SIMONE.

[END HOOK]
RICHARD (cold): Somebody's running my wife at me. I'm going to find the hand and cut it off.

Hard Cut.

## EP22 - S#1  DAY / EXT / Ashford estate — front gate
Characters: Eve, Simone

△ Eve strides down to the front gate where Simone waits against a black car in a sharp coat.

EVE (flat, guarded): I told you to never come near this house. Every inch of it is wired.

SIMONE (business-cold): Then you shouldn't have gone dark. The client called me screaming. His tape is weeks late.

EVE (low): There is no tape. I deleted it. I'm out — I told you that.

SIMONE (cold): You don't get to be out. I still own your real name and everything buried under it.

△ Eve's jaw locks and her hand curls tight at her side.

EVE (low, controlled): You'd burn your own girl over one job.

SIMONE (cutting): One call to Ashford and you're not his trainer. You're the con artist who played his whole house — finished in this city, and so is she.

△ Simone slides into the car and lets the window glide down an inch.

SIMONE (cold): Deliver the wife, or I sell you both. You've got till the weekend.

## EP22 - S#2  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — hallway · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Claire stands at the tall hall window above the gate, watching the black car pull away.

CLAIRE (guarded): Who was that. She had you backed against a car.

△ Eve comes in from the drive and stops short when she sees Claire at the glass.

EVE (careful, deflecting): Old work. It's handled. Don't think about her.

CLAIRE (level): She didn't look handled. She looked like she owns a piece of you I've never seen.

EVE (low): Claire. There are parts of me I can't hand you yet. You have to trust that one.

△ Claire's face closes and she steps back out of Eve's reach.

CLAIRE (quiet, cutting): The last person who told me to trust him was filming my bedroom.

[END HOOK]
△ Claire turns and climbs the stairs without once looking back.

Hard Cut.

## EP23 - S#1  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — master bedroom · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Claire stops at the foot of the bed as Eve steps in and shuts the door behind her.

CLAIRE (hard, guarded): Her name. The woman at the gate. Start there.

EVE (careful): Simone. She ran me before I learned to run myself. That's all you need.

CLAIRE (cutting): "All I need." You keep deciding that for me. He used to do the exact same thing.

EVE (low, thrown): Don't put me next to him. I am nothing like him.

CLAIRE (level): Then tell me your real name. The one she owns. Say it out loud, right now.

△ Eve's mouth opens and closes, and her hand tightens white on the doorframe.

△ Claire watches the silence stretch too long and her face goes hard and still.

CLAIRE (quiet, wounded): You can't. You've had every inch of me. And I don't even know what to call you.

## EP23 - S#2  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — master bedroom · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

EVE (rough, fighting): A name doesn't make it real. I tore up a signed deal for you. I lie to a man who could bury me, every single day, for you.

CLAIRE (shaking her head): He paid you to make me feel chosen. To make me fall. The way you look at me — that was the job.

EVE (breaking, fierce): The job was a tape, and I deleted it. What's left is the part I can't switch off.

△ Claire backs into the bedpost and wraps both arms around herself.

CLAIRE (hollow): I trusted one person in three years. He turned out to be a job wearing a face.

△ Eve reaches out and Claire steps clean past her hand.

CLAIRE (quiet, final): I'll run the con with you. It's smart. I don't have to hand you the rest of me to do it.

[END HOOK]
△ Claire pulls the bedroom door wide and holds it open for Eve to go.

CLAIRE (cold): Sleep in your own room tonight. Partner.

Hard Cut.

## EP24 - S#1  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — greenhouse
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Claire pushes through the glass door of the greenhouse and finds Eve alone among the dark planters.

CLAIRE (quiet, guarded): The walls in this house are thin. I heard you on the phone with her tonight.

EVE (low): Then you heard me tell her to burn me. There's no tape, and now there's no deal shielding me either.

CLAIRE (level): So he can destroy you now. The same way he can destroy me.

EVE (rough, steady): I made us the same on purpose. I couldn't give you a name, so I gave you that.

△ Claire crosses the floor and stops a breath away from Eve.

CLAIRE (low): You handed him the knife that ends you. For me.

EVE (low, claiming): I chose you when it cost me everything I had left. I'd do it again right now.

## EP24 - S#2  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — greenhouse · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Claire fists both hands in the front of Eve's shirt and walks her back into the cool glass wall.

CLAIRE (low, hungry): He paid you to take me. Tonight I'm the one taking you.

△ Eve's steady face cracks and her hands settle on Claire's hips without moving her.

EVE (rough, thrown): Look at you. Running me for once.

△ Claire slides both hands up into Eve's hair and holds her mouth right where she wants it.

CLAIRE (breathless, owning it): No more corners. No more nothing-happened. I'm choosing this out loud.

△ Eve's control snaps and she turns them, pinning Claire back against the glass.

EVE (low, claiming): You chose. Now hold still and let me have what's mine.

△ Eve drags her open mouth down the side of Claire's bared throat.

△ Claire's head drops back against the glass and both her hands lock in Eve's hair.

[END HOOK]
△ On the bench beside them, Eve's phone lights up with one word: SIMONE.

△ Eve glances at the screen once and turns Claire's face back to hers without reaching for it.

EVE (low, against Claire's mouth): Let her ring. Whatever she does next, she does to both of us now.

Hard Cut.

## EP25 - S#1  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — Richard's study
Characters: Richard, Simone (voice)

△ Richard leans back at the monitor wall with his phone on speaker, Simone's voice filling the study.

SIMONE (voice, business-cold): Your trainer isn't stalling, Mr. Ashford. She flipped. She wiped your footage weeks ago, and she's covering for your wife.

RICHARD (thrown, cold): Covering. The girl I paid to sink her.

SIMONE (voice, cold): She shredded a signed contract over your wife. I'm handing her to you before she costs me another cent.

△ Richard sets the phone down and drags the trainer's frozen face wide across the screen.

RICHARD (smug, hardening): So my wife grew a spine. Fine. A spine isn't a brain — the hired girl's the one running her.

△ Richard drags a finger across the blind corner of the frozen gym feed, where the picture always went dark.

RICHARD (cold): I still want my tape. My wife and a girl. Only now I film the help who thought she could out-play me — and I end them both.

## EP25 - S#2  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — sunroom · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Vanessa, Eve

△ Vanessa steps into the sunroom where Claire is trimming flower stems and drops her voice low.

VANESSA (low, careful): Richard just took a call about your trainer. He knows she quit playing for his side.

CLAIRE (guarded, flat): She's a trainer. There's nothing to know.

VANESSA (sweet, knowing): Sweetheart, I've watched the two of you. Save the wife act for his cameras. He's building a trap — move her tonight.

△ Vanessa taps Claire once on the cheek and strolls back out.

△ Eve stops in the far doorway, her face gone tight.

[END HOOK]
EVE (low, grim): He's done guessing, Claire. He's hunting now.

△ Eve lifts her eyes to the corner of the ceiling above Claire.

EVE (low): Every blind spot we've got — he's about to fill each one with a lens.

Hard Cut.

## EP26 - S#1  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — home gym
Characters: Richard, Vanessa

△ Richard crouches in the home gym and screws a fresh lens into the blind corner past the mirrors.

RICHARD (cold): Every dark spot in this house closes today. This corner, the cabana, the greenhouse, the stair landing.

△ Vanessa leans in the doorway, watching him wire the corner they always used to miss.

VANESSA (sweet): You're turning your own home into a jail, darling.

RICHARD (smug): I'm building a stage. Next time that girl puts her hands on my wife, I'll have it in crystal — and I'll end them both.

△ Richard steps back and watches the new lens blink its small red eye.

RICHARD (cold): No more holes in my feed. Not one.

## EP26 - S#2  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — greenhouse · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Eve runs her fingers along the greenhouse glass and stops on a fresh screw hole with a lens sunk behind it.

EVE (low, grim): Here too. He found the greenhouse. He found every corner we had.

△ Claire steps close and looks at the tiny lens fixed right where they stood last night.

CLAIRE (quiet, steady): So last night was the last one we get in the dark.

EVE (rough): If he catches us on one of these, it isn't a divorce anymore. He ends us. Both of us.

△ Claire takes Eve's face in both hands and holds her eyes.

CLAIRE (low, certain): Then we stop hiding in corners. We take the whole house — him watching, all of it.

## EP26 - S#3  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — greenhouse
Characters: Claire, Richard, Vanessa

△ Richard sweeps in through the glass door to check his new corner lens, Vanessa a step behind him.

RICHARD (gleeful, smug): Clear your week, wife. We're throwing a party — the whole city, right here, Saturday.

CLAIRE (quiet, cool): A party. For what.

RICHARD (cold, smiling): To show them all what a perfect marriage looks like. Every camera in this house running.

[END HOOK]
△ Richard turns on his heel and walks back out through the glass.

△ Claire lifts her eyes to the new red light blinking in the greenhouse corner.

Hard Cut.
