# I Married the Woman Hired to Ruin Me — EP9-12

## EP9 - S#1  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — master bedroom · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Claire slides the chain off the track and pulls the door wide open.

CLAIRE (cold, quiet): Get in here before somebody sees you at my door.

△ Eve steps inside past Claire.

△ Claire pushes the door shut behind them.

CLAIRE (level, hard): I'm not saying yes for you. I'm saying it because I want to watch him lose everything he owns.

EVE (low): Then we already want the same thing.

△ Eve reaches for Claire's hand.

△ Claire pulls her arm back and folds both arms across her chest.

CLAIRE (guarded): No. You don't get to hold my hand like the last three weeks were real. You lied to my face every single day.

EVE (rough, thrown): Not the part that counts. And you already know which part that was.

CLAIRE (cold): I don't know one true thing about you. So start there.

EVE (steady, low): I tore his contract in half before I came to this door. All I've got left is your side. Partners?

△ Claire holds Eve's eyes for a long beat.

CLAIRE (quiet, walled): Partners. Not lovers. Not yet.

## EP9 - S#2  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — master bedroom · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Claire opens her palm and shows Eve the tiny lens she pried out of the vent.

CLAIRE (low): This thing watched me sleep. Tell me there aren't more of them in here.

EVE (steady): That was the only eye in this room. Right now we're the only two people alive who know it's dead.

△ Eve lifts her hand toward Claire's face.

△ Claire catches Eve's wrist and holds it still between them.

CLAIRE (cold, breath tight): Every other room, his cameras stay on. So you keep playing the exact thing he paid for. You keep seducing me right in his lens.

EVE (low, thrown): You want him watching that.

[END HOOK]
△ Claire folds Eve's caught hand into a fist and presses it back against Eve's chest.

CLAIRE (cold): I'll let him film me handing him his own funeral.

Hard Cut.

## EP10 - S#1  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — sunroom
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Claire lies back across the long sunroom lounger.

△ Eve leans over Claire and braces one hand on the cushion by her hip.

△ Eve dips her mouth to Claire's ear, her eyes flicking up to the smoke detector on the ceiling.

EVE (whisper): The lens is in that smoke detector. Give him a good show.

△ Claire arches up under Eve and lets her mouth fall open toward the ceiling.

CLAIRE (breathless, loud): We can't— God, we can't, I'm his wife—

△ Eve drags one hand slow up the outside of Claire's thigh.

EVE (silky, loud): Nobody's coming to save you. It's just us.

△ Claire fists a hand in Eve's collar and pulls her down, her chin tipped up to the lens.

CLAIRE (breathless, loud): I shouldn't want this— I can't make myself stop—

△ Claire turns her lips to Eve's ear and drops under the room's hearing.

CLAIRE (whisper, cold): Tell me he's watching. Tell me he's eating this up.

EVE (whisper): He's glued to the screen. You're perfect.

## EP10 - S#2  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — kitchen · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Eve steers Claire into the blind corner behind the pantry wall.

EVE (low, rough): No camera reaches back here. Drop the act. This part's real.

△ Claire boosts herself up onto the edge of the counter.

△ Eve steps in between Claire's knees and sets both hands on her thighs.

CLAIRE (breath breaking): Out there I can play it all day. In here I look at you and still see the girl he paid for.

EVE (rough, thrown): Then look harder. Off his cameras, everything I do is because I want to — not because he bought it.

△ Claire hooks her heels behind Eve's legs and drags her in flush.

CLAIRE (breathless, denying): My body forgives you way too fast. I don't.

△ Eve fists a hand in Claire's hair and holds her mouth a breath away.

EVE (low, wanting): Your heels just pulled me in. That's the part that's true.

## EP10 - S#3  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — Richard's study · Continuous
Characters: Richard, Vanessa

△ Richard freezes the sunroom footage on Claire's open mouth and grins at the screen.

RICHARD (gleeful): Look at her. Coming apart for the hired help. She handed it straight to me.

VANESSA (sweet, bored): So end it. Why is she still wearing your ring.

△ Richard laces his hands behind his head and rocks back in the chair.

[END HOOK]
RICHARD (gleeful): One more week. I let her dig the hole a little deeper — then I file, and she's gone with nothing.

Hard Cut.

## EP11 - S#1  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — Richard's study
Characters: Richard, Vanessa

△ Richard sets his phone on the desk on speaker and signs across a thick stack of transfer papers.

RICHARD (smug): Move all of it tonight. The accounts, the deeds, the offshore holdings — buried under a name she'll never trace, the keys locked in my safe. She can't touch a cent.

VANESSA (sweet): And once it's all sitting in there?

RICHARD (cold, smug): Then on paper I'm a broke man. I file, the court sees nothing, and she can't touch one cent. It's done. There's no undo button.

△ Richard slides the signed stack into a drawer and turns the little key.

RICHARD (gleeful): Every dollar I own just walked somewhere my wife will never reach it.

## EP11 - S#2  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — library · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Eve draws Claire down into the deep leather chair and settles Claire astride her lap.

EVE (low): This chair sits in the one corner he never wired. No lens on us here.

△ Claire braces both hands on the chair back behind Eve's head.

CLAIRE (quiet, thawing): I watched you throw away everything you were owed. You really did burn it all down for this.

EVE (rough, low): For you. Tell me you're starting to believe that.

△ Claire lowers her forehead against Eve's.

CLAIRE (breathless, softer): I'm starting to. Don't make me regret it.

△ Eve slides both hands up Claire's spine and pulls her down flush.

EVE (low, claiming): Nobody else gets to see this version of you. He owned the whole house and never once knew it existed.

## EP11 - S#3  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — dressing room
Characters: Claire, Vanessa

△ Vanessa runs the hangers down Claire's closet rail and pulls the dresses she likes.

VANESSA (sweet, bored): You won't need these where you're headed. I'm calling them early.

△ Claire stops in the doorway and watches Vanessa load up her arms.

CLAIRE (quiet, hollow): Take all of it. He swaps people out the same exact way. One closet emptied, the next one moved in.

△ Vanessa pauses with a red dress half off the rail.

CLAIRE (soft, wounded): I was standing right where you're standing three years ago. Enjoy the view while it's still yours.

△ Claire turns and walks out of the dressing room.

[END HOOK]
△ Vanessa lowers the red dress slowly and stares at the empty doorway.

VANESSA (quiet, thrown): ...One closet emptied. The next one moved in.

Hard Cut.

## EP12 - S#1  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — Eve's quarters
Characters: Eve, Claire

△ Eve turns her phone to Claire, a frozen video of Richard at his desk filling the screen.

EVE (low): I still have a key into his own feed. His camera, his mouth. He names the trust, the accounts, all of it, out loud.

△ Claire takes the phone and watches Richard mouth the words on the small screen.

CLAIRE (quiet, fierce): He moved every dollar he has and filmed himself telling us where. He built our whole case for us.

EVE (low): That clip never expires. Wherever he buries it next, this is the map back to it.

## EP12 - S#2  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — Eve's quarters · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Claire sets the phone face-down on the nightstand and turns back to Eve.

CLAIRE (low, steady): No camera in this room. No act tonight. Nobody's paying me, nobody's making me.

△ Claire fists both hands in Eve's shirt and walks her backward to the bed.

△ Eve drops to sit on the edge of the mattress, looking up at Claire.

EVE (rough, thrown): You're the one moving us this time.

CLAIRE (hungry): I'm done waiting to get talked into it. I want this. I'm choosing it.

△ Claire pushes Eve flat and climbs over her, knees bracketing Eve's hips.

△ Eve grips Claire's waist and pulls her down flush.

CLAIRE (breathless, sure): Not his wife in this room. Not your job. This one's mine.

## EP12 - S#3  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — Eve's quarters · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve, Vanessa (voice)

△ Eve's phone buzzes hard face-down against the nightstand wood.

△ Claire grabs the phone off the nightstand.

△ [PHONE POV] The hall-camera feed shows Vanessa walking straight for Eve's door.

CLAIRE (breath stopping): She's coming down your hall. Right now.

△ A fist knocks hard on the door, three times.

VANESSA (voice, sweet, cold, through the door): Open up, trainer. I want to know what you're really doing to Richard's wife.

[END HOOK]
△ Claire and Eve freeze half-dressed on the bed, both staring at the turning door handle.

Hard Cut.
