# I Married the Woman Hired to Ruin Me — EP33-38

## EP33 - S#1  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — Richard's study
Characters: Richard, Simone (voice)

△ Richard stands at the monitor wall, half the screens gone dead black, a frozen news headline glowing on the last live one.

RICHARD (cold): Half my board walked out of that gala. My accounts freeze by morning. Fine. The house is still mine — and every camera in it filmed exactly what my wife did in here.

△ Richard lifts his phone and thumbs a contact.

RICHARD (into phone): Your girl broke her contract and torched me in front of three hundred people. I paid for a tape. Now I want her gone.

SIMONE (voice, business-cold): Girls who walk on a signed contract don't disappear clean. And the shortest road to her runs through the wife she keeps guarding. Say the word.

RICHARD (smug): Consider it said.

## EP33 - S#2  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — kitchen · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Eve steps in behind Claire at the counter and closes both hands over Claire's hips.

EVE (low): Don't turn around yet. Let me hold you for one second before this gets ugly.

CLAIRE (quiet, leaning back into her): It's already ugly. He looked at me across dinner like he was pricing the furniture again.

△ Eve turns Claire by the waist and cages her against the counter's edge, dropping her forehead to Claire's.

EVE (rough, wanting): He owns a house full of dead screens and a name nobody will touch now. He does not own this.

△ Claire fists a hand in Eve's collar and drags her in flush.

CLAIRE (breathless): Then don't let go tonight. Whatever he does next — don't let go.

EVE (low, claiming): I picked you over the whole payout. I'm not blinking now.

[END HOOK]
△ Eve's phone lights with SIMONE, and she slips into the hall to answer, the kitchen door swinging shut behind her.

△ Richard fills the far doorway and turns the deadbolt behind him.

△ Claire backs into the counter, both hands gripping the cold edge.

RICHARD (cold): Just you and me now, wife. Time you paid for the show you put on.

Hard Cut.

## EP34 - S#1  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — kitchen · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Richard, Eve

△ Richard crosses the kitchen and grips Claire's jaw, forcing her chin up.

RICHARD (cold, brazen): You spread yourself out for some gym girl on my cameras. Every frame is mine. So I'll just take what I already paid to watch.

△ Claire twists her face free and shoves both palms hard against his chest.

CLAIRE (fierce): Get off me. I stopped being yours the day you sold me for a video.

△ Richard catches both her wrists in one fist and pins them flat to the counter.

RICHARD (low, ugly): You don't tell me no. Not in my house.

△ Richard drags his free hand down the strap of Claire's dress until it tears at the shoulder.

△ Claire drives her knee up into him and rakes her nails down his cheek.

△ Eve slams through the door and grabs Richard by the collar.

△ Eve hauls Richard back off Claire and into the far wall.

△ Eve jams her forearm across Richard's throat and holds him pinned.

EVE (low, deadly): You put your hands on her. Look at me while I decide what that costs.

## EP34 - S#2  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — kitchen · Continuous
Characters: Eve, Claire, Richard

△ Eve pulls Claire in against her chest and cups the back of Claire's head.

EVE (rough, shaken): I've got you. He is never getting near you again. Look at me — only me.

CLAIRE (breathless, fisting her shirt): You came.

EVE (low): I'll always come for you.

△ Richard slides down the wall and wipes the blood off his cheek, laughing.

RICHARD (smug, bleeding): Sweet. You want to know who holds her leash, gym girl? The woman who owns your contract. She's already sending someone for you.

[END HOOK]
△ Eve's phone buzzes on the counter, the screen lighting with the name SIMONE.

△ Eve goes still over Claire's shoulder, her eyes locked on the glowing screen.

RICHARD (smug): Right on time.

Hard Cut.

## EP35 - S#1  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — Eve's quarters · Continuous
Characters: Eve, Simone (voice)

△ Eve stands at the dark window with the phone to her ear, the torn contract still on the floor at her feet.

SIMONE (voice, business-cold): He doubled the fee to see you gone, Eve. You know how this ends. And the shortest road to you runs straight through the wife you keep shielding.

EVE (low, cornered): Leave her out of it. This is you and me.

SIMONE (voice, cold): Then stop handing me a lever. Walk away from the woman, and maybe you walk away breathing.

△ Eve lowers the phone and presses her forehead to the cold glass.

## EP35 - S#2  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — master bedroom · Continuous
Characters: Eve, Claire

△ Eve stands in the bedroom doorway with a bag hitched over one shoulder.

EVE (low, wrecked): They'll come at me through you. As long as I'm in this house, there's a target on your back. I can't do that to you.

CLAIRE (breaking): So that's it. You seduce me for his money, you burn your whole life down for me — and now you leave, to save me again.

EVE (rough): This isn't the job. It's the one thing I can still get right. Let me go, and you stay clean.

△ Claire grips the door frame between them, her knuckles gone white.

CLAIRE (barely holding): Don't you dare call leaving love. Everyone in my life left to protect me. Every single one.

△ Eve lifts a hand toward Claire's face and stops just short of her cheek.

EVE (low, breaking): I'll end him from the outside. I promise you that.

[END HOOK]
△ Eve walks out through the front door into the dark, and the lock clicks shut behind her.

△ Claire stands alone in the empty hall, the torn strap of her dress still hanging off her shoulder.

Hard Cut.

## EP36 - S#1  NIGHT / EXT / Ashford estate — front gate · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Claire catches Eve at the iron gate at the end of the drive, headlights sweeping past on the road.

CLAIRE (breathless, furious): You don't get to decide I'm safer without you. I'm done being kept safe. I want you.

EVE (rough, thrown): You have no idea what's coming for me.

CLAIRE (fierce): Then it comes for both of us. I didn't crawl out of that house to lose the one real thing I found in it.

△ Claire fists both hands in Eve's jacket and slams her back against the gate.

EVE (low, breaking): I tried to do one unselfish thing in my whole life.

CLAIRE (against her mouth): Do a selfish one instead. Stay.

△ Eve grips Claire by the waist and lifts her up against the iron bars.

△ Eve kisses Claire hard, one hand fisted in her wet hair.

△ Claire locks both legs around Eve's hips and drags her in closer.

△ Eve presses Claire back into the bars as the rain comes down over them.

EVE (rough, against her throat): I'm done being noble about you. You're mine, and I'm not running from that anymore.

CLAIRE (breathless): Then stop talking about leaving and hold on.

[END HOOK]
△ Claire pulls back just far enough to hold Eve's eyes, water running down both their faces.

CLAIRE (low, certain): No more running. We go back in there and we take every last thing he has.

EVE (rough): Everything.

Hard Cut.

## EP37 - S#1  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — hallway · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve, Richard

△ Richard steps out of the study and plants himself between the lovers and the stairs.

RICHARD (cold, brazen): You dragged her back into my house. After all of it. You really don't learn, do you.

EVE (level): Move, Richard. It's finished. You're just the last one to know.

△ Richard lunges and locks both hands around Eve's throat.

△ Richard drives Eve back into the wall and leans his weight in.

RICHARD (low, ugly): Should've stayed a hired mouth and taken the money.

CLAIRE (screaming): Let go of her!

△ Claire grabs Richard's forearm and wrenches it, her nails digging into his wrist.

△ Eve claws at Richard's fingers, her heels scraping against the floor.

△ Richard shoves Eve down onto the floor and steps back, chest heaving.

RICHARD (cold): Accidents happen to girls who break their contracts. Nobody's even going to ask.

△ Claire drops to her knees and puts herself between Eve and Richard.

CLAIRE (shaking, fierce): You touch her again and I finish you myself.

△ Eve pushes up onto one elbow and points at the small glass lens tucked in the crown molding above him.

EVE (hoarse, steady): You just did that under your own camera. The one you wired to bury her. Smile.

[END HOOK]
△ Richard's head snaps up toward the lens in the molding, the color draining out of his face.

Hard Cut.

## EP38 - S#1  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — Richard's study · Continuous
Characters: Eve, Claire, Vanessa

△ Eve sits at the monitor wall with the choking footage frozen on the center screen.

CLAIRE (quiet, watching it): That's the same camera he was going to use to throw me out with nothing.

EVE (low, hoarse): Now it puts him in a cell. His house, his wiring, his own two hands.

△ Vanessa steps into the study doorway with a folded slip of paper pinched between two fingers.

VANESSA (cool): That drive Claire took off me at the gala is encrypted. I lived off that man two years — I'm the only soul alive who knows his password.

CLAIRE (turning to her): Why hand it to us.

VANESSA (bored, cruel): Because he'd have done to me what he tried to do to her. I'd rather sit back and watch you two gut him.

△ Vanessa sets the slip on the desk and slides it across to Claire.

## EP38 - S#2  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — Richard's study · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Eve threads her fingers through Claire's over the little drive on the desk.

EVE (low): You said you wanted everything he has. This is where we start taking it.

CLAIRE (certain): Tonight. No more hiding, no more running, no more waiting him out.

△ Claire turns the drive over in her palm and lifts her eyes to the wall of Richard's own screens.

[END HOOK]
△ Claire looks straight into the nearest lens, every screen on the wall lit and pointed back at the house.

CLAIRE (low): Your turn to be watched.

Hard Cut.
