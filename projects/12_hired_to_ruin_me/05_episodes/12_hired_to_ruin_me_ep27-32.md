# I Married the Woman Hired to Ruin Me — EP27-32

## EP27 - S#1  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — Richard's study
Characters: Richard, Claire, Vanessa

△ Richard stands at the monitor wall, watching caterers set long tables on the ballroom feed below.

RICHARD (smug): Tomorrow night this house fills with press and every name that matters. You'll be on my arm all evening. The devoted Ashford wife.

△ Claire stops in the doorway, her hands folded low, her chin level.

CLAIRE (quiet): Devoted to what.

RICHARD (cold): The Sterling merger. I sign it live on that stage, every camera on it. You stand there and make me look like a man worth marrying.

△ Vanessa drifts in past Claire and drags one finger down the edge of the desk.

VANESSA (sweet, cruel): One perfect night, sweetheart. It's the last thing he'll ever ask of you.

△ Richard turns from the screens and holds his eyes on Claire a beat too long.

RICHARD (low): And keep that trainer of yours where I can see her. I like knowing where everyone stands.

## EP27 - S#2  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — gala ballroom · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Claire slips between the draped tables and finds Eve crouched at the stage, coiling black cable.

CLAIRE (low): He's putting his whole face on that stage. His name, his money, the merger — all in one room.

△ Eve rises and nods once toward the speaker stacks flanking the stage.

EVE (quiet): Then that's where it ends. Say it, and I run his own voice through every one of those.

△ Claire walks Eve into the dark behind the stage flat and fists a hand in her collar.

CLAIRE (breathless, claiming): Tomorrow I'm on his arm all night. A hundred cameras on me. I don't get to touch you once.

△ Eve catches Claire's wrist and drags it flat against her own chest.

EVE (rough, wanting): Then take it now. Before you have to go smile at him and pretend I'm hired help.

△ Claire pulls Eve down by the collar and kisses her hard, both of them backed into the dark behind the boards.

## EP27 - S#3  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — gala ballroom · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve, Richard

△ Claire breaks the kiss and keeps her forehead pressed to Eve's, both hands still knotted in the collar.

CLAIRE (low, fierce): Tomorrow, the second he lifts that microphone — you give the whole room his voice.

EVE (rough): The night he brags to a ballroom is the night he hangs himself. Good.

[END HOOK]
△ Cut to Richard in his study, leaning close to the ballroom feed at two shapes tangled in the dark.

RICHARD (low, sharpening): Who's down there with the lights off.

Hard Cut.

## EP28 - S#1  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — gala ballroom
Characters: Richard, Claire, Sterling

△ Richard walks Claire through the packed ballroom with her hand tucked over his arm, press cameras flashing at every step.

RICHARD (smug, low): Smile wider. They're already writing the headline. The Ashfords, still golden after three years.

△ Claire lifts her chin and gives the room a slow, warm smile.

CLAIRE (bright, performing): Everyone came. You must be so proud tonight, darling.

△ Sterling steps out of the crowd and shakes Richard's hand, a leather folder tucked under one arm.

STERLING (hearty): Richard. My people have the merger papers up on the stage table, ready for your signature. Quite a room you've built.

RICHARD (smug): Wait till you see the signing. I do love an audience.

△ Claire's smile holds steady as her eyes cut once toward the coat room doors.

## EP28 - S#2  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — coat room · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Claire steps into the coat room between racks of fur and wool, and Eve pulls her deep into the hanging coats.

EVE (low, rough): He's had you on his arm for an hour. Smiling up at him. I watched every second of it.

CLAIRE (breathless): It's a costume. You know it's a costume.

△ Eve presses Claire back into the wall of coats, one hand low on her hip, her mouth at Claire's jaw.

EVE (low, claiming): I know whose name he says and whose skin does this. Say the second one's mine.

△ Claire grabs a fistful of Eve's shirt and drags her flush between the coats.

CLAIRE (breathless, claiming): Yours. Now go — he's about to sign. Get to the booth.

△ Eve drags her open mouth once up the side of Claire's throat, then straightens and smooths her jacket flat.

## EP28 - S#3  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — gala ballroom · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Richard, Eve

△ Claire crosses back to Richard at the foot of the stage and slides her hand over his arm.

RICHARD (smug, to the room): Friends. Before I sign, let me say a word about the woman standing beside me.

△ Richard climbs the steps and lifts the microphone off its stand, the crowd falling quiet.

[END HOOK]
△ Eve slips into the dark booth at the back of the ballroom and rests one finger on the console.

Hard Cut.

## EP29 - S#1  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — gala ballroom
Characters: Richard, Claire, Eve

△ Richard raises the microphone and beams down at Claire from the stage.

RICHARD (smug, to the room): Three years ago I married a woman who has never once given me a reason to doubt her—

△ Claire meets his eyes and gives one small, calm nod.

△ Eve presses the console, and Richard's own recorded voice blasts from every speaker over his live one.

RICHARD (recording): I want my wife filmed with another woman's mouth on her. Bring me that clip and the prenup's toilet paper — she walks out with nothing.

△ Richard freezes at the microphone, his own voice still rolling across the silent crowd.

## EP29 - S#2  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — gala ballroom · Continuous
Characters: Richard, Claire, Sterling

△ The recording keeps playing over the frozen ballroom as guests turn from Richard to Claire and back.

RICHARD (recording): She's starving. A woman can't touch a cent of mine — that's the whole point. She'll never see a girl coming.

△ Claire climbs two steps and takes the second microphone off the podium, calm.

CLAIRE (clear, to the room): He hired a woman to seduce his own wife on camera. So he could throw me out with nothing. You all just heard him.

△ Sterling backs one step toward the crowd, clutching the merger folder to his chest.

STERLING (shaken): Richard — what in God's name is this.

## EP29 - S#3  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — gala ballroom · Continuous
Characters: Richard, Claire

△ Richard lunges for the console cable at the stage edge and rips it, but the recording only cuts to silence — the words already hang in the air.

RICHARD (scrambling, loud): It's doctored! Someone spliced that together — it's a stunt, a cheap stunt!

△ Claire lowers her microphone and looks at him, steady, in front of every lifted phone.

[END HOOK]
CLAIRE (cold, clear): Then say it was doctored to the fifty people who watched your face while it played.

Hard Cut.

## EP30 - S#1  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — gala ballroom · Continuous
Characters: Sterling, Richard, Claire

△ Sterling backs off the stage into the crowd, holding the merger folder up like something dirty.

STERLING (cold, loud): I came here to sign my name next to yours. After what this whole room just heard? I'm not signing anything. Not tonight, not ever.

△ Richard follows him to the stage edge, one hand out, his voice climbing.

RICHARD (rattled, loud): Sterling — wait — it's some jealous woman's theatre, don't you walk out on me—

△ A knot of guests near the stage edges back from Richard, their phones raised at his face.

△ Claire stands still at center stage while every phone in the room stays trained on Richard.

CLAIRE (quiet, cutting): Beg him louder. They can't hear the great Richard Ashford begging from the back.

## EP30 - S#2  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — gala ballroom · Continuous
Characters: Richard, Claire

△ Richard rounds back on Claire at center stage, spit flying, the crowd pressing closer.

RICHARD (losing it): You did nothing! Every dollar in this house is mine, buried in accounts your cheap lawyers will never find—

△ Richard catches himself a beat too late, the whole ballroom hearing the word buried.

△ A ripple of murmurs moves through the crowd as more phones lift.

CLAIRE (quiet, to him): There it is. You just told a room full of reporters you're hiding money. Thank you, darling.

## EP30 - S#3  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — gala ballroom · Continuous
Characters: Vanessa, Richard

△ Vanessa moves out of the crowd into the open floor below the stage, and every head turns to her.

RICHARD (grasping): Vanessa. Tell them. Tell them she's lying — tell them what she really is.

[END HOOK]
△ Vanessa looks up at Richard, then out at the cameras, and takes a slow breath.

VANESSA (calm, clear): Oh, I'll tell them everything, Richard. Starting with where the money's buried.

Hard Cut.

## EP31 - S#1  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — gala ballroom · Continuous
Characters: Vanessa, Richard, Claire

△ Vanessa walks to the foot of the stage and turns to face the crowd, her back to Richard.

VANESSA (calm, clear): I stood at his side for a year. I watched him plan to strip his wife bare and put her on the street. And I finally saw it — I'm only the next one he throws out.

RICHARD (stunned, loud): Vanessa. Shut your mouth. You don't get to—

△ Vanessa lifts a slim black drive between two fingers, high enough for the cameras to catch it.

VANESSA (cold, sweet): The offshore accounts. The shell companies. The name he really hides his fortune under. It's all right here.

△ Claire steps down and crosses the floor to stand beside Vanessa, the two of them facing the crowd together.

## EP31 - S#2  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — gala ballroom · Continuous
Characters: Vanessa, Claire, Richard

△ Vanessa sets the black drive into Claire's open palm and folds Claire's fingers shut over it.

VANESSA (low): Everything he buried so you'd have nothing. Bury him with it.

△ Richard shoves through the crowd toward them, two guests catching him by the arms.

RICHARD (frantic, loud): That's stolen! Those files are private — she has no right, that's my property—

△ Claire closes her fist around the drive and holds it against her chest.

CLAIRE (clear, to the room): He just called the proof of his own fraud his private property. In front of all of you.

## EP31 - S#3  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — service corridor · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Claire slips through a side door into the narrow service corridor and Eve catches her against the steel counter of the pass.

EVE (low, rough): You walked up on that stage with a hundred cameras on you and never blinked. You have no idea what that does to me.

CLAIRE (breathless): Then show me. Fast. They'll notice I'm gone.

△ Eve lifts Claire onto the steel counter and steps between her knees, both hands sliding up her thighs.

CLAIRE (breathless, claiming): His whole empire's on that drive and it's in my hand. We've got him. We actually have him.

△ Eve drags her mouth up Claire's throat and stops at her ear.

EVE (rough, claiming): Then let me be the one thing you touch tonight that isn't his ruin. Mine. Say it.

[END HOOK]
CLAIRE (breathless): Yours. All of it. Don't you dare stop—

Hard Cut.

## EP32 - S#1  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — gala ballroom · Continuous
Characters: Sterling, Richard, Claire

△ Sterling stops in the ballroom doorway with his coat on and turns back to the room one last time.

STERLING (cold, final): The merger's dead. I'm pulling every Ashford holding I'm in by morning. You're finished in this room, Richard.

△ Richard stands alone at the center of the emptying floor as guests file past him toward the doors.

RICHARD (hollow, loud): Come back — all of you — this is my house, this is my night—

△ Claire lifts the black drive so the last cameras catch it in her hand.

CLAIRE (clear, to the room): Everything he hid to leave me with nothing is on here. By morning none of it is his anymore.

## EP32 - S#2  NIGHT / EXT / Ashford estate — gala terrace · Continuous
Characters: Richard, Claire

△ Richard catches Claire by the arm out on the dark terrace, away from the last cameras, his grip hard.

RICHARD (low, dangerous): You didn't think of this. You've never thought of anything in your life. Somebody built this for you — somebody who knew where every camera in my house was.

△ Claire pulls her arm free and steps back against the stone rail.

CLAIRE (level): Let go of me. There's nothing left of you to be afraid of.

RICHARD (cold, quiet): There's plenty left. And whoever's been in my house — in my wife — I'll bury them so deep no drive digs them back out.

△ Richard's eyes move off Claire toward the ballroom doors, where Eve stands watching from the light.

## EP32 - S#3  NIGHT / EXT / Ashford estate — gala terrace · Continuous
Characters: Eve, Richard, Claire

△ Eve crosses the terrace fast and steps in between Richard and Claire, squaring up to him.

EVE (low, hard): You're done for the night. Walk away.

△ Richard looks Eve up and down, slow, and a cold smile pulls at his mouth.

RICHARD (venomous): The trainer. Of course. I paid you to ruin her — and you've been in her bed the whole time.

[END HOOK]
△ Richard steps back off the terrace toward the house, his eyes fixed on the two of them standing together.

RICHARD (cold, low): Enjoy tonight. It's the last one either of you gets.

Hard Cut.
