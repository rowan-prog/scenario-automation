# I Married the Woman Hired to Ruin Me — EP39-44

## EP39 - S#1  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — Richard's study
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Claire sits in Richard's own chair at the dark monitor wall, a phone in her hand, Eve leaning over her shoulder.

CLAIRE (low, steady): Whitlock funds half of everything Richard touches. Send him the clip where Richard laughs about burying the money.

△ Eve reaches past Claire and taps send; a green bar crawls across the screen.

EVE (silky, close): His own voice. His own camera. First brick out of the wall.

△ Claire tips her head back against Eve's stomach and looks up at her.

CLAIRE (quiet, hungry): Three years I ate alone in this house. Now watch me eat it whole.

△ Eve turns the chair, catches Claire's jaw, and kisses her hard.

△ Claire fists Eve's collar and pulls her down over the armrest.

## EP39 - S#2  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — entry hall
Characters: Richard, Vanessa

△ Richard stands in the front hall with his phone to his ear, his knuckles white on a marble console.

RICHARD (rattled): Whitlock, wait — whatever you heard, it's out of context. Whitlock. Whitlock—

△ Richard lowers the dead phone and stares at it.

RICHARD (cold, to himself): He hung up on me. Nobody hangs up on me.

VANESSA (sweet, from the stairs): That's the second call today, isn't it. People are getting nervous.

△ Richard turns on Vanessa, his jaw tight.

RICHARD (sharp): Somebody in my house is leaking me. Find out who's talking.

△ Vanessa holds his eyes a beat, then smiles down at him.

VANESSA (sweet, cool): Of course, darling. I'll look into it.

## EP39 - S#3  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — Richard's study · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Eve sits Claire up in the chair and swipes to a long list of file names on the phone.

EVE (low, certain): Whitlock was one man. This is every partner Richard ever bragged to a camera about robbing.

CLAIRE (quiet, fierce): Then we don't stop at one brick. We pull the whole wall down on him.

△ Claire's phone buzzes; a name lights the screen — VANESSA.

△ Claire turns the screen so Eve can read it.

[END HOOK]
△ INSERT — the phone screen: VANESSA — "I know where he hid all of it. Meet me. Come alone."

Hard Cut.

## EP40 - S#1  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — east wing sitting room
Characters: Vanessa, Claire, Eve

△ Vanessa sits at a glass table, her phone face-up in front of her.

△ Claire steps in, Eve stopping in the doorway behind her.

VANESSA (sweet, tired): Relax. If I wanted you gone, I'd have let him finish it months ago.

△ Vanessa slides the phone across the glass to Claire.

VANESSA (cool, precise): He's wiring the last hidden account out right now — the one he thinks nobody knows. The island, the shell name, the password he was too lazy to change. Freeze it before morning.

CLAIRE (guarded): Why hand me the knife, Vanessa.

△ Vanessa looks up, her smile gone flat.

VANESSA (flat, bitter): Because I watched him plan to throw you out like spoiled fruit. And I finally understood I was next in the bin.

## EP40 - S#2  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — wine cellar
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Claire plugs the drive into a laptop balanced on a wine barrel, Eve leaning in beside her.

△ Columns of account numbers fill the screen.

EVE (low, stunned): He buried more than this house is worth. Twice over.

CLAIRE (quiet, fierce): And every cent of it is about to wear my name where his used to be.

△ Eve closes the laptop and takes Claire's face in both hands.

EVE (rough, thrown): When you have all of it — the money, the freedom — you won't need a girl he bought.

△ Claire grips Eve's wrists and holds them to her own cheeks.

CLAIRE (hungry, certain): He bought a trainer. I kept a wife. Those aren't the same woman.

## EP40 - S#3  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — wine cellar · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Claire opens the laptop again and moves the cursor over a red confirm button.

CLAIRE (steady): The second I move this money, he feels it. Every deal he's propping up comes down.

EVE (low): Once you press it, he's got no floor left to stand on.

△ Claire looks at Eve, then presses her thumb to the key.

[END HOOK]
△ INSERT — the laptop screen: TRANSFER AUTHORIZED — 14 ACCOUNTS REROUTED.

Hard Cut.

## EP41 - S#1  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — master bedroom
Characters: Eve, Claire

△ Eve twists the last hidden lens out of the vent grille and drops it into a glass of water on the nightstand.

EVE (low, satisfied): That's the last eye in this house. He can't watch you anymore.

△ Claire crosses to Eve and slides both hands up under Eve's open jacket.

CLAIRE (hungry, certain): Then stop standing across the room like it's still a job.

△ Eve backs Claire to the bedpost and pins her there by the hips.

EVE (rough, wanting): It stopped being a job the day you told me nobody had looked at you in three years.

△ Claire pulls Eve's mouth down to hers and drags her onto the bed.

## EP41 - S#2  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — master bedroom · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Claire lies back across the bed and pulls Eve down over her by the collar.

CLAIRE (breathless, claiming): Say the part you keep swallowing. The part that isn't the job.

△ Eve braces over her, close enough that their breath mixes.

EVE (rough, thrown): I was hired to ruin you. Instead I go stupid every time you look at me.

△ Claire arches up and locks both arms around Eve's neck.

CLAIRE (hungry): Good. Ruin him with me and stay ruined for me.

△ Eve drops her open mouth to the side of Claire's throat.

△ Claire's back curls up off the sheets.

## EP41 - S#3  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — master bedroom · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve, Richard (voice)

△ A fist slams against the bedroom door, hard enough to rattle the frame.

△ Claire and Eve go still, tangled together in the half-dark.

RICHARD (voice, ragged): Claire! Open this door! My accounts are frozen, the money's gone — what did you DO?

△ Claire sits up slowly, wiping her mouth, her face gone still and calm.

[END HOOK]
△ Claire looks at the shaking door, then at Eve, and does not move to answer it.

CLAIRE (quiet, cold): Let him knock.

Hard Cut.

## EP42 - S#1  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — Richard's study
Characters: Richard, Claire, Eve

△ Richard stabs at his ringing phone at the monitor wall while three screens flash DECLINED.

RICHARD (frantic): It's a mistake, it's a bank error — the deal's still good, I swear it's—

△ A speaker on the desk crackles and Richard's own recorded voice fills the room.

RICHARD (recording): I skim a little off every fund I touch. None of them ever check.

△ Richard freezes, then wheels around to find Claire in the doorway, Eve behind her.

RICHARD (hoarse): That's — where did you get that. That's my private—

CLAIRE (calm, cold): Your camera. Your voice. You wired every room to catch me — and only ever caught yourself.

## EP42 - S#2  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — Richard's study · Continuous
Characters: Richard, Claire

△ Richard lunges for the desk and sweeps a stack of contracts to the floor.

RICHARD (raging): Do you know what I built?! Twenty years! You were nothing — I made you a Mrs. Ashford!

CLAIRE (quiet, steady): You made me furniture. Furniture doesn't fight back. That was your one mistake.

△ Richard snatches up the phone the instant it lights again.

RICHARD (breaking): No — no, please, don't pull the—

△ Richard lowers the phone, and the screen goes dark in his hand.

## EP42 - S#3  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — Richard's study · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve, Richard

△ Claire turns her back on Richard and walks out, Eve falling in beside her.

EVE (low, to Claire): That was the last of his partners on the line. There's nobody holding his rope now.

CLAIRE (quiet): Then there's nothing under him anymore. Only the drop.

△ Behind them, Richard sinks into his chair, staring at a wall of dead screens.

[END HOOK]
△ Richard slumps toward the dead monitors, alone in the dark.

RICHARD (hollow): ...Somebody get me Vanessa.

Hard Cut.

## EP43 - S#1  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — entry hall
Characters: Claire, Eve, Richard

△ Two movers carry a wrapped painting past Richard, who stands in a hall stripped down to bare hooks.

RICHARD (small, dazed): They can't take the art. The art is mine. This house is mine.

△ Claire walks down the staircase with Eve, a folder in her hand.

CLAIRE (level): The house sits in the accounts you hid from me. Those accounts wear my name now. So does the house.

△ Richard stares up at her, his mouth open.

RICHARD (breaking): You planned this. My own wife. How long — how long have you been—

CLAIRE (cold, cutting him off): Longer than you deserve to know.

## EP43 - S#2  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — Richard's study
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Claire stops in the doorway of the emptied study, the monitor wall behind the desk gone black and dead.

CLAIRE (quiet): This is the room he watched me from. Every meal. Every night I ate alone.

△ Claire hooks two fingers in Eve's belt and pulls her into the empty room.

CLAIRE (low, sure): No screens left. So christen it with me. Right where he used to sit.

△ Eve lets herself be pulled, both hands sliding to Claire's waist.

EVE (rough, warm): Look at you. The wife who ate alone, taking the whole house in her teeth.

△ Claire walks Eve back into the dead monitor wall and kisses her, slow and certain.

## EP43 - S#3  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — Richard's study · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Eve picks Claire's buzzing phone off the bare desk and reads it.

EVE (low): He's got one lifeline left. He's begging Vanessa to move what's still hidden before you find it.

CLAIRE (quiet, cold): He's crawling to the one person who already sold him.

△ Claire sets the phone face-up on the bare desk between them.

[END HOOK]
△ INSERT — the phone screen: VANESSA — "He wants me to save him tonight. Watch what I do instead."

Hard Cut.

## EP44 - S#1  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — Richard's study
Characters: Richard, Vanessa

△ Richard grips Vanessa by both arms in the emptied study, a phone shaking in his fist.

RICHARD (desperate): There's one account left he can't reach — you know the name on it. Move it tonight. Save me and it's all yours, I swear.

△ Vanessa lifts the phone gently out of his hand and steps back out of his reach.

VANESSA (sweet, cold): Oh, darling. That account moved an hour ago. To her.

△ Richard's face drains white.

RICHARD (barely out): ...You. It was you. You've been feeding her.

VANESSA (bored, cruel): Since the night I heard you plan my exit too. You taught me how it's done — you just never thought a woman was paying attention.

## EP44 - S#2  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — Richard's study · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve, Richard, Vanessa

△ Claire steps into the doorway, Eve at her shoulder.

△ Richard sinks back against the bare desk.

CLAIRE (level, cold): That was the last one, Richard. Nothing hidden anymore. Nothing left to move.

RICHARD (wrecked, staring up): I had an empire. I had everything.

CLAIRE (quiet): You had a wife you called furniture. Turns out the furniture held the deed.

△ Eve sets a hand at the small of Claire's back.

EVE (low, to Richard): Every room you wired, every dollar you buried — it belongs to her now. That's final.

## EP44 - S#3  NIGHT / EXT / Ashford estate — Richard's study · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Claire and Eve step out onto the balcony off the study, the city lit up below.

EVE (low): No money, no partners. The house is the last thing he's got — and we take that in front of everyone.

CLAIRE (quiet, cold): Not done. He still gets to smile at cameras and call himself a wronged man.

△ Claire turns the black drive over once in her fingers and looks out at the lights.

[END HOOK]
△ Claire closes her hand around the drive, her jaw set.

CLAIRE (quiet, hard): Tomorrow the whole city hears his own voice. Let's introduce him to the room.

Hard Cut.
