# I Married the Woman Hired to Ruin Me — EP45-50

## EP45 - S#1  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — living room
Characters: Richard, Claire, Vanessa

△ Richard lifts a glass before a crowd of investors, a wall of dark monitors mounted behind him.

RICHARD (smug): You've all heard the rumors. Kill them. The Ashford name doesn't fall — not this week, not ever.

△ Claire stands at Richard's shoulder in a pale dress, her hands folded, her eyes down.

RICHARD (smooth): My wife will vouch for me. Steady through every storm. Won't you, darling.

CLAIRE (quiet): Steady. Through every single thing you've done.

△ Vanessa watches from the far wall with a thin, bored smile.

VANESSA (sweet, low): Look at her. Still playing the loyal little wife.

△ Claire raises her own glass toward the crowd and holds it high.

CLAIRE (clear): To my husband. Every word out of his mouth — you should all hear them.

## EP45 - S#2  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — living room · Continuous
Characters: Richard, Claire

△ The wall of monitors snaps to life behind Richard, every screen filling with his own face.

RICHARD (recorded, gleeful): I want my wife filmed with another woman. Then the prenup's toilet paper and she leaves with nothing.

△ Richard wheels toward the screens, his glass frozen halfway to his mouth.

RICHARD (choking): Shut it off. Somebody shut it OFF.

△ The recorded voice rolls on over the crowd as phones lift all across the room.

RICHARD (recorded): The money's already gone where no one can trace it. Every room in this house is wired.

△ Claire lowers her glass and looks straight at Richard.

CLAIRE (quiet): Your house. Your cameras. Your voice. Nobody made you say a word.

[END HOOK]
△ A gray-haired investor sets his glass down hard and walks out without a word.

△ Around the room, the rest of the guests turn their backs on Richard one by one.

Hard Cut.

## EP46 - S#1  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — living room · Continuous
Characters: Richard, Vanessa

△ Richard spins toward Vanessa and grabs her arm in front of the thinning crowd.

RICHARD (cornered): It was her. She moved every account. She built all of this — I barely signed my name.

△ Vanessa rips her arm free and backs away, the smile gone off her face.

VANESSA (sharp): Don't. You begged me to hide that money. I kept every message you ever sent.

△ Vanessa yanks out her phone and holds it up to the room.

VANESSA (flailing): You want proof? He put my name on the empty accounts and kept the full ones for himself!

△ Richard turns to the room with both hands raised.

RICHARD (fast): She's lying. She's been lying to all of you for years—

△ Richard lunges for the phone and comes up empty as Vanessa twists away.

RICHARD (snarling): Hand that to me. Now.

## EP46 - S#2  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — living room · Continuous
Characters: Vanessa, Claire, Richard

△ Vanessa turns to the room, but the guests are already filming Vanessa and Richard.

VANESSA (cracking): No — listen. I came forward. I'm the one who came forward, you understand me—

△ Vanessa stumbles back into the wall and the phone drops out of her hand.

△ Vanessa grabs after the phone, but a guest's foot has already slid it away across the floor.

VANESSA (scrambling): It's all him. Every bit of it is him—

△ Claire crosses the floor and stops in front of Vanessa, perfectly calm.

CLAIRE (quiet): You told me which of my rooms you'd take first. You wanted my closet.

VANESSA (breaking): That's not— you don't get to—

△ Vanessa slides down the wall with both hands over her face.

[END HOOK]
△ Richard looks from Claire to Vanessa on the floor, his mouth slowly falling open.

Hard Cut.

## EP47 - S#1  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — living room · Continuous
Characters: Richard, Claire, Eve

△ Richard staggers to Claire and catches the back of a chair to stay upright.

RICHARD (shaken): Claire. Whatever they showed you, we fix it together. You're still my wife.

△ Claire lifts her chin and looks straight at Richard, her jaw set.

CLAIRE (level): I stopped being your quiet little wife months ago. I just kept letting you film.

△ Eve steps out of the crowd in a sharp dark coat and stops at Claire's side.

RICHARD (thrown): The trainer. Why are you— what is she doing here.

△ Eve slides an arm around Claire's waist and pulls her in close.

EVE (cool): You paid me to ruin her. I did the exact opposite. Every corner of this house was ours.

△ Richard's mouth works but nothing comes out for a long moment.

RICHARD (breaking): You... you were both playing me. From the first day.

△ Claire settles back against Eve and lets herself smile.

CLAIRE (quiet): You said I'd never see a girl coming. I saw her on day one. I picked her.

## EP47 - S#2  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — living room · Continuous
Characters: Richard, Eve, Claire, Vanessa

△ Richard drops into the chair and stares up at the two of them.

RICHARD (hollow): I paid for this. I paid to put her on a camera.

△ Richard reaches out toward Claire from the chair.

RICHARD (grasping): Claire. Please. Don't leave me with nothing.

△ Eve keeps her eyes off Richard and turns Claire toward her by the hip.

EVE (low, to Claire): We're finished here. Come home.

CLAIRE (hungry): Take me home.

[END HOOK]
△ Eve walks Claire out past the wrecked crowd with one hand low on Claire's back.

△ Richard sits alone in the empty chair with Vanessa still crumpled on the floor beside him.

Hard Cut.

## EP48 - S#1  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — Richard's study
Characters: Claire, Richard, Eve

△ Claire walks into the study and sits down in Richard's chair beneath the monitor wall.

RICHARD (drained): The board is gone. The accounts are frozen. You've taken all of it. What's left.

△ Claire sets a slim folder on the desk and slides it across to Richard.

CLAIRE (quiet): The prenup you were going to shred. It stands. The house, my share, all of it comes back to me.

△ Richard stares down at the folder without lifting a hand.

RICHARD (bitter): And if I don't sign it.

△ Eve leans in the doorway and lifts her phone.

EVE (cool): Then the rest of your recordings find an audience. There's a lot more where the first one came from.

△ Richard picks up the pen and signs without reading a line.

CLAIRE (quiet): You bragged that a woman couldn't touch a cent of yours. Every cent is mine now.

## EP48 - S#2  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — master bedroom · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Claire reaches up to the vent grille in the bedroom and pulls the last hidden camera out by its wire.

CLAIRE (quiet): No more lenses. No more corners we have to disappear into.

△ Claire drops the little camera and grinds it flat under her heel.

△ Eve steps in and lifts Claire up onto the edge of the dresser by the backs of her thighs.

EVE (low, wanting): No more blind spots. The whole house is yours. So am I, in every room of it.

△ Claire hooks her legs around Eve and drags her in by the collar.

CLAIRE (breathless): Then quit watching the ceiling and look at me.

△ Eve presses her open mouth to the pulse under Claire's jaw.

[END HOOK]
△ Claire arches up off the dresser and pulls Eve down flush against her.

Hard Cut.

## EP49 - S#1  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — master bedroom
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Claire lies tangled with Eve in the wide bed, the walls bare where the monitors used to hang.

CLAIRE (warm): It's all signed. The house, the accounts, every last thing — in my name now.

EVE (low): Told you. We buried him with his own money.

△ Eve rolls onto her side and drags a slow hand up the length of Claire's bare back.

EVE (low, wanting): No more cameras, no more acting. I get to keep you out loud now.

CLAIRE (breathless): You've had me out loud for a while. I stopped pretending months ago.

△ Claire props up on one elbow and traces a slow line down Eve's collarbone.

CLAIRE (soft): There's still one thing I don't have.

EVE (guarded): Careful. I don't come cheap.

CLAIRE (quiet): Your name. The real one. Not the one they gave you for the job.

△ Eve goes still under Claire's hand.

## EP49 - S#2  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — master bedroom · Continuous
Characters: Eve, Claire

△ Eve lifts Claire's hand off her own chest and folds it between both of her palms.

EVE (thrown): Nobody in that world has this. Once I say it, it's yours to keep.

CLAIRE (breathless): I want it. All of you. The part he never got to pay for.

△ Eve brings Claire's palm up to her mouth and holds it there a beat.

EVE (quiet, real): Nora. My name is Nora.

△ Claire cups the side of Eve's face and holds her eyes.

CLAIRE (whispering): Nora. I've wanted to know that for months.

△ Claire leans in until her lips just brush Eve's.

CLAIRE (whispering): Nora. Mine now.

[END HOOK]
△ Eve rolls Claire under her and pulls the sheet up over them both.

Hard Cut.

## EP50 - S#1  DAY / EXT / Ashford estate — garden
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Claire stands with Eve under an arch of white flowers in the estate garden, both of them in pale suits.

CLAIRE (sure): He paid a stranger to ruin me. Instead he handed me the love of my life.

EVE (silky): Best contract I ever tore up.

△ Eve takes Claire's hands and holds them between the two of them.

EVE (low, real): I came into this house to take everything from you. I'm spending the rest of it giving it all back.

CLAIRE (soft): Keep the house. I only wanted the woman in it.

△ Claire slides a ring onto Eve's finger and holds her eyes.

CLAIRE (quiet): You were hired to ruin me. So finish the job. Ruin me for anyone else, forever.

△ Eve pulls Claire in by the waist and kisses her hard under the flowers.

## EP50 - S#2  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — dining room · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Claire sits at the head of the long dining table where she used to eat alone, two places set.

△ Eve pulls out the chair across from her — the seat that sat empty for three years — and settles into it.

CLAIRE (soft): I set that place every night for a year. Nobody ever once sat down in it.

EVE (low): I'm not going anywhere. Get used to the company.

△ Claire reaches across the table and folds her hand over Eve's.

CLAIRE (quiet): First person who looked at me in three years. Now you're the last thing I see every night.

△ Eve turns Claire's hand over and kisses the inside of her wrist.

CLAIRE (soft): I married the woman he hired to ruin me.

△ Claire lifts her glass toward the empty monitor brackets on the far wall and smiles.

CLAIRE (quiet): Best thing that ever happened to this house.

END.
