# I Married the Woman Hired to Ruin Me — EP17-20

## EP17 - S#1  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — living room
Characters: Claire, Richard, Vanessa

△ Claire sets a pen down on the coffee table in front of Richard and folds her hands in her lap.

CLAIRE (quiet, defeated): I won't fight the divorce. Just leave me enough to start over. That's all I'm asking.

△ Richard laughs and drops back into the armchair.

RICHARD (smug): Start over on what? There's nothing to split. I moved every dollar into Delphine's name months ago. You can't touch a cent.

△ Vanessa perches on the arm of Richard's chair and looks down at Claire.

VANESSA (sweet, cruel): Tell her the best part, Richard.

RICHARD (gleeful): The trainer was mine. I paid her to get you on camera. Every room here is wired — one clip and you'd walk out with nothing. You never saw a girl coming.

△ Claire keeps her eyes down and her hands still.

CLAIRE (quiet): ...You planned all of it.

RICHARD (bored): Every part. Not that it matters — what's furniture going to do with it. Sign the paper and stop setting my table.

## EP17 - S#2  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — pantry off the kitchen · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Eve turns her phone so Claire can see the playback — Richard's face frozen mid-sentence on the small screen.

EVE (low): Every room wired. Including that one. He just read us his whole plan on his own camera.

CLAIRE (quiet, fierce): Play it again. I want to hear him hand it to us.

△ Eve backs Claire against the counter and braces one hand on the shelf beside Claire's head.

EVE (low, claiming): He aimed every lens in this house at you. Tonight they caught him instead.

△ Claire fists a hand in Eve's collar and drags her in until their mouths nearly touch.

CLAIRE (breathless, owning it): Then let's make him watch himself lose everything.

△ Eve lifts Claire onto the counter and steps between her knees, her mouth dropping to Claire's throat.

## EP17 - S#3  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — Richard's study · Continuous
Characters: Claire

△ Claire stands alone in front of the wall of security monitors, the phone in her hand.

CLAIRE (quiet, cold): You wired every room to catch me, Richard.

△ Claire lifts the phone up beside the live feeds, his frozen face glowing on the little screen.

[END HOOK]
CLAIRE (cold): Nice angle.

Hard Cut.

## EP18 - S#1  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — Eve's quarters
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Claire steps into Eve's dark quarters and pushes the door shut behind her.

CLAIRE (quiet): He sent you into this house to take the last thing I had. I keep waiting to hate you for it.

EVE (low, guarded): You should. I let him point me at you.

△ Claire crosses the room and stops close, looking up at Eve.

CLAIRE (quiet, choosing): I don't. You're the one who turned the gun around. I trust that. I trust you.

△ Eve's careful face cracks and she cups the side of Claire's jaw.

EVE (rough, thrown): Say that when it's dangerous. One slip and it ends us both.

CLAIRE (breathless): Then let it be dangerous.

△ Claire fists both hands in Eve's shirt and pulls her down.

## EP18 - S#2  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — Eve's quarters · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Eve walks Claire backward until the back of Claire's knees hit the bed.

△ Claire pulls Eve down over her and locks her legs around Eve's hips.

EVE (low, claiming): No cameras here. No job. Just you choosing me.

CLAIRE (breathless, owning): I've been choosing you for weeks. Stop making me say it and do something about it.

△ Eve drags her open mouth down the side of Claire's throat.

△ Claire arches up off the bed and fists a hand in Eve's hair.

EVE (rough, thrown): I wasn't supposed to fall for the mark. You wrecked that.

## EP18 - S#3  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — hallway · Continuous
Characters: Vanessa, Claire

△ Vanessa stops Claire in the corridor and presses a folded slip into Claire's palm.

VANESSA (low, quick): Delphine flies in Thursday to re-sign the accounts. After that he moves everything one more time, and even the tape won't reach it.

CLAIRE (guarded): Why hand me this.

VANESSA (bored, cold): Because I heard the same tape you did. He'd put my name on the fall the second it suited him.

[END HOOK]
△ Vanessa walks off down the corridor, leaving Claire holding the slip.

CLAIRE (low): Then we do it before Thursday.

Hard Cut.

## EP19 - S#1  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — stairwell landing
Characters: Eve, Claire

△ On the stairwell landing, Eve catches Claire's wrist and pulls her into the shadow under the rail.

EVE (low): Last chance to walk it back. Once we play him that tape, there's no undo.

CLAIRE (quiet, fierce): I've set his table three years. I'm done pretending I'll starve quietly.

△ Eve presses Claire back against the wall and rests her forehead to Claire's.

EVE (rough, claiming): Then we bury him tonight. Together.

△ Claire grips the back of Eve's neck and kisses her hard, once.

CLAIRE (breathless): Together. Now stay out of frame. This part's mine.

## EP19 - S#2  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — Richard's study · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Richard

△ Claire walks in and sets her phone on the desk in front of Richard, the clip already playing.

RICHARD (recorded, from the phone): I moved every dollar into Delphine's name. One clip and my wife walks out with nothing.

△ Richard shoots forward in his chair, staring at the phone.

RICHARD (rattled): That's my house. That's my feed. How do you have that.

CLAIRE (level, cold): You wired every room to catch me. You caught yourself. Every word, on your own camera.

△ Richard lunges for the phone and Claire slides it out of his reach.

CLAIRE (cold): File the divorce. The day those papers land, this tape lands on every partner you have. You don't move a dollar. You cancel Thursday.

RICHARD (cornered): You wouldn't dare put that out.

CLAIRE (quiet): You paid a stranger to film me. Try me.

## EP19 - S#3  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — Richard's study · Continuous
Characters: Richard, Claire

△ Richard's hand shakes over his own phone as he stares at the dark wall of monitors.

RICHARD (low, unraveling): My own cameras. In my own house.

△ Claire picks up her phone and slides it into her pocket, unhurried.

CLAIRE (cold): Your one clean shot at me is dead. Now you just sit here knowing I own your voice.

[END HOOK]
△ Richard turns slowly to the monitor wall and scans the dark screens.

RICHARD (barely out): ...Somebody's in my house.

Hard Cut.

## EP20 - S#1  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — entry hall
Characters: Delphine, Claire, Richard (recorded voice)

△ Delphine stands in the entry hall in a travel coat, a folder held against her chest.

NORA (guarded): Richard said re-sign and fly straight back. Whatever this is, leave me out of it.

△ Claire holds up the phone and plays the clip for Delphine.

RICHARD (recorded, from the phone): It's Delphine's name on all of it. If it ever blows up, she takes the fall, not me.

△ Delphine goes still and her grip tightens on the folder.

NORA (low): ...He said that. About me.

CLAIRE (quiet): He put the fall on your name and the money in his pocket. I'm the only one here not lying to you.

△ Delphine crosses the hall and sets the folder in Claire's hands.

NORA (flat, done): Then it's yours. Every account, every signature. And I'll say what he did to anyone who asks.

## EP20 - S#2  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — Richard's study · Continuous
Characters: Eve, Claire

△ Eve pulls the cables from the server and the wall of monitors goes black.

EVE (low): His eyes are off. Every room in this house just went blind.

△ Claire pushes Eve down into Richard's chair at the dead monitor wall.

△ Claire settles onto Eve's lap, a knee to either side, and takes Eve's face in both hands.

CLAIRE (quiet, owning): He watched me from this chair for three years. Now he's got nothing to watch.

EVE (rough, claiming): You picked his throne to do this on. God, I love how you think.

△ Claire kisses Eve slow and drags Eve's hands down to her hips.

## EP20 - S#3  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — hallway · Continuous
Characters: Richard

△ Down the hall, Richard stands alone with his phone, staring at a still frame of the gym feed.

RICHARD (low, cold): Two months of gaps in the trainer's sessions. Every camera in this house wired — except wherever that girl stood.

△ Richard zooms in on Eve's face frozen on the screen.

[END HOOK]
RICHARD (quiet, dawning): The trainer knew where my lenses couldn't reach. Somebody told her.

Hard Cut.
