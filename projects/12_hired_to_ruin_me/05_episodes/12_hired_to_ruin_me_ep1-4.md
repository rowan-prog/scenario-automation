# I Married the Woman Hired to Ruin Me

## EP1 - S#1  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — Richard's study
Characters: Richard, Vanessa, Eve

△ Richard leans back before a wall of security monitors, swirling a drink.

RICHARD (smug): I want my wife filmed with another woman's mouth on her. Every room in this house is wired. Bring me that clip and the prenup's toilet paper — she walks out with nothing.

△ On one screen, Claire sits alone at a long dining table, her food untouched.

VANESSA (sweet, cruel): Poor Claire. Three years in this house, still setting a place for a man who won't sit down.

EVE (cool): You're sure she wants women.

RICHARD (bored): She's starving. And a woman can't touch a cent of mine — that's the whole point. She'll never see a girl coming.

EVE (flat): It's just a job. You'll get your tape.

## EP1 - S#2  NIGHT / INT / Ashford estate — living room · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Richard, Vanessa

△ Claire sits stiff on a white sofa as Richard walks in with Vanessa on his arm.

RICHARD (bored): Vanessa's taking the east wing. Don't make it weird.

VANESSA (sweet): Gorgeous house. Wasted on someone who never fills it.

△ Richard scrolls his phone without looking up.

RICHARD (cold): She's no trouble. Furniture never is.

CLAIRE (quiet): I've set your place at that table every night for a year. You've never once sat down.

## EP1 - S#3  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — home gym
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Claire stands stiff on a mat in workout clothes as Eve circles her, slow, head to toe.

EVE (silky): So you're the wife. Relax — I don't bite unless you book it.

△ Eve steps in and flattens one palm to Claire's bare stomach, the other spread low on her back.

EVE (low, commanding): Breathe into my hand. Your body knows how, even if you won't admit it.

△ Claire's breath stutters and her stomach tenses under Eve's palm.

CLAIRE (guarded): This is a warm-up. Nothing else.

[END HOOK]
△ Eve slides behind Claire and locks both arms across her waist, chest against Claire's back, mouth at her ear.

△ Claire's eyes fall shut and she melts back into Eve instead of pulling free.

△ Cut to Richard at his monitor wall, grinning at the screen.

RICHARD (gleeful): There she goes. She's going to hand me everything.

Hard Cut.

## EP2 - S#1  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — home gym · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Eve kneels and closes her hand over Claire's fist on a heavy dumbbell.

EVE (silky): Grip here. Let me do the work — you just feel it.

△ Eve rises behind Claire and drags both palms slow up her ribs to just under her chest.

CLAIRE (breathless): That's — that's not where the muscle is.

EVE (low, teasing): I know exactly where everything is. Now widen this knee for me.

△ Eve runs a hand down the back of Claire's thigh and pushes her stance open.

△ Claire's head tips back onto Eve's shoulder, her hips rolling into the touch.

EVE (low, in her ear): Keep telling yourself that. Your body already gave you up.

CLAIRE (breathless, denying): It's just training. That's all this is.

△ Eve turns Claire's chin with two fingers until their eyes meet.

EVE (silky): Then hold my eyes and say it again.

△ Claire opens her mouth and nothing comes out.

## EP2 - S#2  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — dressing room · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Claire stands at a full-length mirror in a half-zipped dress as Eve steps in close behind her and takes the zipper.

EVE (silky): Hold still. Let me.

△ Eve draws the zipper slow up Claire's spine, her knuckles dragging the bare skin the whole way.

△ Claire's back arches a fraction into Eve's hand before she catches herself.

CLAIRE (breathless, denying): It's just a zipper.

EVE (low, in her ear): Then why are you shaking.

△ Eve settles both hands on Claire's bare shoulders and holds her eyes in the mirror.

CLAIRE (quiet, wounded): You're the first person who's looked at me in three years.

△ Eve's hands go still at the nape of Claire's neck.

EVE (low, thrown): ...Don't say that to me.

[END HOOK]
△ Eve turns Claire by the shoulder and leans in, her mouth a breath from Claire's.

△ Claire and Eve both freeze, neither one closing the gap.

Hard Cut.

## EP3 - S#1  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — home gym · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Claire is already on the mat, watching the door, when Eve walks in.

CLAIRE (guarded): You're late. I cleared my whole afternoon — for the session.

EVE (silky): Look at you. Already warm for me.

△ Claire takes Eve's hand and presses it flat to her own waist.

CLAIRE (breathless): Show me the breathing one. For my form.

△ Eve steps in and slides the hand up under Claire's ribs, her thumb brushing higher.

EVE (low, teasing): You dragged my hand here yourself. That's not form.

△ Claire's breath breaks and she leans back into Eve's chest before she catches herself.

CLAIRE (guarded, denying): This is nothing. I'd do this with any trainer.

EVE (low, in her ear): Then look at any trainer the way you're looking at me.

△ Claire's eyes drop to Eve's mouth and stay there a beat too long.

△ Eve's easy smile slips for half a second before she pulls it back on.

## EP3 - S#2  DAY / INT / Escort agency — Eve's room
Characters: Eve, Jade

△ Eve sits on the edge of a bed, the delete screen open on a video file.

JADE (blunt): You've stared at that clip for an hour. Upload it, get paid, walk.

EVE (flat): It's just a job.

JADE (dry): Then why's your hand shaking? You're going soft over a married client.

EVE (low): She looks at me like I'm the first real thing in that house.

JADE (dry): That's the job working. Don't let it work on you. Cash out.

△ Eve takes the phone back and holds it to her chest instead of sending.

## EP3 - S#3  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — Richard's study · Continuous
Characters: Richard, Vanessa

△ Richard spreads divorce papers across the desk as Vanessa reads over his shoulder.

RICHARD (smug): One clean clip and I file. She walks out with nothing.

VANESSA (sweet, cruel): She'll be too ashamed to even fight it.

RICHARD (cold): I'm telling the trainer to finish it tonight.

[END HOOK]
△ Alone at the agency, Eve pulls up the clip of Claire melting back into her arms and hovers her thumb over the delete.

△ The file vanishes from the upload folder.

Hard Cut.

## EP4 - S#1  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — home gym · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Eve glances up at the camera tucked in the ceiling corner, then closes her hand around Claire's wrist.

EVE (silky, for the room): Let's stretch that shoulder out. Over by the wall.

△ Eve walks Claire backward into the far corner and pins her wrist to the wall by her head.

EVE (low, rough): No lens reaches back here. Just you and me now.

CLAIRE (breathless, resisting): We can't. I'm his wife. This is just—

△ Eve presses her whole body in and drops her forehead against Claire's.

EVE (rough, wanting): Your mouth says no. Your hands are already pulling me in.

△ Claire fists both hands in Eve's shirt and drags her closer, arching off the wall.

CLAIRE (breathless, denying): This doesn't mean anything.

△ Eve catches Claire's jaw in one hand, her mouth a breath away.

EVE (thrown): I wasn't supposed to want this. Look at me. Only me.

△ Eve kisses her hard, one hand fisted in Claire's hair.

△ Claire arches into Eve and grips her hip, holding her there.

## EP4 - S#2  DAY / INT / Ashford estate — home gym · Continuous
Characters: Claire, Eve

△ Back under the ceiling camera, Eve holds Claire at arm's length and fixes her posture like a coach.

EVE (silky, performing): Good session, Mrs. Ashford. Same time tomorrow.

△ Claire's chest is still heaving as she smooths her hair and lifts her chin.

CLAIRE (quiet, composed): That's all this was. A session.

△ In the doorway shadow, out of the lens, Eve hooks one finger through Claire's for a second, then lets go.

VANESSA (sweet, cold): Right into the back corner. Convenient.

[END HOOK]
