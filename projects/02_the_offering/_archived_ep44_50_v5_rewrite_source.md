# THE OFFERING — EP44

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Private Chamber. One Month After Birth. Late Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Their chamber. Fire low. The child in the cradle by the bed, asleep. Isolde in his black tunic on the bed. Vael beside her, not in tunic — bare shoulders, scales soft to the elbows.

A quiet night. The first quiet night in eight weeks.

She has been waiting for it.

[KEY CAMERA]
Low warm of the chamber. Slow push behind Vael leaning over her. Held close shot of her face.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: I want to say something.
VAEL: Say it.
ISOLDE: I love you.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He does not move for two seconds. He has known. He has known for a year. He has waited for her to say it. She has waited until the child was out of her body and breathing on her chest and a month gone past.

He puts his forehead against hers.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Say it again.
ISOLDE: I love you.
VAEL: One more.
ISOLDE: I love you.
VAEL: I have loved you since the day you walked through my gate.
ISOLDE: I know.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He kisses her. Slow, low, no pause. His hand goes flat against the small of her back through the tunic. He doesn't stop.

In the cradle, the child stays asleep.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
Outside the chamber window, on the eastern ridge, every dragon lifts its head once at the moment she says it the third time. Audience-only. Thirty pairs of eyes against the moon, then closed.

Hard Cut.

# THE OFFERING — EP45

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Outer Yard. Two Months. Day.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The yard at noon. Five dragon-house heads in human form — Wave-house head, Maren, Tovan, river-grey head, coastal cream head. They have come to see the child.

Isolde at the keep steps with the child wrapped in pale silk. Vael behind her. Her mother's brother and the other south coast elder at the side, watching.

The five house heads walk forward one by one. Each kneels. Each lifts his head only to look at the child — not at Isolde, not at Vael — and then walks back.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the yard. Slow tracking each house head kneeling. Cut close to the child in Isolde's arms — eyes open today, grey-green, looking at the sky.

[DIALOGUE]
WAVE-HOUSE HEAD: My queen. My princess.
ISOLDE: Heard.
MAREN: Heard, my queen.
TOVAN: Heard.
RIVER-GREY HEAD: My princess.
COASTAL CREAM HEAD: My princess.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
After the five have knelt, Vael lifts his hand once. The yard empties. The two south coast elders stay.

Isolde's uncle walks up to the steps. He has not spoken in two months again — broke his silence once at EP41 and gone quiet since.

He kneels at the steps. He puts a small carved wooden bird at Isolde's foot. He has carved it during the two months. He stands and walks back.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: He'll speak again at her wedding.
VAEL: That long.
ISOLDE: That long.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
The wooden bird stays on the step. The child closes her eyes against Isolde's neck — right next to the fifth crescent. Vael's hand at the small of Isolde's back tightens once and settles.

Hard Cut.

# THE OFFERING — EP46

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Private Chamber. Three Months. Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Their chamber. The cradle empty tonight — the child is in the bed between them. Three months old, sleeping on her side, hand against Isolde's collarbone.

Vael on his side facing them. He has been awake watching for an hour.

[KEY CAMERA]
Low warm. Held close shot of the three of them on the bed. Cut to Vael's eyes, open.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (without opening her eyes): You're watching.
VAEL: I'm watching.
ISOLDE: Sleep.
VAEL: I will.
ISOLDE: When.
VAEL: When I stop being afraid.
ISOLDE: When does that happen.
VAEL: I'll tell you.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He puts his palm flat on the child's back. The scales on his hand stay soft tonight. The child does not wake.

He puts his other hand on Isolde's belly through the tunic. Empty now. He keeps it there.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Another.
ISOLDE: I said a year.
VAEL: It's been four months.
ISOLDE: A year.
VAEL: Eight months left.
ISOLDE: Eight months.
VAEL: I'll count them.
ISOLDE: I know you will.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
He puts his forehead against the top of the child's head. He closes his eyes. He sleeps. Forty minutes later, so does Isolde. The three of them stay still until dawn.

Hard Cut.

# THE OFFERING — EP47

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Throne Hall. Four Months. Day.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The throne hall. Two thrones on the dais. The cradle between them — empty today, because the child is in Isolde's lap.

Below the dais: a single VEINE COURT MESSENGER. Plain coat. Not noble. Sent for one purpose.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the hall. Cut to the messenger walking up. Cut to Isolde with the child in her lap.

[DIALOGUE]
MESSENGER: My queen. King Aldric has died.
ISOLDE: When.
MESSENGER: Eleven days ago. Fever. He kept your letter — the one you didn't write but he asked for — under his hand the whole week.
ISOLDE: He died alone.
MESSENGER: He died alone. The court asks who sits Veine.
ISOLDE: Aleran. My mother's brother.
MESSENGER: He is here, my queen.
ISOLDE: Then he rides home tomorrow. Regent for my daughter. Until she chooses to sit it. Or to not sit it.
MESSENGER: My queen.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The messenger goes to one knee. He walks out.

Vael at her right. He has not spoken yet.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: He died alone because you let him.
ISOLDE: I let him.
VAEL: Are you sorry.
ISOLDE: No.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The child closes her fist on Isolde's silver thread on her bodice. She doesn't cry. Isolde looks down at her for ten seconds.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: She has both lines.
VAEL: She has both.
ISOLDE: She doesn't sit either throne until she chooses.
VAEL: Until she chooses.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
SUPER as the messenger leaves: VEINE — REGENCY, ALERAN.

[END HOOK]
Aldric's name is not spoken again in the keep.

Hard Cut.

# THE OFFERING — EP48

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. East Balcony. Five Months. Morning.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The east balcony in spring light. Isolde at the rail, the child on her hip — five months, head up now, looking. Vael beside her.

A letter on the small table beside Isolde. Red wax. Sera's seal.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the balcony. Cut to the letter on the table. Cut to the child watching the ridge.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: She writes again.
ISOLDE: She writes again.
VAEL: What does she want.
ISOLDE: To send her daughter to ours for one summer when they are six.
VAEL: One summer.
ISOLDE: One.
VAEL: Tell her yes.
ISOLDE: I told her yes last month.
VAEL: Tell her yes again.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde turns her head to look at Vael. She is amused, fully, for the first time on screen.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: You like her now.
VAEL: I don't like her.
ISOLDE: You like that she yielded.
VAEL: I like that she yielded.
ISOLDE: That's not the same thing.
VAEL: It's the same thing for me.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The child reaches for the silver thread on Vael's collar. He lets her grip it. She pulls. He doesn't move.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: She's strong.
ISOLDE: She's strong.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
On the eastern ridge, one dragon lifts its head and stays lifted. Watching the balcony.

Hard Cut.

# THE OFFERING — EP49

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Private Chamber. Eight Months After Birth. Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Their chamber. The child in the cradle, eight months old, sleeping. Isolde in maiden silk on the bed. Vael beside her, scales soft.

She has just told him.

[KEY CAMERA]
Low warm. Slow push behind Vael leaning over her. Held close shot of his hand finding her belly through the silk — empty still but his hand goes there anyway.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Already.
ISOLDE: Six weeks.
VAEL: That's not eight months from now.
ISOLDE: I miscounted.
VAEL (slow smile): You did not miscount.
ISOLDE: I miscounted on purpose.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He puts his palm flat on her belly through the silk. He keeps it there.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: How many.
ISOLDE: How many what.
VAEL: How many will you give me.
ISOLDE: How many do you want.
VAEL: All of them.
ISOLDE: All of them.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He kisses her. He doesn't stop. He puts his other hand at the back of her neck under her hair.

The silk strap snaps under his hand without him looking at it. Fourth time. He doesn't count anymore.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
Outside the chamber window, on the eastern ridge, the dragon that has been watching for three months lifts off the rock and circles once — slow, low — and lands again. A greeting. Audience-only.

Hard Cut.

# THE OFFERING — EP50

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. East Balcony. Three Months Later. Morning.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A wide balcony on the east face of the keep. The same direction as the original wasteland and the eastern ridge. The Black Gates of the keep stand open below, the way they opened by themselves in EP01.

Wide, slow morning. Pale sun. No frost.

ISOLDE stands at the balcony rail. Black silk gown — full length, gold-thread embroidery running the length of the bodice and skirt in a dragon-scale pattern, deep-crimson flame embroidery tracing the seams. A gold mantle, ankle-length, off her shoulders. Hair down. Joined pendant outside the collar. On her head, the gold diadem with the two-horn dragon motif worked into it. Belly visible now under the gown — three months along again.

In her arms — wrapped in a length of pale silk — the small dark-haired child. Eleven months. Awake. Watching the sky.

VAEL stands beside Isolde. Black coat over black ceremonial tunic, gold embroidery at the cuffs matching her hem. Sleeves rolled to the elbow. Scales soft and almost invisible. He has one hand at the small of her back. The other at the bottom of her belly, low.

Beyond the balcony — the eastern wasteland. The eastern ridge. The thirty Drakonis-pack dragons on the ridge.

A single wing-shape lifts off the ridge — not in alarm. In greeting. The first dragon rises.

Then a second. Then a third. Then ten. Then twenty. Then all thirty.

They do not roar. They lift in formation. They cross the wasteland from east to west, slow, low enough that their wing-tips throw a moving shadow across the basalt. The shadow runs across the open Black Gates, and across the keep yard, and up the east face of the keep, and across the balcony where Isolde and Vael stand.

Wide. Slow. The same shape as the shadow that crossed the wagons in EP01.

The horses in the keep yard, far below, do not scream this time. They lift their heads.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the balcony with the open Black Gates below. Slow tilt up to the ridge — thirty dragons rising. Wide aerial of the formation flying east to west, low. Slow tracking of the shadow crossing the wasteland, the gates, the yard, the keep face. Cut close to the shadow passing over Isolde's face. Then over Vael's. Then over the small child's. Then over Isolde's belly, low.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: My king.
VAEL: My bride. My mate. The mother of my heir.
ISOLDE: Yours.
VAEL: All four of you now.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The child stirs in Isolde's arms. Opens her eyes for half a second. Closes them again. The shadow has passed.

Vael's hand at the bottom of her belly tightens once and then settles. Her hand on his at the rail goes still over his scaled knuckles.

The thirty dragons reach the western horizon. They bank south, slow. The horizon gets smaller. The morning gets brighter.

[KEY CAMERA]
Pull back to wide of the keep. Tilt up to the lightening sky. Wide held final image — four figures on the balcony (one in her arms, one in her belly), two crowns on the table beside her, the wasteland and the dragons and the open Black Gates below.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

Fade Out.
