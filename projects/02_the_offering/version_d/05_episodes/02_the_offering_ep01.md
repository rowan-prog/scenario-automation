# THE OFFERING — EP01: The One Who Walked In

S#1 — IRON GATE / KEEP FORECOURT / DUSK

[Visual]
A black escort wagon halts at the foot of a basalt gate. Twelve human-kingdom guards in grey livery dismount around it, faces hard.
A clerk in a grey wool coat unrolls a length of parchment.
A guard reaches inside the wagon for ISOLDE's wrist. **ISOLDE — Royal Arrival look: ivory silk gown, pearl trim at the throat, silver-thread sash, white fur-lined cloak, glossy dark-brown hair loose past the shoulder, porcelain skin, grey-green eyes** — twists her wrist out of his grip without looking at him.
She steps down on her own. The hem of the ivory gown drags across iron dust.
Past the gate, the keep's inner courtyard is a black mouth. Cold air spills out of it.
The clerk lifts the parchment.

[DIALOGUE]
CLERK: By treaty. The offering of the human crown.
GUARD: Lady. Your hand —
ISOLDE: I'll walk.
CLERK: They are watching.
ISOLDE: Then let them.

S#2 — KEEP COURTYARD / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
ISOLDE crosses the threshold alone. The twelve guards stay outside the gate. The clerk does not follow.
Inside, the courtyard is a deep stone well — no torches, no welcome line.
Her cloak shifts. A silver chain slips loose from the inner lining of the collar — **her mother's old signet pendant**, a faded sigil engraved on its face. It catches the last gate-light for one breath.
On the keep wall above her, an old rune set into the lintel flickers — **the same beat, the same sigil shape**. Audience-only.
ISOLDE has not looked up. She is looking forward, into the dark.
At the far end of the courtyard, in the shadow under the inner arch, a **gloved black-scaled hand** rests at its owner's side. The fingers do not move.

S#3 — KEEP COURTYARD / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
ISOLDE stops a foot short of the shadow. She does not step back.
The scaled hand rises from the dark. It does not grab — it stops **a palm's width beside her shoulder**, holding the air.
ISOLDE turns her face up to it. She does not look afraid. She looks **at it**.
The hand turns and finds her **inner wrist**. Fingers close around it — gently, but the grip does not let go.
He lifts her wrist between them. The pulse-point in her wrist faces up to him.
A scaled fingertip rests on the pulse.
On her **inner wrist**, a **faint scaled glow** prints once — a brief crescent of light under the skin. It flickers in the same beat as the rune on the wall above.
ISOLDE does not pull her hand back.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (O.S., low): Walked in on your own.
ISOLDE: Yes.
VAEL (O.S.): Good.

[FX]

S#4 — KEEP INTERIOR / GREAT HALL ENTRY / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
A heavy iron door opens inward. Warm low torch-light spills out, but his face is still kept in shadow on the far side of the doorway.
He releases her wrist. He does not step aside — he turns half a step and **walks in beside her**, matching her pace.
The black-scaled hand stays in view, near her hip, not touching.
The Great Hall opens past the entry: long basalt floor, a single iron throne at the far end, two columns of silent retainers in dark leather.
ELARA — **golden-haired bride candidate, ivory and gold silk, sharp jaw** — stands beside the throne. Her smile flattens when ISOLDE crosses the threshold.
KIRAN — **black-haired, hard build, drake-house captain's silver cuirass** — flicks his gaze from ISOLDE to the scaled hand beside her, and back.

[Camera]

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: You belong here.
ISOLDE: I came to find out why.
VAEL: Stay.
ELARA: (to KIRAN, low) That is the offering?
KIRAN: That is what walked in.

S#5 — KEEP INTERIOR / GREAT HALL / NIGHT

[Visual]
ISOLDE stands at the foot of the iron throne dais. She is alone in the open floor.
VAEL stops one step ahead of her. He is **still in profile** — backlit by the throne's fire-pit, only his shape visible: tall, black leather, long black cloak, dark hair tied back. His face is not yet given to the room.
He turns toward her.
A torch in the iron sconce **bursts wider**. Light begins to fall across the line of his jaw —
— and stops there. The frame holds **just before** his face is fully shown.
On her inner wrist, the **faint crescent glow** prints once more. Same beat.
On the back of his scaled hand at his side, **a second faint glow** prints in the same beat — answering hers.

End Image: VAEL turned toward ISOLDE, jaw catching fire-light, face still in shadow. Her inner wrist glows once. His scaled hand glows once. Same beat.
