# THE OFFERING — EP05: Where Your Pulse Is

S#1 — KEEP / NORTH TERRACE / DUSK

[Visual]
The keep's north terrace — a long basalt balcony cantilevered over the ash plain. The sky is wine-red turning iron. Far down the plain, the **second human-kingdom envoy column** is a row of torch-points moving toward the keep.
ISOLDE stands at the terrace's iron rail in Vael's Choice silk. The **crescent bite at her neck has darkened to deeper red** but not faded. The **inner-wrist glow line** stretches a finger's length now, palm-up where she rests her forearm on the rail.
Behind her, on the basalt floor, **VAEL's black boot-step** lands once. He has come up from the inner stair without announcing himself.
He stops three paces behind her.
ISOLDE does not turn.

S#2 — KEEP / NORTH TERRACE / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
VAEL closes the three paces. He stops at her shoulder — not pinning her — and **sets his black-scaled hand on the iron rail beside hers**. His scaled fingers are an inch from her bare forearm.
She still does not turn her head, but she turns her wrist palm-down on the rail — covering her glow.
VAEL's hand does not move.
He turns to look at her profile. His grey eyes have gone dark again, the way they went in the chamber after the bite — restraint, not anger.
**The crescent bite at the side of her neck is half a hand from his mouth.**
He does not bend yet.
She turns her head — slow — until her face is one breath from his.

S#3 — KEEP / NORTH TERRACE / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
VAEL's scaled hand leaves the rail.
He **catches her wrist** — the one she had turned palm-down — and lifts it, **flipping it palm-up between them**. He pulls her wrist against his chest, **the inner-wrist glow pressed flat against the black leather over his sternum**.
The glow **ignites** under the leather — light blooming through the seams of his coat from the inside.
His **other hand** finds her jaw and tilts her face up. His thumb rests on the **point of her chin**, fingers spread along the line of her jaw.
He bends. His mouth comes down — **but stops a half-breath from her lips**.
He moves instead to her **earlobe**. His mouth closes once on the lobe — a small, careful pull — and releases.
Then **the curve of her ear** — a single open-mouthed press.
Then the **side of her neck, just below the first crescent bite from the chamber**. He bites again — **a second crescent, smaller, deliberately placed just under the first, the two crescents stacking like a cuff of red marks** on the same side.
Then **the throat pulse-point** — open mouth, longer hold. His tongue presses once against the pulse and lifts.
Her wrist-glow, still pressed flat against his sternum, **pulses in the same rhythm** as the pulse under his mouth. The two beats lock.
ISOLDE's free hand has gone to **the back of his neck**, fingers in the hair at his nape — holding him there.

[DIALOGUE]

ISOLDE: Then where.
VAEL: Where your pulse is.
ISOLDE: It's there too.

S#4 — KEEP / NORTH TERRACE / NIGHT

[Visual]
VAEL lifts off her throat. His grey eyes have gone very dark again.
He **releases her wrist**. Her wrist falls between them — the glow is now bright enough to throw light up onto the underside of her jaw.
He **steps backward** into the doorway shadow at the terrace's inner stair — one pace, two, three. The dark takes his face.
ISOLDE stays at the rail. She does not call him back. She lifts her own wrist, palm-up, and looks at the glow.
On the rail's iron, where the glow pressed against it earlier, **a faint print of light** has stayed in the metal. It does not fade.

End Image: ISOLDE alone at the iron rail, two stacked red crescents at the side of her neck, wrist-glow lit bright on her palm. VAEL gone into the inner stair shadow. The iron rail keeps a print of light where her wrist rested.
