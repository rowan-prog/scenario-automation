# THE OFFERING — EP03: Mark on the Pulse

S#1 — KEEP / EAST CORRIDOR / NIGHT

[Visual]
A narrow basalt corridor outside ISOLDE's chamber. Two low torches in iron sconces, long shadows. ISOLDE walks the corridor in Vael's Choice gown, alone, her **inner wrist faintly glowing**.
**Two assassins — black hoods, faces wrapped in grey cloth, narrow killing blades drawn** — step out of a side alcove behind her.
ISOLDE turns. She does not scream. She steps backward against the corridor wall.
The first assassin moves in — blade up — for her **throat**.
A black-scaled hand catches the assassin's wrist mid-arc.
**VAEL** rises out of the corridor's far shadow, his other palm opening — and **a low, dark-red flame** licks across his palm, smoke curling up between his fingers.
He closes his scaled fist on the assassin's blade. The steel **bends, then cracks**, then snaps in two.
The second assassin freezes one step from ISOLDE.

S#2 — KEEP / EAST CORRIDOR / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
The first assassin drops at VAEL's feet, wrist crushed. The second assassin lunges past VAEL — for ISOLDE.
VAEL pivots, takes ISOLDE by **a fistful of her hair at the nape** and pulls her **off the wall and against his chest** — her face against the black leather over his sternum, the back of her head cupped in his scaled hand. His shoulder absorbs the second assassin's blade.
The second blade snaps against the black leather. Smoke from the dark-red flame seeps through the leather where the steel struck.
KIRAN sprints in from the corridor's far end. He stops a step short. His eyes register the position of VAEL's hand in ISOLDE's hair and look elsewhere.
VAEL's scaled palm slides off her head and **rests, briefly, at the back of her neck** — his thumb against her nape.
He does not let go.

S#3 — KEEP / ISOLDE'S CHAMBER / NIGHT

[Visual]
The door slams shut behind them.
VAEL turns her — one motion — and puts her **back against the chamber wall**. His scaled forearm braces over her head. His free hand opens flat on the wall beside her hip.
He stays close — chest a hand's width from hers, breath at her temple.
He does not kiss her yet.
His **scaled fingers find her jaw**, lift it. He looks at her throat where the assassin's blade would have gone. There is no cut, but a thin red line has bloomed under the skin in the fear.
His **free hand slides under the silver chain at her collarbones** — and **slips one of her dress shoulder-straps down off her shoulder**. The black silk falls an inch. Her bare shoulder catches lamp-light.
His scaled palm settles **flat on her collarbone, pulse-point of the throat under his thumb**.
He bends and kisses her — **mouth open, hard, no warning**. Her head goes back against the wall, throat exposed.
He breaks off the mouth and **bites once at the side of her neck — a small, deliberate, crescent mark** beside the pulse-point. Red, not deep.
His scaled hand turns her wrist palm-up between them. The **inner-wrist glow is now bright, the size of a thumbprint, deep red-gold**.
He kisses **the wrist glow** — once, slow, his mouth covering the print.
ISOLDE has not pushed him off. Her free hand has settled on the back of his scaled hand and **stayed there**.

S#4 — KEEP / ISOLDE'S CHAMBER / NIGHT

[Visual]
VAEL stops.
He stays at her mouth a half-breath longer — and then **steps one full pace back**. His scaled hand leaves her collarbone. The strap is still off her shoulder. The crescent bite on her neck is still red.
His grey eyes have **gone very dark** — not anger. Restraint.
He does not put the strap back. He does not pull her chain.
He turns half away. His scaled hand closes into a fist at his side. The dark-red flame flickers once across his knuckles and dies.
ISOLDE stays against the wall. She does not adjust the strap either. Her wrist glow has not faded — it is steady on her thigh, where she has rested her hand.
VAEL walks to the chamber door without looking at her.
At the door he stops, hand on the iron.
