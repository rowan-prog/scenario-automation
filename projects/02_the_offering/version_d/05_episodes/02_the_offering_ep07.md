# THE OFFERING — EP07: On My Knee

S#1 — KEEP / WAR COUNCIL ROOM / DAY

[Visual]
The war council room. A great **iron hourglass** stands at the room's far end — black sand falling in a slow stream. The sand pile in the bottom bulb is two-thirds full.
The basalt table. **The new envoy — middle-aged, severe, the human-kingdom diplomat's heavy black cloak with double-silver-crown sigil at the breast, an iron staff in his right hand** — stands at the table's far end. Behind him, **four armored escorts in grey livery, sigil shields raised**.
HALDREN sits to one side of the table — envoy chain still hanging open — silent.
VAEL sits at the head. ISOLDE stands one pace behind his right shoulder, in Vael's Choice silk. Her **wrist glow is bright** at her side, the dark-red trace running along it.
The new envoy strikes the iron staff once on the basalt.

S#2 — KEEP / WAR COUNCIL ROOM / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
VAEL does not stand. He turns his head — slow — and looks up at ISOLDE behind his shoulder.
He raises his **scaled hand** and offers it palm-up — not a take, an offer.
ISOLDE puts her own hand into his.
He **pulls her around the chair-back and down**, settling her **across his thigh** — her hip on his knee, her back against his chest, her face turned three-quarters toward the new envoy. The basalt-edge of the long table is in front of them.
His **left scaled palm settles flat at her waist**. His **right hand gathers a thick fist of her hair at the nape**, lifts it once, lets it fall down her back — and then **keeps the hand in her hair**, knuckles against her scalp.
He turns her face toward the new envoy by **a small pressure of the hand in her hair**.
He brings his **left scaled hand up to the side of her neck** — and **rests his thumb directly over the two stacked crescent bites** above the silver chain.
The new envoy's eyes go to the thumb. To the hair-fist. To the wrist-glow.
The four escorts shift their shields half an inch and stop.
HALDREN looks at the table.
**The dark-red flame** runs once across the back of VAEL's left scaled hand at her neck — and **ISOLDE's wrist-glow flickers in the exact same rhythm** where her hand rests on his thigh.

VAEL: She is on my knee.
ISOLDE: (steady) I came up on my own.

S#3 — KEEP / WAR COUNCIL ROOM / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
The new envoy has stopped speaking. He **shifts his iron staff to his left hand** — a small concession.
HALDREN lifts his head once, looks at the iron hourglass — the sand pile in the bottom bulb is **a finger's width higher**. Time has moved. He looks back at the new envoy.
VAEL's thumb is still on the crescent bites. The hair-fist is still in ISOLDE's hair. ISOLDE has not moved.
She turns her face — by her own — back toward the new envoy. Slow.
She speaks while seated across VAEL's thigh.

ISOLDE: It is now.
VAEL: She speaks for the keep.
NEW ENVOY: My lord — by dusk we will return with —
VAEL: Return with what you like.

S#4 — KEEP / NORTH TERRACE / NIGHT

[Visual]
The terrace from EP05. The same iron rail. Night sky, stars sharp.
ISOLDE stands at the rail again — Vael's Choice silk, **two stacked crescent bites at her neck darker than ever, hair displaced from the hair-fist (not fixed), wrist-glow bright on the rail, the dark-red trace at the wrist now a finger-length-and-a-half**.
VAEL stands behind her at her shoulder. His scaled hand rests on the iron rail beside hers — touching, this time, knuckles to forearm.
Above the keep, **far up in the black sky**, something moves.
A **vast wing-shadow** crosses the moon — once. The moon-light cuts and returns.
Then a **second wing-shadow** — bigger — and a **third**, further out along the ridge.
The basalt under their feet **vibrates** once.
Below in the yard, the four envoy escorts look up. The new envoy looks up.
The basalt **vibrates again** — longer this time. Not a roar. A presence settling.
The wing-shadows hold over the keep.

End Image: ISOLDE and VAEL at the iron rail, his scaled knuckles against her forearm. Above them, three vast wing-shadows over the moon. The basalt vibrating under their feet. ISOLDE's wrist-glow steady on the iron.
