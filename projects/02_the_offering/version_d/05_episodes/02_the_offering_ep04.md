# THE OFFERING — EP04: Not That Word

S#1 — KEEP / WAR COUNCIL ROOM / DAY

[Visual]
A long basalt war table, iron candle-stands burning low at midday. Black ash-light through narrow windows.
**HALDREN — grey-haired, hard cheekbones, the human-kingdom envoy coat in slate wool with the silver crown sigil at the breast, the envoy's chain at the collar** — stands across the table from VAEL. Two human-kingdom escorts in grey livery flank him.
A **rolled human-kingdom standard** — white linen, the silver-crown sigil at its center — lies on the basalt between them.
VAEL sits at the head of the table. ISOLDE stands one pace behind his right shoulder, in Vael's Choice silk. The **crescent bite at her neck is still visible** above the silver chain.
HALDREN's eyes flick to the bite. His mouth tightens. He looks at the wall instead.

[DIALOGUE]
HALDREN: My lord. The crown sends terms.
VAEL: I see them on the floor.
HALDREN: The offering is — held in custody. The crown will pay a — second seal.
VAEL: There is no second seal.
HALDREN: My lord, by the treaty —
VAEL: The treaty ended when she walked in on her own.

S#2 — KEEP / WAR COUNCIL ROOM / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
VAEL stands. He does not raise his voice.
He walks around the basalt table to the rolled human-kingdom standard. He sets the heel of his black boot **on the silver-crown sigil**.
He opens his **left palm flat over the standard** — and the **dark-red flame** blooms again, dropping in a slow curtain from his palm to the linen.
The standard catches in a single low whoosh. The silver-crown sigil chars first — the linen around it goes black, edges curling.
HALDREN's hand goes to the envoy chain at his collar.
The escorts step back one pace.
ISOLDE has not moved. She is watching the silver-crown sigil go black. Her **inner wrist glow** has lifted slightly — a steady pulse, awake.
VAEL closes his palm. The flame retracts into his scaled fingers. The standard keeps burning under his boot.

[DIALOGUE]
HALDREN: My lord — that is the crown's seal.
VAEL: It was.
ISOLDE: (quiet, to HALDREN) Tell my father I watched.
HALDREN: Lady — what should I tell him you are.
VAEL: Not that word.

S#3 — KEEP / EAST COLONNADE / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
The war council has emptied. A long basalt colonnade outside the room — half-shadow, half-noon light striped between columns.
VAEL stops her in the shadow between two columns. He does not pin her this time. He stands a half-step closer than yesterday.
He **lifts her left forearm** between them, palm up. The **inner-wrist glow** is bright and steady — deep red-gold, the size of his thumbprint and slowly extending toward the crease of her wrist.
He opens his **scaled palm just above** her wrist — and the **dark-red flame** drops down onto her glow in a slow, low curtain. It does not burn her. It sinks into the glow.
She breathes in once at the heat.
He traces **the curve of the wrist glow** with one scaled fingertip — slow, all the way to where it dies at the crease. A **second, finer line** of the same dark-red **prints under her skin** along that trace and stays.
The line does not fade.
ISOLDE looks at the new mark. She does not pull her wrist back.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: This is what you are.
ISOLDE: Then say it.
VAEL: Not yet.
ISOLDE: When.
VAEL: Where they can all see it.

S#4 — KEEP / EAST COLONNADE / NIGHT

[Visual]
Same colonnade, hours later. Torch-iron in the sconces now. ISOLDE is still there, leaning her shoulder against a column, her left wrist resting palm-up against the column stone — the dark-red glow line and the wrist crescent both visible.
VAEL stands one column away, his back to her. He is looking out at the basalt yard where the **smoke from the burned standard** is still rising in a thin black line.
He does not turn.
HALDREN crosses the yard below, the envoy chain still at his collar, escorts re-mounted. He looks up at ISOLDE in the colonnade. His mouth opens — and closes.
VAEL speaks without turning.

End Image: VAEL one column away, back turned, looking down at the rising smoke. ISOLDE's wrist palm-up against the column, the new dark-red glow line bright. HALDREN below, looking up, mouth closed.
