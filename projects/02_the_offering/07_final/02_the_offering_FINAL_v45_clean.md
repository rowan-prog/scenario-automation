[DIALOGUE]

[VISUAL/ACTION]

[VISUAL/ACTION]

[VISUAL/ACTION]

## S#3 — Same Room. Before Dawn.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
After. Sheets twisted. Candle low.

[DIALOGUE]

[DIALOGUE]

## S#5 — Veine Palace. Bridal Mirror Hall. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The bridal mirror is black under Isolde's bare palm.

The hall is frozen for one full second.

Then the sound rises — gasps, whispers, chairs shifting, silk moving, a silver cup dropping somewhere off-screen.

No clear words yet. Just the court breaking apart around her.

ISOLDE stands at the altar in reused white silk. Her hand is still on the black glass. Her bare wrist visible. Pearls still pinned in her hair.

KING ALDRIC at the head of the hall — half rising from his throne. STEPMOTHER beside him — a small smile beginning at the corner of her mouth. ADELINE near the bride's side — laughing soundlessly into her gloved hand. LORD CORVIN across the altar — late fifties, heavy rings, soft hands, wet mouth — stepping back one full pace.

They are all reacting.

Isolde is not.

[KEY CAMERA]
One unbroken camera move. No cut.

Start tight on the black mirror under Isolde's palm — the glass still pulsing faintly. Slow rise up her bare wrist — past the white silk sleeve — to her still shoulder — to her jaw — to her face.

Behind her, the court blurs into movement and whispers — out of focus, but loud.

She does not look down.

Her eyes shine. She does not let the tear fall.

She does not look confused.

She breathes once through her nose. Slow.

Her face is calm. Humiliated, but not broken. Resolved.

[END HOOK]
The camera finishes on Isolde's bust shot, slow push-in.

The black mirror still pulsing behind her shoulder.

The court noise rising around her — still no clear word.

Her eyes stay forward.

The frame holds on her face for two beats longer than the audience expects.

Hard Cut.
