# THE OFFERING — EP01

## S#1 — Dust Road. Royal Wagons. Day.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A line of black wagons rolls across grey wasteland. The Veine kingdom's gold-lion banner drags on a long pole. Behind the banner, a smaller iron cart. Bars on every side. A girl inside.

ISOLDE kneels in white fur, ivory silk underneath, hair loose down her back. Wrists chained to the cart floor. Cheek grazed. One pearl in her ear bent. Ankles bare on splintered wood.

HARRIN rides up beside the cart. Thirty. Gold hair pulled back. Court coat. He looks down at her.

HALDREN, a grey-bearded Veine envoy in formal grey, rides behind Harrin. He watches the chain on her wrists and says nothing.

A CROWD on the road. Farmers. Soldiers. A boy with a clay cup. They throw bread crusts. One spits. Another laughs.

ISOLDE pulls her wrist against the chain. The iron bites her skin. She doesn't make a sound. She lowers her head until her hair covers her face.

[KEY CAMERA]
Low wide tracking the cart. Cut to medium of Isolde — chained hands, head dropping. Cut to high angle: Harrin on horseback, sun behind. Cut close: a glob of spit landing on her shoulder. Cut to her chain pulled taut.

[DIALOGUE]
HARRIN: Don't cry. Save your throat for him.
A SOLDIER (laughs).
HARRIN: You ate at our table twelve years. Pay it back tonight.
ISOLDE (low, head down): Shut up, Harrin.
HARRIN: Save it. They don't pay for talking.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
SUPER lower third: THE OFFERING — DRAGON KEEP, EAST OF THE BLACK GATES.
Caption beside Harrin: HARRIN — HEIR OF VEINE.
Caption beside Isolde's wrists: ISOLDE — 23 — TO BE DELIVERED.

[END HOOK]
A shadow runs across the wagons. Wide. Slow. The horses scream.

HARRIN: Now.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

## S#3 — Drakonis Keep. Outer Yard. Dusk.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The yard is bigger than Veine's whole inner court. Black basalt under their boots. The keep rises above them.

The wagons stop. Harrin's men climb down. None of them speak.

Isolde is dragged out by her chain. She lands hard on her knees on stone. The white cloak drags through black dust.

Harrin steps forward, hand on sword hilt.

HARRIN: The bride. As agreed.

Silence.

Then — on the parapet — every torch lights at once. Black flame. No sound.

A figure walks to the parapet rail.

VAEL DRAKONIS. Black coat. Dark hair tied back. Hands on the stone.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide low push toward the gate. Cut to Vael on the parapet — extreme high. Cut down to Isolde on her knees in white. Tilt up the black wall behind him.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Up.
ISOLDE (lifts her chin, voice rough): I'm not yours yet.
VAEL: You're inside the gate.
HARRIN (too loud): My lord, the contract —
VAEL (not looking at him): I read it.
HARRIN: Then accept —
VAEL: I accept her. Not you. Get her up. By the hand. Not the chain.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A guard freezes. Then takes her by the hand. Carefully.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
SUPER on Vael: VAEL DRAKONIS — LORD OF DRAKONIS KEEP.

[END HOOK]
Vael turns from the rail. The parapet torches go out one by one as he walks inside.

# THE OFFERING — EP02

Two MAIDS behind her, hands folded.

HARRIN stops three steps from the bottom.

HARRIN: This isn't the contract.

## S#2 — Drakonis Keep. Isolde's Chamber. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael's palm goes flat on stone above her shoulder. Black scales an inch from her ear.

His other hand finds the silk at her shoulder. He pulls the robe off her shoulder — cut wide for one beat — back close: the robe down to her elbow, shoulder bare, chemise neckline already loose at the collarbone.

## S#3 — Drakonis Keep. Isolde's Chamber. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael's mouth lifts off her throat. He doesn't step back. He listens.

A SHADOW at the window slit. A gloved hand. A dagger.

The ASSASSIN swings inside — black cloth face to wrist, curved knife in reverse grip. He moves for Isolde first, not Vael.

Vael catches the assassin's wrist mid-thrust and twists. The bone cracks. The dagger drops.

Vael keeps the wrist and walks the man backward two steps to the far wall and puts him through it. Black stone breaks loose.

He picks up the dagger. Black fire runs down his arm to his palm. The dagger blade glows red, then white, then sags in his hand — half-melted, useless. He drops the slag on the stone. Black scales run up to his elbow. His eyes — dark red, edge of the iris.

Vael lifts the assassin by the throat.

[KEY CAMERA]
Reverse grip dagger snapping in. Wrist crack. Wall impact, debris. Cut close to Vael's eye — red ring. Wide of the assassin held against the wall.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Who paid.
ASSASSIN (choking): Veine. The brother. Harrin.
VAEL: Again.
ASSASSIN: Harrin of Veine.
VAEL: Louder. So she hears.
ASSASSIN: HARRIN OF VEINE.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde against the wall. Two crescent marks on her throat. Mouth bruised. She watches.

Vael doesn't kill the assassin in front of her. He throws him at KIRAN, who has appeared in the doorway.

[DIALOGUE]
KIRAN: Yard?
VAEL: Yard. Alive.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Kiran drags the assassin out by the broken wrist. Door closes.

Vael turns back to her. Black scales still on his arms. Red still in his eyes. He puts his hand back on her throat — over the marks — and bites a third time, lower. Below the collarbone this time.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
She makes a sound. He doesn't stop. He lifts her off the wall — one arm under her knees, the other under her shoulder blades — and carries her three steps to the wide bed. He sets her down on her back. He drops his own black shirt on the floor — bare chest, scales up to the collarbone, the ring inside his eye full red now.

He bends over her on the bed. One scaled hand at the back of her thigh, lifting it slow against his hip. The other at the side of her ribs, sliding the chemise the rest of the way up. Bare skin against bare scale at her thigh — the scales are hotter than human skin. She gasps. Her fingers close in the bedsheet beside her.

He puts his mouth on hers. He does not stop kissing her as he settles his body between her thighs. Her breath against his mouth catches three times, fast — sharp inhale, then she lets him in, then her hands come up off the sheet and lock in his hair at the back of his head, pulling him closer to her mouth.

When he moves, he moves slow — first. Then not slow. Her back arches off the bedsheet. Her flushed throat goes redder. Her hair sticks to her temple. The three crescent marks on her throat shift as her chin tips back. He keeps one scaled hand at her hip and the other at her wrist above her head — palm flat over her pulse, holding her wrist to the pillow.

She makes the sound again. Twice. The second one is his name without the rest of the word. He puts his face against her hair at her temple and breathes her in, harder, his breath against her ear hot enough that the loose strand of hair against her cheek moves with his breath. The scales at his throat pulse with his pulse.

He does not stop until she does.

# THE OFFERING — EP04

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Isolde's Chamber. Morning After.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The room is half-lit. East slit window, grey dawn just beginning. The bed is wrecked — black sheet pulled off one corner, ivory pillows on the floor. The broken silk strap from last night is on the chair back.

ISOLDE is on the bed edge in a thin white robe pulled hastily over a maiden chemise. Her hair is loose, the half-pin from last night still tangled at her crown. Three crescent marks dark on her throat — two under the jaw, one below the collarbone. Both wrists yellow with bruise.

VAEL is in the room. Black trousers, no shirt yet, sleeves of last night's black shirt thrown across the chair. The black scales run up to his elbows in the morning light, fainter, but visible. He is standing at her window slit, looking east.

He turns from the window before she lifts her head. He looks at her — at her marks first, then at her wrists, then at her mouth.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide reveal of the wrecked bed. Cut close to her wrists — yellow bruise. Cut to her three crescents. Cut to him at the window — black scales on his back muscle. Cut to him crossing to her.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: You stayed.
VAEL: I didn't leave.
ISOLDE: That's not the same.
VAEL: It is.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He stops in front of her. He goes down on one knee — not for ceremony, for her eye level. He puts his thumb under her chin and tilts her face up toward him. He looks at the lowest mark, the one below the collarbone, the deepest.

His mouth tightens, a fraction.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: I bit too low.
ISOLDE: You bit where you meant to.
VAEL: I meant to.

None.

[END HOOK]
He pulls back. He looks at her again.

VAEL: Are you afraid of me.
ISOLDE (after a beat): I should be.
VAEL: That's not what I asked.

## S#2 — Drakonis Keep. Isolde's Chamber. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She doesn't answer the question. He doesn't ask again. He stands.

He picks up his black shirt from the chair. He doesn't put it on. He folds it once and lays it on the foot of the bed — for later, when she wants him covered.

He goes to her wash basin. Black stone, water already poured by some servant before dawn. He takes the linen cloth folded beside it. He wets it. He wrings it. He brings it to her.

He sits beside her on the bed edge. He takes her left wrist — the one with the worse yellow bruise — and presses the cool cloth over it. He doesn't speak.

She watches his hand.

[KEY CAMERA]
Close on his hand wetting the cloth — black scales fading into pale knuckles. Cut to her watching. Cut to the cloth on her wrist. Cut to her face — she has stopped breathing.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (quiet): You don't do this.
VAEL: I do now.
ISOLDE: For who.
VAEL: For you. Don't ask twice.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He moves the cloth from her left wrist to her right. He presses. He does not look up. The cloth darkens on the bruise.

She lifts her free hand. She puts it — not on his face, not on his shoulder — on his forearm. On the black scales. She doesn't recoil.

He stops moving the cloth.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Don't touch the scales if you're afraid.
ISOLDE: I'm not.
VAEL: Then keep your hand there.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She keeps her hand there. The scales under her palm are warmer than human skin. Not hot. Alive.

Under her palm, the scales settle. They were lifted to the knuckle a moment ago — at the door knock outside the chamber that audience does not yet hear. They slide back flat now. Just her hand has done that.

A KEEP STEWARD outside the chamber door, two strides away, lifts her hand to knock for the second time. The steward never finishes the knock. From inside the chamber, the door — heavy oak, iron-banded — shudders once on its hinges. The steward steps back. Sets down the linen she was bringing. Walks away.

Inside the chamber Vael has not turned his head toward the door. His scales are still flat under Isolde's palm. He does not know yet that he made the door move. Isolde sees the iron band on the door shift back into place. She does not say anything about it. She just keeps her hand on his forearm.

[KEY CAMERA]
Close on her palm on his scaled forearm — scales smoothing under her touch. Cut to the chamber door from outside — the iron band shuddering once. Cut to the steward stepping back. Cut to Isolde's eyes finding the door, then finding her own hand on his arm.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
A knock on the chamber door. Kiran's voice — low, not urgent, but not ignorable. Kiran does knock. The door does not shudder for him.

KIRAN: My lord. Veine envoy at the south road. Old man. Not the brother.

Vael does not lift his hand from her wrist.

VAEL (to the door): I'll come down. He waits.

The cloth stays on her wrist.

A smaller hall than the council chamber. Black stone, one long table, no banners on the wall. Two of the keep's black-coat guards at the doors. Kiran beside the table.

VAEL enters from the inner stair. He has put on the black shirt. Sleeves still rolled. Coat over the chair, not on him. He sits at the table head.

ISOLDE walks in behind him. She is dressed for the day now — fitted black silk, ivory pearls at the collarbone, hair pinned. The three crescent marks visible. She does not stand at the window today. She stands at his right shoulder, behind his chair.

The VEINE ENVOY enters from the outer door. Grey beard, the king's close hand. He bows shallow. He looks at Isolde once. He looks longer at the crescent marks on her throat. He looks away.

[KEY CAMERA]

[DIALOGUE]

VAEL: Greetings are for guests.

VAEL: A servant.
ENVOY: I am sorry, my lord.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael turns his head — not toward the envoy. Toward Isolde behind his shoulder. He looks at her. He waits.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (to the envoy, not loud): Look at me.

VAEL: You heard her. You repeat it. Word for word.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael takes her hand on the table. He turns it over. He puts his thumb on the inside of her wrist — over the yellow bruise. He doesn't lift his eyes from her face.

He puts her hand back down.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide on the table. Cut to her hand under his on the table. Cut close to his mouth on her wrist. Cut to the envoy — his beard is wet at the lip. Cut to her — she has not blinked.

[DIALOGUE]

ENVOY: My lord, the king will hear —
VAEL: I know he'll hear. That's the point.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]

# THE OFFERING — EP06

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Great Hall. Sundown.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The Great Hall at evening. Long table, twelve places set. Vael at the head. Kiran at his right. The three old dragon-blood lords on one side. Two empty chairs on the other side: one beside Vael, one near the foot.
