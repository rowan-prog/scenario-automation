# THE OFFERING — EP01: THE CHAINED OFFERING

## S#1 — Dust Road. Royal Wagons. Day.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A line of black wagons crawls across a grey wasteland. The kingdom's colors hang heavy on a long banner — gold lion on cracked white. Behind it, a smaller cart. Iron bars. A girl inside.

ISOLDE kneels in a white fur cloak, ivory silk underneath, hair loose down her back. Wrists chained to the cart floor. Her cheek is grazed. The pearl in her ear is bent. Her ankles are bare against splintered wood.

HARRIN rides up beside the cart. Thirty. Gold hair pulled back. A face that has never lost. He looks down at her as if at livestock.

HALDREN, a grey-bearded Veine envoy in formal grey, rides behind Harrin. He watches Isolde's chains and says nothing.

CROWD on the road. Farmers. Soldiers. A boy with a clay cup. They throw black bread crusts. They spit. One man laughs.

ISOLDE jerks her wrists against the chain. The iron bites her skin. She does not flinch. She lifts her face.

[KEY CAMERA]
Low wide tracking the cart. Cut to medium of Isolde — chained hands, lifted chin. Cut to high angle: Harrin on horseback above her, sun behind him like he owns it. Cut to crowd close: a glob of spit hitting her shoulder. Cut to her chain, taut.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Unchain my wrists, Harrin.
HARRIN: You're not a guest. You're the gift.
ISOLDE: Then have the courage to carry me yourself.
HARRIN: Don't cry. If the dragon burns your voice along with the rest of you, our house might finally save face.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A laugh from a soldier.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: You fed me after my mother died. That doesn't buy my blood.
HARRIN: That's the only thing of yours that pays anything. Save the speech. They like silent offerings.
ISOLDE: Shut up, Harrin.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
SUPER on lower third: THE OFFERING — DRAGON KEEP, EAST OF THE BLACK GATES.
Small caption beside Harrin's face on his close-up: HARRIN — HEIR OF VEINE.
Tiny script beside Isolde's wrists on her close-up: ISOLDE — TO BE DELIVERED.

[END HOOK]
A shadow runs over the wagons. Wide. Cold. The horses scream.

## S#2 — Dust Road. Wing-Shadow. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The shadow is enormous. It crosses the road in one slow sweep — wider than the wagon line, longer than the banner. The sun blinks out for three seconds and comes back colder.

The lead escort horse rears. The rider falls. His helmet rolls into the ditch. The horse keeps running, riderless, back the way they came.

Above the ridge, far off — a black silhouette banks once and is gone. Just the angle of a wing. Just enough to know it was real.

The CROWD that was spitting goes silent. A woman crosses herself. The boy with the clay cup drops it.

HARRIN's smile loses one degree. He doesn't speak.

ISOLDE looks up. Her hand inside the chain stops shaking.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide aerial of the shadow swallowing the road. Tilt up to the ridge silhouette. Cut to Harrin's mouth tightening. Cut close to Isolde's face — for the first time, somebody else is afraid.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (low, to herself): So he's real.
HARRIN: Move the line. Move it.
A SOLDIER: My lord, the lead horse —
HARRIN: Now.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
The Black Gates of the Drakonis Keep open by themselves. Nobody touches them.

## S#3 — Drakonis Keep. Outer Yard. Dusk.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The yard is bigger than Veine's whole inner court. Black basalt under their boots. Torches on iron stakes — unlit. Above them, the keep rises like a wall of teeth. Far up on the parapet, a row of figures watches without moving.

The wagons stop. Harrin's men climb down stiffly. None of them speak.

ISOLDE is dragged out by her chain. She lands hard on her knees on the stone. The white cloak drags through black dust.

HARRIN steps forward, hand on his sword hilt. He is the loudest thing in the yard, and it makes him look smaller.

HARRIN: The bride. As agreed.

Silence.

Then — on the parapet — every torch lights at once. Black flame. No sound.

The figures part. One steps to the rail.

VAEL DRAKONIS. Black coat. Dark hair tied back. Hands resting on stone. From this distance he is a column of shadow. Only the eyes catch the torchlight — grey, slow, finding her.

He doesn't shout. He doesn't need to. The yard hears him.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide low push toward the keep gate. Cut to Vael on the parapet — extreme high. Cut down to Isolde on her knees in white. The contrast: bright, kneeling, small / dark, standing, immense. Slow tilt up the black wall of the keep behind him.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Little offering.
ISOLDE (lifts her chin): I'm not yours yet.
VAEL: You walked through my gate. You are already half of mine.
HARRIN (too loud): My lord, the contract —
VAEL (not looking at him): I read it.
HARRIN: Then accept — 
VAEL: I'll accept her. Not you. Get her up.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A guard moves to lift Isolde. Vael's voice cuts the yard like wire.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Not by the chain. By the hand.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The guard freezes. Looks up. Then, very carefully, takes her by the hand.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
SUPER on Vael's first close-up: VAEL DRAKONIS — LORD OF DRAKONIS KEEP.

[END HOOK]
Vael turns from the rail and walks inside. The parapet torches go out behind him, one by one, like he is taking the light with him.

Hard Cut.

# THE OFFERING — EP02: THE UNDERGROUND ALTAR

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Upper Bridal Hall. Morning.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A hall set for a wedding that will not happen. White silk on long tables. A high seat empty. Six VEINE COURTIERS — three women in pale gowns, three men in the gold-and-white of Veine — stand against the wall. Harrin's MOTHER, the QUEEN, is among them, hand resting on a silver cup. HALDREN stands two steps behind the queen, hands clasped, eyes down.

Two of the keep's BLACK-COAT GUARDS stand at the far doors. They do not move.

HARRIN paces. He laughs once at nothing.

HARRIN (to the queen): He'll bring her down dressed. He'll sit at the head. We'll sign. We leave by sundown.

QUEEN (sipping): If he doesn't burn the chair under you first.

Harrin laughs again. Smaller.

A PRIEST enters — old, thin, carrying a scroll. He bows once to Harrin and once to the queen. The scroll is already half-prepared. ISOLDE OF VEINE — DEPARTED.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the empty hall. Track in on the death-notice scroll on the priest's tray. Hold.

[DIALOGUE]
HARRIN: Have it dated for tonight.
PRIEST: My lord. The seal —
HARRIN: I am the seal. Date it tonight.
QUEEN: Don't cry at her funeral. The court is watching.
HARRIN: I won't. Drakonis will burn what's left of her. Nothing to bury.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
SUPER on the scroll: NOTICE OF DEATH — ISOLDE OF VEINE — TONIGHT'S DATE.

[END HOOK]
The doors at the far end of the hall open. Not Vael. KIRAN — Vael's second. Dry mouth. Bored eyes. Two more guards behind him.

KIRAN: The hall is for guests. Follow me. The offering is being received downstairs.

## S#2 — Drakonis Keep. Underground Stair. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The stair is wide, black stone, lit by black flame torches. It goes down further than a tower should be tall. Harrin's boots echo. The queen's perfume can't reach down here.

At the bottom: an OLD ALTAR carved into the foundation rock. Older than the keep above it. Sigils on it that Harrin doesn't recognize. ISOLDE stands beside it. She is no longer in white fur. She is in a black silk gown — fitted, ivory pearls down the collarbone, silver threading at the shoulder seams. Her hair is pinned high. Her wrist chain is gone.

Two MAIDS stand behind her with their hands folded.

VAEL stands across the altar from her. Black coat. Hands behind his back. He hasn't touched her. He doesn't need to. The light from the torches falls on her like she's already his.

HARRIN stops three steps from the bottom. He sees the altar. He sees her dressed. He sees Vael standing there like the ceremony already happened.

HARRIN: This isn't the contract.
VAEL: It is now.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide reveal of the underground altar. Cut to Isolde — new dress, calm face, fingers gripping the altar edge so hard the knuckles are white. Cut to Harrin freezing on the bottom step. Cut to Vael, calm.

[DIALOGUE]
HARRIN: We have a hall ready upstairs. We have witnesses.
VAEL: You have witnesses for a burial. I read your priest's scroll.
HARRIN (too fast): That's standard. Royal precaution. If anything happened —
VAEL: Nothing will happen. She doesn't die today.
QUEEN (descending the stair): My lord Drakonis, we delivered her in good faith —
VAEL: You delivered her in chains. You can leave in them or without them. Pick.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The Queen's mouth opens. Closes.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (without raising his voice): Priest. Read the line that says she is mine. Read only that line.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The priest's hands shake. He reads.

[DIALOGUE]
PRIEST: ... and shall be received into the keeping of the Lord of Drakonis, as wife or sacrifice, by his choice.
VAEL: Wife.
HARRIN: That's — that's not what we —
VAEL: I'm not choosing. I'm taking.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
SUPER on the priest's scroll close-up: BY HIS CHOICE — WIFE OR SACRIFICE.

[END HOOK]
Vael lifts a hand toward Isolde — palm up, waiting. Doesn't move toward her. Makes her cross. ISOLDE looks at the altar. At her stepmother. At Harrin. Then she walks the three steps and puts her hand in his.

Vael closes his fingers around her wrist. Black scales on the back of his hand flare once. The torch flames bend toward them.

VAEL (to Isolde only): Little offering. You will not be one.

## S#3 — Drakonis Keep. Upper Corridor. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael is walking her out. His hand on her wrist — not pulling, not gentle. A hold. Behind them, Harrin's voice still rising and Kiran's voice cutting it off.

KIRAN (off): The wagons leave at dawn. Or now. Your choice.

ISOLDE keeps pace with Vael without looking at his face. She watches her own feet on black stone. Her free hand is shaking. She makes a fist to stop it. She walks.

Two KEEP GUARDS at the corridor's end bow as they pass. Not to her. To her, by passing through him.

[KEY CAMERA]
Tracking two-shot down the long corridor. Cut to the back of Vael's hand around her wrist — black scales settling. Cut to her trembling free hand making a fist.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Where are you taking me.
VAEL: To a room you can lock from the inside.
ISOLDE: You signed for a wife.
VAEL: I signed for a wife. I will not collect tonight.
ISOLDE: You're not a kind man.
VAEL: No. I'm a patient one. There's a difference.
ISOLDE (V.O., quiet, audience only): He could have left me in the wagon. He didn't. I shouldn't notice that.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
A WINDOW above them — narrow slit. Outside, against the dusk sky, a black silhouette banks once between the towers. Wing-tip. Then gone.

In the courtyard far below — audience only, neither character sees it — a GREY-CLOAKED RIDER hands a folded message to one of Harrin's men, then mounts and leaves the keep by a side gate. The signet ring on his hand is pressed in black wax.

# THE OFFERING — EP03: BEDROOM WALL. FIRST BLOOD.

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Isolde's Chamber. Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A round tower room. Black stone, high ceiling. A wide bed with black sheets, ivory pillows. A window slit looks east, toward the mountains.

ISOLDE is in maiden nightwear — sheer white silk, pearl trim, a thin ivory lace robe over her shoulders. Hair down her back. Pendant — silver chain, tree-and-flame sigil — out of her bodice now, resting between her collarbones. She is alone. She is counting.

She is counting the men who passed under her tower window in the last hour. She has used a piece of charcoal from the hearth to mark them on the inside of her wrist. Seven marks. She rubs them off with her thumb.

A FOOTSTEP outside. Two. She turns toward the door.

KIRAN'S VOICE (through the door, dry): Lord at the door, my lady. He's not knocking. So I am.

VAEL doesn't wait for an answer. He pushes the door open with one hand and closes it behind him with his back. He has dropped his outer coat somewhere below. His sleeves are rolled. The black scales on his hands are darker tonight.

He looks at her once. Just once. And the slow patience he wore in the corridor is gone.

[KEY CAMERA]
Low static of the room. Cut to her wrist marks being rubbed away. Cut to Vael in the doorway — slow push to medium. Cut to her — she stops breathing.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: You said you wouldn't collect tonight.
VAEL: I won't.
ISOLDE: Then leave.
VAEL: I came to mark you. That's not the same thing.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
He crosses the room in three strides. She backs into the wall before she means to. Her shoulder hits stone.

---

## S#2 — Drakonis Keep. Isolde's Chamber. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael's palm goes flat on the stone above her shoulder. He doesn't grip her. He doesn't have to. The black scales on the back of his hand are an inch from her ear.

His other hand finds the thin silk strap at her shoulder. He doesn't slide it. He pulls. The strap snaps clean. The lace robe falls open.

He kisses her — once, on the mouth, no quarter inch of pause, no asking. Then his mouth is on her jaw. Then on her throat. He doesn't stop between them.

She gasps once. Her hands come up between them. She does not push him away. She closes them into the front of his shirt and does not let go.

He bites — just below the line of her jaw, on the soft side of her throat. Not hard enough to bleed. Hard enough to mark.

He moves a half-inch lower and bites again. Two crescent prints. He doesn't pull back to look. He pulls her chin toward him with his thumb and bites the corner of her mouth.

She makes a sound she didn't plan to make.

He still doesn't stop.

[KEY CAMERA]
Hand close — strap snapping. Cut to mouth on throat. Cut to her hands closing on his shirt. Cut to the two crescent marks blooming dark on her throat in the same shot.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (against her throat): Drop it.
ISOLDE: Drop what —
VAEL: Whatever you were counting on the wall.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He bites her again, lower.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Only I leave a mark on you. Anyone else tries, I take the hand.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
A SOUND at the window. Wrong sound. Cloth on stone. Somebody on the wall.

## S#3 — Drakonis Keep. Isolde's Chamber. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael's mouth lifts off her throat. He doesn't step back. He listens.

A SHADOW at the window slit. A gloved hand. A dagger.

The ASSASSIN swings inside in one motion — black cloth from face to wrist, a curved knife in a reverse grip. He moves for Isolde first, not Vael. He knows who he is paid to kill.

Vael moves faster than a man should.

He doesn't draw a blade. He catches the assassin's wrist mid-thrust and twists. The bone gives audibly. The dagger drops on the stone. Vael keeps the wrist and walks the man backward two steps to the far wall and puts him through it — not metaphorically. A divot of black stone breaks loose.

He picks up the dagger and bends it in half with one hand. The metal shrieks. Black scales now run up to his elbow. His eyes are not grey anymore. They are dark red, edge of the iris, like wine seen through black glass.

Outside the window, far above the keep — the keep itself groans. Every torch in the corridor below goes out at once. From the slit window, against the night sky, an enormous shadow with the shape of a winged thing rises behind the tower, big enough to fill the eastern view, and is gone.

The assassin coughs once. Vael lifts him by the throat.

[KEY CAMERA]
Reverse grip dagger snapping into the room. Wrist crack. Wall impact, debris falling. Tilt up to the window: huge wing silhouette filling the sky. Cut close to Vael's eye — red ring.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Who paid.
ASSASSIN (choking): Veine. The brother. Harrin.
VAEL (almost gentle): Say it again.
ASSASSIN: Harrin. Of Veine. He paid.
VAEL: Louder. So she hears.
ASSASSIN: HARRIN OF VEINE.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde, against the wall, two crescent marks on her throat, mouth bruised, says nothing. She watches.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Good. Now sleep.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He doesn't kill the assassin in front of her. He hands him to KIRAN, who has appeared in the doorway like he's been there the whole time.

[DIALOGUE]
KIRAN: Yard?
VAEL: Yard. Alive.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Kiran drags the assassin out by the broken wrist. Door closes.

Vael turns back to her. Black scales still on his arms. Red still in his eyes.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: I'm not done.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He puts his hand back on her throat — over the marks — and kisses her again. Doesn't pause. Doesn't soften.

Later — after she has slipped into uneasy sleep. The chamber dim. Vael sits on the edge of the bed beside her, fully dressed, scales gone soft. He doesn't touch her. He watches her breathe. His fingers hover an inch above the lowest crescent on her throat, then settle on the sheet instead. He does not leave. (Isolde sleeps through it — audience only.)

[DIALOGUE]
None.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
Outside the window, very far off across the wasteland, a single second of red glow — not lightning. Something breathing.

Hard Cut.

# THE OFFERING — EP04: BANNERS BURN. WALL OF BLACK FIRE.

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Council Chamber. Day.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A long black table. A high seat at one end. VAEL in the high seat. KIRAN at his right hand. Three of the keep's older LORDS along the table — old men, dragon-blood, scarred. Around them on the walls: the captured banners of every kingdom that has tried Drakonis in three centuries. Gold lion of Veine is the smallest one, hung lowest.

ISOLDE stands by the eastern window. She is in the daily black silk now — the same dress style as last night's bridal, but the morning version, the lived-in version. Pearl at her collar. Two crescent marks visible on her throat. Hair half-pinned. She is not at the table. She is at the window, watching the road.

The doors open. A VEINE ENVOY enters — older man, grey beard, the king's seal on his chest. He is not Harrin. He is worse — the king's own hand. The one who does the speaking when Harrin can't be there.

He bows shallowly. He looks at Isolde once and then through her.

ENVOY: My lord Drakonis. King Aldric of Veine sends greetings.
VAEL: He sends the man who sent the dagger last night.
ENVOY (perfectly still): I beg your lordship's pardon. I know nothing of a dagger.
KIRAN (mild): Funny. The dagger knew everything about you.

Kiran sets a thing on the table. The assassin's broken dagger. Beside it: a small black wax seal — sliced cleanly in half — with the lion-and-cup mark of Veine pressed into it.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the council. Cut to the broken dagger on black wood. Cut to the wax seal beside it. Cut to the envoy's face — one tiny twitch at the eye corner. Cut to Isolde at the window — she sees the seal. She knows that seal.

[DIALOGUE]
ENVOY (lower now): I came on a marriage matter, my lord.
VAEL: Then let me hear it.
ENVOY: The Veine court has reviewed the contract. There is — a difficulty. The woman delivered is not the woman named in the contract. The crown of Veine regrets to inform Drakonis that the offering was a fraud. The girl is not the king's daughter. She is a runaway servant. We've come to take her back and replace her with a true bride within the moon.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Silence at the table.

Then Vael laughs. Once. A short sound. It is not friendly.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: A servant.
ENVOY: I am sorry, my lord.
VAEL: You're not sorry. You're early.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
SUPER beside the envoy's bow: KING'S CLOSE HAND — VEINE COURT.

[END HOOK]
Vael stands. The black scales on his hands are already showing.

VAEL: Bring it outside.

## S#2 — Drakonis Keep. East Parapet. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The parapet looks east, over the wasteland. Past the wasteland, the road back to Veine, and past the road, the mountains.

Vael walks to the rail. Kiran follows with the gold lion banner of Veine — the small one, the one hung lowest in the council. Two guards bring the envoy, not roughly, but with the certainty of men who can be rough.

ISOLDE walks out beside Vael. She is not invited. She is not stopped. She stands at his right shoulder.

VAEL: You came to call her a runaway servant.
ENVOY: My king's words, my lord.
VAEL: Then your king should watch.

Vael takes the banner from Kiran. Holds it out over the rail. The wind tugs the gold lion.

He doesn't say a spell. He doesn't gesture. He opens his hand.

The banner ignites in midair before it falls. Black flame. The gold lion warps and crawls — for one second, the burning shape looks like a dragon wrapped around a kneeling lion. Then the ash holds shape and drifts down toward the wasteland.

Beyond the rail, far out — the ground answers.

A line cracks across the wasteland. The crack widens. From the crack, a wall of black fire rises. Not a flame. A wall. Three hundred feet high. It runs the length of the horizon. It hisses. The air bends above it.

The envoy stumbles back from the rail. His grey beard is suddenly wet at the brow. The skin of his face is reddening — not from his own shame. From the heat half a mile away.

Far below in the yard, twelve of the Veine outriders who came with him are on their knees before they decided to kneel. Their horses have bolted.

The wall of black fire stands there. It doesn't move closer. It doesn't have to.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide pan along the parapet. Cut to the banner igniting in midair, dragon-ash shape. Slow tilt down past the rail to the wasteland crack opening. Wide aerial of the wall of black fire rising — length of the horizon. Cut close to the envoy: skin going red, beard going damp. Cut to the kneeling outriders far below.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (quiet, beside Isolde): That's a servant's welcome.
ENVOY: My lord — mercy —
VAEL: I'm being merciful. You're still standing.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde turns to the envoy. She is not the offering anymore in this shot. She is the witness.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Tell your king. The girl you sold is the girl who watched this happen. Tell him exactly what I looked like when I watched it.
ENVOY: Lady — please —
ISOLDE: I'm not a lady. According to your court I'm a runaway servant. So go.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
SUPER as the wall rises: WALL OF BLACK FIRE — EAST WASTELAND, DRAKONIS BORDER.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (V.O., quiet under the heat haze, audience only): Everyone sent me away. He didn't.

[END HOOK]
The envoy is led back toward his horse. As he passes Isolde, his shoulder twitches. He drops something at her feet — small, deliberate, fast. A folded square of grey parchment with a black-thumb seal on it. He doesn't look back.

Isolde puts her foot over it before Kiran can see.

Hard Cut.

# THE OFFERING — EP05: THE TERRACE COUNT.

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Isolde's Chamber. Late Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde sits on the wide windowsill. Maiden nightwear — sheer white silk, ivory robe slipped to one shoulder. The pendant rests outside the silk now, on her chest. Her left wrist shows the two old chain bruises healing yellow. Her throat shows the two crescent marks darker than yesterday.

On her thigh, hidden under the robe, the folded grey parchment.

She opens it. Two words, in a small hand: ALDRIC KNEW.

Below the word, a black-thumb seal. The same wax Kiran put on the council table.

She breathes out. She folds the parchment again and tucks it into the bodice lining of her dress lying on the chair — the same lining her mother's pendant used to live in.

A KNOCK. Not the door. The wall. Vael's wall. The chamber next door.

VAEL'S VOICE (through the stone): Come out.

She doesn't go through the wall. She gets up and goes around. She wraps the robe tight. She walks the corridor barefoot.

[KEY CAMERA]
Close on the parchment: ALDRIC KNEW. Cut to her tucking it into the lining. Cut to her bare feet on black stone.

[DIALOGUE]
None.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
She steps through the doorway onto the terrace.

---

## S#2 — Drakonis Keep. East Terrace. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The terrace is half outside. Stone rail, no roof. The wasteland glow from yesterday's wall is gone now. But far east, between the mountain peaks, something is breathing. Slow red. Slow red. Like coals under ash.

VAEL stands at the rail with his back to her. Black shirt, sleeves rolled, no coat. The black scales on his hands go up to the elbow tonight. He doesn't turn when she comes out.

She stops at the doorway. For a beat she just looks at his back — the line of his shoulders, the scaled forearm on the stone rail. He cannot see her looking. Audience can.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (V.O., to herself, audience only): I shouldn't be watching him like this. I do anyway.

ISOLDE: You called me.
VAEL: I asked you out.
ISOLDE: You knocked on the wall.
VAEL: That's how I ask.
ISOLDE (comes to the rail beside him, a hand-width apart, looking east): The mountains are breathing.
VAEL: They're sleeping.
ISOLDE: Those are not mountains.
VAEL: No.
ISOLDE (looks at him for the first time tonight; he still hasn't): How many.
VAEL: Enough.
ISOLDE: Enough for what.
VAEL (breath wrong, slightly off rhythm, like an animal holding still on purpose, scales on the back of his hand pulsing one shade darker then back): For what your stepbrother is about to make me do.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide two-shot at the rail, mountains behind them — slow red glow between peaks. Cut close to his hand on the rail: scales pulsing. Cut close to her counting.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: I've been counting.
VAEL: I noticed.
ISOLDE: A grey rider leaves the south gate every other night. Just past midnight. He carries something small.
VAEL: Yes.
ISOLDE: He stops at the tradesman's hut at the river bend. He hands it off. Then he rides back in by the east gate.
VAEL: Yes.
ISOLDE: That's not your rider.
VAEL: No.
ISOLDE: Whose.
VAEL: Your father's man. Carrying his seal.
ISOLDE: My father.
VAEL: King Aldric. Yes.
ISOLDE (very still): My father knows I'm here.
VAEL: He's known since before you arrived.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
He turns toward her then. His eye-ring is not red. The control costs him. He hooks a finger under her chin.

VAEL: Mine.
ISOLDE: I haven't said that.
VAEL: You will. And then I'll say it back. In that order.

Hard Cut.

# THE OFFERING — EP06: THE SELF-PLACED SEAT.

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Great Hall. Sundown.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The Great Hall of Drakonis Keep at the evening meal. Long table, black wood, twelve places set. Vael at the head. Kiran at his right. The three old dragon-blood lords along one side. Two empty chairs on the other side: one beside Vael, one near the foot.
