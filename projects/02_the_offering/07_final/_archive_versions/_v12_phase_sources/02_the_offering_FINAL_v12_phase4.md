# THE OFFERING — EP17

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Vael's Private Chamber. Same Day. Late Afternoon.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The chamber. The crown-day robes already off her, folded on the chair. Isolde in a thin chemise, the silk pendant outside, the hidden black band still under her hair at the nape.

VAEL closes the heavy chamber door behind him. He locks it. He has not done that in this keep before — not even on the first night. He turns the iron key in the iron lock and drops the key on the table.

He doesn't speak. He crosses the room in four strides. He puts both his hands on her ribs through the chemise. He lifts her against the wall.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the room. Slow push as he locks the door. Close on the iron key dropping. Cut to him crossing in four strides. Close on his hands flat on her ribs through the chemise — black scales to the elbow, knuckles pale on her side. Cut close to her back hitting the wall — her hair shifts and the small black band slides forward an inch.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (breath catching): What happened in that hall.
VAEL: He looked at you too long.
ISOLDE: Who.
VAEL: Aldric. Twice. Once when you walked the aisle. Once when you wouldn't take his hand.
ISOLDE: He is my father.
VAEL: He is the man who sold you. He doesn't get the look.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He puts his mouth on the fifth crescent on her neck — the one he set the night before Aldric arrived. He doesn't bite. He drags his teeth across it, slow, and the mark goes redder.

She breathes through her teeth once. Her hands close on the front of his black coat at his chest. She does not push him away. She pulls the coat open.

His scales run up from his hands to his elbows, then past the elbows for the first time in the keep — up to the shoulder, dark against the lighter skin underneath.

Her palms find the scales at his collarbone. Warm. Alive. She does not recoil. Her hands go up over the scales, behind his neck, into his hair.

[KEY CAMERA]
Close on his teeth dragging across the fifth crescent. Cut to her hand opening his coat. Cut close to scales rising past his elbow to the shoulder — slow. Cut to her palms on the warm scales at his collarbone. Close on her flushed cheek against his jaw.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Look at me. Not at the wall.
VAEL: I see you.
ISOLDE: He looked. Yes. He always will.
VAEL: He doesn't.
ISOLDE: Vael —
VAEL: He doesn't.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He sets her down on the floor — feet on the stone, back still against the wall. He goes down on his knees in front of her. He presses his face against her stomach through the chemise. His scaled hands flat on her hips. He does not move for a long count.

When he lifts his face from her stomach, his eyes are the dark red of EP03 — full ring, not edge.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: No one looks at you while I'm in the room.
ISOLDE: No one will.
VAEL: No one looks at you when I'm not in the room either.
ISOLDE: Then don't leave.
VAEL: I won't.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
A KNOCK at the heavy door. Kiran, low.

KIRAN (off): My lord. South gate guard killed an hour ago. Curved knife. Red wax mark on the cloth.

Vael does not move his face from her stomach.

VAEL (without looking up): Volzaar.
KIRAN (off): The mark is Volzaar's.

Hard Cut.

# THE OFFERING — EP18

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. East Wasteland. Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The wasteland east of the keep. A full moon. Black scorched ground from old fire.

ISOLDE a hundred paces out from the gate. Drakonis bodice still on her. The crowns gone — she left them in the chamber. The hidden black band under her hair. The fifth crescent visible above her collar, redder than usual tonight.

VAEL stands twenty paces out from her. No coat. Black shirt. Sleeves torn off above the elbow.

He doesn't speak. He breathes once.

Scales run from his hands up his arms. Across his chest. The shirt tears down the back and falls away in two halves. His back muscle changes shape. He drops to one knee. His arms become forelimbs. The forelimbs become legs. The legs become massive. The neck extends. The face elongates. Horns push out of his hair. The hair is gone. Scales coat everything.

Forty-five seconds. Slower than it should be.

Where Vael was standing, a black dragon stands. Sixty feet at the shoulder. Eyes the same grey, slow, finding her. The dark red ring inside the grey is on her side now too — both eyes, not one.

He lowers his head. The head bigger than her body. He stops six paces from her. He waits.

Isolde walks forward.

She does not put her hand on the scales of his cheek. Not yet. She puts her cheek against them. Her hair is against the scale. Her breath warm. The scales are hotter than human skin tonight — close to her face.

She closes her eyes.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the wasteland under the moon. Slow tracking of the transformation — scales running, shirt tearing, body changing. Cut wide to the finished dragon. Cut close to Isolde walking. Slow push of her cheek against the warm scales — not her hand. Close on her closed eyes, hair flat against the scale.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (against the scales): You're warmer like this.
VAEL (in her head, low rumble, only her): Always.
ISOLDE: Show me what your father showed Sera. So I can put my hand on it.
VAEL: I don't know what he showed her.
ISOLDE: Then show me what you'd show me.
VAEL: Sit.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The black dragon lowers his foreleg. Isolde climbs into the crook of it. She sits with her back against the foreleg-bone, her body small inside the curve of his elbow. She is the size of a knuckle joint of his finger.

He lifts the foreleg slow. He brings her up to the level of his neck. Then to the level of his jaw.

He breathes out. Black fire — slow, low to the ground — rolls forward into the wasteland away from her. Not a wall. A river. It runs east toward the dragon ridge. The thirty dragons on the ridge open their eyes. Then close them.

[KEY CAMERA]
Slow push behind Isolde sitting in the crook of his foreleg. Wide as he lifts her to his jaw level. Cut to his breath — black fire rolling east, slow. Cut to the ridge — thirty pairs of eyes opening, closing. Cut close to her hand flat on the warm scales beside her cheek.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (in her head): The ridge knows you now.
ISOLDE: All of them.
VAEL: All of them.
ISOLDE: Sera will see.
VAEL: She'll see tonight.
ISOLDE: Good.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He lowers her slow. Foreleg back to the ground. She climbs out of the crook. She walks beside his hind leg toward the keep.

Halfway across the wasteland, he transforms back. Bone clicks. Body shrinks. By the time they reach the gate he is a man again, in his torn black shirt, walking beside her, his scaled palm flat on the small of her back. His shirt is still in two halves at the back — he doesn't bother holding it closed.

Aldric is at the gate. He has watched from the wall. He does not kneel. He just nods, slow.

Vael does not look at him.

[KEY CAMERA]
Slow tracking them walking. Cut to Aldric on the wall — not moving. Close on Vael's scaled palm on her back through the silk. Cut to her — flushed, hair down, his hand at her spine.

[DIALOGUE]
ALDRIC: My lord.
VAEL (without turning): No.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He walks her past Aldric into the keep. He does not stop until they are at the chamber door.

He closes it. He locks it. He puts his torn shirt on the floor.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
SUPER on the dragon ridge in close-up: DRAKONIS RIDGE — KNOWS HER.

[END HOOK]
Outside the chamber window, on the southern horizon, a single red shape lifts off a rock outcrop. Sera. Has felt the ridge open its eyes. Has started flying.

Hard Cut.

# THE OFFERING — EP20

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. East Wasteland. Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The wasteland. Moon thinner tonight. The black dragon already there. Isolde walks toward him from the gate — not in court silk, in a heavy black wool wrap that covers her from throat to ankles. Her hair down over the hidden band.

The black dragon lowers his foreleg. She climbs into the crook of it. He lifts her to his shoulder height. She sits in the curve of his neck against his head.

On the southern horizon, the red shape gets bigger. Closer.

Sera lands a hundred paces out. Red scales. Forty feet at the shoulder. She does not transform. She speaks from dragon form.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the wasteland — black dragon, Isolde sitting on his shoulder, red dragon landing in front of them. Slow tracking around them. Close on Sera's eyes — red ring inside the green.

[DIALOGUE]
SERA (dragon, voice female and deep): Hello, Vael. You hid her well.
VAEL (dragon, voice deeper than hers, aloud now): I'm not hiding her. She's on my shoulder.
SERA: She is.
VAEL: Talk to her.
SERA: To her.
VAEL: To her.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Sera lifts her head one degree. She looks at Isolde directly. Isolde, sixty feet up in the curve of Vael's neck, does not move. She puts her hand flat against the scale beside her thigh.

[DIALOGUE]
SERA (to Isolde, lowering her head another degree to be eye-level with her up there): You wear the pendant.
ISOLDE: I wear both halves.
SERA: That was promised to my mother. Then to me. Years before either of you grew up.
ISOLDE: Then take it up with the dead man who promised it.
SERA: I'll take it up with the man who broke the promise.
VAEL: It was unbroken. My father promised what wasn't his to promise.
SERA (still looking at Isolde): Old law. Two crowns. One mate. Dragon decides. He decides by fight or by yield.
ISOLDE: He decided.
SERA: Tell me how he decided.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde lifts her hand off the scale beside her thigh. She puts it flat on her own belly under the wool. Not visible to Sera from where she stands.

But Isolde's hand stays there. She speaks anyway.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: He decided. He marked me. He carried me. He locked the chamber door behind us last night so no one — including my father — would ever look at me too long again. He woke up this morning and burned the woman who poisoned my mother because she once shared a roof with me. He will do the same to your house if you give him the reason. He hasn't given you a reason yet. He's giving you a way to leave.
SERA: He's giving you a way to ask me to leave.
ISOLDE: I'm asking you to leave.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Sera does not transform back. She does not move for a long count. Then she lowers her head — not a kneel. A look-down. She is checking.

She is checking Isolde's hand under the wool on her belly. From sixty feet away, she cannot see it. She is checking the angle of the hand against the cloth.

She sees the angle.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the wasteland — two dragons facing, Isolde on Vael's shoulder. Close on Isolde's hand flat on her belly under the wool wrap. Cut to Sera's lowered head — eye narrowing, reading the angle from far. Cut close to Sera's eye — the read.

[DIALOGUE]
SERA (slow, almost to herself, only Vael's hearing range — Isolde doesn't hear): She already carries.
VAEL (in her head, not aloud, so Isolde doesn't hear): Yes.
SERA: That early.
VAEL: That early.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Sera lifts her head. She does not say what she just learned. She turns her body half away. Her wings unfold.

[DIALOGUE]
SERA (aloud, for Isolde): I withdraw the challenge tonight. I do not withdraw the claim. I will be back inside the season.
ISOLDE: Come back. We'll be here.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Sera lifts off. Her wings push wind across the wasteland. She rises and turns south.

The black dragon lowers his foreleg. Isolde climbs out of the crook of his shoulder. She walks beside his hind leg back toward the keep. He transforms halfway across. By the gate he is a man, walking beside her, scaled palm on the small of her back.

Aldric on the wall again. He does not speak.

[KEY CAMERA]
Slow tracking them walking back. Cut to Aldric on the wall, watching. Close on Vael's hand on her back. Cut close to Isolde — hand still on her belly under the wool.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
SUPER as Sera takes off: VOLZAAR — WITHDRAWN, NOT FOLDED.

[END HOOK]
Inside the chamber, Vael locks the door. He turns to her. He puts his palm on her belly under the wool for the first time himself.

He doesn't look at her face. He looks at his own palm.

VAEL: How long.
ISOLDE: Six weeks. Three days.
VAEL: You knew before tonight.
ISOLDE: Yesterday.
VAEL: You faced her with this on you.
ISOLDE: With this. With you behind me.

He kneels. His mouth on her belly through the wool. He stays there for a long count.

Hard Cut.
