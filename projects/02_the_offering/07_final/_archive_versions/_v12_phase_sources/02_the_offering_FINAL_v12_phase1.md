# THE OFFERING — EP04

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Isolde's Chamber. Morning After.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The room is half-lit. East slit window, grey dawn just beginning. The bed is wrecked — black sheet pulled off one corner, ivory pillows on the floor. The broken silk strap from last night is on the chair back.

ISOLDE is on the bed edge in a thin white robe pulled hastily over a maiden chemise. Her hair is loose, the half-pin from last night still tangled at her crown. Three crescent marks dark on her throat — two under the jaw, one below the collarbone. Both wrists yellow with bruise.

VAEL is in the room. Black trousers, no shirt yet, sleeves of last night's black shirt thrown across the chair. The black scales run up to his elbows in the morning light, fainter, but visible. He is standing at her window slit, looking east.

He turns from the window before she lifts her head. He looks at her — at her marks first, then at her wrists, then at her mouth.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide reveal of the wrecked bed. Cut close to her wrists — yellow bruise. Cut to her three crescents. Cut to him at the window — black scales on his back muscle. Cut to him crossing to her.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: You stayed.
VAEL: I didn't leave.
ISOLDE: That's not the same.
VAEL: It is.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He stops in front of her. He goes down on one knee — not for ceremony, for her eye level. He puts his thumb under her chin and tilts her face up toward him. He looks at the lowest mark, the one below the collarbone, the deepest.

His mouth tightens, a fraction.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: I bit too low.
ISOLDE: You bit where you meant to.
VAEL: I meant to.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He doesn't apologize. He puts his mouth flat on the lowest mark — not a bite this time, just his mouth. A long second. He breathes out against her skin.

She closes her eyes.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
He pulls back. He looks at her again.

VAEL: Are you afraid of me.
ISOLDE (after a beat): I should be.
VAEL: That's not what I asked.

## S#2 — Drakonis Keep. Isolde's Chamber. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She doesn't answer the question. He doesn't ask again. He stands.

He picks up his black shirt from the chair. He doesn't put it on. He folds it once and lays it on the foot of the bed — for later, when she wants him covered.

He goes to her wash basin. Black stone, water already poured by some servant before dawn. He takes the linen cloth folded beside it. He wets it. He wrings it. He brings it to her.

He sits beside her on the bed edge. He takes her left wrist — the one with the worse yellow bruise — and presses the cool cloth over it. He doesn't speak.

She watches his hand.

[KEY CAMERA]
Close on his hand wetting the cloth — black scales fading into pale knuckles. Cut to her watching. Cut to the cloth on her wrist. Cut to her face — she has stopped breathing.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (quiet): You don't do this.
VAEL: I do now.
ISOLDE: For who.
VAEL: For you. Don't ask twice.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He moves the cloth from her left wrist to her right. He presses. He does not look up. The cloth darkens on the bruise.

She lifts her free hand. She puts it — not on his face, not on his shoulder — on his forearm. On the black scales. She doesn't recoil.

He stops moving the cloth.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Don't touch the scales if you're afraid.
ISOLDE: I'm not.
VAEL: Then keep your hand there.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She keeps her hand there. The scales under her palm are warmer than human skin. Not hot. Alive.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
A knock on the chamber door. Kiran's voice — low, not urgent, but not ignorable.

KIRAN: My lord. Veine envoy at the south road. Old man. Not the brother.

Vael does not lift his hand from her wrist.

VAEL (to the door): I'll come down. He waits.

The cloth stays on her wrist.

## S#3 — Drakonis Keep. Lower Receiving Hall. Mid-Morning.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A smaller hall than the council chamber. Black stone, one long table, no banners on the wall. Two of the keep's black-coat guards at the doors. Kiran beside the table.

VAEL enters from the inner stair. He has put on the black shirt. Sleeves still rolled. Coat over the chair, not on him. He sits at the table head.

ISOLDE walks in behind him. She is dressed for the day now — fitted black silk, ivory pearls at the collarbone, hair pinned. The three crescent marks visible. She does not stand at the window today. She stands at his right shoulder, behind his chair.

The VEINE ENVOY enters from the outer door. Grey beard, the king's close hand. He bows shallow. He looks at Isolde once. He looks longer at the crescent marks on her throat. He looks away.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the smaller hall. Cut to Isolde behind Vael's chair, hand on the chair back. Cut to the envoy's eye on her throat. Cut to Vael — not looking at the envoy yet.

[DIALOGUE]
ENVOY: My lord Drakonis. King Aldric of Veine sends greetings.
VAEL: Greetings are for guests.
ENVOY: I came on a marriage matter. The Veine court has reviewed the contract. The woman delivered is not the woman named. She is a servant. Veine will replace her with a true bride within the moon.
VAEL: A servant.
ENVOY: I am sorry, my lord.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael turns his head — not toward the envoy. Toward Isolde behind his shoulder. He looks at her. He waits.

She steps forward beside his chair. She puts her hand flat on the table in front of him. The three crescent marks visible from the envoy's angle.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (to the envoy, not loud): Look at me.
ENVOY: My lady —
ISOLDE: I said look at me. You came to take a servant back. Tell my father this — the servant is wearing his daughter's pearls and his daughter's marks. Tell him whichever girl he sends next, the marks come first and the contract comes after.
VAEL (quiet, beside her): That's what she says.
ENVOY: My lord —
VAEL: You heard her. You repeat it. Word for word.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael takes her hand on the table. He turns it over. He puts his thumb on the inside of her wrist — over the yellow bruise. He doesn't lift his eyes from her face.

He kisses the bruise. Once. In front of the envoy.

He puts her hand back down.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide on the table. Cut to her hand under his on the table. Cut close to his mouth on her wrist. Cut to the envoy — his beard is wet at the lip. Cut to her — she has not blinked.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Now you go.
ENVOY: My lord, the king will hear —
VAEL: I know he'll hear. That's the point.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
The envoy is led out. As he passes Isolde, his shoulder twitches. He drops a small folded square of grey parchment at her feet. Black-thumb seal on it. He doesn't look back.

Isolde puts her foot over the parchment before Kiran sees.

Hard Cut.

# THE OFFERING — EP05

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Isolde's Chamber. Late Afternoon.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde alone in her chamber. The day dress on. The grey parchment on her lap. She opens it.

Two words: ALDRIC KNEW.

Below: a black-thumb seal.

She doesn't tuck it away. She holds it. She reads it twice.

A FOOTSTEP outside the door. She folds the parchment under her palm.

VAEL pushes the door open with one hand. He doesn't knock. He sees her hand on her lap.

He crosses the room. He stops in front of her chair. He looks at her hand.

[KEY CAMERA]
Close on the parchment unfolded — ALDRIC KNEW. Cut to her folding it under her palm. Cut to him entering. Cut close to his eye on her hand.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Show me.
ISOLDE: It's mine.
VAEL: Show me.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She doesn't move her hand. He waits. He doesn't take it. He waits.

She lifts her palm. He picks up the parchment. He reads it. His face does not change.

He folds it again. He puts it back in her hand. He closes her fingers around it.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: You keep it.
ISOLDE: You aren't going to ask me about him.
VAEL: I'm going to ask you about you.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
He takes her hand — the one closed around the parchment. He pulls her up out of the chair.

VAEL: Walk with me.

---

## S#2 — Drakonis Keep. East Terrace. Dusk.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The terrace is half outside. Stone rail, no roof. The east horizon — mountain peaks, the wasteland between. Quiet tonight. No banners burning. No wall of fire.

Vael and Isolde at the rail. He has not let go of her hand. The parchment is still folded in it, between his palm and hers.

He stops at the rail. He turns her to face him.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Your father sold you to me knowing what I am. The note in your hand says he knew. The man who handed you the note knew. The brother who paid the dagger knew. There is a road south from this terrace, and on that road, my men do not stand. You can walk it tonight.
ISOLDE: With you.
VAEL: Without me.
ISOLDE: To where.
VAEL: To wherever your name still works.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She doesn't answer. She looks at the south. She looks at his face. She looks at his hand on hers — the black scales fainter at dusk, but his grip is exactly the same as the grip he put on her wrist at the altar in EP02.

She turns her hand inside his. She closes her fingers around his thumb. She does not let go.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the terrace. Cut to the south road in deep dusk — empty, quiet. Cut to her hand turning inside his. Cut close to her fingers closing on his thumb. Cut to his face — he sees it.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: My name doesn't work south.
VAEL: It works in Veine.
ISOLDE: Veine sold it.
VAEL: I'm not buying it back. I'm offering you the road.
ISOLDE: I'm not taking the road.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He doesn't say anything. He puts his other hand on her jaw. He turns her face up to him. He puts his mouth on hers — once, not the bite from last night, longer. A different kind of mouth.

When he pulls back, she is still holding his thumb.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Say it again.
ISOLDE: I'm not taking the road.
VAEL: One more time.
ISOLDE: I'm staying.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He doesn't say anything for three beats. Then he takes the parchment out of her closed hand. He doesn't unfold it. He drops it over the rail. It falls into the dark of the keep yard.

He puts her hand against his chest. He covers it with his.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
Kiran appears at the terrace door. He sees the two of them. He sees Vael's hand over hers on his chest. He does not announce. He waits.

Vael does not turn around.

VAEL (without lifting his eyes from her): What.
KIRAN: Veine envoy is on the south road, my lord. He's met a grey rider with your father-in-law's seal. They are riding back together.
VAEL: Then my father-in-law knows tonight.
KIRAN: He does.
VAEL: Good.

The terrace torches behind them burn lower. Vael does not move his hand. Neither does she.

## S#3 — Drakonis Keep. Isolde's Chamber. Same Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The chamber. The bed remade with fresh black sheets. One candle on the side table.

He brings her in by the hand. He closes the door with his back. He does not let go.

He takes her to the bed edge. He sits her down. He goes down on one knee in front of her again — the second time today.

He puts his hand at the back of her neck — under her hair, against the warm skin at the nape. The other hand at her hip.

[KEY CAMERA]
Close on his hand under her hair. Cut to her face — looking down at him. Cut close to his mouth at her collarbone scar — the lowest crescent. Cut to his hand on her hip tightening.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: You said you're staying.
ISOLDE: I said it.
VAEL: Then I'm putting one more mark on you tonight. Not because anyone is watching. Because you said it on the terrace and I want it on your skin before you sleep.
ISOLDE: Where.
VAEL: Where no one else will ever see.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He puts his mouth on her sternum — between the collarbones, just below where the silk meets the skin. He bites. Light at first. Then teeth. A fourth crescent — hers and his and no one else's, ever.

She doesn't gasp. She breathes out once. Her hand goes to the back of his head and stays.

He lifts his mouth. He looks at the new mark. He covers it with his palm. He waits until the print of his palm is the only thing on her skin there, and then he lifts the palm.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: That one's mine.
ISOLDE: They're all yours.
VAEL: That one's the only one no one else sees. Sleep with it.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He stands. He pulls the black sheet up over her shoulder. He kisses the top of her hair once.

He does not leave.

He sits in the chair across from the bed. He puts his black coat over himself. He watches her sleep.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
The candle burns down. He has not moved.

Hard Cut.
