# THE OFFERING — EP46 (v30) — SERA SWEARS

## S#1 — Outer Yard. Dawn.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Sera has been at the gate all night. She has not slept. Her hands on her knees.

Kiran walks out at first light.

[DIALOGUE]
KIRAN: My queen will see you.
SERA (does not move yet): ...
KIRAN: Stand up.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Sera stands. Walks behind Kiran into the keep.

---

## S#2 — Great Hall. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Hall. Isolde in the queen's chair beside Vael. The crown not on her head today — set on the table beside her. Mireille in a small wrap in her lap.

Sera walks the length of the hall. Stops at the foot of the dais. Looks up.

She kneels. Slow. Both knees on the stone.

[DIALOGUE]
SERA: My queen.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde does not gloat. Does not smile.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Stand up. I do not want you on your knees. I want you useful.
SERA: Useful how.
ISOLDE: Eastern border. The council is gone but the road they came from is not gone. Watch it for me.
SERA: My queen — that is a position of trust.
ISOLDE: It is.
SERA: I tried to ruin your child before she was born.
ISOLDE: I know.
SERA: ...
ISOLDE: I am giving you a position you have not earned. If you betray it, I do not give it again. I will burn what is left.
SERA: ...
VAEL (does not look at Sera): She means it.
SERA (after a long beat): Yes, my queen.
ISOLDE: Stand up.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Sera stands. She has not cried — but her eyes are wet.

[DIALOGUE]
SERA: Why.
ISOLDE: Because you are the only one in this keep who knows what it is to want him and not have him. I want someone on my eastern border who knows what wanting feels like. The council remembers wanting too. You will see what they do before anyone else does.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Sera bows once. Walks out.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held wide of the hall. Sera leaving.

---

## S#3 — Corridor After.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael walking the corridor with Isolde. Mireille in his arm now.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: You gave Sera the eastern border.
ISOLDE: I did.
VAEL: She could turn.
ISOLDE: She could. But she will know I am watching. The council will know I gave it to her. Both of those things keep her honest.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: My mother used to say. "Give a hungry person the thing they have starved for, and they will eat it carefully because they know you can take it back."
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: I am giving Sera the thing she has starved for. She has not starved for you. She has starved for purpose. I have given her purpose.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He stops walking. Looks at her.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: You are running this keep already.
ISOLDE: I am.
VAEL: I should sit down for the rest of my reign.
ISOLDE: You will not. You will stand at my shoulder.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: I like you at my shoulder.

---

## S#4 — Chamber. Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Bed. Both. Mireille in the basket beside the bed. He on his back. She on her side, watching him.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: When she is older.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: I want another.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: Not now. Not for two years. But I want another.
VAEL: I will give you anything you want.
ISOLDE: Two years.
VAEL: Two years.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She closes her eyes. He keeps watching her.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held wide.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
On the eastern border that same night — Sera at a watch fire. Alone. Looking east at the dark line of mountains where the council used to live.

She has a purpose. For the first time in her life. She holds it like something she is afraid to drop.

Hard Cut.
