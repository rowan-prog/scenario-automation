# THE OFFERING — EP16 (v30)

## S#1 — Surface Chamber. Morning After Council Departure.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael walks Isolde back up from the inner keep to his surface chamber. The deeper keep is no longer needed. Public mate, public defenses now.

She walks the corridor for the first time as queen-presumptive. Dragons in the corridor bow. She does not acknowledge them. She is studying. Learning who is who.

A small wave from a young dragon — third house, no political weight. She lifts her chin to him. He goes red.

[VO]
ISOLDE (V.O.): Twelve hours ago I was a tribute. This morning I am the lady the keep bows to. Nothing in the world has actually changed. Only the words for it.

---

## S#2 — Chamber. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She walks to the desk. Picks up the small wooden bird. Sets it on the windowsill instead.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Why there.
ISOLDE: The light is better. Your mother carved it. She would want light.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: Tell me about her. Properly. Not the way she died. The way she lived.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: Tonight. Not now. Tonight, in the bed, before we sleep.
VAEL: All right.

---

## S#3 — Corridor. Brief Sera Encounter.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde walking back from the upper terrace alone. Sera at the end of the corridor. They see each other.

Neither steps aside. They walk past each other in the middle of the corridor. Sera's eyes drop. Isolde does not look at her.

That is all.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held wide of the corridor after they pass. Neither has turned.

---

## S#4 — Chamber. Night. Bed.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Hearth low. Both in bed. He on his back. She on her side, watching him. He talks for the first time at length about his mother.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Her name was Liriel. She was small. Smaller than you. Hair like dark copper. She carved the bird when I was four because I would not stop asking what dragons looked like when we were not flying.
ISOLDE: What did you tell her.
VAEL: I told her dragons were quiet animals who waited for the right thing to come close enough to keep.
ISOLDE (after a beat): That is not a thing a four-year-old says.
VAEL: I said it. She wrote it down. I found the paper after she died.
ISOLDE: ...
VAEL: She did not deserve what they did to her.
ISOLDE: No.
VAEL: Sleep.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He turns onto his side. Hand on her belly through her nightdress. Both his palms cover her stomach.

[CUTAWAY]
The orange shimmer at his palms.
Her hand over his.
Her face — calm, eyes still open, thinking.

She closes her eyes.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
Across the keep, in a small unused storeroom, Sera meets the council elder who flew back from the south. They speak quietly. We do not hear the words. We see the elder hand Sera a small black token.

She closes her hand around it.

Hard Cut.
