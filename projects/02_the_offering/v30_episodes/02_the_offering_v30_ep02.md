# THE OFFERING — EP02 (v30)

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Vael's Chamber. Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael's private chamber. Wide. Dark stone, dark wood. A large bed against the far wall. A heavy desk. A weapon rack with one sword on it. One window slit east.

Kiran walks Isolde in. Does not speak. Closes the door behind her from the outside.

Isolde alone. She looks around. Walks the perimeter. Touches the sword on the rack — just two fingers on the hilt. Looks at the bed for a long moment. Walks to the window. Looks east.

She walks back to the center of the room. Turns once. Looks at the floor.

She spits — neatly, deliberately — on the stone in front of the bed.

[KEY CAMERA]
Close: her foot at the spit. Held.

[VO]
ISOLDE (V.O., low): Bride.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She tastes the word and does not like it.

---

## S#2 — Same Chamber. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The door opens. Vael walks in. Closes it. Locks it from the inside. Drops the key on the table.

He sees the spit on the floor. Does not look down.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: You spat on my floor.
ISOLDE: I spat on a word.
VAEL: Same floor.
ISOLDE: Then make me clean it. Husband.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He does not move.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: I'm not your husband. Not yet.
ISOLDE: You said bride.
VAEL: I did.
ISOLDE: Then what is this.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He walks toward her. Slow. Stops two paces away.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: This is me deciding when.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She does not step back.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Take off your dress.
ISOLDE: No.
VAEL: It's filthy. They dragged you in it.
ISOLDE: It's all I have.
VAEL: You have me. There is a bath chamber behind that door. Use it or sleep in their dirt.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde looks at him. Long beat. She walks past him to the bath chamber door. Opens it. Pauses at the threshold. Does not turn around.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Why bride.
VAEL (does not move): Because tribute means they won.
ISOLDE: Did they.
VAEL: Not while you're standing in my keep wearing my name.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She closes the bath door behind her without answering.

Vael stands in the chamber. Looks at the closed door. His right hand opens and closes once. He walks to the chair by the desk and sits. Does not drink anything. Watches the bath door.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held wide of him sitting. The bath door closed.

---

## S#3 — Same Chamber. Later.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The bath door opens. Steam rolls out.

Isolde comes out in his black robe — too big, sleeves down past her hands, neck loose. Hair wet. Skin red from heat. Eyes harder now, not softer.

He looks up. The glass in his hand — knuckles whiten.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Bed.
ISOLDE: Yours or mine.
VAEL: There is one bed.
ISOLDE: Then mine. You sleep on the chair.
VAEL: I sleep where I want in my own chambers.
ISOLDE: Then sleep in the chair. Want it.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He almost smiles. Does not.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Get in the bed.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She walks to the bed. Pulls the cover up. Lies down on her side, facing the wall. Pulls the cover up to her chin.

He stays in the chair. Watching her back.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the chamber. Cut to him in the chair. Cut to her back under the cover. Hold both for a long count.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (does not turn): Are you going to do it tonight.
VAEL: Do what.
ISOLDE: What husbands do.
VAEL: I told you. I'm not your husband yet.
ISOLDE: Then what are you.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Long beat. He does not answer.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Sleep.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She does not sleep. He does not sleep either.

---

## S#4 — Same Chamber. Pre-Dawn.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The candle is gone. Slit window grey with first light. Isolde finally asleep — her breathing slow. Hair across her cheek.

Vael stands. Walks to the bed. Stands over her. Looks at her face for a long count.

He reaches out. His hand hovers above her cheek. Audience-only: scales at his knuckles flicker once. He pulls his hand back.

He walks back to the chair. Sits. Looks at the door.

[KEY CAMERA]
Slow push from his face to his hand on the chair arm. Held.

[VO]
VAEL (V.O., audience-only): I found you. I held my hand a foot from your face. And I cannot keep you yet.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A KNOCK at the chamber door. Light. Kiran.

[DIALOGUE]
KIRAN (off, low through the wood): My lord. Lady Sera is at your chamber door.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael does not move.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Tell her if her shadow crosses this threshold I'll burn it off her.
KIRAN (off): Yes, my lord.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Footsteps go.

From the bed, eyes still closed, just woken:

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Who is Sera.
VAEL: Someone who thinks she's mine.
ISOLDE: Is she.
VAEL: No.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She opens her eyes. Looks at the ceiling.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
Outside the chamber door, Sera has walked away. But not far. At the end of the corridor, she stops. Turns. Looks back at the closed door.

Her face is wrong. She has heard the word she did not expect to hear.

She did not expect *bride*.

She begins to plan.

Hard Cut.
