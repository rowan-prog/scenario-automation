# THE OFFERING — EP20 (v30) — COUNCIL CLOSES

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Outer Yard. Morning.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The keep gate. One dragon walks up to it on foot — not flying. An old man in plain black. The bowing elder from EP15. He carries no weapon. No retinue.

Kiran meets him at the gate. Does not bow this time. Studies him.

[DIALOGUE]
KIRAN: My lord.
ELDER: I am here to speak to your lady. Alone if she will allow. With your lord if she will not.
KIRAN: Wait.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Kiran goes inside. Comes back. Walks the elder to the great hall.

---

## S#2 — Great Hall. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde at the dais — alone. Vael standing along the side wall. The keep's three lords at the back of the hall.

The elder walks to the foot of the dais.

He kneels. Slow — old joints. Stays kneeling.

[DIALOGUE]
ELDER: My lady. The council apologizes. The assassin was not sanctioned by the body. Three members paid for it. They have been removed.
ISOLDE: Removed how.
ELDER: Council business, my lady.
ISOLDE: ...

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She does not push. She studies him for a long beat.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Why did you come yourself.
ELDER: Because thirty years ago I lost a daughter. Public mate. Same way as the lord's mother. The council did not protect her then. The council moved against your bond now. I am the only one of the five who voted to leave you alone. I came to say so to your face.
ISOLDE: ...
ELDER: I am also old. I will be dead before your child can walk. I came so that one of the dragons who voted right is on record kneeling to her.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Long beat.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Stand up.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He stands. Slow.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: I accept the apology. Once. Do not let it become twice.
ELDER: Yes, my lady.
ISOLDE: Walk out. Eat first if you need to. The kitchens will give you what you ask for. Then walk.
ELDER: Yes, my lady.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He turns. Walks out of the hall. Kiran walks him to the kitchens.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held wide of the empty hall. Vael along the wall. Isolde on the dais.

---

## S#3 — Hall After. Vael and Isolde Alone.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He walks to the dais. Stops at the foot of it. Looks up at her in his chair.

She looks down at him.

For a long beat neither speaks.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: He will not come back.
ISOLDE: Then it is finished.
VAEL: It is finished.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She steps down off the dais. Stops in front of him. Looks at his face.

He almost smiles. Half a beat. The first time on screen.

She sees it. Does not comment. Walks past him.

[VO]
ISOLDE (V.O.): I have run my first hall. I did not raise my voice. I let a man old enough to be my grandfather walk out on his own feet. My mother would have been quiet today. She would have understood.

---

## S#4 — Chamber. Evening.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She is at the windowsill. The small wooden bird in her hand. She is studying the carving.

He walks in. Sees her with the bird. Stops at the door.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (without turning): Tell me where she sat to carve this.
VAEL: At a window like that one. Eastern light.
ISOLDE: She would have liked that I sat there too.
VAEL: She would have.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She turns. Holds the bird out.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: I want it mounted on the wall there. So I can see it from the bed.
VAEL: I will have it done tomorrow.
ISOLDE: Tomorrow.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She gives him the bird. He holds it. Looks at it. Closes his hand around it.

She walks past him to the bed. Sits on the edge. Puts her hand low on her belly.

He sits beside her. His hand over hers on her belly.

They do not speak.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held: two hands on her belly, the wooden bird beside them on the bed.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
Outside the keep, far down the eastern road — the elder is walking. Slow. He stops at a stream. Drinks. Walks on.

Far behind him — out of his sight — a single small dark shape on the road, following. Audience-only.

Sera has come back from the south to see him die.

She does not know yet that the keep no longer cares about the council. She is fighting yesterday's war.

Hard Cut.
