# THE OFFERING — EP45 (v30) — QUEEN

## S#1 — Great Hall. Mid-Day. Full Court.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The Great Hall in full court. Every dragon house. Every keep officer. The keep's three lords at the front. Kiran at Vael's left.

A new chair beside the lord's chair — black wood with silver inlay. A small silver crown on a black cushion at the foot of the chair.

Vael at the dais. Black coat. Silver thread at the collar — first time on screen.

Isolde walks in. Black gown — fitted, full sleeves, train short. Hair pinned. Mireille in her arms, in a small black wrap.

She walks the length of the hall. Slow. Dignified. The court bows as she passes.

She reaches the dais. Stops. Turns to face the hall.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the hall. Slow tracking her walking. Cut to Mireille in her arms.

---

## S#2 — Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The oldest keep lord rises. Walks to the foot of the dais. Bows.

[DIALOGUE]
OLDEST LORD: My lord Drakonis. The keep wishes to acknowledge.
VAEL: Acknowledge.
OLDEST LORD: The lady Isolde of Aurelle. Bride of Drakonis. Mate of the lord. Mother of the heir.
VAEL: Continue.
OLDEST LORD: We ask the lord to crown her queen.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael steps off the dais. Takes the small silver crown from the cushion. Walks to Isolde.

She lowers her head one degree. He places the crown on her hair. It does not sit on top — sits low, at the back of the crown of her head, partially hidden under her hair.

Audience-only: same placement as the small black band from the cold open's promise.

She lifts her head.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (to the hall, not loud): The Queen of Drakonis Keep.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Every dragon in the hall goes to one knee. Not gradual — at once. The keep has been waiting for this. Even the two of Sera's house who stayed.

Isolde does not gloat. Does not smile. Just looks at the court.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Stand up.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
They stand.

---

## S#3 — Hall. Continuous.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (to the court): I am not your queen because I married your lord. I am your queen because you bowed. You bowed before he crowned. The court records will show that order. The lord crowned after the court chose.
OLDEST LORD: It will be recorded that way, my queen.
ISOLDE: Good. One more thing.
OLDEST LORD: My queen.
ISOLDE: My daughter Mireille is the heir of this keep. If anything happens to me — and I am the woman who walked into your gate with a chain on her wrist a year ago, so things can happen — she is queen-regent until she is grown.
OLDEST LORD: Recorded, my queen.
ISOLDE: Now. Eat. The hall is open.

---

## S#4 — Chamber. Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Chamber. The silver crown on the table. Mireille in her basket.

Isolde at the windowsill. Vael behind her. His hands at her hips.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: You instructed the court to record that they bowed before I crowned.
ISOLDE: I did.
VAEL: Why.
ISOLDE: Because I want our daughter to read that ledger when she is grown and know that I was their queen first by their choice, not by yours.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He turns her around. Kisses her — slow, long.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: I love you.
ISOLDE: I love you.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held wide. The crown on the table behind them.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
SUPER on the crown: ISOLDE — QUEEN.

[END HOOK]
At the gate of the keep — Sera arrives. Late evening. Dismounts. Walks to the gate alone. Kiran meets her.

[DIALOGUE]
SERA: I am here to swear.
KIRAN: My lord and lady are eating.
SERA: I will wait.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She sits down on the stone outside the gate. Will wait all night if she has to.

Hard Cut.
