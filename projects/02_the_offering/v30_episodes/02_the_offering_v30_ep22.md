# THE OFFERING — EP22 (v30)

## S#1 — Kitchens. Morning.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde walking the kitchens — first time. The keep's HEAD COOK, an older female dragon, stops when she sees her.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: I want to know who carries the food.
HEAD COOK: My lady. The lower steward and his two boys.
ISOLDE: Names.
HEAD COOK: Halric and his sons Ferr and Sel.
ISOLDE: From today, only Halric. Not the boys. Not anyone else. If Halric is ill, the food does not come up.
HEAD COOK: My lady — that means I cannot serve you on his sick days.
ISOLDE: Then I will not eat that day.
HEAD COOK: ...
ISOLDE: We caught one assassin at the south wall. We will not catch the next one through a kitchen boy who does not know what he is carrying.
HEAD COOK: Yes, my lady.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde turns to go. Stops.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: One more thing.
HEAD COOK: My lady.
ISOLDE: The lord has not eaten properly in two months. Tonight, you send up something hot. Whatever you would feed your own son if he was tired.
HEAD COOK (after a beat): Yes, my lady.

---

## S#2 — Corridor. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Kiran walking beside her.

[DIALOGUE]
KIRAN: My lady. Halric is one of Sera's house.
ISOLDE: I know.
KIRAN: He stays?
ISOLDE: He stays. He has bowed. His daughter is in the keep nursery. He will not poison me.
KIRAN: ...
ISOLDE: You disagree.
KIRAN: I do not, my lady. I am surprised.
ISOLDE: By what.
KIRAN: That you decided yourself.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She does not answer.

[KEY CAMERA]
Slow tracking them walking.

---

## S#3 — Chamber. Evening.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Halric himself brings up the dinner tray. Vael does not look at the door. Sets it down on the chamber table.

[DIALOGUE]
HALRIC (bows): My lord. My lady. The cook sent something warm.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He leaves. Door closes.

Vael looks at the food. Looks at Isolde. Then at the food again.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: This is mine.
ISOLDE: It is yours.
VAEL: Who told the cook.
ISOLDE: I did.
VAEL: ...

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He sits. Eats. For the first time on screen, Vael is eating a meal she ordered for him.

She eats with him.

---

## S#4 — Bed. Late.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She is on his chest. His hand in her hair.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: You decided about the kitchen boys today.
ISOLDE: I did.
VAEL: I should have decided about them three weeks ago.
ISOLDE: You had other decisions.
VAEL: I had no decision more important than the food my woman puts in her mouth.
ISOLDE: That is why I decided.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He pulls her closer. Hand at her belly.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Tomorrow you will decide one more thing.
ISOLDE: What.
VAEL: Whatever you want. I will leave the day open for you.
ISOLDE: ...
VAEL: I am giving you the keep one decision at a time. By the time the child is here you will know how to run it.
ISOLDE: ...
VAEL: I will not be the only thing standing between her and the world.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She does not answer. Just holds him tighter.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held wide of the bed. Hearth low.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
Halric, in the kitchens at night, finishes wiping down a counter. The HEAD COOK comes in.

[DIALOGUE]
HEAD COOK: Did the lord eat.
HALRIC: He did.
HEAD COOK: All of it.
HALRIC: All of it.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The head cook nods once. Goes.

Halric stands a long beat in the empty kitchen. Looks at the door the dinner went up through.

Audience-only: he has been in the keep eleven years. The lord has not finished a hall meal in nine of them.

Hard Cut.
