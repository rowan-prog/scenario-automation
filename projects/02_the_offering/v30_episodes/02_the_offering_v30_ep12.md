# THE OFFERING — EP12 (v30)

## S#1 — Great Hall. Same Day. Mid-Afternoon.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The five elders return. Faster than expected. Their faces are grim.

[DIALOGUE]
ELDER ONE: The bond is real. The mate is human. The keep is yours.
VAEL: Continue.
ELDER ONE: But the human must be examined by our healers. And her lineage verified at the human court. We will escort her there ourselves.
VAEL: No.
ELDER TWO: It is not your decision, Lord Drakonis.
VAEL: It is. She does not leave this keep.
ELDER ONE: Then you are in defiance of the council.
VAEL: Then I am in defiance of the council.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Hall freezes.

---

## S#2 — Same Hall. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde steps forward beside Vael. The elders turn to look at her.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: I will answer the council myself. Since I am the one you are discussing.
ELDER ONE: Speak.
ISOLDE: I am the bastard daughter of King Aldric of Veine. My mother was a serving woman. I have no lineage to verify because there is no record of me. I was raised in a corner of the palace and sent here in chains. If you want my lineage confirmed, my father is two weeks' ride west. Write to him. He will tell you I am worthless. He has told everyone else.
ELDER ONE: ...
ISOLDE: But I am also carrying this keep's heir. So decide carefully whether you want to escort the heir's mother into the territory of a king who already tried to dispose of her once.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The five elders look at each other.

[DIALOGUE]
ELDER ONE: We will deliberate further.
ISOLDE: Deliberate fast. I am tired. And pregnant.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The elders walk back out. The hall watches them go.

---

## S#3 — Vael's Chamber. Late.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Chamber. Hearth low. He has not spoken for two hours. She has been writing.

She finishes. Folds the letter. Seals it with black wax — using his seal. He sees her do it. Does not stop her.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: To.
ISOLDE: My father.
VAEL: What does it say.
ISOLDE: That he should not send for me. That if he does, you burn his gate. That I will not write back unless he begins with an apology.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He almost smiles.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: He will not apologize.
ISOLDE: I know.
VAEL: Then why write.
ISOLDE: Because it will arrive on his desk in front of his court. He will have to read it. The court will see it. The court will know who I am now.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Beat. He takes her face in both hands. Long look.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: You will run this keep before I am dead.
ISOLDE: Do not be dead.
VAEL: Will not.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He kisses her. Different than before — no roughness tonight. Slow. Long. Something has shifted.

---

## S#4 — Same Chamber. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He carries her to the bed. Lays her down. Does not undress her.

He covers her with the black sheet. Sits on the bed edge. Hand at her face.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Tonight.
VAEL: No.
ISOLDE: Why not.
VAEL: Because they came back too fast today. Something is wrong. I will sleep at the door tonight.
ISOLDE: Vael.
VAEL: At the door tonight.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He stands. Walks to the chamber door. Pulls a chair over. Sits in it. Sword across his knees.

She watches him from the bed.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Vael.
VAEL: Sleep.
ISOLDE: Come back later.
VAEL: Maybe. Sleep first.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She does. He does not.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held wide of the chamber. Him at the door. Her on the bed.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
Outside, in the courtyard at night — Sera has returned. Alone. She arrives at the gate. She is carrying a small wooden box.

She does not ask permission. She walks straight to the eastern wing of the keep — to where the council elders are staying.

She knocks on their door. They open it.

Audience does not see what is in the box. The elders see. Their faces change.

Hard Cut.
