# THE OFFERING — EP10 (v30)

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Vael's Chamber. Three Days Later. Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Quiet. Too quiet. Sera has been gone three days. The keep is preparing for something Vael will not name.

Kiran at the chamber door. Vael at the desk reading reports.

[DIALOGUE]
KIRAN (low): My lord. The reports.
VAEL: Tell me.
KIRAN: Sera did not go home. She went east. To the old council.
VAEL: She told them.
KIRAN: She told them.
VAEL: All of it.
KIRAN: That the lord of Drakonis Keep has named a human as his bride. That the bond signature is on her. That she carries his heir. That the keep recognizes.
VAEL: How long.
KIRAN: Four days. Maybe five. They will fly.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael nods. Does not look up.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Then four days.
KIRAN: Yes, my lord.
VAEL: Tell the keep. Prepare. Bring the inner stair guards up to double. Close the lower gate from tonight.
KIRAN: My lord.
VAEL: One more thing. Send a rider west.
KIRAN: To Veine.
VAEL: To Veine. Tell King Aldric his daughter is the bride of the Lord of Drakonis Keep. She carries my heir. Any further attempt to reclaim her through any channel — gold, war, prayer — will be answered with my fire.
KIRAN: My lord.
VAEL: Go.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Kiran goes.

---

## S#2 — Same Chamber. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde at the window slit. She has heard most of it.

She walks to him at the desk. Stands beside him.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: The council is coming.
VAEL: In four days.
ISOLDE: For me.
VAEL: For you.
ISOLDE: To do what.
VAEL: Sit down.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She sits on the edge of the desk. Not in the chair. He turns the chair to face her. Stays seated.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Among dragons there is a word for the one we recognize. Not choose. Recognize. Body knows before mind knows. We call it mate. It is not a title. It is a fact. It happens once. It does not repeat.
ISOLDE: And.
VAEL: You are mine.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Long beat.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: When did you know.
VAEL: The moment I saw you.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She does not catch it yet. (The lie. Audience hears it. He saw her six months before.)

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: The day I walked into your hall.
VAEL: ... Yes.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She nods. Files it. Something does not sit right but she cannot find it yet.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Why did you hide it.
VAEL: Because when I name you publicly, you become a target.
ISOLDE: Your mother.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He goes still.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: How do you know that.
ISOLDE: Kiran did not tell me. The keep tells itself. I have been listening.
VAEL: She was named mate by my father. Killed in his hall by men who wanted to break him. I was six. I saw it.
ISOLDE: So you would never have named me.
VAEL: Not until I had killed every man who could reach you.
ISOLDE: And Sera forced your hand.
VAEL: Yes.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Beat. She slides off the desk. Walks to the bed. Sits on the edge.

He follows. Kneels in front of her — eye level. Puts his hands on her knees through the silk.

---

## S#3 — Same Chamber. Continuous.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: I have one more question.
VAEL: Ask.
ISOLDE: Tonight you said you knew me the day I walked into your hall.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: Did you know me before that.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: Vael.
VAEL: ... Ask me again in a week.
ISOLDE: Why a week.
VAEL: Because we are going to live through this week first. And I need you angry at me only after we have lived.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She looks at him a long time. Does not push. Does not let it go either.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: A week.
VAEL: A week.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She nods once. Lifts her hand. Puts it on the side of his face. Holds it there. He turns his face into her palm. Closes his eyes.

[KEY CAMERA]
Close: her palm on his cheek. His eyes closed.

---

## S#4 — Bed. Later.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He holds her. Hand on her belly. Hand at the back of her neck under her hair. He does not undress her tonight. He does not take her tonight.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Why not.
VAEL: Tonight I want my hand here and your hand on mine.
ISOLDE: That is all.
VAEL: That is everything.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She does not argue. Puts her hand over his on her belly. Lets it stay.

[CUTAWAY]
The hearth fire low.
Their two hands on her belly.
His palm — faint shimmer, audience-only.
Her face — calm, eyes open, thinking.
His face — eyes closed.

[VO]
ISOLDE (V.O., quiet): A week. He said a week. He did not lie about the council. He did not lie about his mother. There is one more thing he is not telling me. And it is the thing my body has been trying to tell me since the carriage stopped.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She closes her eyes.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
Outside the chamber window — audience only — the eastern sky has shifted. Five dark shapes are moving across the moon. Slow. Coming.

The council is in flight.

Hard Cut.
