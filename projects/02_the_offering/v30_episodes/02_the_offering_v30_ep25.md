# THE OFFERING — EP25 (v30) — THE NAME

## S#1 — Chamber. Morning. The Midwife.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A woman walks in with Kiran. Mid-fifties. Plain dark dress. Quiet eyes. Hair grey at the temples.

She does not approach the bed without being told. Stops at the door.

[DIALOGUE]
MIDWIFE: My lady.
ISOLDE: Your name.
MIDWIFE: Halren. Of South Cove. Of the house of Aurelle.
ISOLDE: My mother's house.
HALREN: Your mother's house.
ISOLDE: She lived with you.
HALREN: She lived in our care from twelve to seventeen. Then your father came.
ISOLDE: Did you know her.
HALREN: I bathed her three days before her wedding. I would have bathed you the same way if I had been called.
ISOLDE: You were not called.
HALREN: I was not.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Beat. Isolde studies her face. Looks for her mother in it. Does not find a direct resemblance — but something in the eyes.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Why now.
HALREN: Because we heard you live. And we heard whose hall you live in. And we know what they will try to do to your child if no one of your blood is in the keep.
ISOLDE: ...
HALREN: I am offering. You may refuse.
ISOLDE: I am not refusing.

---

## S#2 — Chamber. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Halren bows once. Goes back to the south wing.

Isolde sits on the bed edge. Vael at the windowsill.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Did she lie about anything.
VAEL: No.
ISOLDE: How do you know.
VAEL: I can hear it when people lie close to me. Like the heartbeat. Same place.
ISOLDE: When did you learn that.
VAEL: When I was small. After my mother. I had to know who I could keep near me.
ISOLDE: ...
VAEL: She did not lie.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde nods once.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: She bathed my mother.
VAEL: She bathed your mother.
ISOLDE: My mother was happy in South Cove.
VAEL: Until she went west.
ISOLDE: Until then.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She does not cry. Hand low on her belly.

---

## S#3 — Bath Chamber. Late.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Steam. The deep tub. Isolde in the water. Belly above. Hair pinned up.

Vael behind her in the tub this time. Both his palms flat on her belly. Water moving slow.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: I want her name.
VAEL: Whose.
ISOLDE: Our daughter. I want her name.
VAEL: All right.
ISOLDE: Mireille.
VAEL: Your mother.
ISOLDE: My mother.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: If you have one of your own, say it. We can pick between.
VAEL: ...
VAEL: ... Mireille.
ISOLDE: You did not even pause.
VAEL: I was not going to argue about your mother.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She closes her eyes. Tips her head back against his shoulder.

[CUTAWAY]
His scaled palms on her belly above the water.
Steam.
Her wet hair against his throat.
The shimmer at his palms — slow, constant.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: I love you.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
This time she hears it.

She does not open her eyes.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: I know.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: I will say it back when I have decided when.
VAEL: All right.

---

## S#4 — Chamber. Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Bed. Both of them. She is on his chest. He is awake. She is half-awake.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Mireille.
VAEL: Mireille.
ISOLDE: She will look like my mother.
VAEL: Or you.
ISOLDE: Or you.
VAEL: I would like her to look like you.
ISOLDE: I would like her to look like both.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He puts his hand on her belly. The child moves under his palm. He feels it. Eyes wet at the corners.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (very quiet): She moved.
ISOLDE: She has been moving for two weeks.
VAEL: I felt it.
ISOLDE: I know.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She kisses him. Slow. He closes his eyes.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held wide of the bed. The bird on the shelf in the background.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
SUPER, very small at the corner: MIREILLE — NAMED.

[END HOOK]
Outside the chamber window — eastern sky — a single small star above the ridge. Not a dragon. Just a star.

Hard Cut.
