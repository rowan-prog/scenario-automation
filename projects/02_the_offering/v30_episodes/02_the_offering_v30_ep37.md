# THE OFFERING — EP37 (v30) — THE NIGHT BEFORE

## S#1 — Chamber. Past Midnight.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She has waited an hour. Three more contractions — each one longer. She finally puts her hand on his chest. Shakes him gently.

He wakes instantly.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: It started.
VAEL: How long.
ISOLDE: An hour.
VAEL: An hour.
ISOLDE: I wanted you to sleep.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He sits up. Does not yell. Does not panic. Walks to the chamber door. Opens it.

Kiran outside — has not slept either.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: It started.
KIRAN: Halren.
VAEL: Bring her up. She enters when I open the door. Not before.
KIRAN: Yes, my lord.
VAEL: Four men in the corridor. Two on the balcony. One at the bath door. No one else above the lower stair from now on.
KIRAN: Yes, my lord.

---

## S#2 — Chamber. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He closes the door. Locks it. Walks back to the bed.

Isolde sits up. Another contraction. Longer this time. He at her side, hand at her back.

She breathes through it.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Twelve count.
VAEL: Twelve.
ISOLDE: The fourth.
VAEL: Within an hour.
ISOLDE: Within an hour.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He carries her — does not let her walk — to the wide bed platform. Sets her down on her side. Puts the cold linen against her hairline. Puts his scaled hand at her sternum. Puts the other under her belly.

He waits.

She has another contraction. He breathes with her through it.

---

## S#3 — Chamber. A Knock.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Soft knock. Kiran.

[DIALOGUE]
KIRAN (off): My lord. The midwife.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael does not lift his hand from her sternum.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (without looking at the door): Walk her in. Walk yourself out. Stand with your back against the door. Don't let anyone past you. Not the council. Not the keep. Not the ridge.
KIRAN (off): Yes, my lord.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The door opens. Halren — clean dress, hands washed, no jewelry — steps in slow. Does not look at the bed. Walks to the foot. Stands with her hands clasped and eyes lowered.

The door closes behind her. Kiran's back against it from the outside.

---

## S#4 — Chamber. Setting Up.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Halren begins. Spreads clean linen at the foot of the bed. Lays out small clean instruments — none of them looking like weapons.

She does not approach Isolde without being told. Stays at the foot.

Isolde has another contraction. Vael breathes with her.

[DIALOGUE]
HALREN: My lady. The body is opening. Hours yet. Maybe four.
VAEL: Four.
HALREN: Maybe more.
VAEL: Stay at the foot. You do not put your hand on her without my word.
HALREN: Yes, my lord.
VAEL: My word.
HALREN: My lord.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He puts his face against Isolde's wet hairline. She breathes against his ear.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held wide of the chamber. Hearth. Two oil lamps. The two of them in the bed. Halren at the foot.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
Outside the keep — the eastern ridge. Twenty dragons. All twenty pairs of eyes open. Watching. Not the east tonight. The chamber light.

They will not sleep tonight.

Hard Cut.
