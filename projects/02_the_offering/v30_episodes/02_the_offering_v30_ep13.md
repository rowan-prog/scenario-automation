# THE OFFERING — EP13 (v30)

## S#1 — Vael's Chamber. Pre-Dawn.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Chamber. Vael asleep in the chair against the door — sword still on his knees. Isolde asleep on the bed.

A small sound — wrong sound — at the balcony door.

The balcony door opens. Slowly. A figure slips in. Black cloth from head to wrist. Curved knife.

Isolde wakes — her body knows before her eyes do. She sits up.

The figure moves for her. Fast.

At the same instant — Vael wakes in the chair. Crosses the chamber in two strides. Catches the assassin's wrist mid-thrust. Twists. Bone snaps. Knife drops.

Vael grabs the assassin by the throat. Lifts him off the floor.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the chamber. Close: bone snap. Close: Vael's eye — full red ring.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Who paid.
ASSASSIN (choking): Council. Eastern faction. They voted while you slept.
VAEL: Names.
ASSASSIN: I — I don't — they paid through —

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael's hand closes. Crack. He drops the body. Does not look at it.

He turns to Isolde.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: I am not hurt.
VAEL: I see that.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He crosses to her. Puts his hand on her stomach through her nightdress. Long beat. Closes his eyes.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Pack. We are moving.
ISOLDE: Where.
VAEL: The inner keep.
ISOLDE: There is a deeper keep.
VAEL: There is.
ISOLDE: How long has it been ready.
VAEL: Since the day you arrived.

## S#2 — Inner Keep Chamber. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A chamber deeper than the surface. Stone walls thicker. One door, iron. One window slit, narrow. Smaller bed. Smaller hearth.

Isolde walks the perimeter. Touches the stone. It is warm.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: You built this for me.
VAEL: I built this for whoever I would not let them have. It was empty until last week.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She turns. Walks to him. Puts her hand on his chest.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: I want to ask you something. Don't lie.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: The first time you saw me. Was it the day I walked into your hall.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Long beat. He does not answer.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Ask me again in a week.
ISOLDE: Why a week.
VAEL: Because we are going to live through this week first.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She does not push. Files it. Will ask again.

---

## S#3 — Inner Chamber. Bed.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Late. She is the one who comes to him this time. Sits on the bed edge. Then on his lap. Then over him.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Tonight.
VAEL: Tonight.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She straddles him. Pulls her nightdress over her head. Drops it on the floor.

His hands at her hips. Not gripping. Just there.

She bends. Kisses him. Slow. Sets the pace. He follows.

[CUTAWAY]
Her hands flat on his chest.
His scaled hands at her hips — open, not closing.
Her hair falling forward over her face.
His chest under her palm — heartbeat fast.
Her gasp — half-word, his name.
His face — eyes wet at the corners. Not tears. Almost.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
**Audience-only:** her position above him — same position from the cold open. He sees it. He does not stop. He almost says her name in the way he said it that night. Bites it back.

After. She on his chest. He stroking her hair.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (sleepy): One day this week. You will tell me.
VAEL: One day this week.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She sleeps. He stays awake. Hand on her belly.

[KEY CAMERA]
Slow push on his hand on her belly. The orange shimmer is constant now.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
Outside the inner chamber door, Kiran on guard. He hears something down the outer corridor. Walks to the outer chamber. Finds a small wooden box at the threshold. Sera's mark on it.

Kiran does not open it. Picks it up. Carries it away.

Audience-only flash of what is inside as Kiran tilts it: a single silver mask. Half. Broken at the cheekbone.

Hard Cut.
