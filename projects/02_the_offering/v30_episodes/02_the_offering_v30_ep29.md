# THE OFFERING — EP29 (v30) — THE LETTER FROM A DYING MAN

## S#1 — Chamber. Morning. Kiran Brings the Letter.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Kiran walks in. Letter in hand. Aldric's personal seal — not Veine kingdom seal.

[DIALOGUE]
KIRAN: My lady. From your father.
ISOLDE: ...
KIRAN: The rider says he is dying. The court healer gives him two weeks. Maybe three.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde takes the letter. Does not open it for a long beat. Vael at the desk. Watches.

She breaks the seal. Reads.

[INSERT — page audience-visible:]
> Daughter.
> I am dying. The healer is sure.
> You are not required to come.
> I am writing because I will not be on this side of the wall to apologize when it is too late.
> If you choose not to come, I will accept that. I will leave instructions that you inherit nothing, so that no one tries to drag you home for it.
> If you choose to come, you can stand at the door and refuse the room.
> I am sorry for fifteen years.
> Your father.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She reads it twice. Folds it. Sets it on the desk.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: He apologized.
VAEL: He did.
ISOLDE: Sixteen years late.
VAEL: Yes.

---

## S#2 — Chamber. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She walks to the windowsill. Touches the wooden bird. Stands there a long beat.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: I have to think.
VAEL: Think.
ISOLDE: You will tell me not to go.
VAEL: I will not tell you anything yet. Think first.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He gets up. Walks out of the chamber. Leaves her with it.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held wide of her at the window.

---

## S#3 — Chamber. Late Afternoon.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She is on the bed now. He has come back in.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: I will go.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: I know what you want to say.
VAEL: I will say it once. Then I will stop.
ISOLDE: Say it.
VAEL: You are seven months pregnant. The road is two weeks. The court is full of the woman I burned in our yard. The chance of you not coming back is real.
ISOLDE: ...
VAEL: I have said it.
ISOLDE: All right. Now listen.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: He is the only one in that court who is asking for an apology and not a body. If I do not go and he dies, then my mother died and her killer died and her widower died and none of them ever said anything to me about it directly. I will spend the rest of my life carrying that silence.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: I will go.
VAEL: Then I will go.
ISOLDE: I want you to.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He sits beside her on the bed. Hand at her belly.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: When.
ISOLDE: In three days. We are slow on the road. We arrive in two weeks.
VAEL: Two weeks is too much travel for a woman seven months in.
ISOLDE: Then we go by dragon. As far as you can carry me without it hurting the child.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: You can carry me.
VAEL: I can.

---

## S#4 — Chamber. Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Bed. She is asleep against his chest. He is awake.

He looks at the ceiling. Hand on her belly. The child kicks once. He feels it.

[VO]
VAEL (V.O., audience-only): The road west is the road that nearly killed her once. The road west is the road that ends in her father's hall. The road west is the road she has decided to walk. I will not stop her. I will walk it beside her. And if the man in that hall says one wrong word, I will burn what is left of him before he finishes the sentence.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He closes his eyes. Does not sleep.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held wide of the bed.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
In Veine — King Aldric in his bed. Pale. Thin. He has not slept either. He is holding a small object in his hand: a piece of dried wildflower. Audience does not know what it means yet. But Aldric does.

He closes his hand around it.

Hard Cut.
