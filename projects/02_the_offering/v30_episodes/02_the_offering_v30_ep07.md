# THE OFFERING — EP07 (v30)

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Upper Terrace. Morning.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Bright. Kiran walking the upper terrace with Isolde. She is in a new dress now — black silk, fitted, full sleeves, hair pinned. The bruises on her wrist are fading.

Vael is not here. Audience knows from Kiran's careful eyes that Vael has assigned this walk.

[DIALOGUE]
KIRAN: My lady. The lord asks that you remain inside the upper levels today.
ISOLDE: Because of last night.
KIRAN: Because of last night.
ISOLDE: Did the assassin name his employer.
KIRAN: He was killed before he could.
ISOLDE: By you.
KIRAN: No.
ISOLDE: Then by who.
KIRAN: ... I am not authorized to say.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She stops walking. Turns to him. Studies his face.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Kiran.
KIRAN: My lady.
ISOLDE: Where was the lord six months ago.
KIRAN (long beat): ... I am not authorized to say.
ISOLDE: Was he looking for someone.
KIRAN: I am not authorized.
ISOLDE: That is not a no.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Kiran looks at the floor. Does not lie. Does not answer.

She files it.

[KEY CAMERA]
Slow push on Isolde's face. Recognition starting.

---

## S#2 — Same Terrace. A Beat Later.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She walks the rail. Hand on the stone. Looking east.

[VO]
ISOLDE (V.O.): The lord of this keep was looking for someone six months ago. The lord of this keep claimed me as his bride the day I walked through the gate. He has not slept since I arrived. His hand glows in the dark. His heart goes faster than a man's.

[VO]
ISOLDE (V.O.): My body keeps trying to tell me something my mind has not let it say.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She stops walking. Hand low on her stomach. Audience-only: she does not know what she is doing. Her body does.

She pulls her hand back. Looks at her own hand. Frowns.

---

## S#3 — Drakonis Keep. Lower Receiving Hall. Mid-Afternoon.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A smaller hall than the great one. Vael at the head. Kiran behind him. The three keep lords seated. Isolde at his right — her seat now permanent.

Across the table: a VEINE ENVOY. Different from the one in EP01. Older. Grey beard. The king's close hand.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the hall. Cut to Isolde's hand on the table.

[DIALOGUE]
ENVOY: My lord Drakonis. King Aldric of Veine sends his regards.
VAEL: Regards are for guests.
ENVOY: The court has reviewed the original contract. The woman delivered to your keep is not the woman named in the original treaty. There is concern that the offering was — irregular.
VAEL: Irregular.
ENVOY: She is a servant. The mirror confirms it. Veine will replace her within the month with a true bride of suitable lineage.
VAEL: A servant.
ENVOY: I am sorry, my lord. Veine regrets the confusion.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael turns his head slowly. Looks at Isolde.

She is already standing.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (to the envoy, level): Look at me.
ENVOY: My lady —
ISOLDE: Look at me.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The envoy looks up.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: You came to take a servant back. Tell my father this. The servant is wearing his daughter's mark on her wrist. The servant has been named bride by the lord of this keep. The servant is not going home. Tell my father that whatever girl he sends next will be turned around at this gate without leaving her carriage. Tell my father that if I see his envoy in this keep again, my lord will not be the one who answers him.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael — beside her — does not move. He is watching her.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (quiet, only for the envoy): That's what she says.
ENVOY: My lord, the king —
VAEL: You heard her. You repeat it. Word for word.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael takes Isolde's hand on the table. Turns it over. Puts his thumb on the inside of her wrist — over the fading bruise. He kisses the bruise. Once. In front of the envoy.

Puts her hand back down.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Go.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The envoy is walked out. As he passes Isolde, his shoulder twitches. He drops a small folded square of grey parchment at her feet. Does not look at her. Black-thumb seal on the parchment.

Isolde puts her foot over the parchment before Kiran or Vael sees.

[KEY CAMERA]
Close on her foot covering the parchment.

## S#4 — Vael's Chamber. Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Chamber. Vael at the desk. Isolde at the window slit. The parchment is in her hand now. She has read it.

Two words on it: ALDRIC KNEW.

Below: a black-thumb seal.

She does not show it to Vael. Folds it. Tucks it inside her sleeve.

She turns.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: I want to ask you something tonight. Don't lie.
VAEL (turns from the desk): I won't.
ISOLDE: Take off the mask.
VAEL: What.
ISOLDE: You have one. I do not know what kind it is. Take it off.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He goes very still. Long beat.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: I am not wearing a mask.
ISOLDE: Yes you are.
VAEL: Isolde.
ISOLDE: Take it off. Or I get out of this bed. And I do not come back to it.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He does not move. Cannot — not yet. The council is still in the air. Sera is still in the keep. The first assassin is dead, but a second is on the road.

He cannot tell her tonight.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Not tonight.
ISOLDE: Then catch up.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She walks to the chamber door. Opens it. Walks out. She is wearing only the black sheet from the bed — wrapped around her — and his robe over it. Hair down.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of her walking out the door. Cut to Vael at the desk — stands too late.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
She walks the corridor. Half the keep sees her. Sera sees her from a side hall. Dragons stop and watch. She walks straight. Does not run. Does not look at any of them.

A dragon guard at the corridor end moves to stop her.

Behind her — Vael's voice, low, full force:

VAEL (off, from down the corridor): Touch her and I take the hand.

The guard freezes mid-step.

Isolde keeps walking.

Hard Cut.
