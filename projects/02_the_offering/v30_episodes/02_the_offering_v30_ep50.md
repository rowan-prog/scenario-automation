## S#1 — Inner Garden. One Year Later. Morning.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Mireille — three years old now. Walking. Talking. Standing in the grass at the small pool. Dropping pebbles into the water one at a time. Counting.

[DIALOGUE]
MIREILLE: One. Two. Three. Four. Five.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde on the bench. Soft Drakonis robe. Vael beside her. Sleeves rolled. Scales soft. He has slept full nights for over a year. He looks rested.

Audience-only: Isolde's hand is low on her belly. Subtle. She is sitting differently than the year before.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the small garden in morning light.

---

## S#2 — Garden. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Mireille walks across the garden — steady, no wobble — to her father's knee. Holds out a wet pebble in her small hand. Two-handed grip.

[DIALOGUE]
MIREILLE: Da.
VAEL: Yes.
MIREILLE: Pebble.
VAEL: Pebble.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He takes it. Turns it once. Sets it on the bench beside Isolde — careful, as if the pebble matters.

Mireille lifts her arms. He lifts her up. Sits her on his thigh. Scaled palm flat across her small back. Wide hand on smaller body.

She leans into his palm.

## S#3 — Garden. Continuous.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: She looks like you.
VAEL: She has your eyes.
ISOLDE: She has your dark hair.
VAEL: She has your mouth.
ISOLDE: She has your jaw.
VAEL: She has both of us.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Mireille reaches up. Touches Vael's jaw. Then turns and reaches across to touch Isolde's jaw too.

[DIALOGUE]
MIREILLE: Same.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The parents go still.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: She is right.
VAEL: She is right.

## S#4 — Garden. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Mireille slides off Vael's lap. Goes back to the pool. Drops more pebbles. Counts again.

Vael turns his face to Isolde. Kisses her — slow, no teeth.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: My wife.
ISOLDE: Your wife.
VAEL: My mate.
ISOLDE: Your mate.
VAEL: My queen.
ISOLDE: Your queen.
VAEL: Mother of mine.
ISOLDE: Yours.
VAEL: I love you.
ISOLDE: I love you.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He looks at her hand on her belly. Long beat.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Already.
ISOLDE: Already.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He smiles. The smile we have not seen this fully. He puts his hand over hers on her belly.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held wide of the four of them. Three plus one unseen. Mireille at the pool. Isolde on the bench. Vael beside her. Hand on her belly.

Slow tilt up. Past the walls of the inner garden. Past the keep. Past the eastern ridge.

In the morning light, far above — two dragon silhouettes drift across the sky. Not approaching. Not leaving. Just flying. They are the ridge waking up to the new day. They turn east together.

The shot holds. The garden below stays small. The keep stays small. The world stays small around them.
