# THE OFFERING — EP04 (v30)

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Great Hall. Late Morning.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Court convened. The three old dragon-blood lords of the keep — formal black — along one side. Sera's house at the back of the hall, three of them already packing. Sera herself at the side, dressed for departure she has not made yet.

Vael at the head of the long table. Kiran at his right.

Isolde walks in. Black silk dress — tailored overnight, no train, sleeves to the wrist. Hair pinned. Her right wrist still showing the bruise from EP03 — does not cover it.

She stops at the foot of the table.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the hall. Cut to her wrist — bruise visible. Cut to Vael's hand on the chair arm — knuckles white.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: At my chair. Not behind it.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She walks the length of the table. Sits beside him. The three lords watch. The hall watches.

A YOUNG DRAGON — Sera's house — moves toward Isolde from the side, as if to "escort" her to a lower seat. His hand reaches for her shoulder.

He never touches her.

Vael is across the hall — and then he is not across the hall. He is at the young dragon's side. Audience does not see him cross.

He catches the young dragon's wrist mid-air. Twists. Bone snaps. The young dragon screams. Vael keeps the wrist. Walks the dragon backward two steps. Throws him through the side wall of the hall. Stone breaks. The young dragon does not get up.

Hall silent.

[KEY CAMERA]
Close on the bone snap. Wide on the wall breaking. Close on Vael's eye — full red ring.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (does not raise his voice, to Isolde): Did he touch you anywhere else.
ISOLDE: No.
VAEL: Tell me if he did.
ISOLDE: I said no.
VAEL (to the hall): Anyone else want to put hands on her.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Silence. Even Sera does not move.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Sit. Beside me.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He walks back to his chair. Sits. Puts his palm flat on the back of Isolde's chair. Not on her. On the chair.

Marks the territory.

## S#2 — Same Hall. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Servants drag the young dragon out. The wall has a hole in it where his body went through.

The oldest of the three keep-lords stands.

[DIALOGUE]
OLDEST LORD: My lord. May we speak.
VAEL: Speak.
OLDEST LORD: The lady at your right. Is she.
VAEL: My bride.
OLDEST LORD: We heard.
VAEL: Heard what.
OLDEST LORD: She is the failed bridal mirror from Veine.
VAEL: She is the woman at my right hand. The keep recognizes her or it does not. Decide.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The three lords exchange one look. The oldest bows. The other two bow after him.

[DIALOGUE]
OLDEST LORD: The keep recognizes.

VAEL: Good. Sit down.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The three lords sit. Sera at the side has not bowed. She does not bow. Vael notices but does not act.

Isolde — for the first time — speaks to the hall.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Lord Drakonis.
VAEL (turning his head): My lady.
ISOLDE: My wrist will heal. The boy in the wall will not heal as fast.
VAEL: He will not heal at all.
ISOLDE: I would like that fact stated to his house.
VAEL: His house is at the back of this hall. They have heard.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
At the back — Sera's house. Two of them already on their knees. Sera still standing.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (does not look at Sera, looks at the lords): My lords. I would like one thing made clear to this hall today. If anyone in this keep touches me without my consent — regardless of intent, regardless of rank, regardless of which house they are from — my lord will not consult me before he kills them. Do not test this. He has shown you twice now what testing it looks like. Once with Sera's hand last night. Once with that wall just now.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Sera's face goes white. Vael — sitting at her right — turns his head slowly to look at Isolde. Not anger. Something else.

[DIALOGUE]
OLDEST LORD (after a beat): Understood, my lady.

ISOLDE: Thank you.

[KEY CAMERA]
Slow push around the table. Cut to Sera at the back — eyes wet, jaw set.

## S#3 — Corridor. Later.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Sera walking fast down a side corridor. Vael behind her. She stops only when he says her name.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Sera.
SERA (turning, hot): Twelve years.
VAEL: I know how long.
SERA: You said you would not marry until.
VAEL: Until you grew into yourself. You did not. I did.
SERA: She is a human.
VAEL: She is my bride.
SERA: She is *used*.
VAEL: Say that word again in my hall and I will put your house through the same wall as the boy. Say it where she hears it and I will start with your tongue.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Sera does not speak.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Your three closest are leaving the keep by sundown. You stay. You answer to me. Not to your house. Not to your father. Me.
SERA: My lord —
VAEL: Sera. I am giving you a chance to be useful. The next chance will not be a chance.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He turns. Walks away.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held: Sera in the corridor. Her hand at her own throat where his mark should have been — and never was.

## S#4 — Drakonis Keep. East Balcony. Sunset.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A small high balcony off the upper wing. Vael alone. Stone railing. Eastern wasteland below, lit gold.

He stands at the rail. Does not move for a long beat.

[FLASHBACK]
A great hall — same architecture, decades earlier. CHILD VAEL — six years old — half-hiding behind a stone pillar. A WOMAN on the floor of the hall, gold gown, blood on the floor around her. Two dragon men with swords standing over her. She is not moving. One of the men turns his head — sees the child. The flashback holds for three seconds.

Cut back.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Adult Vael on the balcony. Eyes still on the wasteland.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (one word, to no one): Quiet.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He does not move for a long time.

Behind him, the balcony door opens. Isolde steps out. Does not speak. Walks to the rail. Stands beside him. Not touching.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: I heard what you said to Sera in the corridor.
VAEL: I know.
ISOLDE: You knew I would hear.
VAEL: Yes.
ISOLDE: Why.
VAEL: Because you should know what kind of man stands beside you in that hall.
ISOLDE: I know what kind. I'm not afraid.
VAEL: You should be a little.
ISOLDE: I survived eight years of a court that called me filth. A man who throws a boy through a wall for my wrist is not the most dangerous person I've been in a room with.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He turns his head and looks at her. She does not turn to him.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Who was.
ISOLDE (still looking east): My father's wife.
VAEL: Tell me about her.
ISOLDE: Another night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He nods once. Does not push.

[KEY CAMERA]
Slow tracking around the two of them at the rail. Wide of the wasteland and the keep. Push to her hand on the rail — bruise visible.

He reaches over slow. Covers her bruised wrist with his palm. Does not grip. Just covers.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
Across the courtyard below, Sera's three closest house members ride out the gate. Sera herself watches from a lower window. Does not move.

In Vael's chamber — empty — the small wooden bird on the desk has been moved. By half an inch. Audience-only: someone has been inside the chamber while everyone was at the hall.

Hard Cut.
