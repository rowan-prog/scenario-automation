# THE OFFERING — EP40 (v30) — THE RIDGE WAKES

## S#1 — Chamber. Just After Birth.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The three of them on the bed. Mireille on Isolde's chest. Vael on his side beside them. Hand under the child's small body, palm flat across the bundle.

Quiet. Hearth low. Light through the slit window — first dawn.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (very quiet): She is small.
ISOLDE: She is large for a first.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: Halren said so.
VAEL: I will believe Halren.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Mireille opens her eyes. Looks. Closes them.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: She opens her eyes for half a beat at a time.
ISOLDE: That is what they do.

---

## S#2 — Chamber. An Hour Later.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde asleep. The child against her chest. Vael awake. His scaled fingertip — gentle — at the child's small heel. Touching it. Pulling back. Touching it again.

He has done this for ten minutes.

[VO]
VAEL (V.O., audience-only): I have killed a hundred and twelve men in my life. I have transformed in front of the eastern ridge. I have set fire to iron. I am afraid to put my full hand on this one small foot.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He puts his palm flat on her back through the linen. Wide hand on small body. She does not wake.

He stays there.

---

## S#3 — Chamber. Mid-Morning.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Kiran knocks. Quiet.

[DIALOGUE]
KIRAN (off, low): My lord.
VAEL (does not get up): Speak.
KIRAN: The eastern ridge. Every dragon. Head up. Twenty.
VAEL: For her.
KIRAN: For her.
VAEL: Good.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He goes back to watching the child.

Outside, audience-only: the ridge. Twenty dragons. Heads lifted. All twenty pairs of red-ringed eyes open. They are not roaring. They are just looking. Toward the keep.

Then — one by one — they bow their heads. Toward the chamber light.

---

## S#4 — Chamber. Late Morning.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde wakes. Sees Vael watching the child. Smiles — tired, real.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: How long.
VAEL: An hour.
ISOLDE: She did not cry.
VAEL: She did not. She slept.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He kisses Isolde's forehead. Then — careful — kisses the top of the child's head.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: I love you both.
ISOLDE: I love you back.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He puts his hand on Isolde's stomach — flat where Mireille used to be. Stays there.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held wide of the bed. Slow push. Dawn light.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
Outside — far south. Sera on the road. She has been riding for ten days back from the south. She stops her horse at a crossroad. Has felt — through some old dragon thread — that the child is born.

She turns her horse east. Toward the keep.

She is coming to swear.

Hard Cut.
