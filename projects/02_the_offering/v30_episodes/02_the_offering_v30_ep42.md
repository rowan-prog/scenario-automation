# THE OFFERING — EP42 (v30) — ONE WEEK LATER

## S#1 — Chamber. Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Mireille one week old. Asleep in the iron-bound basket beside the bed.

Isolde on the wide platform in a clean robe. Body softening back to itself but not the same body. Hair down. Bath earlier.

Vael at the edge of the bed. Bare chest. Has not undressed for her in nine weeks.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: You have not slept since she came.
VAEL: I slept two hours yesterday.
ISOLDE: While I was awake.
VAEL: Yes.
ISOLDE: That doesn't count.
VAEL: It counts.

---

## S#2 — Bed. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She lifts her hand. Puts it on his jaw. Turns his face away from the basket toward her.

His eyes don't want to leave the basket. They do.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Look at me.
VAEL: I am.
ISOLDE: She is asleep three feet from you. The basket is locked. The door is locked. The balcony is locked. Kiran is awake. The four corridor men are awake. The two balcony men are awake. The twenty dragons on the ridge are awake. Everyone in the keep above the lower stair is awake except us.
VAEL: I know.
ISOLDE: Look at me.
VAEL: I am.

---

## S#3 — Bed. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She slides her hand down from his jaw to his throat. To his chest. Palm flat on his sternum. Heart fast.

She moves her hand down. To his belly. Below that.

He breathes in once, sharp.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: It has been a week.
VAEL: I know.
ISOLDE: A week and you have not touched me except where the child can be.
VAEL: Halren said wait.
ISOLDE: Halren is in the south wing.
VAEL: Halren said wait.
ISOLDE: It has been a week.
VAEL: It has been a week.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He turns onto his side. Palm flat on her belly through the robe. Other hand at the back of her neck under her hair.

He brings his face to hers. Kisses her — slow, the slowest kiss in the run of the story. No bite. Stays at her mouth a long count.

When he pulls back, his eyes are dark, not red.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Not tonight.
ISOLDE: When.
VAEL: Soon. Not tonight.
ISOLDE: Why not.
VAEL: Tonight I just want to look at you. Child asleep three feet away. You alive. Eating. Whole. I cannot do anything more tonight. Tomorrow.
ISOLDE: Tomorrow.

---

## S#4 — Bed. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He stays on his side. Hand on her belly. Other hand on the back of her neck. He does not let go for the rest of the night.

In the basket, the child sleeps.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held wide. Slow push.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
Just before dawn — the child stirs. Isolde reaches for the basket without sitting up. Vael lifts the child out for her. Sets her against Isolde's chest.

The child quiets at once.

Hard Cut.
