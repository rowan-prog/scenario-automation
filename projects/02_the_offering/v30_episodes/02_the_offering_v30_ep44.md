# THE OFFERING — EP44 (v30) — INNER GARDEN

## S#1 — Inner Garden. One Month Later. Day.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A small inner garden — walled, accessible only from the chamber level. Vael had it built during the pregnancy. Black stone walls, two benches, a small pool.

Isolde walking slow with Mireille — five weeks old — in her arms. Soft daily Drakonis robe. Hair down.

Vael behind her three paces, hand near her elbow but not on it. First time he has let her walk without holding her arm since the bath chamber.

[VO]
ISOLDE (V.O.): Five weeks ago I was on that bed thinking about which of the next sentences would be my last. This morning the sun is on my face for the first time since November. The smallest thing.

---

## S#2 — Bench. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She sits. Slow. Mireille against her shoulder, sleeping. Vael sits beside her — not behind. Arm around her shoulders. Other hand on the small of her back.

He puts his other hand under the child's small bare foot. Covers it with his palm.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: The keep stays closed.
VAEL: It stays closed.
ISOLDE: Sera.
VAEL: She is on the road. Three days out.
ISOLDE: Adeline.
VAEL: Veine. Quiet.
ISOLDE: ...

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She turns her head against his shoulder.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: What will we tell her.
VAEL: About what.
ISOLDE: How she got here.
VAEL (after a beat): That her mother was sent to her father in a chained wagon. That her father took the chain off. That her mother stayed. That her father has never let her mother be looked at by anyone since.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She closes her eyes against the sun.

---

## S#3 — Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Mireille wakes. Grey-green eyes open. Looks at Vael. He lifts his palm off her foot. Covers her small body through the linen — palm flat across the bundle.

She closes her fist around his finger. Hard. Holds.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: She has a grip.
VAEL: She does.
ISOLDE: She will be terrifying.
VAEL: Good.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He almost smiles. Fourth time on screen.

---

## S#4 — Garden. Late Afternoon.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
They stay an hour. He does not push her back inside.

When they finally walk back to the chamber, she leans against him. He carries her the last three paces.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: I could walk those.
VAEL: I wanted to.
ISOLDE: ...
VAEL: It's not weakness when I want to.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She does not answer. Closes her eyes against his chest.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held wide of him carrying her and the child back into the keep.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
Sera on the road. Two days out now. She has slowed her horse. She is thinking — for the first time — about what she will say.

She has not seen Vael in nine months. She has not seen Isolde with the child.

She does not know how to swear. She is going to have to learn.

Hard Cut.
