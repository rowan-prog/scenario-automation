# THE OFFERING — EP34 (v30) — EIGHT MONTHS

## S#1 — Chamber. Morning. A Week Home.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Eight months. Belly very large. She walks the chamber slow. Ankles swollen. Hair down.

Vael in the chair beside the bed. He has not been in the great hall in three days. He runs the keep from the chamber.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: You will rule this keep from here forever if I let you.
VAEL: I would.
ISOLDE: Go to the hall today. The house heads have not seen you in three days.
VAEL: They have seen me.
ISOLDE: From outside the chamber door. Not in the hall.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: Go. Two hours. Take Kiran. Come back.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He stands. Kisses her forehead. Goes.

[VO]
ISOLDE (V.O.): He is more afraid than I am. He should not be more afraid than I am.

---

## S#2 — Hall. Mid-Day.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael in the great hall with three house heads. Discussing — quietly — keep watch rotation. He keeps looking at the inner door that leads back to her.

Kiran beside him.

[DIALOGUE]
HOUSE HEAD: My lord. The east watch.
VAEL: ...
HOUSE HEAD: My lord.
VAEL: Continue.
KIRAN (very low, just for Vael): My lord, she is fine. Halric just brought her tea.
VAEL: ...
KIRAN: The hall, my lord.
VAEL: Right. East watch. Doubled until the child is born. Triple on full moons. Renar wrote me one line and the line was watch the east.

---

## S#3 — Bath Chamber. Late Afternoon.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Steam. Isolde in the tub. Halren the midwife at the side, hands clean.

Halren is not bathing her. Just sitting. The two women have begun to talk.

[DIALOGUE]
HALREN: She comes early.
ISOLDE: How early.
HALREN: Within ten days. Maybe seven.
ISOLDE: ...
HALREN: I have seen this carry pattern before. The bone of your hip. The way she rides high. She is ready.
ISOLDE: He is not ready.
HALREN: He will be ready when she is here. Until then he will not be ready. That is how the dragons are.
ISOLDE: My mother. Was she ready.
HALREN: For you. Yes. She was the most ready woman I have seen carry. She loved you before she met you.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde closes her eyes. Hand on her belly.

---

## S#4 — Chamber. Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Bed. Vael back. Holds her. Hand on her belly.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Halren said ten days. Maybe seven.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: I am ready.
VAEL: I am not.
ISOLDE: I know.
VAEL: I am not.
ISOLDE: She is yours. She is mine. Halren is in the keep. Kiran is at the door. The east is being watched. There is nothing more you can do tonight than hold me.
VAEL: Then I will hold you.
ISOLDE: All night.
VAEL: All night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He kisses her forehead. Closes his eyes. Does not sleep.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held wide of the bed.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
Outside the chamber window — eastern ridge. Twenty dragons in a line. All twenty pairs of eyes open. Not watching the keep. Watching east. Where Renar said to watch.

Hard Cut.
