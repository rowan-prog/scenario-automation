# THE OFFERING — EP49 (v30) — TWO YEARS

## S#1 — Chamber. Night. Two Years After Mireille's Birth.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Mireille — two years — asleep in a small bed against the wall. She has her own bed now. Her hair is darker. Her grip is famous.

Isolde on the wide bed. Vael behind her. Hand on her stomach. Flat.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: You said two years.
VAEL: I did.
ISOLDE: It is two years.
VAEL: It is.
ISOLDE: I want another.
VAEL: I want to give you another.
ISOLDE: Then ask the midwife.
VAEL: I will tomorrow.
ISOLDE: Tonight first.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: Tonight first.

## S#2 — Bed. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He turns onto her. Slow. Kisses her — open mouth, long.

[CUTAWAY]
His hand at her ribs.
Her hand at his back.
His scaled forearm under her shoulder, lifting.
The basket gone — she does not have to listen for it tonight.
Her mouth on his throat.

The sex is the longest of the run. He has been waiting two years.

After. Both. Breathing.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: If it takes.
ISOLDE: If it takes.
VAEL: I will sit at your door for nine months again.
ISOLDE: You will sit beside me. Not at the door.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: We do this together this time.
VAEL: Together.

---

## S#3 — Chamber. Mid-Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Mireille wakes. Cries — small, not loud. She has had a dream.

Isolde gets up. Walks across to the small bed. Picks her up. Carries her back to the wide bed.

Sets her between herself and Vael.

Mireille closes her eyes against her father's shoulder. Sleeps again at once.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Are you watching the room.
VAEL: I am watching the bed.
ISOLDE: That is a smaller room.
VAEL: That is the only room.

---

## S#4 — Chamber. Pre-Dawn.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
All three asleep. Vael, Isolde, Mireille between them.

Outside the slit window — the eastern ridge. Twenty dragons. Resting.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held wide. Slow push.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
The keep is quiet. The world is quiet. The night is quiet.
