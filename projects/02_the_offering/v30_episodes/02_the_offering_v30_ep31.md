# THE OFFERING — EP31 (v30) — THE DEATHBED

## S#1 — Veine Palace. Inner Corridor. Continuous from EP30.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Adeline walks Isolde and Vael through the long palace corridor toward the king's chambers. Quiet. The palace was loud yesterday. It is quiet today.

Servants in the corridor lower their heads as Isolde passes.

[DIALOGUE]
ADELINE: He has not eaten in three days.
ISOLDE: He is conscious.
ADELINE: For now.
ISOLDE: Your mother.
ADELINE: ...
ISOLDE: Where.
ADELINE: She is not in the palace. He sent her away the night he received your letter from Drakonis. She is at our north estate.
ISOLDE: She will not return.
ADELINE: Not while he lives. After — she may try.
ISOLDE: ...
ADELINE: I have already spoken to the court about her not returning.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Beat. Isolde looks at Adeline.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: You are saying that now to me. Have you said it to the court.
ADELINE: I told the council yesterday. The herald has it.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She is not asking for credit. She is reporting. Isolde files it.

---

## S#2 — King's Chamber. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The chamber is dim. Curtains half-drawn. King Aldric on the bed. Smaller than he used to be. Thin face. Hands on the cover.

A SERVANT at the bedside, leaves when Isolde enters.

Adeline does not enter. Closes the door behind her sister. Stays outside.

Vael stays at the door inside.

Isolde walks to the bed. Stops at the edge. Aldric opens his eyes. Sees her.

[DIALOGUE]
ALDRIC: You came.
ISOLDE: I came.
ALDRIC: ... You came.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She sits on the edge of the bed. Does not touch him.

---

## S#3 — Bed. Continuous.

[DIALOGUE]
ALDRIC: I should have killed her sooner.
ISOLDE: Yes.
ALDRIC: I should have raised you.
ISOLDE: Yes.
ALDRIC: I am sorry.
ISOLDE: I know.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Beat. He turns his hand on the cover — open. An invitation. She does not take it.

He understands. Closes his hand. Does not pull it back angry. Just closes it.

[DIALOGUE]
ALDRIC: Your mother. I want you to know.
ISOLDE: ...
ALDRIC: She did not love me. She lived with me because she had to. She loved you for both of us.
ISOLDE: ...
ALDRIC: I thought if I kept silent for fifteen years I was protecting you. I was protecting myself.
ISOLDE: I know.
ALDRIC: Forgive me.
ISOLDE: I am not going to.
ALDRIC: ...
ISOLDE: Not today. I do not know about another day.
ALDRIC: That is more than I have. It is enough.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He closes his eyes. Long beat.

[DIALOGUE]
ALDRIC (eyes still closed): The dragon.
ISOLDE: At the door.
ALDRIC: Tell him.
ISOLDE: Tell him what.
ALDRIC: That if any house in this kingdom tries to drag you back after I am gone, he is to burn the gate. He has my permission.
ISOLDE: He does not need your permission.
ALDRIC: I know. I am giving it anyway. So that the court hears me give it.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He opens his eyes. Looks at Vael across the room.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Heard.
ALDRIC: Good.

---

## S#4 — Bed. Hours Later.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde has stayed. She is in the chair beside the bed. Vael at the doorway. Adeline returned and is sitting in the second chair.

Aldric's breathing has slowed. Eyes closed.

[DIALOGUE]
ALDRIC (very quiet, eyes closed): Adeline.
ADELINE: Father.
ALDRIC: Run it well. She will be watching.
ADELINE: I know.
ALDRIC: ... Isolde.
ISOLDE: I am here.
ALDRIC: Touch my hand once. Only if you can.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She looks at his hand on the cover. Long beat.

She puts her hand on his. Once. Lifts it away.

[DIALOGUE]
ALDRIC: ... Thank you.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He does not open his eyes again. His breathing slows further. After a long beat — stops.

Adeline puts her hand over his face. Closes nothing — his eyes were already closed.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held wide of the chamber. Silence.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
SUPER, small at the corner: KING ALDRIC — DEAD.

[END HOOK]
Isolde stands. Walks to the chamber door. Vael opens it for her. She walks out into the corridor. Does not cry.

She stops in the corridor. Hand on the wall. Long beat.

Then she walks on.

Hard Cut.
