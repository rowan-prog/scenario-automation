# THE OFFERING — EP23 (v30)

## S#1 — Chamber. Morning.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A KEEP CRAFTSMAN — small, careful, deaf in one ear — at the far wall of the chamber. Mounting a small dark wood shelf at eye level from the bed.

Vael at the chamber table watching. Isolde on the bed in his robe, hand on her belly.

The craftsman finishes. Sets the small wooden bird on the shelf. Steps back. Bows.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: A little to the left.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He moves it. Half an inch.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Thank you.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He bows. Goes.

Vael stands. Walks to the shelf. Touches the bird with one finger. Stays there a long beat.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: She liked it on a windowsill.
ISOLDE: She had a windowsill. I have a wall. Same light. Now she gets to be in the room every night when we sleep.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: Is that all right.
VAEL: It is more than all right.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He sits on the bed beside her. Hand on her belly.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held: the bird on the shelf. Push slow.

---

## S#2 — Hall. Afternoon. A Small Dispute.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Lesser hall. Two house heads in front of Isolde — Vael sitting back, deliberately not the one judging today.

The dispute: a young dragon from House A trained a hunting bird that crossed onto House B's outer territory and killed a goat. House B wants compensation.

Isolde listens. Asks two questions. Decides.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: House A pays House B for the goat. Not at human market price. At dragon market price. Halric will set the figure. House A also keeps the hunting bird inside the keep wall until the year turns. After the year, the bird flies free anywhere it likes.
HOUSE A HEAD: My lady. The bird —
ISOLDE: The bird belongs to no one for one year. That is the cost of crossing.
HOUSE A HEAD: ...
HOUSE B HEAD: My lady.
ISOLDE: Both accept.
HOUSE A HEAD: Accepted.
HOUSE B HEAD: Accepted.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
They walk out together.

Vael, after, leaning back in his chair.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: You took the bird from its owner and gave it to no one for a year.
ISOLDE: I gave it to itself.
VAEL: ...

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He almost smiles. Third time on screen.

---

## S#3 — Bath Chamber. Late Afternoon.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Steam. Isolde in the bath. Belly above water. Hair pinned. Skin flushed.

Vael at the side. Sleeves rolled. Pouring warm water onto her shoulder slow.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: You are wearing your sleeves.
VAEL: Habit.
ISOLDE: Roll them higher.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: I want to see the scales.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He rolls his sleeves to the deltoid. Scales visible — soft today, not raised. She reaches out of the water. Puts her wet hand on his forearm.

The scales settle further.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: They flatten when I touch them.
VAEL: They always have.
ISOLDE: Since when.
VAEL: Since the first night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She does not look up. Looks at her own hand on his arm.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held close: her wet hand on his scaled forearm.

---

## S#4 — Bed. Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She is on her side. He is behind her. Hand low on her belly.

He kisses the back of her neck — under her hair. Once.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: That is a different kiss.
VAEL: It is.
ISOLDE: From when.
VAEL: From the night you walked out of the chamber in my sheet.
ISOLDE: That was a different woman.
VAEL: She was you.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She turns in his arms. Faces him. Looks at his face.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: I am going to ask you something. Don't answer if you can't.
VAEL: Ask.
ISOLDE: Are you happy.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: That's not a question I expect you to know how to answer.
VAEL: I do not know how to answer it.
ISOLDE: Then tell me what you would say if you knew.
VAEL: I would say I have not slept properly in nine years, and tonight I will. That is the closest word I have.
ISOLDE: All right.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She closes her eyes. He kisses her forehead. Closes his.

Both sleep.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held long wide of the bed. The shelf with the bird in the background.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
The shelf. The bird. In the moonlight from the slit window — the carved wing catches the light for one held beat.

Hard Cut.
