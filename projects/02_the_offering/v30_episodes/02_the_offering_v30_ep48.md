# THE OFFERING — EP48 (v30) — ADELINE VISITS

## S#1 — Outer Yard. Morning.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A small Veine carriage at the keep gate. Two outriders. No escort army. Adeline steps out. Plain travel coat. Hair simple. No circlet today.

Kiran meets her.

[DIALOGUE]
KIRAN: Lady Adeline.
ADELINE: My lord is my lord's. I am here as a sister, not a queen.
KIRAN: ...

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He walks her into the inner garden.

---

## S#2 — Inner Garden. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde already there. On the bench. Mireille — nine months — standing in the grass, holding herself up on a low rail. Vael at the pool again, ten paces away. Letting them speak.

Adeline sits beside Isolde. Slow. Careful.

[DIALOGUE]
ADELINE: Sister.
ISOLDE: Sister.
ADELINE: She is large.
ISOLDE: She is.
ADELINE: She looks like our father.
ISOLDE: She looks like her father.
ADELINE: ...
ISOLDE: Why are you here.
ADELINE: ...

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Long beat.

[DIALOGUE]
ADELINE: To see if you are happy.
ISOLDE: Why.
ADELINE: ...
ISOLDE: Tell the truth.
ADELINE: So I could tell people you weren't.
ISOLDE (half-smile): You haven't changed.
ADELINE: A little.

---

## S#3 — Garden. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Long beat. They watch Mireille pulling at her own toes. She falls over. Pulls herself back up. Both women laugh — briefly, awkwardly, neither comfortable doing it.

[DIALOGUE]
ADELINE: I am running it well.
ISOLDE: I have heard.
ADELINE: I have closed three of our father's tax houses.
ISOLDE: Good.
ADELINE: I have raised my mother's stipend exactly zero.
ISOLDE: ...
ADELINE: She wrote me last month asking to be brought back to the palace. I did not reply.
ISOLDE: ...
ADELINE: I am not doing this to earn anything. I am doing it because it is what should be done.
ISOLDE: I know.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She looks at Adeline. Long beat.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: You may come back. Once a year. To this garden. Not the hall.
ADELINE: Yes, sister.
ISOLDE: Bring Mireille a small thing each year. Something useful. Not pretty.
ADELINE: Yes, sister.

---

## S#4 — Garden. Late Afternoon.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Adeline stands. Walks to Mireille. Kneels. Puts the back of her hand against Mireille's small cheek. Mireille grabs Adeline's finger. Pulls hard.

[DIALOGUE]
ADELINE: She has the grip.
ISOLDE: She does.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Adeline stands. Walks to the garden gate. Turns at the threshold.

[DIALOGUE]
ADELINE: One more thing.
ISOLDE: Speak.
ADELINE: I never thanked you for not taking the crown.
ISOLDE: I did not do it for you.
ADELINE: I know. I am thanking you anyway.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She goes. The garden gate closes.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held wide. Isolde and Mireille in the garden. Vael walking up from the pool.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
Vael sits beside Isolde on the bench. Mireille climbs into his lap.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: She did not stay long.
ISOLDE: She did not need to.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: I think — for the first time — I do not hate my sister.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: That is a strange thing to think.
VAEL: It is a true thing.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She leans against him. He puts his arm around her shoulders.

Hard Cut.
