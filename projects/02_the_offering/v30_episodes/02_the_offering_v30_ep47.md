# THE OFFERING — EP47 (v30) — DA

## S#1 — Inner Garden. Six Months Later. Day.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Mireille seven months old. Sitting up. Held against Isolde's shoulder. Pulling at her mother's hair.

Vael at the pool washing his hands. Sleeves rolled. Scales soft.

Isolde sits on the bench. Lets Mireille down on her lap. The baby stands — wobbly, holding both her mother's thumbs.

[KEY CAMERA]
Close: Mireille standing on her mother's lap.

---

## S#2 — Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Mireille looks across the garden at Vael. Lets go of one of Isolde's thumbs. Reaches.

Vael at the pool. Has turned. Sees it.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (very quiet): Vael.
VAEL: I see.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He crosses slow. Stops three paces from the bench. Kneels. Holds out one finger.

Mireille reaches. Cannot quite grab it. Leans forward — Isolde's grip on her keeps her safe.

Vael takes one step closer on his knees. His finger reaches Mireille's hand. She closes around it. Hard.

[DIALOGUE]
MIREILLE (small, first clear word): Da.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael does not breathe for a count of five.

Isolde does not breathe either.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: She said it.
VAEL: I heard.
MIREILLE (again, more sure): Da.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He lifts her up. Slow. Both hands. Puts his forehead against her small forehead.

She grabs his hair.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held wide of the three of them. The pool. The walls of the small garden. The sky above.

---

## S#3 — Garden. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He sits on the bench beside Isolde. Mireille on his lap. Holding his finger still.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: She has not said Ma.
VAEL: She will.
ISOLDE: She picked you first.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: I am not jealous. I am proud.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: Da. Two letters. Easier than Ma.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: You are crying.
VAEL: I am not.
ISOLDE: There is something on your face.
VAEL: Steam from the pool.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She does not press. She puts her hand on his thigh.

---

## S#4 — Chamber. Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Bed. Mireille in her basket — bigger now, the iron-bound corners still there. Asleep.

Isolde on her side. Vael on his. Facing each other.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Da.
VAEL: Da.
ISOLDE: She is going to be a person.
VAEL: She is one already.
ISOLDE: A person who walks. Who talks. Who decides.
VAEL: A person who already decided. Today. You saw.
ISOLDE: I saw.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She closes her eyes. He puts his hand on her cheek.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: I love you.
ISOLDE: I love you.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held wide of the bed. The basket. The wooden bird on the shelf.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
Outside the chamber window — first early stars. Eastern ridge — twenty dragons. Resting. Heads down.

The keep is quiet. The keep is small. The keep is theirs.

Hard Cut.
