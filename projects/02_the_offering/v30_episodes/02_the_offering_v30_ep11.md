# THE OFFERING — EP11 (v30)

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Outer Yard. Dawn.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Five old dragons descend onto the basalt. Council elders — formal black, ancient, cold. They land in human form already. Tall, grey, faces sharp.

Kiran meets them at the gate. Bows formally.

[DIALOGUE]
ELDER ONE: We will see the lord. And the human.
KIRAN: The great hall is prepared.

---

## S#2 — Great Hall. Mid-Morning.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael at the dais. Isolde at his right — standing, not sitting. Black silk, hair pinned. The three keep lords on one side. Kiran at Vael's left.

The five elders walk in. Do not bow.

[DIALOGUE]
ELDER ONE: Lord Drakonis. There is a rumor.
VAEL: Speak it.
ELDER ONE: That you have named a human your mate.
VAEL: I have.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Hall ripple. The elders did not expect the clean confirmation.

[DIALOGUE]
ELDER TWO: Prove it. Here. Now.
ISOLDE (before Vael speaks): Prove what.
ELDER TWO: The bond. We will see if the body of the lord answers to yours.
ISOLDE: And if it does.
ELDER TWO: Then we accept. And then we decide what to do about it.
ISOLDE: Decide what.
ELDER ONE: That is council business, child.
ISOLDE: It is my body you are testing. It is not council business.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The elders look at each other. Adjust.

---

## S#3 — The Bond Test.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael steps off the dais. Walks ten paces. Stops with his back to Isolde. Closes his eyes.

For a long beat: nothing.

Then his scales rise — wrists, forearms, upper arms, shoulders. Slow. Involuntary.

His pupils slit when he opens his eyes.

His right palm — at his side — glows. Same orange shimmer audience saw at her belly in EP08.

The five elders see it. Confirm it with a glance.

[DIALOGUE]
ELDER ONE: Bonded.
VAEL: Bonded.
ELDER TWO (to Isolde): Step toward him.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She steps once. His scales rise further. She stops.

[DIALOGUE]
ELDER ONE: Enough.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the test. Push to the shimmer at his palm.

---

## S#4 — Council Withdraws. Vael and Isolde Alone in the Hall.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: What do they decide.
VAEL: Whether you live.
ISOLDE: ...
VAEL: I told you. Public mate is a target.
ISOLDE: So they will try to kill me to break you.
VAEL: They will try.
ISOLDE: Will they succeed.
VAEL: No.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She nods once. Walks to the dais. Sits in his chair.

He follows. Sits in the chair beside it — her chair.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: How long do they take.
VAEL: A day. Two.
ISOLDE: Then we have two days to prepare.
VAEL: We do.
ISOLDE: For what.
VAEL: For their answer. Whichever way it goes.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She puts her hand on the dais arm — the lord's chair arm. Stays there.

[KEY CAMERA]
Close: her hand on the lord's chair. Held.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
SUPER on the council door: COUNCIL — DELIBERATING.

[END HOOK]
The council chamber door — closed. Kiran outside it. He has placed his ear against the wood. He stays there for a long beat. Hears something. Pulls back. His face is wrong.

He walks fast back toward the great hall.

Hard Cut.
