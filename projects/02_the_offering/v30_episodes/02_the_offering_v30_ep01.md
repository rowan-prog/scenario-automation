# THE OFFERING — EP01 (v30)

## S#0 — Cold Open. Magical Festival. Lower City of Veine. Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Floating magical lanterns over a stone square. Music. Costumes. Dancers in masks.

A YOUNG WOMAN in a plain silver mask slips through the crowd alone. No escort. No jewelry. Her cloak is ordinary. Her eyes are not.

A TALL MAN in a plain black mask stops mid-crowd. He turns. The face under the mask is *almost* his — softer jaw, lighter hair, average build. Audience does not know yet that this is a magical disguise.

He sees her across the square. Does not move for three full beats.

He walks straight to her.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the square. Push to his eyes through the mask. Reverse to her eyes through hers.

[DIALOGUE]
HIM (low, just for her): I don't know you.
HER: That's why I came tonight. To not be known.
HIM: Then we have one night.
HER (after a beat): One.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He offers his hand. She doesn't take it for a long second. Then she does.

## S#0b — Small Room Above the Festival. Same Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A bed. One candle. He pulls her mask off slow. We see ISOLDE's face for the first time — young, alive, unguarded.

She reaches for his mask. He stops her hand at the wrist.

[DIALOGUE]
HER: Why.
HIM: Tomorrow you'd have to forget my face. Easier if you never see it.
HER: That's not fair.
HIM (closer): I know.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She kisses him. He kisses back. Slow first. Then not.

[CUTAWAY]
Her hands in his hair.
His mouth on her throat.
Her cloak on the floor.
The candle flame leaning toward the bed.
His scaled hand — half-second flash, his real form leaking through the disguise. She does not see it. Audience does.
Her thighs around his hips.
Her gasp into his mouth.

He does not stop until she does.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
After. Bed. Slick skin. Both breathing. He is on his back. She is on her side, watching him.

[DIALOGUE]
HER: One promise.
HIM: One.
HER: Don't lie to me. Not even the small ones. Not tonight.
HIM: I won't.
HER: Promise me one more.
HIM: What.
HER: Find me again. Even if I run.
HIM: I will.
HER (smaller): Even if I forget.
HIM (long beat): You won't.
HER: I might.
HIM: Then I'll remind you.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He turns his head and kisses her temple. Long. She closes her eyes.

[CUT — pre-dawn. He sleeps. Her eyes open. She watches him for a long moment. Touches his masked face once. Slides out of the bed. Dresses in silence. Crosses to the window.]

At the window she pauses. Looks back at him.

[DIALOGUE]
HER (whisper, almost not audible): I won't forget.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She slips out.

[CUT — full dawn. He wakes. Empty bed. He sits up. Stares at the place where she was. Long beat.]

[DIALOGUE]
HIM (one word): ... Find her.

[CUT — title card.]

## S#1 — Veine Palace. Bridal Hall. Six Months Later. Day.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A formal magical hall. Polished marble. Court arranged in two semicircles. The KING ALDRIC at the head — late forties, iron-grey, weak posture. STEPMOTHER beside him, gold dress, sharp face. Half-sister ADELINE in cream silk, twenty, smug.

At the center: the BRIDAL MIRROR. Silver disc on an altar. The PRIEST in white.

ISOLDE walks in. Plain white silk dress — old, reused. Hair down because no one styled it. Her face from the cold open, but harder. Six months of being watched.

A FIANCÉ across the hall — pale young noble, bored.

The priest gestures her forward.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the hall. Track Isolde walking to the altar. Close: her hand placed on the mirror.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A pulse. The mirror turns black.

The court goes silent.

[DIALOGUE]
PRIEST: She's not a virgin.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Aldric's jaw tightens. The stepmother's mouth pulls back — she is hiding a smile.

ADELINE (loud, performative): I knew it. I knew it. Look at her. Look at her mother's whore blood.

FIANCÉ steps back from the altar like she is contagious.

FIANCÉ: I'm not marrying that.

STEPMOTHER (to the king, leaning in): My lord. Send her to the dragon. Tonight. Before this gets out of the court.

ALDRIC (after a beat): ... Do it.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A GUARD grabs Isolde's arm. Hard.

Isolde does not lower her head. She looks at Adeline. Then at the stepmother. Then at the king. A long beat.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: I gave it away. It was mine to give.

ADELINE: You whore.

ISOLDE: At least I picked the man. You're still picking the dress.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Court reaction — sharp inhale, one cough, one stifled laugh. Adeline's face freezes. The guard walks Isolde toward the door. She walks on her own feet. Does not look back.

## S#2 — Veine Road. Black Carriage. Day-Into-Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
An iron-bound carriage rolls east. Two guards on top. Two horses ahead.

Isolde inside. White silk now grey with dust. Hands chained loosely at her lap. Looking out the slit window.

A GUARD'S VOICE from above, laughing.

[DIALOGUE]
GUARD (above): Word is he'll eat her by morning.

ISOLDE (level, not looking up): Then he'll save you the trouble.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The guards laugh louder.

[VO]
ISOLDE (V.O., quiet): My mother was eight days dying. I watched. I learned three things. Keep your mouth shut. Remember everything. Pay it back later.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She closes her eyes. Hand goes briefly low on her stomach. Audience-only. She doesn't know yet. Her body does.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the carriage on the eastern road. Mountains ahead. Sun dropping behind.

## S#3 — Drakonis Keep. Outer Yard. Dusk.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The keep is bigger than Veine palace by half. Black basalt walls. Dragon-tooth gate.

The carriage rolls in. Stops in the courtyard. Dragons watch from the upper walls — human form, tall, beautiful, cold. Among them at the front: SERA. Red hair. Smiling already.

The guards open the carriage. Pull Isolde out. She stumbles once, finds her feet. Walks forward on her own.

KIRAN — Vael's lieutenant, mid-thirties, dark coat — meets her in the yard. He does not bow. He does not speak. He looks at her for one beat longer than necessary.

[KEY CAMERA]
Slow tracking Isolde walking the yard. Close: Sera's eyes.

[DIALOGUE]
SERA (loud, to the others): That's it? That's the human offering?

ANOTHER DRAGON: She stinks of their court.

ANOTHER: Used.

SERA: He won't even touch this one.

ISOLDE (does not stop walking, does not look at Sera): Then it'll be a short evening for everyone.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Sera's mouth opens. Nothing comes out. Kiran's mouth almost smiles.

## S#4 — Drakonis Keep. Great Hall. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The Great Hall. Ceiling lost in shadow. Black stone. Torches in red-orange flame. At the head — a dais. On the dais — VAEL DRAKONIS.

Black coat. Sleeves rolled. Hands behind his back. Dark hair pulled back. The face we did not see in the cold open: harder, sharper, real.

Beside the dais: a VEINE ENVOY — old, grey-bearded, the king's man.

Isolde stops at the foot of the dais. Looks up.

She sees Vael.

[KEY CAMERA]
Push slow to her face. Recognition — a flicker, then doubt. The face is different. But something. Her hand twitches once at her side.

Cut to Vael's face. Held. Pure cold. Nothing.

Audience-only: half-second flash, his right hand at his side — his fingers tremble once. He stops it. Court does not see.

[DIALOGUE]
ENVOY (stepping forward, formal): My lord Drakonis. King Aldric of Veine sends the offering as agreed. Tribute. The girl is to be —

VAEL (does not look at the envoy): Leave her. Take the gold back to your king. He won't need the rest of the payment.

ENVOY: My lord, the terms — she is to be your tribute, your —

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael steps down off the dais. One step. He stops.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: She is my bride.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The hall freezes.

Sera, at the side: her face goes white. She takes one step forward.

[DIALOGUE]
SERA: My lord —

VAEL (does not turn): Did I stutter.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Sera stops. The envoy's mouth works without sound.

A guard near Isolde reaches for her arm to walk her away.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Don't touch her.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The guard freezes mid-reach.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (to Kiran, not looking at Isolde): My chamber.

KIRAN: My lord.

VAEL: She walks. With me behind her.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He gestures Isolde forward. She walks. He follows three paces behind. The court parts.

Mid-walk, Sera steps into Isolde's path. Close. Speaks low — only for Isolde.

[DIALOGUE]
SERA (whisper): He'll tire of you by sundown.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde stops. Looks at Sera. Long beat. Walks around her without speaking.

Behind her, Vael does not look at Sera either.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Move.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Sera moves.

They reach the inner door. Kiran opens it. Isolde walks through. Vael follows. The door closes.

SUPER on Vael: VAEL DRAKONIS — LORD OF DRAKONIS KEEP
SUPER, lower third: BRIDE.

[END HOOK]
The hall stares at the closed door. Sera at the side — her hand at her own throat, where his mark should have been.

In the corridor behind the door, Vael walks three paces behind Isolde. Does not look at her back. His right hand, at his side, opens and closes once.

He is shaking.

Audience-only: a tiny scale shimmer at the back of his neck, just below his hairline. Mate-bond signature. Held three beats.
