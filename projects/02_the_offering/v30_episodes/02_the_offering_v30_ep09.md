# THE OFFERING — EP09 (v30) — FIRST PAID EPISODE

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Great Hall. Morning.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A keep healer — older woman, dark coat — was called to the chamber at dawn. Word has begun to leak. The three lords, the house heads, Sera and her remaining people — all gathered in the great hall. Vael not present yet.

Sera at the front of the hall. She is smiling — a hard, bright smile.

[DIALOGUE]
SERA (loud, to the room): A healer was called to the lord's chamber this morning.
A LORD'S WIFE: That has been confirmed.
SERA: Then say what we are all saying. She is pregnant. Already. The human whore is pregnant.
ANOTHER DRAGON: By the lord?
SERA: She arrived a week ago. Six weeks pregnant is not the lord's. Do the math.
ANOTHER DRAGON: My lady, that is —
SERA: She arrived a whore. She is still a whore. She brought another man's bastard into our lord's bed and the lord is too proud to admit it.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Hall ripple. Several dragons nodding. Several refusing to look at her.

The hall door opens at the far end.

Vael walks in. He has heard the last line. The whole last line.

He walks down the length of the hall. Slow. Does not raise his voice.

[KEY CAMERA]
Slow tracking Vael walking. Wide pull of the hall reacting.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Repeat what you said.
SERA: My lord, the keep deserves to know —
VAEL: Repeat what you said.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Sera does not look down. But her smile is gone.

[DIALOGUE]
SERA: The child is not yours.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Long beat. The hall does not breathe.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: The child is mine.

SERA: My lord, the timing —

VAEL: The child is mine. Anyone in this hall who says otherwise — say it now. Out loud. So I can hear it.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Silence. Even the lords' wives look at the floor.

## S#2 — Same Hall. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael stops in front of Sera. He does not raise a hand. He does not raise his voice.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Sera. Out of my keep. By sundown. Take three of your house with you. The rest stay and answer to me.
SERA: My lord — you cannot —
VAEL: I can. I am.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Sera does not move.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Sundown.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She walks out. Fast. Her people follow her.

The three keep lords stand. Bow.

[DIALOGUE]
OLDEST LORD: My lord. The hall recognizes.
VAEL: Good. Out. All of you. The hall is closed today.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The hall empties.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the emptying hall. Vael alone at the center.

---

## S#3 — Vael's Chamber. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde has been listening. The chamber door is open a crack. She closes it when she hears him coming.

He walks in. Sees her at the door.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: You should not have claimed it like that.
VAEL: I claimed what is mine.
ISOLDE: It might not be.
VAEL: It is.
ISOLDE: You do not know that.
VAEL: I do.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She stares at him. Long beat.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: How.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He looks at her. Decides. Then does not.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Trust me on this one thing. The child is mine. Mine. We will say nothing else, ever, to anyone.
ISOLDE: Vael —
VAEL: On this one thing. Trust me.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She does not trust him yet. But she does not argue.

She walks to the desk. Picks up the small wooden bird. Holds it. Looks at it.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Whose was this.
VAEL: My mother's.
ISOLDE: She made it.
VAEL: She did.
ISOLDE: For you.
VAEL: For me.
ISOLDE: How old were you when she died.
VAEL: Six.
ISOLDE: How did she die.
VAEL: ...

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He does not answer for a long time. Then:

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Publicly. In a hall. Like this one. By men who wanted to break the lord who loved her.
ISOLDE: Your father.
VAEL: My father.
ISOLDE: Did it break him.
VAEL: For nine years. Then it killed him.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She sets the wooden bird back down carefully. Walks to him. Puts her hand on his chest. Over his heart.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: That is why you said it the way you said it. In the hall just now. The child is mine. Loud. No room left in the sentence for anyone to argue.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: You think if you do not name what is yours loud enough, it will be taken from you.
VAEL: I think that is what happened to my father.
ISOLDE: ...

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She puts her forehead against his sternum. Stays there.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held: her forehead against his chest. His hand at the back of her head.

## S#4 — Drakonis Keep. Outer Parapet. Sundown.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The parapet. Vael at the rail. Isolde at his right. They watch the keep gate.

Below: Sera and three of her house — mounted, packed. Sera at the front. She does not turn around as she rides out the gate.

The other two of her house are still in the keep, sworn to Vael now.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Halfway down the road, Sera stops. Turns her horse around. Looks up at the parapet. Looks not at Vael. At Isolde. At Isolde's body.

She has seen something. The audience can see what Sera sees — a faint heat-shimmer at Isolde's belly through her robe. A second shimmer at Vael's right hand on the parapet stone. Same color. Same pulse.

Sera's face changes. She knows.

[DIALOGUE]
SERA (from below, last shout, just barely audible): Mate or not, I'll be back.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
That word — mate — lands.

Vael does not react. Isolde does. She turns her head and looks at his face.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Mate.
VAEL: She wishes.
ISOLDE: Is that what you would call her.
VAEL: No.
ISOLDE: What would you call her.
VAEL: Someone who is leaving.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Beat.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: What would you call me.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He does not answer.

[KEY CAMERA]
Slow push on his hand on the rail. The faint shimmer at his knuckle.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
SUPER as Sera rides off: VOLZAAR — OUT.

[END HOOK]
Sera, on the road below, has not turned around again. She rides east. Toward the council's territory.

Vael's hand on the rail closes. The shimmer goes out.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (quiet, to Isolde): Inside.
ISOLDE: Inside.

Hard Cut.
