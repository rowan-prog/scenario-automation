# THE OFFERING — EP19 (v30) — THE WOMAN WHO POURED THE CUP

## S#1 — Chamber. Morning. The Letter from Aldric.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Kiran walks in with a sealed envelope. King Aldric's seal — not Veine kingdom seal. Personal.

Isolde opens it. Reads. Hands it to Vael without a word.

[INSERT — page audience-visible:]
> Daughter.
> Your mother was Mireille of South Cove. My first lawful wife, not my second.
> She did not die of fever.
> The woman who poured her cup is captured. She is in my cellar.
> I have known for fifteen years. I have been silent.
> Tell me what you want done with her. I will obey.
> — Aldric.

Vael reads. Sets it down on the desk.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: He has known. Fifteen years.
VAEL: He has been a coward.
ISOLDE: He is being something else now.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: I want her here. Not in his cellar.
VAEL: Then she is here.
ISOLDE: I want to be the last face she sees.
VAEL: Then you will be.

[KEY CAMERA]
Close on the letter. Push slow.

---

## S#2 — Drakonis Keep. Outer Yard. Three Days Later. Dawn.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The yard. An iron plate set on the basalt. Two black-coat guards holding the STEPMOTHER (Isolde's stepmother — Adeline's mother). Chains. Court gown wrinkled. She has been on the road from Veine for three days.

She does not look up.

Isolde at the keep steps. Daily black silk. Belly clear. Hair pinned. Vael beside her. Aldric on the wall above — Aldric himself came with the stepmother to deliver her. He is forced to stand by Vael's terms.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the yard. Cut to the iron plate. Cut to Aldric on the wall — head not lowered.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (to the stepmother, level): Last word.
STEPMOTHER (looks up): I raised you.
ISOLDE: You stood beside my mother's body and told me to stop crying.
STEPMOTHER: I — I —
ISOLDE: You poured her cup. You watched her die. You took her chair. You called me filth in front of her grave.
STEPMOTHER: Spare me.
ISOLDE: For who.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The stepmother does not name anyone. There is no name to give.

---

## S#3 — Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael walks once around the plate. Puts his scaled palm flat on the iron. Black fire runs down his arm to his palm. The iron goes red.

The stepmother is forced onto the plate by the two guards. Dies in three seconds.

Vael lifts his palm. The plate cools.

He walks to Isolde at the steps. Puts both his hands on her shoulders. His palms are warmer than they should be. He lifts her chin with two fingers.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (low, only her): All right.
ISOLDE: All right.
VAEL: Inside.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He walks her inside. Aldric stays on the wall. Does not move.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of Aldric on the wall alone. Held.

---

## S#4 — Chamber. Same Day. Mid-Morning.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde sits on the bed. Vael standing across from her at the window. She has not cried.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: My father is on your wall.
VAEL: He is.
ISOLDE: He will want to see me.
VAEL: He may.
ISOLDE: I am not ready to see him.
VAEL: Then he stays on the wall.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Beat.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: He came in person. He could have sent her with a guard.
VAEL: He could have.
ISOLDE: That means something.
VAEL: It does.
ISOLDE: I will see him. But not today.
VAEL: Tomorrow.
ISOLDE: Tomorrow.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She lies down. Hand on her belly. He sits beside her on the bed. Hand over hers.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
SUPER on the iron plate, post-execution: STEPMOTHER — POISONER, EXECUTED.

[END HOOK]
Aldric on the wall as evening falls. Has not moved. Kiran brings him water. He drinks it. Hands the cup back. Says one word:

ALDRIC: Thank you.

Kiran nods. Goes.

Aldric stays on the wall through the night.

Hard Cut.
