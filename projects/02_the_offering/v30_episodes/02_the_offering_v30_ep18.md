# THE OFFERING — EP18 (v30) — TRANSFORMATION

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. East Wasteland. Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Eastern wasteland. Full moon. Black scorched ground from old fire.

Isolde a hundred paces out from the gate. Heavy black wool wrap that covers her from throat to ankles. Belly visible under the wool now. Hair down. The faint orange shimmer is on her stomach in the moonlight.

Vael twenty paces out from her. Black shirt. Sleeves torn at the shoulder.

He breathes once.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Scales run from his hands up his arms. Across his chest. The shirt tears down the back and falls away in two halves. His back muscle changes shape. He drops to one knee. Arms become forelimbs. Forelimbs become legs. Legs become massive. The neck extends. The face elongates. Horns push out of his hair. Hair is gone. Scales coat everything.

Forty-five seconds. Slow.

Where Vael was — a black dragon. Sixty feet at the shoulder. Wings folded against his back. Eyes — the same grey, with the dark red ring on her side now too. Both eyes.

He lowers his head. Bigger than her body. He stops six paces from her. Waits.

[KEY CAMERA]
Slow tracking of the transformation. Wide of the finished dragon. Close: his eye finding her.

---

## S#2 — Same Wasteland. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde walks forward. Does not put her hand on the scale of his cheek. Puts her cheek there. Hair against scale.

The scale under her cheek settles. The plates were standing on edge a moment ago — heat from the transformation. They flatten under her skin. Just her cheek has done that.

She closes her eyes.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (against the scale): You are warmer like this.
VAEL (in her head, low rumble, only her): Always.
ISOLDE: Can you talk to me like this.
VAEL: While you are touching me. Yes.
ISOLDE: Show me your ridge.

---

## S#3 — Wasteland. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The black dragon lowers his foreleg. Isolde climbs into the crook of it. Sits with her back against the foreleg-bone. He lifts the foreleg. Brings her up to his neck. She climbs the rest of the way. Sits in the curve where his neck meets his shoulder.

He turns his head east. Walks slow toward the eastern ridge.

The ridge — a line of mountains in the moonlight. Then the mountains are not mountains. They are dragons. Twenty of them. Sleeping in a line. They open their eyes when he arrives. Twenty pairs of red-ringed eyes in the dark.

They look at her. None move.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (in her head): They know you now. They will not move on you without my permission.
ISOLDE: And without yours.
VAEL: I will know.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He breathes out. Low black fire rolls forward across the ground in front of them. Not a wall. A river. Slow. Runs east. The ridge dragons close their eyes one by one as the fire passes.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide aerial of the ridge. Twenty pairs of eyes open then closed. Close: Isolde's hand flat against his warm scaled neck beside her.

---

## S#4 — Wasteland → Keep Gate. Same Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He lowers her. Walks slow back toward the keep. She walks beside his hind leg. Halfway across he transforms back. Bone clicks. Body shrinks. By the gate he is a man again — torn shirt, walking beside her, scaled palm on the small of her back.

Aldric — wait, no. Kiran at the gate. Bows.

[DIALOGUE]
KIRAN: My lord. My lady.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
They walk in past him.

[KEY CAMERA]
Slow tracking them. His palm on her back through the wool.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
SUPER on the ridge: DRAKONIS RIDGE — KNOWS HER.

[END HOOK]
On the southern horizon, far away — a single red shape lifts off a rock outcrop. Audience-only. Sera, watching from a distance, has felt the ridge open its eyes. She is leaving for real now. East, after the council.

Hard Cut.
