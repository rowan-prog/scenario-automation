# THE OFFERING — EP03: The First Mark

S#1 — KEEP / SERVICE CORRIDOR / NIGHT

[Visual]
A narrow stone corridor behind ISOLDE's chamber. A water trough set in the wall — its surface still and dark. The trough water gives back the corridor's torches like a black mirror.
An ASSASSIN — slim, ash-grey hooded cloak, no kingdom mark, a thin curved knife — slips along the corridor wall. He passes the trough. His reflection passes a half-beat late.
At the trough's edge: a dropped human-kingdom signet button, small, deliberate. Audience-only.

S#2 — ISOLDE'S CHAMBER / NIGHT

[Visual]
ISOLDE is at the obsidian wall-mirror. She has begun to lift the pendant chain back into the inner collar lining of the **Royal Arrival** ivory gown. Her wrist is bare.
The crescent on her inner wrist prints faintly, on its own, in the same beat as before. She watches it in the obsidian.
Behind her, in the mirror's deep dark, a shape moves into the room. The ASSASSIN — knife low along his thigh.
ISOLDE sees him in the mirror before she turns. Her shoulders set. She does not scream. She steps sideways along the wall.

S#3 — ISOLDE'S CHAMBER / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
The chamber door breaks open. VAEL crosses the room in two strides. His scaled hand catches the assassin's wrist mid-strike. The thin curved knife snaps in his grip — the blade breaks across the scaled palm. No blood on Vael.
He lifts the assassin off the floor by the wrist. He drops him. KIRAN is at the door — takes the body off the floor, drags it out.
Door closes. The hall sound dies behind it.
VAEL turns to ISOLDE. He does not speak. He walks her three paces back until her shoulders meet the obsidian wall-mirror. His scaled hand goes to the wall beside her head. His face is a hand-width from hers.

[Camera]
SMASH on door splintering → HANDHELD TRACKING: VAEL in two strides → ECU: scaled hand on assassin's wrist → MACRO: blade snapping across scaled palm → WIDE: assassin lifted, dropped, dragged out by KIRAN → DOOR CLOSE smash → TRACKING ISOLDE walked back to the obsidian → MEDIUM TWO-SHOT: VAEL's scaled hand against the obsidian beside her head, her ivory shoulder pinned in reflection

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (low, slow, near her mouth): I was three rooms away. I felt the blade leave its sheath. I will not be three rooms away again.
ISOLDE: Then where will you be.
VAEL: Here.

[FX]
door splinter · scaled palm crushing blade · body drag on basalt outside · door close · paired breath, very close


S#4 — ISOLDE'S CHAMBER / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
The obsidian mirror behind ISOLDE doubles them — two figures pressed close, ivory against black leather. Her white hem is mirrored at her feet.
VAEL's other hand finds her **inner wrist**. He turns her wrist up. The crescent under her skin is already printing again.
He bends. His mouth goes to the pulse-point in her wrist — not high on the arm, not the inside of the elbow — the wrist. He sets his teeth there. The bite is slow, deliberate, controlled. He does not break skin badly — but he leaves a **scaled-fingered ring of indentation around her wrist and one crescent of teeth at the pulse**. The crescent under her skin and the bite mark overlap, exactly.
Her drawn breath. Her free hand moves — and does not push him away. It rests against the obsidian mirror beside her own reflected face.
He lifts his head. His scaled hand turns over hers on the obsidian. The hand and the reflected hand match. He covers her hand against the glass.
Her ivory gown's bodice is undisturbed. Her shoulder-seam is undisturbed. The mark is only the wrist. The neck is uncovered, unmarked, untouched.

End Image: ISOLDE's back pressed to the obsidian mirror, VAEL's scaled hand over hers on the glass, the **first bite-mark printed at her pulse-point** in a crescent of teeth that exactly overlaps the scale-glow. The mirror gives the mark back doubled. Her neck is still bare.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (very low, almost a vow): The first mark is at the pulse. The next will not be where they can see it. Not yet.
ISOLDE: And the one they will see —
VAEL: — is the one I will give you in front of them. Not tonight.
