# THE OFFERING — EP01: Two Reflections

S#1 — ASH PLAIN APPROACH / DUSK

[Visual]
A long black wagon crosses a wide ash plain toward a basalt keep that rises like a knife on the horizon. Cold wind tears at the wagon's banner — the human-kingdom crown stitched in dull silver.
Inside the wagon: a polished obsidian travel mirror is bolted to the wall. **ISOLDE — Royal Arrival look: ivory silk gown with pearl trim at the throat, silver-thread sash, white fur-lined cloak, glossy dark-brown hair loose past the shoulder, porcelain skin, grey-green eyes** — is reflected in the black glass. The reflection is what the camera sees first; only after does it cut to her real face.
A clerk in grey wool seals an iron-bound writ across from her. He does not lift his eyes.

[Camera]
WIDE: wagon crawling across grey ash, basalt keep against the sky → INSERT MACRO on banner threads loosening in wind → MEDIUM PROFILE on the obsidian mirror inside the wagon: ISOLDE's reflection only → SLOW DOLLY around the mirror until her real face comes into frame, not her reflection → ECU on the clerk's iron seal pressing wax

[DIALOGUE]
CLERK: The writ is sealed. The crown's hand is done.
ISOLDE: Then take your hand off it.
CLERK: Lady.
ISOLDE: I will carry it myself.

[FX]
wide ash-plain wind · banner cloth-rip · wax-hiss under iron seal · low wagon-wheel grind · sub-bass under the mirror frame


S#2 — KEEP INNER ARCH / NIGHT

[Visual]
The black-scaled hand of VAEL rests on the inner arch of the keep, fingers spread on the basalt. We are inside the keep, looking out at the wagon stopped at the gate. His face is not yet given to the room — only the hand on stone, the line of shoulder under black leather, the long black cloak.
The basalt under his palm is polished to a dark mirror finish. His silhouette is doubled in it.
KIRAN — **black-haired, hard build, drake-house captain's silver cuirass** — stops three paces behind him, head bowed.

[Camera]
ECU on black-scaled fingers spread on basalt, the stone reflecting them → PULL BACK: VAEL in profile silhouette, face withheld → REVERSE OVER VAEL's shoulder: the wagon at the gate, ISOLDE just stepping down → ECU on VAEL's index finger pressing once into the polished basalt as if marking the spot she stands on

[DIALOGUE]
KIRAN: My lord. The offering has arrived.
VAEL: I felt her cross the river an hour ago. Do not speak of her as a thing.

[FX]
basalt under fingertips · single deep heart-thud (his) · slow draft of cold air down the arch · distant wagon-brake


S#3 — KEEP COURTYARD / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
ISOLDE walks into the courtyard alone. The twelve guards stay outside the gate. The clerk does not follow.
The courtyard floor is wet polished basalt — black, mirror-smooth. ISOLDE's ivory gown is reflected upside down in it as she crosses. The white of her hem glows against the black floor.
Her cloak shifts. A silver chain slips loose from the inner collar lining — **her mother's old signet pendant**, a faded sigil on its face. It catches the last gate-light for one breath.
On the keep wall above, an old rune set into the lintel flickers — **the same beat, the same sigil shape**. Audience-only.
At the far end, in the shadow under the inner arch, VAEL's silhouette is still in profile. His scaled hand at his side has not moved.

[Camera]
LOW WIDE: ISOLDE crossing into the mirror-floor courtyard, ivory gown doubled on the wet basalt → TILT DOWN ECU: pendant sliding free of collar lining → CRASH ZOOM up the wall: rune flickers in the same beat → MACRO INSERT: rune sigil ↔ pendant sigil match cut → TRACKING BEHIND ISOLDE forward → ECU on VAEL's scaled fingers in the far shadow, motionless

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: (under breath) Mother.
ISOLDE: Walk.

[FX]
gate iron groan closing behind · pendant chain whisper against silk · low stone rune hum (audience-only) · single doubled heart-thud (hers, then his — from across the court)


S#4 — KEEP COURTYARD / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
ISOLDE stops a foot short of the shadow line. She does not step back.
VAEL's scaled hand rises from the dark. It does not grab — it stops **a palm's width beside her shoulder**, holding the air.
ISOLDE turns her face up toward him. She does not look afraid. She looks **at him**, level.
The hand turns and finds her **inner wrist**. The black-scaled fingers close around it — slowly, not gentle, but the hold does not bruise. He lifts her wrist between them. The pulse-point in her wrist faces up to him.
A scaled fingertip rests on the pulse.
On her **inner wrist**, a **faint scaled glow** prints once — a brief crescent of light under the skin. It flickers in the same beat as the rune above.
On the back of his scaled hand, in the same beat, a second faint crescent answers. The keep wall behind them — polished basalt — shows both glows mirrored.

[Camera]
MEDIUM TWO-SHOT in the shadow line → ECU: black-scaled hand stopping a palm's width from her shoulder → REVERSE on ISOLDE looking up, no flinch → MACRO INSERT: scaled fingers closing on her inner wrist → ECU: pulse beating under skin, scaled fingertip pressing down → MACRO: crescent glow blooming on her wrist → MACRO: matching crescent on his hand-back → WIDER on polished basalt wall — both glows doubled in the mirror finish

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (O.S., low and slow): You did not need to be carried. I was watching for that.
ISOLDE: You were watching.
VAEL (O.S.): Since the river.

[FX]
breath held · low male voice, very close, slow cadence · faint sub-bass under the paired glow · stone silence around them


S#5 — GREAT HALL ENTRY / NIGHT

[Visual]
An iron-bound door opens inward. Warm low torch-light spills out, but VAEL's face is still kept in shadow on the far side of the doorway.
He releases her wrist. He does not step aside — he turns half a step and **walks in beside her**, matching her pace.
Past the entry: a long basalt hall, mirror-polished floor, a single iron throne at the far end, two columns of silent retainers in dark leather.
ELARA — **golden-haired bride candidate, ivory and gold silk, sharp jaw** — stands beside the throne. Her smile flattens when ISOLDE crosses the threshold.
KIRAN flicks his gaze from ISOLDE to the scaled hand beside her, and back.
At the foot of the throne dais, the basalt floor is a black mirror. ISOLDE and VAEL both appear in it, walking together, doubled.
He stops one step ahead of her. A torch in the iron sconce **bursts wider**. Light begins to fall across the line of his jaw — and stops. The frame holds **just before** his face is fully shown.

End Image: VAEL turned toward ISOLDE, jaw catching fire-light, face still in shadow. The mirror-floor at their feet doubles them — two figures, one hers, one his, both glowing once at the wrist and hand-back in the same beat.

[Camera]
PUSH IN through the iron doorway, torch-warmth blooming across ISOLDE's face → TRACKING beside her: VAEL's scaled hand near her hip, no contact → WIDE REVERSE: hall, throne, two retainer columns, polished mirror floor → CLOSE on ELARA's flattened smile → ECU on KIRAN's flick-glance: hand → ISOLDE → back → LOW ANGLE on the mirror floor: their doubled walking shapes → CLOSE PROFILE on VAEL's jaw climbing in light → FREEZE just before face reveal → MACRO INSERT pair: her wrist crescent / his hand-back crescent → same beat

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (low, slow, formal): The wind brought your name across the river before your wagon did. I have kept this hall empty for it.
ISOLDE: You kept it empty for a name?
VAEL: For the one who would answer to it.
ELARA: (to KIRAN, under breath) That is the offering?
KIRAN: That is what walked in beside him.
