# THE OFFERING — EP08: The Band Comes Off

S#1 — ISOLDE'S CHAMBER / MORNING

[Visual]
The obsidian wall-mirror. ISOLDE stands before it. The maid is gone. On the basalt bench beside her: the **Public Mate Display sub-variant** laid out — Vael's Choice base, but with a **wider silver-and-pearl band cut low** for the throat, silver-and-pearl trim down the shoulder seams, and a **hair-pin of pearl set at the crown** for half-pinned hair. Ivory cuffs higher on the wrist.
She is dressing herself. The maid is not in the room. This is her choice.
She lifts the new throat band. She does not yet put it on. She holds it between herself and the obsidian mirror — the reflected band and the real band meet.
She sets it at her throat. She locks the clasp herself. The collarbone bite is fully visible above its low cut.
She lifts the wrist cuff — drawn back further than before. The wrist bite is fully visible at the cuff edge.
She lifts the pearl pin. She sets her hair half-pinned at the crown. Her **uncovered neck**, between band and ear, is given to the air. No marks there. Bare.

S#2 — KEEP / COURTYARD / DAY

[Visual]
The same basalt courtyard as EP01. Day this time. The courtyard floor is wet polished basalt — black mirror.
Three lines of human-kingdom escort have arrived — twelve in full grey livery, sigils on chests, sealed writs. HALDREN at the front. MAREN beside him, his sigil now half-turned in his livery — half-disowned by his own crown.
At the inner arch: VAEL. Long black cloak, dark hair tied back, scaled hand at his side. KIRAN one step behind.
On a raised basalt step at VAEL's right: a single second seat — the same one from the throne dais and the war-table. Moved out now, into the courtyard, in front of the envoys.
The polished courtyard floor doubles everything — envoys upside down, drake-house captain upside down, VAEL upside down, the second seat upside down.
ELARA at the side of the inner arch — ivory-and-gold, no place at the second seat. She has stopped at the threshold.

S#3 — COURTYARD / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
ISOLDE enters at the inner arch — the **Public Mate Display sub-variant**. Pearl pin in crown. Wider silver-and-pearl band low at the throat. Shoulder seams pearled. Ivory cuff drawn back.
She walks down the basalt step. She does not look at HALDREN or MAREN. She looks at VAEL.
She passes him. She steps to the **second seat** in front of the envoys. She sits.
VAEL turns to the envoys. His scaled hand lifts — fingers open. A line of **dark-red flame** climbs along his hand seams.
He **does not** take the banner from the pole. He turns his open scaled palm toward the human-kingdom banner. The flame leaves his hand and lands on the banner. The cloth catches at the bottom edge. It burns up the pole — slow, deliberate.
HALDREN does not step back. MAREN does. Two of the escort line drop to one knee — not from courtesy, from weight.
The polished courtyard floor doubles the burning banner upside down.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (low, slow, formal): The crown sent her in cloth I had not chosen. I burned its first banner. I will burn its second on the same pole. I will not burn its third — because there will not be a third.
HALDREN: My lord — you cannot —
VAEL: I have.

S#4 — COURTYARD / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
ISOLDE rises from the second seat. She turns to face the envoys, the full line, MAREN, HALDREN.
She lifts both hands to her throat.
She **unclasps the silver-and-pearl band herself**. She lifts it off her throat. She lets it fall onto the basalt seat behind her.
Without the band, the **full collarbone bite** is given to the courtyard — not just the crescent above. The whole mark. The whole shape of teeth.
She lifts the ivory cuff at her right wrist and pushes it up to her elbow. The **full wrist bite** — the bite at the pulse — is given.
She does not say a word. She looks at HALDREN, then MAREN, then the line.
The polished courtyard floor doubles her bare throat and bare wrist.
VAEL has not moved. His scaled hand at his side. His eye on her, not on them.
KIRAN goes to one knee — not to VAEL, not to the human crown — toward **ISOLDE**. Twelve drake-house retainers along the inner arch go to one knee with him, one beat behind.
HALDREN's writ falls out of his hand to the basalt.
MAREN goes to one knee — slow, last.

[DIALOGUE]
HALDREN: (after long silence) — my lord —
VAEL (low, slow, formal): Do not speak to me. Speak to the one who unfastened her own band in front of you. Speak to —
[a deep cracking ROAR cuts him off — overhead, off-frame, two drake-house wings landing on the rampart]
VAEL: — She is —
[a second ROAR cuts him off, closer, deeper]

S#5 — COURTYARD / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
The two ROARS overhead settle into a low hum. Two great wing-shadows hold over the courtyard — drake-house wings landed at the rampart, their shadows blanketing the basalt.
ISOLDE turns from the envoys to VAEL.
She lifts **VAEL's scaled hand** — herself — and sets it on her **uncovered neck**, between band-line and ear. The bare neck — the place she has kept for him through all of EP3-7.
Her own hand stays over his on her neck.
The scale-glow on his hand-back and the crescent on her wrist meet, paired, doubled on the polished courtyard floor under their feet.
Her hand is on his hand on her bare unmarked neck. He has not yet bitten there. The neck stays — for now — without a mark.
The envoys are still kneeling. HALDREN's writ on the basalt at his knee. MAREN's livery sigil half-turned.
VAEL begins his sentence again, very low, in a voice meant for her, not for the courtyard.
