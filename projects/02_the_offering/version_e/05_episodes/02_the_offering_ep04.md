# THE OFFERING — EP04: The Burning Banner

S#1 — KEEP / COUNCIL ANTECHAMBER / DAY

[Visual]
A long basalt table. At one end, the human-kingdom banner has been propped against a stand — the dull silver crown stitched on grey. HALDREN stands beside it with two writs and the dropped signet button from the assassin corridor (audience knows where it came from).
At the far end, VAEL stands behind ISOLDE's chair. She has changed — **Vael's Choice look: black silk base with white pearl trim along the bodice, silver-and-pearl band at the throat covering the collarbone area, glossy dark-brown hair half-pinned back, ivory cuffs at the wrist hiding the bite-mark beneath**. Her sleeve is closed. The bite is a private fact under cloth.
At her bodice's inner lining, under the black silk and the pearl trim, the **mother's signet pendant** rests — moved from the Royal Arrival cloak collar to the new bodice inner lining overnight. Out of sight. The chain length adjusted so the pendant face sits flat against her sternum, behind the bodice cloth.
The polished table top is a long black mirror. The banner is reflected upside down on it — the human crown inverted.

KIRAN: He entered with this. From a corridor outside her chamber. It is your kingdom's button.
HALDREN: A button is not a writ.
VAEL (low, slow, formal): A button at the door of a room I have chosen is enough.

S#2 — COUNCIL ANTECHAMBER / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
VAEL lifts the banner from its stand with his scaled hand. He holds it out flat — the human crown facing the long basalt table. The polished table reflects it back, inverted.
He turns his palm up.
On the back of his scaled hand, the crescent prints — strong this time. A line of **dark-red dragon-flame** runs along the inside of his fingers, slow, controlled.
He touches the flame to the banner's bottom corner.
The banner catches. The crown stitching curls. The fire is a slow climb, not a flash — deliberate.
HALDREN steps back a half pace. ISOLDE does not.
The burning banner is doubled on the polished table — a fire upside down on its own reflection.

[DIALOGUE]
HALDREN: My lord. That is the crown's banner.
VAEL: It is cloth. I have burned cloth before.
HALDREN: They will hear of it.
VAEL: I am counting on it.

S#3 — COUNCIL ANTECHAMBER / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
The retainers and HALDREN have been dismissed. Only KIRAN at the door, back turned, half a room away.
VAEL drops the burned banner into the iron brazier. He turns. He walks to ISOLDE's chair.
He kneels — one knee — beside her seat. His scaled hand finds her wrist cuff. He lifts the cuff back, slowly, until the pulse-point bite from EP03 is uncovered. The crescent of teeth, the scale-glow underneath, the faint bruise around it.
He sets his mouth on it again — not a new bite, a return. Slow lip-press along the existing mark. A second small flame runs along his finger and traces the **edge of the bite-mark** on her skin, slow, without burning her. The fire follows the crescent shape exactly. It dies on the inside of her wrist.
ISOLDE's free hand goes — slowly, on her own — to the back of his neck where the dark hair is tied. She does not pull. She does not push. She rests.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (very low, near her wrist): The mark answered today. I felt it through three walls.
ISOLDE: Then I felt yours too.
VAEL: Good. We will not pretend.

S#4 — KEEP / RAMPART / DAWN

[Visual]
ISOLDE stands at the rampart in **Vael's Choice**. The throat band catches dawn light — silver-and-pearl, not chain. The ivory cuff is slightly higher on the right wrist now. A line of the **crescent + bite-mark** prints once, faintly, through the cuff fabric.
VAEL stands at her right. He is not touching her. He is watching the human-kingdom road below — the road that brought the wagon.
A second human-kingdom rider crests a distant rise. The new envoy.
Behind them, on the polished basalt rampart wall, both their reflections are doubled. The scaled hand at his side glows once on its back. The crescent on her wrist glows under cuff in the same beat.

End Image: ISOLDE and VAEL at the rampart, side by side, not touching, both mirrored in polished basalt. Their paired glows answer. The second human-kingdom envoy rides the road below.

VAEL: They will see what I choose for them to see. The rest is between you and the obsidian.
