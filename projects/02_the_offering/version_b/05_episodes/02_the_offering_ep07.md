# THE OFFERING — EP07: ACROSS HIS LAP

S#1 — BLACK CASTLE / COUNCIL CHAMBER / DAY

[Visual] The council chamber is full now. Vael at the long obsidian table — seated in the carved black chair at the head, scaled hand on the carved arm. The new HERALD stands at the far end, ivory-and-gold banner planted before him. Kiran, Haldren, six lesser nobles ranged along the table. Isolde stands at Vael's left shoulder — not seated, no chair set for her, the deep-cut gown showing the marks on her neck clearly. The herald reads from a sealed scroll.

[Camera] WIDE — full council chamber, long table, all seats taken → MEDIUM — Vael at the head, scaled hand on the carved arm → CLOSE — Isolde standing at his left shoulder, marks visible under torchlight → REVERSE WIDE — the herald at the far end, the scroll opened → CLOSE — the king's wax seal on the scroll, broken edge.

[DIALOGUE]
HERALD: The king demands the woman's return. In her place — a marriage of his house, a peace.
KIRAN: A peace that begins with an assassin.
HERALD: The king demands an answer before the seventh hour.

[FX] The scroll unrolling. Dust falling from the herald's road-cloak. The faint groan of Vael's scales.

S#2 — BLACK CASTLE / COUNCIL CHAMBER / CONTINUOUS

[Visual] Vael does not look at the herald. He turns his head toward Isolde. His scaled hand opens at his side — palm up — fingers spread. He says nothing. Isolde looks at the hand. The court watches. She steps in front of his chair, between his knees, and turns. He reaches up — one scaled hand at her waist, the other at the back of her thighs — and DRAWS her down across his lap, sideways, both her legs over the carved arm, her head against his shoulder. The deep-cut gown rides slightly — her bare ankles show above the silk slipper. His scaled hand on her waist holds her against his chest. His other scaled hand rises into her hair — closes on a thick handful at the back of her skull. His thumb settles on the side of her neck — directly on top of the second BITE-MARK. The whole council goes still. Kiran's hand stays steady on his sword. Haldren's eyes stay on the table.

[Camera] CLOSE — Vael's scaled hand opening at his side, palm up → MEDIUM — Isolde stepping between his knees → TRACKING — Vael drawing her down across his lap, sideways → CLOSE — her bare ankle above the silk slipper → MEDIUM — his scaled hand on her waist, anchoring her against his chest → MACRO — his other scaled hand closing in her dark-brown hair → CLOSE — his thumb settling on the side of her neck, directly on the second bite-mark → WIDE REVERSE — the council frozen in their seats, every face caught.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (still to Isolde, not the herald): Are you cold.
ISOLDE: No.
VAEL: Then he can wait longer.

[FX] The complete silence of the council. Silk against carved wood. Her quiet exhale. The slow thrum of his scales against her back.

S#3 — BLACK CASTLE / COUNCIL CHAMBER / WINDOW SIDE / CONTINUOUS

[Visual] Far through the council chamber's high arched window — across the wasteland — on the high northern ridge — a single great WING-SHADOW lifts off the ridgeline. Then another. Then a third. The first DRAGON OUTLINES of the pack become visible against the grey afternoon sky, distant but undeniable. The council, still seated, sees them through the window. The herald turns slowly to follow their eyes. Vael does not turn. His scaled fingertip, on Isolde's neck, traces the line between the two bite-marks. Isolde's wrist — resting across his other scaled hand — pulses GOLD, brighter than it has, in the same rhythm as the wing-beats outside.

[Camera] WIDE — high arched window, wasteland and ridge visible → SLOW PUSH IN — the ridgeline, a wing-shadow lifting → CUT — second wing-shadow → CUT — third → REVERSE — the council, faces turning to the window → CLOSE — the herald turning slowly → CLOSE — Vael, still not turning, his scaled fingertip tracing between the two bite-marks on Isolde's neck → MACRO — Isolde's inner wrist across his other hand, the gold pulsing in time with the distant wing-beats.

[DIALOGUE]
KIRAN (low, eyes on the window): They came down.
HALDREN: My lord — the pack.
VAEL (not turning): They felt her wake.

[FX] Distant wing-beats — felt, then faintly heard. The thrum of Vael's scales against Isolde's back synchronizing with the wing-beats. The herald's breath catching.

S#4 — BLACK CASTLE / COUNCIL CHAMBER / TABLE / CONTINUOUS

[Visual] At the center of the obsidian table, a great brass HOURGLASS — the council's measure — is overturned by Vael with a flick of his free scaled hand. The sand begins to run. Vael lifts his palm a half-inch off the carved arm of the chair. A dark blood-red FLAME rises from his palm — and licks along the back of his scaled hand toward the wrist of his own arm — then crosses to Isolde's wrist resting in his other palm — runs in a thin line of flame along the gold mark beneath her skin. The gold flares brighter where the flame moves over it. The herald takes a step back from the table. The council does not move. Vael speaks toward the herald without taking his eyes from the mark on Isolde's wrist.

[Camera] CLOSE — Vael's scaled fingertip flicking the brass hourglass over on the obsidian → MACRO — the sand beginning to run, the chamber light catching the grains → CLOSE — Vael's palm lifting from the carved arm → MACRO — the dark blood-red flame rising on the back of his scaled hand, crossing to Isolde's wrist → MACRO — the gold mark flaring brighter under the line of flame → REVERSE WIDE — the herald taking a step back from the table, the council still seated → END FRAME — the hourglass running, Vael's flame on the gold mark, the wing-shadows in the window behind.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Tell the king he has until the sand runs.
HERALD: My lord — to do what.
VAEL: To kneel before her — or to lose his banner before her.

[FX] The slow rush of sand inside the brass. The slow crackle of the flame on Isolde's mark. The wing-beats — close enough now to vibrate the chamber windows.
