# THE OFFERING — EP05: NOT YET YOUR MOUTH

S#1 — BLACK CASTLE / HIGH TERRACE / NIGHT

[Visual] A narrow stone TERRACE on the upper east wall of the black castle. The wasteland below is silver-black under the moon. Isolde stands at the obsidian balustrade, fingertips on the cold stone, her dark-brown hair loose. The black silk gown still has the high cut hiding the bite-mark on her collarbone. Her inner wrist glows faintly gold against the dark. Behind her at a distance, the corridor doorway is unguarded — Kiran has cleared the level. From the shadow at the far end of the terrace, Vael steps to the edge of the moonlight — does not yet move toward her. His scaled hand opens and closes once at his side.

[Camera] EXTREME WIDE — the terrace, wasteland silver below, Isolde a single dark silhouette at the rail → SLOW PUSH IN on her hand on the obsidian → CLOSE on her inner wrist, the gold pulse → REVERSE WIDE — Vael at the far end of the terrace, half in shadow → CLOSE — his scaled hand opening and closing at his side.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (without turning): How long have you been there.
VAEL: Long enough.
ISOLDE: Then say what you came to say.

[FX] Cold wasteland wind. The faint hum of the wrist mark — only she hears it. The slow groan of distant stone as Vael shifts.

S#2 — BLACK CASTLE / HIGH TERRACE / CONTINUOUS

[Visual] Vael crosses the terrace in five slow strides. He does not stop a polite distance from her. He stops a breath from her. His scaled hand snatches her wrist — the marked one — and pulls. Her body is drawn into his chest in a single movement, her free hand caught between them. The black scales on his hand meet the gold pulse beneath her skin — and the gold IGNITES — a brighter flare beneath her wrist, threading into the dark obsidian of his hand. His other scaled hand rises to her chin — thumb pressing under her jaw — tilts her face up until her mouth is a hand's-breadth from his. He stays there. He does not lower his mouth.

[Camera] TRACKING — Vael crossing the terrace, slow → CLOSE — his scaled hand catching her wrist → MEDIUM — Isolde drawn into his chest in one movement → MACRO — the gold pulse meeting the obsidian scales, the brighter flare under her skin threading into his scales → CLOSE — his other scaled hand rising to her chin, thumb under her jaw → TIGHT TWO-SHOT — her mouth lifted, a hand's-breadth from his, both holding.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: You are marked.
ISOLDE: I felt it.
VAEL: Do you know what it means.

[FX] Wind. The faint roar of the mark under her skin meeting the scales on his hand. His controlled breath against her mouth.

---

S#3 — BLACK CASTLE / HIGH TERRACE / CONTINUOUS

[Visual] Vael does not kiss her mouth. He lowers his head — and his lips press to her EARLOBE — soft, brief, the heat of his breath at her ear. Then his mouth drags slowly down — to the side of her neck — to a new place above the first BITE-MARK. Teeth close — not as hard as the first — and hold. When he draws back, a second red-pink mark sits beside the first. He drags his mouth down again — to the line of her pulse at the side of her throat — and presses a slow open-mouthed kiss directly on the beat of her artery. His scaled palm has slid from her chin down to flat over her sternum — fingers spread, feeling the climb of her breath. The gold under her wrist is still pulsing into his other hand between them.

[Camera] CLOSE — Vael's mouth bypassing hers, lowering to her earlobe → MACRO — his lips against the soft of her ear → MEDIUM — his mouth dragging down the side of her neck → MACRO — teeth closing on a new place above the first bite, holding → CLOSE — the second red-pink mark on her skin → MACRO — his open mouth pressing on the pulse beat at the side of her throat → CLOSE — his scaled palm flat across her sternum, fingers spread.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Mate.
ISOLDE (barely): That is not a thing your kind says to mine.
VAEL: My kind does not say it twice.

[FX] Her sharp breath. The faint chuff of his exhale against her pulse. The hum of the gold under her wrist climbing.

---

S#4 — BLACK CASTLE / HIGH TERRACE / CONTINUOUS

[Visual] Vael's scaled fingers tighten on her wrist once — then go still. He draws his mouth from her pulse a deliberate inch. His scales darken, blood-red threading deeper. He lifts his head until their mouths are again a breath apart — closer this time — close enough she can feel the warmth of his lower lip. He does not close the distance. He releases her wrist instead — sets it down between them — and steps back from her. One slow step into shadow. Another. The cold of the terrace returns to her skin. Her wrist is still gold-pulsing in the moonlight. Two bite-marks on the side of her neck now. The silver-pearl strap of her gown has slid a half-inch off her shoulder again. From inside the shadow at the far end of the terrace, his voice.

[Camera] CLOSE — his scaled fingers tightening on her wrist, then stilling → MACRO — his mouth drawing back from her pulse → TIGHT TWO-SHOT — their mouths a breath apart, no contact → MEDIUM — Vael stepping back, one slow step → WIDE — Vael two paces into shadow → CLOSE — Isolde alone at the rail, two bite-marks visible, gold wrist pulsing → REVERSE WIDE — Vael half-vanished into the shadow at the far end of the terrace.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (from shadow): Not yet your mouth.
ISOLDE: Why.
VAEL: Because once it is yours, the kingdom has a second war.

[FX] Wind taking the heat from her skin. His scales fading into the dark. A single far wing-beat from above the ridge — answering, much closer than before.
