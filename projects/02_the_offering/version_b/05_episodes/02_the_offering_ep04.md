# THE OFFERING — EP04: THE FLAG IN HIS HAND

S#1 — BLACK CASTLE / OUTER HALL — APPROACH TO COUNCIL CHAMBER / DAY

[Visual] Morning grey through high slit windows. Isolde walks beside HALDREN down the wide corridor toward the council chamber doors. The high carved doors are open ahead — a smaller human-kingdom DELEGATION stands inside the council chamber, holding the standard of Aldric's house — a long banner of pale ivory with a stitched gold sun. The mark of the assassin's master, openly carried. Isolde's collarbone still shows the faint trace of last night's bite under a new high-cut black silk gown. Her inner wrist still glows pale gold beneath her sleeve.

[Camera] WIDE — corridor, morning light slanting from above → TRACKING with Isolde and Haldren toward the open doors → CLOSE — the ivory-and-gold banner held inside the chamber, sun emblem dead center → MACRO — Isolde's collarbone, the trace of the bite-mark visible under the gown's edge → CLOSE — Isolde's inner wrist, pale gold beneath the sleeve.

[DIALOGUE]
HALDREN: They have come for an answer.
ISOLDE: They have already had one.
HALDREN: …They will not call it final.

[FX] Boot heels on stone. Distant council voices — flat, formal. A faint thrum from Isolde's wrist mark — only she hears it.

S#2 — BLACK CASTLE / COUNCIL CHAMBER / DAY

[Visual] The council chamber: long obsidian table, Vael at the far end standing — not seated — scaled hand spread flat on the table edge. KIRAN at his right, in armor. The human DELEGATE bows the banner — and lays the folded ivory-and-gold standard across the obsidian. Isolde enters with Haldren behind her. Vael's grey eyes do not leave her wrist until she stops at his left hand. Then they go to the delegate.

[Camera] WIDE — council chamber, the long table, Vael at the far end → CLOSE — Vael's scaled hand spread flat on obsidian → MEDIUM — delegate laying the banner across the table, ivory-and-gold against black → REVERSE TRACK — Isolde entering, walking down the length of the table → CLOSE — Vael's grey eyes lifting from her wrist to the delegate.

[DIALOGUE]
DELEGATE: My lord. The kingdom asks for the woman's return. The offering was a misunderstanding.
VAEL: It was very clear.
DELEGATE: The king will name terms — a marriage of his own house instead.

[FX] The banner sliding to a stop on the obsidian. The faint groan of Vael's scales — slow, dark. The chamber holding silent.

---

S#3 — BLACK CASTLE / COUNCIL CHAMBER / CONTINUOUS

[Visual] Vael walks slowly along the side of the long table to the laid banner. His scaled hand lifts the ivory-and-gold standard — holds it in front of him. He turns his palm upward. A dark blood-red FLAME rises from his palm and licks at the banner from beneath. The fabric catches — slowly, deliberately. The gold sun emblem chars first, then the ivory blackens. He does not drop it. He holds the burning banner in his palm and lets the delegate watch every inch of it turn to ash. Isolde stands at his left — not flinching from the flame near her wrist. Haldren steps half a pace back at the doorway, hand to chest. Kiran's gauntleted hand drops to his sword.

[Camera] TRACKING — Vael walking down the side of the table to the banner → CLOSE — his scaled hand lifting the ivory-and-gold standard → MACRO — his palm turning up, the dark blood-red flame rising → CLOSE — the gold sun emblem charring black → MEDIUM — the delegate's face going pale → REVERSE — Isolde at his left, the flame inches from her gown sleeve, her face still → WIDE — Haldren a pace back at the doorway, hand to his chest.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: She is no longer a misunderstanding.
DELEGATE: My lord, the king —
VAEL: They will not call you that name again.

[FX] The slow tearing of fabric in flame. The thin hiss of obsidian heating. The delegate's breath catching.

---

S#4 — BLACK CASTLE / COUNCIL CHAMBER / SIDE ALCOVE / CONTINUOUS

[Visual] The delegate and Kiran withdraw. Haldren remains in the doorway — turns his face away from the alcove. Vael takes Isolde by the wrist — the marked one — and draws her two steps into the side alcove where the wall-torch is dim. Ash from the burned banner still clings to his scaled fingertips. He turns her wrist palm-up. With the back of his scaled thumb he wipes a streak of ash across her inner wrist — directly on the gold flicker. Then he lets a slow finger of dark blood-red flame surface from his palm — and runs his fingertip along the gold mark beneath her skin, the flame tracing the same line. The gold under her skin pulses brighter where the flame moves. A new faint scorched-line — burned ash, not skin — sits over the mark. He does not look at the delegate leaving. He looks at her wrist.

[Camera] WIDE — the chamber emptying, Haldren at the doorway looking away → MEDIUM — Vael drawing Isolde two steps into the alcove → CLOSE — his ash-streaked scaled fingers turning her wrist palm-up → MACRO — his thumb wiping ash across her inner wrist, directly on the gold flicker → MACRO — the dark blood-red flame surfacing from his palm, his fingertip tracing the mark beneath her skin → CLOSE — the gold pulsing brighter under the flame.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: They will not call you that name again.
ISOLDE: And what will you call me.
VAEL: Not yet.

[FX] The crackle of ember on her skin. Her breath catching, just once. The slow thrum of his scales. Haldren's footsteps moving away — slow, very deliberate.
