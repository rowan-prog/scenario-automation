# THE OFFERING — EP01: THE SCALED HAND

S#1 — BLACK CASTLE / VAEL'S HIGH CHAMBER / DUSK

[Visual] VAEL DRAKONIS (mid-30s, broad-shouldered, lustrous black hair tied back, grey eyes, sharp jaw — Dragon Lord) stands at a tall arched obsidian window, back to camera. His right hand rests on the polished black sill — the back of his hand is plated in obsidian-and-blood-red SCALES. Far below, across the grey wasteland, a single iron carriage rolls toward the gates. The scales on his hand pulse once — a thin dark-gold thread surfacing under the black, then gone. KIRAN (drakon house captain, leather and silver armor) kneels two paces behind him, helm under arm.

[Camera] WIDE — chamber interior, Vael against the open window, sky bleeding grey behind him → PUSH IN on the obsidian sill, his scaled hand → MACRO on the scales — the dark-gold thread surfacing then fading → tight LOW ANGLE on his profile, eyes never leaving the carriage → wide three-quarter — Kiran kneeling in the foreground, Vael unmoving.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (without turning): She's here.
KIRAN: My lord.
VAEL: Open the gates when she stands alone.

[FX] Wind howling outside. The faint groan of distant carriage wheels. Vael's scales humming — low, almost a heartbeat.

S#2 — BLACK CASTLE / OUTER GATES / CONTINUOUS

[Visual] The iron-bound carriage halts before the great BLACK GATES. ISOLDE (mid-20s, lustrous dark-brown hair flowing past her shoulders, grey-green eyes, porcelain pale skin, slender wrists) steps down. She wears a long royal gown of ivory silk with silver embroidery, a white fur mantle, pearl details at the throat. Against the grey wasteland and the black walls — a single point of brightness. The HEAD ENVOY (cold, gloved) yanks the royal SIGNET RING from her finger. He wipes his mouth on a black handkerchief and drops it onto the back of her bare hand. HALDREN (grey hair, grey diplomat's mantle, hands folded) does not move with the others.

[Camera] WIDE — gates rising behind the carriage, Isolde a flash of ivory in the grey → CLOSE — gloved hand twisting the signet off her finger → MACRO INSERT — handkerchief falling onto her hand → CLOSE — Isolde's chin, lifted, eyes fixed forward → WIDE — carriage pulling away, Haldren standing five paces behind her like a pale post.

[DIALOGUE]
HEAD ENVOY: The kingdom no longer calls you by name.
ISOLDE: Then it does not need to call me at all.
HALDREN (flat): I remain. The offering must be witnessed.

[FX] Carriage wheels groaning across frozen ground. The first wasteland wind. No music — only the wind.

S#3 — BLACK CASTLE / OUTER GATES / GROUND BEFORE THE DOORS / CONTINUOUS

[Visual] Isolde stands alone before the great black doors. The first wall-torch above the keystone ignites. Where the firelight touches stone, an ancient RUNE carved above the gate flickers — once. Twice. Inside her white fur mantle, sewn close to her ribs, an old dark-metal SIGNET on a thin chain slips loose. The etched sigil on the pendant is the same shape as the rune above the gate. Pendant and rune flicker in the same rhythm. The envoys' backs are turned. Haldren stands behind her — he sees firelight on her mantle but cannot read what it touches. Isolde's free hand presses the pendant back beneath the silver embroidery. Then — the great black doors groan and split open from the inside. A massive WING-SHADOW sweeps across the firelight and falls over her shoulders.

[Camera] MACRO — the rune above the gate flickering, audience-only beat → TILT DOWN — the signet slipping free beneath her mantle, frame-matched to the rune sigil → CLOSE — her hand pressing it back under the silver embroidery → WIDE — gates parting from within → TILT UP — wing-shadow descending → HOLD on Isolde looking up, the shadow consuming her face.

[DIALOGUE]
HALDREN (half-step back, voice low): Stay where you stand.
ISOLDE: I have nowhere else to stand.

[FX] A soft, ancient rune-chime — audience-only, in the same key as the pendant. Heavy castle gate groan. A single low wing-beat — felt more than heard.

S#4 — INSIDE THE GATES / FORECOURT / CONTINUOUS

[Visual] Isolde stands inside. The gates have shut behind her. Black stone walls rise on every side. The wall-torches above are sparse — most of the courtyard is dark. From the far end of the dark, slow footsteps. A FIGURE emerges from shadow — but only the lower edge of him resolves into the firelight: black leather, a long mantle, and the black-scaled HAND at his side. The scales catch the torchlight — obsidian and dark blood-red. His face stays inside the shadow. The hand rises. It does not touch her shoulder. It stops in the empty space beside her wrist. He is close enough that she can feel the heat of his palm without contact.

[Camera] WIDE — Isolde alone inside the dark courtyard → SLOW TRACKING from the dark wall toward her, a figure resolving lower edge first → CLOSE on the scaled hand at his side → MEDIUM — Isolde holding her ground, eyes fixed forward into the shadow where his face should be → CLOSE — the scaled hand rising and stopping a breath from her wrist.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (from inside the shadow, low): Don't lower your eyes.
ISOLDE: I haven't.
VAEL: Then give me your hand.

[FX] Castle bells, distant. The wing-beat replaced by a slow low thrum beneath the score — almost a second heartbeat.

---

S#5 — INSIDE THE GATES / FORECOURT / CONTINUOUS

[Visual] Vael's scaled fingers close around the inside of Isolde's wrist. Slowly, he lifts her hand into the firelight — turns it palm-up, exposing the soft inner skin. A black-scaled fingertip — warm — presses against the pulse point of her inner wrist. He holds it there. Beneath her skin, a faint pulse of LIGHT — pale gold, like buried embers — flickers once under the pressure of his fingertip. The mark is so subtle only she can see it, and his hand. Her eyes widen by a fraction. Vael's scales darken — dark blood-red threading through obsidian. His thumb stays on her pulse a beat longer than it should. His other hand rises from his side, scaled, moving slowly toward her jaw. It stops a breath from her chin — does not touch — and holds there in the firelight.

[Camera] CLOSE — scaled fingers closing on her wrist → MACRO on her inner wrist, the gold flicker under the skin → HOLD on his thumb on her pulse → TIGHT — Isolde's eyes, the held breath → CLOSE — his other scaled hand rising into the light, fingers angled toward her chin → HOLD — the hand stopping just short of her jaw, scales darkening, fingertips suspended.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: I see.
ISOLDE (barely above a breath): What do you see.
VAEL: That I won't be sending you back.

[FX] The low thrum sharpening — closer to a heartbeat, doubled. Torchlight licking the scales. A single distant wing-beat from the high ridge — answering.
