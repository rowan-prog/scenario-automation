# THE OFFERING — EP08: THE WORD THAT DID NOT FINISH

S#1 — BLACK CASTLE / OUTER COURTYARD BEFORE THE GREAT GATES / DUSK

[Visual] The sand of the brass hourglass has run. The outer courtyard before the great black gates. The human-kingdom DELEGATION — the new herald, twelve full-armor ESCORT GUARDS, the king's seal-bearer — all assembled in a wedge beneath the open gates. Behind them the ivory-and-gold banner is held high. The dusk is deeper than usual — a dark cold red bleeding through the grey. On the high northern ridge above the castle, the dragon pack is now visible — twelve clear silhouettes against the bleeding sky, slow circling. Inside the castle gates, the COURT and BRIDE CANDIDATES have lined the inner steps of the great hall — every face turned outward. Elara stands at the front of the bride candidates, gown pale.

[DIALOGUE]
HERALD (to the open gates): The hour. The king demands the woman.
SEAL-BEARER: Or war.
COURTIER (whisper, inside): The lord comes.

[FX] Wing-beats from above the ridge — slow, audible now. The brass hourglass on a side stand — last grain falling. Wasteland wind through the open gates.

S#2 — BLACK CASTLE / INNER STEPS OF THE GREAT HALL / CONTINUOUS

[Visual] From inside the great hall — through the inner-doors and across the high stone steps — Vael walks slowly out, scaled hand at Isolde's lower back. Isolde walks at his right, the deep-cut black silk gown showing the marks on her neck unmistakable now even at distance. The court parts. Kiran follows two paces behind with a folded second ivory-and-gold BANNER — the same kind the herald carries — over his arm. Haldren walks with him, head bowed. They emerge at the top of the high steps where the inner courtyard meets the outer. Vael stops one step above the highest step. The whole delegation across the courtyard is below the line of his boot.

[Camera] WIDE — the inner-doors opening, Vael and Isolde walking out → TRACKING — slow walk through the parting court → CLOSE — Vael's scaled hand at the small of Isolde's back → MEDIUM — Kiran following with the folded ivory-and-gold banner over his arm → WIDE — Vael stopping at the top step, the delegation in the courtyard below → CLOSE — Isolde at his right, marks on her neck under the wall-torches.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (to Kiran, not the delegation): Bring the colors.
KIRAN: My lord.
HERALD (below, calling up): My lord — the king's terms —

[FX] The court holding still. The slow wing-beats above. The crunch of Kiran's armor as he steps forward with the banner.

S#3 — BLACK CASTLE / OUTER COURTYARD / AT THE BOOT OF THE STEPS / CONTINUOUS

[Visual] Kiran walks down the high steps and drops the folded ivory-and-gold BANNER at the boots of the herald and seal-bearer. Vael raises his scaled hand, palm down — and a dark blood-red FLAME pours from his palm in a thin line — strikes the folded banner at the delegation's feet. The banner CATCHES — the gold sun emblem chars to black first, then the ivory turns to ash. Vael does not lower his hand. The escort guards stagger one half-step back. The herald looks up the steps. Isolde, at the top step, watches the banner burn without flinching. Vael lets the flame run down his hand — to the inside of his wrist — and across to Isolde's wrist, which he has lifted in his other scaled hand. He runs the line of flame along the gold mark on her inner wrist. The mark FLARES — a hard pulse of pale gold, in time with a wing-beat above.

[Camera] WIDE — Kiran walking down the steps with the banner → CLOSE — the folded ivory-and-gold landing at the herald's boots → MACRO — Vael's palm above the high step, dark blood-red flame pouring down → CLOSE — the banner catching, gold sun blackening → REVERSE — the delegation staggering a half-step back → CLOSE — Isolde at the top step, watching, not flinching → MACRO — Vael's flame running down his own hand, across to Isolde's lifted wrist, along the gold mark → CLOSE — the gold mark flaring in time with a wing-beat above.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (down at the delegation): These were the colors that sent her.
HERALD: My lord —
VAEL: They burn before her now.

[FX] The fabric flaring. The thin hiss of obsidian heating. The escort armor creaking as they shift back. The wing-beats above climbing in tempo.

S#4 — BLACK CASTLE / OUTER COURTYARD / HIGH STEP / CONTINUOUS

[Visual] Vael's scaled hand goes from her wrist down to her waist — closes — and LIFTS Isolde up one full step higher, onto the step where he stands. Now they stand level — both above the delegation, both above the court below. His scaled fingers slide into her dark-brown hair at the side of her skull and TILT her head — gentle, deliberate — toward him, exposing the side of her neck with the two BITE-MARKS turned out toward the delegation. The herald and the king's seal-bearer can see them perfectly. The escort guards — twelve in armor — can see them. The bride candidates inside on the lower steps can see them. Vael's other scaled hand rises — and his SCALED PALM closes flat over the side of her neck, fingertips pressing directly on the second bite-mark, blood-red flame surfacing under his scales to outline the mark in fire. He lowers his head toward her mouth — slowly — close enough his lower lip almost touches hers. He starts to speak.

[Camera] CLOSE — Vael's scaled hand at her wrist sliding down to her waist → MEDIUM — Vael lifting Isolde up one step higher beside him → WIDE — the two of them on the same high step, court and delegation below → CLOSE — his scaled fingers sliding into her hair at the side of her skull → MEDIUM — Vael tilting her head, exposing the bite-marks toward the delegation → MACRO — twelve guards looking up at the marks → REVERSE — bride candidates inside, Elara's face white → CLOSE — Vael's scaled palm closing flat on her neck, fingertip pressing on the second bite-mark, blood-red flame surfacing under his scales to outline the mark in fire → TIGHT TWO-SHOT — Vael lowering his head, mouth a breath from hers.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (loud, to the courtyard, beginning the declaration): She is not your offering. She is my —

[FX] His voice carrying across the courtyard. The slow blaze on the side of her neck. The hush of the entire courtyard holding for the next word.

S#5 — BLACK CASTLE / OUTER COURTYARD / HIGH STEP / CONTINUOUS

[Visual] From the high ridge above the castle — the dragon pack ROARS. Twelve throats — together. The sound hits the courtyard like a wall. Vael's last word does not finish. His mouth stays a breath from Isolde's — but he turns his head a fraction toward the ridge. The delegation's escort guards — every single one of the twelve, armor and all — BUCKLES. Knees hit cobblestone. The herald drops to one knee. The seal-bearer falls forward, hand catching the stone. Below the steps, the court goes to its knees in a wave. Haldren bows his head fully. Elara is on her knees, gown crumpled. Vael's hand stays on the side of Isolde's neck. The blood-red flame is still under his scales there. He looks at her — does not finish the word. Isolde — eyes on him — slowly raises her free hand, lays her palm down over the back of his scaled hand on her neck, and CLOSES her fingers around it. The black scales catch a hard pulse of gold from her palm where it meets them.

[Camera] EXTREME WIDE — the ridge above the castle, twelve dragon outlines roaring at once → REVERSE EXTREME WIDE — the outer courtyard, the wall of sound hitting → CLOSE — Vael's mouth, a breath from hers, the word stopping → TILT — Vael's head turning a fraction toward the ridge → WIDE — twelve guards buckling, knees to cobblestone in unison → CLOSE — the herald dropping to one knee → CLOSE — the seal-bearer falling forward, hand catching stone → REVERSE WIDE — the court inside going to its knees in a wave → CLOSE — Haldren's bowed head → CLOSE — Elara on her knees, gown crumpled → REVERSE TIGHT — Vael's eyes on Isolde, mouth still a breath from hers, the word unsaid → CLOSE — Isolde's free hand rising → MACRO — her palm closing over the back of his scaled hand on her neck → MACRO — her fingers tightening around his scales → MACRO — black scales catching a hard gold pulse from her palm.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (low, only she can hear, the unfinished word): —
ISOLDE (looking at him, her hand on his scales): Finish it.
VAEL (low, almost a smile, almost a warning): On my mouth. Not yours.

[FX] The dragon pack roar holding — fading. The hush after the roar. The slow gold pulse beneath her palm on his scales. A single far wing-beat — closing.
