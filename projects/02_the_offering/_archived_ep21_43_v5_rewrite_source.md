[VISUAL/ACTION]
The east balcony. Isolde seven months — large now, sitting in a wide cushioned chair Vael has had moved out here in the night. The belly fills the lap of her robe. The five crescents visible.

A small bag at her feet. A letter inside, sealed in red wax. The wax pressed by a southern coast house — Mireille's mother's family.

Vael behind the chair, one hand on her shoulder.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the balcony in morning light. Cut to Isolde reading the letter slow. Cut to Vael's hand on her shoulder.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: My mother's brother.
VAEL: He writes you.
ISOLDE: He writes to the child. He wants to see her once. He wants to send her one gift. He says he watched my mother die and he never spoke after that.
VAEL: Did he.
ISOLDE: He says he stopped speaking the year I was sold.
VAEL: Twelve years.
ISOLDE: Twelve.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She folds the letter back into its bag. She does not cry. She just closes the bag.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: He may come.
ISOLDE: After the birth.
VAEL: After the birth.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
She turns her head up to Vael over the back of the chair. He bends and kisses her forehead. He doesn't speak.

Below the balcony, in the yard, two of the southern coast house's older men walk in slow — escorted. They sit on the basalt and wait. They are not coming up. They are just here to be near.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Their chamber. The bed wider than before — Vael has had a second bed frame built beside the first, lower, where the midwives can move. Clean linen stacked at the foot.

Kiran is the only one allowed to bring her food.

# THE OFFERING — EP39

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Private Chamber. Late Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The chamber. Two oil lamps. The midwife — one of the south coast women, sent ahead by Isolde's uncle two months ago — moves quietly between the linen and the bed.

Isolde on her back on the bed. Sweat at her hairline. Hand crushing Vael's wrist. The belly tight under the silk.

Vael beside her on the bed, fully scaled to the shoulder. He has not transformed but the dragon is at the edge of his skin.

[KEY CAMERA]
Low warm of the room. Held shot of Isolde's hand on Vael's wrist — already bruising. Cut close to her face — eyes closed, jaw set.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (between breaths): Don't transform.
VAEL: I won't.
ISOLDE: If you transform you can't hold my hand.
VAEL: I won't.
ISOLDE: Promise.
VAEL: Promised.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The midwife between the bed and the foot of the second frame.

[DIALOGUE]
MIDWIFE: Three more hours, my lady. Maybe four.
ISOLDE: Four.
MIDWIFE: My queen.
ISOLDE: Four.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She doesn't scream. She does not even breathe loud. She just holds the wrist.

Outside the chamber, in the corridor, the four guards have taken position with their backs to the door. They do not move. They do not speak. They do not look at anyone who walks the corridor.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
Two hours pass on screen in compressed cuts — three contractions, the midwife's hands, fresh water, Vael's scales rising and falling with each contraction, Isolde's eyes closed, the lamps burning lower.

At the fourth hour, the midwife lifts her head.

MIDWIFE: My lady. Now.

Hard Cut.

# THE OFFERING — EP40

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Private Chamber. Just Before Dawn.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The chamber. The pale beginning of dawn through the slit window. The midwife at the foot of the bed, both hands working. Vael at Isolde's shoulder, her hand still crushing his wrist.

The push.

Then the first cry.

A girl. Dark hair already. Wet. Loud.

The midwife wipes her clean fast. She sets her on Isolde's chest. The child stops crying once she is against her mother.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held close shot of Isolde looking down at the child. Slow tilt up to Vael — for the first time in the run of this story, his face is wet under his eyes. Not crying. But wet.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: She doesn't have scales.
VAEL: She has your hands.
ISOLDE: She has your jaw.
VAEL: Already.
ISOLDE: Already.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The child opens her eyes. Grey-green. Same as her mother. Same as Aldric.

She closes them again.

Vael leans down. He puts his forehead against Isolde's. He stays there for ten seconds.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: My bride.
ISOLDE: Your wife.
VAEL: My mate.
ISOLDE: Your mate.
VAEL: The mother of my heir.
ISOLDE: Your heir.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He lifts his face. He looks at the child on her chest. He puts one scaled finger — gently, scales soft — against the child's tiny palm. The child closes her fist on his finger. Strong.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
SUPER on the child: PRINCESS MIREILLE OF DRAKONIS — FIRSTBORN.

[END HOOK]
Outside the chamber window, on the eastern ridge, every dragon lifts its head at once. Thirty pairs of eyes open against the dawn. They do not roar. They just look east.

The wave-house head, in human form in the yard below, sees the eyes open. He walks slowly inside to inform the assembly that has gathered.

Hard Cut.

# THE OFFERING — EP41

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Throne Hall. Three Days Later. Day.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The throne hall. Two thrones on the dais — Vael's black-wood and Isolde's silver. A new small carved seat between them, low: the cradle.

In the cradle, the child. Three days old. Asleep.

Below the dais: every dragon-house head present at the keep. Wave-house, Maren, Tovan, river-grey, coastal cream — all in half-form or human, all on one knee.

Behind them: the two southern coast house elders, Isolde's mother's brother among them. He has not spoken yet in nine days. He stands at the back.

Behind them: Aldric in plain coat. He has not been invited up. He stands at the rear, where the doors close. He is allowed to look once.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the hall. Slow tracking the rows of kneeling heads. Cut to Aldric at the rear — eyes on the cradle. Cut to the cradle close.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: This is my heir.
WAVE-HOUSE HEAD: Heard, my lord.
VAEL: Her line is my line. Her name is hers. Her mother sat where my queen sits because she was chosen, not because she was born. The child is born and chosen both.
WAVE-HOUSE HEAD: Heard, my lord. Heard, my queen.
VAEL: Bow once. Then leave.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The five dragon-house heads bow once. They walk out.

Isolde's mother's brother does not move. He stays at the back. After the others leave, he walks the length of the hall slow. He stops at the foot of the dais. He goes to one knee.

[DIALOGUE]
UNCLE (low, first words in twelve years): My niece. My queen. Your mother saw you in a dream the year she died. She told me. I never said. I'm sorry.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde does not speak. She lifts the child from the cradle and walks down the dais to him. She puts the child in his arms for two seconds. He holds her like she is glass. The child does not cry.

Then Isolde takes the child back. She walks back up to the dais.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
Aldric at the rear of the hall. He has not moved. He has not knelt. Isolde lifts one finger to him. He walks out the back door, alone.

Hard Cut.

# THE OFFERING — EP43

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Outer Yard. Three Weeks. Morning.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The yard at morning. The child three weeks old, wrapped in pale silk, in Isolde's arms. Isolde in a wide-cut Drakonis bodice — recovering, not back to her old shape yet, no rush.

The two southern coast house elders at the keep steps, including her mother's brother. They have stayed. They are not leaving.

Aldric at the gate, alone, mounted. He has been at the south wing for three weeks. He has not been invited up. Today he leaves.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the yard. Cut to Aldric at the gate. Cut to Isolde at the keep steps with the child.

[DIALOGUE]
ALDRIC (across the yard, raised voice): My queen.
ISOLDE: Father.
ALDRIC: That's the first time.
ISOLDE: That's the last time too.
ALDRIC: I understand.
ISOLDE: Then leave.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Aldric rides out of the gate. He does not look back.

Vael at Isolde's shoulder, hand at the small of her back. Her mother's brother and the other elder at her other shoulder.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Done with him.
ISOLDE: Done.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She turns away from the gate. She walks back up the keep steps with the child. Vael behind her. Her uncle and the other elder follow at a respectful distance.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
SUPER as the gate closes: VEINE — CLOSED, KEPT AT THE GATE.

[END HOOK]
At the top of the keep steps, the child opens her eyes for half a second. Grey-green. The shadow of the eastern ridge passes once across her face — thirty dragons lifting their heads on the ridge in greeting.
