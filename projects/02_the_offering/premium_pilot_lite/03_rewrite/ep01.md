# THE OFFERING — EP01: THE OFFERING ARRIVES

S#1 — BLACK GATE / WASTE ROAD / DUSK

[VISUAL / ACTION]

A bronze chain drags across burnt cobble. The chain is heavy, royal, stamped with the human kingdom's seal. At the end of it: ISOLDE — slender, porcelain pale, dark hair loose down her back, in a long ivory gown beneath a white fur cloak, silver thread catching the last grey light. A single bright shape walking through a grey waste.

Behind her, the human escort halts at a black stone line cut into the road. None of them step past it. The chief envoy, HALDREN — older, grey-haired, gaunt in slate diplomat's robes — opens a small leather pouch and tips the kingdom's signet ring onto the ground beside her feet. Behind him, the kingdom standard hangs limp on its pole, half lowered.

A guard cuts the chain at her wrists. The bronze coils strike the cobble flat. The sound is bigger than it should be in the open air. ISOLDE does not look down at it.

Above them, set into the lintel of a great black gate, a row of ancient runes is carved into the basalt. As ISOLDE steps forward, one rune — only one — flickers, a slow heartbeat of red light. None of the humans see it.

Inside her white fur cloak, against the inner silk, an old silver pendant the size of a coin slips loose for a single beat. A tree-and-flame sigil. The same shape, smaller, as the rune above. She catches it without looking and tucks it back. None of the humans see this either.

[KEY CAMERA]
WIDE: Tiny bright ISOLDE walking a dark cobble road toward an enormous black gate.
INSERT: The chain ends snapped open at her wrists. Bronze on stone.
MACRO: Old rune in basalt above the gate, one heartbeat of red.
MACRO: Silver pendant slipping inside her cloak, tree-and-flame sigil.
CLOSE: ISOLDE's face. Not afraid. Not pleading.

[DIALOGUE]
HALDREN: Walk inside. No turning back.
ISOLDE: I was told to walk.
HALDREN: Then walk.

S#2 — BLACK GATE / THRESHOLD / CONTINUOUS

[VISUAL / ACTION]

The gate is half open. Beyond it: black air, the smell of old fire, the deep low sound of something larger than a man breathing in the dark. ISOLDE stops on the threshold. Behind her, the human escort is already retreating up the waste road. They do not look back. Halfway up the slope, HALDREN does. Only HALDREN.

From inside the gate, a hand emerges into the cold grey light. Only a hand — large, the back of it patched in black, ridged scales running across the knuckles. The scales are not jewelry. They are part of him. Behind the hand, a tall shape held in the gate's darkness. A black leather greatcoat. A throat. The face stays in shadow.

The scaled hand stops a finger's length from the side of ISOLDE's jaw. Not yet touching. Around them, the cobble seems quieter, as if the air itself has narrowed.

[KEY CAMERA]
CLOSE: The scaled hand entering grey daylight. Knuckles black, polished, alive.
OVER ISOLDE'S SHOULDER: The half-open gate. Shape in the dark.
INSERT: HALDREN halfway up the slope, glancing back once.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (from the dark): Look up. At me.

S#3 — INNER COURT / FIRST TOUCH / CONTINUOUS

[VISUAL / ACTION]

ISOLDE crosses the threshold. The gate shuts behind her without a hand on it. The court inside is black basalt, ringed with low torches, ribbed with the bones of a previous burning that no one bothered to scrape off. Smoke clings to the columns.

VAEL steps out of the shadow now. Tall — well past her — broad, dark-haired loose to the shoulder, grey eyes, a hard jaw, a black leather greatcoat over a high collar. The scales on the back of his hand catch a torch and answer it: a soft black sheen. He does not bow. He does not greet her.

He reaches for her right hand. The movement is calm and final. His scaled fingers turn her wrist inside up, lifting it toward the torchlight as if checking a weight. He brushes his thumb once across the soft skin over her pulse.

The skin under his thumb glows. A faint pulse of red, the same heartbeat as the rune on the gate. Once. Twice. Then dark.

ISOLDE's breath catches for the first time. She does not pull the hand back.

In the high black arch above them, the same rune answers from the basalt. Audience-only — she does not see it. He does.

INSERT: His scaled fingers turning ISOLDE's wrist inside up.
MACRO: Red glow under his thumb, twice, on the underside of her wrist.
MACRO: The rune above the arch answering — same heartbeat.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Closer.
ISOLDE: I am close.
VAEL: Closer than that.

S#4 — INNER COURT / NAMING / CONTINUOUS

[VISUAL / ACTION]

He keeps her wrist. He does not pull her — he keeps her, the difference is in the weight of his hand. KIRAN — a lean dragon-blood officer in dark scaled armor, silver at the shoulder — has come up behind VAEL, holding a folded black silk in both hands. He waits without speaking.

VAEL turns ISOLDE's wrist a little, looking at the underside, then up at her face. He speaks low, not for anyone else in the keep.

VAEL: They sent you as an offering.
ISOLDE (level): They did.
VAEL: They will not call you that again.

He releases her wrist. The skin where his thumb was is faintly red, still. He nods once at KIRAN. KIRAN bows to her — a short, exact bow — and steps aside, holding the folded black silk out.

VAEL: The clothes they sent you in burn. Tonight. Where they can see.

ISOLDE looks at the white fur cloak on her shoulders. She does not flinch. She does not argue.

ISOLDE: You wanted me changed.
VAEL: I want them to see you changed.

He turns toward the inner hall. Behind him, a wide doorway opens onto another, deeper space — black silk, torches, the low sound of voices. The court of the keep. People waiting.

[DIALOGUE]

ISOLDE: You wanted me changed.
VAEL: I want them to see you changed.

[END HOOK]
He walks ahead of her into the deeper hall. She does not follow yet. The pendant at her bodice slips a quarter inch and rests. Above her, the rune fades back into stone.
