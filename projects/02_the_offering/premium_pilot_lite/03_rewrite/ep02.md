# THE OFFERING — EP02: VAEL'S CHOICE

S#1 — KEEP CHAMBER / DRESSING / NIGHT

[VISUAL / ACTION]

A high black room walled in basalt. A standing iron mirror. Two silent dragon-blood maids in plain charcoal smocks. The white fur cloak and the ivory gown lie folded on a low stone bench, already separated, already not hers anymore.

ISOLDE stands in a thin shift, bare-armed, the small silver pendant resting between her collarbones. The mark on the inside of her right wrist — a thumb-shaped faint red — has not gone away since the gate.

The maids bring up the new gown. Heavy black silk, fitted close at the ribs. A high lace collar in white. A narrow silver chain laid flat down the bodice. Tiny pearl beads stitched along the seams at the shoulders. Cuff edges in white. Nothing low cut. Nothing soft.

They dress her. They do not speak. As they fit the bodice tight at her ribs, one maid lifts the silver pendant, lifts the cloak's inner silk, and threads the pendant chain into the inner lining of the new bodice. The pendant slides into a small hidden seam against her ribs. Hidden, not removed.

In the mirror: a girl who arrived in white and is leaving the room in black. Pale skin. White at the throat. Silver and pearl threading the black, like winter through ash. She does not yet look like a bride. She looks like a name being chosen.

[KEY CAMERA]
WIDE: The two gowns lying on the stone bench — ivory folded behind, black gown lifted up.
INSERT: Maid's hands lacing the silver chain flat down the bodice, pearls along the shoulder seams.
MACRO: Maid's hand tucking the silver pendant into the inner seam of the new bodice. The sigil disappearing inside the silk.
CLOSE: ISOLDE's face in the iron mirror. She does not smile. She does not flinch.

[DIALOGUE]
MAID 1 (low): He wants the throat covered. The wrist visible.
ISOLDE: The wrist.
MAID 2: He said the wrist.

S#2 — GREAT HALL / DOORWAY / NIGHT

[VISUAL / ACTION]

Twin doors of black iron part. The keep's great hall is full: dragon-blood officers along the walls in dark scale; the bride-candidates of the lesser human houses near the front, ELARA at their head — gold hair, gold thread, gold gown, cold mouth; the human envoy HALDREN at a side dais, watching; KIRAN stationed at the foot of the high seat.

At the head of the hall: a high black seat on a single step. Beside it, on the same step, an empty matching seat. VAEL sits in the first. Dark coat. Hands loose. He has been waiting.

ISOLDE steps into the doorway. The hall sees her. ELARA sees her. HALDREN sees her. The room takes one slow breath.

She walks the length of the hall. The pearls along her shoulder seams flicker at every step. Her wrist mark catches torchlight once, twice — the same heartbeat as last night.

ELARA's mouth tightens. She is in gold. The girl walking past her is in black with white at the throat. The hall has just understood which one was chosen first.

INSERT: ELARA's gold-gloved hand closing slowly on the back of a chair.
CLOSE: VAEL watching her cross the hall. He doesn't smile. He doesn't move.

[DIALOGUE]
ELARA (under her breath, to a maid): Pretty. For an offering.

S#3 — GREAT HALL / THE EMPTY SEAT / CONTINUOUS

[VISUAL / ACTION]

ISOLDE reaches the foot of the high step. VAEL does not rise. He lifts one hand and taps the scaled back of his knuckles, twice, on the armrest of the empty matching seat beside him. Two quiet sounds. The whole hall hears them.

ELARA's voice from below the step — sharper, public now.

ELARA: That seat is not for you.

VAEL does not look at ELARA. He looks at ISOLDE. He stands. The black coat falls open at his hip. He steps down one step. He puts a wide hand at the small of ISOLDE's back. The other hand takes her by the side, just under the ribs.

He lifts her — not violently, but completely — up that one step and sets her into the matching seat. Her boot heels strike the basalt at the same time as his. Her bodice catches against his coat for a breath and lets go.

He does not move away. He stands beside her, one scaled hand resting on the back of her seat, his fingers turned inward so the tips touch the side of her hair. Not seizing — settling.

He looks past ISOLDE, not at ELARA but at the whole front rank of bride-candidates.

VAEL: It is now.

S#4 — GREAT HALL / HAIR / NIGHT

[VISUAL / ACTION]

The hall is still standing because no one has been told to sit. VAEL takes a single strand of ISOLDE's hair between two scaled fingertips and slowly runs his hand down the length of it, once, to the small of her back. The hall watches him do it. He is not hurried.

ISOLDE does not flinch and does not lean in. She keeps her shoulders square, her hands flat on the armrests, breathing carefully through her nose. The mark on the inside of her right wrist — visible because the gown leaves the wrist bare — flushes red once. Audience-only: the small rune embedded in the back of his high seat, just behind her shoulder, flickers once at the same beat.

HALDREN, at the side dais, takes a half step back. He does not bow. Not yet. But his shoulders have lowered.

ELARA stares straight ahead. She does not sit. She has not been told to sit.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (without looking up): Sit.

The hall sits. ELARA sits last.

[END HOOK]
VAEL leans down a fraction, his mouth close enough to ISOLDE's ear that only she hears it. His scaled hand has not left her hair.

VAEL (only to her): No one calls you what they called you in the road. Not in this hall.
