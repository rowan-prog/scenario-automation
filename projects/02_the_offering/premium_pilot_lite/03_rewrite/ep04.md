# THE OFFERING — EP04: THE BANNER

S#1 — COUNCIL CHAMBER / KEEP / MORNING

[VISUAL / ACTION]

A long black basalt table. Iron sconces. The keep's council room. KIRAN stands at one end. Two dragon-blood officers along the wall.

At the far end, HALDREN with three human envoys — sashes in slate and gold, the kingdom seal high at the shoulder. Between them on the table: a folded standard of the human kingdom. Red field, gold sun, the same seal that was on the chain at the gate, and on the assassin's dagger pommel last night.

ISOLDE stands beside the long table, half a pace behind VAEL's empty chair. She is back in the black silk gown with the silver chain down the bodice. The pearls at her shoulder seams. The small crescent of teeth marks under her ear is half-hidden under her loose hair — visible only if you know to look. Her right wrist sleeve is short. The thumb-print mark on her inner wrist is still there from last night, darker now.

VAEL enters from the side door, late on purpose. He does not greet the envoys. He does not sit. He walks the length of the table to where the folded standard is. He picks it up — the whole banner, pole and all — and holds it loosely in his scaled right hand.

VAEL: This came in with you. Yesterday.
HALDREN'S COMPANION: It is the kingdom's banner. As is required.
VAEL: A blade came in with you. Last night.

The room holds its breath.

S#2 — COUNCIL CHAMBER / BURN / CONTINUOUS

[VISUAL / ACTION]

VAEL turns the banner sideways so the gold sun catches the morning light. The scales on the back of his right hand darken further — the line of black moves up to his wrist, and the bare skin at the inside of his palm warms, then glows. A slow dull red. Then deeper. A smoldering vein in the palm.

He grips the banner cloth in that palm. The red field of the kingdom catches at the edge of his hand. A line of black bloom opens in the silk. Smoke. Then fire — slow, lazy, contained, not spreading to the room. The banner burns down the length of the cloth in his fist.

HALDREN does not move. His companion does — a half step back. KIRAN does not move. ISOLDE does not move. The pole falls. The cloth is gone before the pole hits stone.

VAEL drops the smoking pole onto the long basalt table between them. The seal on the pole is intact. The cloth — the kingdom's red — is ash on his palm.

VAEL: They will not call her that again.

HALDREN looks at him. He does not ask what name. He looks at ISOLDE for one moment. Then back to VAEL.

HALDREN: You burn a kingdom standard.
VAEL: I burn the part that called her an offering.

S#3 — UPPER GALLERY / AFTER THE COUNCIL / DAY

[VISUAL / ACTION]

A stone arcade above the great hall. Tall narrow windows. The dragon-blood officers have left. HALDREN's envoys have left. KIRAN remains at the far end of the gallery, out of earshot, deliberately turned away.

ISOLDE walks the length of the gallery and stops at one of the windows. The waste road from yesterday is just visible from here — a thin grey thread out to the horizon. She lifts her right hand into the morning light and turns it. The thumb-print mark on her inner wrist is darker than yesterday. She does not know yet that it deepened under his mouth last night.

VAEL crosses the gallery behind her. He does not speak first. He stops at her shoulder, one half step behind. The ember in his palm has cooled. The scales on the back of his hand are still darker than they were a week ago.

He reaches forward and takes her right wrist again, gently this time, and lays it over the windowsill so the underside catches the daylight. His scaled fingers settle around her forearm. His thumb hovers over the thumb-shaped mark.

VAEL: Do not show this to them.
ISOLDE: What is it.

He does not answer with a word. He lays his thumb over the mark — exactly over it — and presses. The red shape under his thumb warms. A second pulse of red traces from the print, up the inside of her wrist toward the bend of her elbow, in a thin slow line, like ink drawn through skin by an unseen pen. It stops halfway up her forearm. It cools. It stays — a thin red trace, fresh, not bleeding.

S#4 — UPPER GALLERY / NAMING THE TRACE / CONTINUOUS

[VISUAL / ACTION]

VAEL keeps her wrist on the sill. The red trace is settling — already lighter than it was a minute ago, but still there. The new line of it is small enough to be hidden by a sleeve. The first thumb print remains, deeper than ever.

He turns her hand over and folds her fingers closed across the inside of her wrist himself, so the marks are hidden in her own fist. He does not let go right away. His scaled thumb passes once across the back of her hand. The crescent of teeth marks under her ear, still half hidden by her loose hair, faces the morning sun for one beat.

He speaks low. Not the council voice. The other one.

VAEL: I do not let them have what is mine.

ISOLDE looks down at her closed fist. She does not unclench it. She does not pull her hand from his.

ISOLDE: What did you do to my wrist.
VAEL: Nothing they can undo.

He releases her hand. She keeps it closed.

[END HOOK]
He turns and walks back down the gallery. KIRAN falls in behind him at the far arch. ISOLDE stays at the window, her right fist still closed, the thin red trace under the skin of her forearm pulsing once, alone, where only she can feel it.
