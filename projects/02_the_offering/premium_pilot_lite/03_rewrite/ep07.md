# THE OFFERING — EP07: HOURGLASS AND KNEE

S#1 — COUNCIL CHAMBER / KEEP / MORNING

[VISUAL / ACTION]

The long black basalt table. The morning's grey light. Eight dragon-blood officers in dark scaled armor, two on each side and four standing at the wall. KIRAN at the head of the table opposite VAEL's high-backed chair.

On the table, set between the officers: an enormous hourglass — black iron frame, twin glass bulbs the size of a man's chest, sand the color of red copper. The upper bulb is more than half full. The sand is running. The grains striking the lower bulb are loud in the basalt room.

A second item on the table beside it: a small folded square of red parchment. Sealed in black wax with a kingdom mark — not the same kingdom mark as on the first envoy's banner. A different sigil. Audience-only: this is ALDRIC's personal seal, not the kingdom's open seal.

VAEL stands at his chair. He does not sit. He looks at the hourglass, not at the parchment.

KIRAN: They want her face at the gate by sundown. Both envoys.
VAEL: The first envoy lowered his head to her last night.
KIRAN: This is from a different mouth.

S#2 — COUNCIL CHAMBER / SHE IS BROUGHT / CONTINUOUS

[VISUAL / ACTION]

The side door opens. ISOLDE enters between two dragon-blood officers — not held, accompanied. She is in the black silk gown with the silver chain down the bodice. The two crescents on her throat are uncovered today. The red trace on her right forearm is now visible above the cuff line if her sleeve rides up — and it does, when she walks.

She stops three paces from VAEL's chair. The officers step back to the wall. She is alone between him and the hourglass.

She looks at the hourglass first. Then at the red parchment. She does not ask what it is.

VAEL: Come here.

She does. She crosses the three paces. He does not move toward her. He turns to his chair — high-backed, wide-armed — and sits in it. Then he lifts one scaled hand from the armrest and holds it out, palm up, between his knees.

VAEL: Here.

The officers along the wall do not move. KIRAN does not move. The basalt room is breathing very quietly. ISOLDE goes to the front of the chair and stops. He reaches forward, takes her at the waist with both hands, and draws her down. Not into his lap from the side. Across his lap, facing slightly outward, so her back is half along his chest and her legs cross his lap to one side. Her silk gown hisses against his coat once.

S#3 — COUNCIL CHAMBER / SEATED ACROSS HIM / CONTINUOUS

[VISUAL / ACTION]

He settles his right scaled hand on her waist. His left hand comes up to the side of her neck — at the crescent he made the second night — and his thumb rests there, on top of his own teeth marks, calmly, in front of every officer in the room.

ISOLDE's spine is straight. She does not shrink. She does not curl in. She does not lean away from him either. Her hands rest in her own lap, folded.

His scaled right hand slides up her bodice from her waist along the silver chain. He stops at her shoulder. He picks up the long length of her loose dark hair where it falls down her back and gathers it once in his hand — not a pull, a hold. He keeps the hair in his hand for as long as he wants to.

KIRAN turns one of the dragon-blood officers fully to face the wall. The officer faces the wall. KIRAN turns back to face VAEL. He has done this neatly enough that it is a courtesy, not a rebuke. KIRAN does not turn away himself. He continues to look at VAEL's face, not at ISOLDE.

VAEL (to the table, not to her): Read the parchment.

KIRAN reads it. He keeps his voice level. The parchment is short.

KIRAN: "Return the human girl by sundown. By any name. By any condition. Her body is required for the kingdom's accounting."

The basalt room does not breathe.

VAEL's scaled hand at ISOLDE's waist tightens half a degree. His left thumb at her throat presses lightly into the crescent. The red trace on her right forearm — visible because her sleeve has fallen back across his thigh — flushes the same beat as his hand. The scales on the back of his hand pulse to match. They are in time.

VAEL: They want her body.
KIRAN: Yes.
VAEL: I want them to come closer to see what they are asking for.

S#4 — COUNCIL CHAMBER / SHADOWS ON THE RIDGE / CONTINUOUS

[VISUAL / ACTION]

VAEL does not stand. He keeps ISOLDE across his lap. Across the chamber, the tall slit windows behind KIRAN show a strip of the basalt ridge beyond the keep. Something passes across the windows. Then again. Then a third time.

Through the windows: a long low shape moves across the ridge above the keep, then another, then another. Wings folded. Three of them. Then five. Then more — out of frame. Their bodies are not shown — only their long shadows passing along the ridge wall, dark on dark, larger than anything on the basalt should be. The hourglass on the table answers with a small tremor of red sand.

KIRAN: Lord. The riders are at the ridge.
KIRAN: All of them.
VAEL: Let them come.

He turns his head a fraction toward ISOLDE. He does not move his right hand from her waist. His left thumb stays at her throat. He lowers his voice for only her.

VAEL: When they call you, they will call you by what they sent you as. Do not answer.
ISOLDE: I will not answer.
VAEL: Look at me. Only me.
ISOLDE: I see you.

The red trace on her right forearm reaches a fraction higher up her arm. The scales on the back of his right hand at her waist deepen another shade.

[END HOOK]
The hourglass tilts once on its iron frame as another wing-shadow crosses the ridge. ISOLDE — across his lap, his thumb at her throat, his hand on her waist, her wrist bare on his thigh — keeps her eyes on his face. Only his face.
