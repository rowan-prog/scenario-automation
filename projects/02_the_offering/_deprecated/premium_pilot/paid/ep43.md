# THE OFFERING — EP43: I Am Not

S#1 — MOUNTAIN BASE / DUSK (THREE DAYS LATER)

[Visual]
The force at the base of the mountain. The shrine is at the top — visible faintly as a spire. The dark robe is up there. He has had three days to prepare.

KIRAN: The pass is too steep for the drakes to land near the shrine. We climb on foot the last part.
ISOLDE: How long.
KIRAN: Two hours.
COURT-MAGUS: He will have wards.
ISOLDE: He always has wards.
KIRAN: My queen. The wider valley drakes will hold the lower mountain. Twelve from the keep climb with us.
ISOLDE: Good.

She looks at the granddaughter on GROVE KEEPER's back.

ISOLDE: She stays at base.
GROVE KEEPER: No. She is line. She comes.
ISOLDE: She is three.
GROVE KEEPER: She is line.

ISOLDE looks at the girl. The girl looks back.

ISOLDE: She comes.

S#2 — MOUNTAIN PASS / LATE EVENING

[Visual]
The climb. Twenty-five carriers + ISOLDE + VAEL + KIRAN + COURT-MAGUS + the granddaughter. The pass is narrow. They move slow, careful. Carrow and the keep drakes circle low — escort but cannot land yet.

About halfway up, the dark robe's wards begin — dark stones along the path. The COURT-MAGUS passes his hand over each. The wards die.

COURT-MAGUS: He left these. He did not have time to fortify.
ISOLDE: Then we are faster than he expected.
COURT-MAGUS: Yes.

S#3 — MOUNTAIN SHRINE / NIGHT

[Visual]
They reach the shrine. An ancient stone structure — three pillars and an open altar at the center. The dark robe stands at the altar — he has built his counter-pole here. Around him, a circle of eight new acolytes (he has recruited more).

ISOLDE walks forward — staff in hand, crown on her head. The twenty-five carriers spread around her in a wide circle. The keep drakes land on the surrounding cliff edges.

DARK ROBE: You brought all of them.
ISOLDE: I brought all of us.
DARK ROBE: The line will not save you.
ISOLDE: The line is here.
DARK ROBE: I have one more piece. From your western grove.
ISOLDE: Then you fall now.

She raises the staff. The carriers raise their hands. Twenty-five gold threads extend from each carrier — joining at ISOLDE, forming a single thick gold cord.

The dark robe raises his hands. He pulls the dead piece from his cloak. He holds it forward.

DARK ROBE: This was the last queen before yours. Before your mother. Before your grandmother. The line of the queen who started the kingdom — buried for two hundred years. I have it.

He drives the piece into the counter-pole. The counter-pole flares dark — much darker than at the temple or the hill fort. This piece is more powerful.

S#4 — MOUNTAIN SHRINE / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
The dark counter-pole pulses. The dark robe chants. The eight acolytes chant. The dark light spreads — toward ISOLDE.

The granddaughter — three years old — steps out from behind GROVE KEEPER. She walks toward ISOLDE. Carriers gasp. GROVE KEEPER calls but does not move to stop.

The granddaughter takes ISOLDE's free hand — the one without the staff.

GRANDDAUGHTER: Auntie. I came.
ISOLDE: I see you.
GRANDDAUGHTER: He says you are the offering.
ISOLDE: I was.
GRANDDAUGHTER: Then he is wrong.

The girl looks at the dark robe.

GRANDDAUGHTER: She is not the offering.

The girl's small wrist mark — the line mark — flares bright. The line through her — the youngest carrier — adds her strength to ISOLDE's cord.

ISOLDE looks at the dark robe. Her voice is clear and direct. Not minimal. Not declarative. Direct. Personal.

ISOLDE: I am not your offering.

The line tree at the keep — far away — pulses bright in answer.

ISOLDE: I never was.

She speaks the second sentence even louder. Both sentences land in the dark robe's counter-pole. The counter-pole flickers. The eight acolytes' chants stumble.

ISOLDE drives the staff forward — into the dark counter-pole.

Gold against dark. The whole shrine fills with gold light.
