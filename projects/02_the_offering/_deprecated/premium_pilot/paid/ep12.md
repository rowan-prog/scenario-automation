# THE OFFERING — EP12: We Sleep

S#1 — VAEL'S KEEP / VAEL'S WING / EVENING

[Visual]
The bedchamber. Larger now — moved furniture, low table for war maps in the corner. ISOLDE is dressed in a darker gown — high-collared, but the sleeves are open at the wrist, the three left-wrist marks visible. The new flame-curve on her right collarbone shows above the collar.

VAEL is at the corner table, reading dispatch reports. His scaled hand traces the road on the map. He looks up when she enters. He does not return to the report.

ISOLDE: You said sleep.
VAEL: I said we sleep. Not this hour.
ISOLDE: When.
VAEL: After the first column reaches the river.
ISOLDE: That is tomorrow.
VAEL: Yes.

She crosses to him. She stops at the corner table. Her hand goes to the map — flat, palm down, across the road line.

ISOLDE: Stop them at the river.
VAEL: I plan to.
ISOLDE: Without me at the rampart.
VAEL: With you in the keep.
ISOLDE: They will say you hid me.
VAEL: They will say what they say.

He takes her wrist — gently — and turns her hand off the map. He brings her hand to the side of his face. He holds it there.

VAEL: I have not had a mate for two hundred years.
ISOLDE: That long.
VAEL: Yes.
ISOLDE: Then four days is not very long.

S#2 — VAEL'S KEEP / VAEL'S WING / NIGHT

[Visual]
The bed. They have stopped speaking. VAEL has her gown down to her waist — the bite mark on her right collarbone now matched by his mouth, kissing it slowly. His scaled hand spreads flat across her back — fingers wide. His gauntleted hand at her ribs.

She arches into him. Her own hand goes to the scales of his hand at her back — fingers between the scales. The scaled hand presses harder.

She breaks. Half-inch. Her breath at his ear.

ISOLDE: Not the first night.
VAEL: No.
ISOLDE: But.
VAEL: But this.

He bends his head. His mouth at her left wrist — at the stacked marks + flame-curve. He licks once. The flame-curve flares. Her body responds — she pushes against him.

He breaks his mouth from her wrist. He looks at her.

VAEL: When we sleep — really sleep — it will not be like this.
ISOLDE: How then.
VAEL: Different.

S#3 — VAEL'S KEEP / RAMPART / DEEP NIGHT

[Visual]
ISOLDE alone on the rampart. Dressed again, dark cloak over the gown. She watches the road. The dust column has stopped — the brother's army has made camp. The five fires are scattered now — many fires. Forty-eight of them, by KIRAN's count.

Carrow lands beside her, silent. ISOLDE does not turn.

ISOLDE: He is asleep.
CARROW (low chuff, recognizable as agreement)
ISOLDE: He has not slept in days.

She reaches into the lining of her cloak — the place where the pendant has lived since EP1. She brings the pendant out. She looks at it. The sigil on it has dimmed in the days since EP3 — the sigil pulse, audience-only, no longer matches the rune above the gate as cleanly as it did.

ISOLDE: Why.

Carrow noses at the pendant. Carrow's gold trace at the jaw — the same gold as ISOLDE's wrist marks — touches the pendant. The pendant's sigil pulses once, bright. Then fades again.

ISOLDE: There is something.

She closes her hand on the pendant. She puts it back in the lining.

S#4 — KINGDOM ROAD / BROTHER'S CAMP / DEEP NIGHT

[Visual]
A larger camp than expected. Many tents. A central tent — larger, dark-colored, no banner. Inside: a man at a table. Older than VAEL would be in human years but his face is unlined — like he has held youth. His name is unspoken. He reads a letter sealed in the same blacker seal as the second writ at EP6.

He smiles. Just slightly.

He sets the letter down. He lifts a small object from a velvet cloth — a stone amulet, dark, the size of a thumbprint. He turns it. On its underside: the same sigil as ISOLDE's mother's pendant. The same crest as on the assassin's collar.

He closes his hand on the amulet.

BROTHER: She does not know it yet.
