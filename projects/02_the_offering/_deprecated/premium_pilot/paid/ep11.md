# THE OFFERING — EP11: Four Days

S#1 — VALLEY ROAD / NIGHT (FAR FROM KEEP)

[Visual]
A small column of scouts — six men, mounted, no banner. They wear dark leather with bone clasps. The lead scout — older, scarred face — raises a hand. They stop. He looks at the sky.

Above them, a single drake passes — slow, deliberate. A patrol. The lead scout watches it. He does not draw his bow.

LEAD SCOUT: Mark it.
SCOUT 2: Marked.
LEAD SCOUT: One.
SCOUT 2: They sent one.
LEAD SCOUT: They sent one for now.

The drake passes. The scouts move on.

S#2 — VAEL'S KEEP / VAEL'S WING / DAWN

[Visual]
ISOLDE asleep in the wide bed. VAEL sits at the edge of the bed, dressed, scaled hand resting on the bedpost. He is watching her. The five marks visible — wrist marks on her left arm extended on the coverlet, collarbone marks at her throat where the gown has shifted in sleep.

She wakes. Sees him. Does not move.

ISOLDE: How long.
VAEL: Three hours since you slept.
ISOLDE: Watching me.
VAEL: Yes.
ISOLDE: Why.
VAEL: Because in four days I will not.

She reaches up. Her fingers touch his scaled hand on the bedpost. He turns his hand. He takes hers. He brings her hand to his mouth. Kisses her knuckles. Once. Slowly.

S#3 — VAEL'S KEEP / INNER COURTYARD / MORNING

[Visual]
The twelve drakes are awake. KIRAN moves among them with ISOLDE — teaching her positions, names, signals. Twelve drakes have twelve names. ISOLDE repeats them. She lays her hand on each drake's jaw in turn. Each closes its eyes once. Recognition.

The last drake — slightly smaller than the others, scales darker — does not close its eyes. It looks at ISOLDE longer than the others.

KIRAN: That one is the youngest.
ISOLDE: Her name.
KIRAN: We have not named her.
ISOLDE: Why.
KIRAN: We wait for the mate to name the youngest.

ISOLDE looks at the young drake. She raises her marked left hand. She lays it flat on the side of the young drake's jaw. The drake leans into her palm. Audience-only: the gold of ISOLDE's wrist marks flares once, and a thin trace of the same gold appears in the young drake's scales — at the jaw, where her palm rests.

ISOLDE: Carrow.

KIRAN looks at her. KIRAN bows half-shallow. The young drake — now Carrow — closes her eyes and presses her snout to ISOLDE's shoulder.

S#4 — VAEL'S KEEP / RAMPART / MIDDAY

[Visual]
VAEL on the rampart. The captain of the watch beside him. They look at the southern road. The five fires from EP10 are gone — replaced by a single column of dust on the horizon. Closer. Faster than expected.

VAEL: They moved at night.
WATCH CAPTAIN: Two days, not four.
VAEL: Send Carrow.

The watch captain looks at him.

VAEL: She has a name now. Send her.

The watch captain bows. He raises a horn — a long curved bone horn — and blows once. From below, a young drake — darker than the others — lifts off the courtyard. Carrow flies low and fast across the valley toward the dust column.

ISOLDE arrives at the rampart. She watches Carrow fly.

ISOLDE: What is she doing.
VAEL: Counting.
ISOLDE: Counting what.
VAEL: How many actually mounted. How many trained.

Carrow circles the dust column once — far enough that arrow range cannot reach. She turns and flies back.

S#5 — VAEL'S KEEP / RAMPART / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
Carrow lands on the rampart beside ISOLDE. She is breathing hard. ISOLDE lays her hand on Carrow's jaw — Carrow leans in immediately.

KIRAN climbs up to join them. He reads Carrow.

KIRAN: Two hundred and seventy mounted. Twelve in dark leather with bone clasps — scouts already returned, this is the main body. Forty-eight in lighter armor — drake-trained. The rest — siege equipment, pack horses, support.
VAEL: Two hundred and seventy.
KIRAN: Forty-eight trained.
VAEL: Twelve drakes against forty-eight trained.
KIRAN: It was a count, my lord.

VAEL turns to ISOLDE.

VAEL: Two nights.
ISOLDE: Then.
VAEL: We sleep.
