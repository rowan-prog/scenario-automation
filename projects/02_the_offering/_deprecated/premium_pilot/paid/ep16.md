# THE OFFERING — EP16: Returned

S#1 — VAEL'S KEEP / OUTER GATE / MORNING

[Visual]
A small rider — alone, hood up — approaches the keep gate. ELARA. She has returned. She dismounts. She does not enter — she waits at the threshold. KIRAN watches from above. He recognizes her.

ELARA (raising her voice to the wall): I bring news for the mate.
KIRAN (from above): What news.
ELARA: The brother. I saw his camp on my way home. I turned back.

KIRAN orders the gate opened. ELARA enters.

S#2 — VAEL'S KEEP / ONYX HALL / DAIS / MIDDAY

[Visual]
ELARA at the foot of the dais. ISOLDE on the high seat (since EP8). VAEL standing behind it. ELARA is not in court dress — travel clothes, road dust.

ELARA: I saw their camp two nights ago. They had a fire pattern.
ISOLDE: Pattern.
ELARA: I have seen the pattern before.
VAEL: Where.
ELARA: My grandmother's house. She kept a tapestry. It had the same shape.
ISOLDE: Whose house was your grandmother.
ELARA: Velden — the old line. Before my grandmother married into my family, she was old line.
ISOLDE: Then you are.
ELARA: Distantly. Not close enough for the gift.

ISOLDE looks at her. ELARA is younger than ISOLDE.

ISOLDE: Why did you turn back.
ELARA: Because I was sent to be a bride and I was not chosen. But if the brother takes the keep — I become useful again. To them.
ISOLDE: As a bride for him.
ELARA: Or as a hostage. Either way I would not survive it.
VAEL: Why bring it to us.
ELARA: Because you let me leave.

S#3 — VAEL'S KEEP / WAR ROOM / AFTERNOON

[Visual]
ELARA draws the fire pattern on parchment. It is the same sigil — exactly — as ISOLDE's pendant. HALDREN sees it. HALDREN tenses.

HALDREN: That is the old crown.
KIRAN: Not the kingdom crown.
HALDREN: No. The crown before the kingdom. The one my king's grandfather took from his own brother.
ISOLDE: The line was always there.
HALDREN: The line was always there.
VAEL: And the brother — your king's brother — is using it as a signal.
HALDREN: He is calling the line.
ISOLDE: There are others.
HALDREN: There are.
ELARA: Some of them are with the brother now.
KIRAN: Are they trained.
ELARA: Some.

S#4 — VAEL'S KEEP / RAMPART / NIGHT

[Visual]
ISOLDE on the rampart. VAEL behind her. The brother's camp visible — fires still arranged in the sigil pattern. ELARA stands at a distance, with KIRAN.

VAEL (low, to ISOLDE only): He wants you.
ISOLDE: Yes.
VAEL: For the line.
ISOLDE: Yes.
VAEL: He cannot have you.
ISOLDE: I know.

A long silence. Then she turns to him.

ISOLDE: But he can have what is in my mother's box.
VAEL: The stone.
ISOLDE: Yes.
VAEL: We hide it.
ISOLDE: We use it.
VAEL: How.
ISOLDE: I do not yet know.

VAEL raises his scaled hand. He lays it flat on her marked wrist. The three crescents and the flame-curve all glow brighter together.

VAEL: We will know.
