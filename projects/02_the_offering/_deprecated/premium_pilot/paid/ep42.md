# THE OFFERING — EP42: Three Words

S#1 — VAEL'S KEEP / LINE TREE COURTYARD / DAWN

[Visual]
ISOLDE alone at the line tree before anyone else is up. She holds the staff. She places her hand on the tree's bark. The tree pulses with her. She closes her eyes — listening to the line.

ISOLDE (quiet, to the tree alone): Where is he.

A pause. The tree begins to glow — slow. Threads radiate. They search.

Then one thread brightens — west. A different west than before. A high mountain.

ISOLDE: There.
ELARA (entering): My queen.
ISOLDE: He is at a mountain shrine in the west. Three days by drake.
ELARA: We mount today.
ISOLDE: We mount today.

S#2 — VAEL'S KEEP / VAEL'S WING / MORNING

[Visual]
VAEL packs his travel gear. ISOLDE watches him.

ISOLDE: This is the last fight.
VAEL: Yes.
ISOLDE: What happens after.
VAEL: We come home. We rest. We rule.
ISOLDE: Both.
VAEL: Both.
ISOLDE: I have not been to Velden since the brief days last week.
VAEL: We will go. After.
ISOLDE: As queen.
VAEL: As queen.

He stops packing. He crosses to her.

VAEL: I want to say something.
ISOLDE: Say it.
VAEL: It will be the only time I say it.
ISOLDE: Why only.
VAEL: Because I am a dragon. We do not say words we mean. We say them once. To make them real.
ISOLDE: Vael.
VAEL: I love you.

A long silence.

ISOLDE: I love you.
VAEL: That is the only time you say it.
ISOLDE: Yes.
VAEL: Then we have both said it.
ISOLDE: We have.

He pulls her to him. He holds her — long. He does not kiss her.

S#3 — VAEL'S KEEP / INNER COURTYARD / MIDDAY

[Visual]
The assembled force prepares for departure. ISOLDE in traveling cloak — Velden crown on her head this time. The line carriers (those who can ride) on drakes. Twenty-five total. BROTHER, KIRAN, HALDREN, COURT-MAGUS, ELARA, GROVE KEEPER, GRANDDAUGHTER (on the GROVE KEEPER's drake).

LEAD SCOUT and the brother's army stay to hold Velden — Vael's keep — and the line tree.

ISOLDE: We return when he is dead.
LEAD SCOUT: My queen.
ISOLDE: Or we do not.
LEAD SCOUT: Then we hold for you regardless.
ISOLDE: Yes.

She mounts Carrow. VAEL mounts the fifth drake. The carriers mount. The wider valley drakes — forty-eight — lift off in formation overhead.

S#4 — WESTERN SKY / LATE AFTERNOON

[Visual]
The force in the air. The high mountain visible far ahead — snow-capped, sharp, ancient. As they fly, Carrow's gold trace at the jaw pulses brighter. The line tree at the keep glows visibly even from this distance — they can feel it.

ISOLDE looks at VAEL on his drake parallel to hers. He looks at her. They do not speak. They have already said what they needed to say.

Carrow flies on toward the mountain.
