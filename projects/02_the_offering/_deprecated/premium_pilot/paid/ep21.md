# THE OFFERING — EP21: Bound

S#1 — VAEL'S KEEP / VAEL'S WING / DAWN

[Visual]
ISOLDE wakes. VAEL is beside her — asleep. Both still naked under the coverlet. The dawn light is just touching the room. ISOLDE turns her head and looks at her left arm.

The three crescents and the flame-curve are still there — burning steady. But a new mark has appeared overnight: a full bracelet around her wrist — gold, dark-edged, complete circle. Mate-seal mark.

She raises her right hand and touches the new bracelet. It pulses warm.

She looks at VAEL. His scaled knuckles — on the bedding between them — show four full gold threads now, also forming a bracelet under the scales.

ISOLDE: Vael.
VAEL (waking slowly): Yes.
ISOLDE: Look.

He looks. He raises his scaled hand. He turns it. The bracelet under the scales is complete.

VAEL: The seal.
ISOLDE: We are sealed.
VAEL: We are.

He brings his scaled hand to her bracelet — palm against wrist. The two bracelets touch. Both pulse — once — bright together.

S#2 — VAEL'S KEEP / INNER COURTYARD / MORNING

[Visual]
ISOLDE walks among the twelve drakes. She wears a long sleeveless tunic — the bracelet around her left wrist visible, plus all four previous marks. Carrow is the first to come to her. The others follow. Each drake noses against her wrist. Each carries a faint gold trace now — at the jaw, at the wing-joint, at the throat.

KIRAN watches.

KIRAN: They have been pulled into the line.
ISOLDE: When.
KIRAN: Last night. They all closed their eyes when the gold light filled the wing.
ISOLDE: All twelve.
KIRAN: All twelve. They are bound to you as the line.

ISOLDE looks at Carrow.

ISOLDE: She named me her queen.
KIRAN: She did.

S#3 — VAEL'S KEEP / ONYX HALL / DAIS / MIDDAY

[Visual]
Council assembled. HALDREN, KIRAN, ELARA, plus three other lieutenants of VAEL. ISOLDE on the high seat. VAEL standing behind. ISOLDE wears the bracelet visible. The Velden crown is not on her head — it is in the carved box.

ISOLDE: My uncle ALDRIC will move within days.
HALDREN: He has already begun. The kingdom council was called at dawn.
ISOLDE: What will he do.
HALDREN: He cannot recall the crown. The line will not give it back. He must hold his throne another way.
KIRAN: Which way.
HALDREN: He will denounce her as the offering — as one no longer human, taken by the dragon. He will call all gift-bearing lines to him.
ISOLDE: How many.
HALDREN: At least twelve of the old line still alive that I know of. There may be more.
ISOLDE: Will they come.
HALDREN: Some. He has nothing to offer them but blood-right. You have the line and the crown and the mate. They come to you.
ISOLDE: Send word. To each.
HALDREN: Through the line.
ISOLDE: Through the line.

She places her hand on the carved box. The three items inside pulse.

ELARA: Twelve more carriers.
ISOLDE: Twelve more.

S#4 — VAEL'S KEEP / VAEL'S WING / EVENING

[Visual]
VAEL and ISOLDE alone again. He stands behind her at the corner table. He has unfastened the back of her gown to the waist — slow, without urgency. The bracelet on her left wrist, the marks on her collarbone, the bite mark above the collar — all visible.

His scaled hand goes to her spine. Traces from base of neck down to the small of her back. She does not turn.

VAEL: How many days now.
ISOLDE: Until.
VAEL: Until you are crown.
ISOLDE: Twenty. Maybe less.
VAEL: Twenty nights.
ISOLDE: Yes.

She turns. He bends. He kisses her — long, deep. The gown slides off her shoulders. He picks her up — both arms — and carries her to the bed.
