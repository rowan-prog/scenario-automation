## S#3 — KEEP STEPS / ROAR / CONTINUOUS

[VISUAL / ACTION]
Two wing-shadows low over the forecourt. Twelve escorts drop to one knee at the same beat. ENVOY HEAD on one knee. Writ to the cobble.

KIRAN at VAEL's right doesn't kneel — lowers his head to ISOLDE for a full beat.

VAEL turns to ISOLDE. Fistful of her hair at the nape, public. Tips her head. Scaled palm covers the three bites at her throat.

ISOLDE doesn't wait. She raises her own free hand. Lays her bare palm flat on the back of his scaled hand at her throat. Then she lifts that same hand to her mouth — and presses her lips to the back of his scaled hand — open mouth — slow — held.

[KEY CAMERA]
WIDE (short): wing-shadows over the forecourt.
MEDIUM: twelve kneeling.
ECU: writ on the cobble.
ECU: KIRAN's head lowered.
ECU: fistful of hair at nape.
ECU: scaled palm at the bites.
ECU: ISOLDE's bare palm on the back of his scaled hand at her own throat.
ECU: ISOLDE's lips on the back of his scaled hand — open mouth — held — public.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: She is —
(Second roar — closer — cuts him.)

[END HOOK]
ISOLDE lifts her mouth off his hand. Her hand stays on the back of his hand. She does not let go.

## S#5 — KEEP STEPS / PAYWALL / CONTINUOUS

[VISUAL / ACTION]
VAEL turns ISOLDE's face to him by the hand at her nape. He bends. He lowers his mouth a finger's width above hers. Holds.

He almost closes the distance. He pulls back the last fraction. Doesn't kiss her.

ISOLDE rises onto her toes. She closes the fraction herself. Closed-lip press — public — held — long.

She lifts off.

[KEY CAMERA]
ECU: VAEL's mouth a finger's width from ISOLDE's.
ECU: ISOLDE rising on her toes.
ECU: closed-lip press — public — long held.
ECU: ISOLDE's hand still on the back of his scaled hand at her own throat.
WIDE (short): forecourt — twelve kneeling, banner ash, the press of mouths above them.
MACRO INSERT: scale-light at her wrist — bright, steady.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: She is —
(Third roar — overhead — cuts him.)
ISOLDE: (low, to VAEL only) Say it.
VAEL: …
(Fourth roar — longest — overhead.)
ISOLDE: (low, to VAEL only) Then I will.
VAEL: …

[END HOOK]
ISOLDE opens her mouth — to say it — to say his name back at him, or the new name on the writ, or both. Pack roars a fifth time. The word is buried. ISOLDE's hand on the back of VAEL's hand at her own throat does not let go.

Hard Cut
