# THE OFFERING — EP35 (v30) — PROMISE

## S#1 — Chamber. Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Wide bed platform — Kiran had a second mattress added beside the first. Linen stacked at the foot. Two stone pitchers of warm water by the bed.

Isolde on her side. Vael behind her. Hand on her belly. Both awake.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Promise.
VAEL: Promised.
ISOLDE: Don't transform.
VAEL: I won't.
ISOLDE: My hand.
VAEL: Stays in mine.

---

## S#2 — Bed. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She turns in his arms. Faces him. Lifts her hand. Puts it on his cheek.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: When she comes — will you let the midwife stay.
VAEL: As long as she keeps her hands on you only when I tell her to.
ISOLDE: And after.
VAEL: She stays in the south wing while you heal. Then south.
ISOLDE: She comes back if I want her.
VAEL: She comes back if you want her.
ISOLDE: ...

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He kisses her palm. The mark of his bite on the heel of her palm from EP03 is faded now — only memory. He kisses it anyway.

---

## S#3 — Bed. Late.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She is asleep. He is awake. Has not slept properly in three nights.

He gets up. Walks to the chamber door. Opens it.

Kiran outside. Bow.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (very low): The keep is closed.
KIRAN: My lord.
VAEL: From tonight. Lower stair guards doubled. No one above the lower stair except you, Halren, Halric, and me. Anyone else — turn them back.
KIRAN: Yes, my lord.
VAEL: How long until the council issue is fully past us.
KIRAN: They have been silent for two weeks. The last word from the east was Renar's letter.
VAEL: Watch the east.
KIRAN: Watching.
VAEL: ...

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He closes the door. Locks it. Goes back to bed.

---

## S#4 — Bed. Dawn.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She wakes. Sees him at the bedside, not in the bed.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Vael.
VAEL: Here.
ISOLDE: Come back.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He gets in. Hand on her belly.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: You stood at the door at midnight.
VAEL: Yes.
ISOLDE: You went to Kiran.
VAEL: Yes.
ISOLDE: You closed the keep.
VAEL: I did.
ISOLDE: ...
VAEL: Halren said seven days. It is the fourth day.
ISOLDE: I know.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She closes her eyes. He keeps his hand on her belly.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held wide. Dawn light through the slit window.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
Outside the chamber window — audience-only — in the south wing, Halren the midwife at her own window. Light. She is preparing — sterilizing instruments, folding clean cloth, mixing herbs.

She has done this many times. Audience sees her face. Confidence. She knows. The lady will give birth in two days.

She is ready.

Hard Cut.
