# THE OFFERING — EP05 (v30)

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Vael's Chamber. Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Late. Chamber lit by hearth only. Isolde at the window slit looking east. She has on the black silk from the hall earlier — has not changed.

Vael enters. Silent. Closes the door. Locks it.

She does not turn.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Why didn't you stop them earlier. In the hall. Before the boy touched me.
VAEL: Because I needed to see who would move first.
ISOLDE: And.
VAEL: Three of them in the hall thought about it. Two pulled back. One did not. I'll handle the two tomorrow. The one is already handled.
ISOLDE: You are going to kill two more dragons over my arm.
VAEL: Yes.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She turns. Looks at him from the window.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Why.
VAEL: Because you walked into this keep alone and nobody stood for you. I will stand for you. They will learn what that means.
ISOLDE: I'm not your wife.
VAEL: Not yet.
ISOLDE: When.
VAEL: When I decide.
ISOLDE: When *I* decide.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He looks at her. Beat. The line lands different than she expected.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Then decide soon. They need to hear it from your mouth too.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She does not answer. Walks past him to the bed. Sits on the edge. Does not lie down.

He stays at the door. Watches her.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the chamber. Slow push to her sitting on the bed. Cut to him at the door — hand on the iron.

## S#2 — Drakonis Keep. Great Hall. Next Day. Mid-Morning.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A smaller convocation than yesterday. The three lords. Kiran. Sera at the side — staying, but visibly leashed now. Half the keep's house heads.

Vael at the head. Isolde at his right.

A pair of dragon men brought before the dais — both bound at the wrist. Yesterday's "two who thought about it" from the hall.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: You looked at her too long yesterday.

DRAGON A: My lord, we did not —
DRAGON B: My lord, mercy.

VAEL: Sera. Stand up.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Sera stands at the side.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: These two are of your house. Are they not.
SERA (after a beat): Yes, my lord.
VAEL: One of them is your cousin. The other your uncle.
SERA: Yes.
VAEL: Their hands or their tongues. Pick.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Sera's face — held a long beat. The two men below her looking up.

[DIALOGUE]
SERA (quiet): My lord. I will not pick.
VAEL: That is the wrong answer.
SERA: It is the only answer I will give.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Long beat. Vael turns his head to Isolde. Looks at her.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: My lady.
ISOLDE: My lord.
VAEL: Pick.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She is not ready for that. Beat. She stands.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Neither.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The hall reacts. The three lords look at her.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Neither tongues nor hands. They will be marked at the back of the neck so the keep can see who looked too long at me yesterday. They will wear the mark in this keep for the rest of their lives. If they look too long at me again, then you take the tongues. Not before.

VAEL (long beat): As my lady commands.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A KEEP MAGE steps forward. Pulls the bound dragons' collars down. Touches a black-fire fingertip to the back of each neck. Both scream once. The mark sets — a small dragon coil. Permanent.

Vael's hand on the chair arm flexes once. He is angry. He is also doing what she said.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (to Sera): Lady Sera. Thank you. You spoke for your house. That counts.
SERA (does not bow but does not speak back): ...
ISOLDE: Sit down.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Sera sits. The two marked dragons are walked out.

The oldest of the keep lords bows his head one degree to Isolde. He does not need to say anything.

[KEY CAMERA]
Slow tracking around the hall. Cut to Vael's hand opening on the chair arm — relaxing.

## S#3 — Corridor After. Walking Together.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael and Isolde walking the upper corridor. Kiran ten paces behind. She still in black silk. He without his coat.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: You spared two of Sera's house.
ISOLDE: I made an example without making an enemy.
VAEL: She will still be your enemy.
ISOLDE: She will be slower about it.

VAEL: Where did you learn to read a hall like that.
ISOLDE: My father's court. I learned by being the smallest person in every room.

VAEL: You were never the smallest. They just told you that you were.
ISOLDE: ...

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She stops walking. Turns to him in the corridor. Looks up at his face.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: She speaks for me, in that hall, from now on. You said it.
VAEL: I said it.
ISOLDE: Did you mean it.
VAEL: I meant it.
ISOLDE: Then if I decide tomorrow that you should not kill two more dragons over yesterday's looking — you do not.
VAEL: That is what it means.
ISOLDE: ...

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She looks at him a long beat. Searching his face. Something almost surfaces — recognition, again — and then she shakes it off.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: I want to walk the upper terrace. I have not seen the keep in daylight from above.
VAEL: Kiran will take you.
ISOLDE: I asked you.
VAEL: ... Then me.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He offers his arm — formally, the first time. She takes it. They walk.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of them walking the upper corridor toward the terrace door.

---

## S#4 — Drakonis Keep. Upper Terrace. Mid-Afternoon. Into Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A high stone terrace above the keep. View east — the wasteland, the distant ridge. View south — the road back to Veine. View north — the dragon council's territory.

She walks the rail. He stays one step behind, watching her.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: What is east.
VAEL: The wasteland. Then the ridge. Then the council's territory.
ISOLDE: The council.
VAEL: The old dragons. The ones who decide.
ISOLDE: Decide what.
VAEL: Whether a dragon lord has made a wrong decision.
ISOLDE: Like marrying a human.
VAEL: That would be one.
ISOLDE: Have they decided about us yet.
VAEL: They are deciding now.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She does not turn. Looks east.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: How long.
VAEL: A week. Maybe less.
ISOLDE: And when they decide.
VAEL: We will hear from them.
ISOLDE: With words or with men.
VAEL: With both.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She nods. Files it. Walks further along the rail.

She stops at the south side of the terrace. Looks at the road back to Veine. Long beat.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (does not approach): If you want it, the south road is empty tonight. My men are not on it. You could walk it.
ISOLDE: With you.
VAEL: Without me.
ISOLDE: Where would I go.
VAEL: Anywhere your name still works.
ISOLDE: My name does not work south.
VAEL: It works in Veine.
ISOLDE: Veine sold me.
VAEL: Yes.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She turns and looks at him for a long beat.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: You are letting me leave.
VAEL: I am.
ISOLDE: Why.
VAEL: Because if you stay, I want it to be because you stayed. Not because the gate was locked.
ISOLDE: ...

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She walks back across the terrace to him. Slow. Stops a hand's width from him.

She puts her hand on his chest. Over his sternum. Through his shirt.

She feels his heart.

[KEY CAMERA]
Close: her hand on his chest. His face — does not move.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Your heart goes faster than a man's.
VAEL: Always around you.
ISOLDE: Is that you. Or is that what you are.
VAEL: Both.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She does not move her hand.

[CUTAWAY]
A flash — half a second. The masked man in the small room above the festival. Her hand on his chest then too. Same heartbeat — faster than a man's.

Cut back to the terrace.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She blinks. Loses the thread. Frowns slightly. Does not remove her hand.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: I am not walking south tonight.
VAEL: Good.
ISOLDE: I am not walking south because I do not have anywhere to walk to. Not because of you.
VAEL: I know.
ISOLDE: But I am also not walking south because of something I cannot name yet.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: Do you know what I cannot name.
VAEL (after a beat): Ask me again in a week.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He puts his hand over hers on his chest. Covers it. Holds it there.

The terrace torches behind them burn lower. The south road in the gold light is empty.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
Below the terrace, far down — at the southern keep wall — a single small shape moves in the shadow. A figure climbing the outer stone. Slow. Patient. Knife on the back.

Audience-only.

Hard Cut.
