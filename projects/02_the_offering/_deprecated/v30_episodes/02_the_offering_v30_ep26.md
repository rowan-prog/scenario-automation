# THE OFFERING — EP26 (v30)

## S#1 — Chamber. Morning. Sixth Month.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Belly larger. Isolde walks the chamber slowly. He is at the desk reading reports. He has not been outside the keep in a week.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: You have not slept.
VAEL: I slept.
ISOLDE: Two hours.
VAEL: That counts.
ISOLDE: It does not.

---

## S#2 — Chamber. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She walks to him at the desk. Closes the report.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Bed. Now.
VAEL: It is morning.
ISOLDE: I did not ask you what time it was.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He stands. Looks at her. Almost smiles. Goes to the bed. Lies on his back.

She climbs on. Sits on his hips. Belly between them. Both her hands flat on his chest.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: You will sleep now. I will stay here. I will not move until you sleep.
VAEL: That is not how sleep works.
ISOLDE: It is now.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He looks at her for a long beat. Closes his eyes.

His hands rest on her thighs through the silk. Open. Not gripping.

He sleeps. Within four minutes. The first real sleep on screen for him.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held wide of the bed. Her sitting over him. Slow push.

---

## S#3 — Bed. Hours Later.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She is still there. He wakes. Sees her watching him.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: How long.
ISOLDE: Four hours.
VAEL: You did not move.
ISOLDE: I said I would not.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He sits up under her. His hand at the small of her back.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: My back will not work for a week.
ISOLDE: Worth it.
VAEL: Worth it.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He kisses her. Slow. Long. His hand at her belly.

---

## S#4 — Chamber. Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Hearth low. Both in bed. Hand on her belly. He is awake. She is asleep.

[VO]
VAEL (V.O., audience-only, quiet): I have spent nine years thinking I would die alone in this keep. I have spent six months trying to find a woman I once held for one night. She is here. She is asleep on my arm. My child is moving under my hand. Tomorrow there will be a new thing to be afraid of. Tomorrow.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He closes his eyes. Sleeps. Second time on screen.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held long wide of the bed.

None.

A bird on the windowsill outside the slit. Small dark feathers. Not the council mark — a real bird. It looks in. Flies away.

Just a bird.
