# THE OFFERING — EP15 (v30) — PUBLIC MATE + QUEEN-PRESUMPTIVE

## S#1 — Inner Chamber. Dawn.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She opens the chamber door. He is on the floor against it. He stands when she opens it.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: One question.
VAEL: Ask.
ISOLDE: Did you ever consider letting me leave the keep — six months ago, if you had found me — without claiming me.
VAEL: No.
ISOLDE: Why.
VAEL: Because I had already chosen you the night you took my mask off.
ISOLDE: I did not take it off.
VAEL: I felt you trying. That was enough. The choice was already made.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Long beat.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Come inside.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He comes in. She closes the door.

---

## S#2 — Bed. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She does not say anything else. Walks to the bed. He follows. She takes off her own dress. He takes off his shirt. She on top of him again. Long, slow — first time with both of them knowing.

No dialogue for most of it. The sex is different now. Audience hears her breath. His breath. Once she stops mid-act and stares at his face and says one word —

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: You.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
And keeps going.

After. Both breathing.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: What do I get for forgiving you.
VAEL: Anything.
ISOLDE: Today I want the council out of your keep before sundown.
VAEL: They have ruled in favor of the escort. Their votes will not reverse easily.
ISOLDE: I am not asking you to change their votes. I am asking you to put them outside the gate by sundown.
VAEL: How.
ISOLDE: Tell them you have already named me publicly. As mate. As queen-presumptive. As the mother of the next heir. And that any escort to my father's court will pass through your fire first.
VAEL: That triggers war.
ISOLDE: It triggers a choice. Either they fight you over me — in which case you have already broken Sera's faction and the council in one move. Or they back down — in which case you have taken the question off the table for a generation. Pick.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Long beat.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: You should have been ruling your father's kingdom.
ISOLDE: He did not think so. I will write him a letter about it.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He looks at her a long time. Then he stands. Reaches for his coat.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Get dressed.

---

## S#3 — Great Hall. Full Court. Mid-Day.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Every dragon in the keep. The five council elders along one wall. Sera's two remaining house members near them.

Vael at the dais. Isolde at his right. She is wearing a black gown the keep tailored overnight. Belly visible now if you know to look.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (to the hall, not loud — does not need to be): I have named her my mate.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Hall reacts.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: I name her now my queen-presumptive. The keep recognizes her child as my heir. The council's escort offer is refused. Anyone who attempts to remove her from this keep — for any reason — will burn at this gate.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Half the dragons in the hall go to one knee. Half do not.

[DIALOGUE]
ELDER ONE: Lord Drakonis. You are in defiance.
VAEL: I am.
ELDER ONE: Then we have nothing further.
VAEL: You have until sundown. Walk or fly.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The elders walk out. Three of them. Two stay one beat longer. Look at Isolde. One of those two bows. The other does not.

[DIALOGUE]
SERA: My lord. My house —
VAEL: Your house chose. You stay or you go.
SERA (after a beat): We stay.
VAEL: Then you swear to her. Now.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Sera looks at Isolde. Long beat. Sera kneels.

[DIALOGUE]
SERA: My queen.

ISOLDE (does not gloat): Stand up.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Sera stands.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: I do not trust you. But I will let you stay. Understand the trade.
SERA: Understood.

---

## S#4 — Parapet. Sunset.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The four elders fly east as dragons — full transformation in the courtyard, lift off. Audience-only: one of the four lags behind. Half a beat. Then catches up.

Vael and Isolde on the parapet. His arm around her shoulders. Her hand low on her belly.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Will they come back.
VAEL: In a season. With more.
ISOLDE: Then we have a season.
VAEL: We have a season.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Long beat. She looks east at the four shapes against the sunset.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: One of them stayed.
VAEL: One of them stayed.
ISOLDE: He bowed to me.
VAEL: He did.
ISOLDE: Why.
VAEL: Because his daughter was killed thirty years ago. Public mate. Same way as my mother.
ISOLDE: ...
VAEL: He came here to vote against the escort, and lost. Now he stays.
ISOLDE: Will the others know.
VAEL: They will know by tomorrow.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the parapet. The two of them silhouetted. Eastern sky red.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
SUPER as the elders fly east: COUNCIL — DEPARTED. SPLIT.

[END HOOK]
Below the parapet, far down in the eastern wasteland — one of the four flying elders has separated from the others. Audience-only: that one banks south and goes invisible against the horizon.

It is not flying back to the council. It is flying back toward the keep — quietly, from behind.

Sera, in her room, sees it through her window. She smiles for the first time since EP09.

Hard Cut.
