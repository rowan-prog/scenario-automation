# THE OFFERING — EP32 (v30) — THE SUCCESSION

## S#1 — Veine Palace. Throne Room. Next Day.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The throne room. Court in full. The king's body in state at the front — covered, candles. Adeline beside it in formal mourning.

The CHIEF HERALD reads the king's will. Long preamble. Then:

[DIALOGUE]
HERALD (reading): "To my eldest daughter Isolde of the house of Aurelle, born of Mireille of South Cove my first lawful wife, I confirm legitimacy and lineage. I leave to her the right of first claim to the throne of Veine."

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The court reacts. The councilors at the front turn to each other. Adeline does not move. Her face does not change.

[DIALOGUE]
HERALD (continues): "If she refuses or absents herself, the crown passes to my second daughter Adeline of the house of Veine."

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde steps forward. Slow — belly. Vael three paces behind her. She stops in front of the throne.

She turns to the court.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: I refuse.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Court ripple.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: I am the queen-presumptive of Drakonis Keep. I carry an heir. I will not divide my attention between two crowns. The throne of Veine passes to my sister Adeline.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Adeline does not move.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: I retain — and I say this for the court to hear — the right of return. If at any time the kingdom of Veine becomes a place I do not wish to see standing, I will return and answer that. The kingdom should be run such that I never have to.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The court is silent.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (to Adeline): Sister.
ADELINE: Sister.
ISOLDE: Take the crown.

---

## S#2 — Throne Room. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Adeline walks to the throne. She does not sit. The chief herald places the small golden circlet on her head — not a full crown. Veine's regent circlet, until coronation.

Adeline turns and faces the court.

[DIALOGUE]
ADELINE: I accept the regency. I will accept the crown at proper coronation when the year of mourning has passed.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She turns to Isolde.

[DIALOGUE]
ADELINE: Sister. I will run it well.
ISOLDE: I know you will.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She does not say anything about earlier years. Neither does Adeline. They both know.

---

## S#3 — King's Tomb. Late Afternoon.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A small inner garden. The Veine royal tomb. The king's body — wrapped in white — being carried by four nobles. Adeline at the front of the procession. Isolde and Vael at the back.

The body is placed inside the tomb. The stone is set in place.

Isolde stands at the tomb for a long beat. Hand on her belly. Vael at her shoulder.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (quiet, to the stone): I touched your hand once. That was all I had. It was more than you gave my mother. Adeline will run it well or I will come back.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She turns. Walks out of the garden.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the garden. Pull back slow.

---

## S#4 — Palace Guest Chamber. Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A guest chamber. Vael sitting on the edge of the bed. Isolde in his arms. He has not let go of her since they left the tomb.

She is not crying. Has not cried at all. He does not push her to.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: You refused a crown today.
ISOLDE: I did.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: I do not want a crown that comes from a man I touched once on his way out of the world.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: I want a crown that comes from a man who waited six months to find me and could not.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: That one I will take. When you give it.
VAEL: It is yours when you want it.
ISOLDE: I want it after I rest. After she is born. After we have been home a while.
VAEL: Then after.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She closes her eyes. Stays in his arms.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held wide. Slow push.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.
