# THE OFFERING — EP27 (v30)

## S#1 — Lower Yard. Morning. A Small Hurt.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A young dragon — eleven or twelve in dragon years, the youngest in the keep — has fallen from the upper wall. Broken arm. Crying. Other young dragons crowd around.

Isolde happens to be on the lower terrace and sees it. Walks down.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (to the crowd): Move.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
They move. She kneels beside the young dragon. Looks at the arm.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: What is your name.
YOUNG DRAGON (crying): Renn.
ISOLDE: Renn. Look at my face. Not at the arm. The arm hurts because the bones are talking to each other. They will stop talking when the healer comes.
RENN: It hurts.
ISOLDE: It will hurt for ten minutes more. Then less. Look at my face.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He looks at her. Stops crying. The KEEP HEALER arrives. Splints the arm.

Isolde stays kneeling beside the boy until the splint is on. Then stands.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the yard. The young dragons watching her.

---

## S#2 — Corridor After. Vael.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael in the corridor. He has watched from above.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: You knelt on the basalt for twenty minutes.
ISOLDE: I know.
VAEL: With the child.
ISOLDE: The child was fine.
VAEL: Next time, ask me to kneel with you.
ISOLDE: Why.
VAEL: Because I will catch you when you stand back up.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She does not argue. He puts his hand at the small of her back. Walks her to the upper levels.

---

## S#3 — Chamber. Afternoon.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She is in his robe. He is on the bed beside her. Hand on her belly.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: The young dragon — Renn. He was scared of me before today.
VAEL: He was scared of you because his mother told him you would kill him for crossing your path.
ISOLDE: His mother.
VAEL: She is in Sera's house. She has stayed.
ISOLDE: ...
VAEL: He is not scared of you now.
ISOLDE: No.
VAEL: His mother will hear about it by sundown.
ISOLDE: Good.

---

## S#4 — Bed. Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Her hand on his chest. His heart. Faster than a man's. Same as always.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Today you said one of the dragons would stop being afraid in ten minutes.
ISOLDE: He did.
VAEL: How did you know.
ISOLDE: Because my mother said the same thing to me when I broke my wrist falling from a horse. The bones talk to each other. They stop talking.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: She was good at hurt. She had her own.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: I have not thought about her like that — about her being good at something — in a long time.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: Today I taught a young dragon something my mother taught me. It is the first thing of hers I have given anyone.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She closes her eyes. He kisses her forehead.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held wide.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
At the gate of the keep, in the lower yard — Renn (the young dragon) standing with his splinted arm. A woman in front of him — his mother. She slaps him across the face.

He does not cry this time.

He looks up at the upper terrace. Where Isolde was earlier.

Audience-only: he has decided who he serves.

Hard Cut.
