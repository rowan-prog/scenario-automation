# THE OFFERING — EP17 (v30) — THE LETTER WEST

## S#1 — Chamber. Morning. Writing.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde at the desk. Quill. Black wax. A single page. She writes — slow, deliberate.

[INSERT — page audience-visible as she writes:]
> Father.
> The Dragon Lord has named me his mate. I carry his heir.
> I am not coming back.
> Do not send for me.
> If you send for me, he burns your gate.
> Do not write back unless you have something to say that begins with an apology.
> — Isolde of Drakonis Keep

She folds it. Seals it. Hands it to Kiran.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Two riders. Different roads. One arrives, the other survives.
KIRAN: My lady.

---

## S#2 — Veine. King's Hall. Days Later.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
King Aldric on his small throne. The stepmother at his side. Adeline behind. A herald hands the king the folded sealed page.

He breaks the wax. Reads. Reads again. His face does not change.

He hands the page to the stepmother. She reads it. Her face changes. She tears it in half.

The king picks up the pieces from the floor without speaking.

[DIALOGUE]
STEPMOTHER: She has gone mad. The dragon has put magic on her.
ALDRIC: She has not gone mad.
STEPMOTHER: My lord, you cannot —
ALDRIC: I read it.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Adeline looks at the floor. Then at her mother. Then at her father. Says nothing.

[KEY CAMERA]
Close on Aldric holding the torn pieces.

---

## S#3 — Drakonis Keep. Surface Chamber. Afternoon.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde walking the upper terrace alone. Vael in the hall with the two stayed elders — closing political loose ends.

She stops at the south corner of the terrace. Looks west. Toward Veine.

[VO]
ISOLDE (V.O.): I have not thought of him in two weeks. My father. The man who sold me. Today I sent him a letter telling him not to write to me. The letter is the first thing he has ever received from me directly. Eighteen years in his court and the first thing he hears from my mouth is a refusal.

[VO]
ISOLDE (V.O.): My mother would have laughed.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She turns. Walks back inside.

---

## S#4 — Chamber. Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Bed. He has come in late. She is awake.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: The letter went out.
VAEL: He will get it in two weeks.
ISOLDE: He will not answer.
VAEL: He may.
ISOLDE: He will not.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: That is all right. I sent it for the court. Not for him.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: Come to bed.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He undresses. Gets in. Pulls her against him. Hand on her belly.

He does not touch her sexually tonight. Just holds her.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held wide of the bed. Slow push.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
In Veine, the same night. Aldric on his small throne, hall empty. Holding the torn letter in two hands. He has not spoken for an hour.

The stepmother enters. Approaches him.

ALDRIC (without looking up): Get out.
STEPMOTHER: My lord —
ALDRIC: Get out of my hall. Now. Tonight.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The stepmother freezes. The king has not given her an order in six years.

She goes.
