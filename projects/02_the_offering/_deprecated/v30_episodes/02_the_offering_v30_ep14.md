# THE OFFERING — EP14 (v30) — MASK REVEAL

## S#1 — Inner Keep Chamber. Morning.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael gone. Out dealing with the assassin's body, the council, Sera's box (which Kiran has intercepted and now hidden).

Isolde alone in the inner chamber. She walks the perimeter. Studies the walls. Finds a small hidden panel behind the headboard. Opens it.

Inside: a shallow cabinet. Personal objects.

- The small wooden bird from his outer chamber (he moved it down).
- A folded letter — yellow with age, unread by her.
- A child's drawing — primitive, a dragon and a small figure.
- And —

A piece of silver mask. The right half. Broken at the cheekbone.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She picks it up. Holds it. Looks at it.

She holds it up to her own face.

[FLASHBACK]
EP01 cold open — her own mask. Same edge. Same break point. But this is *his* mask — black originally, magicked silver by the festival's ambient charm. Or no, this is silver. Pure silver.

She blinks. Holds the half-mask up beside her own face in the small mirror over the hearth.

The cheek line matches.

Her hand begins to shake. She lowers the mask. Looks at it in her palm.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held close on her hand with the half-mask. Push slow.

[VO]
ISOLDE (V.O., very quiet): No.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She sits down on the bed. Mask in her open palm. Does not move.

---

## S#2 — Inner Chamber. Later.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael returns. Finds her sitting on the bed, mask half in her open palm.

He stops at the door.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (does not look up): Sit down.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He sits. Across from her on the chest at the foot of the bed.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: You said ask in a week. I am asking now.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: Was the first time you saw me the day I walked into your hall.
VAEL: No.
ISOLDE: When was the first time.
VAEL: Six months ago.
ISOLDE: Where.
VAEL: A festival. The masquerade in the lower city of Veine.
ISOLDE: What were you doing there.
VAEL: Looking for nothing. Found you.
ISOLDE: And after.
VAEL: You left in the dawn. I woke. You were gone.
ISOLDE: And.
VAEL: I have been looking for you for six months.
ISOLDE: Quietly.
VAEL: Because the only way to lose a woman like you faster than running from her is to announce her.
ISOLDE: Your mother.
VAEL: Yes.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She is breathing through her teeth. Hand on the mask tightening.

---

## S#3 — Same Chamber. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She stands. Walks the room once. Slow.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Did you know it was me when I walked into the hall.
VAEL: Yes.
ISOLDE: When you said "She is my bride" in front of the court — you knew.
VAEL: Yes.
ISOLDE: When you put me in the chamber — you knew.
VAEL: Yes.
ISOLDE: When I told you "there is a man I gave that to and I do not even know his name" — you knew.
VAEL: Yes.
ISOLDE: When I told you the child cannot be yours — you knew.
VAEL: Yes.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Long beat.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Get out.
VAEL: Isolde —
ISOLDE: Get out. I am not running. I am not going to the gate. I want one night without your face in this room. Go.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He stands. Walks to the door. Stops at the threshold. Does not turn around.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: I will tell you the rest tomorrow. The whole thing. Every part. Ask anything.
ISOLDE: Go.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He goes.

The door closes.

---

## S#4 — Inner Chamber. After.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde alone. She sits on the bed for a long time with the mask half in her hand. Does not cry. Eventually she lies down. Hand on her stomach.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (very quiet, to herself): So I did not dream you.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Beat.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: And you did not dream me back.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She closes her eyes. Single tear from the corner of her eye. Just one.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held close on her face. Held.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
Outside the inner chamber door, in the corridor — Vael on the floor. Back against her door. Does not move. Kiran walks down the corridor with a cup of water. Sets it on the floor beside Vael. Vael does not touch it.

Kiran goes.

Vael stays. All night.

Audience-only: at one point, his hand opens against the door behind his head. The shimmer is on his palm. He puts his palm flat against the door. As if he is trying to touch her through it.

Hard Cut.
