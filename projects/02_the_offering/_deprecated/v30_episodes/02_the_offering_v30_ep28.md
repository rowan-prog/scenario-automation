# THE OFFERING — EP28 (v30) — SEVEN MONTHS, SLOW INTIMATE

## S#1 — Bath Chamber. Day.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Steam thick. Isolde in the bath. Seven months. Belly large above the water. Hair pinned. Face flushed.

Vael at the side. Sleeves to the shoulder. Scales visible. He has dismissed the chamber maids. Refills the water himself.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: You are not careful.
VAEL: I am careful.
ISOLDE: You will burn your hand on the pitcher.
VAEL: My hand stopped burning a long time ago.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He pours water slow down her shoulder. The water trails over her belly. She closes her eyes.

He puts both his palms on her belly above the water. The child shifts under his palm.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (very quiet): She knows my hand.
ISOLDE: She has known your hand for two months.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: She kicks more when you are there. When you leave, she sleeps.
VAEL: ...

---

## S#2 — Bath. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He leans down. Kisses her belly through the wet skin. Slow.

She lifts her wet hand out of the water. Puts it on his cheek. He turns his face into her palm.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: When she is born.
VAEL: When she is born.
ISOLDE: I will be afraid.
VAEL: Yes.
ISOLDE: I want you in the room.
VAEL: I will be in the room.
ISOLDE: You will not transform.
VAEL: I will not transform.
ISOLDE: Promise.
VAEL: Promised.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He kisses her wet palm.

[KEY CAMERA]
Close: his mouth on her palm. Steam.

---

## S#3 — Chamber. Late Afternoon.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She is in his robe. Hair drying. He carried her from the bath.

He sits behind her on the bed. Combs her hair with his fingers. Slow.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: This is a strange thing for you to be doing.
VAEL: It is.
ISOLDE: How did you learn it.
VAEL: I have not done it. I am doing it.
ISOLDE: For the first time.
VAEL: For the first time.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She closes her eyes. Tips her head back against his chest.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: I love you.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He stops combing. Beat.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: ... Say it again.
ISOLDE: I love you.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He puts his face against her hair. Stays there.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: I love you.
ISOLDE: I know.

---

## S#4 — Bed. Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Both. Slow intimate. Her belly between them. He is on his side. She is on her side facing him. He is careful — but not careful enough to be cold.

[CUTAWAY]
His hand at her ribs.
Her hand on his back.
Her breath against his shoulder.
His mouth on her throat.
The shimmer at his palm at her belly between them.

After. Both breathing.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Tonight you said it first.
ISOLDE: I did.
VAEL: Why tonight.
ISOLDE: Because I decided.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He laughs. Quiet. First laugh on screen.

She does not see his face. She feels the laugh in his chest under her palm.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held wide. The bird on the shelf. Hearth low.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
Outside, far west — on the road from Veine, a single rider is coming. Slow. Carrying a small folded letter sealed with King Aldric's personal mark.

Hard Cut.
