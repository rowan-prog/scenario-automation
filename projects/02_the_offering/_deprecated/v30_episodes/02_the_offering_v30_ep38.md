# THE OFFERING — EP38 (v30) — LABOR (PART 1)

## S#1 — Chamber. Three Hours In.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The chamber through the night. Two oil lamps. Halren at the foot of the bed, moving slow, hands clean.

Isolde on her back on the wide platform. Sweat at the hairline, at the collarbone, in the hollow of the throat. Hand crushing Vael's wrist hard enough to bruise.

Vael on the bed beside her. Scales at his shoulders. Dark red ring full. He has not transformed. He has promised.

[DIALOGUE]
HALREN: Four hours, my lady. Maybe three.
VAEL (low, dangerous): If you say "maybe" to her once more —
HALREN (faster, head down): Four hours, my lord. I will say four hours.
VAEL: Four.
ISOLDE (between breaths): Vael.
VAEL: Here.
ISOLDE: Don't.
VAEL: I won't.

---

## S#2 — Contraction.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A long one. Bed shifts under her. Vael's scales rise up his throat to the jaw. The dragon at the edge of his face — bone shifting at the temples.

He puts his face against her wet hairline. Breathes against her ear, fast, in time with her. Pulls back. Features not fully shifted. At the edge.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (against her hair): You are going to keep breathing.
ISOLDE: I am.
VAEL: I am not going to transform.
ISOLDE: You are not.
VAEL: I am going to keep my hand in yours.
ISOLDE: You are.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The contraction passes.

---

## S#3 — Halren Approaches.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Halren reaches forward slow — clean hands — toward Isolde's hip. Before her fingertip touches the silk, the scales along Vael's forearm closest to her snap up sharp. His head turns toward her. Halren stops mid-reach.

Isolde — without opening her eyes — moves her own hand from Vael's wrist down to his forearm. Covers the raised scales with her palm. Scales under her hand flatten. Scales further up stay sharp.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (low, dangerous): You stay at the foot. You do not put your hand on her without my word.
HALREN: My lord.
VAEL: My word.
HALREN: Yes, my lord.

---

## S#4 — Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The next contraction. Longer. Isolde does not scream — has not screamed once tonight — but the sound through her teeth is rough, low.

Vael's scales come up the side of his face. One hand stays in hers. The other goes flat on her belly. The dragon at the edge — he is shaking with not transforming.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (against her hair, very low, audience only): I would burn this room. The keep. Your father's wing. The world. If I lose you because of her.
ISOLDE (eyes still closed, breath catching): I know.
VAEL: I am not going to.
ISOLDE: You are not.
VAEL: Because you asked me not to.
ISOLDE: Because I asked.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The contraction passes. Halren lifts her face.

[DIALOGUE]
HALREN: My lady. The body is opening fast. The next hour.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held wide of the bed. The hearth. Halren at the foot.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
The next contraction starts.

[DIALOGUE]
HALREN: My lady. Push when you next breathe.

Hard Cut.
