# THE OFFERING — EP41 (v30) — THREE DAYS

## S#1 — Chamber. Three Days Later.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Mid-morning. Light through the slit. Isolde sitting up against the pillows. Mireille three days old, asleep against her chest under the loose tunic. Belly soft now.

Vael beside her. Arm around her shoulders. Other hand on the child's small foot under the linen.

Kiran at the chamber door, low.

[DIALOGUE]
KIRAN (off): My lord. The keep is asking when they may bow.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: When I am out of this bed.
VAEL: When you are out of this bed.
KIRAN: Yes, my lady.

---

## S#2 — Chamber. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde shifts. Lifts the tunic so the child can nurse. The child finds it. Begins.

Vael does not look away. Does not look at her body — looks at her face.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Does it hurt.
ISOLDE: No. It pulls.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: I had heard it would hurt at first.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: I have a higher threshold now. Twenty hours of labor will do that.
VAEL: ...

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He laughs. Quiet. Second time on screen.

She smiles. Closes her eyes. Mireille feeds.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held wide of the bed. Slow push.

---

## S#3 — Chamber. Afternoon.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Halren has been allowed back up. Quick check. Child is well. Lady is well.

[DIALOGUE]
HALREN: My lady. You may walk the chamber tomorrow. Slow. Not far.
ISOLDE: When can I leave the chamber.
HALREN: A week. Maybe ten days.
ISOLDE: I want to be at her naming.
HALREN: Two weeks. The keep can wait two weeks.
VAEL: The keep will wait two weeks.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Halren bows. Goes.

Vael lifts the child carefully off Isolde's chest. Holds her for the first time alone — both her hands fit in his one palm. He does not breathe for a long moment.

He puts her against his own chest. Walks the chamber once with her. Slow. Talking to her under his breath in dragon-rumble — audience does not hear words.

---

## S#4 — Chamber. Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Bed. Isolde sleeps. Mireille in a small basket beside the bed — Vael had it made in the last month. The basket is iron-bound. Audience-only: those iron bands are forged from the chain that bound her wrists in EP01. She does not know yet.

Vael awake. Watching. Hand on Isolde's belly — flat where Mireille used to be.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (very quiet, to himself): Mine. Mine. Mine.
ISOLDE (eyes still closed, half-asleep): Ours.
VAEL: Ours.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held wide.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
The eastern ridge — twenty dragons. Heads lifted. Watching the chamber.

Bowed.

Hard Cut.
