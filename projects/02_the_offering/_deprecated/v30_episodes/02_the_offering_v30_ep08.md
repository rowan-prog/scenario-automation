# THE OFFERING — EP08 (v30) — FREE→PAID PAYWALL

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Outer Corridor → Keep Gate. Continuing from EP07.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde walks. Sheet wrapped around her, his robe over. Hair down. Half the keep watching.

Sera in a side hall watches her walk past. Says nothing. The look on Sera's face — she does not know what to do with this.

Isolde reaches the inner keep gate. Then the outer gate. Then the keep gate itself.

She stops at the gate. Hand on the stone. The sun is just starting to rise behind the eastern ridge.

She does not turn.

Vael walks up behind her. Ten paces. Stops there.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of them at the gate. Eastern sun rising.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Are you going to stop me.
VAEL: No.
ISOLDE: Why not.
VAEL: Because if I do you will never forgive me. And if I don't you will come back.
ISOLDE: I might not.
VAEL: You will. Not for me. For yourself.
ISOLDE: Explain that.
VAEL: You do not have a kingdom. You have me, this keep, and a story nobody outside this wall will believe. You walk that gate, you walk into nothing.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She stands at the gate a long time. Hand on the iron. Wind blowing the sheet against her body.

Her free hand — without her thinking — goes low on her stomach.

She feels something. Not pain. Pressure. A weight that was not there yesterday.

Recognition.

Her eyes go wide. She closes them.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (quiet, not to him): Oh.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She turns around. Walks back to him. Stops in front of him. Looks up at his face.

For a long beat she just looks. Trying to see *through*. He stands still and lets her look.

She does not see it. The disguise still holds — magical, deep. But something is loose now. She feels closer.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: I will come back inside. On one condition.
VAEL: Name it.
ISOLDE: Stop lying.
VAEL: About what.
ISOLDE: Whatever it is you are not telling me. I do not need the whole story tonight. But I need you to stop pretending nothing is happening that I can feel happening.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Long beat.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: All right.
ISOLDE: Promise.
VAEL: Promised.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She walks past him back into the keep. He follows.

## S#2 — Vael's Chamber. Daylight.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He carries her in. Not because she cannot walk — because he wants to. Sets her on the bed. Pulls the robe back up over her shoulders.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: What did you feel. At the gate.
ISOLDE: Don't make me say it yet.
VAEL: Tell me one thing then. Are you sick. Should I send for someone.
ISOLDE: No. The opposite.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He goes still.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: ... How long.
ISOLDE: I do not know. Weeks.
VAEL: Six months.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She looks at him. Sharp.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: What did you say.
VAEL (correcting himself fast): Six weeks. Maybe. Could be more.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She holds his gaze. Files it. She heard "six months."

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Could be more.
VAEL: Could be.

## S#3 — Same Chamber. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He sits on the edge of the bed. Puts his hand on her stomach. Through her robe. Does not move it.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Whose.
ISOLDE: What kind of question is that.
VAEL: I have to ask.
ISOLDE: It cannot be yours. You have barely —
VAEL: Then whose.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She does not answer. He waits.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (finally): A man with no name. One night. Six months ago.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He does not move his hand from her stomach.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Why did you not tell me.
ISOLDE: I did not know until just now.
VAEL: Just now.
ISOLDE: At the gate.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Long beat. He looks at his own hand on her belly. Then at her face.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Then it is mine.
ISOLDE: That is not how this works.
VAEL: It is now. Whoever planted it. It is mine. The child is born here. Raised here. Yours and mine. We name it together. We bury it together if it dies. We crown it together if it lives.
ISOLDE: You would raise another man's child.
VAEL (his hand tightens slowly on her belly): There is no other man. There is a man you remember. There is me. Those are the only two men in this story. And I am here. He is not.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Long beat. She does not argue.

She lifts her own hand. Puts it over his on her belly. Lets it stay there.

[KEY CAMERA]
Close on the two hands on her belly. Held.

## S#4 — Same Chamber. Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Bed. He is behind her, front to her back. Hand on her belly. They have not spoken in an hour.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Sera will know.
VAEL: Sera will know within the day.
ISOLDE: She will use it.
VAEL: Yes.
ISOLDE: She will say the child is not yours.
VAEL: I will say the child is mine. And the keep will believe me. Because I am the only one who can say it.
ISOLDE: ...
VAEL: Sleep.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She sleeps. Eventually.

His hand on her belly. Held.

[CUTAWAY]
His palm — audience-only — glowing faintly through her robe. Mate-bond signature. He is *sensing* his own child.

He closes his eyes. Knows.
