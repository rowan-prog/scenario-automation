# THE OFFERING — EP21 (v30)

## S#1 — Corridor. Morning.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde walking the upper corridor for the first time in formal Drakonis black — long, silver thread at the collar, train short. Belly visible. Her own choice of dress.

Two SERVANTS pass her. They bow. Not casual. Not nervous. Just bow. The first time the keep's servants have bowed to her without being told.

She returns the nod. Small.

[VO]
ISOLDE (V.O.): The keep has decided what I am to it. Before I did.

---

## S#2 — Hall. Mid-Morning.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Smaller meal in the lesser hall. Vael at the head, three keep lords, Kiran. Isolde at Vael's right.

A house head — the youngest of three — addresses Vael with a question about a border guard rotation.

Isolde answers instead. Short. Correct. The house head pauses. Looks at Vael. Vael nods once. The house head accepts the answer.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (low, only her, after): When did you learn the border guard rotation.
ISOLDE: Three nights ago. From Kiran's papers I happened to see.
VAEL: Happened.
ISOLDE: Happened.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He half-smiles. Second time on screen.

---

## S#3 — Bed. Afternoon.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She is napping. He sits beside the bed. Hand on her belly through the silk. He does not move for an hour.

She wakes. Eyes still half-closed.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: How long.
VAEL: An hour.
ISOLDE: You did not sleep.
VAEL: I will when you are awake.
ISOLDE: That is not how it works.
VAEL: It is now.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She does not argue. Puts her hand over his on her belly.

---

## S#4 — Chamber. Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Hearth low. He has taken her in his arms in the bed. Not sex tonight. Just held.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: When she is born.
VAEL: When she is born.
ISOLDE: Who holds her first.
VAEL: You.
ISOLDE: Then you.
VAEL: Then me.
ISOLDE: Then no one. For a long time.
VAEL: No one.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held: both their hands on her belly. The faint shimmer at his palm steady now.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
Outside, far east — the old elder walks on. Sera is half a day behind him. She has not made contact.

Hard Cut.
