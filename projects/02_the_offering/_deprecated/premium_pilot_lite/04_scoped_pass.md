## A. English Pass — Anti-AI Dialect / 번역체 스캔

### 스캔 카테고리
- em-dash 남용
- "It wasn't X, it was Y" 구문
- 과잉 parallel structure
- poetic phrasing (시적 반복·"the way she X, the way she Y")
- 번역체 ("How dare you", "I shall not", "Yes, my lord")
- 설명 dialogue (캐릭터가 상황 설명)
- 캐릭터 voice 일치 (VAEL = compact / ISOLDE = restrained 4-9 words)

### EP별 변경

#### EP01
- Em-dash 사용: 의도된 단호한 cadence — 보존. (예: `Tall — well past her — broad`).
- VAEL voice — `"Look up. At me."` / `"Closer."` / `"They sent you as an offering."` — compact, possessive. PASS.
- ISOLDE voice — `"I was told to walk."` (5 단어) / `"I am close."` (3 단어) / `"You wanted me changed."` (4 단어). 모두 restrained·자기 변명 0. PASS.
- HALDREN voice — `"Walk inside. No turning back."` — formal, short. PASS.
- 번역체 0건. 변경 없음.

#### EP02
