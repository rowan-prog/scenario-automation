# THE OFFERING — EP03: THE WALL

S#1 — DRAGONKEEP / NIGHT GARDEN / NIGHT

[Visual]
ISOLDE walks alone along a narrow garden path along the inner wall. Black stone, low frostfire torches, a single thorn tree. Her **black silk and pearl** gown catches the torchlight. From behind a column an ASSASSIN steps out — human kingdom colours stripped from his coat, blade already low at his hip. ISOLDE sees him. She does not run. She turns to face him.

[Camera]
TRACKING ISOLDE along the path → CLOSE on a column passing → WHIP PAN as the ASSASSIN steps out → CLOSE on the blade at his hip → CLOSE on ISOLDE's face turning toward him, not flinching → ECU on her hand finding the pendant inside her bodice.

[DIALOGUE]
ASSASSIN: Princess.
ISOLDE: I am not your princess.
ASSASSIN: My king disagrees.
ISOLDE: Then your king should have done it himself.

[FX]
Boot on gravel. Steel sliding from leather. Wind hissing through the thorn tree. Her single steady inhale.

S#2 — DRAGONKEEP / COURTYARD / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
The ASSASSIN lunges. A scaled black hand intercepts the blade mid-air — VAEL has come down the wall behind ISOLDE without sound. His scaled fingers close around the blade. The blade snaps in his fist like dry wood. The ASSASSIN's face breaks. VAEL throws him backward into the dust. KIRAN steps out of the shadow at the gate and drags the man away. VAEL does not look at the body being hauled off. He looks at ISOLDE. The scale plates along the back of his hand are darker than at the start of the scene.

[Camera]
WIDE of the courtyard, the ASSASSIN lunging → INSERT on the scaled hand catching the blade → ECU on the blade snapping → CLOSE on the ASSASSIN's face → TRACKING KIRAN dragging him off → CLOSE on VAEL's hand, the dark scale deepening → CLOSE on his eyes finding ISOLDE.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: You did not run.
ISOLDE: I was deciding which of you was worse.
VAEL: And?
ISOLDE: I'm still deciding.
VAEL: Inside.

[FX]
Blade snapping crack. Assassin's body hitting the dust. KIRAN's boots dragging. VAEL's slow breath. A low growl from somewhere under the keep.

S#3 — DRAGONKEEP / ISOLDE'S CHAMBER / NIGHT

[Visual]
The door slams open and shut. VAEL walks her backward across the room. Her back hits the wall. His scaled hand closes around a fistful of her hair at the nape and tilts her head up. His other scaled hand finds the silver-piped neckline of her gown and pulls the **dress shoulder strap down off her left shoulder**. Scaled fingers press flat against her collarbone, then her pulse point at the side of her throat. He bends and presses his mouth to her mouth — hard, no warmup. When he draws back his teeth catch her lower lip briefly. Then he turns her head with the fistful of hair and sets his mouth against the side of her neck. He bites. Not breaking skin — but enough to leave a clean tooth-shaped mark blooming red at the side of her throat. His scaled hand finds her inner wrist, where the first scale-light bloomed, and brings the wrist up between them. He lays his mouth against the scale-light mark and the mark pulses bright under his lips. ISOLDE's breath shudders out.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: They sent a knife after you.
ISOLDE: They will send another.
VAEL: They will fail.
ISOLDE: Why are you doing this?
VAEL: Because you stood still.

[FX]
Door slamming, latch failing to catch. Silk pulling against pearl thread. His low exhale at her throat. Bite contact — soft, low. Her one shaky inhale. A faint hum of light from the wrist mark.

S#4 — DRAGONKEEP / ISOLDE'S CHAMBER / NIGHT (CONTINUOUS)

[Visual]
VAEL releases her. He steps back one full pace. The shoulder strap is still down off her shoulder. The bite mark at her throat is visible. The scale-light at her wrist glows faintly. His own scaled hand has darkened plate along the back; he tucks it behind his back. He does not look at her again. He turns and walks out. The door closes. ISOLDE stays against the wall, one hand pressed to her own throat at the bite. The pendant inside her bodice flickers once, alone, on her chest.
