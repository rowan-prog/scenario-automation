# THE OFFERING — EP06: THE RIGHT HAND

S#1 — DRAGONKEEP / GREAT HALL / DAY

[Visual]
The Great Hall in daylight. Court assembled — courtiers in long rows, candidate women in a row of their own, ELARA already seated in the chair at the LEFT of the throne (the candidate position). KIRAN stands at the wall. HALDREN stands at the human-kingdom row. The throne dais holds two chairs — VAEL's seat at centre, the second chair at the right. The second chair is empty. ISOLDE is not yet in the hall.

[Camera]
WIDE of the hall, the assembly rows → PAN across the courtier faces → CLOSE on ELARA at the left chair, smug → CLOSE on KIRAN at the wall → CLOSE on HALDREN in the row → TILT to the empty right chair on the dais.

[DIALOGUE]
ELARA (to a candidate next to her): He has not arrived.
CANDIDATE: She has not arrived either.
ELARA: She does not need to.
CANDIDATE: Where is she.
ELARA: Hiding what she's been doing.

[FX]
Low murmur. Court fans rustling. A herald's staff tapping the floor twice. Silence.

---

S#2 — DRAGONKEEP / GREAT HALL / ENTRY — ISOLDE'S CHOICE

[Visual]
The side door — not the main door — opens. ISOLDE enters from the side aisle alone. **Black silk and pearl gown.** Her hair is dressed simply, the two bite marks at her throat partly visible under the high collar. The dark-red wrist mark visible on her hand. She does not walk down the centre aisle. She walks up the side stairs of the dais without ceremony, crosses behind VAEL's empty seat, and sits in the right-hand chair before anyone has announced her. The hall goes silent. ELARA stands halfway up before realising and sits down again.

[Camera]
TRACKING ISOLDE in from the side door → CLOSE on her face, calm → INSERT on her wrist mark visible at her hand → TRACKING her up the dais stairs → CLOSE on ELARA half-standing → CLOSE on ELARA sitting back down, frozen → WIDE of the dais, ISOLDE in the right chair, the centre chair still empty.

[DIALOGUE]
HERALD: My lady — the lord has not —
ISOLDE: Then he will arrive to find me waiting.
HERALD: That is not the protocol.
ISOLDE: The protocol changed.
ELARA: It did not.
ISOLDE: It did.

[FX]
Side door opening, heavy. Her single step on the stair. The hall going still all at once. A second herald's tap on the floor. ELARA's chair creaking.

---

S#3 — DRAGONKEEP / GREAT HALL / DAIS — VAEL ARRIVES

[Visual]
The main door opens. VAEL enters in his **black leather, silver-thread, dark cloak**. He walks the centre aisle. His eyes find ISOLDE in the right chair before they find anything else. He does not break stride. He climbs the dais and sits in the centre chair. He reaches across the small distance between the chairs and takes her hand. He lifts it slowly. He bends and lays his mouth on the back of her hand — then turns the wrist and lays his mouth along the line of the dark-red scale-light mark on her inner wrist, slowly, all the way from heel of hand to wrist crease. The mark brightens under his lips. The whole hall sees it. His other scaled hand closes on a single long lock of her hair at her shoulder, and slowly draws down its full length while his mouth is still on her wrist. He releases. HALDREN, at the row, bows his head — a slight bow, the first true one.

[Camera]
WIDE of the centre aisle, VAEL entering → TRACKING VAEL up the aisle → CLOSE on his eyes finding her in the right chair → TRACKING him up onto the dais → CLOSE on him sitting → ECU on his hand reaching for hers → CLOSE on her hand placed in his → ECU on his mouth on the back of her hand → TRACKING his mouth along the inner wrist scale-light → ECU on the mark brightening under his lips → CLOSE on his other scaled hand drawing down the lock of her hair → WIDE of the hall reacting → CLOSE on HALDREN bowing his head → CLOSE on ELARA, face stone.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: You did not wait for me.
ISOLDE: I am tired of waiting.
VAEL: Good.

[FX]
Boots on the centre aisle. Throne creaking under his weight. Soft contact of his mouth on her wrist. A held breath across the hall. Pearl thread snagging once on his cuff. A faint hum from the wrist mark.

S#4 — DRAGONKEEP / GREAT HALL / END

[Visual]
A distant horn — long, low, a kingdom's emissary horn — sounds from beyond the gate. The hall heads turn. A herald rushes in from the main door. KIRAN steps forward from the wall. VAEL does not release ISOLDE's hand. He turns his face to the herald. ISOLDE's hand stays in his.

[Camera]
WIDE of the hall, heads turning to the door → CLOSE on the herald rushing in → CLOSE on KIRAN stepping forward → CLOSE on VAEL's hand still over hers → CLOSE on VAEL's face turning to the herald → CLOSE on ISOLDE's face, settled — Hard Cut.

[DIALOGUE]
HERALD: My lord — a second emissary.
KIRAN: With twelve.
VAEL: Twelve what.
KIRAN: Armed.

[FX]
Horn from beyond the gate, long and low. Herald's boots running. KIRAN's gauntlet at his side. The hall going still again. A single deep growl from outside the keep — not human.

Hard Cut.
