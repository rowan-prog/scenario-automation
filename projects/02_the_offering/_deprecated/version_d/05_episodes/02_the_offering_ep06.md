# THE OFFERING — EP06: She Walked Up

S#1 — KEEP / GREAT HALL / EVENING

[Visual]
The Great Hall is filling. The iron throne. The low iron bench to its right. Two retainer columns thicker than ever — twenty per side.
ELARA stands near the throne, one step back from her usual mark — the iron bench has pushed her line.
KIRAN at the foot of the dais. He carries a folded report. He sets it on the dais step at VAEL's feet without going up.
VAEL is already on the throne. His **scaled fingers tap the iron arm closest to the bench — the slow four-beat from EP02**.
The far doors open.

S#2 — KEEP / GREAT HALL / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
ISOLDE walks in alone. **Vael's Choice silk, the two stacked crescent bites at the side of her neck above the silver chain, the wrist-glow line bright on her left forearm, palm-up at her side.**
She does not stop at the foot of the dais this time.
She walks straight to the three steps and **climbs them on her own**. No hand offered. No hand taken.
She walks past KIRAN — past ELARA — past the iron throne — and **sits on the low iron bench beside VAEL's throne**, on her own.
She turns her wrist palm-up on her thigh — the **glow line is now a thumb-and-finger long, deep red-gold**, the dark-red trace VAEL printed under the skin in the colonnade still bright along its length.
ELARA's gold cuff drops two inches at her side.
KIRAN's mouth twitches — almost a smile.
VAEL has not looked at ISOLDE while she climbed. He looks at her now — only after she sits.

VAEL: It is.

S#3 — KEEP / GREAT HALL / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
VAEL stands from the throne. Not to step down — to **turn toward her on the dais level**.
He takes her **left hand** off her thigh — the one with the glow line — and lifts it palm-up. The whole hall sees the glow.
He bends and **presses his mouth to the back of her hand**. Slow. Not a courtly graze — a held closure.
Then he **turns her hand over** — palm up — and **drags his mouth along the length of the glow line**, from the heel of her palm to the crease of the wrist, **his lips covering the dark-red trace** all the way.
At the crease he **opens his mouth** — full, slow — and stays there a beat.
**The wrist-glow ignites** — bright enough that the dais lamps dim against it for one breath. The dark-red trace under the skin **pulses once with him**.
He lifts off the wrist.
His **other scaled hand** finds the **end of her hair** at her shoulder — gathers a strand — and **slides his hand down the length of it, slow, all the way to the tip**, the way you sheathe a blade. The hair-strand drops back across her shoulder.
The two stacked crescent bites at the side of her neck are **right above the silver chain, in full hall light**.

ISOLDE: Say it again.
VAEL: Mine.

S#4 — KEEP / GREAT HALL / NIGHT

[Visual]
The far doors open.
**HALDREN** enters — alone this time, no escort, the envoy chain still at his collar but **unhooked at the link**, hanging open. He has walked the basalt floor from the outer gate without ceremony.
He sees: ISOLDE on the iron bench beside VAEL, hand still in VAEL's. The hair-strand sheath. The two crescent bites in full hall light. The wrist-glow.
He stops a step short of the dais.
He **lowers his head** — a slow, deliberate quarter-bow — not deep, but enough. The envoy chain swings open at his collar.
ELARA's eyes go to HALDREN, then to ISOLDE, then to the empty step where ELARA herself used to stand. Her jaw locks shut.
ISOLDE has not let go of VAEL's hand.

End Image: HALDREN at the foot of the dais, head lowered a quarter-bow, envoy chain hanging open at the collar. ISOLDE on the iron bench, hand still in VAEL's, two crescent bites in full light, wrist-glow bright. ELARA's jaw locked.
