# THE OFFERING — EP02: The Seat Beside

S#1 — KEEP / HALDREN'S APPROACH / NIGHT

[Visual]
HALDREN — **grey-haired envoy in human-kingdom slate-and-grey livery, kingdom sigil at the chest** — walks the basalt corridor toward the great hall, two writs under his arm. The polished floor doubles him as he goes.
A small black-glass alcove off the corridor. KIRAN is waiting in it. He steps out — Haldren startles, hand to his belt.
KIRAN extends one open palm. The writs do not transfer. Yet.

[Camera]
TRACKING beside HALDREN's boots on mirror basalt, his reflection walking under him → ECU on the writ-seal at his chest → SMASH from black-glass alcove: KIRAN's silver cuirass entering frame → MEDIUM TWO-SHOT: KIRAN's open palm, HALDREN holding the writs back

[DIALOGUE]
HALDREN: I will read them to your lord. Not to a captain.
KIRAN: Then read them slowly. He has chosen where she will sit.
HALDREN: Where she sits is not his to choose. She is offered.
KIRAN: She walked in on her own.

S#2 — ISOLDE'S CHAMBER / NIGHT

[Visual]
A high stone room. One full-length **black-glass mirror** stands against the wall — polished obsidian, no silver. ISOLDE stands in front of it in her ivory gown. The mirror gives her back as a dark twin: ivory glowing against the obsidian field.
A maid lays out the **Royal Arrival** look on a basalt bench. Beside it, untouched, a second gown waits, folded — black silk base, white pearl trim along the bodice, silver-and-pearl band for the throat, fine white embroidery down the seams. **Vael's Choice — laid out, not yet worn.**
ISOLDE lifts the pendant from inside her cloak's collar lining. The faded sigil catches the lamp. She presses it once to the obsidian mirror, watches the doubled sigil — pendant face and reflected face — meet.

[Camera]
WIDE LOW: chamber, obsidian mirror full height, ISOLDE small in front of it → MEDIUM PROFILE: maid laying gowns → INSERT MACRO: black silk base + pearl trim folded → ECU: pendant lifted to the mirror surface, the two sigils nearly touching → REVERSE on ISOLDE's face — the obsidian reflection over her shoulder, twinned

[DIALOGUE]
MAID: My lord left the new one. He said only — when you choose.
ISOLDE: When I choose.
MAID: Yes, Lady.
ISOLDE: Then I have not yet.

[FX]

S#3 — GREAT HALL / DAIS / EVENING

[Visual]
The hall is full now. Two long retainer tables. ELARA stands at the right of the throne, ivory-and-gold, hand resting on the throne arm as if marked.
The iron throne dais has a second seat at its right — low, basalt, narrow. Empty.
VAEL is on the throne. His **face is now given to the room** for the first time: grey eyes, the line of jaw, dark hair tied back. Black leather, long black cloak. His scaled hand rests on the throne arm. The polished armrest under it is a small black mirror. The scaled fingers and their reflection meet.
ISOLDE enters at the far end — still in the **Royal Arrival** ivory gown, the **Vael's Choice** untaken. The hall flinches at the white against the black floor.
HALDREN reads from a writ from the floor below.

[Camera]
WIDE on the throne dais, second seat empty → ECU on VAEL's grey eye opening on Isolde at the far end → CLOSE PROFILE: ELARA's hand tightening on the throne arm → LOW WIDE: ISOLDE walking the mirror floor, her ivory doubled under her → MACRO INSERT on her wrist: the crescent print still faintly there → ECU on HALDREN reading

[DIALOGUE]
HALDREN: By treaty of the human crown, the offering is delivered. The seat she takes in your hall is the seat of an offering.
VAEL (low, slow, formal): The seat of an offering is not in my hall.
HALDREN: My lord —
VAEL: I have set a second seat beside mine. You will watch which one she chooses, and you will not name it for her.

[FX]
writ unrolling · two columns of retainers held in stillness · low torch-line breath · the scrape of a small basalt seat already in place


S#4 — GREAT HALL / DAIS / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
ISOLDE walks the length of the hall. She does not look at ELARA. She does not look at HALDREN. She looks at the second seat.
She reaches the foot of the dais. Two steps up — the small basalt seat at VAEL's right.
She lifts her ivory hem. She steps up. She sits in the second seat.
VAEL's scaled fingers drum the throne arm once — a single, weighted beat — and then his hand turns and rests on the back of hers where it lies on the basalt seat. He does not grip. He covers.
The polished throne-arm beside them mirrors the two hands stacked.
On the back of his hand, the crescent prints once. On her inner wrist, the same beat answers — under the ivory cuff.
HALDREN's face goes still. ELARA's smile cracks at the corner.

End Image: ISOLDE seated at the right of the throne in ivory, VAEL's scaled hand covering hers, both hands doubled in the polished throne arm. Hall held in stillness.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (low, slow, formal): The seat beside me was empty for a long time. It is no longer.
ISOLDE: You did not ask.
VAEL: I will not ask you for what you have already given me by walking up the steps.
HALDREN: My lord. This is irregular.
VAEL: It is what walked into this hall.
ELARA: (low, to herself) — second seat —
