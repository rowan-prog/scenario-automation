# THE OFFERING — EP06: The Hall Sees

S#1 — GREAT HALL / DAIS / NIGHT

[Visual]
The hall is fuller than in EP02. HALDREN at the floor below the dais. A second envoy beside him — **MAREN, a younger human-kingdom officer in a sharper grey livery, hair severely tied back, kingdom sigil polished**. Two writs in MAREN's hand.
ELARA at the right of the throne, hand on the throne arm, knuckles white now.
VAEL on the throne. His scaled hand on the throne arm. The polished armrest mirrors the hand. The second seat at his right — empty. Waiting.
ISOLDE has not yet entered.
The polished basalt floor in front of the dais is a long black mirror. The doors at the far end of the hall throw a sliver of light across it.

[DIALOGUE]
MAREN: My lord. I am sent in addition to the envoy already present. The crown has read the burning of its banner. It does not consider it answered.
VAEL (low, slow, formal): It has not been answered. It has been begun.
HALDREN: My lord. The second writ asks for the return of the offering.
VAEL: She is no longer that word.

S#2 — GREAT HALL / DAIS / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
The hall doors open. ISOLDE enters alone.
**Vael's Choice** — black silk base, silver-and-pearl band at the throat, ivory cuffs. But the band tonight sits **deliberately low**. The collarbone bite from EP05 is uncovered above the band — visible to anyone the length of the hall away. The ivory cuff at the right wrist is also slightly drawn back — the wrist bite shows at the cuff edge.
She walks the length of the polished mirror floor. Her ivory-trimmed black silk is doubled under her — black on black, white pearl trim doubled.
MAREN catches sight of the collarbone bite. His writ-paper folds slightly in his hand. HALDREN's grey livery throat catches.
ELARA's knuckles release the throne arm and find her own collarbone, where there is none.

[Camera]

[DIALOGUE]
HALDREN: (low to MAREN) Do not name what you see.
MAREN: It is already named.
HALDREN: Then read the second writ now. Before — before —
MAREN: I have not the breath.

S#3 — GREAT HALL / DAIS / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
ISOLDE reaches the foot of the dais. She does not pause. Two steps up. She sits in the second seat — same seat as EP02, this time **fully chosen**, no question in the room.
She turns her body slightly toward VAEL. She lifts her **right hand** with the cuff drawn back at the wrist. She sets it, palm down, on the throne arm beside his scaled hand.
He does not move his hand to cover hers this time. He turns his hand under hers. **She** covers **his**.
The hall sees it. HALDREN drops his gaze. MAREN's writ-paper does not fold further — it simply lowers. ELARA's hand at her collarbone closes into a fist.
A thin **dark-red flame** climbs along the seams of his scaled fingers, contained, slow — and dies on the back of his hand under her palm.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Read the second writ, envoy.
MAREN: Lady —
ISOLDE: I will hear it from the seat I am in.
VAEL (low, slow, formal): The seat she is in is the one she has chosen twice.

S#4 — GREAT HALL / DAIS / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
MAREN tries to read. His voice carries the kingdom's words, but his eyes will not stop returning to the collarbone bite.
He stops. He folds the writ.
HALDREN steps half a pace away from him — disowning him in front of the hall.
KIRAN, at the dais foot, lifts his head slowly to ISOLDE on the second seat. He inclines — not a full bow, but a tilt that the hall sees. A first drake-house captain's tilt to her.
VAEL turns his head — slowly — to ISOLDE. He does not speak.
She does not look away from MAREN. Her hand stays on the back of his scaled hand. The collarbone bite catches the torch-light.

End Image: ISOLDE on the second seat, her hand over VAEL's scaled hand on the throne arm, collarbone bite visible above the silver-pearl band, MAREN's writ folded mid-read, KIRAN's first tilt toward her, HALDREN stepped away from his own envoy. The polished floor before the dais doubles all of it.

[DIALOGUE]
MAREN: — I cannot finish.
HALDREN: You will finish in the kingdom's voice or you will not return in it.
ISOLDE: Then he will return in another voice.
VAEL: He will return.
