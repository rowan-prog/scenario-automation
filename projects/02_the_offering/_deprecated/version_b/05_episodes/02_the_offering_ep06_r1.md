# THE OFFERING — EP06: SHE WALKS HERSELF

S#1 — BLACK CASTLE / ISOLDE'S CHAMBER / DAY

[Visual] Morning light through high arched windows. The black silk gown laid across the bed has a deep-cut neckline — Vael's earlier order delivered, no longer the high-cut style. Isolde stands in shift, the two bite-marks on the side of her neck and the trace of the first one on her collarbone visible without the gown. She looks at them in a long polished obsidian mirror — slow, deliberate. Her inner wrist is now permanently faint-gold, the mark no longer fading between pulses, with the thin scorched-line from the council chamber's flame still tracing above it. She lifts the deep-cut gown herself. Does not call the attendants. Two attendants enter — go still at the doorway. She finishes the lacing in front of the mirror.

[Camera] WIDE — chamber, light slanting, gown laid across bed → CLOSE — the bite-marks on her neck, the faint earlier mark on her collarbone → MACRO — her inner wrist, gold no longer fading, the thin scorched-line above the mark → MEDIUM — Isolde lifting the dark deep-cut gown herself → WIDE — attendants at the doorway, going still → CLOSE — Isolde's hands lacing the bodice in the mirror, eyes on her own marks.

[DIALOGUE]
ATTENDANT 1: My lady — shall we cover the throat.
ISOLDE: No.
ATTENDANT 2: The court will see.
ISOLDE: That is the point.

[FX] Silk over skin. The slow, even climb of her breath. Bells calling the hall — distant.

S#2 — BLACK CASTLE / GREAT HALL / DAY

[Visual] The court already seated. Vael at the throne — scaled hand on the high armrest of the seat to his right, the same chair from EP2. ELARA stands in the lower tier — gold gown, jaw set, no chair near the dais this time. Haldren at the side, hands folded. The double doors open. Isolde enters — alone — no escort. The deep-cut gown shows both bite-marks on the side of her neck and the faint earlier mark on her collarbone clearly under the wall-torches. The court intakes breath in one wave. She does not stop at the foot of the dais. She walks up the dais steps without invitation — and seats herself in the chair beside Vael's throne. The whole hall freezes.

[Camera] WIDE — the hall, court seated, Elara below the dais → MEDIUM — Vael's scaled hand on the high armrest, waiting → REVERSE WIDE — the doors opening, Isolde alone → CLOSE — the bite-marks on her neck visible under torchlight → TRACKING with Isolde down the center, no escort → CLOSE — Elara's jaw cracking → WIDE — Isolde climbing the dais steps without invitation → MEDIUM — Isolde lowering herself into the chair beside the throne → REVERSE — the court frozen in one wave.

[DIALOGUE]
COURTIER (whisper, low): She seats herself.
HALDREN (quiet, to himself): The lord did not call her.
ISOLDE (looking ahead, calm): He did not need to.

[FX] The wave of court breath. Her gown settling over the chair. The slow tap of Vael's scaled fingertip on the armrest — once, after she sits.

S#3 — BLACK CASTLE / GREAT HALL / DAIS / CONTINUOUS

[Visual] Vael does not look at the court. He turns in his throne toward Isolde — lifts her marked hand from the armrest of her chair. He raises it slowly into the wall-torch light — palm-down, then turns it palm-up. The pale gold mark on her inner wrist is steady now — no flicker, no fade — and the thin scorched-line traced over it by his flame remains, unfaded above the gold. He lowers his head and presses his mouth to the back of her hand first — slow, formal, holding too long. Then he turns her wrist and drags his lips along the inner wrist — directly over the gold mark — a slow trace from the heel of her palm to the hollow of her elbow. His other scaled hand has slid into the long ends of her dark-brown hair behind her — gathered a strand, threaded it through his fingers, drawn it slowly down across her bared collarbone. The court watches every inch of it. Elara's hand is white on the rail in front of her.

[Camera] WIDE — Vael turning in his throne toward Isolde → CLOSE — his scaled hand lifting her marked wrist from the armrest → MEDIUM — Vael lowering his head, mouth to the back of her hand → MACRO — his lips on the back of her hand, holding too long → CLOSE — his mouth dragging slowly along the gold mark on her inner wrist → MACRO — the scorched-line over the gold, unfaded → MACRO — his other scaled fingers drawing a strand of her dark-brown hair down across her bared collarbone → REVERSE — the court watching, Elara's white-knuckled hand on the rail → CLOSE — Haldren's eyes lowering.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (low, against her wrist): The mark holds.
ISOLDE: I know.
VAEL: Then say it where they can hear.
ISOLDE (to the hall, even): The mark holds.

[FX] The slow drag of his mouth on the soft of her wrist. The hush of the court — no breath in. The faint pulse of the gold mark, steady. A single far wing-beat — answering, close.

S#4 — BLACK CASTLE / GREAT HALL / CONTINUOUS

[Visual] Vael lowers her wrist back to the armrest — does not let go. Haldren steps from the side wall — crosses to the foot of the dais — and BOWS. Not a courtier's small bow. A formal bow, head dropping, hands folded. Elara, on the lower tier, has gone perfectly still. Her gold gown trembles once. Behind the great doors at the far end of the hall, a HERALD strides in — fresh from the road, dust on his cloak, ivory-and-gold colors. A SECOND human-kingdom envoy. His banner is larger than the first. He stops at the doorway. The hall goes still around him. Vael's scales darken — blood-red threading deep — but he does not let go of Isolde's wrist.

[Camera] MEDIUM — Vael lowering her wrist to the armrest, his hand staying → TRACKING — Haldren crossing to the foot of the dais → CLOSE — Haldren bowing, head dropping → REVERSE — Elara on the lower tier, gold gown trembling → WIDE — the great doors, a herald entering with a larger banner → CLOSE — Vael's scales darkening on Isolde's wrist → REVERSE — Isolde's eyes lifting to the herald at the far end → END FRAME — Vael's scaled hand still over hers, the herald in the doorway behind.

[DIALOGUE]
HALDREN: My lady. The kingdom recognizes.
HERALD (from the doorway, loud): The king of the human kingdom asks audience.
VAEL: Let him be the one to wait.

[FX] Haldren's voice clean and slow. The new herald's voice cutting the silence. The slow groan of Vael's scales — dark.
