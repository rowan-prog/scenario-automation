# EP1 — THE GATE

## S#1 — IRON GATE / NORTHERN WASTE / DAWN

[VISUAL / ACTION]
A wagon stops at a black iron gate that rises out of grey waste. Twelve human escorts in IRONHOLD livery dismount. They tip a wooden crate forward. The crate doors fall open in the mud.

ISOLDE steps down on her own foot. Tall. Steady. Ivory silk gown, white fur cloak, pearl drops at the ears, dark brown hair loose past the shoulders, grey-green eyes calm. Against the grey waste her white reads like a struck match.
