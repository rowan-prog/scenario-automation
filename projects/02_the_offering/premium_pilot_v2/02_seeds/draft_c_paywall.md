## S#3 — KEEP STEPS / ROAR / CONTINUOUS

[VISUAL / ACTION]
Two wing-shadows low over the forecourt. The light dims a beat. Twelve IRONHOLD ESCORTS drop to one knee at the same beat. ENVOY HEAD steps back, then kneels. The writ falls from his hand to the cobble — face down.

KIRAN at VAEL's right doesn't kneel — lowers his head to ISOLDE for a full beat.

VAEL turns to ISOLDE. Fistful of her hair at the nape, public. Tips her head. Scaled palm covers the three bites at her throat — covering and showing.

## S#5 — KEEP STEPS / PAYWALL / CONTINUOUS

[VISUAL / ACTION]
ISOLDE looks at the writ in the ENVOY HEAD's hand. She does not take it. She looks at VAEL. VAEL looks at her. He does not take it.

VAEL turns ISOLDE's face to him by the hand at her nape. He doesn't kiss her. He lowers his mouth to a finger's width from hers. Holds.

[KEY CAMERA]
ECU: writ — face up — in ENVOY HEAD's hand on his knee. (Audience-only: a single name visible on the writ — not the name ISOLDE has been called all her life. The audience reads it. ISOLDE does not yet.)
ECU: VAEL's mouth a finger's width from ISOLDE's.
ECU: ISOLDE's hand on the back of his scaled hand at her throat — holding.
WIDE (short): forecourt — twelve kneeling, banner ash, writ in the air between them.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: She is —
(Third roar — overhead — cuts him.)
ENVOY HEAD: My lord — the name —
VAEL: Quiet.
ISOLDE: (low, to VAEL only) Say my name.
VAEL: …
(Fourth roar — longest — overhead. ISOLDE's hand on Vael's scaled hand tightens.)

[END HOOK]
VAEL's mouth opens. The name does not come. ISOLDE looks down — at the writ — at the name on it. She sees it for the first time. Her face does not change.

Hard Cut
