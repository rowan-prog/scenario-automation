# THE OFFERING — EP10: Witness

S#1 — VAEL'S KEEP / OUTER RAMPARTS / DAWN

[Visual]
Twelve drakes on the ramparts. Their wings folded but visible. Massive bodies — each as long as the keep wall is high. KIRAN walks between them, calm, hand resting on the shoulder of one as he passes. The drakes do not move when he passes. They watch ISOLDE — who has emerged onto the rampart in a dark cloak, the wrist marks visible at her sleeve cuff.

The closest drake lowers its head. Slowly. Toward her.

ISOLDE does not step back. She raises her left hand — the marked one. The drake's snout meets her palm. Its breath is warm. The other eleven turn their heads to watch.

KIRAN: Bend.

ISOLDE bends one knee — just one — and lays her hand on the side of the drake's jaw. The drake closes its eyes once. Then opens them. The eleven other drakes lower their heads in sequence. A wave.

S#2 — VAEL'S KEEP / WAR ROOM / MIDDAY

[Visual]
The maps from EP7 are replaced with new ones. Velden's positions marked in red ink — armies mobilizing. The hourglass from EP6 sits empty on the table corner. A new one beside it — sand falling, just begun.

VAEL at the head. KIRAN at his right. HALDREN — still in the keep, not departed — at the table, on the kingdom side but no longer wearing kingdom colors. ISOLDE stands at the window. She watches the road south.

HALDREN: He has called the second instrument.
VAEL: Name it.
HALDREN: My king's brother.
KIRAN: The disinherited one.
HALDREN: He keeps a private army at the border. Older than the crown's army. Smaller. Better.
VAEL: How many.
HALDREN: Three hundred. Mounted. Trained against beasts.
KIRAN: Twelve drakes against three hundred trained against beasts.
HALDREN: My lord — they were trained in the last drake war.

Silence. VAEL looks at ISOLDE at the window.

VAEL: How long.
HALDREN: Five days to the keep.

S#3 — VAEL'S KEEP / VAEL'S WING / DUSK

[Visual]
A larger chamber than EP3's. Vael's wing. Dark stone, wide bed. ISOLDE has been moved here — formally — at midday. Her cloak is on a chair. The five marks are visible: bite above collar, two stacked wrist crescents + flame-curve on left wrist, bite + flame-curve on right collarbone.

VAEL enters. He stops at the door.

VAEL: Five days.
ISOLDE: I heard.
VAEL: They are good. The brother's men.
ISOLDE: How good.
VAEL: Good enough.

He crosses to her. Stops one pace away. He raises his scaled hand. He does not touch her. He hovers it just above the new flame-curve on her right collarbone.

VAEL: I will not have you in a war.
ISOLDE: You already do.
VAEL: I mean — not as a prize.
ISOLDE: I came here to die. I am not a prize.

She steps forward. She closes the one pace. Her chest meets his scaled hand. His palm settles on the new bite mark + new flame-curve. The flame-curve under his palm pulses bright once.

VAEL: Mate.
ISOLDE: Mate.

He bends. Kisses her. Long. His other hand — gauntleted — at her lower back. Pulls her against him. Her hands rise — one fists in his collar, one rests at the side of his face. The kiss does not break.

S#4 — VAEL'S KEEP / RAMPART / NIGHT

[Visual]
Far across the valley, where the single fire was at the end of EP9 — now there are five fires. Spread in a line. The brother's army is on the road.

Below the rampart, the drake-pack has moved from outside the keep to the inner courtyard — they sleep now, twelve curled bodies, in the courtyard. They are guarding from inside.

ISOLDE and VAEL stand at the rampart. Behind them, KIRAN posts a single drake on the far end. ELARA is gone — she left the keep at noon, on the kingdom road, returning home. Her absence is noted.

ISOLDE: She left.
VAEL: She knew.
ISOLDE: Knew what.
VAEL: That she was not it.

ISOLDE turns. She looks at the five fires.

ISOLDE: Five days.
VAEL: Four.

He raises the scaled hand. Across his knuckles, in the night, the gold thread under the scales is brighter than before. The paired gold and her wrist crescents pulse together — visible to each other now, not just audience-only.
