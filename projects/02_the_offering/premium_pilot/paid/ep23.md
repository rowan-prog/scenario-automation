# THE OFFERING — EP23: The Price Begins

S#1 — VELDEN CITY / OLD PALACE / DAWN

[Visual]
ALDRIC in the throne room. The kingdom crown is absent (it's at Vael's keep). Aldric wears a smaller, simpler dark band on his head — a regent's circlet, not the crown. He is alone with three advisors and a court-magus.

ALDRIC: Find them all.
COURT-MAGUS: All old line carriers.
ALDRIC: All.
ADVISOR 1: My lord. If they have already gone to the keep —
ALDRIC: Bring back what they left behind. Books. Boxes. Anything bearing the sigil.
COURT-MAGUS: Burn them.
ALDRIC: Burn them.

The court-magus bows.

S#2 — DISTANT KINGDOM TOWN / NIGHT (PARALLEL)

[Visual]
A small stone house in a market town. Inside: an old woman — older than the eldest carrier at the keep — sits by a low fire. She holds a small carved box. There is a crash at the door. Six soldiers enter. Bone-clasp leather but with kingdom regular insignia, not the brother's army.

OLD WOMAN: I know why you have come.
LEAD SOLDIER: My king commands the line.
OLD WOMAN: My queen called it home.
LEAD SOLDIER: There is no queen.
OLD WOMAN: There is one. You are looking at her granddaughter.

She does not rise. She does not resist. The lead soldier looks at her carved box. He moves toward it. The old woman closes her eyes.

The box on her lap pulses once — bright. The soldier reaches for it. He freezes — hand mid-air. He cannot move closer.

OLD WOMAN: It will not come with you.

The lead soldier raises his sword. He brings it down on the box.

The box shatters. The contents — a pendant, a stone — fly across the room. The pendant lands at the soldier's foot. He picks it up. He drops it instantly — burned.

The stone lands at the old woman's feet. She picks it up. She holds it in her palm.

OLD WOMAN: Tell him I did not give it up.

The lead soldier raises his sword again — toward her this time.

S#3 — VAEL'S KEEP / TOWER ROOM / SAME MOMENT

[Visual]
ISOLDE alone with the stone. She wakes from something — a vision, a connection. Her left arm — the bracelet — has gone cold. She looks at it. It still glows but dimmer.

She reaches for the stone. She hums the three notes. She calls.

ISOLDE: Mother.
ISOLDE: I felt one go out.

A pause. The stone answers — voices, not just her mother's.

ISOLDE: He killed one of us tonight.

She closes her eyes. She listens.

ISOLDE: I cannot speak to her. She is gone.

Tears — quiet. Not loud. Her bracelet flickers.

She rises. She walks to the door.

ISOLDE (calling): KIRAN. Wake the pack.

S#4 — VAEL'S KEEP / INNER COURTYARD / NIGHT

[Visual]
The pack assembled. KIRAN, HALDREN, the three lieutenant women, ELARA. VAEL beside ISOLDE. ISOLDE in a hastily-thrown traveling cloak, bracelet glowing again brighter from anger.

ISOLDE: My uncle has begun the price. He is killing the carriers.
HALDREN: How many.
ISOLDE: One. So far. There will be more.
KIRAN: Tonight.
ISOLDE: Carrow.

Carrow steps forward.

ISOLDE: Carry me to the nearest known carrier still alive. Now.
VAEL: I come with you.
ISOLDE: Yes.

They mount. Carrow's wings open. Another drake — a fifth — comes forward for VAEL.

ISOLDE: KIRAN. Hold the keep.
KIRAN: My queen.

She does not correct him this time. She does not say "Not yet."
