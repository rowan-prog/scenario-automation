# THE OFFERING — EP37: A Western Grove

S#1 — VAEL'S KEEP / WAR ROOM / DAWN

[Visual]
KIRAN returns from a routine sweep. He carries grim news.

KIRAN: A western grove was burned overnight.
ISOLDE: Burned.
KIRAN: With the carrier inside.
ISOLDE: He found her.
KIRAN: Yes.
ISOLDE: How did he reach her before we did.
KIRAN: She was very old. Eighty. She did not move quickly.
ISOLDE: Was she alone.
KIRAN: She lived alone.
COURT-MAGUS: He has dead pieces from her tree. He is rebuilding.

A silence.

ISOLDE: I should have moved faster.
VAEL: We could not be everywhere.
ISOLDE: The stain wins when I am slow.

She looks at her wrist. The bracelets are still bright but she is paler today.

ISOLDE: We hunt him now.

S#2 — VAEL'S KEEP / TOWER ROOM / MORNING

[Visual]
ISOLDE alone with the stone. She hums. She reaches — looking for the dark robe through the line. The line resists. The line cannot find him — he is shielded.

ISOLDE: He hides.
ELARA: He hides well.
ISOLDE: Then we find him by other means.

She turns to the line tree's branch staff. The new tree at the keep is fully grown now.

ISOLDE: The tree will find him.
ELARA: How.
ISOLDE: The tree knows what is killed. He killed western grove. The tree will know where the smoke went.

She places her hand on the staff. The staff glows. She walks out — toward the line tree.

S#3 — VAEL'S KEEP / LINE TREE COURTYARD / MORNING

[Visual]
ISOLDE places the staff against the line tree's trunk. She speaks low — words inaudible — a query. The tree begins to glow. From the tree's branches, smoke-like gold tendrils extend outward — moving across the sky, searching.

The tendrils find a direction. They point — east-northeast.

ISOLDE: He is east-northeast.
COURT-MAGUS: There is an abandoned hill fort there. It is on the original map of the twelve sites.
ISOLDE: How far.
KIRAN: Six hours by drake.
ISOLDE: We leave in one hour.

She turns to VAEL.

ISOLDE: This time we do not let him escape.
VAEL: This time we do not.

S#4 — EAST-NORTHEAST HILL FORT / AFTERNOON

[Visual]
The drakes arrive over an abandoned hill fort — half-collapsed stone, isolated. At the fort's center: another counter-pole being assembled. The dark robe + four new acolytes (he has recruited fast).

This time, the dark robe sees the drakes coming. He has prepared. As the first drakes dive, dark light bursts up from the fort's perimeter — defensive wards. Three drakes are caught in the dark light. They land hard, wings smoking.

ISOLDE on Carrow circles outside the wards.

ISOLDE: Land beyond the wards.
KIRAN: My queen.
ISOLDE: We walk in.

She lands. She raises the staff. The staff glows. She walks toward the wards. The wards hiss. As her staff passes the boundary — the dark light parts for it.

She walks into the fort.
