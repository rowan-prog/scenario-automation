# THE OFFERING — EP13: The River

S#1 — VALLEY / OAKWATER RIVER / DAWN

[Visual]
A wide shallow river. The brother's army on the far bank — two hundred and seventy mounted. Forty-eight in lighter armor at the front, longer spears. The lead among the forty-eight is older, scarred face — the same lead scout from EP11. The dust has settled. They are formed in a wedge facing the keep side.

On the keep side: the keep's wall garrison plus twelve drakes. Carrow at the rear. The other eleven in a curved arc — wing-tips touching. VAEL stands at the center of the arc, on foot, scaled hand open.

ISOLDE is not present.

LEAD SCOUT (across the river): Yield her.
VAEL: She is not yours.
LEAD SCOUT: She is not yours either.
VAEL: She is herself.

The lead scout raises a hand. The forty-eight spears come level.

VAEL: Last word.
LEAD SCOUT: Yield her.

VAEL does not answer. He lowers his scaled hand to the river. The water touches his scales. He turns his palm up. The river ripples backward — against the current — toward the brother's army's side.

S#2 — OAKWATER RIVER / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
The river runs backward. Against the current. A wave rises against the brother's army's bank — small at first, then taller. The forty-eight spear-front breaks formation as horses shy. The lead scout shouts orders.

The eleven drakes lift off — one wing-beat each, synchronized. They are over the river in three beats. Carrow lifts from the rear, behind them.

The forty-eight drake-trained men set their spears. The first wave of drakes — six of them — dive low. The forty-eight loose their spears at the dive. Three drakes turn the spears with wing-edges. Three take the spears in the shoulder — minor wounds. The drakes' jaws open. Fire — short controlled bursts — onto the forty-eight's spear-line.

Sixteen of the forty-eight go down in the first pass. Twelve are burned. Four are crushed.

The remaining thirty-two retreat behind the cavalry line.

The drakes loop and turn. They do not pursue further. They return to the keep-side bank.

The lead scout sounds a horn — withdraw. The brother's army falls back from the river.

S#3 — VAEL'S KEEP / VAEL'S WING / EVENING (SAME DAY)

[Visual]
VAEL has returned. He has a long cut on his left arm — gauntleted side. Not deep but bleeding. ISOLDE meets him at the wing door. She looks at the cut. He has not bothered to bandage it.

ISOLDE: How many.
VAEL: Sixteen.
ISOLDE: Theirs.
VAEL: Theirs.
ISOLDE: Yours.
VAEL: Three drakes wounded. Light. Their backs.
ISOLDE: Your arm.
VAEL: Yes.

She takes him inside. She closes the door behind them. She sits him on the bed edge. She brings a basin of water and a strip of dark cloth. She rolls back his gauntleted sleeve.

The cut runs from elbow to wrist — clean, narrow.

She washes it. Slowly. Her fingers on the inside of his forearm — gentle. He watches her face, not the cut.

VAEL: First time.
ISOLDE: First what.
VAEL: First time someone has bound my arm.
ISOLDE: Two hundred years.
VAEL: Yes.

She presses the cloth against the cut. She binds it. She ties it. She holds her hand over the binding for a moment longer than necessary.

ISOLDE: Sleep tonight.
VAEL: Yes.

S#4 — VAEL'S KEEP / VAEL'S WING / DEEP NIGHT

[Visual]
The bed. VAEL asleep — actually asleep for the first time in days. ISOLDE awake. She lies on her side facing him. Her left wrist — the three marks — is between them on the bedding. Audience-only: the marks pulse with each of his breaths. Slow. Synchronized.

She does not sleep yet. She watches him.

After a long time, she reaches and brushes a strand of his dark hair from his temple. He does not wake. His scaled hand — at rest on the bedding — twitches once. The gold thread under the scales surfaces, then settles.

She rests her hand over his scaled hand.

She closes her eyes.
