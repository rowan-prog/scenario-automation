# THE OFFERING — EP48: Bond Manifest (HEAVY GATE)

S#1 — VELDEN CITY / OLD PALACE / GREAT SQUARE / DAWN (CORONATION DAY)

[Visual]
The great square — outside the palace and around the line tree. The tree is fully grown now — its branches arch over the entire square. The square is filling — thousands of citizens, the twelve southern houses' thirty-six unarmed lords, the keep's full force, the twenty-five carriers in a special circle around the tree's trunk, drakes on every visible rampart.

The dawn light is just beginning. The line tree begins to glow — softly, golden.

ISOLDE — at the palace doors — emerges. She wears a queen's dress — dark with gold-threaded sigil. The Velden crown is in her hands, not yet on her head. She walks slowly toward the tree.

VAEL emerges behind her. He wears formal dark robes — no court armor. He walks one pace behind her.

The crowd is silent.

S#2 — VELDEN CITY / GREAT SQUARE / LINE TREE / MORNING

[Visual]
ISOLDE reaches the line tree. The carriers form a circle around her. She stops at the foot. She raises the crown. She turns to face the crowd.

ISOLDE: I am the line. I am the queen. I take the crown.

She places the crown on her head.

The line tree pulses. Gold light cascades down through the branches over the square. Every wrist mark in the carriers glows. Every drake on every rampart roars — twelve from the keep, forty-eight from the wider valley, sixty total. The roars sustain.

The crown on her head — the same Velden crown she has worn before — transforms. Not visibly grander, but its dark metal turns to gold, and small new branches sprout from the crown itself — the crown becomes a circlet of small living gold-tree branches with gold leaves.

The southern lords — thirty-six — fall to their knees. Then the second row of crowd. Then the third. The whole square is on its knees.

VAEL alone stands beside her.

S#3 — VELDEN CITY / GREAT SQUARE / LINE TREE / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
The roars fade. The crowd remains kneeling. ISOLDE turns to VAEL.

ISOLDE: Mate-King.
VAEL: Yes.
ISOLDE: Stand here.

He walks to stand at her right side. She turns to the crowd.

ISOLDE: This is my mate. He is named Mate-King. He rules with me. Both keeps are ours. Velden city is ours. The line is mine. The dragon-bond is his. Together we hold the kingdom.

The crowd remains kneeling.

ISOLDE: And I will name what we pay for this crown.

A silence — anticipating.

ISOLDE: The line carries a price. The price is years. I will not see eighty. I will see perhaps fifty. Perhaps less. Velden knows this. Velden chose me knowing this.

The crowd murmurs — not in disagreement. In understanding.

ISOLDE: My mate — he carries some of the price with me. He will live longer than I will. But the bond will not let him live past me. We die together. Whenever the day comes.

VAEL bows his head once — public acknowledgment.

ISOLDE: Until that day — we rule.

The crowd rises in waves. One by one. They do not cheer. They simply rise. The acknowledgment is the rising.

S#4 — VELDEN CITY / GREAT SQUARE / EVENING

[Visual]
Coronation day continuing. ISOLDE and VAEL stand together at the line tree. The crowd circulates — many touch the tree's bark as they pass, many touch ISOLDE's hand or VAEL's hand. The carriers — twenty-five — are accepting witnesses one by one.

By evening, the southern lords have all sworn — one by one, kneeling in turn at ISOLDE's feet. Twelve houses, thirty-six lords. Each places his fist on the ground at her feet.

LAST SOUTHERN LORD: My queen.
ISOLDE: Rise.
LAST SOUTHERN LORD: We did not know what we waited for.
ISOLDE: You waited for the line to choose.
LAST SOUTHERN LORD: We did.
ISOLDE: The line has chosen.

He rises. He steps back.

ISOLDE and VAEL turn — they walk slowly back to the palace. The crowd does not stop. The witnessing continues into the night.
