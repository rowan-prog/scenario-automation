# THE OFFERING — EP28: Brother at the Gate

S#1 — VAEL'S KEEP / OUTER GATE / DAY (FIVE DAYS LATER)

[Visual]
The brother's army at the road — three hundred minus eighteen (the ones who returned to ALDRIC). The brother walks alone toward the gate. He carries the amulet. He stops at the threshold and sets it down. He spreads both hands — empty.

BROTHER: I come unarmed.
VAEL (at the gate): Walk in.

He walks in. The gate does not close behind him — his army at the road in formation, waiting, weapons sheathed.

S#2 — VAEL'S KEEP / ONYX HALL / DAIS / MIDDAY

[Visual]
ISOLDE on the high seat. The Velden crown — for the first time — on her head. The line tree visible through a window — already taller, branches spreading.

The brother enters. He stops at the foot of the dais. He looks at the crown on her head. He looks at the tree through the window.

He kneels. Both knees. Forehead to stone.

BROTHER: My queen.

A long silence. ISOLDE does not speak immediately.

ISOLDE: Rise.

He rises.

ISOLDE: Why.
BROTHER: Because you grew the tree.
ISOLDE: And before the tree.
BROTHER: Before the tree, I was waiting to see if you were really the line.
ISOLDE: I am.
BROTHER: I am yours.

S#3 — VAEL'S KEEP / INNER COURTYARD / AFTERNOON

[Visual]
The brother's army marches in. Three hundred minus eighteen. They form ranks in the courtyard around the line tree. They watch ISOLDE — she walks among them. Each rider sees her crown, the bracelets, the tree.

KIRAN positions twelve drakes around the courtyard — visible but not threatening.

LEAD SCOUT (the one from EP11·EP13): Queen.
ISOLDE: Lead Scout.
LEAD SCOUT: I commanded the men who broke the river. I take responsibility.
ISOLDE: You broke the river under orders.
LEAD SCOUT: I would do it again under different orders.
ISOLDE: Different.
LEAD SCOUT: Yours.
ISOLDE: Then you do so.

She walks past him. He bows shallow as she passes. The men around him bow shallow as she passes. All three hundred minus eighteen, in waves, bowing as she walks.

S#4 — VAEL'S KEEP / VAEL'S WING / NIGHT

[Visual]
ISOLDE removes the crown. She places it in the carved box. She does not look tired tonight — energy from the day. VAEL is at the corner table. The book is open.

VAEL: Aldric will hear by tomorrow.
ISOLDE: Yes.
VAEL: He will move within three days.
ISOLDE: Yes.
VAEL: How.
ISOLDE: I do not know yet.
VAEL: Whatever he does — we meet him.
ISOLDE: Whatever he does.

She crosses to him. She lays her hand on the book.

ISOLDE: The price.
VAEL: I have not forgotten.
ISOLDE: We do not know what it is.
VAEL: No.

A pause.

ISOLDE: When I held the seed today — when the tree grew — I felt something draining.
VAEL: What.
ISOLDE: Not strength. Time.
VAEL: Time.
ISOLDE: My time.

A longer pause.

VAEL: How much.
ISOLDE: I do not yet know.

He pulls her to him. He does not speak. Hard Cut.
