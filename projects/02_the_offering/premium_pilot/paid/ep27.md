# THE OFFERING — EP27: The Court-Magus

S#1 — VAEL'S KEEP / ONYX HALL / DAIS / MIDDAY

[Visual]
A single hooded rider arrives at the keep — unannounced, alone. He carries the kingdom's blacker seal. KIRAN admits him reluctantly. The figure stops at the foot of the dais. He pulls back the hood.

The court-magus from EP23. Older than VAEL's keep age, but human-old, not unlined.

COURT-MAGUS: My king sends terms.
ISOLDE: I have given terms. He refused them.
COURT-MAGUS: New terms.
ISOLDE: Speak.

He raises a small bundle. He sets it on the dais step. He unwraps it. A withered branch — once silver. The line tree.

COURT-MAGUS: He has cut the tree of the line.
ISOLDE (rising): The —
COURT-MAGUS: The first tree. From the first queen.

She crosses down to the step. She picks up the branch. It is dead. Cold.

ISOLDE: When.
COURT-MAGUS: Three nights ago. After he burned the carriers, he turned to the tree.
ISOLDE: The line still answers.
COURT-MAGUS: The line still answers for now. The tree dies — the line will dim, then fade. Over years.
ISOLDE: Then I will replant.
COURT-MAGUS: The seed is in my keeping.

He takes from his cloak — a small wooden box. Inside: a single seed, gold-veined.

COURT-MAGUS: I bring it. I leave my king.

S#2 — VAEL'S KEEP / TOWER ROOM / EVENING

[Visual]
ISOLDE in the tower. The seed sits on the stone floor. The dead branch beside it. The carved box open with all the items. She holds the seed in her palm. Carrow visits — pokes her head through the tower window. Carrow's gold trace flares at the jaw. The seed in ISOLDE's palm warms — heats — then sprouts. A small green thread emerges.

ISOLDE: It will grow.
COURT-MAGUS (entering with VAEL): It will grow in a queen's hand. Only there.
ISOLDE: Then I plant it.
COURT-MAGUS: Where.
ISOLDE: In my mate's keep. At the heart.
VAEL: At the courtyard.
COURT-MAGUS: A new tree.
ISOLDE: A new tree.

S#3 — VAEL'S KEEP / INNER COURTYARD / NIGHT

[Visual]
The twelve drakes assembled around the center of the courtyard. Carriers (twenty-two + the children) gathered around in a wider ring. KIRAN, HALDREN, ELARA, VAEL — all present.

ISOLDE kneels. She digs with her bare hands into the stone — the stone parts for her, soft. She places the seed in the depression. She covers it with her palm.

She hums the three notes.

The ground glows. The seed sprouts — visibly, fast. A small silver shoot. The shoot grows. In a minute it is a sapling. In another minute it is the height of a person. In another it has begun to branch.

The twelve drakes lower their heads in unison. The twenty-two carriers — and Mira and the other children — all kneel. KIRAN kneels. HALDREN kneels. ELARA kneels.

VAEL alone stands. ISOLDE alone stands.

She turns to him.

ISOLDE: The line is renewed.
VAEL: It is.

S#4 — VAEL'S KEEP / VAEL'S WING / NIGHT

[Visual]
ISOLDE and VAEL alone again. She is exhausted but lit. The bracelet is brighter than ever — and a new ring has appeared just above the bracelet. A second bracelet — finer.

ISOLDE: Look.
VAEL: A second mark.
ISOLDE: The line accepted me as queen.
VAEL: It marks you twice.
ISOLDE: I think it will mark me again when more of the line returns.
VAEL: How many marks.
ISOLDE: Until the line is whole.

She looks at him. He pulls her to him.

VAEL: I would have you with all of them.
ISOLDE: You will.
