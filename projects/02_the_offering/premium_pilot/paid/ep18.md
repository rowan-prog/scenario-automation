# THE OFFERING — EP18: Speak Through

S#1 — VAEL'S KEEP / TOWER ROOM / DAWN

[Visual]
A small tower room — highest point in the keep. Single window facing south. ISOLDE alone with the stone. ELARA at the door, not inside. VAEL on the rampart below, watching the southern road.

ISOLDE sits on the stone floor. She holds the stone between her palms. She hums the three notes. The stone warms. She thinks of her mother. She thinks of the brother.

Audience-only: a thin gold thread runs from the stone in her hands, out the window, across the valley, and lands on the brother's tent.

S#2 — KINGDOM ROAD / BROTHER'S CAMP / SIMULTANEOUS

[Visual]
The brother sits at his table. The amulet from EP12 is open on the velvet. He has been waiting. The amulet warms. He places his hand over it. He closes his eyes.

Audience-only: the gold thread enters the amulet. The brother's expression does not change.

BROTHER (eyes still closed): I hear you, niece.

ISOLDE (voice from the stone — audible to the brother only): Uncle.

BROTHER: You did not die.
ISOLDE: I was not sent to die. I was sent so you would be the heir.
BROTHER: You are correct.
ISOLDE: Did my mother know.
BROTHER: She knew when they put her in the ground.

A pause. The brother's expression does not change.

BROTHER: Come to me, niece. I will not harm you. You are blood.
ISOLDE: You harmed my mother.
BROTHER: My brother harmed her. I was on the road. I was not there.
ISOLDE: But you took the line afterward.
BROTHER: I held it for you. You were a child.
ISOLDE: You are holding it still.
BROTHER: Because you would not have known to take it.
ISOLDE: I know now.

S#3 — VAEL'S KEEP / TOWER ROOM / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
ISOLDE in the tower. The exchange continues — she hears him in her head and speaks back the same way. ELARA at the door watches her but cannot hear.

BROTHER (through stone, in her mind): If you take the line, you become the queen they sent you to die to prevent.
ISOLDE: I know that.
BROTHER: My brother will not surrender the crown.
ISOLDE: Then he will lose it.
BROTHER: And the army at the river — that army is mine. I called them.
ISOLDE: Then call them off.
BROTHER: I will not. Because if you become queen, you become my queen. I will hold the army at the river until you say yes.
ISOLDE: I will not say yes.
BROTHER: Then I come for you myself. In three days.
ISOLDE: Come.

She releases the stone. Her hands tremble. ELARA crosses the threshold — finally — and kneels beside her.

ELARA: Did he answer.
ISOLDE: He answered.
ELARA: What did he say.
ISOLDE: He comes in three days.

S#4 — VAEL'S KEEP / VAEL'S WING / NIGHT

[Visual]
ISOLDE tells VAEL. He listens.

VAEL: Three days.
ISOLDE: Three.
VAEL: He comes alone.
ISOLDE: He thinks I am blood and he comes for me as blood.
VAEL: He is blood. But he is not yours.
ISOLDE: No.
VAEL: I will be at the gate.
ISOLDE: I will be at the high seat.
VAEL: Yes.
ISOLDE: When he sees us together.
VAEL: He will know what is here.

A long silence. Then VAEL crosses to her. He takes her face in both hands — scaled and gauntleted.

VAEL: When he is gone.
ISOLDE: When he is gone.
VAEL: That night.

She presses her forehead to his. The five marks all glow steady.

ISOLDE: That night.
