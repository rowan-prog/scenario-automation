# THE OFFERING — EP09: She Is Mine

S#1 — VAEL'S KEEP / ONYX HALL / DAIS / CONTINUOUS FROM EP8

[Visual]
The braziers have gone out. The hall is dark except for the gold glow from ISOLDE's wrist marks and the new forming flame-curve on her collarbone. Her hand is still over VAEL's scaled hand at her own collarbone. The third wing-roar has just faded.

VAEL looks at her — closer than the half-inch he held in EP8 S#5. He moves the scaled hand from her collarbone to her jaw. He lifts. He bends. He finishes the line. He does not speak it aloud. He speaks it against her mouth.

VAEL: She is mine.

Then he closes the half-inch. His mouth on hers. Hard. Open. His tongue pushes in immediately. Her lips part for him. She gasps once. He swallows it. The braziers around the hall reignite — all twelve at once. The dark hum becomes gold light.

The court — the twelve armed escorts on their knees, HALDREN on one knee, the captain still standing — sees. ELARA at the back of the hall sees.

He breaks. A half-inch. His scaled thumb rests on her bottom lip.

S#2 — VAEL'S KEEP / ONYX HALL / DAIS / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
On ISOLDE's collarbone, under VAEL's scaled palm — gone now, moved to her jaw — the new flame-curve completes. A third mark. Now ISOLDE's wrist has crescent + crescent + flame-curve, and her collarbone has bite mark + flame-curve. Five marks total. The wing-roar outside settles into a low rumble.

The captain takes a step back. His hand is no longer on his sword. He looks at the kneeling escorts — they have not moved. He looks at HALDREN — one knee, head low.

CAPTAIN: I cannot.
VAEL: Cannot what.
CAPTAIN: Kneel.
VAEL: Then walk out.
CAPTAIN: My king.
VAEL: Has nothing here.

The captain looks at ISOLDE. ISOLDE meets his eyes.

ISOLDE: The kingdom no longer knows my name. (EP1 echo, deliberate)
CAPTAIN: It will burn for it.
ISOLDE: It already did.

She tilts her head toward the scorched iron pole visible through the open hall door — the one where the kingdom standard burned in EP4.

The captain turns. He walks out. HALDREN does not rise. KIRAN does not move. The twelve escorts remain on their knees.

S#3 — VAEL'S KEEP / ISOLDE'S CHAMBER / NIGHT

[Visual]
The chamber. ISOLDE sits on the bed. Her gown still half-open from the EP8 court display — the new bite mark on her collarbone visible. The new flame-curve completed beside it. VAEL stands at the door — does not cross. KIRAN has just left them. The door is closed.

VAEL takes one step forward. He stops.

ISOLDE: Come.

He crosses. Stops at the bed. Looks down at her. He kneels — one knee, beside the bed. Not subservient. Eye level. His scaled hand rests on the bed beside her thigh, not touching her.

VAEL: Tell me when.
ISOLDE: When what.
VAEL: When I stop.

She lifts her left hand — the one with three wrist marks — and lays it over his scaled hand. She presses it onto her thigh through the gown.

ISOLDE: Don't.

His scaled hand closes on her thigh — fabric, then the warmth through it. His gauntleted hand rises and undoes the upper clasp of her gown — the one above the EP3 bite mark. The fabric falls open further. He lays his gauntleted palm flat on her chest, just above the heart.

VAEL: Mate.

This time he says it as declaration, not question.

ISOLDE: Yes.

S#4 — VAEL'S KEEP / ISOLDE'S CHAMBER / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
VAEL rises from the kneel. He climbs onto the bed beside her. The scaled hand goes back to her hair — full fist this time, at the nape. The gauntleted hand cradles the side of her face. He bends. He kisses her again — this kiss is longer. His tongue is in her mouth immediately. She turns into him. Her hand goes to his chest. The other hand goes to the back of his neck.

His scaled palm slides down — from nape, to spine through the open back of her gown, to the small of her back. He pulls her against him. Her thigh slides over his.

His mouth breaks from hers. Goes to her throat. Bites again — at the pulse — but soft this time, no mark. A kiss in the shape of a bite. He moves down. The kiss goes to her new collarbone bite mark. He licks it once. Audience-only: the flame-curve there pulses — bright — then settles.

He raises his head. He looks at her.

VAEL: Not tonight.
ISOLDE: Why.
VAEL: Because the pack arrived two hours ago. They must witness.
ISOLDE: Witness what.
VAEL: The first night.
