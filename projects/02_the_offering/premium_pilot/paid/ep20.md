# THE OFFERING — EP20: Two Crowns (HEAVY GATE)

S#1 — VAEL'S KEEP / VAEL'S WING / EARLY MORNING

[Visual]
ISOLDE wakes. She is on the wide bed. The under-gown is fully off — beside the bed. She lies bare under the dark coverlet. VAEL beside her — same. His scaled hand is at her left wrist. The three crescents + flame-curve glow steady. Last night they went further than EP15 — but he held the last threshold. First night still ahead.

She turns to him. He is awake — watching her again.

ISOLDE: How long this time.
VAEL: Two hours since you slept.
ISOLDE: You watched me all night.
VAEL: I slept three hours.

She rolls toward him. Her bare chest meets his. His scaled arm closes around her back.

ISOLDE: Why did you stop.
VAEL: Because the first night should not be the night before the queen takes Velden.
ISOLDE: You are formal.
VAEL: I am traditional.
ISOLDE: Two hundred years of tradition.
VAEL: Yes.

She presses her forehead to his.

ISOLDE: Today I take Velden.
VAEL: Through the line.
ISOLDE: Through the line.
VAEL: Then through the line tonight.

S#2 — VAEL'S KEEP / TOWER ROOM / MORNING

[Visual]
ISOLDE in the tower. Pendant + twin pendant + stone — all three on the stone floor before her. ELARA at the door. KIRAN at the rampart below. HALDREN in the hall.

ISOLDE places her hand over all three items. She hums the three notes. The items pulse together.

Audience-only: a gold thread runs from her hand, out the window, across the valley, past the brother's army (still at the river, holding), and reaches Velden — the city's old palace. The thread enters through a window.

ISOLDE: Mother.

A pause.

Then ISOLDE's eyes open wide.

ISOLDE: She answers.
ELARA: Your mother.
ISOLDE: Yes. Not the dead one. The line. The line answers in her voice.

ISOLDE listens. ELARA cannot hear. ISOLDE's expression shifts — she is hearing many voices, the whole line going back.

Then she speaks into the stone.

ISOLDE: I am here. I take the line. I am queen by blood. The mate is here. Witness.

The three items pulse, all together. Then settle.

ISOLDE: The line has heard.

S#3 — VELDEN CITY / OLD PALACE / SIMULTANEOUS

[Visual]
The kingdom king — ALDRIC — finally seen on-screen, directly. Older than VAEL would look. In his throne room. He is alone — court not yet assembled. On the wall above his throne: the kingdom crown, mounted on a small dark cushion. The crown itself is plain dark metal, two-bar simple.

The crown lifts off its cushion. Slowly. It floats.

ALDRIC sees it. He rises from the throne — slow.

The crown floats toward an empty space three paces from the throne — and rests on the empty air at the height of a head that is not there. Hovering.

ALDRIC: Show yourself.

A figure begins to appear under the floating crown — not solid. A projection. ISOLDE. She is wearing the gown she was wearing in the tower. The pendant is at her throat. She is faintly translucent — but recognizable.

ISOLDE (projection): I take the line.
ALDRIC: You die in my brother's keep.
ISOLDE: I live in my mate's keep. I rule Velden through the line.
ALDRIC: You cannot.
ISOLDE: I am.

The crown above her projected head lowers — and sits on her head. The crown is not translucent. It is solid. It has crossed over.

ALDRIC: Bring it back. (to no one — there is no one in the throne room)
ISOLDE: It will not come back.

The projection fades. The crown sits where her head was — hovering. Then it lowers — settles on the empty floor three paces from the throne.

The kingdom crown is no longer on its cushion. The kingdom crown is on the floor.

ISOLDE removes the crown. She holds it in both hands. She looks at it.

ISOLDE: I want it. But not on my head until he is gone.
ELARA: Aldric.
ISOLDE: My uncle.

She places the crown in the carved box — alongside the two pendants and the stone.

ISOLDE: When he is gone, I will wear it.
ELARA: Yes, your majesty.

S#5 — VAEL'S KEEP / VAEL'S WING / NIGHT

[Visual]
The bedchamber. VAEL knows what has happened. He has seen the carved box. He looks at ISOLDE — fully naked again, on the bed. He is on the bed beside her, also fully naked. The five marks all blazing now — paired with three new gold threads at his scaled knuckles + a new one beginning at his wrist.

VAEL: Queen of Velden.
ISOLDE: Mate.
VAEL: Tonight.
ISOLDE: Tonight.

He bends — kisses her with full mouth, full tongue, no holding back. His scaled hand slides down her body — past her sternum, across her stomach, to the inside of her thigh. This time it does not settle. It slides up. The gauntleted hand at her breast.

She arches into him. Her hands fist in his hair. The kiss does not break.

He breaks at last — to look at her.

VAEL: Now.
ISOLDE: Now.

He moves over her. The five marks blaze blinding. The room fills with gold light. Audience-only: outside the window, all twelve drakes on the ramparts close their eyes at once. The night holds.
