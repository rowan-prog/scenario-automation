# THE OFFERING — EP47: The Day Before (HEAVY GATE)

S#1 — VELDEN CITY / OLD PALACE / DAWN

[Visual]
ISOLDE alone in the queen's chamber. The Velden crown is on the bedside table. Tomorrow she is crowned. Today is preparation.

She wakes. VAEL is not in the chamber. She rises. She walks to the window. The palace courtyard below is being prepared — banners, garlands, witnessing stones for the carriers. The line tree at the palace stands taller than the palace itself now.

VAEL enters.

VAEL: You are awake.
ISOLDE: I have been for a while.
VAEL: You should sleep more.
ISOLDE: I cannot. Tomorrow.
VAEL: Tomorrow.
ISOLDE: You.
VAEL: I have been on the rampart. Looking south.
ISOLDE: Why south.
VAEL: A feeling.

S#2 — VELDEN CITY / OLD PALACE / RAMPART / MIDDAY

[Visual]
ISOLDE on the rampart with VAEL. They look south. Far in the distance — a thin column of smoke. Another beyond it. Then a third. Three fires.

ISOLDE: Three.
VAEL: Three.
ISOLDE: What.
VAEL: I do not know. But south is where the brother's loyal southern lords held. After he came over to you — some of his lords did not.
ISOLDE: They were not at the river.
VAEL: They were not. They held the southern lands.
ISOLDE: And now.
VAEL: I think they have made fires.
ISOLDE: For what.
VAEL: Signal.
ISOLDE: To whom.

KIRAN climbs up.

KIRAN: My queen. A southern messenger has arrived. He requests an audience.
ISOLDE: Bring him.

S#3 — VELDEN CITY / OLD PALACE / THRONE ROOM / AFTERNOON

[Visual]
The southern messenger — older, weather-beaten. He kneels — but only one knee — at the foot of the throne.

SOUTHERN MESSENGER: My queen. The southern lords ask permission to attend your coronation. They were not present when you took the line.
ISOLDE: They held the south for my uncle.
SOUTHERN MESSENGER: They held the south for themselves. They did not love my king.
ISOLDE: But they did not come to me.
SOUTHERN MESSENGER: They were waiting.
ISOLDE: For.
SOUTHERN MESSENGER: For your coronation. They will not bend until they see the line tree crown you. Then they bend.
ISOLDE: They want to see.
SOUTHERN MESSENGER: They want to see.
ISOLDE: Then they come.

VAEL: How many.
SOUTHERN MESSENGER: Twelve houses. Each will send three. Thirty-six lords.
ISOLDE: All armed.
SOUTHERN MESSENGER: As honor guard.
ISOLDE: Or as backup if the line does not crown me.
SOUTHERN MESSENGER: My queen — that is my truth. Yes.

A pause. ISOLDE looks at VAEL.

ISOLDE: They come unarmed past the city gates. Their armor and weapons remain at the gates. They enter as witnesses.
SOUTHERN MESSENGER: I will deliver that.
ISOLDE: Tomorrow at dawn they may enter.

S#4 — VELDEN CITY / OLD PALACE / QUEEN'S CHAMBER / NIGHT

[Visual]
ISOLDE and VAEL alone. The night before coronation.

ISOLDE: They will see.
VAEL: They will.
ISOLDE: And if the line does not crown me.
VAEL: It will.
ISOLDE: But if.
VAEL: It will, Isolde.
ISOLDE: How do you know.
VAEL: Because the line is here. The tree is full. The carriers are here. The mate is here. The drakes are here.
ISOLDE: All right.

A pause.

ISOLDE: Vael.
VAEL: Yes.
ISOLDE: Tomorrow when I am crowned — whatever the price has not yet taken — I want to acknowledge it.
VAEL: How.
ISOLDE: At the coronation, I name the price. So all see what we pay.
VAEL: Yes.
ISOLDE: Velden knows the queen is paying.
VAEL: Velden knows.

He kisses her. Long. Then he holds her.
