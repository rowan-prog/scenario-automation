# THE OFFERING — EP24: Carry Them Home

S#1 — KINGDOM COUNTRYSIDE / NEAR DAWN

[Visual]
Carrow flies low and fast. ISOLDE on her back — cloak billowing. Behind them, the fifth drake — VAEL on its back. Below: a kingdom village, just waking. Smoke rising — six kingdom soldiers approaching a small house at the edge.

ISOLDE leans forward. She points. Carrow dives.

S#2 — KINGDOM VILLAGE / EDGE / DAWN

[Visual]
Carrow lands between the soldiers and the small house. Wings spread wide. The soldiers stop — drawn weapons, but they have not yet seen the rider. ISOLDE swings down. She walks forward. The bracelet on her left wrist visible. The pendant at her neck. She has no weapon.

LEAD SOLDIER: Who.
ISOLDE: The queen.
LEAD SOLDIER: There is no —
ISOLDE: Look at the dragon. Look at this.

She raises her left wrist. The bracelet blazes. The soldiers freeze. They have heard the rumors but not seen.

LEAD SOLDIER: The offering.
ISOLDE: The queen.

A second drake lands behind Carrow — VAEL. He does not dismount.

LEAD SOLDIER: Lord of the keep.
VAEL: Yes.
LEAD SOLDIER: You will not have her. (gestures to the small house)
ISOLDE: She is already mine. She is line.

The lead soldier looks back at his five. He looks at the drakes. He drops his sword.

LEAD SOLDIER: We yield her.

The other five drop their swords also.

S#3 — KINGDOM VILLAGE / INSIDE SMALL HOUSE / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
ISOLDE enters. The house is small, lit by a single candle. A young woman — perhaps thirty — clutches a small box. She is shaking.

YOUNG CARRIER: They were going to —
ISOLDE: I know.
YOUNG CARRIER: Who are you.
ISOLDE: The line called you.
YOUNG CARRIER: It called me last week. I did not understand.
ISOLDE: Come with us.
YOUNG CARRIER: How.
ISOLDE: On the dragon.

The young carrier looks past her — sees Carrow through the open door. She makes a small sound.

ISOLDE: She will not hurt you.
YOUNG CARRIER: I know.

She rises. She clutches her box. ISOLDE takes her by the elbow. They walk out together.

S#4 — VAEL'S KEEP / OUTER GATE / DAY

[Visual]
Carrow and the fifth drake return. The young carrier dismounts with ISOLDE — visibly steadier. KIRAN at the gate. He sees the new arrival. He bows half-shallow.

KIRAN: Queen.
ISOLDE: Bring her in.

KIRAN takes the young carrier in.

ISOLDE turns to VAEL.

ISOLDE: There are eleven more I know of. I need to reach them tonight.
VAEL: Some are with him.
ISOLDE: He cannot kill them. They are his blood. He will use them.
VAEL: He may kill those who refuse to join him.
ISOLDE: Then I reach them first.
VAEL: Twelve hours of riding for each.
ISOLDE: Twelve hours of flying.
VAEL: With ten more to go.
ISOLDE: I sleep tomorrow.

He looks at her. He takes her marked wrist.

VAEL: The price.
ISOLDE: I know.
VAEL: It is starting.
ISOLDE: Yes.
