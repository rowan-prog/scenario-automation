# THE OFFERING — EP14: Second Instrument

S#1 — KINGDOM ROAD / BROTHER'S CAMP / MORNING

[Visual]
The brother — older, unlined — walks among the wounded. Three hundred minus sixteen down at the river. He stops at one wounded man — burns on shoulder, alive. He kneels. He places a hand on the wound. The man tenses — but the burns close. New skin. The man rises.

The brother does not speak. The man bows.

The lead scout watches. The lead scout's face does not change.

LEAD SCOUT: Three more wounded need it.
BROTHER: Bring them.

S#2 — VAEL'S KEEP / WAR ROOM / MIDDAY

[Visual]
HALDREN at the table. KIRAN. VAEL — his cut from yesterday already healed (faster than human). The map shows the brother's camp position — they have not moved. Two days since the river battle.

HALDREN: He healed them.
KIRAN: All sixteen.
HALDREN: Twelve in fighting form again. Four still down.
VAEL: How.
HALDREN: My king's brother holds it. The old line.
VAEL: The old line.
HALDREN: The line my king disowned to take the crown.

ISOLDE enters. She has been listening from the corridor — she does not pretend she has not.

ISOLDE: Disowned.
HALDREN: Yes.
ISOLDE: Why.
HALDREN: The old line carries the gift. Healing. Sometimes other things.
ISOLDE: Then my king has not the gift.
HALDREN: No.
ISOLDE: But the brother does.
HALDREN: Yes.
ISOLDE: And I.

The table is silent. HALDREN does not answer.

VAEL looks at ISOLDE.

VAEL: That is what the pendant is.
ISOLDE: My mother's.
VAEL: Your mother held the line.

S#3 — VAEL'S KEEP / ISOLDE'S OLD CHAMBER (NOT WING) / EVENING

[Visual]
The smaller chamber from EP3·EP5. ISOLDE has come here alone. She kneels at the foot of the bed. From under the bed she draws a small carved box — the only thing she brought from Velden besides the pendant. She opens it.

Inside: three things. The pendant. A folded piece of parchment, very old. A small dark stone — the same kind of stone as the brother's amulet from EP12, but smaller.

She unfolds the parchment. It is a family genealogy — written in old hand. Names. Generations. The last name on it: her mother's. Then a blank.

She traces her finger up the names. Three generations up — there is a name that has been struck through. Two generations above that — another name struck through.

She closes the parchment. She holds the small dark stone in her palm. It is warm.

ISOLDE (quietly, alone): They sent me to die because I am next.

S#4 — VAEL'S KEEP / VAEL'S WING / NIGHT

[Visual]
ISOLDE enters. She holds the carved box. VAEL is at the corner table again. She sets the box on the table. She opens it.

ISOLDE: My mother.
VAEL: I know.
ISOLDE: She was the heir.
VAEL: Yes.
ISOLDE: My king is not.
VAEL: Your uncle is not.
ISOLDE: My uncle's brother — the one healing his soldiers — he is closest.
VAEL: He is closest by gift. You are closest by blood.
ISOLDE: I cannot be sent back.
VAEL: No.
ISOLDE: I never could.

She puts a hand on the stone in the box. It pulses once.

ISOLDE: They sent me here to die because if I lived they would have had to acknowledge me.
VAEL: Yes.
ISOLDE: And now.
VAEL: And now you live.

He stands. He crosses to her. He takes her wrist — the marked one — and holds it. His scaled palm against her three marks.

VAEL: And now you are mine.
ISOLDE: I am.
VAEL: And tomorrow night.
ISOLDE: Tomorrow night.
