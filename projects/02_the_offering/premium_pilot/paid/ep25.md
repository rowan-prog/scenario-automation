# THE OFFERING — EP25: Seven Saved Two Lost

S#1 — VAEL'S KEEP / VAEL'S WING / EVENING

[Visual]
ISOLDE has not slept. Her face is pale. Carrow is in the courtyard with the other drakes — exhausted too. ISOLDE in the bedchamber. VAEL waiting. The book is open on the corner table.

ISOLDE: Seven came back with me.
VAEL: Two.
ISOLDE: Two more reached too late.
VAEL: You did all you could.
ISOLDE: It was not enough.

He crosses. He pulls her against his chest. She rests there.

VAEL: Sleep.
ISOLDE: Three more remain alive but with him.
VAEL: He will keep them alive. They are his blood. Tomorrow you reach for the next.

She nods against his chest. Her bracelet glows faintly — exhausted but holding.

S#3 — KINGDOM COUNTRYSIDE / DAWN

[Visual]
Carrow lifts off the keep ridge — alone, no rider. She crosses to a small town across the river. She lands at the edge. A girl — eight years old — at a well draws water. She looks up.

Carrow lowers her head. The girl reaches for Carrow's snout. The drake closes her eyes once.

The girl looks at her own wrist. A small faint gold mark — about the size of a coin — appears under her skin. New. Just born.

The girl smiles.

GIRL: I know you.
CARROW (head-press — recognition)
GIRL: Take me to her.

The girl climbs onto Carrow's back. Carrow lifts off.

S#4 — VAEL'S KEEP / OUTER GATE / MORNING

[Visual]
Carrow lands. The girl dismounts. KIRAN sees. ISOLDE arrives at the gate — woken by KIRAN.

ISOLDE: Carrow.
KIRAN: She brought one.
ISOLDE: Without us.
KIRAN: Without us.

ISOLDE kneels. Eye level with the girl.

ISOLDE: What is your name.
GIRL: Mira.
ISOLDE: How old.
MIRA: Eight.
ISOLDE: How did you know.
MIRA: I dreamed of the dragon. She came for me.

ISOLDE looks at the girl's wrist — at the faint new mark.

ISOLDE: You are line.
MIRA: She told me.
ISOLDE: Who.
MIRA: The dragon.

ISOLDE looks at Carrow. Carrow lowers her head. ISOLDE places her hand on Carrow's jaw.

ISOLDE: The line passes through the drakes too.
KIRAN: Yes.
ISOLDE: Then we have more than we counted.
