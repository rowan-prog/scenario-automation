# THE OFFERING — EP26: The Drakes Carry

S#1 — VAEL'S KEEP / INNER COURTYARD / MORNING

[Visual]
Three more children — two girls, one boy — brought by three different drakes. Each child has a small new wrist mark. None had a carved box at home; the line is recognizing them directly through the drakes now.

KIRAN counts. ELARA brings them inside. ISOLDE watches.

KIRAN: Twenty-two now.
ISOLDE: Twenty-two carriers in the keep.
KIRAN: Yes.
HALDREN (arriving): The kingdom will not stop.
ISOLDE: He cannot. The drakes find faster than his soldiers.

S#2 — KINGDOM ROAD / BROTHER'S CAMP / AFTERNOON

[Visual]
The brother sits at his table. The amulet is on the velvet. The kingdom messenger has just arrived. Bone-clasp lieutenants stand around.

MESSENGER: My king commands the army recalled.
BROTHER: My king commands.
MESSENGER: He will send another to take charge if you do not yield.
BROTHER: He has burned five of the line so far. He should not be issuing further commands.
MESSENGER: My lord —
BROTHER: Tell my brother I have decided.
MESSENGER: What.
BROTHER: I do not return to Velden. I go to the keep.

The messenger blinks.

MESSENGER: To attack.
BROTHER: To kneel.

S#3 — VAEL'S KEEP / TOWER ROOM / NIGHT

[Visual]
ISOLDE alone with the stone. She hums. She reaches.

Audience-only: the gold thread reaches her uncle's camp. The brother sits at his table — he places hand on amulet.

ISOLDE: You decided.
BROTHER: I did.
ISOLDE: Why.
BROTHER: Because my brother has begun killing the line. I gave him my honor when I held the army at the river. He has spent that honor. I will not stand with him.
ISOLDE: He will call you traitor.
BROTHER: He will.
ISOLDE: When do you come.
BROTHER: Five days. The army will follow me.
ISOLDE: All of them.
BROTHER: Most. Some will return to my brother. Most are mine.

She pauses.

ISOLDE: I do not yet trust you.
BROTHER: You do not need to. You will see.
ISOLDE: When you arrive — at the gate — bring the amulet. Place it in front of the gate. Then enter unarmed.
BROTHER: As you wish.

She releases the stone.
