[Visual]
ISOLDE awake — early light. She is in the wide bed alone. VAEL is gone — she can hear him in the next chamber, moving. She places her hand on her stomach. VAEL enters with a small bowl of warm food.

VAEL: You are awake.
ISOLDE: I am.
VAEL: How.
ISOLDE: How am I awake.
VAEL: How is — anything.
ISOLDE: I am pregnant.
VAEL: Yes.
ISOLDE: You knew.
VAEL: I felt the third pulse last night.
ISOLDE: Will it survive the price.
VAEL: The line carries through. The mate-bond shares the carrying. Our child will live.
ISOLDE: Then there is a successor.
VAEL: There is.

S#2 — VELDEN CITY / GREAT SQUARE / LINE TREE / MIDDAY

[Visual]
The final coronation ceremony. The full court of Velden + Vael's keep + southern lords + twenty-five carriers + sixty drakes + thousands of citizens. The line tree-crown — gold-leafed — on ISOLDE's head. The Velden crown — the original kingdom crown — held on a cushion at the foot of the line tree.

ISOLDE picks it up. She holds both crowns — the new gold living one and the old metal one.

ISOLDE: The kingdom's old crown was my uncle's. My grandfather's. His grandfather's. They held it without the line. I do not need it.

She places the old crown at the base of the line tree. The tree absorbs it — the metal dissolves into the gold-blazing earth at the tree's foot. Gone.

ISOLDE: From now — only the line tree crown. Only what the line accepts.

She turns to the crowd. She lays her hand on her stomach.

ISOLDE: And there is a successor.

The crowd murmurs — soft, joyful.

ISOLDE: My child will be queen or king after me — and Vael's child after him.

VAEL: Our child.
ISOLDE: Our child.

She turns to VAEL.

ISOLDE: Mate-King. Vael of the keep. King of Velden. Father of the future line. Will you bind to me — as I bind to you — for as long as we live.
VAEL: I will.
ISOLDE: And after.
VAEL: And after.

She places her hand on his chest — over his heart. He places his scaled hand on hers — over her stomach.

The line tree glows — bright, sustained. The crown on her head glows. The bracelets on her wrist glow. The paired threads under his scales glow. All in time.

S#3 — VELDEN CITY / GREAT SQUARE / LINE TREE / AFTERNOON

[Visual]
The crowd has begun the final witnessing — each citizen, each lord, each carrier in turn comes to the tree and places their hand on the bark. The tree pulses with each touch. Each person leaves a small gold thread of their own — a personal contribution to the line.

The line tree grows visibly larger as the day proceeds. By the end of the witnessing — by evening — the tree is significantly taller than it was at dawn. Its branches arch over the entire square. Gold leaves shimmer in the late sun.

ISOLDE and VAEL remain at the base — accepting each witness. The carriers — twenty-five plus the granddaughter — remain in their circle. The drakes — sixty — remain on the ramparts and rooftops.

KIRAN (at sunset, after the last witness): It is done.
ISOLDE: It is done.
VAEL: It is.
KIRAN: My queen. My king.
ISOLDE: My friend.

She turns to him. She removes the gold-leaf circlet briefly. She touches one of the smallest gold leaves to KIRAN's forehead — leaving a faint gold mark there.

ISOLDE: You are line now too.
KIRAN: My queen — I am not blood.
ISOLDE: The line is not blood. The line is who chooses it.

KIRAN places one hand on his forehead — feels the mark. He looks at it.

KIRAN: Yes, my queen.

S#4 — VELDEN CITY / OLD PALACE / GARDEN / NIGHT

[Visual]
ISOLDE and VAEL in the small garden behind the palace — where ALDRIC is buried. They have left the public witnessing. The carriers, drakes, crowd are at the square — the witnessing continues into the night.

ISOLDE sits on a stone bench. VAEL beside her. The line tree visible from here — its glow lights the garden.

ISOLDE: Tomorrow we return to the keep.
VAEL: For a few days.
ISOLDE: Then back to Velden. Or — the keep. Wherever the line takes us.
VAEL: Both.
ISOLDE: Both.

A long pause.

ISOLDE: My uncle.
VAEL: He is part of the line now.
ISOLDE: Forgiven.
VAEL: Returned.
ISOLDE: There is a difference.
VAEL: There is.

She places her hand on the small gold pulse on her stomach.

ISOLDE: When the child comes.
VAEL: When.
ISOLDE: We tell her about him.
VAEL: Or him.
ISOLDE: Or him. Either way.
VAEL: We tell.

A small smile.

ISOLDE: This is our beginning.
VAEL: This is our beginning.

He pulls her against him on the bench. She leans into his chest. The line tree's gold light over the garden. The drakes on the rooftops behind them. The carriers in the square continuing the witnessing.

She closes her eyes.

He looks up at the line tree — at the stars beyond it.

VAEL (softly, alone — almost to himself): The bond holds. The line holds. The queen holds.

He looks down at her — sleeping against his chest.

VAEL: All of it holds.

The line tree pulses one last time — long and slow and sustained. The drakes around the city pulse their gold traces in answer. The carriers in the square pulse their wrist marks. ISOLDE's bracelets glow steady against VAEL's chest. The third pulse — the child — glows under her hand on her stomach.

VAEL closes his eyes too. Just for a moment. Then he opens them. He keeps watching her. He keeps watching the line tree. He keeps watching the future.

The camera draws back. The garden becomes small. The line tree becomes tall against the night sky. The witnessing of Velden continues in the gold light beyond the garden wall — gold threads rising from each carrier's hand on the bark, each small contribution joining the tree.

A wind moves through the garden — soft, the same low wind that crossed the gate at the beginning. It carries the sigil hum that audience first heard at Vael's Keep — but quieter now, settled. The hum that was the line calling itself home.

The wind passes. The garden holds. The line tree glows. ISOLDE sleeps against VAEL. VAEL watches. The witnessing continues.

The image holds.

The image holds.

The image holds.

Fade Out.
