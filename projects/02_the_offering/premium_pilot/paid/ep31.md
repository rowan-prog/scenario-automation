# THE OFFERING — EP31: Inside Velden

S#1 — VELDEN CITY / MAIN STREET / AFTERNOON

[Visual]
ISOLDE rides Carrow at walking pace through the city. The people line the streets. They do not cheer. They watch. Some have heard ALDRIC's confession at the gate — the message has spread. Others have not. They all see the crown, the bracelets, the dragon.

A small child runs from the crowd — eight or nine, dirty, no parent visible. She holds a small wilted flower. She offers it.

ISOLDE dismounts. She kneels. She takes the flower. The flower revives in her hand — gold-edged.

CHILD: My grandmother said you would come back.
ISOLDE: When did she say.
CHILD: When the dragons came.
ISOLDE: Where is she.
CHILD: She died last year.
ISOLDE: What did she look like.
CHILD: She had a mark like yours.

ISOLDE looks at the child's wrist. A small faint gold mark — the line. ISOLDE places her hand over it. The mark warms.

ISOLDE: What is your name.
CHILD: Wren.
ISOLDE: Walk with me.

S#2 — VELDEN CITY / OLD PALACE / OUTER GATES / EVENING

[Visual]
ISOLDE arrives at the palace gates. The gates are closed. ALDRIC has retreated inside. The dark robe's presence is unknown. BROTHER's army holds the south gate; LEAD SCOUT holds the north; KIRAN has positioned twelve drakes on the city walls — visible.

ISOLDE stops at the palace gate. VAEL beside her.

ISOLDE: He hears us.
VAEL: He hears.
ISOLDE: Aldric. Walk out. Now.
ALDRIC (from inside, hidden): I will not.
ISOLDE: Then I come in.
ALDRIC: I will not surrender.
ISOLDE: You stopped having a choice.

She turns to LEAD SCOUT.

ISOLDE: Open the gate. No fire — no killing if it can be avoided.
LEAD SCOUT: Yes, my queen.

S#3 — VELDEN CITY / OLD PALACE / OUTER GATES / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
LEAD SCOUT moves to the gate. He places his hands on the central seam. He calls to his men — six riders bring iron pikes. They wedge the pikes between the gate doors. They push. The gates resist — kingdom craftsmanship — but begin to give.

Carrow walks forward. The drake's massive body adds weight. She pushes her shoulder against the gate. The hinges groan. The gate gives.

The gate is open.

Inside: an empty courtyard. Kingdom guards in formation. They have not fled. They wait. Their captain steps forward.

PALACE CAPTAIN: My queen.

He kneels. The kingdom guards behind him kneel.

PALACE CAPTAIN: I have served Velden longer than I served the king. I serve the line.

ISOLDE: Where is he.
PALACE CAPTAIN: In the throne room. The dark one is with him.

S#4 — VELDEN CITY / OLD PALACE / THRONE ROOM CORRIDOR / DUSK

[Visual]
ISOLDE walks the corridor. VAEL beside her. PALACE CAPTAIN leading. BROTHER and KIRAN behind. They reach the throne room door. The PALACE CAPTAIN steps aside.

PALACE CAPTAIN: I cannot enter. The dark one's wards prevent.
ISOLDE: I will.

She raises the line tree branch staff. The staff glows. She touches the door with it. The wards — visible dark lines on the wood — fade. The door opens.

Inside: ALDRIC on the throne. The dark robe figure standing behind. The kingdom crown still gone.

ALDRIC: Niece.
ISOLDE: Aldric. Get up.

ALDRIC does not move.

DARK ROBE: She is weaker than yesterday. The stain grows.
ISOLDE: It does. But not enough.

She steps forward. VAEL with her.
