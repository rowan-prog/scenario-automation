# THE OFFERING — EP29: Aldric Moves

S#1 — VELDEN CITY / OLD PALACE / DAWN

[Visual]
ALDRIC alone. The court-magus is gone. His advisors are reduced — two remain. The kingdom crown is still not on his head. He sits on the throne. Across the room, a tall figure enters — a man in dark robes, older even than ALDRIC. His face cannot be seen — hooded.

DARK ROBE: My king. The line has chosen.
ALDRIC: It has not chosen.
DARK ROBE: It has chosen. The tree has been replanted.
ALDRIC: Then we burn it.
DARK ROBE: The tree was planted by a queen. The tree cannot be burned by other than her hand.
ALDRIC: Then her hand burns it.
DARK ROBE: How.
ALDRIC: She has a price. She has felt it.
DARK ROBE: You knew.
ALDRIC: I knew when her mother held the line. Her mother carried the price too. Her mother is dead.
DARK ROBE: My king. How did you know.
ALDRIC: Because I killed her mother. With my own hand. When her mother declared.
DARK ROBE: I see.

A long pause. The dark robe steps closer.

DARK ROBE: Then we use the price.

S#2 — VAEL'S KEEP / TOWER ROOM / SIMULTANEOUS

[Visual]
ISOLDE wakes — a vision. She is in the tower. The stone in her hand. Her bracelets are flaring — not steady. The new mark above the first bracelet pulses irregular.

She sees — through the line — a flash of her mother. Her mother is speaking. The flash ends.

ISOLDE: Mother.
ISOLDE: Why did you come now.

A second flash. The line carriers' voices — many. They are saying one thing.

ISOLDE: He killed her.

She sits on the floor.

ISOLDE: He killed her himself.

ELARA enters.

ELARA: My queen.
ISOLDE: My uncle. Aldric. He killed my mother. With his own hand.
ELARA: ... You did not know.
ISOLDE: No. I assumed she died of fever. I was eight.
ELARA: My queen — I —
ISOLDE: Wake VAEL. War council in one hour.

S#3 — VAEL'S KEEP / WAR ROOM / MORNING

[Visual]
Full council. VAEL, KIRAN, HALDREN, BROTHER (the uncle), LEAD SCOUT, COURT-MAGUS, ELARA, three lieutenants, three line carriers (eldest, middle, youngest). ISOLDE stands at the head — does not sit.

ISOLDE: My mother declared the line twenty years ago. My uncle Aldric killed her himself.
COURT-MAGUS: I knew of the rumor.
ISOLDE: It is no longer rumor.
HALDREN: Then he murdered my queen's mother and took the crown from his own brother. This is — there are no words.
ISOLDE: He must answer.
BROTHER: He will not surrender it. He never has.
ISOLDE: Then I take him.
VAEL: When.
ISOLDE: Now. I move on Velden tomorrow.
HALDREN: My queen — the army — the readiness —
ISOLDE: The army is here. The brother's army is here. The drakes are here. The line is here. I move tomorrow.

A silence.

LEAD SCOUT: Order us.
ISOLDE: At dawn. We ride at dawn.

S#4 — VAEL'S KEEP / VAEL'S WING / NIGHT

[Visual]
ISOLDE in bed. VAEL beside her. Both quiet. The bracelets glow steady but the new mark above continues to pulse irregular.

ISOLDE: Will I survive it.
VAEL: I do not know.
ISOLDE: The price.
VAEL: We do not know.
ISOLDE: If I do not —
VAEL: You will.
ISOLDE: But if I do not —
VAEL: I follow you.
ISOLDE: Vael. No.
VAEL: I follow you.
ISOLDE: The pack.
VAEL: The pack follows the bond. The pack follows me.

A long silence. She turns toward him. He pulls her against his chest.

ISOLDE: One night.
VAEL: One night.

He kisses her — long, fierce. Different from the gentle kisses of recent days. This one knows tomorrow.
