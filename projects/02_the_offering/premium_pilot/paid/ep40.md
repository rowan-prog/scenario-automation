S#1 — VELDEN CITY / OLD PALACE / TOWER / MIDNIGHT

[Visual]
ALDRIC asleep in his witness chamber. A faint dark light enters through the window — uninvited. It coalesces into a thin shape — the dark robe figure. He has come back.

DARK ROBE: Aldric.
ALDRIC (waking): You returned.
DARK ROBE: I needed your blood. The line knows yours.
ALDRIC: What for.
DARK ROBE: To break one of them.
ALDRIC: One.
DARK ROBE: She will not survive losing one of the line. The shared price comes from each carrier. Lose one — the others bear it. Lose two — they break. The line breaks at three.
ALDRIC: You take my blood. You break one. I take the line back.
DARK ROBE: Yes.
ALDRIC: With what.
DARK ROBE: With a curse on my blood. A counter-line curse. When the line tries to take more from me — it cannot. The curse rebounds. The line dies in its source. The queen dies.

ALDRIC sits up. He looks at the door — locked from outside, but he is unguarded inside.

ALDRIC: Do it.

S#4 — VELDEN CITY / PALACE TOWER / SIMULTANEOUS

[Visual]
The dark robe at ALDRIC's chamber. A bowl of blood between them. The dark robe is chanting. The pull on Mira is failing — he is being pushed back through the line.

DARK ROBE: She has too many holding her.
ALDRIC: Then choose another.
DARK ROBE: It is too late. The line locked.
ALDRIC: My blood was for nothing.
DARK ROBE: Your blood — it is still locked into the connection. The line knows you now.

He looks at ALDRIC. ALDRIC is paling. The line is pushing back through the connection — coming for ALDRIC's blood.

DARK ROBE: The counter-line curse. I did not finish it.

He raises his hands — to finish the curse. To make ALDRIC's blood immune.

But the line strikes first. Twenty-five carriers pushing the curse back through Aldric's blood, through the bowl, into Aldric himself.

ALDRIC convulses. The blood in the bowl boils. The dark robe pulls his hands back — too late.

ALDRIC: I — no —

A flash of gold from the bowl. ALDRIC's body — he convulses again — then is still. The line has taken what he gave.

The dark robe turns toward the window. He flees.
