# THE OFFERING — EP38: Inside the Wards

S#1 — HILL FORT / INNER COURTYARD / AFTERNOON

[Visual]
ISOLDE walks toward the counter-pole. VAEL behind her. The acolytes form a circle. The dark robe stands at the pole.

DARK ROBE: You came alone through the wards.
ISOLDE: I came with my mate.
DARK ROBE: One drake-mate against four mages and me.
ISOLDE: And the staff.
DARK ROBE: And the staff.

He raises his hands. The dark light pulses inward from the wards — now turned toward her. ISOLDE plants the staff. Gold blazes from the staff base. Gold against dark. The four acolytes chant. The pull is hard.

ISOLDE: Vael.
VAEL: Beside you.

He places his scaled hand on the staff alongside hers. His scales burn. He adds his strength. The gold pushes harder. The dark light pushes back.

The dark robe steps forward.

DARK ROBE: Together you are stronger. Apart you are bound.

He raises a hand toward VAEL. Dark light shoots at VAEL specifically. ISOLDE pivots — places her bracelets between VAEL and the dark light. The dark light hits her bracelets. ISOLDE staggers.

But the dark light has not reached VAEL. The stain on his scaled hand actually shrinks a fraction — pushed back by her bracelets' impact.

S#2 — HILL FORT / INNER COURTYARD / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
ISOLDE steadies herself. The bracelets are darker now — more stain. But VAEL is safer. The dark robe is enraged.

DARK ROBE: You traded your life for his moment of safety.
ISOLDE: I will trade more.
DARK ROBE: How much.
ISOLDE: Until you are dead.

She raises the staff. She points it at the acolytes — not at the dark robe.

The staff fires gold light at the four acolytes. The four chant harder — they resist. But their resistance breaks one by one. Each falls in turn — not dead, just unconscious, their dark connection severed.

Four down. Only the dark robe remains.

The dark robe pulls another flame from his sleeve. He marks the floor — portal again.

DARK ROBE: I will be back.
VAEL: How many times must we have this conversation.
DARK ROBE: One more.

He steps through. ISOLDE attempts to follow — Vael catches her arm — she is too weak now.

S#3 — HILL FORT / INNER COURTYARD / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
ISOLDE collapses. VAEL catches her. He kneels with her on the courtyard floor. KIRAN runs in — he sees.

VAEL: She used too much.
KIRAN: Carrow.

Carrow flies in low — landing in the courtyard. VAEL lifts ISOLDE. He carries her to Carrow's back. Carrow's gold trace at jaw is pulsing weakly — sympathetic.

KIRAN: We get her home.
VAEL: Yes.

Carrow lifts off with ISOLDE on her back, VAEL behind her holding her. The other drakes follow. The hill fort is left to KIRAN and the keep's lieutenants to dismantle.

S#4 — VAEL'S KEEP / VAEL'S WING / NIGHT (HOURS LATER)

[Visual]
ISOLDE in bed. Unconscious. VAEL beside her — not asleep. COURT-MAGUS examining the bracelets. ELARA at the bed foot.

COURT-MAGUS: She will wake. But she has burned much.
VAEL: How much.
COURT-MAGUS: Days. Perhaps weeks. From the price.
VAEL: She is paying.
COURT-MAGUS: She is paying.

ISOLDE stirs. Her eyes open — slow.

ISOLDE: Vael.
VAEL: Yes.
ISOLDE: I am still here.
VAEL: You are.
ISOLDE: How long.
COURT-MAGUS: Six hours.
ISOLDE: I have not died.
VAEL: No.

She lifts her marked wrist. The stain on the bracelets is large but not crushing. The bracelets still glow.

ISOLDE: I will live.
VAEL: For now.
ISOLDE: For long.
VAEL: Long enough.

He leans down. He kisses her forehead.

ISOLDE: Sleep.
VAEL: I will not sleep tonight.
ISOLDE: I know.

She closes her eyes.
