# THE OFFERING — EP39: The Way Through

S#1 — VAEL'S KEEP / VAEL'S WING / DAWN (TWO DAYS LATER)

[Visual]
ISOLDE awake — pale but lucid. VAEL beside her on the bed. She has not left the bed in two days. ELARA brings water. The GROVE KEEPER (from EP36) has been brought in to advise.

ISOLDE: I cannot keep doing what I did at the fort.
GROVE KEEPER: No. That uses up your years.
ISOLDE: Then how do I face him next time.
GROVE KEEPER: You let the line answer instead of yourself.
ISOLDE: I have been doing that.
GROVE KEEPER: You have been doing it through you. You can let it do it without you.
ISOLDE: Explain.
GROVE KEEPER: The line is not just a queen. It is all carriers. When you fight, you fight as queen. When the line fights, all twenty-five carriers fight together.
ISOLDE: I have not asked them.
GROVE KEEPER: You did at the temple — but only briefly. The longer you let the line act through you — the less you carry alone.
ISOLDE: It is the price they shared.
GROVE KEEPER: Sharing reduces the cost. But it must be willing on their side. And on yours.

S#2 — VAEL'S KEEP / LINE TREE COURTYARD / MIDDAY

[Visual]
All twenty-five line carriers gathered around the line tree. The drakes — twelve from the keep plus the wider valley forty-eight now visible at the keep ramparts. KIRAN, HALDREN, BROTHER, ELARA, COURT-MAGUS, GROVE KEEPER all present.

ISOLDE — walking with effort, supported by VAEL — to the foot of the tree.

ISOLDE: I have asked you to come here. Not to live. To fight beside me.

A silence.

ISOLDE: Not with your bodies. With your line. When the dark one fights me again — I will not face him alone. I will face him with all of you. The cost will be ours together.

The eldest carrier steps forward.

ELDEST: We have lost one already. We choose.

The other carriers — all twenty-five — step forward in unison. The GROVE KEEPER's granddaughter steps forward.

GRANDDAUGHTER: Me too.
ISOLDE: You are too young.
GROVE KEEPER: She is line. She decides.
GRANDDAUGHTER: I decide.

A long silence. ISOLDE looks at the granddaughter. Maybe three years old.

ISOLDE: Then together.

S#3 — VELDEN CITY / PALACE TOWER / EVENING

[Visual]
ALDRIC at the window of his tower chamber. He has watched the line tree at the keep through the window every day. The tree is now visible from Velden city — it has grown that tall.

He sees the gathering at the courtyard. He sees ISOLDE — pale, supported. He sees the carriers.

A small smile. Not warm — calculating.

ALDRIC (to himself): She has stopped using herself. She has the line working through her now.

He turns from the window. He sits at the table. He picks up a small object he has carved from a piece of wood while in this chamber — a tiny crown.

ALDRIC: When the dark one strikes, the line itself will be at risk. All of them.

He sets the wooden crown down. He smiles wider.

ALDRIC: And then he will need only break one of them to take the line itself.

S#4 — VAEL'S KEEP / VAEL'S WING / NIGHT

[Visual]
ISOLDE in bed. VAEL beside her. She is regaining color slowly. The stain on her bracelets has shrunk further — the carrier link is helping. VAEL's dark patch has shrunk too — the load distributed across all twenty-five.

ISOLDE: I feel them.
VAEL: All twenty-five.
ISOLDE: Yes.
VAEL: How is it.
ISOLDE: Quieter than I expected. They are not voices in my head. They are presences.
VAEL: Good.
ISOLDE: When he comes again — I am not alone.
VAEL: You are not.
ISOLDE: And we end him.
VAEL: We end him.

She turns toward him. They lie face to face.

ISOLDE: After he is ended.
VAEL: Yes.
ISOLDE: Quiet years.
VAEL: Quiet years.

She kisses him. Slow. Tired but real. She falls asleep against his chest. He stays awake — but lays his head close to hers.
