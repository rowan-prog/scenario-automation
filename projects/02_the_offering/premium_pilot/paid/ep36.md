S#1 — VAEL'S KEEP / WAR ROOM / DAY

[Visual]
Map of the kingdom. Pins mark the locations of old line carriers (now mostly safe at the keep) and the locations of known old growths of the original line tree (twelve sites — one of which they destroyed at the northern temple).

COURT-MAGUS: Eleven sites remain. He needs only one to begin again.
ISOLDE: Then we secure them all.
HALDREN: Some are abandoned. Some are guarded by — survivors.
ISOLDE: Survivors of what.
HALDREN: When my king's grandfather took the crown — there were carriers who refused to flee. They held their groves.
COURT-MAGUS: They are still there.
ISOLDE: Three generations later.
COURT-MAGUS: The line keeps them.
ISOLDE: Then they come to me, or I go to them.
BROTHER: I can ride to four of them. I know which.
ISOLDE: Take a drake escort. Bring them back if they wish — protect the groves either way.
BROTHER: Yes.

S#2 — DISTANT EASTERN GROVE / TWO DAYS LATER

[Visual]
BROTHER and three drakes (and three drake riders he has selected from VAEL's pack) arrive at a small forest grove. Inside the grove: a small dwelling — old, well-kept. A woman emerges. She is perhaps fifty. She wears an old gown with the sigil — embroidered, faded.

GROVE KEEPER: I felt you coming.
BROTHER: The line called.
GROVE KEEPER: I have been here forty years. My mother sixty before me. We have never left this grove.
BROTHER: My queen invites you.
GROVE KEEPER: Your queen.
BROTHER: My niece.
GROVE KEEPER: Old line.
BROTHER: Direct.
GROVE KEEPER: Then I come.

She turns. She enters her dwelling. She emerges with a small carved box and a young child — perhaps three — at her hip.

GROVE KEEPER: My granddaughter. I have been raising her.
BROTHER: She comes too.

The woman mounts behind a drake rider. The child she carries.

S#3 — VAEL'S KEEP / LINE TREE COURTYARD / DAY

[Visual]
GROVE KEEPER and her granddaughter arrive at the keep. ISOLDE meets them at the gate. The GROVE KEEPER walks straight past ISOLDE to the line tree — she places her hand on the bark. The tree pulses warmer for her than for ISOLDE.

GROVE KEEPER: Younger than mine was.
ISOLDE: Mine.
GROVE KEEPER: Yours. But not yours alone. The line knows itself.

She turns to ISOLDE. She lowers herself — slow. A full bow, not court-formal, more — earthly. Knee, hand, forehead.

GROVE KEEPER: My queen.
ISOLDE: Rise.

The keeper rises. The granddaughter watches everything.

S#4 — VAEL'S KEEP / VAEL'S WING / NIGHT

[Visual]
ISOLDE in bed. VAEL beside her. The dark patch on his scaled hand has grown larger now. ISOLDE's stain has shrunk further. The mate bond is slowly transferring.

VAEL: We received twelve more carriers across the eleven groves.
ISOLDE: Twelve.
VAEL: BROTHER and the others should return in three days.
ISOLDE: Then all are gathered.
VAEL: All known.

She looks at his hand. Then her own bracelet.

ISOLDE: You are taking more.
VAEL: I can.
ISOLDE: It will become a problem when it spreads to your throat.
VAEL: It will not.
ISOLDE: How do you know.
VAEL: Because I am older than you. I will hold it longer.
ISOLDE: Until.
VAEL: Until we find a way to dissolve it.
ISOLDE: How.
VAEL: I do not yet know.

She rolls toward him. She kisses him — slow.

ISOLDE: Vael.
VAEL: Yes.
ISOLDE: We end this together.
VAEL: Together.
