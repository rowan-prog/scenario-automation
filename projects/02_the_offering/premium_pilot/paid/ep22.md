# THE OFFERING — EP22: They Come

S#1 — VAEL'S KEEP / OUTER GATE / MORNING

[Visual]
Three riders at the gate. All women — different ages. The eldest grey-haired, the middle in dark traveling cloak, the youngest perhaps ELARA's age. They wear no kingdom colors. Each carries a small carved box.

KIRAN at the gate.

ELDEST: I was called by the line. I bring my piece.
MIDDLE: I was called. I bring mine.
YOUNGEST: I was called. I bring mine.

KIRAN opens the gate.

S#2 — VAEL'S KEEP / ONYX HALL / DAIS / MIDDAY

[Visual]
The three women at the foot of the dais. Each has opened her box. Each contains: a pendant, a stone, sometimes other items. The pendants are not all identical — some are fragments of the same larger sigil, some are slightly different patterns. The line is fractured into pieces, kept by different lines.

ISOLDE sits on the high seat. Velden crown still in the carved box at her side.

ELDEST: When my grandmother died, this passed to me. I am not strong enough to use it. But it answered when the line called.
MIDDLE: I held mine through three sons. None could carry. It answered yesterday.
YOUNGEST: I have had mine three months. I am twenty-two. I knew nothing of the line until the call came.

ISOLDE rises. She walks down. She takes each piece — gently. She holds them. Each piece warms in her hand.

ISOLDE: They are the same line.
ELDEST: They are.
ISOLDE: Will you stand with me.
ELDEST: That is why we came.

The three women kneel — not full subservience, half-knee acknowledgment. ISOLDE places each piece on the dais step.

ISOLDE: Rise.

They rise.

S#3 — VAEL'S KEEP / WAR ROOM / AFTERNOON

[Visual]
HALDREN at the map. New marks on the map — small flags representing each old line carrier known. Twelve in addition to the three present. Some are with ALDRIC. Some are independent. Some may yet come.

HALDREN: He has six committed to him. He has called the rest.
KIRAN: Six against fifteen.
HALDREN: With us — including her — fifteen against six. But.
VAEL: But what.
HALDREN: He has the kingdom army. We do not.
ISOLDE: We have the dragon pack.
HALDREN: Twelve dragons.
ISOLDE: Twelve dragons in this keep. There are more.
KIRAN: There are.
HALDREN: How many.
KIRAN: We do not announce them.
HALDREN: My lord.
VAEL: Tell him.
KIRAN: Forty-eight in the wider valleys. Mostly young.
HALDREN: Forty-eight more.
VAEL: They do not come to fight. They come to witness.
ISOLDE: Some will fight if asked.
VAEL: Yes.

HALDREN looks at the map. He recalculates.

HALDREN: Then we have an army.
ISOLDE: We do.

S#4 — VAEL'S KEEP / VAEL'S WING / NIGHT

[Visual]
ISOLDE in the bed reading from a thick old book — the line's records, brought by the eldest carrier. VAEL beside her, reading over her shoulder. Both naked under the coverlet. Bracelet glowing soft.

ISOLDE (reading): "And the first queen was carried by the line into the dragon-bond, and her bond bore the seal of both. The seal is a bracelet around the wrist of the queen, and a bracelet under the scales of the dragon-mate."
VAEL: True so far.
ISOLDE: "When the seal is whole, the queen rules both. The line answers. The dragon pack obeys. The kingdom yields."
VAEL: We are at "kingdom yields."
ISOLDE: Soon.

She turns the page.

ISOLDE: "But when the seal is whole, the queen carries the price."
VAEL: Price.

She looks up. Their eyes meet.

VAEL: What price.

She reads the next line silently. Her face changes. She closes the book.

ISOLDE: It does not say. Only that there is one.

A pause. He takes her hand. The bracelets touch.

VAEL: Whatever it is.
ISOLDE: Whatever it is.
