# THE OFFERING — EP07: THE RIDGE

S#1 — DRAGONKEEP / COUNCIL ROOM / DAY

[Visual]
The same long black stone table from the burned-banner scene. The ash has been wiped. The second emissary delegation sits along one side — six men in formal kingdom dress, twelve armed escorts standing along the walls. HALDREN at one end. KIRAN at VAEL's right. ISOLDE at VAEL's left. A large hourglass sits at the centre of the table — black sand, half-full, falling. The delegation leader, AMBASSADOR VEREN, lays a heavy parchment scroll across the table.

[DIALOGUE]
VEREN: One hour to comply.
KIRAN: Or.

VAEL: Which is.
VEREN: An army at the southern pass within three days.

S#2 — DRAGONKEEP / COUNCIL ROOM / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
VAEL turns to ISOLDE in the chair beside him. His scaled hand closes around her wrist, then around her waist. In a single motion he lifts her and pulls her sideways onto his lap — across his thigh, facing the delegation. His scaled hand at her waist holds her steady. His other scaled hand closes in her hair at the nape — a firm fist, not gentle. His scaled fingers turn her head slightly to the side so the second bite mark on the side of her throat is visible to the entire delegation. The delegation freezes — VEREN's jaw locks. The twelve escorts shift along the walls. VAEL extends his free scaled hand over the table; a faint dark-red flame curls along the back of his knuckles. ISOLDE's wrist scale-light pulses in the same rhythm as the flame on his hand — once, twice, three times — visible to all.

[DIALOGUE]
VEREN: My lord —
VAEL: She will hear the terms with me.
VEREN: That is not —
VAEL: It is.
ISOLDE (to VEREN): Read them.

[FX]
Chair scraping back as he lifts her. Silk sliding on leather. Her single sharp inhale. Twelve sets of armour scraping at the walls. The hourglass sand still falling. Low growl outside — closer now.

S#3 — DRAGONKEEP / NORTH WINDOW / EVENING

[Visual]
A high narrow window looking out at the black ridge above the keep. ISOLDE stands at the window. The wasteland is dimming. Along the spine of the ridge, two enormous shadows shift — not visible as forms, only as movement, the outline of folded shoulders and dragging tails along the rock. KIRAN steps in behind her at the window.

[Camera]
WIDE of the ridge from the window, dim → TRACKING ISOLDE at the window → CLOSE on her hand on the stone sill, wrist scale-light still pulsing faint → PUSH IN to the ridge outside → INSERT on the two shadows shifting along the spine of the ridge → CLOSE on KIRAN stepping in behind her.

[DIALOGUE]
KIRAN: They've come down a level.
ISOLDE: From where.
KIRAN: The high ledges.
ISOLDE: Why now.
KIRAN: They've heard him.
ISOLDE: Heard him what.
KIRAN: Decide.

[FX]

S#4 — DRAGONKEEP / THRONE / NIGHT

[Visual]
The throne room empty. VAEL alone, seated. The hourglass from the council has been carried in and placed on a small black table beside his chair. The sand is more than half down. VAEL extends his scaled hand and lays it flat against the curve of the glass. Dark-red flame ripples along the back of his hand and presses faint heat-marks against the glass surface. He turns his head as a side door opens — ISOLDE steps in. She walks to the throne. She stops at the arm of his chair. She does not sit. She places her own hand over his scaled hand on the hourglass. The wrist scale-light brightens between her fingers. The dark-red flame on his hand crawls up under her palm. Neither of them moves it away. The sand keeps falling.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: How long.

ISOLDE: And then.
VAEL: Then I decide it for them.

Hard Cut.
