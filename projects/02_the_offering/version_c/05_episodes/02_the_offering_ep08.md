S#3 — DRAGONKEEP / GREAT HALL / EMISSARY ENTRY — BANNER BURN

[Visual]
The twelve armed escorts march in two columns up the centre aisle. AMBASSADOR VEREN at the head, banner-bearer behind him with the new banner unfurled. They stop at the foot of the dais. VEREN unrolls the second scroll and begins to read. VAEL stands. He descends the dais steps. He walks to the banner-bearer and his scaled hand closes around the banner pole itself. He pulls the banner free from the bearer's grip. He drops the banner on the floor at the feet of the twelve escorts. Dark-red flame rolls out of his scaled palm. The banner ignites against the polished stone of the centre aisle and burns to ash in seconds — at the feet of the delegation, in front of the entire court.

S#4 — DRAGONKEEP / GREAT HALL / DAIS — PUBLIC CLAIM

[Visual]
VAEL turns from the burned banner and walks back up the dais steps. He extends his scaled hand to ISOLDE in the right chair. She places her hand in his. He does not seat her again — he draws her up out of the chair and pulls her by the waist up one step higher than his own chair, onto the small upper dais reserved for the lord's standard. He sets her there in front of the entire court — above his own chair, above the candidate row, above the delegation. His scaled hand closes in her hair at the nape — a firm fist — and tilts her head sideways and slightly down toward him. The full length of her throat is exposed to the hall — both bite marks visible. His other scaled hand opens flat against the side of her neck where the second bite-mark sits and presses there, pressing the mark against her own skin, in full view. The candidate row is silent. ELARA's hand on her chair has gone white. VAEL bends his face down toward hers, his mouth at the level of her ear. He begins:

S#5 — DRAGONKEEP / GREAT HALL / END — THE ROAR

[Visual]
The first word breaks against a roar — a roar that comes from outside the keep, above and around it at once. The roar is enormous — many throats together, not one. Dust falls from the high beams of the hall. The stained-glass behind the dais shakes in its lead. The twelve armed escorts at the foot of the dais drop to their knees in formation — every knee at once, every face down, weapons clattering to the stone. AMBASSADOR VEREN sinks to his knees last, slowest. VAEL's word is gone — the rest of the bride-declaration has been swallowed by the roar. ISOLDE's free hand — the one not held by his at her hair — closes around the scaled back of his hand at her neck. She presses her own hand over his. The dark-red wrist scale-light on her inner wrist burns full bright — permanent now, no longer flickering. His scaled hand under hers darkens through the plate. The court does not move. The roar continues outside. HALDREN, last of all, lowers his head all the way to the floor at the human-kingdom row, the first true bow.

(the roar swallows the rest)

VAEL (under the roar): I will.
