# THE OFFERING — EP04: THE BANNER

S#1 — HUMAN ROYAL HALL / MAP ROOM / DAWN

[Visual]
ALDRIC stands over a map of the northern provinces. A COURIER kneels at the table edge with a sealed report. ALDRIC breaks the seal, reads, sets it on the brazier. The paper curls and burns. He gestures to a tall standard at the wall — the human kingdom's banner — and signals two heralds to take it down. They wrap it for travel.

[DIALOGUE]
ALDRIC: He kept her.
COURIER: He seated her, Majesty.
ALDRIC: Send the banner. Send Haldren the new instructions.
COURIER: And if he burns it?
ALDRIC: Then I will know what to do next.

S#2 — DRAGONKEEP / COURTYARD / DAY

[Visual]
The first emissary procession arrives at the keep gates: six riders, one banner pole, the human kingdom's standard unfurled at the front. HALDREN waits at the gate. The procession leader unrolls a list of terms on horseback. KIRAN watches from the wall. ISOLDE stands at a high arrow-slit window in her **black silk and pearl** gown, watching down. The bite mark at her throat is half-hidden by the high collar; her inner wrist rests on the stone sill, and the faint scale-light is visible.

[DIALOGUE]

S#3 — DRAGONKEEP / COUNCIL ROOM / LATE DAY

[Visual]
A long black stone table. VAEL at the head — the torchlight catching his face fully now: hard jaw, grey eyes that have darkened toward the centre, the scaled plating climbing the back of his right hand. The emissaries on one side. HALDREN at one end. KIRAN at VAEL's right. ISOLDE stands behind VAEL's left shoulder — not seated. The banner is laid out across the table. The EMISSARY reads three terms: return the offering / accept new candidate / pay tribute. VAEL listens. He extends his scaled hand over the banner. His palm opens. A pulse of dark-red flame rolls along the centre of his palm. The banner ignites at the cloth. The emissaries jerk back from the table. The banner burns to ash between them in seconds. The room holds.

[Camera]
WIDE of the council room, banner on the table → CLOSE on VAEL's face fully lit by torch → PUSH IN on the EMISSARY reading → INSERT on the three terms on the parchment → CLOSE on ISOLDE standing behind VAEL's shoulder → TRACKING VAEL's scaled hand extending over the banner → ECU on the dark-red flame rolling on his palm → INSERT on the banner igniting at centre → WIDE of the table, the emissaries pulling back → CLOSE on VAEL's face through the smoke.

[DIALOGUE]
EMISSARY LEADER: Return the offering. Accept the new candidate. Or pay —
VAEL: She is not a candidate.
EMISSARY LEADER: That is not your decision to make alone.
VAEL: It is.
HALDREN: My lord — the Crown will hear this as a declaration of —
VAEL: Let it.

[FX]
Parchment crackling. Banner igniting — a low whoosh. Cloth burning to ash, fast. A chair scraping back. Emissary's sharp inhale.

S#4 — DRAGONKEEP / COUNCIL ROOM / EMPTY / EVENING

[Visual]
The room is cleared. Emissaries gone. KIRAN gone. HALDREN at the doorway pretending to read a scroll. VAEL stands at the table where the banner burned. The ash is still there. ISOLDE walks the length of the table and stops at his side. VAEL turns and his **scaled hand, still warm with the dark-red flame faintly glowing along the back of the knuckles**, closes around her inner wrist. With one slow finger he traces a thin curve of dark-red flame along the line of her wrist, following the scale-light mark, completing a second curve over the first. The scale-light brightens beneath the flame. When his finger lifts, the flame is gone — but a faint dark-red shadow remains layered over the original scale-light.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: They will not call you that again.
ISOLDE: Call me what.
VAEL: The offering.
ISOLDE: Then what will they call me.
VAEL: They will be told in time.

[FX]
Footsteps fading down the outer hall. Ash flaking on the table. The low hiss of the dark-red flame along his hand. Her one held breath.

Hard Cut.
