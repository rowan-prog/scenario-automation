# THE OFFERING — EP01: THE GATE OPENS

S#1 — HUMAN ROYAL HALL / DECREE CHAMBER / DAWN

[Visual]
KING ALDRIC's hand presses the wax seal onto a folded decree. The wax bears the human kingdom's sigil. ISOLDE kneels on the stone floor in her **arrival look — ivory silk gown with silver-thread embroidery, a white fur cloak fastened at the shoulder, pearl drops at her ears, hair brushed loose**. A clerk steps forward and unfastens the small silver pendant at her collarbone — a worn signet shaped in an old rune. The clerk sets it on the edge of the table beside an open velvet pouch and turns away to fetch a second list. ALDRIC does not look at her. ISOLDE rises slowly from the kneel; as her hand passes over the table's edge she palms the pendant off the table and into the inner lining of her fur cloak. She is upright before the clerk returns. ALDRIC does not see it. The clerk lifts the empty pouch as if the pendant is still inside and ties it shut.

[Camera]

[DIALOGUE]
ALDRIC: The seal is set.
CLERK: And the pendant, Your Majesty?
ALDRIC: Buried with the rest.
ISOLDE: It was my mother's.
ALDRIC: She was a mistake too.

[FX]
Wax hisses. The clerk's footsteps on stone. Heavy door closing in the distance. A single low bell.

S#2 — WASTELAND ROAD / CARRIAGE INTERIOR / DUSK

[Visual]
ISOLDE sits alone in a small carriage. The fur cloak is wrapped around her against the cold. Her hand slips inside the cloak's inner lining and closes around the signet pendant — she stole it back. Outside the window, ash-grey plains stretch to a black ridge. HALDREN rides alongside on horseback, watching her through the glass.

[Camera]

[DIALOGUE]
HALDREN: Don't look back, Princess. The road only goes one way.
ISOLDE: I wasn't looking back.

[FX]

S#3 — DRAGONKEEP GATES / TWILIGHT

[Visual]
The carriage stops at the foot of a black stone fortress carved into the cliff. The gates loom — an old archway worked with weathered runes. ISOLDE steps down alone. Her white cloak is the only colour in the grey landscape. HALDREN does not dismount. The driver wheels the carriage around and rides back the way they came. ISOLDE is left standing at the gate with no escort, no luggage.

[Camera]
EXTREME WIDE of the keep above her, the gate a small black mouth at the cliff's base → TRACKING from behind ISOLDE walking the last steps to the gate → CLOSE on HALDREN reining his horse, turning away → WIDE of the carriage disappearing into the ash → CLOSE on ISOLDE's face, alone.

[DIALOGUE]
HALDREN: The keep will open for you. Or it won't.
ISOLDE: And if it doesn't?
HALDREN: Then nothing of you returns.

[FX]
Hooves retreating. Wind howling along the cliff. Stone shifting somewhere above. Distant deep rumble.

S#4 — DRAGONKEEP COURTYARD / NIGHT

[Visual]
The gate grinds open on its own. ISOLDE steps through into a courtyard of black stone. Old runes line the archway above her head — and as she passes beneath, one of them flickers faintly. Inside her cloak the signet pendant flickers in the same rhythm — audience sees both. A shadow moves on the wall above her. A scaled black hand — clawed, segmented dark plating along the back — descends from the dark and stops in the air just beside her shoulder. It does not touch. Then it closes around her inner wrist and lifts her hand into the light. Scaled fingers press her pulse point. A pale glow blooms on her inner wrist where his fingers rested.

[Camera]
TRACKING with ISOLDE under the archway → INSERT on the rune flickering above her → INSERT on the pendant flickering inside her cloak, same rhythm → WIDE of the empty courtyard, her small white figure → PUSH IN on the shadow descending the wall → ECU on the scaled hand stopping beside her shoulder → CLOSE on the scaled fingers closing on her inner wrist, lifting → ECU on her wrist, the scale-light blooming.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (O.S., low): Stand still.
ISOLDE: I am standing still.
VAEL (O.S.): No. You're shaking.
ISOLDE: That's the cold.
VAEL (O.S.): Inside. Now.

[FX]
Stone gate grinding shut. Low resonance under the runes. Heartbeat thud in the silence. Scale plate scraping faintly against her bracelet.

S#5 — DRAGONKEEP INNER STAIR / NIGHT

[Visual]
ISOLDE walks up the inner stair. VAEL walks ahead of her — only his back is visible: black leather, silver-thread, dark cloak, broad shoulders, the scaled black plating climbing the back of his hand and disappearing under the cuff. He stops on the landing. He begins to turn his head toward her.
