# Divorcing the Janitor, Begging the Billionaire

## EP14 - S#1  Day / Interior / Press Conference Hall
Characters: LAURA, REPORTERS

△ Laura sits at a long table crowded with microphones, camera flashes firing, one hand resting flat on her stomach.

REPORTER: Ms. Hayes — is it true you're pregnant?

LAURA: Yes. I'm carrying a child. And this city deserves to know what the Chairman of Veil really is, behind that curtain of his.

△ The reporters press closer, recorders thrust across the table toward her.

LAURA: For five years he controlled me. He cut me off from everyone, accused me of things I never did, and when I finally tried to leave — he forced himself on me. This baby is the proof. I stayed quiet to protect his precious name. Not anymore.

## EP14 - S#2  Day / Interior / Press Conference Hall · Continuous
Characters: KIERAN, JACE, LAURA

△ [chairman: charcoal suit] Kieran walks in through the doors at the back of the hall, and every camera swings around to him.

REPORTER (O.S.): That's him — that's the Chairman!

△ Jace steps into the aisle and plants himself square in Kieran's path.

JACE: Where do you think you're going? She's done with you. She's mine now. Must sting — a big Chairman who couldn't even hang on to his own wife.

KIERAN (past Jace, to Laura across the hall): Laura. This is your last chance. Admit what you did. Apologize. Right now, and I'll let it all go.

## EP14 - S#3  Day / Interior / Press Conference Hall · Continuous
Characters: LAURA, KIERAN, EMILY, REPORTERS

△ Laura stands up behind the table, chin lifting, playing straight to the cameras.

LAURA: Apologize? Everything I did, I did for you. For five years. You're the one who should be on his knees. I have nothing to apologize for.

△ [chairman: charcoal suit] Kieran turns his head, and Emily steps up beside him with a sealed folder in her hands.

[END HOOK]
LAURA (pointing at Kieran, to the room): You all saw it. You're all witnesses. He came here to threaten a pregnant woman on camera. That is who the Chairman of Veil really is.
