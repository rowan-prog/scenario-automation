# Divorcing the Janitor, Begging the Billionaire

## EP2 - S#1  Night / Interior / Veil Tower Lobby · Continuous
Characters: JACE, WALTER, EMPLOYEES

△ Jace shoves Walter backward by the collar. The old man's feet skid in the wet marble and he goes down hard; the wine bottle rolls loose as his head cracks against the desk's edge.

WALTER (dazed): ...you little animal—

△ A thin line of blood runs from Walter's temple across the black marble. He tries to push up and slips in it.

JACE (to the crowd, laughing): The janitor's got a daddy. Look at it — a walking retirement home, bleeding all over my nice clean floor.

△ Employees ring the scene, phones raised, filming. Nobody steps in.

BYSTANDER (filming): Ten bucks says the old drunk started it.

## EP2 - S#2  Night / Interior / Veil Tower Lobby · Continuous
Characters: KIERAN, WALTER, JACE

△ [janitor: gray coveralls] Kieran drops the mop and crosses the lobby fast, going down on one knee in the blood beside Walter.

KIERAN: Dad. Dad — look at me. Stay with me.

WALTER (gripping his sleeve): I'm fine. It's nothing. Don't— don't you dare blow it now. Not for me.

△ [janitor: gray coveralls] Kieran presses his sleeve to his father's temple; his jaw locks, but he stays down in the gray coveralls.

KIERAN (low, to Walter): Alright, Dad. Not here. Let me get you to a doctor — then no more hiding.

JACE: Aww, touching. Two broke nobodies bleeding on my floor. So this is what your Christmas cards look like.

## EP2 - S#3  Night / Interior / Veil Tower Lobby · Continuous
Characters: LAURA, KIERAN, WALTER, JACE

LAURA (O.S.): What is this circus in my lobby?

△ Laura strides back in through the glass doors, taking in the blood, the old man on the floor, the janitor kneeling in it.

△ Laura's gaze lands on Walter's bleeding face, then slides away to the wine stain on the marble.

LAURA: Kieran. Of course. Wherever there's a mess on my floor, there you are, kneeling in the middle of it.

KIERAN: Your boy just put my father's head through the marble. Look at him.

[END HOOK]
LAURA (stepping over the blood, right up to Kieran): You again. Always dragging your mess through MY lobby. Your father, your problems, your sad little life — get it out of my building. Now.

Hard Cut.
