# Divorcing the Janitor, Begging the Billionaire

## EP9 - S#1  Night / Interior / Grand Gala Ballroom · Continuous
Characters: LAURA, JACE, KIERAN, GUESTS

△ Laura's dropped champagne glass bursts on the marble at her feet; she doesn't look down, her eyes locked on the janitor at the foot of the staircase.

LAURA (a laugh cracking out of her): The Chairman. Him. If HE'S the Chairman, then I'm the Queen of England.

△ [janitor: gray coveralls] Kieran sets one boot on the bottom stair and starts up, unhurried, the mop cart abandoned behind him.

JACE (grabbing Laura's arm, voice thin): Babe, it's a trick. It's gotta be a trick. That's the janitor. I poured coffee on that guy's head.

△ The guests draw back from the staircase, phones lowering, a widening ring of silence around the two of them.

GUEST (low): That's the mop from the Veil lobby. I've walked past him a hundred times. That is the same man.

## EP9 - S#2  Night / Interior / Grand Gala Ballroom · Continuous
Characters: KIERAN, EMILY, GUESTS

△ [janitor: gray coveralls] Kieran climbs the staircase and grips the zipper at his chest, ripping the gray coveralls open to the waist.

EMILY (into the microphone, level and clear): Take a good look. The man you waited all night for has been mopping your floors for five years.

△ [chairman: charcoal suit] Kieran pulls the coveralls off and drops them over the railing; underneath, a charcoal suit, dark and cut to the bone.

GUEST (O.S., hushed): That's really him. That's the Chairman of Veil.

△ [chairman: charcoal suit] Kieran stops at the top of the staircase, facing the silent room.

EMILY: The tower you rent. The loan that keeps your lights on. The partnership you've chased across this city for a year. Every piece of it is his. It always has been.

## EP9 - S#3  Night / Interior / Grand Gala Ballroom · Continuous
Characters: LAURA, KIERAN

△ Laura stares up the staircase at the man in the charcoal suit, her broken champagne glass glittering at her heels.

LAURA (a cracked whisper): Last night. I called security on you. I threw you out of my own lobby last night.

△ [chairman: charcoal suit] Kieran looks down the length of the staircase at her, unhurried and still.

[END HOOK]
KIERAN: You wanted a meeting with the Chairman. Here I am. You said something about your knees.
