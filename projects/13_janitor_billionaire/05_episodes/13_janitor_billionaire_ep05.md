# Divorcing the Janitor, Begging the Billionaire

## EP5 - S#1  Night / Interior / Veil Tower Lobby · Continuous
Characters: KIERAN, LAURA, JACE

△ [janitor: gray coveralls] Kieran stands over the smashed bottle, facing Laura across the wine and glass.

KIERAN: The trainer. That's your story. You expect me to believe a random employee just happens to be wearing your matching Christmas necklace?

LAURA: It's — it's a common design. Anyone could have one.

JACE (cutting in, stung): Common? Babe, tell him about Paris. Tell him what you said in that suite — that he's a soft, broke nothing, and I'm the only real man who's ever put his hands on you.

△ [janitor: gray coveralls] Kieran looks at Laura, and something behind his eyes goes quiet and cold.

KIERAN: I was never an option to you. Not once in five years. Was I.

## EP5 - S#2  Night / Interior / Veil Tower Lobby · Continuous
Characters: LAURA, KIERAN, WALTER

△ Laura's face hardens. She turns her back on Jace and rounds on Kieran and Walter, stabbing a finger at the two of them.

LAURA: Look at the two of you. My husband in a mop-uniform. His father bleeding on my floor. A pair of beggars who wandered in off the street — no wonder Jace got the wrong idea. You humiliate me just by standing here. Get out. Get out of my company. Both of you.

WALTER (dragging himself upright, shaking with rage): Beggars? You spoiled, ungrateful girl — do you have any idea whose roof you are standing under right now?

LAURA: Your roof, old man? You can't even stand up straight.

## EP5 - S#3  Night / Interior / Veil Tower Lobby · Continuous
Characters: KIERAN, LAURA

△ [janitor: gray coveralls] Kieran's face closes, hard and final. He wipes the wine from his hands and looks at Laura like a stranger.

KIERAN: From the moment your boy toy cracked my father's head open, you stopped being my wife.

LAURA: Don't be dramatic. You don't get to end anything — we have a company, a name, a deal on the table. You need me.

[END HOOK]
KIERAN (quiet, final): I want a divorce.

Hard Cut.
