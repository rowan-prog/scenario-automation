# Divorcing the Janitor, Begging the Billionaire

## EP7 - S#1  Day / Interior / Laura's HQ Office
Characters: LAURA, ASSISTANT

△ Morning light floods the office. Laura stares at her phone, thumbing through screen after screen of red DECLINED banners.

LAURA: Declined. Declined — frozen? Every account is frozen? Call the bank. Call them right now.

ASSISTANT (shaking, phone in hand): Ms. Hayes, it isn't just the bank. The investors — they all pulled out overnight. Every single one. And there's a notice taped across the front doors.

## EP7 - S#2  Day / Interior / Veil Tower Lobby
Characters: LAURA, EMPLOYEES

△ Laura shoves through the glass doors. A printed notice is taped across them: PROPERTY OF VEIL HOLDINGS — VACATE BY 8:00 A.M.

△ Employees stream past Laura carrying boxes, none of them meeting her eyes.

LAURA (grabbing one by the arm): Where do you think you're going? Get back to your desks—

EMPLOYEE (pulling free): There are no desks by tonight, Laura. The whole floor's repossessed. By Veil.

## EP7 - S#3  Day / Interior / Stripped HQ Office
Characters: LAURA, ASSISTANT

△ Laura stands alone in her emptied office, movers hauling the last of the furniture out behind her.

ASSISTANT: The building. The lease. The loan that floated us through the last two rounds. It all traces back to one place — Veil Holdings. The same Veil Holdings that owns this tower and half the block.

LAURA: Then I go to Veil. I go straight to whoever runs it.

ASSISTANT: That's the problem. Nobody has ever seen the Chairman. No photo, no name. He runs this whole city from behind a curtain. If he wanted us dead — Ms. Hayes, we're already dead.

## EP7 - S#4  Day / Interior / Stripped HQ Office · Continuous
Characters: LAURA, JACE

△ Jace hovers in the doorway, suddenly small without his swagger.

JACE: Babe... if the money's really gone, then... what happens to us?

LAURA (rounding on him): Us? There is no "us" if I lose this company. Shut up and let me think.

△ Laura turns to the window, jaw tight, staring out at the VEIL TOWER letters blazing across the plaza.

[END HOOK]
LAURA: There's one man alive who can undo all of this. The Chairman of Veil. Nobody gets a meeting with him — so I'll make him come to me. A gala. Every investor in the city, one room. And when he finally shows his face, I'll get on my knees to that faceless bastard if that's what it takes.
