# Divorcing the Janitor, Begging the Billionaire

## EP10 - S#1  Night / Interior / Grand Gala Ballroom · Continuous
Characters: LAURA, KIERAN, EMILY

△ Laura drops to both knees on the marble at the foot of the staircase, both hands reaching up toward Kieran.

LAURA: Kieran — Chairman — please. Five years. Give me one more chance. I'll fix everything, tonight. Name it and it's yours.

△ [chairman: charcoal suit] Kieran comes down the stairs and stops three steps above her, looking down.

EMILY (stepping in at his side): A chance? You lost all your chances a long time ago.

## EP10 - S#2  Night / Interior / Grand Gala Ballroom · Continuous
Characters: ATTORNEY, LAURA, KIERAN

△ An attorney steps out of the crowd and holds a folder open in front of Laura's face — divorce papers, a pen clipped to the top.

ATTORNEY: The divorce papers are ready, Chairman.

△ Laura grabs the cuff of Kieran's trousers with both hands.

LAURA: We never fought. Five years, not once. Just tell me what to change and I'll change it—

KIERAN: If I weren't the Chairman, would you still be on your knees right now?

## EP10 - S#3  Night / Interior / Grand Gala Ballroom · Continuous
Characters: KIERAN, EMILY, LAURA

△ [chairman: charcoal suit] Kieran steps around Laura's reaching hands and walks past her without slowing.

KIERAN (to Emily): The deal she came here to beg for. Where does it stand.

△ Emily turns a slim tablet around so the frozen crowd can see the screen.

[END HOOK]
EMILY: That partnership you chased for a year? Terminated this morning — his signature. And that is the smallest thing you're about to lose.
