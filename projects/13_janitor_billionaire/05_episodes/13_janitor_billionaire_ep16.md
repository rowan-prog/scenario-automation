# Divorcing the Janitor, Begging the Billionaire

## EP16 - S#1  Day / Interior / Press Conference Hall · Continuous
Characters: LAURA, JACE, POLICE OFFICER

△ An officer takes Laura by the arm; she twists toward Jace and stabs a finger at him.

LAURA: The abuse story wasn't me — it was him! Jace made me say it. The projects, the money, all of it was his idea. Arrest HIM.

△ A second officer pulls Jace forward by the shoulder.

JACE: I gave you an idea. YOU built the fraud. You called the press. You moved the money — every dollar. Don't you dare pin this on me!

POLICE OFFICER: Save it for the unit. Both of you. Move.

## EP16 - S#2  Day / Interior / Veil Tower Lobby
Characters: KIERAN, WALTER

△ [chairman: charcoal suit] Kieran stands alone in the middle of the Veil Tower lobby, morning light stretched across the polished black marble.

WALTER (O.S.): Still checking if the floor shines?

△ Walter walks in through the glass doors, the bandage gone from his temple, steady on his feet, the old lunchbox in one hand.

KIERAN: No more night shifts, Dad. I'm done playing dead.

△ Walter crosses to him and pushes the lunchbox into his hands.

WALTER: Five years you mopped this floor to watch that woman show you who she was. Do your old man one favor and never do anything that stupid for a woman again.

## EP16 - S#3  Day / Interior / Veil Tower Lobby · Continuous
Characters: KIERAN, EMILY, WALTER

△ Emily crosses the lobby with a tablet and stops beside the two of them.

EMILY: The divorce is final. The fraud file is with the prosecutor. And the board is downstairs — they'd finally like to meet the man who's owned this city from behind a mop.

KIERAN: Let them wait a little longer. Five years I waited on her. They can give me one quiet morning.

△ [chairman: charcoal suit] Kieran walks to the mop cart still parked by the elevators and rests his hand on the handle, giving it one slow push.

SFX: SQUEAK.

KIERAN: I'm keeping the cart. This floor's mine — it always was.

△ Emily holds the elevator open, watching him a moment longer than she needs to; Walter huffs a quiet laugh and steps inside.

EMILY: Whenever you're ready, Chairman. Your new life's waiting upstairs.

△ [chairman: charcoal suit] Kieran leaves the cart where it stands and crosses to the open elevator, Walter and Emily at his shoulders, as the gold doors slide shut on the gleaming lobby.
