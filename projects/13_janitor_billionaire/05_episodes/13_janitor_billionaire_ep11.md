# Divorcing the Janitor, Begging the Billionaire

## EP11 - S#1  Night / Interior / Grand Gala Ballroom · Continuous
Characters: LAURA, EMILY, GUEST

△ Laura shoves herself up off the marble, swaying, jabbing a finger at Emily.

LAURA: You're lying. I built Hayes Beauty myself, from nothing. Every dollar in that company, I earned—

EMILY: You think your little company survived this long on its own? He cleaned up every mess you made. Every quarter you came up short, the money was already there before you found the hole. Without him, you'd have been on the street four years ago.

△ Emily turns the tablet toward Laura: five years of transfers down one column, all from a single account.

GUEST (an investor, backing away): We never funded her. We funded Veil's stake in her company. It was his name we were buying the whole time.

## EP11 - S#2  Night / Interior / Grand Gala Ballroom · Continuous
Characters: JACE, KIERAN, LAURA

△ Jace edges away from Laura, palms up, sliding toward Kieran with a stretched smile.

JACE: Mr. Chairman — sir — I never knew. She swore up and down you were nobody, just some janitor. This is all her. I've got nothing to do with any of it.

△ Laura wheels on Jace, her face twisting.

LAURA: You put your hands on his father. You started all of this. Don't you dare crawl to him now.

JACE: I did exactly what YOU told me to do. Every single word. You want to go down? You go down alone.

## EP11 - S#3  Night / Interior / Grand Gala Ballroom · Continuous
Characters: KIERAN, EMILY, LAURA

△ [chairman: charcoal suit] Kieran raises one hand, and the guests nearest him go still.

KIERAN (to Emily): Show them the rest.

△ Emily drops a thick bound ledger open on the display table, page after page red-stamped.

[END HOOK]
EMILY: Four projects. Two billion dollars, raised from the investors in this room. Not one of them was ever real. Every dollar routed straight into her personal accounts.

Hard Cut.
