# Divorcing the Janitor, Begging the Billionaire

## EP12 - S#1  Night / Interior / Grand Gala Ballroom · Continuous
Characters: LAURA, JACE, EMILY

△ Laura backs away from the display table, shaking her head at the red-stamped pages.
