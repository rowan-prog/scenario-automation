# Divorcing the Janitor, Begging the Billionaire

## EP13 - S#1  Night / Interior / Grand Gala Ballroom · Continuous
Characters: APPRAISER, JACE, ATTORNEY

△ An appraiser in white gloves holds an old cellar photograph up beside the frozen still on the screen, matching the label.

APPRAISER: I catalogued this bottle myself, for the Chairman's private cellar. A century-old vintage — one of nine left in the world. At auction, no less than fifteen million.

JACE (staggering back): Fifteen — no. It was fifteen grand. Fifteen thousand. That's what it said. Fifteen thousand—

△ The attorney steps forward and reads from a single page.

ATTORNEY: Destruction of high-value property. Under the law, three to five years — plus fines that'll wipe you out.

## EP13 - S#2  Night / Interior / Grand Gala Ballroom · Continuous
Characters: JACE, KIERAN

△ Jace drops to his knees on the marble, hands clasped in front of him.

JACE: Mr. Chairman, please — I swear I didn't know. It was a mistake. I'll do anything, anything you want.

△ [chairman: charcoal suit] Kieran looks down at him without moving.

JACE (voice hardening): ...And if you bury me? I've got videos. Her and me — the penthouse, the Hilton, the car, all of it. I go down, they all go public. Think about that precious name of yours.

KIERAN: Save the entire livestream. More than enough evidence. You broadcast the whole thing yourself.

## EP13 - S#3  Night / Interior / Grand Gala Ballroom · Continuous
Characters: JACE, LAURA

△ Jace scrambles across the floor and grabs the hem of Laura's gown.

JACE: Laura — baby — the fifteen million, you have to help me. You've always got money. Get me out of this.

LAURA (ripping the gown out of his hands): You cost me my career, destroyed my marriage, and now you want me to dig you out? Get the fuck away from me.

[END HOOK]
△ Laura turns from him, pulls out her phone, thumbs to a contact, and lifts it to her ear, her jaw setting hard.

LAURA (into the phone, low and fast): It's Laura Hayes. Bring every camera you have. I'm ready to tell the whole city what the Chairman of Veil did to me behind closed doors. You'll want this one.
