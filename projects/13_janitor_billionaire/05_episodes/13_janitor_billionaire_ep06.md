# Divorcing the Janitor, Begging the Billionaire

## EP6 - S#1  Night / Interior / Hospital Corridor
Characters: KIERAN, WALTER, EMILY

△ [janitor: gray coveralls] Kieran walks beside a gurney as nurses wheel Walter through swinging doors, a fresh bandage taped over his temple.

WALTER (gripping Kieran's hand): Five years you let that woman wipe her feet on you. For what? For a face on a floor?

KIERAN: For a clean divorce and a clear conscience. I have both now. Rest, Dad. I'll handle the rest.

△ [janitor: gray coveralls] Emily meets Kieran in the corridor, coat still on from the street, a tablet in her hand.

EMILY: The whole city watched that stream, Chairman. Every second of it. Say the word, and I bury her before sunrise.

## EP6 - S#2  Night / Interior / Hospital Corridor · Continuous
Characters: KIERAN, EMILY

△ [janitor: gray coveralls] Kieran stops walking. When he turns to Emily, the tiredness is gone; his voice drops low and even.

KIERAN: Cut it. All of it. The funding line, the lease on her floor, the bridge loan my shell company's been quietly carrying since the day she opened. She has twenty-four hours.

EMILY: Her whole company runs on that money. Pull it and she wakes up with nothing but a name.

KIERAN: Twenty-four hours, Emily. Not a minute more.

## EP6 - S#3  Night / Interior / Laura's HQ Office
Characters: LAURA, JACE

△ Laura paces her glass office, phone to her ear, Jace sprawled across her couch.

LAURA (into phone): I don't care what the janitor thinks. Let him cry about a divorce all he wants — he's got nothing. He IS nothing.

△ Laura tosses the phone aside and pours herself a drink, laughing.

LAURA: Five years I carried that man. I built this company myself, from the ground up. I don't need that old janitor's charity, and I don't need his permission to breathe.

JACE: That's my girl. So — the vice-CEO office. When do I move my stuff in?

## EP6 - S#4  Night / Interior / Veil Holdings Operations Floor
Characters: EMILY, ANALYST

△ Emily stands over a wall of screens, analysts moving fast around her; account balances flip from black to frozen red, one after another.

EMILY: Freeze the operating accounts. Recall the bridge loan in full. Eviction notice on Hayes Beauty, effective 8 a.m. And copy every investor on the funding withdrawal — all of them, tonight.

ANALYST: She'll wake up to nothing. You want me to hold something back, give her a cushion—

EMILY: No. The Chairman said twenty-four hours. It all goes at once.

△ Emily lifts her phone to her ear.

[END HOOK]
EMILY (into phone): It's done. Every line, cut. By morning, she has nothing.

Hard Cut.
