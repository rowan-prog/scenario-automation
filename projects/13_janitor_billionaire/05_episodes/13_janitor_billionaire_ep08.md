# Divorcing the Janitor, Begging the Billionaire

## EP8 - S#1  Night / Interior / Grand Gala Ballroom
Characters: LAURA, ASSISTANT, GUESTS

△ A vast ballroom blazing with chandeliers and champagne towers. Laura moves through it in a silver gown, greeting investors with a bright, brittle smile.

LAURA (under her breath, to her assistant): Is he confirmed? Tell me the Chairman confirmed he's coming.

ASSISTANT: His office said only that someone will come in his name. That's all they would give us.

△ Laura stops before a mirrored column and rehearses, quietly, to her own reflection.

LAURA (barely audible): Chairman. I'll do anything. Name it — anything at all — and my company is yours.

## EP8 - S#2  Night / Interior / Grand Gala Ballroom · Continuous
Characters: GUESTS, JACE, LAURA

△ Guests cluster near the great doors, whispering behind their glasses, phones ready.

GUEST: Thirty years, and not one person alive has seen his face. They say the last man who tried to photograph him never worked in this city again.

GUEST: Whoever the Chairman backs tonight owns the whole coast by Christmas.

△ Jace tugs at his rented collar beside Laura, out of place among the tuxedos.

JACE: What do I even say to a guy like that?

LAURA: You say nothing. You stand there and you look expensive. Let me do the begging.

## EP8 - S#3  Night / Interior / Grand Gala Ballroom · Continuous
Characters: LAURA, GUESTS, MC

△ The lights dim. A spotlight swings up to the great double doors at the head of the staircase. The room falls silent, every face turned up, waiting.

MC (at the podium): Ladies and gentlemen — please welcome the man who holds this city in the palm of his hand. The Chairman of Veil Holdings.

△ The double doors sweep open. No limousine silhouette. No entourage. No suit.

SFX: SQUEAK... SQUEAK... SQUEAK...

## EP8 - S#4  Night / Interior / Grand Gala Ballroom · Continuous
Characters: KIERAN, LAURA, EMILY, GUESTS

△ [janitor: gray coveralls] Kieran pushes a mop cart through the doors, its wheel squeaking, and rolls it slowly down the center of the parting crowd.

△ Laura's champagne glass freezes halfway to her lips; the blood drains from her face.

LAURA: Kieran—? What are you— you can't be in here. Security. SECURITY—!

△ [janitor: gray coveralls] Kieran stops the cart at the foot of the staircase and looks up at Laura, unhurried, while the entire ballroom stares.

△ Emily steps to the podium and lifts the microphone out of the frozen MC's hand.

[END HOOK]
EMILY (into the microphone, gesturing to the man in the gray coveralls): Ladies and gentlemen — the Chairman of Veil Holdings.

△ Every head in the ballroom turns from the podium to the janitor. Laura's glass slips from her fingers.

Hard Cut.
