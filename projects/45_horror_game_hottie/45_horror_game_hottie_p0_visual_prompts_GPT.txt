FORMAT FOR EVERY IMAGE
Horizontal landscape, 3:2 (1536x1024).

STYLE FOR EVERY IMAGE
Photorealistic cinematic still from a live-action horror drama. Setting: a lavish 1960s Las Vegas casino-hotel built like a Greek temple, frozen in time since the night it vanished in a desert sandstorm: white marble columns and statues, gold trim, red carpet, rows of vintage slot machines, glowing hot pink and turquoise neon, the sandstorm glowing outside the windows. Retro Vegas glamour turned eerie; not gothic, no castle, no candles, no fog. Shot on ARRI Alexa, 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain, soft flattering beauty lighting, flawless youthful skin. All characters are adults; the women are gorgeous young women around 19 to 21, each a natural, fresh-faced beauty with no cosmetic-surgery look and no lip filler. No text, no watermark.

SPLIT IMAGES (IMAGES 1 TO 8)
Each of these is a side-by-side diptych in one horizontal frame, split down the middle by a thin clean line. Both halves show the same moment from the same camera angle: the same woman, same face, same outfit, same pose, same background and the same horror around her. Only her body changes. LEFT HALF = what the terrified wedding guests see: her cursed monster form. RIGHT HALF = what one man sees: her true human form from before the curse.

THE FOUR WOMEN (keep the same faces in every image)

IVY, the bride, the queen of this deadly wedding
- True human form: a breathtakingly beautiful young woman of about 21 with Western features, fully human, a timeless ethereal beauty that looks lit from within. Long glossy jet-black hair, luminous fair skin with a warm rosy glow, rare amber-gold eyes, natural rose lips, slender and elegant.
- Cursed lich-queen form: a queen of the dead and the most powerful witch in the room, the same breathtaking face and figure, eerie and alluring at the same time, the kind of monster some men cannot stop staring at. Her skin is flawless porcelain white and corpse-cold with a faint pearly sheen. Thin black veins spread from the center of her chest across her collarbone, up the side of her neck and down her arms like delicate black lace tattoos. Set into her chest where her heart should be, a jewel glows icy blue. Black lips, fingertips that look dipped in ink, and the tip of a long, slender black tongue slowly touching her upper lip as she smiles. A crown of gold and pale bone grows out of her head, and her long black hair drifts upward as if underwater. Her eyes glow ice-blue with no visible pupils. Her shadow on the wall behind her is a skeleton queen in a crown. A beautiful corpse, not rotting and not skeletal. Palette: porcelain white, black, gold and icy blue; no green on her.

THE ZOMBIE BRIDESMAID, cool and deadpan
- True human form: a stunning young woman of about 20 with Western features and a fresh, youthful face, fully human. Tall and slim, sleek straight chestnut-brown hair pulled back tight, pale blue eyes, a calm, unreadable, deadpan face.
- Cursed zombie form: the same beautiful face and slim figure, a zombie girl stitched together from more than one body. Her skin is a clear, pale mint green, clean and smooth like porcelain with a faint glow, never muddy or grey. Her face is a little grotesque yet strangely pretty, like a stitched-together doll: a neat black seam runs from her hairline down across one eye to her jaw, joining two slightly different shades of green skin; the corner of her mouth is stitched into a faint permanent half-smile; two tiny silver staples sit at her temple. Neat black stitches also circle her throat and ring both wrists. One eye is clear icy blue, the other milky white with a faint glow. Blue-black lips. Not rotting: elegant and eerie, like a high-fashion zombie.

THE WEREWOLF BRIDESMAID, wild and teasing
- True human form: a stunning young woman of about 20 with Western features and a fresh, youthful face, fully human. Petite and athletic, long wild copper-red hair, green eyes, light freckles, a wild, teasing grin.
- Cursed werewolf form: the same pretty face, a werewolf girl with a beast's body. The animal ears and tail of her costume become real in exactly the same spot: tall furry wolf ears grow from her copper-red hair and a big bushy wolf tail sweeps out behind her. Thick tawny-red fur grows over her shoulders and down her arms to big clawed hands. Her legs are furred, backward-bending wolf legs that tear through her stockings and end in clawed paws instead of heels. Her eyes glow bright amber and her grin shows long fangs.

MADISON, the college queen bee
- True human form: a gorgeous 20-year-old American college girl, the sorority queen bee and the hottest girl on campus, fully human. A natural, fresh-faced all-American beauty with no cosmetic-surgery look: soft round youthful cheeks, natural pink lips with no filler, light natural makeup, big bright blue eyes, a bubbly, slightly ditzy smile, long silky, bright classic golden-blonde hair (unmistakably blonde, not platinum, not ash), and a naturally curvy, athletic cheerleader figure.
- Cursed succubus form: the same golden-blonde college girl, still stunning and youthful. Her whole body's skin is clearly, unmistakably pink, a rich rose-pink like flawless pink body paint on real skin in a big-budget fantasy film: obviously not a human skin tone, yet photoreal with natural skin texture, not cartoonish and not neon. Small curved glossy black horns rise from her golden-blonde hair, black bat wings spread behind her shoulders, and a slender black tail ending in a heart shape curls around her leg. Her pupils are glowing pink hearts. Playful, glamorous and a little dumb.
