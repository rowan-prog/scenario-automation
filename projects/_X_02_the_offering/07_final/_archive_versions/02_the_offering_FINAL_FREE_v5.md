# THE OFFERING — EP01: THE OFFERING ARRIVES

S#1 — BLACK GATE / WASTE ROAD / DUSK

[VISUAL / ACTION]

A bronze chain drags across burnt cobble. The chain is heavy, royal, stamped with the human kingdom's seal. At the end of it: ISOLDE — slender, porcelain pale, dark hair loose down her back, in a long ivory gown beneath a white fur cloak, silver thread catching the last grey light. A single bright shape walking through a grey waste.

Behind her, the human escort halts at a black stone line cut into the road. None of them step past it. The chief envoy, HALDREN — older, grey-haired, gaunt in slate diplomat's robes — opens a small leather pouch and tips the kingdom's signet ring onto the ground beside her feet. Behind him, the kingdom standard hangs limp on its pole, half lowered.

A guard cuts the chain at her wrists. The bronze coils strike the cobble flat. The sound is bigger than it should be in the open air. ISOLDE does not look down at it. Her dark hair lifts once with the wind off the basalt and falls back across her bare collarbone. Her mouth is closed. Her face is set the way a face is set when its owner has already finished crying.

Above them, set into the lintel of a great black gate, a row of ancient runes is carved into the basalt. As ISOLDE steps forward, one rune — only one — flickers, a slow heartbeat of red light. None of the humans see it.

Inside her white fur cloak, against the inner silk, an old silver pendant the size of a coin slips loose for a single beat. A tree-and-flame sigil. The same shape, smaller, as the rune above. She catches it without looking and tucks it back. None of the humans see this either.

S#2 — BLACK GATE / THRESHOLD / CONTINUOUS

[VISUAL / ACTION]

The gate is half open. Beyond it: black air, the smell of old fire, the deep low sound of something larger than a man breathing in the dark. ISOLDE stops on the threshold. Behind her, the human escort is already retreating up the waste road. They do not look back. Halfway up the slope, HALDREN does. Only HALDREN.

From inside the gate, a hand emerges into the cold grey light. Only a hand — large, the back of it patched in black, ridged scales running across the knuckles. The scales are not jewelry. They are part of him. Behind the hand, a tall shape held in the gate's darkness. The shadow of a black leather greatcoat shaped over wide shoulders. A throat. The face stays in shadow.

The scaled hand stops a finger's length from the side of ISOLDE's jaw. Not yet touching. Around them, the cobble seems quieter, as if the air itself has narrowed.

S#3 — INNER COURT / FIRST TOUCH / CONTINUOUS

[VISUAL / ACTION]

ISOLDE crosses the threshold. The gate shuts behind her without a hand on it. The court inside is black basalt, ringed with low torches, ribbed with the bones of a previous burning that no one bothered to scrape off. Smoke clings to the columns.

VAEL steps out of the shadow now.

Tall — well past her — broad-shouldered, the kind of body that takes a doorway sideways. Dark hair loose to the shoulder, damp at the ends from washing. Grey eyes set deep under a hard, straight brow. A hard jaw. A mouth that has not smiled in a long time. A throat with a clean tendon visible at the side, just above the high collar of the coat. The black leather greatcoat hangs open at the chest where a dark silk shirt is laced loose enough to show the line of his collarbone and the start of the broad muscle beneath it. The scales on the back of his right hand catch a torch and answer it: a soft black sheen, alive. He does not bow. He does not greet her.

He looks at her — not at the cloak, not at the gown, not at the kingdom that sent her. He looks at her the way a hunter looks at a body the dogs dragged to his door: clean appraisal. Wrist. Throat. Mouth. Back to the wrist.

He reaches for her right hand. The movement is calm and final. His scaled fingers turn her wrist inside up, lifting it toward the torchlight as if checking a weight. He brushes his thumb once across the soft skin over her pulse.

The skin under his thumb glows. A faint pulse of red, the same heartbeat as the rune on the gate. Once. Twice. Then dark.

ISOLDE does not pull the hand back. She closes her free hand into a fist at her side and keeps it closed. She does not look away from his face.

In the high black arch above them, the same rune answers from the basalt. Audience-only — she does not see it. He does.

S#4 — INNER COURT / NAMING / CONTINUOUS

[VISUAL / ACTION]

He keeps her wrist. He does not pull her — he keeps her, the difference is in the weight of his hand. KIRAN — a lean dragon-blood officer in dark scaled armor, silver at the shoulder — has come up behind VAEL, holding a folded black silk in both hands. He waits without speaking.

VAEL turns ISOLDE's wrist a little, looking at the underside, then up at her face. He speaks low, not for anyone else in the keep. The voice is not loud. It carries because it does not have to be loud.

VAEL: Your kingdom sent you to me as an offering.
ISOLDE: They sent me to die.
VAEL: They will not call you either of those again.

He releases her wrist. The skin where his thumb was is faintly red, still. He nods once at KIRAN. KIRAN bows to her — a short, exact bow — and steps aside, holding the folded black silk out.

VAEL: The white you arrived in — it burns tonight. In front of every house that watched you walk in it.

ISOLDE looks at the white fur cloak on her shoulders. She does not flinch. She does not argue. She lifts her own right hand — the one he just held — and unhooks the silver clasp at her throat herself. The cloak slides down her back into KIRAN's arm before he can reach for it. She stands in the ivory gown alone, cold at the collarbone, and does not lower her eyes.

ISOLDE: Burn it where they can see, or it doesn't count.
VAEL: That is the only place I burn anything.

He turns toward the inner hall. Behind him, a wide doorway opens onto another, deeper space — black silk, torches, the low sound of voices. The court of the keep. People waiting.

# THE OFFERING — EP02: VAEL'S CHOICE

S#1 — KEEP CHAMBER / DRESSING / NIGHT

[VISUAL / ACTION]

A high black room walled in basalt. A standing iron mirror. Two silent dragon-blood maids in plain charcoal smocks. The white fur cloak and the ivory gown lie folded on a low stone bench, already separated, already not hers anymore.

ISOLDE stands in a thin shift, bare-armed. The thumb-shaped faint red on the inside of her right wrist has not gone away since the gate. Around her collarbone, where the fur cloak lifted at the road, the skin is still faintly cold. Her dark hair is loose down her back. She does not look at the new gown yet. She looks at the iron mirror and at her own face in it.

The maids bring up the new gown. Heavy black silk, fitted close at the ribs. A high lace collar in white. A narrow silver chain laid flat down the bodice. Tiny pearl beads stitched along the seams at the shoulders. Cuff edges in white. Nothing low cut. Nothing soft.

They dress her. They do not speak. As they fit the bodice tight at her ribs, one maid lifts the silver pendant from the discarded cloak, lifts the new bodice's inner silk, and threads the pendant chain into the inner lining of the new gown. The pendant slides into a small hidden seam against her ribs. Hidden, not removed. Audience-only: the maid pauses for a half-beat over the tree-and-flame sigil — recognizes something — does not say. She finishes the seam without looking up.

In the mirror: a girl who arrived in white and is leaving the room in black. Pale skin. White at the throat. Silver and pearl threading the black.

S#2 — GREAT HALL / DOORWAY / NIGHT

[VISUAL / ACTION]

Twin doors of black iron part. The keep's great hall is full: dragon-blood officers along the walls in dark scale; the bride-candidates of the lesser human houses near the front, ELARA at their head — gold hair, gold thread, gold gown, cold mouth; the human envoy HALDREN at a side dais, watching; KIRAN stationed at the foot of the high seat.

At the head of the hall: a high black seat on a single step. Beside it, on the same step, an empty matching seat. VAEL sits in the first.

He is dressed for being looked at. The black leather greatcoat is gone — replaced by a fitted black silk and leather tunic, the laces at the throat undone two more notches than a council man would wear. The line of his throat shows. The clean tendon at the side of his neck shows. The dark silk lies flat across the broad muscle of his chest. The sleeves are pushed back from the wrists. The scaled right hand rests open on the armrest, palm down, taking up the whole armrest. Hair loose to the shoulder. Grey eyes already turned to the door before it opens.

He has been waiting.

ISOLDE steps into the doorway. The hall sees her. ELARA sees her. HALDREN sees her. The room takes one slow breath.

She walks the length of the hall. The pearls along her shoulder seams flicker at every step. Her right wrist sleeve is set short — the thumb mark on the inside catches torchlight once, twice — the same heartbeat as last night.

VAEL does not look at any of the other women. ELARA is in front of him, gold, and he does not see her. The second candidate from the left lowers her chin and waits for his glance and does not get it. He does not look at the hall, either. He looks at ISOLDE. He looks at her the whole way down the floor — not at her gown, not at the silver chain, not at the pearls. At the wrist. Then the mouth.

ELARA's gold-gloved hand closes slowly on the back of a chair until the knuckles whiten. She has worn that gown for him for three months. He has not looked at it.

S#3 — GREAT HALL / THE EMPTY SEAT (LADDER RUNG 1: BESIDE SEAT) / CONTINUOUS

[VISUAL / ACTION]

ISOLDE reaches the foot of the high step. VAEL does not rise. He lifts one hand and taps the scaled back of his knuckles, twice, on the armrest of the empty matching seat beside him. Two quiet sounds. The whole hall hears them.

ELARA's voice from below the step — sharper, public now.

ELARA: That seat is for the chosen. She walked in dragging the chain. She is not chosen.

VAEL does not look at ELARA. He looks at ISOLDE. He stands. He is taller than the room expected. The fitted tunic shows the line of his shoulder and the taper down to the waist where the belt sits low. He steps down one step.

He does not put his hand at her shoulder. He puts one wide hand at the small of ISOLDE's back. The scaled hand goes to her side, just under the ribs, fingers spreading flat against the silk. Both hands. He lifts her — not violently, but completely — up that one step and sets her into the matching seat. Her boot heels strike the basalt at the same time as his. Her bodice catches against the front of his tunic for a breath. Through the silk, against her own ribs, she feels the steady deep thud of his heart and how slow it is. Then he lets her go.

He does not step back. He stands beside her, one scaled hand resting on the back of her seat, his fingers turned inward so the tips touch the side of her hair. Not seizing — settling. From the front rank, where ELARA stands, the hall can see only one thing on the step: a Dragon Lord with his hand laid in the hair of a girl in a black gown.

He does not look at ELARA. He speaks to the whole front rank of bride-candidates.

VAEL: I do not choose. I take. This chair is taken.

# THE OFFERING — EP03: THE BLADE AND THE WALL

S#1 — ISOLDE'S CHAMBER / KEEP / NIGHT

[VISUAL / ACTION]

A small black-walled chamber. Tall narrow window, no glass — only a wrought iron grate over the night. Single low bed in black silk. A washstand. A torch in an iron bracket throwing the room in red.

ISOLDE sits on the edge of the bed in her black silk gown from the hall — silver chain still down the bodice, white at the throat, pearls along the shoulder seams. The bodice is laced tight. Her dark hair is loose. Her right wrist is bare. The faint thumb-shape on the inside of her wrist is still there, fainter now, like a thumbprint that won't wipe off.

She lifts both hands into the torchlight and turns them over. Steady. She has counted what was taken from her: the chain on the road, the cloak, the ivory gown, the name. The count is finished. What comes next is not counting.

Behind her, the iron grate over the window scrapes. Once. Soft. Wrong.

She does not turn. She moves her hand down very slowly to the washstand and lifts a small silver letter knife from the tray. She holds it down along her thigh so it cannot be seen from the window.

S#2 — ISOLDE'S CHAMBER / THE ASSASSIN / CONTINUOUS

[VISUAL / ACTION]

A figure drops down behind her — a thin assassin in soft black, face wrapped, soft-soled boots, a curved dagger in his right hand. The dagger has a kingdom mark on the pommel. Audience-only — the same seal that was on the chain at the gate. Smaller, beneath that, a second mark scratched into the metal in a finer hand: a black-wax sigil signature. Audience-only.

He moves fast. He goes for the side of her neck.

ISOLDE turns into the strike. She uses the letter knife — not to kill, only to deflect the dagger away from her throat into her shoulder. The dagger bites. Black silk darkens at the collarbone. She does not cry out.

The chamber door does not open. It is broken — torn off the iron hinge, one slab, with a single shove of a shoulder. VAEL is in the doorway.

Black coat now over the open-laced tunic. Wet at the boots from running. Grey eyes already gone darker. The scales on the back of his right hand are darker, denser, glistening — they have moved. His mouth is set in something that is not quite a man's expression anymore.

He crosses the room in three strides.

S#3 — ISOLDE'S CHAMBER / BREAKING / CONTINUOUS

[VISUAL / ACTION]

VAEL takes the assassin's dagger hand at the wrist. He does not wrestle him. He simply closes his scaled fingers around the wrist and turns. There is a sharp clean snap. The dagger drops. The assassin folds with a sound he could not stop. VAEL bends, lifts the dagger off the floor by the blade, looks once at the kingdom seal on the pommel — and at the second smaller sigil under it. His jaw works. He shows it to no one.

KIRAN appears at the broken doorway. VAEL passes the dagger to him hilt first.

VAEL: Two seals. Kingdom's loud. The other quiet. Find the quiet hand. Bring me his wrist before sunrise.
KIRAN: Yes.

KIRAN bows once, leaves. The assassin is dragged out behind him by two dragon-blood guards. None of this takes long.

VAEL: Drop the blade. I will not ask twice.

ISOLDE: They sent it for my throat. I keep what reaches my hand.
VAEL: Your hand reaches me now. Drop it.

S#4 — ISOLDE'S CHAMBER / THE WALL (LADDER RUNG 2: HAND + WALL) / CONTINUOUS

ISOLDE (low, breath caught): Don't —

His scaled hand goes up the side of her throat — over the line of her jaw — and turns her face up to him. He does not stop. The other hand drops to her right shoulder, and with one calm motion he slides the black silk strap of the gown down off that shoulder, baring her collarbone, the small wound under the silk now visible, a thin shallow cut already closing.

She looks down. Her palm against his chest has closed. Her fingers have wound into the silk of his tunic in a fist. She is not pushing. She is holding him there. The hand that said no is the hand keeping him close.

He looks at the cut. He looks at her mouth.

He kisses her — hard, the way a man kisses something he is also marking. Her free hand comes up to his shoulder. She does not push it away. He bends his head and presses his mouth once over the shallow cut on her shoulder — not a kiss, a measure — and his scales darken another shade against her skin. Then he straightens slowly, smoothes the black silk strap back up over her bared shoulder with his scaled hand, and steps one full step back.

ISOLDE's fist on his tunic does not open until he is the one who steps away. When her hand falls, the silk where she gripped it is creased. She looks at the crease, not at him.

# THE OFFERING — EP04: THE BANNER

S#1 — COUNCIL CHAMBER / KEEP / MORNING

[VISUAL / ACTION]

A long black basalt table. Iron sconces. The keep's council room. KIRAN stands at one end. Two dragon-blood officers along the wall.

At the far end, HALDREN with three human envoys — sashes in slate and gold, the kingdom seal high at the shoulder. Between them on the table: a folded standard of the human kingdom. Red field, gold sun, the same seal that was on the chain at the gate, and on the assassin's dagger pommel last night.

ISOLDE stands beside the long table, half a pace behind VAEL's empty chair. She is back in the black silk gown with the silver chain down the bodice. The pearls at her shoulder seams. The small crescent of teeth marks under her ear is half-hidden under her loose hair — visible only if you know to look. Her right wrist sleeve is short. The thumb-print mark on her inner wrist is still there from last night.
