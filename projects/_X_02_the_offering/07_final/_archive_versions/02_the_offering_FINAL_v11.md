# THE OFFERING — EP01

## S#1 — Dust Road. Royal Wagons. Day.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A line of black wagons rolls across grey wasteland. The Veine kingdom's gold-lion banner drags on a long pole. Behind the banner, a smaller iron cart. Bars on every side. A girl inside.

ISOLDE kneels in white fur, ivory silk underneath, hair loose down her back. Wrists chained to the cart floor. Cheek grazed. One pearl in her ear bent. Ankles bare on splintered wood.

HARRIN rides up beside the cart. Thirty. Gold hair pulled back. Court coat. He looks down at her.

HALDREN, a grey-bearded Veine envoy in formal grey, rides behind Harrin. He watches the chain on her wrists and says nothing.

A CROWD on the road. Farmers. Soldiers. A boy with a clay cup. They throw bread crusts. One spits. Another laughs.

ISOLDE pulls her wrist against the chain. The iron bites her skin. She doesn't make a sound. She lowers her head until her hair covers her face.

[KEY CAMERA]
Low wide tracking the cart. Cut to medium of Isolde — chained hands, head dropping. Cut to high angle: Harrin on horseback, sun behind. Cut close: a glob of spit landing on her shoulder. Cut to her chain pulled taut.

[DIALOGUE]
HARRIN: Don't cry. Save your throat for him.
A SOLDIER (laughs).
HARRIN: You ate at our table twelve years. Pay it back tonight.
ISOLDE (low, head down): Shut up, Harrin.
HARRIN: Save it. They don't pay for talking.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
SUPER lower third: THE OFFERING — DRAGON KEEP, EAST OF THE BLACK GATES.
Caption beside Harrin: HARRIN — HEIR OF VEINE.
Caption beside Isolde's wrists: ISOLDE — TO BE DELIVERED.

[END HOOK]
A shadow runs across the wagons. Wide. Slow. The horses scream.

## S#2 — Dust Road. Wing-Shadow. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The shadow crosses the road in one slow sweep. Wider than the wagon line. The sun goes out for three seconds and comes back.

The lead escort horse rears. The rider falls. His helmet rolls into the ditch.

Above the ridge — a black silhouette banks once and is gone. One angle of a wing. Enough.

The crowd stops moving. The boy drops his clay cup.

Harrin's smile loses one degree. He says nothing.

Isolde looks up. Her hand inside the chain stops shaking.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide aerial of the shadow on the road. Tilt to the ridge silhouette. Cut to Harrin's mouth tightening. Cut close to Isolde's face.

[DIALOGUE]
HARRIN: Move the line.
A SOLDIER: My lord — the lead horse —
HARRIN: Now.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
The Black Gates of Drakonis Keep swing open. No hand on them.

## S#3 — Drakonis Keep. Outer Yard. Dusk.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The yard is bigger than Veine's whole inner court. Black basalt under their boots. The keep rises above them.

The wagons stop. Harrin's men climb down. None of them speak.

Isolde is dragged out by her chain. She lands hard on her knees on stone. The white cloak drags through black dust.

Harrin steps forward, hand on sword hilt.

HARRIN: The bride. As agreed.

Silence.

Then — on the parapet — every torch lights at once. Black flame. No sound.

A figure walks to the parapet rail.

VAEL DRAKONIS. Black coat. Dark hair tied back. Hands on the stone.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide low push toward the gate. Cut to Vael on the parapet — extreme high. Cut down to Isolde on her knees in white. Tilt up the black wall behind him.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Little offering.
ISOLDE (lifts her chin, voice rough): I'm not yours yet.
VAEL: You walked through my gate. The yet is short.
HARRIN (too loud): My lord, the contract —
VAEL (not looking at him): I read it.
HARRIN: Then accept —
VAEL: I accept her. Not you. Get her up. Not by the chain. By the hand.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A guard freezes. Then takes her by the hand. Carefully.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
SUPER on Vael: VAEL DRAKONIS — LORD OF DRAKONIS KEEP.

[END HOOK]
Vael turns from the rail. The parapet torches go out one by one as he walks inside.

Hard Cut.

# THE OFFERING — EP02

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Upper Bridal Hall. Morning.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A hall set for a wedding that isn't going to happen. White silk on the long tables. A high seat empty. Six VEINE COURTIERS against the wall — three women in pale gowns, three men in Veine gold-and-white. The QUEEN — Harrin's mother — stands by the silver cup. HALDREN two steps behind her, hands clasped, eyes down.

Two of the keep's BLACK-COAT GUARDS at the far doors. They do not move.

HARRIN paces. He laughs at nothing.

HARRIN (to the queen): He'll bring her down dressed. We sit at the head. We sign. We leave by sundown.
QUEEN (sipping): If he doesn't burn the chair under you first.

Harrin laughs again. Smaller.

A PRIEST enters — old, thin, carrying a scroll already half-prepared. ISOLDE OF VEINE — DEPARTED.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the empty hall. Track in on the scroll on the priest's tray.

[DIALOGUE]
HARRIN: Date it tonight.
PRIEST: My lord — the seal —
HARRIN: I am the seal. Date it tonight.
QUEEN: Don't cry at her funeral.
HARRIN: I won't. He'll burn what's left. There won't be a body.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
SUPER on the scroll: NOTICE OF DEATH — ISOLDE OF VEINE — TONIGHT.

[END HOOK]
The doors at the far end open. Not Vael. KIRAN. Vael's second. Dry mouth.

KIRAN: The hall is for guests. Follow me. The offering is being received downstairs.

## S#2 — Drakonis Keep. Underground Stair. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The stair is wide black stone. Torches in black flame. It goes down further than a tower should be tall.

At the bottom: an OLD ALTAR carved into the foundation rock. Older than the keep above. ISOLDE stands beside it. No longer white fur. Black silk gown — fitted, ivory pearls down the collarbone, silver thread at the shoulder seams. Hair pinned high. Wrist chain gone.

Two MAIDS behind her, hands folded.

VAEL stands across the altar from her. Black coat. Hands behind his back.

HARRIN stops three steps from the bottom.

HARRIN: This isn't the contract.
VAEL: It is now.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide reveal of the underground altar. Cut to Isolde — fingers white on the altar edge. Cut to Harrin freezing. Cut to Vael.

[DIALOGUE]
HARRIN: We have a hall upstairs. We have witnesses.
VAEL: You have witnesses for a burial. I read your priest's scroll.
HARRIN (too fast): Royal precaution. If anything —
VAEL: Nothing happens. She doesn't die today.
QUEEN (descending): My lord Drakonis, we delivered her —
VAEL: You delivered her in chains. You leave in them or without them. Pick.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The Queen's mouth opens. Closes.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Priest. The line that says she's mine. Read only that.
PRIEST (shaking): ... shall be received into the keeping of the Lord of Drakonis, as wife or sacrifice.
VAEL: Wife.
HARRIN: That's — that's not —
VAEL: I'm taking.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
Vael walks around the altar. He takes Isolde's wrist. Not a hold — a grip. Black scales on the back of his hand. The torch flames lean toward him.

Hard Cut.

# THE OFFERING — EP03

## S#2 — Drakonis Keep. Isolde's Chamber. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael's palm goes flat on stone above her shoulder. Black scales an inch from her ear.

His other hand finds the silk strap at her shoulder. He doesn't slide it. He pulls. The strap snaps. The robe falls open.

He kisses her — once, mouth open, no quarter inch of pause. Then his mouth is on her jaw. Then on her throat. He doesn't stop between them.

She gasps. Her hands come up between them. She doesn't push him. She closes them in the front of his shirt and holds.

He bites — just below the line of her jaw. Hard enough to mark.

He moves a half-inch lower and bites again.

He pulls her chin toward him with his thumb and bites the corner of her mouth.

[KEY CAMERA]
Hand close — strap snapping. Cut to mouth on throat. Cut to her hands on his shirt. Cut to two crescent marks blooming dark on her throat.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (against her throat): Only I leave a mark on you.
ISOLDE (breath): Vael —
VAEL: Anyone else tries, I take the hand.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]

## S#3 — Drakonis Keep. Isolde's Chamber. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael's mouth lifts off her throat. He doesn't step back. He listens.

A SHADOW at the window slit. A gloved hand. A dagger.

The ASSASSIN swings inside — black cloth face to wrist, curved knife in reverse grip. He moves for Isolde first, not Vael.

Vael catches the assassin's wrist mid-thrust and twists. The bone cracks. The dagger drops.

Vael keeps the wrist and walks the man backward two steps to the far wall and puts him through it. Black stone breaks loose.

He picks up the dagger and bends it. The metal shrieks. Black scales run up to his elbow. His eyes — dark red, edge of the iris.

Vael lifts the assassin by the throat.

[KEY CAMERA]
Reverse grip dagger snapping in. Wrist crack. Wall impact, debris. Cut close to Vael's eye — red ring. Wide of the assassin held against the wall.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Who paid.
ASSASSIN (choking): Veine. The brother. Harrin.
VAEL: Again.
ASSASSIN: Harrin of Veine.
VAEL: Louder. So she hears.
ASSASSIN: HARRIN OF VEINE.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde against the wall. Two crescent marks on her throat. Mouth bruised. She watches.

Vael doesn't kill the assassin in front of her. He throws him at KIRAN, who has appeared in the doorway.

[DIALOGUE]
KIRAN: Yard?
VAEL: Yard. Alive.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Kiran drags the assassin out by the broken wrist. Door closes.

Vael turns back to her. Black scales still on his arms. Red still in his eyes. He puts his hand back on her throat — over the marks — and bites a third time, lower. Below the collarbone this time.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
She makes a sound. He doesn't stop.

Hard Cut.

# THE OFFERING — EP04

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Council Chamber. Day.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A long black table. Vael at the head. Kiran at his right. Three of the keep's older LORDS along the table — old men, dragon-blood, scarred. On the walls, the captured banners of every kingdom that has tried Drakonis. The gold lion of Veine is the smallest one, hung lowest.

ISOLDE stands by the eastern window. Daily black silk — the same dress shape as last night's, but the lived-in version. Two crescent marks visible on her throat. The third sits lower under the silk collar. Hair half-pinned.

The doors open. A VEINE ENVOY — grey-bearded, the king's seal on his chest. Not Harrin. Worse. The king's close hand.

He bows shallow. He looks at Isolde once and through her.

ENVOY: My lord Drakonis. King Aldric of Veine sends greetings.
VAEL: He sends the man who sent the dagger.
ENVOY: I know nothing of a dagger.
KIRAN: The dagger knew everything about you.

Kiran sets the broken dagger on the table. Beside it: a small black wax seal, sliced in half. The Veine lion-and-cup mark on it.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the council. Cut to the broken dagger on black wood. Cut to the wax seal beside it. Cut to the envoy's eye corner. Cut to Isolde at the window — she sees the seal.

[DIALOGUE]
ENVOY: I came on a marriage matter.
VAEL: Then say it.
ENVOY: The Veine court has reviewed the contract. The woman delivered is not the woman named. She is a servant. Veine will replace her with a true bride within the moon.
VAEL: A servant.
ENVOY: I am sorry, my lord.
VAEL: You're not sorry. You're early.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
SUPER beside the envoy: KING'S CLOSE HAND — VEINE COURT.

[END HOOK]
The doors at the far end of the chamber open. HARRIN walks in. Court coat. Sword on him. He has ridden back from the road. Mud on his boots.

HARRIN: I came to take her back. She's mine to take back.

## S#2 — Drakonis Keep. East Parapet. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael stands. Black scales on his hands already showing. He walks past Harrin without looking at him.

VAEL: Outside. Both of you.

The parapet looks east. Past the wasteland, the road back to Veine, and past the road, the mountains.

Vael walks to the rail. Kiran follows with the small gold-lion banner of Veine. Two guards bring the envoy and Harrin, not roughly.

ISOLDE walks out beside Vael. She is not invited. She is not stopped. She stands at his right shoulder.

Vael takes the banner from Kiran. Holds it over the rail.

He doesn't say a word. He opens his hand.

The banner ignites in midair. Black flame. The gold lion warps. The ash drifts down.

Beyond the rail — a line cracks across the wasteland. The crack widens. From the crack, a wall of black fire rises. Three hundred feet high. The length of the horizon.

The envoy stumbles back from the rail. His face goes red — half a mile of heat.

Far below in the yard, twelve of Harrin's outriders are on their knees.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide pan along the parapet. Cut to the banner igniting. Slow tilt past the rail. Wide aerial of the wall of black fire. Cut close to the envoy: skin red, beard wet. Cut to the kneeling outriders below.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (quiet, beside Isolde): That's the welcome for a servant.
ENVOY: My lord —
VAEL: You're still standing. Don't push it.
HARRIN: My father will hear —
VAEL: Yes. He will.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde turns to the envoy. Not loud.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Tell your king the servant watched it.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
SUPER on the wall: WALL OF BLACK FIRE — EAST WASTELAND, DRAKONIS BORDER.

[END HOOK]
The envoy is led back toward his horse. As he passes Isolde, his shoulder twitches. He drops a small folded square of grey parchment at her feet. Black-thumb seal on it. He doesn't look back.

Isolde puts her foot over the parchment before Kiran sees.

Hard Cut.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
