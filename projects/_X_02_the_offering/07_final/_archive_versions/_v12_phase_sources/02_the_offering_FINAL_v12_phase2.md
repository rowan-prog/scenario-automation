# THE OFFERING — EP07

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Lower Receiving Hall. Morning.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The smaller hall from EP04. Black stone, one long table, the three old dragon-blood lords seated. Kiran. Haldren standing near the wall, his Veine sash off his shoulder.

HARRIN against the wall, disarmed. Two black-coat guards on him. Mouth tight.

ELARA sits at the foot of the long table — a chair Vael allowed last night. Pale gown, gold hair high. She has not slept. Her hands fold and unfold on the wood.

VAEL at the head. Black shirt, sleeves rolled. Scales banked.

The doors open. ISOLDE walks in.

She does not look at Harrin. She does not look at Elara. She walks the length of the hall and stops at the head. Vael pushes his chair back from the table. He pats his thigh once with the back of his fingers.

She sits across his lap. Sideways. Skirt clean. One of his arms around her waist, the other on the table.

Elara's hand stops moving on the wood.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the hall. Cut to Isolde walking past Elara without a glance. Slow push of her sitting across Vael's lap. Close on Vael's arm around her waist. Close on Elara's hand frozen on the table. Cut to Harrin against the wall — eyes down.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (to the lords, without lifting his eyes from her): She speaks for me at this table. If she gives a name, I give the hand. If she gives a no, the no holds.
OLDEST LORD: My lord. The replacement bride is at the foot of the table. Veine has offered her this morning, by Haldren's hand.
VAEL: I see her.
OLDEST LORD: She is the king's gift, my lord.
VAEL (still not looking at Elara): She is in the wrong chair.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde turns her head on Vael's shoulder. She looks at Elara for the first time. She does not speak.

Elara's chin drops.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (to Elara, only now turning): Stand.
ELARA: My lord —
VAEL: Stand.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Elara stands. Her gown is heavy. Her hands shake at her sides.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Look at her. Not at me.
ELARA (to Isolde): My lady.
VAEL: Who is she.
ELARA: ... The lady of the keep.
VAEL: Who am I.
ELARA: The Lord of Drakonis.
VAEL: Whose.
ELARA: ... Hers.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The three old lords do not move. Haldren bows his head one degree.

Vael's thumb on Isolde's hip tightens once and releases.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Out. Back to Veine. Tell your king I read the offer. The chair at the foot is empty by sundown. The chair at the head is taken.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Elara walks out. She does not look at Isolde again. She does not look at Vael.

The door closes.

Isolde puts her hand flat over Vael's hand on her waist.

[KEY CAMERA]
Close on Elara standing, gown trembling. Close on Vael's eye — does not flicker. Cut to Isolde's hand over Vael's on her waist. Cut to the three lords' faces — read it.

[DIALOGUE]
None.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
Vael turns his hand under hers. He laces his fingers through hers. On the table.

In front of the three old lords. In front of Haldren. In front of Harrin against the wall.

VAEL (to Harrin, without turning toward him): You watched.
HARRIN (dry): I watched.

## S#2 — Drakonis Keep. Lower Receiving Hall. Later.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde has moved off his lap. She stands beside his chair now, one hand on the chair back. Vael's hand stays on her thigh through the black silk. Not high. Not low. Just there.

Haldren steps forward. He sets three folded grey squares of parchment on the table.

[DIALOGUE]
HALDREN: My lord. My lady. The king's grey rider has carried these out of Drakonis on three separate nights. I have intercepted them. Each bears the king's close-hand seal.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He does not unfold them. He puts them down and steps back. He does not look at Isolde when he places them. He places them and bows once.

Vael does not touch them.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (to Isolde, not the lords, not Haldren): You read them or you don't.
ISOLDE: I don't.
VAEL: Why.
ISOLDE: Because if I read them my father is bigger in this room than you. He isn't.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The oldest of the three lords lifts his chin one degree. He does not smile. He does not need to.

Vael picks up the three parchments. He does not unfold them. He passes them to Kiran.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Burn them.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Kiran walks to the fireplace at the far wall. He drops the three parchments into the slow black flame. The wax cracks. The paper goes.

Vael's hand moves up Isolde's thigh once — a slow pull of the silk — and stops at her waist again. He puts his face against her stomach for one beat. He does not say anything.

She puts her hand on the back of his head.

[KEY CAMERA]
Close on Haldren placing the three parchments. Close on Isolde's "I don't." Wide as Kiran walks the parchments to the fire. Close on black flame eating wax. Cut to Vael's face against her stomach. Cut to her hand on his head.

[DIALOGUE]
HARRIN (from the wall, dry): You don't want to know what's in them.
ISOLDE (without turning toward him): I know enough.
HARRIN: He sold you.
ISOLDE: He sold me. You delivered me. I am sitting in the only chair that didn't sell me. The rest is paper.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
Vael lifts his face off her stomach. He looks at the wall — at Harrin — for the first time today.

VAEL: Take him to the lower hall. Don't touch his hand yet. I'll do that part myself. Tomorrow.

The guards walk Harrin out. He does not look back at Isolde.

Vael's hand stays on her waist.

Hard Cut.

# THE OFFERING — EP08

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Isolde's Chamber. Afternoon.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The chamber. Late winter light through the slit window. The bed remade. The black silk laid out on the foot of the bed.

Isolde at the window. She wears a thin chemise, the silk not on yet. Her three crescent marks visible. Her wrists no longer bruised — pale now, only memory of yellow.

VAEL enters. He has not bothered with sleeves today. Black undershirt loose, scales visible to the elbow on both arms. He closes the door.

He stops at the foot of the bed. He looks at the silk laid out for the evening.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Don't put that on tonight.
ISOLDE: Then what.
VAEL: Nothing yet.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He crosses to her at the window. He puts his hand on the small of her back through the chemise. He turns her to face him. He looks at her three crescents. He counts them with his thumb. One. Two. Three.

The fourth — the hidden one below the sternum — he does not touch. He covers it with his palm flat through the chemise.

He stands close enough that her hair touches his jaw.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: My father sat in that chamber across the hall before me. He kept three women in this keep. One in the upper tower. One in the lower hall. One nameless one in this room. None of them sat at his table.
ISOLDE: You're telling me your father had three wives.
VAEL: I'm telling you my father had three women.
ISOLDE: Why are you telling me.
VAEL: Because today my house lords saw a woman at my table. The first one. They didn't ask me her name. They asked you yours.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He puts his forehead against hers. He does not kiss her. Not yet.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Is that what you wanted.
VAEL: I didn't know I wanted it. I know now.
ISOLDE: I'm not your father's third woman.
VAEL: You are not anyone else's anything.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She lifts both hands to his jaw. She holds his face. She kisses him — first, this time, before he can move. Slow. Not the bite from EP03. Not the open mouth from EP05. Hers.

He lets her. For three beats. Then he lifts her by the waist and sets her on the windowsill behind her. His hands stay on her hips.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the chamber. Close on his palm flat over her hidden fourth mark through the chemise. Close on her hands taking his face. Cut to her kissing him — her initiative, not his. Cut to him lifting her to the sill.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: You went first.
ISOLDE: I did.
VAEL: Do it again.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She does.

He kisses back this time. His hand goes flat against her back through the chemise. Slow. Steady. No teeth tonight.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
A KNOCK at the door. Not Kiran's. The heavy one. Older fist.

The OLDEST LORD'S voice through the door, low and respectful for the first time in this hall:

OLDEST LORD: My lord. My lady. The eastern ridge is moving.

Vael does not lift his mouth off hers immediately. He finishes the kiss. Then he turns his head toward the door.

VAEL: We come down.

## S#2 — Drakonis Keep. East Parapet. Dusk.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The east parapet. Vael at the rail. Isolde at his right shoulder. She has put on the black silk now — fitted, ivory pearls at the collarbone, hair pinned. Three crescent marks bare.

Beyond the wasteland, beyond the wall of mountains — the eastern ridge moves.

It does not crawl. A length of mountain detaches. And is, then, a black dragon. Three hundred feet. Scales like wet obsidian. One massive head, two pairs of horns. Wings unfold and the eastern sky goes dark.

It does not roar. It settles its head on its forepaws. It looks at the keep.

It is looking at her.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide aerial — mountain detaching into dragon. Slow tilt down the body. Close on the dragon's eye — the same red ring as Vael's. Cut to the dragon's gaze finding Isolde. Cut close to her — she has not stepped back.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (quiet): That is you.
VAEL: That is me.
ISOLDE: Why now.
VAEL: It came up when my house lords stood for you. It wanted to see what you looked like.
ISOLDE: And.
VAEL: And it is not going back to sleep.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The dragon lifts its head one inch. The wind off its breath crosses the parapet. Her hair moves. She does not.

She turns toward Vael. She puts her hand flat against his chest — over his sternum, where she put her hand on the terrace in EP05.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: It knows me.
VAEL: It knows you. So does every house lord on this continent now. So will your father when he arrives.
ISOLDE: He's coming.
VAEL: He's coming.
ISOLDE: Tomorrow.
VAEL: Tomorrow.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The dragon's head settles back on its forepaws. The eye does not close. It watches both of them.

Vael takes her hand off his chest. He turns it over. He puts his mouth on the inside of her wrist. The dragon's eye watches.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the parapet with the dragon in background of the shot. Slow push to Vael's mouth on her wrist. Close on the dragon's red-ring eye — does not blink.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (against her wrist): If he comes tomorrow, you stand where you stood in the hall today. At my chair. Not behind it.
ISOLDE: I will.
VAEL: One more thing.
ISOLDE: What.
VAEL: When he asks for you back — and he will — you don't answer him. You answer me. In front of him.
ISOLDE: With what word.
VAEL: The one you said on the terrace.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She does not say it now. She closes her hand around his thumb again. The way she did in EP05.

Behind them, the dragon's eye narrows once. Approval.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
SUPER along the ridge: DRAKONIS RIDGE — WATCHING.

[END HOOK]
The keep bell rings once. Sundown. A guard on the south wall raises a long horn.

A grey rider rounds the bend of the south road. Fresh horse. Black wax on his belt — but a wax larger than Harrin's. King Aldric's own seal.

Vael does not move from the rail. Neither does she.

VAEL: Tomorrow morning.

Hard Cut.

# THE OFFERING — EP09

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Vael's Private Chamber. Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael's chamber. Wide fireplace, slow black flame. Wide bed. Heavy door closed. No guards inside. The keep has gone quiet because Vael wanted it quiet.

ISOLDE in maiden nightwear. Sheer white silk, pearl trim, ivory lace robe. Pendant outside the silk. Three crescent marks dark. The fourth — under the chemise — hers only.

VAEL has dropped the day coat. Loose black tunic. Scales banked. He sits on the edge of the wide bed. He is waiting for her.

She stops three paces from him. She does not come closer yet.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Tomorrow morning my father walks in with one hundred men.
VAEL: He does.
ISOLDE: And you keep me.
VAEL: I keep you.
ISOLDE: That isn't a question I'm asking. That's the line I want you to say. Say it.
VAEL: I keep you.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She crosses the three paces. She kneels — not for him, beside him. She puts her cheek against his thigh through the trousers. She does not look up.

He puts his hand on the back of her head. His thumb in her hair, slow.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the chamber. Slow black flame in the fireplace. Cut to her three paces. Close on her kneeling beside him, cheek to his thigh. Cut to his hand sliding under her hair.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Look at me.
ISOLDE (does not lift her head): No. Not yet.
VAEL: Why not.
ISOLDE: Because if I look at you now I won't be able to look at him tomorrow.
VAEL: Look at me.
ISOLDE: ... In a moment.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He doesn't say it again. He waits.

She lifts her head. Slow. Her hair falls back off her face. Her eyes are wet. They do not spill.

He puts his thumb on her bottom lip. He does not press.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: My bride.
ISOLDE: Yours.
VAEL: Say it back the whole way.
ISOLDE: I am yours.
VAEL: Say what you aren't.
ISOLDE: I am not his.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He lifts her up by both elbows. He sets her between his knees, standing. He puts both hands at her waist. He pulls the lace robe off her shoulders. He pulls the chemise down to her waist.

He puts his mouth on the fourth mark — the hidden one. The one no one else has seen. He bites it again. Light teeth. He sets the mark deeper.

She gasps. She closes her hands in his hair.

[KEY CAMERA]
Close on her hands in his hair. Close on his mouth at the fourth mark. Slow push around them as the chemise falls. Close on her face — eyes closed, breath shaking, no tears.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (against her sternum): This one is the only one he will not see tomorrow.
ISOLDE: Then put another somewhere he will see.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He looks up at her. He has heard what she said. He stands.

He puts his mouth on the side of her neck — below the second crescent, above her shoulder. He bites. Hard enough this time to leave a fifth crescent. A new one. Bright red, not yet dark.

He pulls back. He looks at it. It is in the exact place a high collar will not hide.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: He'll see.
ISOLDE: Good.
VAEL: Get in the bed.
ISOLDE: With you.
VAEL: With me.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
She gets in the bed. He follows. He pulls the black sheet over them both. The slow black flame in the fireplace settles low.

He does not let go of her.

## S#2 — Drakonis Keep. Vael's Private Chamber. Late Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Same chamber. Hours later. Candle guttered. Slow black fire still alive. The bed half-shadow.

Isolde asleep against his chest. Her hand on his sternum. The fifth crescent on her neck dark now, set in.

Vael not asleep. He looks at the ceiling. His hand moves slow up and down her back through the chemise she has put back on.

A KNOCK at the heavy door. Two knocks. Kiran.

KIRAN (off, low): My lord. A box. From Veine. The king's own seal. It came directly. Not by Harrin's hand. Not by an envoy. It came by a single grey rider.

Vael turns his head toward the door. He does not move his hand from her back.

[KEY CAMERA]
Low light of the chamber. Close on her hand on his sternum — five fingers spread. Close on the new fifth crescent dark on her neck. Cut to him looking at the ceiling. Cut to the door — heavy, closed.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (low, through the door, not loud enough to wake her): Bring it inside. On the table. Don't speak again.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The door opens. Kiran enters. He sets a small wooden box on Vael's chamber table. Black wax on top: King Aldric's personal ring. Larger than Harrin's.

Kiran does not look at the bed. He bows. He goes.

Vael does not move yet. He stays where he is for a long moment. Isolde's breath against his chest.

He moves her hand off his sternum. He kisses her palm. He sets it on the pillow beside her face. He gets out of the bed.

He crosses to the table. He looks at the box. He does not break the seal yet.

He turns. He looks back at her on the bed. Five crescent marks visible.

[KEY CAMERA]
Slow tracking out of the bed. Cut to the box on the table. Cut to Vael at the table. Cut wide of him looking back at the bed. Slow push to the box.

He breaks the seal.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He lifts the lid.

Inside: a single folded sheet. King Aldric's hand. Two lines.

Vael reads silent. He stands very still.

He carries the page back to the bed. He sits on the edge. He waits for her to wake.

She wakes — feels his weight on the edge. She opens her eyes.

He does not show her the page yet.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: From him.
VAEL: From him. His own hand. Not the court's.
ISOLDE: First time.
VAEL: First time.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He gives her the page. She reads.

ALDRIC (V.O., his voice for the first time, dry, patient): My daughter. I sold you to bury your mother's name. Tomorrow I come to take it back.

She lowers the page. She puts her hand flat against her sternum. The hand falls back to her lap.

She looks at Vael.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: He calls me his daughter.
VAEL: He's never written that word about you before.
ISOLDE: He wrote it tonight.
VAEL: He wrote it the night before he comes.
ISOLDE: He'll ask for me back.
VAEL: He'll ask.
ISOLDE: With what does he ask.
VAEL: His own army. Not his court.
ISOLDE: And you.
VAEL: I keep you.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He kisses her — slow. Hand flat against her back through the silk. The fifth crescent visible through his fingers.

In the fireplace, the slow black flame stutters once and steadies.

[KEY CAMERA]
Close on the king's seal cracked open. Close on the two lines. Cut to her hand falling to her lap. Close on Vael's mouth on hers. Close on the fifth crescent under his fingers.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
SUPER as the page lowers: KING ALDRIC — TOMORROW MORNING.

[END HOOK]
Vael lifts his mouth off hers. He looks at the door.

VAEL: Tomorrow he kneels.
ISOLDE: Tomorrow.

Hard Cut.
