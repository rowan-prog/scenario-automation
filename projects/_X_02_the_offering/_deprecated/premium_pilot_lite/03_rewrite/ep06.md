# THE OFFERING — EP06: SHE SITS

S#1 — GREAT HALL / EVENING SEATING / NIGHT

[VISUAL / ACTION]

The great hall again. The high black seat. The empty matching seat beside it on the single step. The hall is filling — dragon-blood officers, the bride-candidates at the front (ELARA at their head, still in gold), HALDREN on the side dais.

A new candidate tonight: a thin pale girl in lavender silk, brought forward by an older woman of her house. She walks the length of the hall and stops at the foot of the high step, eyes lifted. She has been told this is her chance.

VAEL sits in the high seat. He does not stand. He does not look at her.

KIRAN, at his usual station, raises a single open hand — a low, almost private gesture. The lavender candidate hesitates. Her older patroness puts a hand on her arm. They step back into the front rank without sitting on the empty step. ELARA's mouth tightens. She knows what this means.

The hall waits. The matching seat stays empty.

TRACKING: Lavender candidate walking the hall length, stopping at the step.
INSERT: VAEL not looking up. Hands loose. Scaled right hand on the armrest.
INSERT: KIRAN's open hand. A quiet refusal.
PROFILE: ELARA's mouth tightening. Cold.

[DIALOGUE]
LAVENDER CANDIDATE'S PATRONESS (very low, to KIRAN): My niece has been promised the audience.
KIRAN: Stand back.
PATRONESS: She has been —
KIRAN: Stand back.

S#2 — GREAT HALL / SHE WALKS UP / NIGHT

[VISUAL / ACTION]

From the side arch — the inner doorway, not the great front doors — ISOLDE enters the hall.

She is in the black silk gown with the silver chain down the bodice. White at the throat. Pearls along the shoulder seams. Her dark hair is loose tonight — uncovered. The crescent under her right ear is visible. The crescent on the left side of her throat is visible. Two clean teeth marks at the same height on either side of her throat, plain as silverwork.

She does not walk down the length of the hall. She walks straight across from the side arch. Her path is short. The hall sees her by the time she has taken two steps.

She reaches the foot of the high step. She does not wait for VAEL to tap the armrest. She lifts the front hem of the gown by a finger and steps up onto the single step. She turns. She sits in the empty matching seat. She sets both forearms on the armrests. Her right wrist sleeve falls back. The thumb-shaped mark on the inside is dark red tonight, with the thin red trace climbing higher up her forearm than yesterday.

VAEL turns his head slowly toward her. He does not show his face changing, but the scales on the back of his right hand on the armrest darken further.

S#3 — GREAT HALL / PUBLIC HAND / NIGHT

[VISUAL / ACTION]

VAEL reaches across the small gap between the two seats. He picks up ISOLDE's right hand. He lifts it off her armrest, turns it palm down across his lap, and brings the back of her hand up to his mouth. He kisses the back of her hand once, slowly, with the hall watching.

He turns her hand over. He turns it inside up. The thumb mark and the red trace are visible to anyone in the front rank.

He lays the inside of her wrist against his mouth. He does not just kiss it. He drags his lips along the trace — from the thumb mark up to where the red has reached on her forearm — slowly. His scaled fingers cradle her hand the whole time. The trace warms under his mouth. Visibly. The line of red gets a fraction longer. By the time his mouth has reached the highest point of the trace, the trace has reached a fraction higher.

He keeps her wrist there. He does not put it back on her armrest.

He lifts his other hand — the one without scales — and runs it once down the length of her loose dark hair to the small of her back, exactly the way he did three nights ago, exactly where the whole hall can see. He stops with his fingers wound loosely into the ends of her hair at her waist. He does not let go.

ELARA, in the front rank, is no longer holding the back of her chair. Her hands have dropped to her sides. Her mouth is open a fraction.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (only to her, but audible to the front rank): The wrist stays bare in this hall.
ISOLDE: It will.

S#4 — GREAT HALL / WITHDRAWAL / NIGHT

[VISUAL / ACTION]

HALDREN, on his side dais, steps down off the dais. He walks across the basalt floor and stops at the foot of the high step. He does not look at VAEL. He looks at ISOLDE. He lowers his head — not the deep bow of a subject, but the considered lowering of an envoy who has just changed his mind. It is not yet the kingdom's lowering. It is his.

HALDREN: The kingdom withdraws its claim. On her name.

He does not say what the kingdom called her. He does not need to. The whole hall heard it called at the gate four nights ago.

His three companions behind him are still. One opens his mouth to protest. The other puts a hand on his arm. They keep silent.

HALDREN lifts his head. He looks at VAEL now. He does not bow to VAEL.

HALDREN: I will send the message tonight.
VAEL: Send it tomorrow.

VAEL has not moved his hands. One is still holding ISOLDE's wrist, lips a finger's length from her skin. The other is still wound into the ends of her hair.

ELARA turns and walks the length of the hall out the side arch. She does not run. She does not look at anyone. She is the only one moving.

[KEY CAMERA]
WIDE: HALDREN stepping down from his dais.
LOW: HALDREN's feet crossing the basalt to the foot of the step.
PROFILE: HALDREN lowering his head toward ISOLDE.
WIDE (9:16): ELARA walking the length of the hall, exiting through the side arch.
CLOSE: ISOLDE's face. She does not smile. She does not move. The thumb mark on her wrist deepens slowly under VAEL's hand. Audience-only.

[DIALOGUE]
HALDREN: The kingdom withdraws its claim. On her name.
HALDREN: I will send the message tonight.
VAEL: Send it tomorrow.

[END HOOK]
KIRAN crosses to VAEL's side and lowers his voice for only VAEL — and, because ISOLDE is right there, only just for her too.

KIRAN: Lord. A second envoy is on the ridge. New seal.
VAEL (without looking up from ISOLDE's wrist): Let them come.
