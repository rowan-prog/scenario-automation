# THE OFFERING — EP08: THE WORD THAT WON'T LAND

S#1 — GREAT HALL / SECOND ENVOY ARRIVES / DUSK

[VISUAL / ACTION]

The great hall has been opened. The twin black iron doors have been thrown back to show the courtyard outside, and beyond the courtyard, the long waste road. The dusk sky behind it is bruised red.

A second human envoy stands in the open doorway. Twelve mounted human guards behind him at attention, twelve standards in their hands. The envoy holds the red parchment from this morning aloft like a sentence. His face is older than HALDREN's, harder, his sash heavier.

Inside the hall: dragon-blood officers along the walls, three deep tonight. KIRAN at the foot of the high step. HALDREN at his side dais, but no longer in slate diplomat's robes — in plain grey. The other bride-candidates along the front. ELARA at their head, in gold, but with her eyes on the basalt floor in front of her feet.

VAEL sits in the high seat. The empty matching seat beside him on the single step is empty.

ISOLDE is not in the hall. Not yet.

S#2 — GREAT HALL / BANNER, SECOND TIME / CONTINUOUS

[VISUAL / ACTION]

VAEL stands. He walks down off the single step. He crosses the basalt floor to the open doors. He does not stop in the doorway. He walks out into the courtyard. The dragon-blood officers part for him. KIRAN follows two paces behind. HALDREN follows three paces behind KIRAN.

VAEL reaches the second envoy. He does not speak first. He reaches up and takes the kingdom's red parchment out of the envoy's hand — and out of the envoy's grip, with the same calm finality he used on the assassin's wrist.

VAEL turns to the twelve mounted guards. He lifts the parchment in his scaled right hand. The hand warms. The scales on the back of it darken further. The bare skin of his palm glows. The parchment catches.

A single moving line of fire eats the parchment from his palm out to the edge, slow, lazy, contained. The black wax seal melts last. The ash drops at the feet of the second envoy. Then VAEL reaches for the foremost standard.

He pulls the standard out of the lead guard's hand. The guard does not resist. VAEL drops the pole onto the cobble at the second envoy's feet — and lights the cloth, not the pole, with the same palm. The kingdom's red field burns down in his fist. The pole stays. Ash piles in front of the envoy's boots. The envoy's face does not change.

VAEL: She is not returning.

S#3 — GREAT HALL / SHE IS LIFTED UP / CONTINUOUS

[VISUAL / ACTION]

VAEL turns from the cobble and walks back across the courtyard, back through the open twin doors, back into the hall. KIRAN follows. The second envoy is brought after them — escorted, not held, by two dragon-blood officers. The mounted guards stay outside in the courtyard. The hall sees the open doors and the twelve standards behind them.

ISOLDE has been brought to the foot of the high step from the side arch while VAEL was outside. She is in a sub-variant of the black silk gown tonight — the same gown, but with a wide band of silver-and-pearl across the upper chest and collarbone, hair half-pinned with a single pearl at the crown, the rest loose down her back. The wide pearl band is set lower than the lace collar of the earlier nights. The two crescents on her throat are uncovered. They are higher than the band. The hall sees them.

VAEL crosses the basalt floor straight to her. He does not stop at the step. He takes her at the waist with both hands. He lifts her — completely — off the basalt up onto the single step beside him. Her boots strike the step at the same time as his. He keeps his hands at her waist a beat longer than he needs to. He turns her gently to face the open doors. The hall and the doorway and the twelve mounted standards all see her face at once.

The pearls on her chest catch the torchlight. The two crescents on her throat catch it cleaner.

S#4 — GREAT HALL / HAIR, JAW, WRIST / CONTINUOUS

[VISUAL / ACTION]

VAEL stays beside her on the step. He turns to face her fully. His scaled right hand goes up the side of her throat — over the right-side crescent — and his palm covers it. The whole hall watches him do it. His left hand goes into her hair at the back of her neck, gathers a fist of it, and pulls her face up to his, tilting her chin with the angle of her hair.

He brings his face down to a finger's length above her mouth.

His scaled palm at her throat drops to the band of pearls across her collarbone. He lays his palm flat across the pearls. He drags his palm — slowly — sideways, until it is over the left side of her throat too, and over the second crescent. He stops there. Both crescents are now beneath his hand in plain sight of the hall.

Then he lifts her right wrist with his other hand. He turns it over inside up. The thumb mark on the inner wrist is dark, the red trace has reached almost to her elbow tonight, and the scales on his hand at her throat answer the trace with the same beat. He lays the inside of her wrist against his mouth. He kisses the mark. He keeps his mouth there.

The hall — for the first time — fully understands what they are seeing.

S#5 — GREAT HALL / THE WORD THAT WON'T LAND / CONTINUOUS

[VISUAL / ACTION]

VAEL straightens. He keeps her wrist in his scaled hand. His other hand stays at the back of her hair. He turns them both — together, the way two figures turn on a single pivot — to face the open doors, the second envoy, the twelve mounted standards, the dusk.

He lifts his voice. Not loud. Carrying.

VAEL: She is —

A wing rushes overhead. Not in the hall. Above the keep. A single huge dark shape passes across the bruised red dusk in the open doorway. A second pass behind it. Then a third. Then a long heavy thrum of more wings beyond the ridge.

A first roar — open, throated, longer than any horn the kingdom has ever blown — comes down from above the keep and crashes through the courtyard. The twelve mounted standards rear. Their twelve riders go to one knee in the cobble. Twelve standards drop. The second envoy does not kneel — but his right hand opens. The red parchment ash from his feet scatters.

VAEL has not finished the sentence. The word that comes after "is" has not arrived. The hall is on its feet. The hall did not see him say it.

ISOLDE looks up at him. Her right hand, still in his scaled grip, turns inside his hand. She does it deliberately. She takes hold of his right hand at the back, where the scales are densest. She closes her own fingers over his scaled knuckles — small, pale, certain. She does not let go.

She lifts her face to his. Her mouth opens.

ISOLDE (one word, low, half lost in the second roar): Yours.

VAEL bends to her mouth. He stops a quarter inch above it. The roar overhead climbs. The twelve riders are still on one knee in the courtyard. The hall is silent in the way of something about to break.
