# THE OFFERING — EP05: TERRACE, PULSE, BITE

S#1 — UPPER TERRACE / KEEP / NIGHT

[VISUAL / ACTION]

A high stone terrace cut into the back of the keep. The basalt ridge of the mountain drops straight down on the far side, black against a black sky thick with stars. A low parapet. No torches up here — only one iron brazier at the far end, burning low. The air is cold enough to see breath.

ISOLDE stands at the parapet. She is back in the black silk gown with the silver chain down the bodice. A thin black wool wrap over her shoulders against the cold. Her dark hair has been gathered loosely off her neck and pinned with a single silver pin so the wound under her ear from two nights ago is not visible. The thumb mark on her inner wrist is hidden under the long sleeve tonight — but the thin red trace up her forearm, fainter now, is still under the silk.

She lifts her right hand into the brazier's red glow and pushes the silk sleeve back a few inches. The trace shows up faintly. She turns her wrist and looks at it. She does not understand what it is yet. She knows it gets stronger when he is near.

Behind her, no sound. He simply is there. VAEL has come up onto the terrace from the inner stair without speaking. Black coat. The scales on the back of his right hand are still dark and dense. He stops two paces behind her at the parapet.

VAEL: Cover that.

She does not. Not yet. She pushes the sleeve a quarter inch further up.

S#2 — UPPER TERRACE / WRIST AND JAW / CONTINUOUS

[VISUAL / ACTION]

He crosses the two paces in a half second. He does not stop in front of her — he steps in past her shoulder, against the parapet, blocking the night with the line of his body. His scaled right hand catches her right wrist where the sleeve is pushed back. He does not yank. He folds his hand around her forearm and lifts it up between them so the inside is between his chest and her chest. The red trace flickers — once — twice — bright against pale skin, suddenly more visible than it has been all day.

His other hand, the one without scales, comes up under her chin. Two fingers and a thumb at the underside of her jaw. He tilts her face up to his. Their breath is close enough to mix in the cold air. His mouth is just above hers.

He does not kiss her mouth.

His mouth moves to the side of her face instead — to the soft skin just behind the corner of her jaw, under her ear. He sets his lips there for one long beat. He inhales against her skin. He drags his mouth — slowly — down the side of her throat, over the place she had pinned her hair to hide, brushing the crescent he left there two nights ago. He does not bite. Not there. Not yet.

His mouth moves down to the inside of the underside of her jaw, where her pulse is. He sets his mouth on her pulse, openmouthed, hot, and stays there for one heavy breath. Her pulse against his lips. The sound is wet enough that she hears it.

Her eyes close. Her hand at her side closes into a fist. Her hand that he is holding does not pull back.

S#3 — UPPER TERRACE / SECOND BITE / CONTINUOUS

[VISUAL / ACTION]

He moves his mouth back up the side of her throat. He stops at the place opposite the crescent he made two nights ago — the side of her throat where there is no mark yet. He bites. Once. Hard enough to leave a second crescent, clean, twin to the first, on the other side. She inhales sharply. Her hand on his coat at his chest opens flat. She does not push him.

He releases the bite. He stays close, his mouth not quite leaving her skin. His scaled hand still holds her wrist between them. The red trace under her sleeve is pulsing in time with his breath.

He lifts her wrist between them, deliberately, until the inside is in front of both their faces. He turns it inside up. He sets his mouth on the thumb-shaped mark on her inner wrist and presses. The mark deepens — visibly — the same way it did two nights ago. The red trace up her forearm warms. The skin under his mouth is hot. He stays there longer than is necessary. When he lifts his mouth, the mark is darker than it has been.

He sets her wrist back down on the parapet with care. He does not let go.

ISOLDE looks at the wrist, at him, at the wrist. She does not flinch. She does not pull away. The faint outline of the new crescent on the right side of her throat is already showing under the silver pin in her hair.

ISOLDE: What does that mean. On my wrist.
VAEL: It means you are not theirs.
ISOLDE: Then whose.
VAEL: You already know.

S#4 — UPPER TERRACE / NOT YET / CONTINUOUS

[VISUAL / ACTION]

He lets her wrist go. He does not step back yet. His scaled hand passes once along the side of her throat — over both crescents, the old one and the new one — and stops at her collarbone. His thumb rests at the hollow of her throat. He looks at her mouth. He brings his face a half breath closer. Then he stops himself there.

He does not kiss her mouth. He sets his forehead against the side of her temple for one beat. His breath against her ear is uneven for the first time. Then he straightens. He pulls the silk sleeve down over the red trace on her forearm himself, with the back of his scaled fingers. He smoothes the silver pin in her hair. He covers the new crescent with the loose strand he displaced.

He steps back. He does not turn yet.

VAEL: Inside. Now.
ISOLDE: Stay.

It is the first time she has used the word toward him. He stops. He looks at her — wrist, throat, mouth. He does not come back.

VAEL: Not yet.

He turns. He walks the length of the terrace and down the inner stair. The brazier flickers at the far end. ISOLDE stays at the parapet, both hands flat against the stone, both crescents on her throat now under loose hair, the thumb mark and the red trace under her sleeve quietly burning.

The red trace is reaching higher up her forearm than yesterday. Her free hand goes up — slowly — to the side of her throat. To the new crescent. Her fingers stay there.
