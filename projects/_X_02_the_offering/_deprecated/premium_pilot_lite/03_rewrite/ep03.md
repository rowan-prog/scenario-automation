# THE OFFERING — EP03: THE BLADE AND THE WALL

S#1 — ISOLDE'S CHAMBER / KEEP / NIGHT

[VISUAL / ACTION]

A small black-walled chamber. Tall narrow window, no glass — only a wrought iron grate over the night. Single low bed in black silk. A washstand. A torch in an iron bracket throwing the room in red.

ISOLDE sits on the edge of the bed in her black silk gown from the hall — silver chain still down the bodice, white at the throat, pearls along the shoulder seams. The bodice is laced tight. Her dark hair is loose. The mark on the inside of her wrist is still there, fainter now, like a thumbprint that won't wipe off.

She lifts her right hand into the torchlight and turns it. Once. The faint shape is still there.

Behind her, the iron grate over the window scrapes. Once. Soft. Wrong.

She does not turn. She listens. She moves her hand down very slowly to the washstand and lifts a small silver letter knife from the tray. She holds it down along her thigh so it cannot be seen from the window.

[KEY CAMERA]
MACRO: Underside of ISOLDE's right wrist. Faint thumb-shaped red.
INSERT: Iron grate moving a finger's width. Soft scrape.
CLOSE: ISOLDE's hand lifting the silver letter knife. Holding it flat to her thigh.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (very low, to herself): I do not kneel for them.

S#2 — ISOLDE'S CHAMBER / THE ASSASSIN / CONTINUOUS

[VISUAL / ACTION]

A figure drops down behind her — a thin assassin in soft black, face wrapped, soft soled boots, a curved dagger in his right hand. The dagger has a kingdom mark on the pommel. Audience-only — the same seal that was on the chain at the gate.

He moves fast. He goes for the side of her neck.

ISOLDE turns into the strike. She uses the letter knife — not to kill, only to deflect the dagger away from her throat into her shoulder. The dagger bites. Black silk darkens at the collarbone. She does not cry out.

The chamber door does not open. It is broken — torn off the iron hinge, one slab, with a single shove of a shoulder. VAEL is in the doorway.

Black coat. Wet at the boots from running. Grey eyes already gone darker. The scales on the back of his right hand are darker, denser, glistening — they have moved.

He crosses the room in three strides.

[KEY CAMERA]
WHIP PAN: Assassin dropping behind her.
CLOSE: Curved dagger meeting the letter knife at the throat. Sliding to her shoulder. Black silk darkening.
MED: VAEL in the broken doorway, scaled hand already at his side, already wrong.
LOW WIDE: VAEL crossing the chamber in three strides.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Hand.

He says it to the assassin, not to her.

S#3 — ISOLDE'S CHAMBER / BREAKING / CONTINUOUS

[VISUAL / ACTION]

VAEL takes the assassin's dagger hand at the wrist. He does not wrestle him. He simply closes his scaled fingers around the wrist and turns. There is a sharp clean snap. The dagger drops. The assassin folds with a sound he could not stop. VAEL bends, lifts the dagger off the floor by the blade, looks once at the kingdom seal on the pommel, and shows it to no one in particular.

VAEL (to KIRAN somewhere in the corridor): Take this. Track who held it last.

KIRAN appears at the broken doorway, takes the dagger from him in both hands, bows once, leaves. The assassin is dragged out behind him by two dragon-blood guards. None of this takes long.

The chamber is suddenly very quiet. The torch is still red. ISOLDE is still standing by the bed, the letter knife in her right hand, black silk dark and wet at the left shoulder, the silver pendant chain through the lining at her ribs catching torchlight in a single white line.

VAEL turns to her. He is not breathing fast. He is breathing wrong — slow, deep, careful, the way something inhuman tries to remember not to break a thing.

VAEL: Drop it.

ISOLDE: I had it for him.

VAEL: Drop it.

She drops the letter knife on the bed.

S#4 — ISOLDE'S CHAMBER / THE WALL / CONTINUOUS

[VISUAL / ACTION]

VAEL crosses the last step between them. He does not stop. He puts a wide hand at the front of her shoulder, not the wounded side — and walks her two steps back into the black basalt wall. Her back hits the stone. The silver chain down her bodice slides flat. Her dark hair fans against the wall behind her.

His scaled hand goes up the side of her throat — over the line of her jaw — and turns her face up to him. The other hand drops to her right shoulder, and with one calm motion he slides the black silk strap of the gown down off that shoulder, baring her collarbone, the small wound under the silk now visible, a thin shallow cut already closing.

He looks at the cut. He looks at the underside of her wrist where the mark is. He looks at her mouth.

He kisses her — hard, the way a man kisses something he is also marking. Her hands come up. They do not push him. One settles flat against his chest and stays there. The other stays at her side, fingers curling slightly in. She is breathing through her nose. She is not pulling away.

While he kisses her, his scaled hand slides from her jaw down to the side of her neck. He bites the soft place under her ear, hard enough to leave a mark — one mark — a small crescent of teeth. She inhales sharply through her teeth. She does not say no.

He breaks the kiss only enough to speak.

VAEL: They sent a blade for you tonight.

He lifts her right hand by the wrist. He turns it so the inner wrist faces up. He sets his mouth on the faint red thumb mark and presses his lips there. The mark deepens — visibly — under his mouth. Then he straightens slowly, smoothes the black silk strap back up over her bared shoulder with his scaled hand, and steps one full step back.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: They sent a blade for you tonight.
ISOLDE: Don't stop.
VAEL: Not yet.

[END HOOK]
He steps back. He looks at her — wrist, throat, mouth, the small fresh crescent under her ear. He turns toward the broken doorway. He does not look back.

VAEL: Don't move. Until I return.
