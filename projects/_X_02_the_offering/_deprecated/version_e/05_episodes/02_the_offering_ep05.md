# THE OFFERING — EP05: The Collarbone

S#1 — KEEP / TERRACE / NIGHT

[Visual]
A wide terrace cut into the basalt above the courtyard. A long shallow **reflecting pool** runs the length of the terrace — black water, still. Stars overhead are doubled in it. Two torches at the rail.
ISOLDE stands at the edge of the pool, in **Vael's Choice**. The silver-and-pearl band at the throat is slightly loosened tonight — half a finger of skin shows above it. The pearl is set low. The ivory cuff at the right wrist sits a touch higher than yesterday. The bite-mark beneath is at the edge of cuff-line.
She is looking down into the pool. Her face is doubled there, but the doubled face is slightly older — the way the dark water flattens her cheekbone, lengthens her eye. A faint echo of the **pendant sigil** prints on the pool surface for one frame, audience-only — gone before she sees it.
A second silhouette enters the pool reflection — VAEL. He is behind her, three paces back. The pool gives him before she hears him.

[Camera]
WIDE LOW: terrace, reflecting pool, doubled stars → ECU: ISOLDE's profile at pool edge → MACRO INSERT: pool reflection of her face → FLICKER FRAME: pendant sigil on pool surface, audience-only → REVERSE: VAEL's reflection entering the pool from behind, before his real body shows in frame → SLOW WIDE: VAEL stepping into frame three paces behind her, real and reflected at once

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: I felt you on the stair.
VAEL (low, slow, formal): The mark on your wrist felt me three steps before the stair did. It is faster than I am tonight.
ISOLDE: It does not ask.
VAEL: Neither do you.

S#2 — TERRACE / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
He closes the distance. He does not touch the band at her throat. He does not touch the cuff at her wrist. He sets his **scaled palm flat on the basalt rail** beside her hand. The polished rail under the scaled palm gives back the crescent on his hand-back.
She turns to face him. He is a hand-width taller.
He lifts his **other** hand — not scaled — and finds the silver-and-pearl band at her throat. With one slow finger he hooks the band lower. Half a finger more of bare skin opens. The **collarbone** is given to the night.
His mouth does not go to her mouth. His mouth goes to the collarbone — slow lip-press first, then the slow set of teeth.
The second bite. Set lower than the wrist mark. Set on the collarbone, on the right side, far enough below the throat that the band, when returned, will not cover it.
He lifts his head. He puts the band back into its place. The new mark is now visible — a crescent of teeth at the collarbone ridge, **just above the pearl band**.
Her right hand has gone — slowly, on its own — to the back of his neck. Hair-tie under her fingers. She does not pull.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (almost whispered against her collarbone): The first was where the kingdom looks for a pulse. This one is where they will not be able to pretend.
ISOLDE: They will see it tomorrow.
VAEL: Yes.

S#3 — TERRACE / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
He steps back half a pace. He does not leave.
He turns his **scaled hand** up, palm up, between them. He waits.
She lifts her right hand. She sets her palm down into his scaled palm. The crescent on her wrist meets the crescent on his hand-back — through the cuff, through nothing else. The polished basalt rail catches the doubled image.
A line of **dark-red flame** runs along the seams of his fingers — same as the banner-burn, but contained. It does not climb to her cuff. He does not let it.
**A second silhouette** moves at the far end of the terrace — ELARA, in her ivory-and-gold, has stepped out. She stops at the threshold. She sees the two of them at the pool. She does not come closer. She watches the new mark above the throat band.

[DIALOGUE]
ELARA: (from the threshold) My lord. The hall is set.
VAEL: Tell the hall to wait.
ELARA: My lord —
VAEL: Wait.

S#4 — KEEP / CORRIDOR OUTSIDE GREAT HALL / NIGHT

[Visual]
ISOLDE walks the polished corridor toward the great hall. **Vael's Choice** is now slightly different on her: silver-and-pearl band sits where it sat — but the **collarbone bite is visible above it**. The ivory cuff is also higher on the right wrist. Both marks can be seen now.
VAEL walks beside her. Not touching. His scaled hand at his side.
The polished corridor floor doubles them — ivory beside black leather. The reflection of the collarbone mark catches torchlight under her chin.
At the end of the corridor, doors to the great hall. Behind them, voices — HALDREN raised, a new envoy's voice answering, ELARA's voice between them.

End Image: ISOLDE and VAEL walking the polished corridor side by side, the collarbone bite uncovered above the band, both crescents printing once at the same beat. The hall doors closed in front of them. The argument inside audible.

VAEL (low, slow, formal): They will look at what I have given them to look at. The rest stays under the cloth.
