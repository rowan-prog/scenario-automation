# THE OFFERING — EP07: Wing-Rush

S#1 — KEEP / WAR ROOM / DAY

[Visual]
A round basalt war-room table. A polished black slab on top — its surface reflects the ceiling and everyone leaning over it.
Maps of the human-kingdom road. Small carved tokens — kingdom riders, drake-house standards.
VAEL stands at the table. KIRAN beside him. HALDREN at the opposite side, hands flat on the slab — his reflection looking up at him from the black.
ISOLDE enters in **Vael's Choice**, collarbone bite uncovered above the band, wrist cuff drawn back. She does not stop at the threshold. She walks to VAEL's right at the table. Position taken, not asked.

[Camera]

[DIALOGUE]
HALDREN: My lord. The crown will send more.
VAEL (low, slow, formal): I am counting on it.
KIRAN: The drake-houses will hear within the day.
ISOLDE: Then they will hear it from us first.

[FX]

S#2 — WAR ROOM / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
VAEL drags a carved token across the polished slab — the human-kingdom rider token. He sets it under his scaled hand. His hand is reflected in the polished slab — and so is the token under it.
He turns to ISOLDE. He gestures to a low stool he has placed beside his chair at the table. Not a captain's stool. Not a clerk's stool. The **second seat** moved from the throne dais — basalt, narrow, his choice.
She does not sit yet. She lifts her right hand, palm up, between them — the cuff drawn back, the wrist bite visible.
He sets his scaled hand into hers, palm to palm, in front of HALDREN and KIRAN. The crescent on his hand-back and the crescent on her wrist meet — open, in daylight, in the war room.
HALDREN drops his eyes to the slab. KIRAN does not.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Sit beside me here. Not because I have called you. Because you have come.
ISOLDE: I will sit because I have come. I will leave because I choose.
VAEL (almost a vow): Then sit.

[FX]

S#3 — WAR ROOM / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
ISOLDE sits at his right at the war-table. The polished black slab now reflects two faces at the head of the table — his and hers.
VAEL's scaled hand rests on her thigh, on top of the black silk, just above the knee. Not under the skirt. Above the cloth. Possessive — public to the war-room — but cloth-bounded.
He bends to read the map with her. His hair touches her temple.
The distant rush sound again — louder this time. A wing-beat. The basalt walls take it as a low vibration.
HALDREN's writ-pages on the slab flutter — once.
KIRAN lifts his head toward the ceiling. He has felt it.

[Camera]

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (low, near her temple): The wing was four valleys west an hour ago.
ISOLDE: And now.
VAEL: Closer.
KIRAN: The first will land at the rampart by dusk.
HALDREN: (almost to himself) — they are coming —

[FX]
distant wing-rush, low and deep · writ-pages fluttering once · basalt walls humming low · scaled hand on silk · paired crescent steady


S#4 — KEEP / RAMPART / DUSK

[Visual]
ISOLDE and VAEL on the rampart. Same place as EP04 dawn. The polished basalt parapet doubles them again.
Above the keep — the first drake-house wing crosses the dusk sky. Not a full body shot — a **shadow wing across the parapet**, sweep of black scale catching last light.
The wing-shadow passes across ISOLDE — a moving dark across her ivory pearl trim, across the collarbone bite. The bite catches the wing-shadow and looks lit-from-under for a frame.
VAEL is behind her now, one half-step. He does not touch the collarbone. His scaled hand finds the **back of her right hand** at the rampart rail and stays there. The polished parapet doubles both hands stacked.
A second wing-shadow — passing the other way.
HALDREN, KIRAN, MAREN watch from the rampart stair. HALDREN's hand has come up to his throat once and dropped.

End Image: ISOLDE and VAEL at the rampart at dusk, two wing-shadows passing overhead, the collarbone bite caught in moving wing-light, his scaled hand over hers on the parapet rail, the polished basalt doubling them.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: They are coming for what is here.
VAEL (low, slow, formal): They are coming for what has already chosen to stay.
ISOLDE: Then let them land.
VAEL: Tomorrow. In the courtyard. With the envoys watching.
