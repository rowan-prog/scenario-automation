# THE OFFERING — EP03: TEETH ON THE COLLARBONE

S#1 — BLACK CASTLE / UPPER CORRIDOR / NIGHT

[Visual] Isolde walks back toward her chambers — escort gone, only a single attendant ahead carrying a candle. The black silk gown moves with her, the embroidery catching pulses of corridor torchlight. In the shadows behind a stone column, a dark FIGURE — a human-kingdom ASSASSIN, hooded, slender, a thin curved blade held flat against the wall. The attendant turns the corner, candle gone. Isolde alone in the half-dark. The assassin steps from the column. Her dark-brown hair stirs at the back of her neck — she does not see him yet.

[Camera] WIDE — the long corridor, attendant ahead, Isolde trailing → TRACKING with Isolde, gown moving over stone → CLOSE on the embroidery catching torchlight → WHIP PAN — column, hooded figure pressed to stone, blade flat against the wall → CLOSE on Isolde's nape, hair lifting at the chill → REVERSE — the figure stepping out, blade rising.

[DIALOGUE]
ATTENDANT (offscreen, fading): I'll bring the basin to your chamber, my lady.
ISOLDE: Thank you.
ASSASSIN (low, almost whisper): The kingdom never forgets its promises.

[FX] Boot heels far ahead — receding. The drag of fabric on stone behind her. The faint dry whisper of a blade leaving its sheath.

S#2 — BLACK CASTLE / ISOLDE'S CHAMBER / NIGHT — CONTINUOUS

[Visual] Isolde turns into her chamber doorway, sees the assassin a half-pace behind — pivots. The thin blade slashes — catches only the silver embroidery at the bodice. She steps back into the chamber, knocking over a low brass stand. The assassin advances. He raises the blade for the kill. From behind him in the doorway — Vael steps into the chamber, scaled hand already moving. He catches the blade mid-strike, scaled fingers closing around the steel. The blade BENDS in his fist. Then it SNAPS. The assassin pulled off-balance, thrown across the room into the stone wall. Slides to the floor — unconscious, alive. Vael stands between Isolde and the body. Scaled fingers stained red where the broken steel cut him.

[Camera] WIDE — chamber doorway, Isolde turning, the blade arc → CLOSE — the silver embroidery slashed across the bodice → MEDIUM — Isolde stepping back, the brass stand falling → REVERSE WIDE — Vael in the doorway, scaled hand reaching → MACRO — scaled fingers closing on the blade → MACRO — the blade bending, then snapping → TRACKING — assassin flung into the wall → CLOSE — Vael's bleeding scaled hand, knuckles white around the broken hilt.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Don't look away from me.
ISOLDE: I'm not.
VAEL (to the body, low, final): The kingdom sent you.

[FX] Brass stand clattering. The screech of metal as the blade bends. The body hitting the stone wall. Vael's slow, controlled breath.

S#3 — BLACK CASTLE / ISOLDE'S CHAMBER / WALL BESIDE THE BED / CONTINUOUS

[Visual] Vael drops the broken hilt. Closes the distance in three strides. His scaled hand closes around a thick handful of Isolde's dark-brown hair at the back of her skull — not pulling — and draws her toward him. The other scaled hand finds her waist and turns her — sets her back against the stone wall beside the bed. His scaled palm slides up her side, across her ribs, to the side of her neck — thumb under her jaw, lifting her chin until she has to look at him. His other hand slides the silver-and-pearl SHOULDER STRAP of the black silk gown down — off her shoulder — exposing the line of her collarbone and the porcelain skin beneath. His scaled fingers move there — palm flat on her collarbone, fingertip pressing into the hollow at the base of her throat where her pulse beats. He stays there a beat. Then he lowers his head and his mouth comes down on hers — hard, claiming, the heat of his breath and the press of his lower lip against hers. He breaks the kiss only to drag his mouth down along her jaw — and his teeth close on the side of her neck above the collarbone. He holds. When he draws back, a red-pink BITE-MARK is left on her skin. Then he lifts her wrist — the one with the faint inner mark — and presses his mouth to the gold flicker beneath her skin. His scaled tongue traces the mark once. The gold pulses brighter under his lips.

[Camera] TRACKING — Vael crossing the room, scaled hand reaching for her hair → CLOSE — his scaled fingers closing in the back of her dark-brown hair → MEDIUM — her back set against the stone wall beside the bed → CLOSE — his scaled hand sliding up her side, thumb under her jaw → MACRO — the silver-and-pearl strap sliding off her shoulder, the collarbone bared → CLOSE — his scaled palm flat on her collarbone, fingertip pressing into the hollow at her throat → TIGHT TWO-SHOT — his mouth coming down on hers → CLOSE — his mouth breaking from hers, dragging along her jaw → MACRO — teeth closing on the side of her neck above the collarbone, holding → CLOSE — the red-pink mark left on her skin as he draws back → MACRO — her wrist lifted, his mouth pressing on the gold flicker, the mark pulsing brighter.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Tell me you understand.
ISOLDE (barely): I understand.
VAEL: Say it again, when I let you go.

[FX] His controlled breath against her skin. Silk friction against stone. The slow thrum of his scales — louder, doubled with her pulse. The catch of breath when his teeth close.

S#4 — BLACK CASTLE / ISOLDE'S CHAMBER / WALL BESIDE THE BED / CONTINUOUS

[Visual] Vael holds his mouth against her wrist a beat longer. Then his scales darken, blood-red threading deeper through obsidian — and he forces himself a half-step back. His scaled hand falls from her chin. The other still holds her wrist for a count. Then it lets go. He does not lift the shoulder strap back into place. He looks at the bite-mark on her neck — at the gold-pulsing wrist — at the slipped strap on her shoulder — and his jaw works. He turns away from her toward the unconscious assassin on the floor. Isolde stays against the stone wall, the strap still off her shoulder, the mark on her neck unhidden, her wrist glowing faintly. Her hand rises — touches the bite on her neck once. Across the chamber, Vael looks back at her over his shoulder, scaled hand opening and closing once at his side.

[Camera] CLOSE — Vael's scales darkening, dark blood-red surfacing → MEDIUM — his hand falling from her chin → CLOSE — his scaled fingers letting her wrist go, slow → WIDE — Vael stepping back from her, scaled hand opening and closing at his side → REVERSE CLOSE — Isolde against the wall, the slipped strap, the bite-mark, the gold-pulsing wrist all in one frame → CLOSE — her fingertips touching the mark on her neck → REVERSE — Vael at the doorway, looking back over his shoulder, scaled hand clenched.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Have the body sent to Haldren. Let the kingdom see what was sent.
ISOLDE: …Why did you stop.
VAEL: Because I haven't yet decided what you are to me.

[FX] The slow easing of his breath. The soft drop of her hand from her neck. The low thrum of his scales fading back toward steady. A single far wing-beat from above the castle ridge.
