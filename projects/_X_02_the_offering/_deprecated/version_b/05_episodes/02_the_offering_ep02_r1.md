# THE OFFERING — EP02: THE SEAT BESIDE HIM

S#1 — BLACK CASTLE / VAEL'S WAR ROOM / NIGHT

[Visual] Vael stands over a long obsidian table, palms flat on the stone. His scaled hand catches the brazier light. KIRAN at attention by the door. A folded square of black silk in Kiran's gloved hand. Through the high doorway, far down the corridor, Isolde walks under guard escort toward her chambers — only the trailing silver embroidery of her royal gown visible. Vael's grey eyes follow her without moving.

[Camera] WIDE — war room, brazier glow, Vael at the table → TILT DOWN — his scaled hand spread flat on obsidian → MEDIUM — Kiran with the folded silk, head bent → CUT THROUGH the doorway — Isolde's silver trailing through the dim corridor → CLOSE — Vael's grey eyes, no expression, no blink.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Burn the gown they sent her in.
KIRAN: My lord — the ivory was the kingdom's mark.
VAEL: That's why. Have her dressed in this. Then bring her to the hall.

[FX] Brazier low crackle. Boot heels on stone — receding. The faint hum of Vael's scales — slow, steady.

---

S#2 — BLACK CASTLE / ISOLDE'S CHAMBER / NIGHT — CONTINUOUS

[Visual] Two attending women hold the ivory royal gown taut between them — and pull, tearing the silver embroidery at the seam. The gown falls in a pile on the obsidian floor. The white fur mantle is folded away. A new gown is laid across the bed — black silk, finely tailored, with silver and pale pearl embroidery threaded along the bodice and down the sleeves, an oblique neckline that bares the collarbone, the inside of the wrists left clean of any sleeve. Isolde stands in shift and bare feet. She does not protest. She lifts the dark silk herself.

[Camera] WIDE — chamber, the torn ivory gown on the floor, attendants stepping back → TILT DOWN to the discarded mantle → MEDIUM — the black silk laid across the bed, embroidery catching candlelight → CLOSE on Isolde's bare wrists, the faint inner-wrist mark from the gate still there → MEDIUM — Isolde lifting the dark gown over her arms.

[DIALOGUE]
ATTENDANT 1: The kingdom's mark goes into the fire, my lady.
ISOLDE: Then so does the name.
ATTENDANT 2 (soft): The lord said — only this.

[FX] Silk falling. The cracking of embroidery thread being torn. A distant set of bells — the great hall calling.

---

S#3 — BLACK CASTLE / OUTER GREAT HALL CORRIDOR / NIGHT

[Visual] Isolde walks the long corridor toward the great hall in **the new gown — black silk over the floor, silver-and-pearl embroidery along the bodice and sleeves, a thin silver chain looped at the waist, oblique neckline baring the line of her collarbone, the porcelain inside of her wrists visible, dark-brown hair brushed long and loose down her back.** The torn ivory she arrived in is gone. The faint inner-wrist mark catches torchlight as she passes. Haldren walks two paces behind, expression frozen. The double doors of the great hall stand open. Inside — the court already waiting.

[Camera] TRACKING — long corridor, Isolde moving toward camera, the dark gown trailing → CLOSE — the embroidery along her bodice catching the wall-torch light → LOW ANGLE on her bare wrists — the inner-wrist mark visible → MEDIUM — Haldren two paces behind, jaw set → WIDE — the open doors of the hall ahead, court figures already turning.

[DIALOGUE]
HALDREN (low, not breaking pace): The kingdom expected you in their colors.
ISOLDE: The kingdom is no longer my colors.
HALDREN: …

[FX] Soft court murmur ahead, growing as the doors widen. Her silk gown sliding over stone. A single distant wing-beat — high above the castle.

---

S#4 — BLACK CASTLE / GREAT HALL / CONTINUOUS

[Visual] The court turns as Isolde enters. At the far end of the hall stands a great black throne. To its right — a high carved chair on the same dais. Beside that chair, ELARA (golden hair, pale gold gown, cold beauty) is already waiting, hand on the chair's armrest, claiming the seat. Other bride candidates ranged below in cooler attendance. Vael stands beside his throne — not yet seated. His scaled hand rests on the high armrest of the chair to his right. As Isolde reaches the foot of the dais, his black-scaled fingers slowly tap the chair armrest — once, twice, three times. Elara's hand falls from the chair.

[Camera] WIDE — the hall, the throne lit, Elara at the high chair, Vael standing beside it → REVERSE WIDE — Isolde entering, the court turning as one → CLOSE — Vael's scaled fingers tapping the armrest, three slow beats → CLOSE — Elara's hand slipping off the wood → MEDIUM — Isolde walking down the center aisle, eyes on the chair.

[DIALOGUE]
ELARA (forced calm): My lord. The seat was prepared for me.
VAEL: It was prepared for whoever I name.
COURTIER (whisper): …the offering walks in his colors.

[FX] Court murmur freezing into silence. Her footfalls on stone. The slow rhythmic tap of his scaled fingertips on the wood.

---

S#5 — BLACK CASTLE / GREAT HALL / CONTINUOUS

[Visual] Isolde reaches the dais. Vael's scaled hand leaves the armrest, drops to her waist — closes there — and lifts her up onto the dais beside him in one movement. He turns her gently and sets her into the high chair. The court watches. Then his scaled fingers move up — slide through a single thick strand of her dark-brown hair, draw it slowly across his palm, and let it fall against her collarbone. His hand returns to the armrest beside hers. The inner-wrist mark on her arm — pale gold under the torchlight — pulses once, brighter than before, just where her wrist rests on the wood beside his hand. Elara's face cracks. Haldren stops at the edge of the hall, hands folded — silent.

[Camera] WIDE — Vael lifting Isolde onto the dais → CLOSE — his scaled hand on her waist → MEDIUM — Isolde seated, his hand still on her → CLOSE — his scaled fingers drawing through her hair → MACRO on her inner wrist — the gold pulse, brighter → REVERSE — Elara's face cracking, fast snap → CLOSE on Vael's hand finally settling beside Isolde's on the armrest.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (to the court, low, final): She sits at my right.
ISOLDE (eyes on him, quiet): For tonight.
VAEL: For longer.

[FX] The court silence holding. The slow tap of his scaled fingers resuming — beside her wrist now, on the armrest. The distant wing-beat answering, far above the castle.
