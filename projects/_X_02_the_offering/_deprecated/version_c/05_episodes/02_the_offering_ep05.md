# THE OFFERING — EP05: THE TERRACE

S#1 — DRAGONKEEP / OUTER CORRIDOR / NIGHT

[Visual]
ISOLDE walks the corridor with a single attendant a step behind. ELARA waits in the cross-corridor — gold-and-white candidate gown, hair pinned high. ELARA steps directly into ISOLDE's path. Two other candidate women hover behind her. ISOLDE stops. The candidate women look at the bite mark at her throat, then at the second curve of dark-red shadow on her inner wrist. ELARA's smile sets.

[Camera]
TRACKING ISOLDE down the corridor → CLOSE on the attendant behind her → WHIP PAN to ELARA stepping into the path → CLOSE on the two candidate women clocking the throat mark → CLOSE on them clocking the wrist mark → ECU on ELARA's smile setting.

[DIALOGUE]
ELARA: How is the gown.
ISOLDE: Warm.
ELARA: He gave the same gown to the last one.
ISOLDE: Did he seat her.
ELARA: He did not.
ISOLDE: Then it is not the same gown.

[FX]
Heels stopping on the polished stone. Silk rustling. One candidate's sharp inhale. A distant fortress bell, single low note.

S#2 — DRAGONKEEP / NORTH TERRACE / NIGHT

[Visual]
A high open terrace at the edge of the keep — the wasteland below, the black ridge ahead. ISOLDE stands at the stone rail. Her **black silk and pearl gown** with the silver-thread piping. Her hand on the rail, the wrist scale-light pulsing faintly under the moon. Behind her the door to the terrace opens without sound. VAEL steps out, scaled hand on the door, dark cloak falling behind him.

[Camera]
WIDE of the terrace from below, the keep tall behind her → TRACKING up to ISOLDE at the rail → CLOSE on her hand on the stone, wrist scale-light visible → SLOW PAN to the door behind her opening → CLOSE on VAEL stepping out → ECU on his scaled hand still on the door.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Are you a habit now.
VAEL: You are.
ISOLDE: Of what.
VAEL: Of the wall I keep meeting.

[FX]
Wind across the terrace. Cloak shifting. Door closing without latching. A single low rumble far on the ridge.

---

S#3 — DRAGONKEEP / NORTH TERRACE / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
VAEL crosses the terrace. His scaled hand closes around her inner wrist and pulls her around to face him, then pulls her hard against his chest. The wrist scale-light flares between their bodies where his hand grips her. His other scaled hand lifts under her chin and tilts her face up. Their mouths are a breath apart. He does not close that distance. He bends and presses his mouth instead just below her ear, then along the line of her jaw, then to the side of her throat opposite the first bite, then to the pulse-point at the base of her throat. At the second-to-last contact he sets his teeth — a clean second bite mark blooms on the other side of her throat, mirroring the first. He lifts her inner wrist between them again and lays his mouth flat against the scale-light. The light brightens under his lips. He lifts his face. He does not kiss her mouth.

[Camera]
TRACKING VAEL crossing the terrace → CLOSE on his scaled hand closing on her inner wrist → WHIP PAN as he turns her → CLOSE on the wrist scale-light flaring between them → ECU on his scaled hand lifting her chin → CLOSE on their mouths a breath apart, held → TRACKING his mouth bending below her ear → CLOSE on his mouth along her jaw → ECU on his teeth setting at the side of her throat — second bite mark blooming → ECU on his mouth flat against her wrist scale-light, the light brightening → CLOSE on her face, eyes still closed → CLOSE on his face lifting, his mouth not on hers.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Tell me to stop.
ISOLDE: I won't.
VAEL: Then I will.

[FX]
Wind dropping low. Her one shuddered inhale. His low exhale at her throat. Pearl thread catching once on his scaled cuff. The scale-light humming faintly.

---

S#4 — DRAGONKEEP / NORTH TERRACE / NIGHT (END)

[Visual]
VAEL releases her wrist. He steps back into the shadow at the doorway. The terrace door does not open behind him — he simply does not step back through it. He turns his head sideways, scaled hand pressed against the doorway, knuckles dark with the deepening plate. ISOLDE stays at the rail, both new bite marks at her throat, the wrist scale-light still glowing. She does not call after him. The pendant inside her bodice flickers once. VAEL does not turn. He walks back into the keep.

[Camera]
WIDE of the terrace, the distance between them → CLOSE on his scaled hand at the doorway → CLOSE on her wrist scale-light still glowing → INSERT on the pendant flickering once at her chest → TRACKING VAEL turning, walking back inside → CLOSE on ISOLDE's face — Hard Cut.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Don't follow.
ISOLDE: I wasn't going to.
VAEL: I know.

[FX]
Boot turning slow. Cloak dragging at the threshold. Wind picking back up. The pendant's single soft note. Her steady exhale.

Hard Cut.
