# THE OFFERING — EP02: VAEL'S CHOICE

S#1 — DRAGONKEEP / VAEL'S ANTECHAMBER / MORNING

[Visual]
VAEL stands at a tall window — face still mostly turned away, only the cut of his jaw and the dark of his hair visible. A row of maids hold three gowns over their arms — all funereal grey, the sort the keep used to dress past offerings before the execution rites. His scaled hand reaches out and pinches the hem of the closest gown between two clawed fingers. He releases it.

[Camera]
WIDE of the antechamber, maids in a line, VAEL at the window → TRACKING toward the gowns → ECU on the scaled fingers pinching the grey hem → CLOSE on the hem releasing, falling → CLOSE on VAEL's profile, still turned to the window.

[DIALOGUE]
KIRAN: The arrival gowns, my lord. Standard issue.
VAEL: Burn them.
KIRAN: All three?
VAEL: All three. Bring the black. With the silver. And the pearl.
KIRAN: That's a bride's gown, my lord.
VAEL: I know what it is.

[FX]
Cloth slipping back over the maid's arm. KIRAN's gauntlet at his side. Fire crackling in a brazier across the room. A long exhale from VAEL.

S#2 — DRAGONKEEP / ISOLDE'S CHAMBER / DAY

[Visual]
ISOLDE stands in the centre of the room. Maids strip the arrival cloak from her shoulders, the ivory silk gown next. The signet pendant — still hidden in the inner lining of the cloak — is missed by the maids; ISOLDE's hand catches it as the cloak comes away and she palms it into the bodice of her shift. A new gown is laid out on the bed — **black silk base, white-pearl seed embroidery along the bodice and sleeves, silver-thread piping at the neckline, a fitted high collar with a single dropped pearl**. The maids' faces are stunned. ISOLDE lifts her chin.

[Camera]
WIDE of the chamber, maids around her → TRACKING with the cloak coming off → INSERT on ISOLDE's hand palming the pendant into her shift → PUSH IN on the new gown on the bed → ECU on the pearl drop at the collar → CLOSE on ISOLDE's face, recognising what the gown is.

[DIALOGUE]
MAID 1: His order, my lady.
MAID 2: For tonight.
ISOLDE: Tonight is what?
MAID 1: A hall. With the court.
ISOLDE: And this is what they wear in his hall?
MAID 1: No, my lady. Only you.

[FX]
Silk sliding against skin. Pearl button rattling. Brushes through her hair. A heavy door closing somewhere down the corridor.

S#3 — DRAGONKEEP / GREAT HALL / EVENING — REVEAL

[Visual]
The doors of the Great Hall part. ISOLDE enters in the **black silk gown with white-pearl seed embroidery and silver-thread piping** — the lone bride-cut figure in a hall full of dark courtiers. ELARA — gold-blonde, in candidate-white-and-gold — sits at the high seat at VAEL's left, smug. HALDREN observes from the side, a half-step out of the human kingdom's row. Every face in the hall turns to ISOLDE. The musicians falter for a beat. ELARA's hand tightens on the carved arm of her chair.

[Camera]
WIDE of the hall doors opening from ISOLDE's POV → TRACKING with her walking the centre aisle → CLOSE on ELARA's smug face → PAN across the courtiers' heads turning → CLOSE on HALDREN watching, his mouth tightening → ECU on ELARA's hand whitening on the chair arm.

[DIALOGUE]
COURTIER: Is that…
COURTIER: That's a bride's gown.
ELARA: It is not.
HALDREN: She does not belong in that gown.
ISOLDE (to HALDREN, passing): Tell him.

[FX]
Doors creaking open. A lute string snapping. Whispers rising. ISOLDE's heel on the polished stone, single tap.

S#4 — DRAGONKEEP / GREAT HALL / THRONE DAIS / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
VAEL stands at the dais, half in shadow — his back to the hall, scaled hand resting on the carved arm of a second chair set beside his own. As ISOLDE approaches, his scaled fingers tap the wood of the second chair — once, twice, three times — slow. Every courtier hears it. ELARA's face freezes. VAEL turns. He extends his scaled hand. He does not speak. ISOLDE places her hand in his. He guides her up onto the dais and seats her in the second chair — his chair's twin — at his right. As she sits, his other hand closes briefly on a single lock of her hair at her shoulder, slides down its length, releases. The hall is silent.

[Camera]
LOW ANGLE on the dais, VAEL's back, the second chair beside him → INSERT on his scaled fingers tapping the chair arm — tap, tap, tap → CLOSE on ELARA's face freezing → CLOSE on VAEL's hand extending toward ISOLDE → TRACKING with ISOLDE up onto the dais → ECU on her sitting in the chair → ECU on his hand catching a lock of her hair at the shoulder, drawing down its length → CLOSE on her face, eyes lifting to his — Hard Cut as his face turns toward the hall, the torchlight catching half of it for the first time.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Sit.
ELARA: My lord — that seat is —
VAEL: Mine to give.
HALDREN: The kingdom did not send her for this.
VAEL: Then the kingdom should not have sent her at all.

[FX]
Three slow taps on the chair arm — each louder than ambient. A held breath across the hall. Cloak settling. Hair sliding through his scaled fingers. A single deep rumble from somewhere far above the keep.

Hard Cut.
