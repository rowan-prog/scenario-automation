## 3. WORLD RULES (MINIMAL)

Viewer needs to know only these:

1. **Magic exists.** It is *practical*, not mystical-poetic.
2. **Dragons.** Live in their own keep east of the human kingdoms. They can take human form. They can magically disguise that human form further (face / hair / height) — this is a known skill, not a secret.
3. **Bridal mirror.** Human magical bridal test. Hand on it. If untouched, mirror stays silver. If not, mirror turns black. Whole court sees. The test is real magic. There is no faking it.
4. **Mate bond.** Dragon biology. Once a dragon recognizes their mate, body reacts: scales rise involuntarily near them, body heat spikes, pupils slit, scent imprint. *Audience-visible cues* on Vael whenever Isolde is in danger or near another man.
5. **Public mate = target.** Old law: enemies kill a dragon lord's known mate to break the dragon. Vael's mother was killed this way. Vael hides his mate identity (public = "bride" only) until EP10-12 forced reveal.

That's it. No factions, no councils, no prophecies, no chosen one nonsense.

## 4. CHARACTERS (FIVE NAMES + ONE FLASHBACK)

### ISOLDE (23, bastard princess)
- Mother was a serving woman the king favored briefly. Mother died (likely killed by the queen / stepmother) when Isolde was eight.
- Raised inside the palace but treated as half-servant. Wears reused dresses. Eats at the corner of the table.
- Survival voice: "Keep your mouth shut. Remember everything. Pay it back later."
- The masquerade night was the first thing she ever did for herself. She does not regret it.
- After the bridal mirror fails: shipped to the dragon as tribute. Walks in.
- **Voice profile:** Direct, dry, fast comebacks. Never begs. Inner voice (audience-only) shows the strategist underneath.
- **Body knows:** Every time Vael touches her she gets a flash of the masked stranger. She thinks she's losing her mind. She isn't.

### VAEL DRAKONIS (early 30s, dragon lord)
- Inherited the keep at 19 after his mother was killed.
- Has not taken a public lover since. Court assumes he's cold.
- Six months ago, in disguise at a magical festival, met a masked stranger. The first woman who ever looked at *him* and not at the title.
- Woke alone. Spent six months hunting her — quietly. Never publicly. (Public hunt = "this woman matters to me" = she becomes a target.)
- She walked into his keep last week. As tribute. As a discarded whore from a human court.
- **Voice profile:** Short. Low. Command. Never explains. Never raises voice.
- **The contradiction (the show's engine):**
  - In public: claims her instantly as **bride**. Brutal authority. Anyone who insults her gets broken.
  - In private: rough, possessive, struggles to stop. Has not slept since she arrived.
  - In secret (audience-only): refuses to call her **mate** out loud. Refuses to claim her body fully. Will not father a child publicly until he can keep her alive.
- Already too late on the last point. She's already pregnant from the masquerade night.

### SERA (red dragon, late 20s)
- Self-appointed Vael's future mate. Twelve years of waiting. Never officially engaged but tells everyone she is.
- Beautiful, cruel mouth, no real power.
- Leads the slut-shaming charge against Isolde.
- *Audience function:* she's the mirror that says out loud what other dragons whisper. She makes the slut-shaming visible so Isolde can rebut it.
- Not a strategist. Not a plotter. Just a jealous woman with a sharp tongue and dragon teeth.

### ADELINE (Isolde's legitimate half-sister, ~20)
- Stepmother's daughter. Trained to step on Isolde from birth.
- Leads the slut-shaming charge in the human court (EP01).
- *Audience function:* she's why viewers hate the human kingdom. Performative cruelty.
- Appears EP01, returns EP30+ when Isolde is dragged back to human court for politics.

### KING ALDRIC (Isolde's father, Veine kingdom)
- Weak. Henpecked by stepmother.
- Sold Isolde to the dragon to remove her as a political embarrassment.
- Will try to reclaim her EP25+ once it becomes politically useful (when news of her status reaches him).

### VAEL'S MOTHER (flashback only, ~3 seconds, EP04)
- The previous dragon lord's mate. Publicly declared.
- Killed by enemies in a public hall when Vael was a child.
- Single flashback cut: young Vael, mother on the floor, blood, his face.
- Establishes Vael's "never publicly mate" rule. One cut. That's it.

### KIRAN (Vael's lieutenant)
- Loyal. Knows about the masquerade hunt. Knew before Isolde arrived. Did not tell her.
- *Audience function:* the only other character who knows what Vael knows. His silence in front of Isolde is itself a tension cue.
