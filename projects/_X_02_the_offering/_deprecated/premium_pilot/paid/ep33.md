# THE OFFERING — EP33: Read It Aloud

S#1 — VELDEN CITY / MAIN SQUARE / MORNING

[Visual]
The square is full. The kingdom army, the brother's army, the keep garrison, the city's people. ISOLDE on a raised platform. ALDRIC in chains beside her. HALDREN holds the parchment.

HALDREN: Read by my queen's order.

He reads the confession aloud. The full text. Mother's murder. Crown stolen. Carriers burned. Assassins. Dark magic alliance. Each line lands.

By the end, the crowd is silent. Then one voice. Then ten. Then hundreds.

VOICES: Queen.

ISOLDE raises her hand. The crowd stills.

ISOLDE: My uncle has confessed. He will be held in the palace tower. He will not die. He will not rule. He will witness.

She looks at ALDRIC.

ISOLDE: That is your sentence.

S#2 — VELDEN CITY / OLD PALACE / TOWER / AFTERNOON

[Visual]
ISOLDE places ALDRIC in the palace's highest tower — a single chamber with a window. The room is bare except for a bed, a table, a single chair. ALDRIC is no longer in chains. The room is locked from outside only.

ISOLDE: You will live in here. You will eat. You will sleep. You will see the line tree grow through that window.
ALDRIC: A prison.
ISOLDE: A witness chamber.
ALDRIC: It is the same.
ISOLDE: It is not. A prison breaks you. This makes you watch.

She turns to leave.

ALDRIC: Niece.
ISOLDE: Aldric.
ALDRIC: You will not survive the price.
ISOLDE: I will.
ALDRIC: You are eight years younger than your mother was.
ISOLDE: She was poisoned by you. Not the price.
ALDRIC: Yes.
ISOLDE: Goodbye, uncle.

She leaves. The door closes. From inside, a key turns.

S#3 — VELDEN CITY / OLD PALACE / WAR ROOM / EVENING

[Visual]
War council. ISOLDE, VAEL, BROTHER, KIRAN, HALDREN, COURT-MAGUS, ELARA.

ISOLDE: Where would the dark one go.
COURT-MAGUS: North. There is an old temple — abandoned three hundred years. Before the line was named.
HALDREN: A counter-line temple.
COURT-MAGUS: Yes.
ISOLDE: How long to reach.
KIRAN: Two days by drake.
ISOLDE: Two by drake. He went by portal.
COURT-MAGUS: Then he is there now. He needs days to begin.
ISOLDE: We move tomorrow.
COURT-MAGUS: My queen — you have the stain. You should rest.
ISOLDE: I rest tonight. We move tomorrow at dawn.

S#4 — VELDEN CITY / OLD PALACE / QUEEN'S CHAMBER / NIGHT

[Visual]
A bedchamber, larger than Vael's wing. Velden's queen's chamber. ISOLDE has not slept here before. VAEL is with her. The room is unfamiliar — old wallpaper, old furniture from her mother's time.

ISOLDE walks to the bed. She sits on it. She lays her hand on the coverlet.

ISOLDE: She slept here.
VAEL: Yes.
ISOLDE: I do not remember her well.
VAEL: She was your mother.
ISOLDE: She was eight years older than I am now when he killed her.
VAEL: Yes.
ISOLDE: I will live longer.
VAEL: You will.

He crosses. He sits on the bed beside her. The bracelets glow. The stain is still there but the bracelets are pressing it back, slow.

ISOLDE: I am tired tonight.
VAEL: I will hold you.

They lie down together — fully clothed. He pulls her against his chest. She closes her eyes.
