# THE OFFERING — EP41: Velden Mourns

S#1 — VELDEN CITY / OLD PALACE / THRONE ROOM / DAY

[Visual]
The Velden court — full attendance for first time since the change of crown. ALDRIC's body lies on a dais — not on the throne. Wrapped in dark cloth. ISOLDE sits on the actual throne for the first time since arriving. VAEL beside her on a smaller seat — newly placed.

Court Herald: The kingdom mourns its former king.
ISOLDE: He killed his queen-sister. He betrayed the line. He will be remembered for what he was.
COURT HERALD: For what he was.
ISOLDE: All of it. The good parts. The bad parts. Velden does not erase history.

S#2 — VELDEN CITY / OLD PALACE / GARDEN / EVENING

[Visual]
A small garden behind the palace. The body has been buried. ISOLDE and VAEL alone. KIRAN and BROTHER at the gate, watching but not entering.

ISOLDE: He was my uncle.
VAEL: He was.
ISOLDE: I do not feel — what I thought I would feel.
VAEL: What.
ISOLDE: Grief.
VAEL: He killed your mother.
ISOLDE: Yes. But — he was the only family I knew growing up. After my mother died.
VAEL: He raised you.
ISOLDE: Distantly. From a distance, as a child should be raised. He had me brought up in court. I had tutors. He sent me to the keep at sixteen because I asked too many questions about my mother.
VAEL: You did not know.
ISOLDE: I knew. I did not let myself know.

She looks at his grave.

ISOLDE: Goodbye, uncle.

S#3 — VAEL'S KEEP / LINE TREE COURTYARD / NIGHT

[Visual]
ISOLDE has returned to the keep. The line tree is taller still. The carriers gathered — all twenty-five, plus drakes, plus VAEL. They watch the tree.

A new branch — full of gold leaves — has grown on the tree. ISOLDE walks to it. She places her hand on the bark beside the new branch.

GROVE KEEPER: It is a memorial.
ISOLDE: For Aldric.
GROVE KEEPER: For the line returning what he stole.
ISOLDE: He is part of the line again.
GROVE KEEPER: He is now.
ISOLDE: That is mercy.
GROVE KEEPER: That is the line. The line does not hate. The line returns.

S#4 — VAEL'S KEEP / VAEL'S WING / NIGHT

[Visual]
ISOLDE and VAEL in bed. Both clothed in nightwear. ISOLDE traces the dark patch on VAEL's scaled hand with her finger. It is smaller than before.

ISOLDE: Yours is fading.
VAEL: Mine is fading.
ISOLDE: Mine is too.
VAEL: When the dark robe is gone — they may fade entirely.
ISOLDE: Or they may stay.
VAEL: We do not know.

She lays her head on his chest.

ISOLDE: I have not asked you something important.
VAEL: Ask.
ISOLDE: You have lived two hundred years. You have not had a mate.
VAEL: No.
ISOLDE: Did you want one.
VAEL: I wanted whichever the line sent.
ISOLDE: That is not the same as wanting.
VAEL: I am older now than you will ever be. I have learned to wait for what the line decides. When you arrived — I knew the line decided.
ISOLDE: Before the marks.
VAEL: Before the marks.

A pause.

ISOLDE: Vael.
VAEL: Yes.
ISOLDE: When I die.
VAEL: When.
ISOLDE: Not now. Eventually.
VAEL: Yes.
ISOLDE: Live afterward.
VAEL: I will not.
ISOLDE: Vael.
VAEL: I have decided. The bond would not let me live alone afterward anyway. But I would not even if it would.
ISOLDE: That is sad.
VAEL: It is true.

She closes her eyes against his chest.
