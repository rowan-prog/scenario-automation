# THE OFFERING — EP19: He Comes Alone

S#1 — VAEL'S KEEP / OUTER GATE / MIDDAY (THREE DAYS LATER)

[Visual]
A single rider on the road. No escort. The brother. He wears the bone-clasp leather of his lieutenants — no banner. He carries a small package wrapped in dark cloth. His face is unlined.

VAEL waits at the keep gate. The twelve drakes are positioned along the ramparts — visible. KIRAN at VAEL's side. HALDREN at the back.

The brother dismounts at the threshold.

BROTHER: I am here.
VAEL: You are.
BROTHER: I want to see her.
VAEL: She wants to see you.
BROTHER: Then bring her.
VAEL: She is at the high seat. You come to her.

The brother smiles — small, controlled.

BROTHER: As you wish.

S#2 — VAEL'S KEEP / ONYX HALL / DAIS / SHORT TIME LATER

[Visual]
ISOLDE on the high seat. Dressed formally — dark gown, hair loose. The five marks all visible (sleeve raised, collar arranged). The pendant on a chain around her neck — first time worn since EP1. The dark stone in her right hand.

The brother enters. He stops at the foot of the dais. He does not bow.

BROTHER: You wear it.
ISOLDE: I do.
BROTHER: It does not become you.
ISOLDE: It becomes blood.
BROTHER: That is true.

He looks at her marks. He notes each one.

BROTHER: He has marked you.
ISOLDE: I have marked him.
BROTHER: How.
ISOLDE: (raises her left wrist — the three crescents + flame-curve burning) — Look at his hand.

VAEL — beside the high seat — raises his scaled hand. Three gold threads under the scales, paired with her three crescents. The brother sees. He nods once.

BROTHER: A true mate.
VAEL: Yes.
BROTHER: That changes nothing.
ISOLDE: It changes everything.

S#3 — VAEL'S KEEP / ONYX HALL / DAIS / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
The brother lifts his package. He unwraps it. Inside: a small carved object. It is the mirror of ISOLDE's mother's pendant — the same sigil, but inverted. He sets it on the foot of the dais.

BROTHER: This was your mother's twin. It was the queen's marker. Whoever holds both is the queen.
ISOLDE: You held it.
BROTHER: I held it for you.
ISOLDE: And now.
BROTHER: I give it to you. With one condition.

He looks at VAEL.

BROTHER: Send my niece with me. Through the line, she will rule Velden. Her mate stays here. Once a year, she will visit. She will rule the line. He will rule his keep.
VAEL: No.
BROTHER: I was speaking to her.
ISOLDE: He spoke for me.
BROTHER: Then you refuse.
ISOLDE: I refuse the splitting. I take both. He is here. Velden is mine. Both at once.
BROTHER: That is war.
ISOLDE: That was already war when you brought three hundred mounted.
BROTHER: I brought them as honor guard for my queen.
ISOLDE: You brought them because you thought I would say yes.
BROTHER: Yes.
ISOLDE: I do not.

The brother picks up the twin pendant. He weighs it in his hand. Then he sets it down again — at the foot of the dais.

BROTHER: I leave it here. Whether you take it is yours. The line will know.

He turns. He walks out.

ISOLDE: Take your army with you.
BROTHER (without turning): I will leave it at the river. It is not for harm. It is for testimony. When you come for the line in Velden, they will follow you.

He exits.

S#4 — VAEL'S KEEP / ONYX HALL / DAIS / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
The brother is gone. The twin pendant sits at the foot of the dais. ISOLDE rises from the high seat. She walks down. She picks up the twin. She holds it in her free hand — her own pendant on her neck, the twin in her hand, the stone in her right hand.

The three together — they pulse. Once. All three in rhythm.

ISOLDE: It is mine.

VAEL stands at the foot of the dais. He does not move.

VAEL: He is letting you take Velden.
ISOLDE: He is. For now.
VAEL: He will turn.
ISOLDE: When.
VAEL: When my brother — my king — sees that we are joined and refuses to release the crown.
ISOLDE: How long.
VAEL: Weeks.

She lifts her face to him.

ISOLDE: Tonight.
VAEL: Tonight.

She closes her hand on the twin pendant. She walks past him. He follows.
