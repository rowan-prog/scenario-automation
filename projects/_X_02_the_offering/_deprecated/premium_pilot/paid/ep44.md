# THE OFFERING — EP44: The Dark Robe Falls

S#1 — MOUNTAIN SHRINE / DEEP NIGHT

[Visual]
The gold light has filled the shrine. The eight acolytes are unconscious — collapsed in their circle. The counter-pole is destroyed — split down the center by ISOLDE's staff. The dead piece inside is dissolving — gold absorbing it.

The dark robe is on his knees in the gold light. His hood has fallen back. He is older than expected — perhaps eighty in human years. His skin has faint gold-line markings — he was once line, before he turned.

ISOLDE walks to him.

ISOLDE: Who were you.
DARK ROBE: I was — the carrier who chose the dark when my queen died. Two hundred years ago.
ISOLDE: Why.
DARK ROBE: She was my mate. She died from the price. I have lived since then, trying to break the line so no other queen would carry the price.
ISOLDE: That is grief.
DARK ROBE: That is grief.

A long silence. ISOLDE looks at him. She does not strike. The granddaughter at her side watches.

ISOLDE: Why did you kill my mother's grandmothers.
DARK ROBE: To stop the line. So the price would not take another queen.
ISOLDE: I will pay the price gladly.
DARK ROBE: You will.
ISOLDE: But I will live.
DARK ROBE: For a time.

ISOLDE crouches. She lays her hand on his shoulder. Gold passes from her into him. The dark in him fades. The gold lines on his skin brighten — he is line again.

He looks up.

DARK ROBE: You took my dark.
ISOLDE: The line took your dark. The line returns.
DARK ROBE: She loved me.
ISOLDE: I know.

He bows his head. He stops moving. After a moment, he is still. Two hundred years of grief released. He has died — peacefully, line returned to him.

[Visual]

ISOLDE: Take them home.
COURT-MAGUS: They served him for years.
ISOLDE: They followed grief. Send them home.

VAEL stands beside ISOLDE.

VAEL: Two hundred years.
ISOLDE: He carried it all.
VAEL: He killed many.
ISOLDE: He did.
VAEL: But.
ISOLDE: He was line. He paid for what he did when he turned. He has finished.

She touches her bracelets. The stain is shrinking. Visibly.

ISOLDE: The dark is gone from us too.
VAEL: It is.

S#3 — MOUNTAIN SKY / RETURNING / MIDDAY

[Visual]
The force flying home. Twenty-five carriers + ISOLDE + VAEL + drakes + the wider valley drakes. The mountains receding behind them. The line tree at the keep visible far ahead — even now, pulsing as if it knows they return.

KIRAN flies parallel to ISOLDE.

KIRAN: We received word from Velden. The line tree there has bloomed. First time in three hundred years.
ISOLDE: A second tree.
KIRAN: A second tree.
ISOLDE: Two trees. Two seats. Vael's keep and Velden city.
KIRAN: Two.
ISOLDE: Then we rule both.

She looks at VAEL parallel.

ISOLDE: From both.
VAEL: From both.

LEAD SCOUT: My queen.
ISOLDE: He is dead.
LEAD SCOUT: All of them.
ISOLDE: All of them.

The crowd — all gathered — kneels. One by one. All twenty-five carriers, the brother's army, the keep garrison, LEAD SCOUT himself.

VAEL alone stands. ISOLDE alone stands.

ISOLDE: Rise. Tomorrow we begin the coronation.
