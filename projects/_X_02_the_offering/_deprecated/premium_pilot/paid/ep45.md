# THE OFFERING — EP45: Two Trees

S#1 — VAEL'S KEEP / LINE TREE COURTYARD / DAWN

[Visual]
ISOLDE at the line tree. She places her hand on the bark. The tree responds — full pulse. The bracelets glow. The stain has shrunk to a small dark dot. Almost gone.

ISOLDE: We were sealed.
VAEL: We are sealed.
ISOLDE: We are crowned together. Soon.
VAEL: Soon.

GROVE KEEPER + the granddaughter join them. The granddaughter places her small hand on the bark beside ISOLDE's hand.

GRANDDAUGHTER: I am line.
ISOLDE: You are.
GRANDDAUGHTER: Forever.
ISOLDE: Forever.

S#2 — VAEL'S KEEP / ONYX HALL / MIDDAY

[Visual]
Coronation preparation. Court assembling. ISOLDE on the high seat. VAEL on a parallel seat — slightly lower (consort, not co-equal — for tradition reasons, even though she insists they rule equally elsewhere).

KIRAN: My queen. The Velden delegation has arrived. They wait at the outer gate.
ISOLDE: Bring them in.
KIRAN: Some of them are... old line who refused to come to you before.
ISOLDE: Bring them in.

The delegation enters — twelve men, twelve women — all of various ages. Most look uncertain. They have been with ALDRIC's court but did not flee.

LEAD DELEGATE: My queen. Velden requests official coronation in our city. The line tree there has bloomed. We would have our queen seen by our people.
ISOLDE: When.
LEAD DELEGATE: Ten days hence.
ISOLDE: Then ten days.
LEAD DELEGATE: And — my queen — we ask that the consort be officially named at the same ceremony.
ISOLDE: He will be.
LEAD DELEGATE: His title.
ISOLDE: Mate-King. Equal to me. Different role.
LEAD DELEGATE: There has not been such a title.
ISOLDE: There is now.

S#3 — VAEL'S KEEP / VAEL'S WING / EVENING

[Visual]
ISOLDE and VAEL alone. The night before the journey to Velden for coronation.

ISOLDE: Mate-King.
VAEL: That is your invention.
ISOLDE: It is.
VAEL: I will accept it.
ISOLDE: You will not be lord-of-the-keep alone anymore.
VAEL: I never was alone. I had KIRAN. The drakes.
ISOLDE: You will be king of Velden too.
VAEL: With you.
ISOLDE: With me.

She crosses to him. She lays her head against his chest.

ISOLDE: Years from now.
VAEL: Yes.
ISOLDE: I will be old. You will be unchanged.
VAEL: I age slower.
ISOLDE: How much slower.
VAEL: I age in hundreds. You will age in tens. By the time I see fifty years pass, you will see four hundred.
ISOLDE: I will not.
VAEL: You will live to seventy maybe. Eighty.
ISOLDE: With the price.
VAEL: With the price.
ISOLDE: That is enough.
VAEL: I do not think so.
ISOLDE: It must be.

He kisses her — slow. They stay together a long time.

S#4 — VAEL'S KEEP / LINE TREE COURTYARD / NIGHT

[Visual]
The line tree at night. ISOLDE and VAEL stand before it together. The carriers — all twenty-five — emerge from their quarters. They gather around the tree.

ISOLDE: Tomorrow we leave for Velden. Ten days hence I am crowned there. Vael is named Mate-King.
GROVE KEEPER: We come.
ISOLDE: All of you.
GROVE KEEPER: All of us. We are line.

The carriers form a circle around ISOLDE and VAEL. They join hands. The line tree pulses. The twenty-five marks on the carriers all glow brighter together. ISOLDE's bracelets blaze. VAEL's paired threads under his scales blaze.

The dark stain — what remains — flickers and dies completely.

ISOLDE: It is gone.
VAEL: It is gone.
