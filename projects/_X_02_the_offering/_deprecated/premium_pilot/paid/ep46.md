# THE OFFERING — EP46: On the Road

S#1 — KINGDOM ROAD / DAY

[Visual]
The procession — ISOLDE, VAEL, KIRAN, BROTHER, twenty-five carriers, lieutenants, drakes overhead — on the road to Velden. People come out of villages to watch. They line the road. Many bow as the procession passes.

A small village halfway. ISOLDE stops Carrow at a child crying by the road. She dismounts.

CHILD: My mother is sick.
ISOLDE: Where.
CHILD: There.

ISOLDE walks to the small house. Inside: a woman in bed, fevered. ISOLDE places her hand on the woman's forehead. The fever fades — visibly. The woman opens her eyes.

ISOLDE: She will live.
CHILD: How.
ISOLDE: The line.
CHILD: My grandmother said the queen would come.
ISOLDE: She was right.

S#2 — KINGDOM ROAD / EVENING

[Visual]
The procession continues. The line carries a soft glow now — visible to all. VAEL rides beside ISOLDE.

VAEL: You healed her.
ISOLDE: I did.
VAEL: That is the line growing in you.
ISOLDE: Or me growing into the line.
VAEL: Both.
ISOLDE: I have more powers I did not have before.
VAEL: You will keep growing.

She looks at her bracelets. The marks are settled. No stain. The bond marks are full.

ISOLDE: Will I age slower now.
VAEL: I do not know. Possibly. The mate bond may have changed your span.
ISOLDE: To match yours.
VAEL: That has never been recorded.
ISOLDE: But possibly.
VAEL: Possibly.

She smiles — small, hopeful.

S#3 — VELDEN CITY / OUTSKIRTS / EVENING (THREE DAYS LATER)

[Visual]
The procession approaches Velden. The city is visible — the second line tree blooming over the palace, visible above the walls. People are crowding the gates — they have come out to greet her.

KIRAN: My queen. The city is ready.
ISOLDE: How many people.
KIRAN: Thousands at the gate. More inside.
ISOLDE: They have not seen me as queen.
KIRAN: They have heard.

She looks at VAEL.

VAEL: They will see now.
ISOLDE: Yes.

S#4 — VELDEN CITY / GATES / DUSK

[Visual]
ISOLDE rides through the city gates on Carrow. The line tree at the palace is visible — taller than at the keep, gold-leafed. The crowd parts. They have heard the rumor of healing — they have heard about the dark robe being defeated.

ISOLDE rides slow through the streets. She does not dismount. She lets them see her on the dragon. The carriers and procession follow behind.

She reaches the palace gate. She dismounts there. VAEL beside her. The Velden delegation waits.

LEAD DELEGATE: My queen. The line tree is ready.
ISOLDE: Three days.
LEAD DELEGATE: Three days hence the coronation.
ISOLDE: Three.

The crowd in the square is silent — watching. ISOLDE turns. She raises her staff. The crowd kneels — in waves, one block at a time, until the whole square is kneeling.

VAEL alone stands beside her.

ISOLDE: Rise.

The crowd rises.

ISOLDE: Tomorrow we walk among you. Three days from now I am crowned.
