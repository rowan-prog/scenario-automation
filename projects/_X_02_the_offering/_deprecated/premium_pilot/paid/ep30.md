S#2 — VELDEN CITY / SOUTH GATE / MID-MORNING

[Visual]
The kingdom army at the south gate — assembled in defense. The kingdom crown still absent from ALDRIC's head. The dark robe figure beside ALDRIC on the city wall. The kingdom army numbers about one thousand foot, two hundred mounted — a city garrison, not field army.

ISOLDE rides into the open space before the gate. She does not approach within bow range — but close enough to be heard.

ISOLDE: Aldric.
ALDRIC (from wall): Niece.
ISOLDE: I have come for the answer.
ALDRIC: Answer.
ISOLDE: Why my mother died.
ALDRIC: Fever.

A long pause.

ISOLDE: I will say it again. Why my mother died.
ALDRIC: You ride with the dragon. You wear the crown stolen from me.
ISOLDE: The crown returned to its line. Why my mother died.
ALDRIC: Because she would have ended me.
ISOLDE: Then you ended her first.
ALDRIC: I did.

The kingdom army shifts. They have not heard this. They hear it now.

ISOLDE: You hear him. Your king killed his sister-in-law to take a crown that is not his.
ALDRIC: Hold them.

The kingdom army does not move.

ALDRIC: Hold —

The dark robe steps forward beside ALDRIC. He raises his hands. Dark light pulses around them — kingdom magic.

DARK ROBE: She has a price. We take it now.

S#3 — VELDEN CITY / SOUTH GATE / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
The dark light from the dark robe shoots toward ISOLDE — across the open ground. It moves slow, deliberate, targeting her bracelets directly.

The bracelets blaze in defense. Carrow rears. ISOLDE raises the line tree branch — she points it.

The dark light hits the bracelets. ISOLDE's body convulses — she clutches the staff. The bracelets fight back — gold against dark.

VAEL drives his drake forward. He places his own scaled hand on the dark light beam. His scales burn red — the dark light fights his hand. He keeps it there.

The bracelets and Vael's hand together push the dark light back.

But the dark light has touched ISOLDE. A new mark — black — appears beside the bracelets. Small. Like a stain.

DARK ROBE: She has the stain now.

ISOLDE looks at the stain on her wrist. The bracelets glow brighter — pushing the stain. The stain shrinks but does not disappear.

ISOLDE (loud, to the kingdom army): I am the line. Your king killed my mother. He is no longer king. Yield.

A long silence. Then — the kingdom mounted captain (different from the captain in EP7) steps his horse forward — out of formation. He raises one fist — empty.

KINGDOM MOUNTED CAPTAIN: I yield.

A second captain raises his fist. Then a third. Then half the army. Then most.

The dark robe figure turns. He walks back into the city. ALDRIC stands on the wall alone.

ISOLDE: Aldric. Walk down.
ALDRIC: Niece — the price is taking you.
ISOLDE: It is taking. I am still here.
ALDRIC: For now.
ISOLDE: Walk down.

He does not. He turns. He walks back into the city after the dark robe.
