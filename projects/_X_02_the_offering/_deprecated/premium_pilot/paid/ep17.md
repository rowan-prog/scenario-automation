# THE OFFERING — EP17: The Stone

S#1 — VAEL'S KEEP / ISOLDE'S OLD CHAMBER / MORNING

[Visual]
ISOLDE alone with the carved box. She holds the small dark stone in her palm. ELARA enters quietly — invited.

ELARA: My grandmother used to hum to her tapestry. I think she was speaking to the line.
ISOLDE: How.
ELARA: I do not know. I was a child.
ISOLDE: Show me what you remember.
ELARA: I remember a sound.

ELARA hums — a low, three-note sequence. The stone in ISOLDE's palm warms.

ISOLDE looks at it. She tries the same three notes. The stone warms more.

She closes her hand on the stone. She closes her eyes. She thinks of her mother.

The stone pulses. ISOLDE flinches — eyes open. The flame-curve on her right collarbone has brightened. Three notes ringing in her own throat without her opening her mouth.

ISOLDE: It speaks back.
ELARA: It does.

S#2 — VAEL'S KEEP / INNER COURTYARD / MIDDAY

[Visual]
ISOLDE stands among the twelve drakes. She holds the stone. She hums the three notes. Every drake turns its head toward her. Carrow steps forward and lowers her snout. The other eleven follow — twelve drakes in a circle around her, all heads lowered.

The drakes are listening.

ISOLDE: The line was theirs too.
VAEL (entering): The line was the first to bond with mine.
ISOLDE: Then the line breaks if I die.
VAEL: It would have.

VAEL puts his scaled hand on her marked wrist. The three marks pulse. The twelve drakes raise their heads in unison.

S#3 — VAEL'S KEEP / WAR ROOM / EVENING

[Visual]
ISOLDE places the stone on the war map — directly over the brother's camp position. She closes her hand around it briefly. She lifts her hand. The stone glows. On the map, in the same place — a faint reverse glow appears. Brother's camp answering.

HALDREN: He has the same kind of stone.
KIRAN: Or he is using something else and the line connects.
ISOLDE: The stone is the line.
HALDREN: Yes.
VAEL: Can he hear you through it.
ISOLDE: I do not know yet.
ELARA: My grandmother said the line answers only to the one who carries it most clearly.
ISOLDE: Then he hears me only if I let him.
ELARA: Yes.

ISOLDE places the stone back in the box. She closes the box.

ISOLDE: Tomorrow I let him.

S#4 — VAEL'S KEEP / VAEL'S WING / NIGHT

[Visual]
ISOLDE in bed. VAEL beside her. He is not asleep. He watches the ceiling.

VAEL: You will speak to him.
ISOLDE: Yes.
VAEL: Through the stone.
ISOLDE: Yes.
VAEL: What will you say.
ISOLDE: That I am here. That I have the line. That if he wants me, he must come for me himself.
VAEL: He will come.
ISOLDE: Yes.

VAEL turns to her. His scaled hand on her cheek.

VAEL: Then he comes to where you are.
ISOLDE: Yes.
VAEL: And what is here.
ISOLDE: You are here.
VAEL: And the twelve.
ISOLDE: And the twelve.

He kisses her — slow, long. His scaled palm under her jaw. Her hand at his shoulder. The kiss does not break for a long time.
