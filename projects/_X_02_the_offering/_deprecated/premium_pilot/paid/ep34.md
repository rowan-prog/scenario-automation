S#2 — NORTHERN RIDGE / EARLY AFTERNOON

[Visual]
The drakes land on a ridge overlooking a valley. In the valley below: an old temple — half-collapsed stone, vines, surrounded by dead earth. The earth around the temple is gray — life leached. A small structure has been newly built at the temple's center — a counter-pole, the dead branch piece set in iron.

The dark robe is visible — at the counter-pole. He is reading from a scroll. Around him: six dark-cloaked attendants.

ISOLDE: He has acolytes.
COURT-MAGUS: Always. Counter-mages collect them.
ISOLDE: Six.
COURT-MAGUS: Six is enough to anchor a counter-tree. If they complete the rite — the line begins to wither.
ISOLDE: When does the rite complete.
COURT-MAGUS: At sundown.

ISOLDE looks at the sky. Maybe four hours.

ISOLDE: We attack before sundown.
VAEL: Drakes from above.
ISOLDE: Drakes from above. The line carriers I left at the keep — call them.
COURT-MAGUS: They cannot reach in time.
ISOLDE: They do not need to be physically here. The line is here.

She places her hand on the line tree staff. The staff glows. Audience-only: at Vael's keep, the line tree itself glows in answer. The carriers in the keep all turn their heads toward Velden — toward ISOLDE.

S#3 — NORTHERN RIDGE / LATE AFTERNOON

[Visual]
ISOLDE has the line carriers ready — not physically here but spiritually linked through the line tree at the keep. ELARA, the eldest, the middle, the youngest, Mira, the children, the new carriers — all twenty-three at the keep, all participating in the line link.

ISOLDE holds the staff. The staff blazes. The line link is established — visible as gold thread going from her staff to Vael's keep and back. The thread is bright enough to be seen across the valley.

At the temple below, the dark robe sees the gold. He raises his head. He calls out something to his six acolytes. They form a tighter circle around the counter-pole. They speed their chant.

ISOLDE: Now.

VAEL gestures. The twelve drakes lift off — synchronized. They dive toward the temple.

S#4 — TEMPLE VALLEY / LATE AFTERNOON

[Visual]
The twelve drakes hit the temple courtyard. Three drakes engage the six acolytes — fire bursts. Three of the acolytes go down. The other three scatter but maintain the chant from new positions. Six more drakes engage the dark robe directly — but his counter-pole protects him in a dome of dark energy.

Carrow lands at the temple gate. ISOLDE dismounts. VAEL beside her. They walk forward.

DARK ROBE: You are too late. The rite is at three-quarters.
ISOLDE: Then we stop it now.

She raises the staff. She points at the counter-pole.

DARK ROBE: The pole answers only to dark.
ISOLDE: I answer to gold.

She drives the staff into the ground at her feet. The line tree branch at the staff tip extends down into the dead earth. The dead earth blazes — gold spreading from the staff, outward, across the temple grounds.

The acolytes' chant breaks. The dome around the dark robe flickers.
