# THE OFFERING — EP35: The Pole

S#1 — TEMPLE VALLEY / EVENING

[Visual]
Gold has spread to the temple's outer wall. The dead earth is alive again — small green shoots beneath the gold. The dark robe's dome is flickering badly now. He sees it. He turns toward his hidden door (the same kind as in Velden palace). He pulls a flame from his sleeve.

ISOLDE: Stop him.

Carrow lifts off with one wing-beat — crosses the temple courtyard in a single jump — and lands between the dark robe and the door.

DARK ROBE: I will return.

He throws the dead branch piece toward Carrow. The piece flies past her — toward ISOLDE.

VAEL intercepts. His scaled hand catches the dead branch piece. His scales burn red. He drops the piece — to the ground. The piece hits the now-living earth.

The earth absorbs it. The dead piece dissolves into the gold-blazing ground. Gone.

DARK ROBE: No —

The dome around him collapses. The drakes close in. The dark robe pulls his cloak around himself. A flash of dark light. He is gone — escaped through a small concealed portal that none can pursue.

S#2 — TEMPLE VALLEY / NIGHT

[Visual]
The temple is silent. The six acolytes — three dead, three captured by drakes. The counter-pole is unhooked — KIRAN dismantles it. The dark robe's portal mark is cold.

ISOLDE walks the temple grounds. The line tree branch staff is still in her hand. The gold has stopped spreading — settled into the ground. A new sapling has begun to grow at the temple's center — where the counter-pole was. The line is reclaiming the place.

VAEL beside her.

VAEL: He left the piece. He will not have it.
ISOLDE: He will make another.
VAEL: How.
ISOLDE: There must be other dead pieces. He took only one from Velden.
VAEL: We find them.
ISOLDE: Yes.

She turns to him. His scaled hand is darker now — the touching of the dead branch piece has left a small dark patch.

ISOLDE: Vael —
VAEL: It is small. It will fade.
ISOLDE: Or you have a stain now too.
VAEL: Or I do.

S#3 — VAEL'S KEEP / LINE TREE COURTYARD / DAWN

[Visual]
Return to the keep. The line tree has grown taller in the days they were gone. ISOLDE walks to it. She places her hand on the bark. The tree pulses warm.

ELARA approaches.

ELARA: My queen. Two more carriers arrived overnight.
ISOLDE: Total.
ELARA: Twenty-five.
ISOLDE: The line is full.
ELARA: Not yet. There are five more known of.
ISOLDE: Send for them.
ELARA: Already done.

ISOLDE removes the crown. She places it at the base of the tree — as offering. The tree absorbs it. Then returns it — now larger, more intricately wrought.

ISOLDE: It is offered. It is taken. It is given back.
ELARA: The tree accepts you.
ISOLDE: It accepts.

S#4 — VAEL'S KEEP / VAEL'S WING / EVENING

[Visual]
ISOLDE and VAEL alone. He shows her his scaled hand. The dark patch from EP35 S#2 has not faded — it has grown slightly.

VAEL: It is a stain.
ISOLDE: Yes.
VAEL: It is on me now.
ISOLDE: It is.
VAEL: I will share yours.
ISOLDE: Vael.
VAEL: You did not ask. The mate bond shares.

She looks at her own wrist. The stain on her bracelet — has shrunk slightly. Some of it has moved to him.

ISOLDE: How.
VAEL: The bond.
ISOLDE: That is the price.
VAEL: One of them.
ISOLDE: There may be more.
VAEL: There may.

He pulls her to him. She lets him. They sit on the bed. He places his scaled hand on her bracelet. The two marks — his stain, her stain — fight each other through the bond. Both glow gold against the dark.

ISOLDE: Together.
VAEL: Together.
