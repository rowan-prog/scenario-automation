# THE OFFERING — EP32: Throne Room

S#1 — VELDEN CITY / OLD PALACE / THRONE ROOM / EVENING

[Visual]
ISOLDE crosses to the foot of the throne. VAEL beside her. BROTHER and KIRAN at the door. The dark robe begins to step backward — toward a hidden door behind the throne.

ISOLDE points the line tree staff at ALDRIC.

ISOLDE: You killed my mother. You took the crown that did not belong to you. You burned five of the line. You sent assassins. You tried to kill me through the dark.
ALDRIC: All of it.
ISOLDE: Confess it in writing.
ALDRIC: Why.
ISOLDE: Because Velden needs to know.
ALDRIC: Velden will know.
ISOLDE: In writing. Now.

She points the staff at a small writing desk by the throne. The desk slides forward — magical movement. Parchment and quill rise into position.

ALDRIC: I refuse.
ISOLDE: The line takes from you if you refuse.

ALDRIC looks at the desk. He looks at the staff. The dark robe is still moving backward — slow, watching.

ALDRIC: Write what.
ISOLDE: Truth.

ALDRIC rises. He moves to the desk. He picks up the quill. He writes.

S#2 — VELDEN CITY / OLD PALACE / THRONE ROOM / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
ALDRIC writes. ISOLDE watches. The dark robe reaches the hidden door — pulls something from his robe — a small piece of the line tree's first branch (the dead one from EP27). He opens the hidden door. He slips through.

VAEL: Vael.

ISOLDE turns.

VAEL: He has a piece of the dead tree.
ISOLDE: After him. KIRAN.

KIRAN moves. He pursues through the hidden door. BROTHER stays.

ALDRIC has finished writing. He sets down the quill. He has signed.

ALDRIC: I have written all of it.

ISOLDE takes the parchment. She reads. The confession includes: mother's murder, the dark robe's identity (a former line-mage who turned), assassins sent, dark magic alliance, killing of carriers.

ISOLDE: This is all of it.
ALDRIC: All.
ISOLDE: Then you yield.
ALDRIC: I yield to my queen.

He kneels at the foot of the throne. The throne is empty.

S#3 — VELDEN CITY / OLD PALACE / HIDDEN PASSAGE / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
KIRAN runs through the hidden passage. Dark corridor — old stone. He hears footsteps ahead. He runs faster.

The dark robe reaches an open courtyard inside the palace. A second hidden door — leads outside the city. The dark robe pulls a small flame from his sleeve — burns a marking on the floor. A portal opens.

KIRAN arrives just as the dark robe steps through.

KIRAN: Stop.
DARK ROBE: She has a moment more. I take this piece. I make the counter-tree.

He steps through. The portal closes. KIRAN runs to the marking on the floor — it has burned out.

KIRAN: My lord. He escaped.

S#4 — VELDEN CITY / OLD PALACE / THRONE ROOM / NIGHT

[Visual]
ALDRIC in chains — light chains, kingdom-made. He sits on the throne room floor, not on the throne. BROTHER stands behind him as guard. ISOLDE on the throne — actually sitting now. VAEL beside the throne (standing).

KIRAN enters. He bows.

KIRAN: He escaped, my queen.
ISOLDE: With the dead branch piece.
KIRAN: Yes.
ISOLDE: He plans a counter-tree.
KIRAN: He said so.
ISOLDE: He cannot grow it. He is not the line.
KIRAN: But the dark magic.
ISOLDE: Yes.

She looks at her wrist. The black stain has darkened slightly since EP30. The bracelets are still bright but the stain has not been pushed completely back.

ISOLDE: He will be found.
VAEL: Soon.
ISOLDE: Tonight we hold Velden. Tomorrow we find him.
VAEL: Tomorrow.

She turns to ALDRIC.

ISOLDE: You. Sleep tonight. Tomorrow we read your confession in public.

ALDRIC says nothing.
