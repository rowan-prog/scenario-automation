# THE OFFERING — EP49: He Unfolds (HEAVY GATE)

S#1 — VELDEN CITY / OLD PALACE / RAMPART / DAWN (DAY AFTER CORONATION)

[Visual]
ISOLDE on the rampart. The line tree-crown — the gold-leafed circlet — on her head. She is alone — VAEL went down to the line tree at dawn. She watches the city wake up.

VAEL climbs the rampart steps. He stops beside her.

VAEL: Today.
ISOLDE: What.
VAEL: Today I show what I am.
ISOLDE: I have seen what you are.
VAEL: You have seen parts.
ISOLDE: I have seen the scaled hand. The flame palm. The wing-shadow.
VAEL: Today I show whole.
ISOLDE: Why today.
VAEL: Because Velden has seen the queen. Velden has not seen the king. They have seen a man at her side. They have not seen what the bond joined her to.
ISOLDE: A dragon.
VAEL: A dragon.
ISOLDE: How.
VAEL: At the line tree at midday. Where everyone watches.

S#2 — VELDEN CITY / GREAT SQUARE / MORNING

[Visual]
The square fills again. Word has spread — the Mate-King will show himself. The crowd is bigger than yesterday's coronation. The southern lords return — this time in armor, allowed inside the city as honorary guard.

The carriers form their circle around the tree. The drakes — sixty visible — wait on every rampart and rooftop. KIRAN coordinates positions.

ISOLDE arrives at the tree's foot. VAEL beside her. The crowd is silent.

VAEL: I have not done this in front of humans for two hundred years.
KIRAN: Before that.
VAEL: Once. To prove who I was. To my first lord.
KIRAN: He was your father.
VAEL: He was.
KIRAN: He died fifty years before I was born.
VAEL: Yes.

ISOLDE looks at VAEL.

ISOLDE: It is time.
VAEL: It is.

He steps away from her — to a clear space in the square. The crowd gives him room. The carriers form their circle around the tree but leave a wider perimeter for him. The drakes around the city watch.

He raises his scaled hand. The bracelet under the scales blazes. The paired threads with ISOLDE's marks blaze in answer.

S#3 — VELDEN CITY / GREAT SQUARE / LINE TREE / MIDDAY

[Visual]
The sun reaches midday. VAEL stands in the clear space. He closes his eyes. His body changes — slow at first.

His skin darkens. Scales bloom across his shoulders, down his back, along his arms. The gauntleted hand becomes fully scaled. His hair extends into a longer, thicker dark mane. His eyes — when he opens them — are fully non-human now. The pupils vertical.

He continues. He grows taller. His shoulders broaden. From his back — slowly — wings unfold. Massive. Black with crimson under-leather. They open fully — at full extension, they span twice the width of the line tree.

His mouth opens. Now there are no human teeth — pointed. A long muzzle has begun to form. His face has shifted. He is no longer fully human-shaped.

He grows further. The crowd has stepped back further. The clear space has become much larger. He is now at perhaps thirty feet tall — too large for human form but not yet full drake.

He pauses there.

VAEL (his voice deeper, layered with a roaring undertone): This is half.
ISOLDE: Half.
VAEL: This is what I show.

He continues. His shoulders narrow into a serpentine torso. His legs become full hind legs of a drake. The wings widen further. He grows taller still — until he is at full drake form, larger than Carrow, larger than any drake on the ramparts.

He is fully a dragon now. The line tree at his side. ISOLDE at his foreleg.

The square is silent. The southern lords have all knelt — without orders. The drakes around the city — all sixty — have lowered their heads. The carriers have lowered their heads too.

VAEL (now in drake-voice — half roar half words): The bond is dragon. The crown is queen. The line is ours.

He extends one massive wing over ISOLDE — protective canopy.

ISOLDE looks up at him. She reaches and lays her hand on his foreleg. Her palm against his scales.

ISOLDE: Mate.
VAEL (drake): Mate.

S#4 — VELDEN CITY / GREAT SQUARE / LINE TREE / AFTERNOON

[Visual]
VAEL — still in full drake form — has been still for hours. The line tree's gold light flows up his scales. The carriers and crowd have not left.

ISOLDE has remained beside him.

By late afternoon, VAEL begins to fold. The drake form recedes — slowly. Wings re-fold and absorb. Body narrows. Scales recede to only the previous half-arm. Eyes become almost human again (slight vertical hint remains). He is — by sunset — back in human form, fully dressed in his coronation robes again as if he had never changed.

He breathes.

ISOLDE: That is the only time.
VAEL: The only public time. Maybe one more — far in the future — to a successor.
ISOLDE: We have no successor.
VAEL: There may yet be one.

He lays his hand on her stomach — gently, deliberate.

ISOLDE looks at him.

ISOLDE: Now.
VAEL: Now.

She lays her hand over his.
