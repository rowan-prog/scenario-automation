# THE OFFERING — EP03 (v30)

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Vael's Chamber. Morning.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Full light through the slit. Bed unmade. Vael — black shirt, sleeves rolled — at the chamber door. Kiran outside the door, low voice.

[DIALOGUE]
KIRAN (off): My lord. The lower yard. The eastern guard found something at the wall.
VAEL: Something or someone.
KIRAN: Both.
VAEL: I'll come.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He turns to the bed. Isolde sitting up against the headboard, his robe still on.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Stay in the chamber. Door stays locked. Kiran will be at it.
ISOLDE: For how long.
VAEL: An hour. Maybe two.
ISOLDE: And if I want to leave.
VAEL: You don't, today.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He leaves. The door locks from outside.

She gets out of bed. Walks to the chamber door. Tries the handle. Locked.

[VO]
ISOLDE (V.O.): Door locked. Same as home.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She walks the chamber. Opens drawers — slowly, deliberately. Inventory. Nothing personal. No letters. No portraits. No woman's things anywhere.

She finds a small carved bird on the desk — wood, worn from handling. Picks it up. Looks at it. Sets it back down exactly where it was.

[KEY CAMERA]
Close: the bird. Push to her face.

## S#2 — Same Chamber. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A loud thump at the chamber door. Voices outside.

[DIALOGUE]
SERA (off): Move.
KIRAN (off): My lady, the lord has —
SERA (off): I said *move*, Kiran.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The door opens. Sera walks in. Red hair high, gold gown, eyes hot. Kiran behind her — face tight, did not stop her.

Isolde does not move. Stands beside the desk. Still holding the small wooden bird.

[DIALOGUE]
SERA: So this is the bride.

ISOLDE (sets the bird down carefully): You walked past a locked door for this.

SERA: I walked past Kiran. The door opened itself for me.

ISOLDE: Then go ahead.

SERA: Go ahead what.

ISOLDE: Whatever you came here to do. You have a window. Make it count.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Sera walks the chamber once. Looks at the bed — slept in. Looks at the robe on Isolde's shoulders. Recognizes it.

[DIALOGUE]
SERA: You're wearing his robe.
ISOLDE: I'm wearing what was in the bath chamber.
SERA: He's never given that robe to anyone.
ISOLDE: Then ask him why he gave it to me. Or don't. I don't care.

SERA: He'll tire of you. By the end of the week. Two weeks at most.

ISOLDE: Maybe.

SERA: I've waited twelve years for him.

ISOLDE (looks at her): You had twelve years and still don't have him. I had one night and still do.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Sera's smile drops. Two steps forward. She grabs Isolde's wrist — hard enough to bruise. Pulls.

Isolde does not pull back. Lets her have the wrist. Looks at Sera's hand on her arm.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (low, level): Let go.
SERA: Or what.
ISOLDE: Or you find out what a human can do when she's already been called worse than anything you can think of.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Behind Sera — the chamber door has not closed since she came in. A shadow falls across the floor.

Vael in the doorway.

He does not speak. Sera does not see him. Isolde does. Does not react.

Vael walks into the chamber. Slow. Sera turns at the last second.

She lets go of Isolde's wrist.

[DIALOGUE]
SERA: My lord.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael does not look at her. He walks past her to Isolde. Takes Isolde's wrist — the one Sera just held — in both his hands. Looks at the red marks. His scales rise from his wrist to his elbow. Slow. Held.

He does not let go of her wrist. He turns his head to Sera. Still does not speak.

[DIALOGUE]
SERA (smaller): My lord, I came to —
VAEL: Out.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Sera walks out. Fast. Kiran closes the door behind her from the inside. Stands against it.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held close: his scaled hand around her bruised wrist.

## S#3 — Same Chamber. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael still has her wrist. Does not look at her face. Looks at the bruises.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (low, to Kiran without turning): I want her house out of the keep by sundown.
KIRAN: Yes, my lord.
VAEL: She stays. Under guard. Her three closest go.
KIRAN: Yes.
VAEL: Out.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Kiran goes. Door closes.

Vael finally lifts his eyes to Isolde's face.

His scales — still up to his elbow — flicker in the light.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Your hand has scales on it.
VAEL: It does.
ISOLDE: Like the men outside.
VAEL: Like them. More than them. I am their lord.

ISOLDE (does not pull her wrist away): Does the dragon do that when you are angry.
VAEL: When I am about to lose control of what I am.
ISOLDE: Are you about to lose control.
VAEL: I have been about to lose control since the carriage stopped in my yard.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She does not breathe for a beat.

He lets her wrist go. Walks her backward — not rough, just steady — until her back is against the stone wall. His hand goes flat above her shoulder.

His other hand at her jaw. Lifts her face up. He looks at her.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of them against the wall. Push close to his hand at her jaw. Close to her eyes — confused, not afraid.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Tell me who he was.
ISOLDE: Who.
VAEL: The man you gave it to.
ISOLDE: One night. No name.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He kisses her. Not slow. Open mouth. His knee comes between her thighs through the robe.

She does not push him off. Her hands come up to the front of his shirt and grip.

He bites her lower lip. Hard.

She bites him back. Harder.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He pulls back. Blood on his lip. He looks at her. He smiles. Half a second.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Better.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He kisses her again. Slower this time. Mouth open. She moans into him. She did not mean to. She does.

He stops. Pulls back.

[KEY CAMERA]
Close: his lip — bleeding. Hers — swollen. His blood on her chin.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: You want him.
ISOLDE (breathing hard): I want him.
VAEL: You want me.
ISOLDE: ...
VAEL: Say it.
ISOLDE: I want him. You're standing here.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
His hand at her jaw tightens once. Then releases. He steps back. Looks at her.

He turns. Walks to the chamber door. Opens it. Walks through. The door closes.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde alone against the wall. Slides down it slow. Sits on the floor in his robe. His blood on her chin.

She lifts her hand to her mouth. Wipes the blood. Looks at it on her fingers.

[VO]
ISOLDE (V.O.): He stopped. Why.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Her free hand — without her thinking about it — goes to her stomach. Low. Held.

Beat.

She looks down at her own hand on her belly. Frowns. Pulls the hand away.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held close on her hand pulled back from her belly.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
Outside the chamber door, in the corridor — Vael against the opposite wall. His hand at his mouth. His blood on his fingers. His scales up to his shoulder now.

He closes his eyes. Breathes once. Slow.

Audience-only: a single dark scale comes off his forearm and falls to the floor.

Hard Cut.
