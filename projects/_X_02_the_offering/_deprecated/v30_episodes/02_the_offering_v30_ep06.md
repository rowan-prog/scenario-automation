# THE OFFERING — EP06 (v30)

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Vael's Chamber. Late Same Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The chamber. Hearth low. Isolde at the chamber door. She is still in the black silk from the terrace. She has just walked here from the upper terrace alone.

Vael walks in behind her. Closes the door. Locks it.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: An assassin tried the south wall an hour ago.
VAEL: Kiran caught him at the outer guardhouse.
ISOLDE: Who paid.
VAEL: We will know by morning.
ISOLDE: Then you'll tell me by morning.
VAEL: Yes.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She turns. Faces him. He stays two paces away.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Tonight.
VAEL: Tonight what.
ISOLDE: You. Me. The bed.
VAEL: Are you sure.
ISOLDE: I am tired of asking myself that question. So yes.

[KEY CAMERA]
Close on his face. Push slow.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: If I touch you all the way tonight, I will not be careful.
ISOLDE: I have been handled carefully my whole life. It did not save me.
VAEL: ...
ISOLDE: Teach me what you mean.

## S#2 — Same Chamber. Continuous. Bed Scene.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He walks to her. Slow first. Then not. He pulls the silk off her shoulder — one strap, then the other. Drops it down to her waist. His mouth at her throat. He bites — not hard. A test.

She doesn't flinch.

He bites harder.

She gasps. Pulls his head closer with her hand in his hair.

He lifts her. Walks her three steps to the bed. Drops her on it. Black silk falls the rest of the way off as he does it. He stands over her at the edge of the bed for a long beat. Looks at her.

Then he is on the bed with her. His mouth on her throat, her collarbone, her sternum. His scaled hand pins her wrist above her head. The other hand at her hip, lifting.

[CUTAWAY]
The black sheet under her back pulling tight.
His scales rising past his elbow.
Her hand in his hair — pulling.
His knee between her thighs.
Her hairline darkening with sweat.
His pulse at the side of his throat — fast.
Her gasp — half-word, his name without the rest of the word.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Mid-act she stops. Stares at him. Half a second.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (breath): ... Have we —
VAEL (low, against her mouth): We haven't.
ISOLDE: Then why does this —
VAEL: Why does this what.
ISOLDE: Nothing. Don't stop.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He doesn't stop. He kisses her harder so she cannot think.

Her hand on his chest — over his heart. Beat. The heartbeat under her palm — same as the masquerade night.

She closes her eyes. Hard.

He sees her face. His scales rise up his throat. He does not stop.

She comes. He doesn't until she does. After.

Both breathing. He stays inside her for a long beat. Does not move off her.

[KEY CAMERA]
Slow push on his face — wet at the corners. Not tears. Almost.
Close on her face — eyes closed, jaw soft.

## S#3 — Same Chamber. After.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Bed. Both on their sides. Her back against his chest. His arm around her waist. His scaled hand resting low on her belly through nothing — bare skin to bare skin.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: One promise.
VAEL (goes still): What.
ISOLDE: Don't lie to me. Not even the small ones. Not tonight.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He does not answer for a long beat.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: ... I won't.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She does not notice the pause. She closes her eyes.

He watches her until her breath slows. Then he gets up. Slowly. Wraps her in the black sheet so the cold does not reach her. Walks to the balcony door. Opens it. Steps out.

Stands in the cold air for a long time. Hand half-clenched at his side. The hand glows faintly. He almost said it. He didn't.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of him on the balcony. Push to his hand — glow. Pull back.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
In the outer guardhouse below, Kiran stands over a body. The captured assassin — already dead. Not by Kiran. Kiran turns. Looks at the rafters. There is a small black feather on the floor beside the body.

Audience-only: that mark belongs to the council. The council voted faster than expected. The first attempt is already over. There will be a second.

Hard Cut.
