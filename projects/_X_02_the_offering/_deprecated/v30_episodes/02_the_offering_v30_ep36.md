# THE OFFERING — EP36 (v30) — FALSE ALARM

## S#1 — Chamber. Late Afternoon.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Isolde walking the chamber slow. Halren in the chair by the bed. Vael at the desk reading reports — pretending to read.

Isolde stops mid-step. Hand hard on her belly. Hand hard on the bed post.

Vael up before her hand has finished closing on the post. At her side in two strides.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: It is starting.
VAEL: Halren.
HALREN (already up): My lord. Hand on her wrist. Twelve count.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Halren puts her hand on Isolde's wrist. Counts. Halfway through the contraction, the count slows.

The contraction passes.

[DIALOGUE]
HALREN: Not yet.
ISOLDE: It felt like —
HALREN: It felt like. But not yet. Body practicing.
VAEL: How long.
HALREN: Hours. Maybe a day.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael does not let go of Isolde's hand.

---

## S#2 — Chamber. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He walks her to the bed. Sits her down. Kneels in front of her.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: You will sit until it starts again.
ISOLDE: I cannot sit for a whole day.
HALREN: You can walk. Slow. Not far. The chamber wall and back.
ISOLDE: ...

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She walks the chamber wall. Slow. He walks beside her. Hand at the small of her back.

She walks for an hour. No contractions. They stop. He sits her in the chair by the bed.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: I am tired.
VAEL: Sleep.
ISOLDE: You will not sleep.
VAEL: I will sit here. You sleep.

---

## S#3 — Chamber. Evening.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She is asleep in the chair. He has not moved. Halren reads in the corner.

A second contraction. Isolde wakes mid-clench. Long beat.

Vael at her side again. Halren's hand on her wrist.

[DIALOGUE]
HALREN: Eight count.
VAEL: That is one.
HALREN: That is one. Sit still.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The contraction passes. They wait. Another fifteen minutes — nothing.

[DIALOGUE]
HALREN: Body still practicing. Sleep tonight. I think tomorrow.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Halren bows. Goes back to her own room.

---

## S#4 — Bed. Night.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He carries her to the bed. Lays her down. Lies down beside her on the wide platform.

Hand on her belly. Hand at her face.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Tomorrow.
VAEL: Tomorrow.
ISOLDE: You will be here.
VAEL: I will be here.
ISOLDE: Whatever happens.
VAEL: Whatever happens.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She closes her eyes. He does not.

The slit window — late light. The keep is quiet. The ridge is quiet. Even the east is quiet.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held wide of the bed. Slow push.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
None.

[END HOOK]
Just before midnight — Isolde's eyes open. Sharp. Her hand on her belly is tight.

She does not say anything. She does not wake him.

She just opens her eyes and feels it.

It is starting.

She lets him have one more hour of quiet first.

Hard Cut.
