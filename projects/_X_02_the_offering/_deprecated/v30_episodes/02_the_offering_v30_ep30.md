# THE OFFERING — EP30 (v30) — WEST BY WING

## S#1 — Drakonis Keep. Outer Yard. Pre-Dawn.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Yard. Kiran with travel rolls. Halren the midwife — she will fly behind, on horseback, with a small escort. She will arrive a day after.

Isolde in heavy black wool wrap. Belly large. Vael in black — sleeves torn off above the elbow. He will transform.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: I am ready.
VAEL: Tell me before you are sick. I will land.
ISOLDE: I will tell you.
KIRAN: My lord. The keep is yours when you are gone.
VAEL: The keep is hers. You answer to her by letter every two days. If she says return, you return.
KIRAN: Yes, my lord.

---

## S#2 — Outside the Gate. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael walks twenty paces out. Transforms. Same forty-five-second sequence — quicker for audience this time.

Isolde climbs the foreleg. Sits in the crook of his shoulder, against his neck. Bundled in the wool.

He looks back at her. Eye level — black dragon, red ring.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (in her head): Hold tight.
ISOLDE (aloud, to no one): Always.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He lifts off. The keep falls below them. The eastern ridge dragons open their eyes briefly. Then close them. Permission.

---

## S#3 — Flight. Three Days Compressed.

[MONTAGE]
Sunset — flying west.
Night — landed in a forest clearing. He folded around her. She slept against his shoulder.
Dawn — flying.
Noon — landed by a river. Halren and her escort caught up. They ate.
Afternoon — flying again.
Second sunset — Veine palace visible on the horizon.
Third dawn — flying low across the Veine plain. The court has seen them coming. Bells ringing.

[VO]
ISOLDE (V.O.): I left this kingdom in chains in a black iron carriage. I am returning on the shoulder of the dragon that kingdom sent me to. The road I left on has not changed. I have.

---

## S#4 — Veine Palace Courtyard. Late Morning.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
A black dragon lands in the central courtyard. The court is in ranks — every noble, every guard, every steward. Adeline at the front, in dark mourning gold, hands folded.

Vael lowers his foreleg. Isolde climbs down. She walks alone the last three paces to Adeline.

Vael transforms back. Stays close. Sleeves and shirt regrown by the magic of the transformation. Black coat now.

Adeline looks at Isolde. Long beat. Adeline bows — deep, formal.

[DIALOGUE]
ADELINE: Sister.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Every noble behind Adeline bows. The whole courtyard.

Isolde does not bow back. Looks at Adeline a long beat.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Take me to him.
ADELINE: Yes, sister.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Adeline turns and walks toward the inner palace. Isolde follows. Vael three paces behind.

The court parts.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide of the courtyard. Slow tracking the three of them walking in.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
SUPER on Adeline: ADELINE — REGENT OF VEINE
SUPER on Isolde: ISOLDE OF DRAKONIS — RETURNED

[END HOOK]
Inside the palace, the inner doors close behind them. The court outside remains bowed for a long beat after they are gone.

Hard Cut.
