# THE OFFERING — EP33 (v30) — HOMECOMING

## S#1 — Veine Palace Courtyard. Dawn.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Three days after the funeral. Isolde and Vael at the central courtyard. Halren the midwife — has been keeping pace — already there.

Adeline arrives in plain dress, no circlet. She has come to see them off.

[DIALOGUE]
ADELINE: Sister.
ISOLDE: Sister.
ADELINE: If you need anything from this kingdom — letter, gold, court — write.
ISOLDE: I will not need anything.
ADELINE: I know. Write anyway.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Beat. Isolde looks at her a long time.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: I should hate you.
ADELINE: You should.
ISOLDE: I am tired.
ADELINE: That is honest.
ISOLDE: I do not promise anything. But I do not promise the other thing either.
ADELINE: That is enough.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Adeline bows. Slow. Deep. Isolde does not bow back.

---

## S#2 — Same Courtyard. Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael walks twenty paces. Transforms. Same forty-five-second sequence — court watches.

Isolde climbs his foreleg. Settles in the curve of his neck against his shoulder.

He lifts off. Two arcs over the courtyard — slow — and then east.

Halren follows on the road with her escort. Will be three days behind.

[KEY CAMERA]
Wide aerial of the courtyard as the dragon lifts. Court watching.

[VO]
ISOLDE (V.O., over the flight): My father is dead. My sister wears a circlet. The kingdom that threw me away has bowed to me. I am flying home on the wing of the man it threw me to. I do not understand most of this. I do not need to. Home is east now.

---

## S#3 — Flight. Two Days Compressed.

[MONTAGE]
Late afternoon — flying east over the plain.
Night — landed in a forest. He folds around her. She sleeps against his neck.
Dawn — flying again. Mountains visible.
Mid-day — flying over the dragon ridge. The ridge dragons open their eyes briefly. Permission. Close them.
Late afternoon — Drakonis Keep visible.

---

## S#4 — Drakonis Keep. Courtyard. Late Afternoon.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He lands in the courtyard. Kiran at the gate. The keep is in formation — every dragon house, every keep servant, every guard. They have known the dragon was returning since the eastern ridge opened its eyes an hour ago.

Vael transforms back. Black coat. Walks beside Isolde.

[DIALOGUE]
KIRAN: My lord. My lady. The keep welcomes you home.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
The whole courtyard bows. Not formal — relieved. They were not sure she would come back.

Isolde walks the courtyard. Slow. Looks at the faces. She knows half of them by name now. Renn the young dragon is at the front of the children's row, his arm still in the splint. He grins when she sees him.

She lifts her chin to him. He goes red.

[KEY CAMERA]
Slow tracking Isolde walking the courtyard.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (to Kiran, quiet): Anything I need to know.
KIRAN: My lady. Two things.
ISOLDE: Tell me inside.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She walks into the keep. Vael at her shoulder.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
SUPER on Drakonis Keep: HOME.

[END HOOK]
Inside the great hall, Kiran sets down two folded letters on the table.

[DIALOGUE]
KIRAN: The first is from Sera. She is in the south. She asks to swear to you in person before the child is born.
ISOLDE: ...
KIRAN: The second is from the elder. The one who came alone. He died on the road four days ago. He left a single line. For you.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Kiran hands Isolde the second letter. She opens it.

[INSERT — single line audience-visible:]
> Walk well, my lady. Watch the east. — Renar of the Old Council.

She closes it. Hand to her belly.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE: Watch the east.
VAEL: We will.

Hard Cut.
