# THE OFFERING — EP39 (v30) — LABOR (PART 2)

## S#1 — Chamber. The Push.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She pushes. Long. Hard. Eyes closed.

Vael's hand crushed in hers. Both his other hands — yes, two now, scales raised — at her belly and her sternum. He has stayed human. Barely.

She breathes out. Halren at the foot, working.

[DIALOGUE]
HALREN: Again. Next breath.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She pushes again. Longer. Sound through her teeth — rough, low. The bed shifts.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (against her hair): Breathe.
ISOLDE: I am.
VAEL: One more.
ISOLDE: I cannot —
VAEL: One more.

---

## S#2 — Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Another push. Her grip on his wrist tightens to bone. Audience-only: bruise from elbow to fingertips. He does not move his arm.

Halren's voice changes — sharper, faster.

[DIALOGUE]
HALREN: Head. My lady. Head.
VAEL: ...
HALREN: Push.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She pushes again. Hard. The bed shifts again.

She does not breathe for a long beat. Then she does.

[DIALOGUE]
HALREN: One more.

---

## S#3 — Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She pushes — last time.

A high small sound — newborn cry.

Halren's hands working fast. Linen. Clean knife.

[DIALOGUE]
HALREN: A girl. A girl. My lord. My lady. A girl.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She wraps the child in clean linen. Does not lift her yet. Waits.

---

## S#4 — Continuous.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Vael does not look at the child first. He looks at Isolde's face. He lifts her wet hair off her cheek with his scaled fingers. Scales softening as they touch her skin.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: You are still here.
ISOLDE: I am still here.
VAEL: You.
ISOLDE: Me.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
He puts his forehead against hers. Long count. His scales slowly slide back down his throat. Face shifts back fully human. Eyes still full red — but the bone at the temple stops shifting.

After a long beat he lifts his face.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (to Halren, low): On her.
HALREN: My lord.
VAEL: On her chest. Don't lift the child until I say.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
Halren sets the wrapped child on Isolde's chest. Skin to skin at the collar where the silk has been pulled open.

The child stops crying once she is against her mother.

Vael looks down at her for the first time. Mireille opens her eyes once. Grey-green. Same as Isolde. Same as Aldric.

She closes them.

[KEY CAMERA]
Held close. The three of them. Forehead to forehead. The child between.

[DIALOGUE]
ISOLDE (very quiet, against his forehead): She has your hands.
VAEL: She has your eyes.
ISOLDE: She has your jaw.
VAEL: She has both of us.

[GRAPHIC/UI]
SUPER, small at the corner: MIREILLE — BORN.

[END HOOK]
The midwife stays at the foot of the bed, head down, waiting.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL (without looking at the midwife): Out.
HALREN: My lord — the cord —
VAEL: Cut the cord. Then out. Kiran walks you to the south wing. You stay there until I send for you.
HALREN: Yes, my lord.

[VISUAL/ACTION]
She cuts the cord. Walks to the door without lifting her face. Kiran opens it from outside. Walks her out. Closes it.

The chamber door locks from inside.

The three of them are alone.

Hard Cut.
