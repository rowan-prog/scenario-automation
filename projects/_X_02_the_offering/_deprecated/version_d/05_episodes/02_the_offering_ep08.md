# THE OFFERING — EP08: Say the Word

S#1 — KEEP / GREAT HALL / DAY

[Visual]
The Great Hall in full assembly. The iron throne. The low iron bench raised onto a **second, taller step** beside it overnight — equal height to the throne's seat, not below.
**The new envoy** at the foot of the dais, double-silver-crown sigil at the breast, iron staff in hand. Behind him, **the four armored escorts in grey livery, sigil shields raised — and eight more behind them, twelve in total, the second envoy's full escort, the human crown's second seal column**.
HALDREN to one side — envoy chain hanging open, his cloak pinned with **a single drake-feather** at the collar (new).
ELARA at the far edge of the dais — pushed back two steps from her old mark, **gold cuff loose at her throat**.
KIRAN at the foot of the dais, hand on his sword hilt — not drawn.
A **rolled second human-kingdom standard** lies on the basalt between VAEL's dais and the envoy — fresh white linen, the **double-silver-crown sigil** at its center.
VAEL on the iron throne, scaled hand resting on the iron arm. ISOLDE not yet in the hall.
The far doors open.

S#2 — KEEP / GREAT HALL / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
VAEL stands. He walks down the three steps. The hall does not breathe.
He stops at the rolled second standard. He sets his **black boot heel on the double-silver-crown sigil** the same way as EP04.
He does not open his palm yet.
He looks up — past the envoy, past the twelve escorts — at the **upper iron arches** of the hall.
The arches are open to the night sky beyond.
Above them, far up — a **wing-shadow** crosses once.
VAEL opens his **left scaled palm** flat over the rolled standard. The **dark-red flame** drops in a slow, low curtain. The standard catches. The double-silver-crown sigil chars first — black eats outward.
The **back of VAEL's scaled hand** — now in plain sight of the entire hall — **lights from inside**, a deep red-gold glow blooming along the scales. The glow is the **same color and rhythm** as the trace on ISOLDE's wrist.

S#3 — KEEP / GREAT HALL / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
The far doors open.
ISOLDE steps in alone. **Vael's Choice silk — but altered overnight: the black silk gown now has fine silver-and-pearl trim down the shoulder seams, the silver chain at the collarbones replaced with a wider band of pearl and silver, hair half-pinned with pearl at the crown, the rest flowing loose. The two stacked crescent bites at her neck deliberately uncovered — chain band cut low to show them. Wrist-glow steady and bright, the dark-red trace now from heel of palm to mid-forearm.**
She walks the basalt floor alone — past the twelve escorts, past the new envoy, past KIRAN, past ELARA.
She stops at the foot of the dais.
VAEL turns to her. He steps toward her — and **takes her waist with the black-scaled hand** the same way as EP02 — but this time he does not lift her up the three steps.
He lifts her **up the four steps** — onto the **raised iron bench**, throne-height.
He sets her down. Then he **takes her left hand**, the wrist-glow hand, and lifts it palm-up between them. The wrist-glow ignites against his scaled hand-back glow — **two glows touching, two rhythms locked**.
He **turns her by the waist** so her body faces the new envoy and the twelve escorts squarely.
He puts his **right scaled hand into her hair at the nape — a thick fist** — and pulls her head **back and to one side**, exposing the side of her neck with the two crescent bites to the full hall.
He **leans in** — his **mouth a thumb's width** from her bared neck.
He does not bite. Not yet. He **breathes against the crescents** once.
ISOLDE has not closed her eyes. She is looking at the new envoy.

S#4 — KEEP / GREAT HALL / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
VAEL lifts his mouth off her neck. His face is still beside her face. The hand in her hair stays.
He turns — by the waist — and pulls her **half against his chest**, so the **side of her neck with the two crescents** is still angled to the hall, and her **wrist-glow hand** is up in front of them — **palm to palm** with his scaled hand-back glow.
He opens his mouth to speak.
He begins —

VAEL: She is —

A **roar** breaks across the sky outside the upper iron arches.
Not one roar. **Many**. Dragon-throat sub-bass — three, four, more — overlapping, settling over the keep from above. The basalt **shakes**. Dust falls from the arches.
The twelve human-kingdom escorts — every one — **drop to one knee**. Sigil shields go down to the basalt with twelve dry thuds. The new envoy's iron staff slips half from his grip and clacks against the stone.
HALDREN does not kneel — he was already half-bent.
ELARA crumples a step backward into the wall.
VAEL has not finished the sentence. **The word after "is" never comes.**
His scaled hand-back glow has flared bright enough to throw red-gold across her face.
ISOLDE has not flinched at the roar.
She **lifts her own free hand** — the right one — and **takes the back of VAEL's scaled hand**. Her fingers close over the glowing scales.
She lifts his scaled hand off her waist — and **brings it up to her mouth**.
She **presses her lips to the back of his scaled hand** — directly over the deep red-gold glow on the scales.
Held.
His scaled hand-back glow **flares again under her mouth** — and **does not fade**. The glow on her wrist **brightens to match**, and **stays**. Both glows stay lit, paired, after she lifts her mouth.

S#5 — KEEP / GREAT HALL / END

[Visual]
Held tableau, after the roar.
The hall floor: **twelve escorts on one knee, sigil shields flat to the basalt**. The new envoy's iron staff fallen sideways across the floor. ELARA against the wall, eyes shut. HALDREN still half-bent. KIRAN standing — and **slowly going to one knee himself**, head lowered.
The dais: **VAEL on the raised step beside the iron bench. ISOLDE on the iron bench at throne-height. His scaled hand still in her right hand, lifted between them, the glow on the back of his hand still bright under the last memory of her mouth. Her wrist-glow lit and locked to it. The two stacked crescent bites at her neck angled to the hall.**
The word VAEL did not finish has not been spoken.
The hall holds.
The dragon-roar settles into a low ground-shaking hum that does not stop.
