# THE OFFERING — EP02: Beside Me

S#1 — KEEP / GUEST CHAMBER / NIGHT

[Visual]
A wide guest chamber in basalt, low warm lamps. A tall mirror in iron frame.
ISOLDE stands at the foot of the mirror in the Royal Arrival ivory gown. The white fur cloak is over her shoulder.
At the mirror's foot, **the silver chain with her mother's signet pendant lies coiled on a small basalt shelf — she has just unpinned it from the Royal Arrival cloak**.
A row of three pale maids enter with folded silks across their arms — **the human-kingdom mourning silks her father's escort delivered**: drab grey, stiff, with the human crown's mourning sigil pinned at the collar. The maids spread the silks on the bed.
ISOLDE looks at the mourning silks. She does not move.
The door behind her opens once. **VAEL — first full reveal: tall, black-leather riding coat, long black cloak over one shoulder, dark hair tied back at the nape, hard angular jaw, grey eyes, the black-scaled hand at his side** — steps in two paces and stops. He looks at the silks on the bed. Then at her.
He turns his face to the youngest maid.

MAID: My lord. The silks the human escort sent.
VAEL: Burn them.
ISOLDE: I won't wear what they sent.
VAEL: Then you won't.
MAID: My lord, what does she —
VAEL: Black silk. Pearl. Silver. Bring it.

S#2 — KEEP / GREAT HALL / EVENING

[Visual]
The Great Hall is fuller tonight: fifteen retainers in dark leather along both walls. **ELARA — golden hair coiled, ivory and gold silk gown, gold cuff at the throat** — stands near the iron throne, one step closer to it than yesterday.
**KIRAN — silver cuirass, black armband of the drake house, hard build** — stands at the foot of the dais.
VAEL sits on the iron throne. His left hand rests on the throne's arm. The **black-scaled fingers** lift, once — and tap the iron arm in a slow, four-beat rhythm. Wait. Wait. Wait. Wait.
ELARA notices. Her smile sharpens. She mistakes the rhythm for impatience and **steps half a foot closer to the throne, ready to be seated**.
VAEL's fingers stop tapping. He does not look at ELARA. He looks at the hall's far doors.
KIRAN follows his gaze and shifts his stance.

[DIALOGUE]
ELARA: (low, to KIRAN) He's calling.
KIRAN: Not for you.
ELARA: Then for —
VAEL: (without looking at her) Step back.

S#3 — KEEP / GREAT HALL / CONTINUOUS

[Visual]
The far doors open.
ISOLDE steps in alone. **Vael's Choice look — first reveal: black silk gown, ivory and pearl trim down the bodice, a fine silver chain across the collarbones, a thin silver cuff at one wrist, dark-brown hair half-pinned with pearl, the rest loose down her back.** The white fur cloak is gone. Her mother's signet pendant has been moved to the **inner lining of the new gown's bodice** — invisible to the hall, but its outline catches the lamp-light against the silk for one breath as she crosses the floor. Her **inner wrist glow** is faintly visible above the silver cuff in the lamp-light.
The hall stops moving.
ELARA's gold cuff lowers half an inch. KIRAN's mouth opens, then closes.
ISOLDE walks the length of the basalt floor on her own. She stops at the foot of the throne dais.
VAEL stands.
He does not speak. He steps down to her level — three steps down the dais. He takes her **waist** with the black-scaled hand. The fingers are gentle but the grip closes.
He lifts her up the three steps, one motion, and **sets her down beside him** — to the right of the iron throne. The seat there is a low iron bench that was not there yesterday.
The hall watches.
VAEL's free hand finds **one strand of her loose hair** at her temple, lifts it, and lets it fall — once, slow. His thumb grazes the inner-wrist glow as the hand withdraws.
A second **faint crescent glow** prints on her inner wrist, deeper than yesterday's. It does not fade as fast.

[DIALOGUE]
VAEL: Beside me.
ISOLDE: That's not a question.
VAEL: No.

S#4 — KEEP / GREAT HALL / NIGHT

[Visual]
ELARA's smile is gone. She stares at the new iron bench. KIRAN watches the wrist glow on ISOLDE's arm and writes nothing down.
VAEL has retaken the throne. His scaled hand rests now on the iron arm closest to ISOLDE's bench — palm down, fingers slightly toward her.
ISOLDE looks at her own inner wrist for the first time tonight. The glow there is **two-thirds the size of his hand** — a small crescent, faint but stable.
She turns her wrist over, palm up, and lets it sit on her own thigh, glow up.
VAEL sees it.
His scaled fingers slide, once, across the iron arm — toward her bench — and stop **a hand-span** short of her wrist.

End Image: VAEL's scaled fingers stopped a hand-span from ISOLDE's wrist-glow, both visible in one tight frame. ELARA half-shadowed at the throne's far side, mouth open. KIRAN expressionless.
