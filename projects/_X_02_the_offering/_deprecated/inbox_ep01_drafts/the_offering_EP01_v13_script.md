## EP1 - S#2  Night / Interior / Veine. Fire-Festival House. Same Night.
Characters: Isolde, Vael, Lord Corvin (memory), King Aldric (memory), festival crowd

△ A private festival house. Rain outside, crowd inside, lanterns over the floor.

ISOLDE (VO): The spell on this house has one rule. Walk out, and you forget every face. Everyone forgets yours.

△ Isolde stands alone at the edge of the crowd. No escort, no crown. Rain-dark wine silk under a charcoal velvet cloak, fitted at the waist. Rain in her hair.

On-screen text: ISOLDE / king's unwanted daughter

△ [Flash cut — memory] Corvin's ringed hand lifts Isolde's chin while he speaks over her to Aldric.

LORD CORVIN (memory, to Aldric): Don't worry about her temper, Your Majesty. A few nights in my bed will fix that.

△ Back to Isolde in the festival crowd.

ISOLDE (VO): After my mother died, I stopped getting choices. In a month, they sell me to Corvin. Not tonight. Tonight I choose who touches me.

△ [Insert] A marriage contract — Corvin's name, a wedding date one month away, a wax seal beside Isolde's name.

On-screen text: SOLD TO LORD CORVIN / ONE MONTH LEFT

△ Across the hall, Vael sees Isolde. Their eyes meet.

△ Vael walks straight to Isolde. Men shift aside before they know why.

△ [KEY] Firelit crowd. Vael's eyes, Isolde's eyes, his hand near hers.

VAEL (low, watching her mouth): You keep looking at me.
ISOLDE (breathless, almost smiling): So do you.
VAEL (low): Then come with me. Upstairs.
ISOLDE: You say that to every girl here?
VAEL: No.
ISOLDE: Why me?
VAEL (low): I came here for a reason. Then I saw you.

△ Isolde's smile fades. Her eyes drop to Vael's mouth. She does not step back.

△ Vael steps closer. His coat brushes Isolde's skirt. He still does not touch her.

ISOLDE: And if I go upstairs with you?
VAEL: I close the door.
ISOLDE: Then?
VAEL (low): Then tell me what you want first.

△ Isolde's hand closes around Vael's sleeve. She pulls him toward the stairs herself.

ISOLDE (VO): No real names. No titles.

△ Vael ignores the crowd. He only looks at Isolde.
