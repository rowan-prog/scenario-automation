# THE OFFERING (My One-Night Stand Is the Dragon Lord Who Bought Me) — EP01

---

 1-1  Night / Interior / Drakonis Keep. Vael's Chamber. Storm. Flash-Forward.
Characters: Isolde, Vael

△ Rain on the black window.
△ Isolde on her back on the bed. Vael over her, braced on straight arms, shirt open.
△ Lightning. Vael's face lit clear — sharp jaw, dark eyes, wet hair.
△ Vael's right fist grips the sheet by Isolde's head.

VAEL (low, rough): Tell me to stop.

△ Isolde grips Vael's forearm. She does not push him off.

ISOLDE (VO): It rained that night too.
ISOLDE (VO): The first night I chose.

On-screen text: WEEKS EARLIER

---

 1-2  Night / Interior / Veine. Fire-Festival House. The First Night.
Characters: Isolde, Vael · (memory insert: Lord Corvin, King Aldric)

△ A crowded festival house. Rain on the windows. Firelight, music, wine cups.
△ Isolde stands alone at the edge of the crowd. Charcoal cloak, dark wine silk, wet hair.

On-screen text: ISOLDE / KING'S UNWANTED DAUGHTER

ISOLDE (VO): The spell on this house has one rule. Walk out, and you forget every face. Everyone forgets yours.

△ Memory insert — a banquet hall. Lord Corvin grips Isolde's chin and turns her face up. King Aldric stands beside Corvin and says nothing.

LORD CORVIN (memory, to Aldric): Don't worry about her temper, Your Majesty. A few nights in my bed will fix that.

△ Back in the festival house. Isolde watches the crowd.

ISOLDE (VO): After my mother died, I stopped getting choices. In a month, they sell me to Corvin. Not tonight. Tonight I choose who touches me.

△ Across the hall, Vael watches Isolde. He stands a head taller than the men around him.
△ Their eyes meet. Vael crosses the crowd to her.
△ Vael stops in front of Isolde. His hand lifts and stops just short of her wrist.

VAEL (low): You keep looking at me.
ISOLDE (breathless, almost smiling): So do you.
VAEL (low): Then come with me. Upstairs.
ISOLDE (testing): You say that to every girl here?
VAEL: No.
ISOLDE: Why me?
VAEL (low): I came here for a reason. Then I saw you.

△ Isolde's eyes drop to his mouth. She does not step back.
△ Vael steps in. His coat brushes her skirt. He does not touch her.

ISOLDE: And if I go upstairs with you?
VAEL: I close the door.
ISOLDE: Then?
VAEL (low): Then tell me what you want first.

△ Isolde takes his sleeve and pulls him toward the stairs.

 1-3  Night / Interior / Small Room Above the Festival. The Night She Chose.
Characters: Isolde, Vael

△ The door bangs open. Isolde and Vael come in kissing. Vael kicks the door shut.
△ Vael backs Isolde to the wall beside the bed. She pulls his mouth back to hers.

VAEL (against her mouth): Too fast?
ISOLDE (breathless): Not fast enough.

△ Vael pins both her wrists to the wall above her head. Her body lifts into his.
△ He lifts her by the thighs. Her legs lock around his waist. He carries her to the bed and lowers her down.
△ Isolde pushes his shirt off his shoulders. Her palms run down his bare chest.

△ [INSERT/ECU] Black scale flashes on Vael's wrist — gone before Isolde sees it.

△ Isolde pulls Vael down by the open shirt. Her dress slides off her shoulders.

△ Vael stops. Holds over her. Not moving.

VAEL (rough): Tell me to stop.
ISOLDE (breathless): Don't you dare.

△ Isolde and Vael have sex. Rain on the dark window.

△ [JUMP CUT — bed center] Vael over Isolde, her knee hooked high at his side. Her back lifts off the sheet.

ISOLDE (breathless): Look at me.

△ Vael's eyes lock on hers. He does not look away.

△ [JUMP CUT — bed edge] Isolde pulls Vael down by the shirt. Her heel drags down the back of his thigh. Her ankles lock at his waist.
△ [KEY] Vael's broad chest and shoulders over Isolde.
△ [WIDE — 2-shot] Both bare in the rainlight. Vael's back over Isolde, her body arched under him.

[END HOOK]
△ Isolde's back arches off the bed. Rain hard on the window.
△ [INSERT/ECU] The black scale stands up on Vael's wrist beside Isolde's cheek. Isolde's eyes are shut. She does not see it.
