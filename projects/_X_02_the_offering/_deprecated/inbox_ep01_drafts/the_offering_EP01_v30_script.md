## EP1 - S#2  Night / Interior / Veine Fire Festival. Festival House. Storm.
Characters: Isolde, Vael, Lord Corvin (memory), King Aldric (memory), festival crowd

△ Inside a private festival house. Rain beats the shutters. Lanterns swing above masks, wine cups, and wet cloaks.

ISOLDE (VO): The spell on this house has one rule. Walk out, and you forget every face. Everyone forgets yours.

△ Isolde stands alone at the edge of the crowd: no escort, no crown, rain-dark wine silk under a charcoal velvet cloak.

On-screen text: ISOLDE / KING'S UNWANTED DAUGHTER

△ [Memory flash] Lord Corvin's ringed hand lifts Isolde's chin while Corvin speaks over her to King Aldric.

△ King Aldric sits on the dais, one hand on the throne arm, watching Corvin without moving.

LORD CORVIN (memory, to Aldric): Don't worry about her temper, Your Majesty. A few nights in my bed will fix that.

△ Back in the festival crowd. Isolde's jaw tightens.

ISOLDE (VO): After my mother died, I stopped getting choices. In a month, they hand me to Corvin. Not tonight. Tonight I choose who touches me.

△ Isolde looks across the hall.

△ Across the firelit crowd, Vael sees Isolde.

△ Isolde and Vael look at each other through the moving bodies.

△ Vael walks straight toward Isolde. Men in masks step aside and open a path for Vael.

△ Vael stops close in front of Isolde. His hand lifts, then stops an inch from Isolde's wrist.

VAEL (low, watching her mouth): You keep looking at me.
ISOLDE (breathless, almost smiling): So do you.
VAEL (low): Then come with me. Upstairs.
ISOLDE (testing): You say that to every girl here?
VAEL: No.
ISOLDE: Why me?
VAEL (low): I came here for a reason. Then I saw you.

△ Isolde's smile fades. Her gaze drops to Vael's mouth.

△ Vael steps closer until his coat brushes Isolde's skirt. He still does not touch her.

ISOLDE: And if I go upstairs with you?
VAEL: I close the door.
ISOLDE: Then?
VAEL (low): Then tell me what you want first.

△ Isolde grips Vael's sleeve.

△ Isolde pulls Vael toward the stairs. Vael follows her without looking back at the crowd.

△ A few masked guests glance over, then turn back to the music. No one calls out a name or title.
