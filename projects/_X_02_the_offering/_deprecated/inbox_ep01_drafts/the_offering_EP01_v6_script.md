# THE OFFERING (My One-Night Stand Is the Dragon Lord Who Bought Me) — EP01

## 1-1  Night / Interior / Drakonis Keep. Vael's Chamber. Storm. Flash-Forward.
Characters: Isolde, Vael

△ Rain hammers the tall black window. Lightning throws the room white, then drops it back to dark.
△ Isolde lies back across dark sheets. Vael braces over Isolde on straight arms — open shirt hanging off his shoulders, bare chest above her.
△ [Tight 2-shot] The narrow gap between their bodies. Vael's right fist twists into the sheet beside Isolde's head.
△ Lightning catches Vael's face — wet hair, dark eyes, sharp jaw.

VAEL (low, rough): Tell me to stop.

△ Isolde's fingers curl around Vael's forearm, holding him there instead of pushing him off.

ISOLDE (VO): It rained that night too.
ISOLDE (VO): The first night I chose.

On-screen text: WEEKS EARLIER

## 1-2  Night / Interior / Veine. Fire-Festival House. The First Night.
Characters: Isolde, Vael, Lord Corvin (memory), King Aldric (memory), festival crowd

△ Inside a crowded festival house. Rain streaks the shutters. Lanterns swing over the crowd; firelight, wine cups, wet cloaks.
△ Isolde stands alone at the edge of the crowd — no escort, no crown. Charcoal velvet cloak over rain-dark wine silk, rain still in her hair. The crowd laughs too loud around her.

On-screen text: ISOLDE / KING'S UNWANTED DAUGHTER

ISOLDE (VO): The spell on this house has one rule. Walk out, and you forget every face. Everyone forgets yours.

△ [Memory flash] A banquet hall. Lord Corvin's ringed hand grips Isolde's chin and tilts her face up. On the dais, King Aldric watches and does not stop Corvin.

LORD CORVIN (memory, to Aldric): Don't worry about her temper, Your Majesty. A few nights in my bed will fix that.

△ Back in the festival crowd. Isolde's jaw sets.

ISOLDE (VO): After my mother died, I stopped getting choices. In a month, they sell me to Corvin. Not tonight. Tonight I choose who touches me.

△ [Insert] A marriage contract — Corvin's name, a wedding date one month off, a wax seal pressed beside Isolde's name.

On-screen text: SOLD TO LORD CORVIN / ONE MONTH LEFT

△ Isolde lifts her eyes from the contract and looks across the hall.
△ Across the firelit crowd, Vael walks straight at Isolde — men shift aside before they know why. He stands a head taller than them. Lantern light holds his face: dark eyes, wet hair, sharp jaw.
△ Vael stops close in front of Isolde. His hand lifts, stops a breath short of her wrist — not touching.

VAEL (low, watching her mouth): You keep looking at me.
ISOLDE (breathless, almost smiling): So do you.
VAEL (low): Then come with me. Upstairs.
ISOLDE (testing): You say that to every girl here?
VAEL: No.
ISOLDE: Why me?
VAEL (low): I came here for a reason. Then I saw you.

△ Isolde's gaze drops to Vael's mouth. Isolde does not step back.
△ Vael steps in until his coat brushes her skirt. Vael still does not touch her.

ISOLDE: And if I go upstairs with you?
VAEL: I close the door.
ISOLDE: Then?
VAEL (low): Then tell me what you want first.

△ Isolde closes her hand around Vael's sleeve. She pulls first; Vael follows her toward the stairs without looking back at the crowd.

## 1-3  Night / Interior / Small Room Above the Festival. The Night She Chose.
Characters: Isolde, Vael

△ The door bangs open. Isolde and Vael come in already kissing — rainlight behind them, firelight inside. Vael kicks the door shut and the festival music below cuts off.
△ Vael walks Isolde backward and presses her shoulders to the wall beside the bed. Isolde fists both hands in his collar and drags his mouth harder onto hers.

VAEL (against her mouth): Too fast?
ISOLDE (breathless): Not fast enough.

△ Vael catches both of Isolde's wrists and pins them to the wall above her head. Isolde arches off the wall into him, eyes open.
△ Vael grips the backs of Isolde's thighs and lifts her from the wall. Her legs lock around his waist.
△ [Camera tight on their bodies, not the room] Vael carries Isolde three steps to the bed and lowers her onto the dark sheets, following her down.
△ Isolde pushes Vael's open shirt off his shoulders and drags both palms down his bare chest. Her fingers catch on hard ridges at his wrist.

△ [Insert / ECU] Black scales surface on Vael's wrist, half-hidden under Isolde's fingers — gone in an instant.

ISOLDE (VO): Something's wrong under my hand.

△ Isolde hooks that hand behind Vael's neck and pulls him down anyway. She is not being pulled under; she is choosing him.
△ Isolde's cloak drops to the floor. Her dress slides off her shoulders.

△ Vael goes still over Isolde, holding his weight off her. The bed creaks once, then stills.

VAEL (rough): Tell me to stop.
ISOLDE (breathless): Don't you dare.

△ Isolde fists a hand in Vael's hair and pulls his mouth back to hers.

## 1-4  Night / Interior / Small Room Above the Festival. Bed. The Sex.
Characters: Isolde, Vael

△ Rain sheets down the black window. Firelight moves over the bed and the clothes on the floor.
△ Isolde and Vael have sex on the bed.
△ [Jump cut — bed center] Vael moves over Isolde, one hand hooking her knee high at his side. Isolde's back lifts off the sheet; her open mouth drags against his shoulder.

ISOLDE (breathless): Look at me.

△ Vael's eyes lock on Isolde's. Vael does not look away while his body keeps moving over hers.

△ [Jump cut — bed edge] Isolde pulls Vael down by the open shirt. Her heel drags down the back of his thigh; her ankles cross and lock at the small of his back.
△ [Wide 2-shot, storm-lit] Both bare in the rainlight — Vael's broad back and shoulders working over Isolde, her body arched under him, her eyes still open.
△ [Insert / ECU] Vael's left fist clenches in the sheet until black scales stand up along his wrist.
