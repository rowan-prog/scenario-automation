 1-1  Night / Int.  Drakonis Keep · Vael's Chamber  (flash-forward · storm)
CHARACTERS: Vael, Isolde

△ Rain hammers the narrow black window in sheets. Water snakes down the glass.
△ Isolde lies back across dark sheets, hair fanned out, throat bare. Vael braces over her on straight arms — open shirt off his shoulders, bare chest slick, breath dragging rough.
△ Vael's right fist twists into the sheet beside Isolde's head. Knuckles white.
△ Lightning cracks. The white flash floods his open shirt and the hard line of his jaw — the rest of his face stays drowned in the dark.

VAEL (low, rough): Tell me to stop.

△ Isolde's fingers curl around Vael's forearm and dig in. She doesn't push him off.

ISOLDE (V.O.): This started the night I finally got to choose. I chose a stranger. I had no idea what he was.

 1-2  Night / Int.  Veine · Fire-Festival House  (she picks a stranger)
CHARACTERS: Isolde, Vael · (memory: Lord Corvin, King Aldric)

△ A low-beamed festival house. Rain drums the shutters. Paper lanterns sway and drag moving light across a packed, sweating crowd.
△ Isolde stands backed to the wall at the crowd's edge, a half-full cup forgotten in her hand. Charcoal cloak, wine-dark silk cinched at the waist, rain still beading in her loose hair.

> ISOLDE · the king's unwanted daughter

ISOLDE (V.O.): One rule in this house — walk out that door and every face in here is gone. Yours too.

△ [FLASH · memory] A banquet hall. Lord Corvin's ringed hand clamps Isolde's chin and tilts her face up while he talks past her to King Aldric on the dais.

LORD CORVIN (memory, to Aldric): Don't worry about her temper, Majesty. A few nights in my bed fixes that.

△ Back to the festival. Isolde's jaw sets. She drains the cup and knocks it down onto a passing tray.

ISOLDE (V.O.): In a month they hand me to Corvin. Tonight — for once — I choose who touches me.

△ Across the floor, a tall man in black stops mid-stride. Lantern light catches his shoulders and the edge of his jaw; the low beams keep the rest of his face in shadow. He cuts straight through the crowd to Isolde.
△ [KEY] Firelight between them. His eyes lock on hers. His hand lifts and stops a breath short of her bare wrist — not touching.

VAEL (face unseen, low): You keep looking at me.
ISOLDE (breathless, almost smiling): So do you.
VAEL (face unseen, low): Then come upstairs with me.
ISOLDE (testing him): You say that to every girl here?
VAEL (face unseen): No.
ISOLDE: Why me?
VAEL (face unseen, low): I came here for one reason. Then I saw you.

△ Isolde's almost-smile drops. Her gaze falls to his mouth and stays. She doesn't step back.
△ The man steps in until the front of his coat brushes her skirt. He still doesn't lay a hand on her.

ISOLDE: And if I go upstairs with you?
VAEL (face unseen): I close the door.
ISOLDE: Then?
VAEL (face unseen, low): Then you tell me what you want first.

△ Isolde closes her fist around his sleeve and pulls him through the crowd toward the back stairs, leading.

ISOLDE (V.O.): He keeps asking what I want. Nobody's asked me that since my mother died.

 1-3  Night / Int.  Small Room Above the Festival  (the one night she chose)
CHARACTERS: Isolde, Vael

△ The door bangs open against the wall. Isolde and Vael stumble in already kissing, mouths open. Vael kicks the door shut behind him.

△ Vael shoves one hand into her wet hair and presses his whole body into her against the wall.

VAEL (face unseen, against her mouth): Too fast?
ISOLDE (breathless): Not fast enough.

△ Vael catches both of Isolde's wrists and pins them to the wall above her head in one hand. Her chest lifts off the wall into his.

ISOLDE (V.O.): I don't know his name. I don't want to. I just want one night that's mine.
