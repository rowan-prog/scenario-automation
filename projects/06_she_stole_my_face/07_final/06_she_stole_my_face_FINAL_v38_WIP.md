# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP02

On the screen: Cross Manor. A podium. ETHAN. And beside him — Lena's own face, smiling into the live camera. Chyron: *STERLING HEIRESS ENGAGED — LIVE NOW.*

# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP04

A side gate opens for security. Lena slips through with the press pool.

Ethan steps out. Mara behind him — Lena's gown, Lena's pendant — pressing close, performing fragile.

MARA
(soft, to Ethan)
She's scaring me. How does she even know our names —

LENA
Ethan. It's me. The pendant is mine. Look inside it — LUMEN MEUM. Look.

ETHAN
Stop saying that.

VICTORIA glides in. Cold. Crack — she backhands Lena. The bandage tears open. Blood.

VICTORIA
(public, cold)
I have no idea who you are. And you're bleeding on my steps.

MARA
(to the crowd, sweet)
She used to be my friend. I don't know what happened to her.

Mara lifts the pendant to her lips. Kisses it. For the cameras.

Two guards drag Lena down the wet stone. Forty phones.

I'm LENA STERLING —

Someone who knows you're not crazy.

She gets in.

# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP05

## S#1. Lena's Apartment · Day

Lived-in by someone else. Mara's perfume. Mara's jacket on the chair. Lena's closet — her own clothes gone, a row of new ones in Mara's taste.

At the very back, behind a false panel her mother built herself — Helena's wedding dress. Untouched. Mara didn't take it. Didn't know it was there.

Lena lifts it down. Zips it into a garment bag.

LENA
(low)
Whatever else they take. Not this.

## S#2. Living Room · Continuous

The front door unlocks from the outside. MARA walks in — Lena's old key, Lena's pendant — like she lives here.

MARA
(soft, like nothing happened)
You're hard to keep up with.

She sits on Lena's couch. Crosses her legs. Sips Lena's water.

MARA
(stands, gentle)
Good girl.

At the door she lifts the pendant off her own neck. Kisses it. In front of Lena.

MARA
Take it back if you can. It'll never feel like yours again.

She walks out.

# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP08 [PAYWALL]

## S#1. Bridal Room C → Ballroom · Continuous

The door bursts. Two of Victoria's guards. Mara on the floor, performing collapse, blood on her mouth.

MARA
(weeping)
She came at me — please — get her out —

Cuffs. They drag Lena down the staff corridor — which cuts behind the ballroom. Quartet playing. A room full of guests. Mara, brought through the private passage, already on the blessing dais beside Ethan.

Lena throws her body sideways. One guard's grip slips. Three seconds. She crashes through the ballroom doors.

## S#2. Ballroom · Continuous

The music stops. Every face turns. Lena — catering uniform, cuffed, blood on her lip, hair half out of the wig.

LENA
(shouting, voice cracking)
I'M LENA STERLING! THAT WOMAN HAD SURGERY TO LOOK LIKE ME! SHE STOLE MY FACE!

Silence. Forks in the air. Phones up.

Mara, at the podium, doesn't move for one full second. Then —

MARA
(into the mic, breaking voice)
Oh god. Somebody help her.

She steps off the dais, walks toward Lena like a friend toward a wounded animal. Tears already on her cheeks — real-looking.

MARA
(soft, all sweetness)
She's been off her meds. Her mother died and she never came back. Please — don't film her like this —

[KEY CAMERA]
- WIDE Mara's open hands reaching for Lena · the perfect picture of a worried friend
- ECU phones lifting everywhere · catching Mara's compassion + Lena's screaming
- ECU Victoria at the rail · already nodding · already sad

Guards drag Lena back. Her heel scrapes a long ugly streak across the parquet.

LENA
(screaming as she's pulled out)
ASK HER WHAT THE PENDANT SAYS — ASK HER —

The doors close. Music resumes. Mara already at Ethan's side, sobbing into his shoulder.

## S#3. Noah's Penthouse · Late Night

Lena on the kitchen floor, phone in hand. Live feed.

[INSERT] Mara at the Cross Manor press balcony, a neat strip over the split lip.

(soft, brave)
I just want her to get help. (beat — into the camera) Lena. If you can hear this. I love you. Come home.

MARA (ON SCREEN)
(sweet)
I'll wear my mother's pendant down the aisle. She'd want me to.

LENA
She just called my dead mother hers. On live TV. And they clapped.

(low)
I know.

LENA
She wants to be me on camera. In front of the whole country.

She's terrified of it. Now every lens in the city's pointed at her. So I stop screaming that I'm Lena. I make HER drop the mask on one of those cameras — where they all have to watch.
