## 1. Production Authority

Production may refine or adjust staging, blocking, specified camera shots, pacing, and visual detail when it better serves the scene's core purpose and impact, especially in key payoff, reveal, intimacy, reversal, or cliffhanger scenes. The intended story result, relationship beat, emotional result, and continuity must remain intact. Dialogue must be followed as written; any dialogue change must be reported in advance.

## 2. Core Pleasure

Primary screen appeal: a stolen face, a world that worships the thief, and the real woman they all call crazy - until the fake breaks herself on camera.

- The pleasure is dramatic irony. The audience sees the whole face-swap in EP01-02. We know; the world doesn't. Never shoot it as a whodunit.
- The fake wins because she is shameless and the world is dumb - not because she is clever. Tone is makjang: broad, brazen, absurd AND infuriating. Do not make it tasteful, cool, or prestige.
- Cruelty is relational and intimate, not spectacle. The intensity axis is how personal and casual the cruelty is (peers consuming Lena as content, a friend's wrong flower, a saint act whispered three feet away) - NOT scale, crowds, or set-piece size. No fantasy/male-gaze "bigger room" lens.
- Money beats: EP01 cold open (two identical faces, scar ECU), EP08 paywall ballroom + Mara's "I forgive her. I do." rehearsal INTERCUT, EP18 the snarl on camera, EP37 staircase self-harm, EP47-48 wedding crash into confession, EP49-50 the turn and reclaim.
- Two planted tells the audience holds all series: the scar (cold-open ECU -> EP01 end hook mirror -> EP03 live-screen gust -> pays off EP49) and the engraving (EP01 graveside thumb -> EP02 "Something in Latin. Who cares." -> EP03 bare-neck clutch -> pays off EP14/19/48). Shoot them as deliberate plants.

## 3. The Logic Behind It (not on the page)

- **The world believes the fake from EP09 to EP48.** Only Lena and Noah (and we) know. Interim cracks are about Mara's likability, never the secret: the snarl clip that won't die (EP19 -> EP30/35 -> EP49), one private believer (the woman in pearls, EP26), sponsors and friends drifting (EP36). Forbidden: anyone deducing the swap early.
- **Proof never works - that's the point.** No scar photo, document, or CCTV convinces anyone. The truth only lands when Mara destroys herself by confessing (EP48). Never solve a scene with evidence.
- **Lena is wronged but not a doormat.** She takes it without breaking, lands visible un-reversed wins from the middle on (EP11 bracelet text, EP18 snarl, EP19 clip, EP26 believer, EP44 bait), and lands the killing blow herself at EP48. Noah sets the table; he does not take the punch.
- **"Princess" is Mara's word - never give it to anyone else.** Her private sign-off ("Sleep good, princess" EP01-02, "you princesses" EP07) boomerangs at EP10: she answers a live Q&A "what did your mom call you?" with "Princess" - and Lena: "Mom called me bug. Princess is what SHE calls me."
- **Tone cues in dialogue:** the short parentheses (1-3 words: `(sweet, cruel)`, `(low)`, `(flat)`) are binding emotional direction for that line, written so non-native crews cannot misread tone. Do not ignore or invert them.

## 4. Character Handoff

- **On-screen title:** `LENA / ERASED HEIRESS`
- **Core expression:** the real heiress, erased in one night, disbelieved by the whole city.
- **Must read as:** contained and observant, never weepy; wronged but never pitiful; a woman holding still while the world calls her crazy.
- **Voice:** plain, level, short under pressure. No speeches, no poetry. Signature: "I'm Lena." Cracks only in private, and briefly.
- **Beauty/body:** 30, top-tier casting, fine V-jaw, moss-green eyes, porcelain skin, ink-black straight hair to mid-back, long neck, upright. Identical to Mara but **no scar**, and **bare neck EP02-49**.

## 5. Telling Lena and Mara Apart (the #1 production task)

Same face. In every shot, distinguish them by - in this order:

1. **The scar (Mara only):** below the left ear + right inner wrist. Normally hidden by hair/styling; shown in close confirmation cuts and the three planted beats (EP01 cold open / EP01 end hook / EP03 screen gust).
2. **Wardrobe:** Lena restrained slate/navy/ivory - Mara glossier stolen looks, then black, then stripped.
3. **Hair:** Lena lower and cleaner - Mara more styled.
4. **Attitude:** Lena still and contained - Mara performed-sweet or vulgar.
5. **Context:** whoever the world is adoring = Mara; bare neck = Lena.

**Never hold both faces in a long static shared frame that asks "which one is real?"** Use OTS, cutaways, position, single coverage. (EP01 cold open is the deliberate exception - it cuts to black before resolving the second face.)

- Vertical milking: one situation held across an episode bundle in 1-2 spaces (gala EP17-19, shower EP25-26, wedding EP33-34, staircase EP37, conservatory EP46-49). Every episode ends unresolved.
