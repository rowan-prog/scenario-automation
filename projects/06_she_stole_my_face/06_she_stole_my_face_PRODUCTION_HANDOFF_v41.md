## 2. The logic behind it (not on the page)

- **This is not a mystery.** The audience sees the whole face-swap across EP01-02 — nothing to "reveal" or keep suspenseful. The pleasure is dramatic irony: we know, the world doesn't. Never shoot it as a whodunit.
- **The world believes the fake from EP09 to EP48.** Only Lena and Noah (and we) know the swap that whole stretch.
- **Interim cracks are about her likability, NOT the secret.** From the paid middle on, a minority may doubt Mara's *character* — the small snarl clip that won't die (posted EP19, growing EP30/EP35, paid off EP49), one private person who quietly believes Lena (the woman in pearls, EP26), and Mara's own sloppiness costing her sponsors and old friends (EP36). None of this means anyone deduces the actual swap — that stays unknown until EP48. **Forbidden:** the world figuring out the swap early. **Allowed and wanted:** Mara cracking as a vulgar, sloppy fake people start to dislike.
- **The fake wins because she's shameless and the world is dumb — not because she's clever.** Tone is makjang: broad, brazen, absurd *and* infuriating. Villains are vulgar and never sympathetic. Do not make it tasteful, cool, or prestige.
- **Cruelty is relational and intimate, not spectacle.** This is modern + female-skewing. The intensity axis is how personal and casual the cruelty is (peers consuming Lena as content, a friend's wrong flower, a saint act whispered three feet away), NOT scale / crowds / spaces / set-piece spectacle. Don't import a fantasy/male-gaze "bigger crowd, bigger room" lens.
- **Proof never works — that's the point.** No one is convinced by the scar, photos, or any evidence. The truth only lands when the fake destroys herself by confessing (EP48). So never solve a scene with a document, file, CCTV, or gadget.
- **Lena is wronged but not a doormat.** She takes it without breaking and works to make the fake crack in public — and from the middle on she lands visible, un-reversed wins (EP18 the snarl on camera, EP19 the clip that won't die, EP26 the first believer, EP44 baiting Mara to the wedding).

## 5. Telling Lena and Mara apart (the #1 production task)

Same face. In every shot, distinguish them by — in this order:

1. **The scar (Mara only):** a faint curved scar just below the **left ear** + one on the **right inner wrist**. Normally hidden by hair/styling; shown only in close confirmation cuts — plus the three deliberate plant beats in EP01-03 (§3). Same position EP01-50 (fresh and pink in EP01-03 — the surgery is days old; faint thereafter).
2. **Wardrobe:** Lena restrained slate / navy / ivory · Mara glossier stolen looks → black → stripped.
3. **Hair:** Lena lower and cleaner · Mara more styled.
4. **Attitude:** Lena still and contained · Mara performed-sweet, or vulgar in private.
5. **Context:** whoever the world is adoring = Mara · bare neck = Lena.

**Never hold both faces in a long static shared frame that asks "which one is real?"** — use OTS, cutaways, position, single coverage. (EP01 cold open is the deliberate exception — but it cuts to black before resolving the second face.)
