# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP1-8 (free block · new treatment)

## EP1 - S#1  Day / Exterior / Glenmoor Cemetery — The Day Before
Characters: Lena · (intercut: Mara, Tessa)

△ Noon light on a marble headstone: HELENA STERLING. Lena kneels in a pale coat, composed, a gold-edged Cross-family invitation in one hand.

LENA (quiet, to the stone): Tomorrow your name stands next to theirs, Mom. In front of everyone.

△ [ECU] Lena's thumb turning the pendant at her throat, finding the worn engraving inside — LUMEN MEUM.

LENA (barely): "My light." You only ever called me that. Nobody else even knows it's in here.

△ [INTERCUT] A clinic back room, blinds drawn. Mara stands at a mirror, a photo of Lena taped to the glass beside her reflection — the same face, twice.

△ [ECU] Mara's fingertip tracing a thin curved scar under her own left ear, down to the jaw.

MARA (to the mirror, trying the name on): Lena Sterling. (smoother) Lena Sterling. The future Mrs. Cross.

△ Tessa stands at a terminal behind Mara, an appointment card open.

△ [ECU] The screen — the patient-name field already reads: STERLING.

TESSA (low, not looking up): Booked under Sterling. Like you wanted. Lena tells me everything. She always has.
MARA (sweet, cold): That's what makes you useful. (her fingertip on the photo) Twenty years I carried her bags and smiled. Tomorrow I stop smiling and start being her.

## EP1 - S#2  Afternoon / Interior / Cross Manor · Ballroom — Rehearsal
Characters: Ethan, Victoria, staff

△ An empty ballroom mid-setup. Ethan stands at the podium with a card, a tech adjusting the mic. Victoria watches from the front row.

ETHAN (reading, testing it): Tonight the Cross family welcomes — Lena Sterling. (to Victoria) It still sounds new in my mouth.
VICTORIA (cool): Make it sound certain. The whole country watches tomorrow.

△ [ECU] The card in Ethan's hand: ...PROUD TO ANNOUNCE LENA STERLING...

△ Ethan sets the card down and says it once more to the empty room.

ETHAN (quiet): Lena Sterling.

## EP1 - S#3  Night / Exterior / Clinic Service Lane — The Drop
Characters: Lena · (phone: Mara)

△ Lena's phone lights up in a dark car: MARA. Lena answers.

MARA (V.O. — phone, warm, urgent): Come to the clinic lane, now. It's about tomorrow — I have to show you before anyone else sees it.
LENA (getting out of the car): I'm here. Where are—

△ Headlights flare down the lane. Lena lifts a hand against the glare.

△ [ECU] The pendant chain slipping out of Lena's fingers. The pendant hits the asphalt.

△ Lena bends for it. A second pair of shoes steps into frame, closer.

[END HOOK]
△ [ECU] A hand closes over the pendant first — a diamond bracelet on the wrist.

△ The headlights swallow the frame. Cut to white — a surgical lamp snapping on.

On-screen text: EP2 NOW. / SHE CAME TO HELP A FRIEND. SHE WOKE UP A STRANGER.

## EP2 - S#1  Day / Interior / St. Brandt Hospital · Private Room — Waking Up Wrong
Characters: Lena · (TV: Mara, Ethan)

△ A dim private room. Lena lies in bed, the lower half of her face wrapped in surgical bandages. A nasal clip, a monitor beeping steady, a patient ID band on her wrist.

△ [ECU] Lena's eyes open slowly, unfocused, then sharpening.

△ A wall TV plays — a live broadcast fading up.

△ [Insert] On the TV — Mara, wearing Lena's face, stands beside Ethan at a podium. Helena's pendant at Mara's throat. Mara smiles into the camera.

△ Lena's hand rises toward the screen, shaking. Then Lena's fingers find the bandages on her own face instead.

△ [ECU] Lena's hand pressing the bandage along her jaw — feeling a face she can't see.

LENA (muffled, behind the bandage): ...what did you do to me.

△ Lena pushes up off the pillow. The nasal clip pulls loose. The monitor beep spikes faster.

[END HOOK]
△ [Insert] On the TV — a host leans to the mic, beaming: "Lena Sterling. The future Mrs. Cross."

△ Lena grabs the bed rail and drags herself toward the screen.

LENA (cracking, small): That's — that's my name.

On-screen text: EP3 NOW. / SHE'S WEARING MY FACE ON LIVE TV.

Hard Cut.

## EP3 - S#1  Day / Exterior → Interior / Cross Manor · Engagement Hall — Locked Out
Characters: Lena, Ethan, Mara, Victoria, guards, guests

△ A coat thrown over a hospital gown. Lena runs up the manor steps, hair loose, a bandage edge still taped to her jaw.

△ Two guards catch Lena's arms at the doors.

GUARD (flat): No invitation, no entry, ma'am—
LENA (shouting past them, into the hall): I'm LENA STERLING! That woman is wearing my face!

△ Lena's voice carries into the hall. At the podium, Ethan stops mid-sentence and looks toward the doors.

△ Mara, beside Ethan, slides her hand into his and leans to his ear.

MARA (soft, sad, sweet): Another one. They keep coming — women who want to be me. (a small sigh) Don't let her ruin tonight.
ETHAN (turning back to the room, cold): Security. Handle it.

△ The guards haul Lena backward out the doors. Lena's heels drag across the marble.

△ Lena drops to her knees outside her own engagement, the doors swinging shut in front of her.

[END HOOK]
△ [Insert] Through the closing gap — on the far stage, Mara lifts the pendant at her throat toward the cameras.

MARA (into the mic, clear, smiling): I'm Lena Sterling.

△ The doors seal shut. Lena's view goes dark.

On-screen text: EP4 NOW. / NOBODY BELIEVES THE REAL ONE.

Hard Cut.

## EP4 - S#1  Night / Interior / Noah Keene's Office — One Frame
Characters: Noah · (screen: Lena, Mara)

△ A dark office, the city behind glass. Noah sits at a wall of monitors — surveillance photos of Lena, a movement log, her clip from the manor steps. A Cross-family contract lies open on the desk: SUBJECT — L. STERLING (CLAIMANT).

NOAH (low, replaying her clip): "Wearing my face." Somebody's paying a lot of people to call you crazy.

△ Noah pulls up clinic-lane CCTV from the night of the accident and scrubs it slow.

△ [Insert] CCTV — Lena at the headlights, the pendant slipping from her hand to the asphalt.

△ [Insert] CCTV, the next moment — a hand enters from the edge of frame and lifts the pendant.

△ Noah freezes it. Zooms.

△ [ECU] The wrist in the still — a diamond bracelet.

△ Noah drags a second image alongside: Mara at the engagement, same wrist, same bracelet.

[END HOOK]
△ Noah sets the two frames side by side — the same bracelet in both.

NOAH (quiet): That wasn't an accident. Somebody was waiting for her.

On-screen text: EP5 NOW. / THE MAN PAID TO WATCH HER JUST SAW THE TRUTH.

Hard Cut.

## EP5 - S#1  Day / Interior / Brandt Clinic · Dr. Celia's Office — Deleted
Characters: Lena, Noah, Celia

△ Lena and Noah step into a clinic office. Dr. Celia stands, blocking her monitor with her body.

CELIA (tight): There's no record of any patient like that. You should both leave.

△ Noah lays a printout on her desk — the CCTV still, the bracelet circled in red.

△ Celia's mouth closes. She looks at it a long moment, then slowly turns her monitor toward Noah.

△ Noah leans in and restores a deleted file. The screen fills.

△ [Insert] Screen — Mara's pre-op photo. Mara's post-op photo: Lena's face. Beside them, a news still: LENA STERLING — ENGAGED.

△ [Insert] Screen — a consultation record under a second name: TESSA HALE. The notes list Lena's schedule, the one night she'd be alone.

LENA (going very still): Tessa. My Tessa. She gave them the night.

[END HOOK]
△ [ECU] The deleted sheet's last line — a signature: TESSA HALE, the ink crisp on the screen.

LENA (quiet, breaking): My best friend booked the night they took my face.

On-screen text: EP6 NOW. / THE FRIEND WHO HELD MY HAND SOLD IT.
