## WORLD-RULES
- FLAGGED-FACE RULE: Lena's face is flagged at Cross-controlled venues and she is removed on sight as 'the stalker.' Established early and reinforced: turned away at the Cross gate (EP3), backhanded and dragged off the front steps (EP4), dragged from the ballroom (EP8), removed from the luncheon (EP13), the cemetery (EP14), the gala (EP18-19), the bridal shower (EP25-26), the wedding (EP33-34). Stated explicitly EP32 ('Your face is flagged at every door. The second you're seen, you're the stalker who crashed the wedding'). To get into Cross venues Lena must disguise/sneak: catering uniform + wig (EP7, EP25), press pool (EP4), wig + a guest's spare invitation (EP37), or Noah's security routing her through a press overflow gate away from the flagged front doors (EP33). At her OWN venue (the conservatory, EP47) the rule is inverted — Lena is the host and Mara is the one removed.
- IMPOSTOR-BELIEVED RULE (genre engine): The world believes Mara IS Lena Sterling from the engagement (EP2-3) until the public confession (EP48-49). Throughout, the more loudly Lena claims her own identity, the crazier/more like a stalker she looks — screaming 'I'm Lena' is self-defeating (EP3, EP8, EP9, EP12). Strangers turn it into a punchline (EP12). The institutions actively reinforce it: the hospital is told she'll 'say strange things' (EP3), the doorman has her registered out (EP9), Victoria/the Cross side publicly recast every confrontation as Lena tormenting a grieving bride. Lena's winning strategy is to STOP claiming her identity and instead make Mara expose herself on camera. The truth is finally established not by evidence (the recording was wiped EP8) but by Mara's own confession in front of cameras (EP48) plus her involuntary scar-flinch (EP49).
- CROSS SIDE CONTROLS THESE VENUES/LEVERS: Cross Manor (gates, front steps, ballroom, garden, suites, banquet hall — EP3,4,8,20,25,29,30,31,36,39), Harbor General hospital (the Cross family directs staff to keep people away — EP3), Lena's old Sterling apartment building (registered to Mara; doorman Hector enforces it; building security walks Lena out — EP9), the press/media narrative (Victoria and Mara reliably reframe events; reporters nod along — EP4,8,14,19,29,42), guards/security at every Cross event, the original wedding venue (pressured to drop Lena+Noah via Mara's press call — EP43). The Cross side also has investigative reach (an investigator traces Lena's new number through Noah's account — EP11).
- WHAT THE CROSS SIDE DOES NOT CONTROL: Noah Keene's penthouse (Lena's safe hideout, EP8 onward), Noah's car and security detail, Noah's office, the rooftop restaurant (EP28), and ultimately the glass conservatory on the water that Noah books for his own wedding (EP43,46,47) — explicitly chosen as a venue where 'the cameras are mine' (EP44,45). Noah's resources are the counterweight to Cross control: he plants catering crew (EP7), supplies the new phone (EP9), routes Lena past flagged doors (EP33), seats the skeptic reporter front row, and runs a private livestream at his own wedding (EP47).
- EVIDENCE-DOESN'T-WIN / CAMERA-FIRST RULE: Stated by Noah EP10 — going to police with DNA/fingerprints won't work while every camera shows Lena attacking the bride ('they bury you as unstable before the test means anything'); 'Cameras first. Then the rest.' The hard evidence (the recorded real-Mara video) is destroyed EP8, so the resolution is engineered to be public/visible — Mara must be caught faking on camera in a room that all has to watch. Truth in this world is whatever the loudest person/the cameras show, until Mara's own mouth + reflexive scar-flinch flip it.
- VIOLENCE-DIRECTION (per genre standard): Physical violence against Lena from the Cross side is limited to a slap/backhand (Victoria EP4) and guards grabbing/dragging her (EP4,8,13,18-19,25,33-34); no high-intensity male physical assault on Lena. The only high-impact physical harm is self-inflicted by Mara (the staged stair-fall, EP37) and Lena-on-Mara (the open-palm slap EP7) — woman-on-woman. Ethan only verbally warns Lena (EP27).
