# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP14-19 (v2 line)

## EP14 - S#1  Morning / Interior / Mara's Suite
Characters: Mara · (phone: press contact)

△ Mara at her vanity, no makeup, Helena's pendant at her throat. A frozen clip on Mara's phone — two old donors mid-murmur, not looking at her.

MARA (into the phone, low, vicious): They're still chewing on yesterday because of HER. Set up the grave visit this morning. Get me every camera you can.

△ Mara tips her chin up and tests a soft, wet voice at her own reflection.

MARA (to the mirror, sugary): Hi, Mom. I brought your favorite. (her own voice again, flat) Yeah. They'll eat that up. And the lunatic disappears for good.

## EP14 - S#2  Day / Exterior / Glenmoor Cemetery · Continuous
Characters: Lena, Mara, Groundskeeper, Young Reporter, press

△ Helena Sterling's headstone. A small press pool in a half-circle. Mara in black, arms full of white lilies, kneeling.

MARA (at the headstone, soft, for the cameras): Hi, Mom. I brought your favorite.

△ A reporter writes it down. Behind the tree line, Lena in a dark hood grips a branch until it cracks.

LENA (V.O.): My mother hated lilies. They were the flower at her own mother's funeral.

△ Mara lays the lilies on the stone and kisses it. The cameras push in close on Mara.

△ Lena steps out from the tree line into the open.

LENA (low, shaking): Those are funeral flowers. She hated them. You don't even know what you put on her.

△ The whole press pool swings to Lena — the hooded woman walking up on a grieving daughter at a grave.

MARA (stepping back, frightened, perfect): Please. Not here. Not at my mother's grave.

△ A groundskeeper steps between Lena and Mara.

GROUNDSKEEPER (hard): Ma'am. Back up. Have some respect.

△ Lena lifts both hands and turns to walk out on her own.

△ Mara crosses the grass and pulls Lena into a hug — the forgiving saint, arms wide for every lens. Mara's mouth goes to Lena's ear.

MARA (low, mocking, only to Lena): She's dead, sweetheart. She can't help you. Nobody can. I'm her daughter now and there's nothing you can do about it. Smile for the cameras.
LENA (barely, only to Mara): She was MY mother. One day a camera catches what you really are.

△ Lena pulls free and walks.

△ Mara stands alone, dabbing her eyes for the room.

△ A few feet back, one young reporter holds her phone down at her side, not filming. The young reporter watches Mara.

△ Walking out, Lena's eyes catch the one lens in the cemetery not pointed at her — the young reporter's.

## EP14 - S#3  Day / Interior / Noah's Car · Continuous
Characters: Lena, Noah

NOAH: One day she says the wrong thing to the wrong room. I'll have you in it.
LENA (slow): One of them put her phone down. The young one. She watched Mara the whole time, not me.
NOAH: Then remember her face.

## EP15 - S#1  Night / Interior / Noah's Penthouse · Bedroom
Characters: Lena, Noah

△ Low light. Lena stands at the window in one of Noah's shirts. Noah comes in. Lena turns to face him.

LENA (low): Out there I'm a liar. A stalker. A crazy woman who paid to look like an heiress. But in here?

△ Lena crosses to Noah. Noah takes Lena's face in both hands.

NOAH (low): Lena.

△ Noah kisses Lena — slow, then not slow. Lena fists both hands in Noah's shirt and pulls him in.

△ Noah lifts Lena. Lena wraps her legs around Noah and he carries her to the bed.

△ Noah's mouth goes to the bare place at Lena's throat. Lena grabs his shoulders and stops shaking.

LENA (breath, against Noah's neck): Right now — I'm nobody's copy. I'm just me.

△ [ECU] Noah's hand spread flat at the small of Lena's back, pulling Lena down onto him.

△ [ECU] Lena's mouth open against Noah's shoulder — breath, not words.

△ [ECU] Lena's fingers laced hard behind Noah's neck.

△ After. Lena's cheek on Noah's chest. Noah's hand in Lena's hair.

LENA (quiet): First time I've felt like a person since the clinic.
NOAH (low): Then we do this every night until out there feels like in here.

[END HOOK]
△ [INSERT] Across town — Mara at her vanity, scrolling. On the phone screen: a paparazzi shot, Noah Keene's hand at the small of an unnamed woman's back, the two of them leaving his building. The woman's face is turned away.

△ Mara goes still.

MARA (low, seething): Of course she ran to Noah Keene. She lost everything — down to the face off her own head — and she STILL gets a man holding her like that. (slamming the phone down) She doesn't get to be happy. Not while I've got her whole life.

Hard Cut.

## EP16 - S#1  Day / Interior / Noah's Office · Glass Front
Characters: Noah, Lena, Reece · (phone: client)

△ A glass-walled office. Noah at his desk. Lena on the couch, watching a muted morning show on the wall screen. Reece, one of Noah's men, in the doorway.

REECE (flat): It's on three channels now. They're not even talking about Mara anymore. They're talking about you.

△ [INSERT] Wall screen — a daytime panel, two hosts grinning. Caption under a photo of Noah and the turned-away woman: *THE FIXER & THE FACE-STEALER — A LOVE STORY?*

HOST (on screen, gleeful): So the security consultant the Crosses hired to handle the stalker is now... handling her. You can't write it.

△ Lena puts the screen on mute and stands.

LENA (quiet): They're using us to make me look crazier. A stalker who seduced the guard.
NOAH (flat): They're using me to make you look like a gold-digger with a type. Same play, new face.

△ Noah's desk phone lights up. Noah answers, listens, says nothing for a beat.

NOAH (into the phone, even): Say that again.
CLIENT (V.O., curt): We're out, Keene. Cross said so. Nobody hires the man sleeping with the lunatic.

△ Noah hangs up. The line goes dead in his hand.

REECE (flat): That's the second one today. They're calling everyone on your book.

## EP16 - S#2  Day / Interior / Noah's Penthouse · Bedroom · Later
Characters: Lena, Noah

△ A duffel open on the bed. Lena folds the few plain clothes she owns into it.

△ Noah comes in and stops in the doorway.

NOAH (flat): What's this.
LENA (not stopping): They're tearing your whole business down to get to me. Every client. I'm not standing here watching them ruin you for keeping me.
NOAH: So you walk out the service door and what. Sleep where.
LENA (low): Somewhere that doesn't cost you a client every morning.

△ Lena reaches for the duffel. Noah catches the strap and holds it.

NOAH (flat, no give): I've lost clients to worse than this. Cross spends a week telling the city I'm dirty. He's not the first.
LENA (shaking): They'll bury you.
NOAH: Then they bury me with you in the building, not crying on some curb where the next two men with phones find you.

△ Noah takes the duffel off the bed and drops it on the floor by the wall.

NOAH (low): You don't get to leave to protect me. I pulled you off that curb. I don't unpull it because the phone rang.

△ Lena looks at the duffel on the floor. She doesn't pick it up.

[END HOOK]
△ [INSERT] Cross Manor · study. Mara stands over Eileen, the pendant at Mara's throat.

MARA (low, vicious): The guard's the only one still holding her up. So take him out from under her. Burn his name down to nothing.
EILEEN (flat): And the woman?
MARA (sweet): Take the man away. She breaks for good.

## EP17 - S#1  Night / Interior / Helena Sterling Memorial Gala
Characters: Mara, Lena, Noah, guards, guests

△ A grand black-tie room in Helena's name. Cameras on every wall. Mara at the podium, glowing, the pendant on. The room on its feet, applauding.

△ Lena comes in on Noah's arm — a wrap and dark glasses keeping her face down, Noah's name getting them past the door. People part for Noah.

△ Mara, at the mic, finds Lena across the floor.

MARA (soft, radiant, into the live mic): My mom can't be at my wedding. So I'm bringing her the only way I can — her name, her favorite room. I'll marry him right where she'd have wanted me to.

△ The room rises again. Applause.

LENA (V.O.): She's selling her own wedding off my dead mother. And the whole room is clapping.

△ Lena starts walking toward the stage — calm, steady, through the crowd.

△ [ECU] Mara at the mic, the room on its feet behind her.

△ [ECU] Lena moving between the tables, not stopping.

△ [ECU] Noah a half-step behind Lena, letting her go.

[END HOOK]
△ A guard steps into Lena's path. Lena moves around the guard, eyes on Mara on the stage.

NOAH (low, at Lena's shoulder): You sure.
LENA (not slowing): She can't walk away from a microphone. Watch.

## EP18 - S#1  Night / Interior / Helena Sterling Memorial Gala · Floor · Continuous
Characters: Lena, Mara, Young Reporter, guests

△ Lena reaches the foot of the stage. The room half-turns. Mara, smiling at the mic, sees Lena coming and keeps smiling for the lenses.

△ Lena doesn't shout. Lena pitches her voice just loud enough to carry.

LENA (level, carrying): Twenty years copying me and you still flinch when somebody laughs too close. Look at you up there. They're all clapping — and you know you're a fake every single second of it.

△ [ECU] Mara's radiant face goes wrong. The sweet drops clean off.

MARA (snarling at Lena, forgetting the mic is live, ugly): Shut your mouth, you jealous nobody — nobody wants you, that's why you—

△ The room hears it — the sweet grieving bride snarling into a live mic. A beat of stunned quiet. Phones swing up.

△ Three feet back, the young reporter from the cemetery lifts her phone and films Mara's face.

△ [ECU] Mara's snarl, full and ugly, on the big room screen behind her.

△ [ECU] Phones swinging up across the floor.

△ [ECU] The cemetery reporter filming Mara, not Lena.

[END HOOK]
△ Mara's mouth fights back to sweet — too late, the snarl already out. Mara reaches up to wipe at her eyes.

△ The reporter keeps filming. Lena, at the foot of the stage, almost smiles.

LENA (quiet, just for Mara): There's the real you. On camera this time.

Hard Cut.
