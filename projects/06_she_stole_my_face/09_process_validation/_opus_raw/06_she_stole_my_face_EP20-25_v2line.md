# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP20-25 (v2 line)

## EP20 - S#1  Night / Interior / Cross Manor · Mara's Suite
Characters: Mara · (phone: Eileen)

△ Mara paces her suite, scrolling fast on her phone. The phone sits on speaker on the dresser.

MARA (rattled, vicious): She keeps knowing things. The flowers. The pendant. She got me to snap on a live mic. People are starting to look — and I want her gone for good.
EILEEN (V.O., flat): Then stop reacting. Take something so big nobody can ever picture it as hers.

△ Mara stops pacing.

MARA (smug, the sweet voice already rehearsed): I'll put her mother's name on the foundation. Sign it "Helena's daughter" in front of the whole country. Once they all watch me cry over a dead woman like she's mine, that stalker can scream all she wants — nobody takes it back.
EILEEN (V.O., cold, pleased): Now you're listening.

△ Mara hangs up. She walks to the mirror and looks at her own face.

△ [ECU] Mara sweeps a strand of hair down over her left ear — the old habit.

MARA (low, sour): I'll own every piece of her. It still won't feel like mine — and I hate that.

[END HOOK]
△ Mara stares at her reflection, jaw set.

MARA (cold): Doesn't matter. After the wedding it's all mine, and nobody can touch a piece of it.

## EP21 - S#1  Night / Interior / Noah's Penthouse
Characters: Lena, Noah

△ Lena sits on the couch, hollowed out from the gala fight. Noah sets an old phone in Lena's hand.

NOAH (low): You said you were forgetting her voice.

△ Noah presses play.

HELENA (V.O., old voicemail, warm): Lena, sweetheart, it's Mom. Call me back when you land. I love you. Stand up straight.

△ Lena's face crumples and she cries, the phone pressed to her ear.

LENA (barely): Where did you—
NOAH (even): Your old phone. They never wiped the backup. It's yours now. This one, nobody can take.

△ Lena holds the phone to her chest. Noah puts an arm around her.

[END HOOK]
LENA (quiet, into his chest): She's out there making my mother into her wedding. And you just gave her back to me.
NOAH (certain): They took her from you. I'll keep giving her back. Whatever I can find.

Hard Cut.

## EP22 - S#1  Day / Interior / Bridal Salon
Characters: Mara, Lena, attendants

△ Mara stands at a three-way mirror in a wedding gown. Two attendants in black fuss at the hem and waist. Mara turns to admire herself.

MARA (to an attendant, sweet): My mom always said simple. Her style. (to her reflection, preening) Look at me. This was wasted on her. I actually fill it out.

△ Lena walks into the salon in plain clothes.

LENA (level): That's the dress I always wanted. She even took that.
MARA (not turning, to the attendants): See? This is what I deal with. She follows me everywhere. (sweet, to Lena) Did you pick my shoes too, sweetie?

△ The attendants step in around Lena, blocking her path.

LENA (flat): You can't even stand still in it. You'll never be me.
MARA (loud, delighted, for the room): I'm getting married and she still won't leave my wedding alone. (light, to an attendant) Don't bother with security. She's harmless. Just sad.

△ The attendants laugh. One of them takes Lena by the elbow and walks her toward the door.

△ At the door, Lena stops. She turns back and speaks low, just for Mara.

LENA (quiet, just for Mara): Enjoy the fittings. Somewhere between the toast and the first dance, somebody's going to make you laugh — your real laugh. I'll be watching when it happens.

△ Mara goes still in the mirror, the gown half-pinned.

△ An attendant holds the door open, eyes down, as Lena walks out.

[END HOOK]
△ [INSERT] Mara's phone screen — a mirror selfie in the gown, caption: *Said yes to the dress 👰🤍 Mom would've cried.* Thousands of hearts.

△ One attendant kneels at the hem, pins in her mouth. She looks up at Mara and does not smile.

MARA (low, to her reflection): Nobody's looking at the dress. They're looking at me.

Hard Cut.

## EP23 - S#1  Night / Interior / Noah's Penthouse · Bedroom
Characters: Lena, Noah

△ Late. The dark city behind the glass. Lena turns to Noah.

LENA (quiet): You keep choosing me. This whole city's telling you not to.
NOAH (low): The city's wrong about you. I'm not.

△ Noah kisses Lena. Deep, slow. Lena pulls Noah down hard by the collar.

△ [ECU] Lena's hands frame Noah's face.

△ [CU] Noah's hand drags up the bare line of Lena's spine. Lena arches into it.

△ [ECU] Lena's mouth at Noah's ear, saying his name on a breath.

△ After. Lena's head on Noah's bare chest, his arm around her.

LENA (small): When this is over—
NOAH (certain): When this is over, you say your name out loud and nobody flinches. I'll be right next to you.

[END HOOK]
△ [INTERCUT] Cross Manor · bedroom. Mara stares at a blurry photo on her phone — Noah and a woman in a coat. Mara sets the phone face-down.

MARA (seething): She lost everything and she's still smiling at him. Not for long. I'll take that too.

Hard Cut.

## EP24 - S#1  Night / Interior / Noah's Penthouse / Cross Manor · Bedroom · Intercut
Characters: Lena · (phone/intercut: Mara, Ethan's shadow)

△ Lena's phone rings — unknown number. Lena answers.

△ [INTERCUT] Mara lies back on the Cross Manor bed in Lena's silk robe. Behind Mara, Ethan's shadow moves across the bathroom glass.

MARA (sultry, gloating): Ethan just kissed me goodnight. Slow. Hands everywhere. He thinks it's you — but it's my mouth he wants now. You should hear the way he says your name when the lights are off.
LENA (flat): You're disgusting.
MARA (smug): I'm comfortable. Big difference. I wear your robe, I sleep on your side, and when he says your name in the dark, I say yes to it. He's reaching for you and getting me, every single night, and he can't tell. That's how much you mattered.

△ [INTERCUT] Lena grips the phone, knuckles tight.

MARA (mocking, contemptuous): You're out there screaming at strangers in a wig. I'm in your bed wearing your face, and your man likes it better. Buy yourself a nice dress and cry about it. Bye, sweetie.

△ Mara hangs up. She lies alone on the wide bed and stares at the ceiling.

△ Mara reaches over to Lena's empty side of the bed, flat-palmed, and pulls her hand back.

## EP24 - S#2  Night / Interior / Noah's Penthouse · Continuous
Characters: Lena, Noah

△ Lena sets the phone down. Her hands shake. Noah watches Lena.

NOAH (low): What did she say.
LENA (cracking): That he kisses her with my face on. That he says my name to her in the dark, and she answers to it.
NOAH (quiet, hard): He's kissing a stranger and calling it you. I know exactly who you are. Come here.

△ Lena goes to Noah. He holds her.

[END HOOK]
LENA (low, against his chest): She answers when he says my name. And the whole city calls me the one who broke in.
NOAH (certain): Three weeks. Then they watch her crack on camera and the name goes back to you.

## EP25 - S#1  Day / Exterior / Cross Manor · Garden
Characters: Lena, Mara, Victoria, guests

△ A garden bridal shower in full bloom. Mara stands center as the radiant bride-to-be, guests around her.

△ Lena, in a catering apron and a pinned wig, slips in along the catering line. She moves toward a small circle of older women in pastel who knew Helena.

LENA (low, to the circle of women): You knew my mother. Look at her — really look at her. Ask her one thing from before all this.

△ A woman in the circle gasps and steps back. Across the garden, without turning, Mara raises her voice for everyone.

MARA (tearful): She found my shower. She found my SHOWER. Victoria — please —

△ Victoria turns on Lena.

VICTORIA (loud, ugly): You had surgery to look like my son's bride and now you're crashing her party. Get that thing away from my family.
A GUEST (backing off): Somebody — call someone —

△ Lena stands surrounded. The women who loved Helena edge back from Lena, faces tight with disgust.

△ [ECU] Mara cries, then smirks — Victoria is doing the work for her.

△ Victoria points Lena out across the garden, loud, for the crowd.

[END HOOK]
LENA (breaking, to the circle of women): You held me at her funeral. You don't know me now?

△ The garden goes silent. Every face turns toward Mara, waiting to see if the bride wants Lena gone.

Hard Cut.
