# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP45-50 (v2 line)

## EP45 - S#1  Night / Interior / Noah's Penthouse · Bedroom
Characters: Lena, Noah

△ Night, the city dark behind the glass. Lena stands at the window in one of Noah's shirts.

△ Noah comes up behind Lena and stops close at her back.

LENA (quiet): Last night before I'm somebody's wife. Somebody who chose it.
NOAH (low): Come to bed.

△ Lena turns. Noah kisses her — slow, then harder — and eases the shirt off her shoulders.

△ [ECU] Noah's mouth at the back of Lena's neck.

△ [ECU] Lena's back arches into Noah's palm.

△ [ECU] Lena's face pressed to Noah's throat, slow breath.

△ After. Lena's head rests on Noah's chest.

LENA (quiet): If she comes tomorrow—
NOAH (even): Then she leaves our wedding in front of everyone. I gave the front row to that reporter — the one who never once bought her act. She earned the seat more than anyone in this city.
LENA (quiet): The one who looked twice.
NOAH (low): Sleep. Tomorrow you marry me.

[END HOOK]
△ [INSERT] Across the city — Mara in a black dress at a dark window, phone to her ear.

MARA (into the phone, sweet): Of course I'll be there. Family doesn't miss family's big day.

△ Mara ends the call and lowers the phone.

MARA (flat): I wouldn't miss watching my own face get married.

## EP46 - S#1  Morning / Interior / Conservatory · Bridal Suite
Characters: Lena, Noah's Aunt · (intercut: Mara, Ethan, Victoria, Cameraman)

△ Sea light pours through the glass. Lena stands in a slip, hair pinned soft and low.

△ [ECU] Helena's wedding dress on the stand — clean ivory silk, no train.

LENA (quiet): That's my mother's dress. The one Mara never found.

△ An older woman from Noah's family fixes Lena's veil.

LENA (quiet): I don't have anyone to walk me in.
NOAH'S AUNT (gentle): Then walk yourself in. Let them all watch you arrive on your own two feet.

△ Lena looks at her own face in the glass, in the dress nobody took.

LENA (low, to her reflection): Okay. Today nobody takes anything.

△ [INTERCUT] Mara, in the black dress, slides into a car. Ethan and Victoria climb in beside Mara. A cameraman gets in last.

ETHAN (flat): You said you'd handle this quietly.
MARA (sweet): I am. I'm just going to go say hello.

△ [INTERCUT] The car pulls onto a coast road, water on one side.

[END HOOK]
△ [HIGH ANGLE] The car runs down the coast road toward the headland, the conservatory glass far ahead.

Hard Cut.

## EP47 - S#1  Day / Interior / Conservatory · Aisle
Characters: Lena, Noah, Officiant, Young Reporter, Woman in Pearls, guests · (then: Mara, Cameraman, Ethan, Victoria)

△ [HIGH ANGLE] The glass aisle, sea light behind it, a small crowd seated — Noah's people on one side, a handful for Lena on the other.

△ The young reporter sits front row, a clear angle. A small livestream camera runs on a stand at the side.

△ Lena walks herself down the aisle, alone, head up. Noah waits at the end, watching Lena.

△ Lena reaches Noah.

NOAH (low, just for Lena): You walked in on your own feet.
LENA (soft): You were waiting at the end. That's all I needed.
LENA (steady): Everybody called me crazy. You didn't. You believed me when it cost you everything. That's why I'm here.
NOAH (low): Best thing it ever cost me.

△ The officiant lifts a hand.

OFFICIANT: Then by the—

△ The conservatory doors bang open.

△ Mara strides in down the aisle in the black dress, tears already running. The cameraman comes in at Mara's shoulder, lens up.

△ Ethan and Victoria come in right behind Mara.

MARA (screaming, to the room): STOP! Stop the wedding! That woman is not who she says she is — she's a stalker, she tried to KILL me, and now she's faking her way into Noah Keene's life like she faked everything else.

△ The crowd freezes in their seats. The cameraman's lens holds on Mara.

△ [ECU] Mara in black at the head of the aisle, wet-faced.

△ [ECU] The cameraman's lens, the red light on.

[END HOOK]
MARA (to Noah, sweet over the venom): Noah, sweetheart — she's done this before. I'm the real Lena Sterling. Ask her. Ask her right here, in front of everyone.

Hard Cut.

## EP48 - S#1  Day / Interior / Conservatory · Aisle · Continuous
Characters: Lena, Noah, Mara, Young Reporter, Cameraman, Ethan, Victoria, guests

△ Mara stands in the aisle, the cameraman's lens live, the room frozen. Nobody in the crowd moves toward Lena. The guests watch Mara.

△ Noah steps between Mara and Lena.

NOAH (flat): You walked into my wedding. In black. With a camera.
MARA (faltering, then louder, tearful): I came to save you. She's dangerous—
NOAH (level): The only one faking anything here is you. You're not invited. Get out.

△ Mara's eyes go across the room for a face that will turn for her. None turn. Noah steps back and leaves Mara the floor.

△ Lena steps off the dais and walks right up to Mara — same face to same face. Lena speaks low.

LENA (quiet, cruel): You've got my face, my ring, my mother's name — and underneath it you're still the girl who carried my bags for twenty years, dreaming of being me. You can wear me. You'll never be me. Every day you're scared sick someone looks close and sees the bag-girl under the paint.
LENA (after a beat): Look around. They're all about to.

△ [ECU] Mara's face breaks. Mara's eyes go to the lifted phones.

MARA (the sweet gone, vicious, loud): I took your face because it was always supposed to be mine. I carried your bags for twenty years and you never even looked at me. So I took it. The name, the money, the man — all of it was supposed to be MINE—

△ The room goes dead silent. Mara stops, mouth still open.

△ Lena looks at Mara.

LENA (quiet, level): Say that again. Slower.

△ Mara's mouth opens, then shuts. Mara's own cameraman keeps rolling. Phones lift across the room. The young reporter's lens holds steady, front row.

△ [ECU] Mara's face, the mask off, in front of everyone.

△ The crowd stares at Mara. No one moves to help Mara.

[END HOOK]
△ [ECU] The red recording light on the cameraman's lens.

Hard Cut.

## EP49 - S#1  Day / Interior / Conservatory · Aisle · Continuous
Characters: Lena, Noah, Mara, Ethan, Victoria, Young Reporter, Woman in Pearls, guests

△ Mara stands in the glass room, the crowd staring. For the first time the guests turn toward Mara, not Lena.

△ A guest leans to another.

GUEST (low): ...what did she just say.

△ The murmur builds, the faces turning at Mara now.

△ Ethan stands and stares at Mara.

ETHAN (hollow): Say it again. You took... whose face?

△ Lena steps forward. The room goes quiet to hear Lena.

LENA (level): I screamed it for months and you called me crazy. She says it once and you finally hear it.
LENA (after a beat, steady): I'm Lena Sterling. Mara drugged me, took my face, took my life — and you all helped her do it.

△ Mara's hand flies up to cover the scar under her ear. The whole room catches it.

MARA (scrambling, tears rushing back): No — no, she's confusing you, I'm Lena, I've always been—

△ Nobody in the crowd moves toward Mara. The faces stay on Mara.

△ Victoria stands.

VICTORIA (ice): Get her out. She never sets foot near a Cross again.

△ Ethan turns from Mara to Lena and takes a step, hand half up.

ETHAN (shaking): Lena. I didn't — I swear I didn't know—
LENA (not looking at Ethan): You had months to know. You called me crazy on camera and let them drag me down your own steps.
NOAH (cold, stepping in front of Ethan): She's done talking to you.

△ Ethan stops where he stands.

△ Lena walks past Ethan to Mara, down on the floor in the middle of the aisle. Lena reaches down and unclasps Helena's pendant from Mara's throat. Mara grabs for it, weak, too late. Lena lifts it away clean.

LENA (quiet, certain): This was always mine.

△ Lena closes her hand around the pendant. Mara sinks to the floor with empty hands.

△ The young reporter lowers her camera.

△ The older woman in pearls crosses out of the crowd to stand beside Lena. Then a second guest. Then a third.

△ Mara kneels alone in the middle of the aisle, wearing Lena's face. No one reaches for Mara. The cameraman's lens keeps rolling.

△ [ECU] Lena's hand closed around the pendant.

[END HOOK]
LENA (level, to the room): I said it for months and you called me crazy. So I made her say it herself.

Hard Cut.
