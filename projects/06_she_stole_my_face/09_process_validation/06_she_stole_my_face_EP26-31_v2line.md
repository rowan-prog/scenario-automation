# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP26-31 (v2 line)

## EP26 - S#1  Day / Exterior / Cross Manor · Garden · Continuous
Characters: Lena, Woman in Pearls, guests

△ Lena stands surrounded in the catering apron and pinned wig. The circle of older women in pastel stands frozen around Lena.

LENA (cracking): You cried at her funeral. I stood right next to you. Look at me. You know it's me.

△ One woman turns her back. Then another. The whole circle turns away — all but one.

△ An older woman in pearls holds Lena's eyes and does not turn.

LENA (quiet, only to her): You braided my hair at the lake house. You know my voice.

△ The woman in pearls stays put while the others turn. Her eyes fill.

## EP26 - S#2  Day / Exterior / Cross Manor · Front Gate · Continuous
Characters: Lena, Woman in Pearls, Noah, guards

△ Two guards walk Lena toward the gate, one at each arm. Behind them, the woman in pearls leaves the shower early — alone, not staying for the bride.

△ The woman in pearls reaches the gate and catches Lena's hand. The woman leans in close, fast, only for Lena.

WOMAN IN PEARLS (low, fast): I braided your hair every summer at that lake house. I know your voice. (a breath) I can't say it in there. They'd eat me alive.

△ The woman in pearls presses Lena's hand once and walks off through the parked cars.

△ Noah's car stops at the gate. Noah is out and at Lena's side.

NOAH (to the guards, low): Don't put a hand on her. I've got her.

△ Noah walks Lena out himself, past the guards.

LENA (shaken): The whole circle turned their backs. The ones who buried my mother with me.
NOAH (even): All but one.
LENA (low): She couldn't say it inside. But she held my hand the way she used to. Somebody in that house still knows me.
NOAH (certain): And she walked out of the bride's party to do it.

△ A manor room. Mara stands alone with the phone, staring at the empty chair in the photo.

## EP27 - S#1  Day / Exterior / Noah's Penthouse · Street
Characters: Lena, Ethan

△ Lena comes out of the building. Ethan stands at the curb, planted there, waiting for her.

ETHAN (clipped): You need to stop. You show up at her mother's grave. Her salon. Her shower. People are scared of you.
LENA (level): Ethan. Three years. You know my voice. You know me.
ETHAN (cold): The woman I'm marrying is Lena. You paid a surgeon to look like her. Stop embarrassing yourself.
LENA (quiet): You can't look me in the eye.

△ Ethan looks at the pavement, then lifts his chin and stares past Lena's shoulder.

ETHAN (sharp): And tell your billionaire to stay out of my wedding. Whatever Keene's doing, it isn't love. He picked you up off a curb. You're a charity case. He'll get bored and drop you back where he found you.

LENA (quiet): You're jealous. Of him. With me.
ETHAN (too fast): Of a stalker? Don't flatter yourself.

△ Ethan turns and walks off too fast.

△ Lena does not move. Lena calls after Ethan's back, level.

[END HOOK]
LENA (loud, to his back): You'd rather marry a stranger than admit a woman made a fool of you.

△ Ethan's stride breaks half a step. Ethan does not turn around. Ethan keeps walking.

## EP28 - S#1  Night / Interior / Rooftop Restaurant
Characters: Lena, Noah, diners

△ Noah sits across from Lena at a rooftop table, out in the open, no wrap, no glasses. Noah keeps his hand over Lena's on the cloth.

△ Nearby diners turn and stare at Lena. Noah holds each stare until that diner looks back down at the table.

LENA (low): People are looking. You don't have to do this where everyone can see.
NOAH (calm): I want them looking. I want every one of them to see exactly who I picked.

△ Lena smiles. She catches it and presses her mouth flat.

LENA (low): I forgot what it feels like to be picked in front of everyone.

△ Two tables over, a diner lifts a phone and takes a photo of Lena and Noah.

## EP29 - S#1  Day / Exterior / Cross Manor · Gates

## EP29 - S#2  Day / Interior / Noah's Penthouse · Continuous
Characters: Lena, Noah · (screen: Mara)

LENA (low): She's painting me as the threat. So when I show up to stop the wedding, I'm the dangerous one and she's the victim.

LENA (steady): One reporter. The one who's already watching her. She films the part the others won't.

## EP30 - S#1  Night / Interior / Cross Manor · Mara's Suite
Characters: Mara · (phone: Eileen) · (intercut: Lena, Noah)

△ Mara sits on the edge of the bed in the dark. The replacement wedding gown hangs across the room. A phone glows on speaker beside Mara.

MARA (small): I can't sleep. I keep seeing her walk into the wedding.
EILEEN (V.O., flat): Then put more men on the doors.
MARA (uneasy): He looks at me sometimes like he's trying to remember something.
EILEEN (V.O., cold): Then kiss him until he stops thinking. I raised a coward. You got the face and the ring. Stop crying because she found the better man.
MARA (small, bitter): She got a man who'd do anything for her. I got one who keeps his eyes shut so he doesn't have to look at me.
EILEEN (V.O., cold): Take her name. Take the house. Take the man. Leave her outside begging. That's how you sleep at night, baby.

△ Mara taps the phone dark. Mara scrolls, then stops, thumb frozen on the screen.

LENA (quiet, to Noah): I can't make a whole city believe me. So I hand one reporter every slip Mara's made on camera, and let her be the one asking, on the record.

△ Lena drags the grabs into an email to the cemetery reporter and sends it.

△ Mara opens the stolen account. Mara taps an old saved video and props the phone against the lamp.

## EP31 - S#1  Night / Interior / Cross Manor · Banquet Hall
Characters: Mara, a guest, Ethan, Victoria, guests · (outside: Lena)

△ The night before the wedding. Mara sits at the head of the long table in white. Guests crowd the tables, glasses up.

LENA (V.O.): The night before she marries Ethan in my name.

△ [INTERCUT] Outside the hall, at a lit service window in the dark. Lena watches through the glass, hood up. Lena does not try the door.

△ At the head table, a guest leans toward Mara with her phone face-up in her hand, loud enough for the table to hear.

GUEST (bright): Mara — that reporter still won't drop it. All the little things you keep getting wrong about your own mother. People are starting to wonder. Tell me it's nothing.

△ Mara holds her smile. The smile stays up one beat too long. The nearest guests go quiet to listen.

MARA (recovering, sweet, an edge under it): People will say anything for clicks. My mother and I had our own language. (lifts her glass) To family.

△ A few glasses lift. A few stay flat on the table.

△ The guest sets her phone down face-up, the reporter's post still glowing.

△ [ECU] Mara's glass raised, smile bright, the table half-quiet around her.

△ Ethan looks up from his plate at Mara. Ethan frowns.

△ [INTERCUT] In the dark at the service window, Lena watches the quiet spread around Mara. Lena steps back into the dark.
