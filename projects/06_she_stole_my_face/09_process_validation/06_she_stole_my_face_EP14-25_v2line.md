## EP14 - S#2  Morning / Exterior / Glenmoor Cemetery
Characters: Lena, Mara, Groundskeeper, Young Reporter, press

△ Walking out, Lena's eyes catch the one lens in the cemetery not pointed at her — the young reporter's.
