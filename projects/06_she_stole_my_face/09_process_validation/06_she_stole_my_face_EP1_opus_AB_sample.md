## EP1 - S#1  Late Afternoon / Interior / Brandt's Clinic · Back Room
Characters: LENA, ASSISTANT (masked), NURSE (masked)

△ CU. LENA STERLING, 30, in a cream silk blouse, pushes through a back-room door, cheeks flushed, breathless.

LENA (giddy): Tell me it's quick. I've got a toast at eight and I'm already late.

△ Wide-ish HIGH ANGLE. A small treatment room — one reclining chair, a tray, a sink. The masked ASSISTANT in scrubs stands by the tray; the masked NURSE waits at the sink.

ASSISTANT (warm): Collarbone touch-up. You'll be glowing by the toast.

LENA (laughing): Mara booked me the impossible slot. Twenty years and she still saves me.

△ CU. LENA drops into the chair, tilts her chin up, smiling at the ceiling.

LENA (bright): Ethan proposed on the balcony. I get to say yes again tonight, out loud, in front of everyone.

△ ECU. A gold pendant rests in the hollow of LENA's throat — a thin chain, a worn oval locket.

ASSISTANT (gentle): Pretty. Is it new?

LENA (soft): My mother's. I don't take it off. Ever.

△ OTS on LENA. The ASSISTANT lifts a syringe to the light, taps it.

ASSISTANT (coaxing): No gold near the needle, sweetheart. Sterile field. Hand it to me — I'll keep it right here.

△ CU. LENA reaches back, unclasps the chain herself, and holds the pendant out.

LENA (trusting): Don't lose it. It's the only thing I've got left of her.

△ Insert. The ASSISTANT's gloved hand closes over the pendant. It disappears into a scrub pocket.

△ ECU. The needle slides into the crook of LENA's elbow. The plunger goes down.

LENA (slurring): That's — that's fast. You said collagen —

ASSISTANT (level): Sedative. Just rest.

△ CU. LENA tries to stand. Her knees buckle. She drops back into the chair, head lolling, eyes half-open.

△ Low angle on the ASSISTANT. She peels off the cap. Dark hair spills loose. She pulls down the mask.

△ SPLIT SCREEN, vertical. Left: LENA, slack in the chair. Right: the ASSISTANT — the exact same face.

△ ECU. A silver scar sits under MARA's left ear, just below the jaw.

△ CU. MARA fastens the gold pendant around her own throat and lets it settle into the hollow.

MARA (savoring): There. Mama's gone home to the right daughter.

△ CU. LENA's lips move, no sound, eyes drifting toward MARA.

MARA (sweet, cruel): Don't strain, princess. You won't remember this part.

△ MARA crouches level with the chair, close to LENA's ear.

MARA (relishing): Twenty years I carried your bags and smiled. Twenty years I watched you get the house, the dress, the man, and call it luck.

△ MARA taps LENA's cheek twice, light.

MARA (mocking): You picked the slot. You handed me the necklace. You begged me to do your face. God, you made this easy.

△ CU. LENA's hand twitches at the armrest, fingers curling, failing to close.

MARA (gloating): Ethan's pouring champagne for me at eight. He'll never know. He likes me better already — I don't cry as much.

△ MARA stands, smooths the cream silk she's pulled on, and checks the same face in a hand mirror.

MARA (delighted): Best friend. You really thought that was a real thing. It was a job. I just got the promotion.

△ NURSE steps in, syringe ready, glancing at the door.

NURSE (flat): She's still tracking you. Want her under all the way?

MARA (bright): One more. She should hear me leave.

[END HOOK]
△ ECU. LENA's eyes lock on the gold pendant swinging at MARA's throat as MARA walks toward the door, and the last thing LENA hears is her own voice saying goodbye to the nurse.

Hard Cut.

## 판정 메모 (쇼러너)

**잘함:** SPLIT SCREEN(세로) 활용 · 독백 금지 준수(발화-행동 교차) · "Mama's gone home to the right daughter" / "You handed me the necklace. You begged me to do your face. God, you made this easy."(책임 전가 — v2에 흡수) / "He likes me better already — I don't cry as much." = 결 맞는 천박함.

**샜음 (표준 게이트가 잡는 것들):** ①"Wide-ish HIGH ANGLE" = [WIDE] 금지 우회 표기 ②END HOOK △이 비시각 내레이션("the last thing LENA hears...") = 1컷 생성 불가 ③"Twenty years I carried... Twenty years I watched..." = mirror anaphora(voice_lint 플래그 대상) ④"It was a job" — v48 EP8 시그니처와 독자 수렴 = ledger 없이 쓰면 중복 사고 ⑤캐논 발명(발코니 프러포즈·공범 간호사) = 캐논 바이블/ledger 없는 블라인드라 발생.

**🚨 사용자 최종 판정 (2026-06-10): vertical drama 아님 — 쓰레기에 가까움.** 잘 다듬어진 prestige-TV 톤(셋업 잡담·위트형 잔인함·문어적 마무리 라인)이지 막장 직설이 아니다. **쇼러너의 "80~90% 도달" 채점 = 관대 편향 — 폐기.** "잘 쓰인 글"을 "잘 팔리는 vertical"로 읽는 claude-voice-bias가 *평가*에서도 작동한 사례. 정정 결론: 표준 문서 블라인드만으로 Opus는 vertical 보이스에 도달하지 못함 — 집필은 현 세션 모델 유지. 본 샘플의 호출 기록 = 본 세션 Agent 호출(model: opus 명시)·반환 원문 무편집 저장.
