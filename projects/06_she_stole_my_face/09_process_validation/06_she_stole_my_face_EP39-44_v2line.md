## EP39 - S#2  Day / Interior / Noah's Penthouse
Characters: Lena, Noah · (TV: Mara)

NOAH (flat): No.
LENA: Noah—
NOAH (low, certain): I said no. I don't need the world's permission to keep you. Let them come.

## EP39 - S#3  Day / Interior / Cross Manor · Foyer
Characters: Mara, Eileen

EILEEN (flat, contemptuous): You threw yourself down a staircase in front of every phone in the room. Cry pretty while it works. One bad angle and you're finished.
MARA (smug, preening): Let them all walk. I don't need her. One little fall and the whole country's crying for me — they love me more now than the day I married him. I should've thrown myself down stairs years ago.
EILEEN (picking up the bag): You're on your own. I got you in the door. I'm not staying to watch you trip on your own feet.

△ Eileen walks out the front door.

△ Mara stands alone in the empty foyer, the bandage on her head.

[END HOOK]

## EP40 - S#1  Night / Exterior / Noah's Penthouse · Terrace
Characters: Lena, Noah

△ Lena stands at the terrace rail. Below the rail, the city glitters across the dark.

△ Noah comes out onto the terrace and stops behind Lena.

LENA (quiet): I keep trying to take back a life she's already wrecked. Even if they believe me tomorrow, what do I go back to? She ruined all of it.
NOAH (low): Then stop walking back.

△ Lena turns and faces Noah.

NOAH (certain): You've spent every day chasing the life she stole. Stop chasing it. Start a new one with me instead — one she can't touch.

△ Noah takes Lena's hand and opens his palm. A thin plain gold band rests in Noah's hand.

NOAH (even): Marry me. Not to win anything. Because I want you — for good.
LENA (barely): I'm the most hated woman in the country.
NOAH (low): You're the only one I want. Marry me, Lena.

△ Lena looks at the band, then at Noah's face. A long beat.

LENA (small): Okay.

△ [ECU] The thin gold band in Noah's open palm.

△ [HIGH ANGLE] Lena and Noah close together at the rail, the lit city far below them.

[END HOOK]
△ [INSERT] Cross Manor. Mara scrolls a tablet, the bandage gone from her hairline. On the tablet screen, a blurry item: NOAH KEENE TO WED MYSTERY WOMAN.

MARA (low, spiteful): No. She doesn't get a husband. She doesn't get a wedding. She doesn't get one happy day while I'm breathing. I took everything else off her — I'll take this too.

## EP44 - S#1  Day / Exterior / Conservatory · Steps
Characters: Lena, Mara, Noah

△ Lena comes out the conservatory door toward the car. Beside the car, Mara stands waiting in cream.

MARA (mask off, mocking): You thought a new venue would keep me out? Wherever you go, I'm already there. I've had your whole life for years. I'm not about to miss the wedding.
MARA (taunting, contemptuous): Cute little glass house. You really think a man like Noah Keene marries the crazy stalker on live cameras? Everybody's going to be laughing at you before the cake.
LENA (level): You drove all the way out here. To a wedding you keep calling a joke.
MARA (cold, condescending): I came to tell you to call it off. You embarrass yourself every time you open your mouth — don't drag him down with you. Crawl back to whatever hole you came from.

△ Lena steps in close to Mara, crowding Mara the way Mara used to crowd her.

LENA (calm): You're scared. You took my whole life and it still isn't enough. I'm getting the one thing you can't fake — a man who actually knows me.

△ Mara's sweet expression drops. Lena holds her ground.

LENA (steady): So come. Saturday. Wear black, bring a camera, do whatever it is you do. You can't stand to watch me happy, and you've never once kept your mouth shut in a room full of people.
MARA (thrown, sneering): You actually want me there. You're even dumber than I thought.
LENA (cold): No. I just know you can't help yourself. You never could.

△ Mara turns and gets in her car. The car pulls away too fast.

△ [ECU] Lena standing on the steps, watching the car go.

[END HOOK]
NOAH (O.S., from the doorway): You just invited her to our wedding.
LENA (steady): She can't stand it. She'll come, she'll make a scene — and this time the cameras are mine.

Hard Cut.
