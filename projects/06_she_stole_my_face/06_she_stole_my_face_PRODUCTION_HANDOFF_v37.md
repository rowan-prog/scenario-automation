## 2. The logic behind it (not on the page)

- **This is not a mystery.** The audience sees the whole face-swap in EP01 — nothing to "reveal" or keep suspenseful. The pleasure is dramatic irony: we know, the world doesn't. Never shoot it as a whodunit.
- **The world believes the fake from EP09 to EP48.** Only Lena and Noah (and we) know the truth that whole stretch. Never let the world quietly "start figuring it out" — that kills the engine.
- **The fake wins because she's shameless and the world is dumb — not because she's clever.** Tone is makjang: broad, brazen, absurd *and* infuriating. Villains are vulgar and never sympathetic. Do not make it tasteful, cool, or prestige.
- **Proof never works — that's the point.** No one is convinced by the scar, photos, or any evidence. The truth only lands when the fake destroys herself by confessing (EP48). So never solve a scene with a document, file, CCTV, or gadget.
- **Lena is wronged but not a doormat.** She takes it without breaking and works to make the fake crack in public.

## 3. Calls already made (don't re-open)

- **The two women have the SAME face** (surgery). Telling them apart is the #1 job → §5.
- **Mara is Lena's jealous friend who stole her face — NOT a sister or relative.** Play no secret-family angle.
- **Ethan is a coward who took the easy lie — NOT a regretful, pining ex.** No longing; he simply turns away at the end.
- **Victoria is vulgar old-money power, not an elegant schemer.** She says ugly things out loud.
- **EP37 — Mara throws herself down the stairs on purpose.** We see her do it; the room doesn't. Not an accident, not ambiguous.
- **Mara is never redeemed.** She ends alone in a bare room. Don't soften or sympathize with her exit.
- **Heat = T2 only** (kiss / clothed), Lena ↔ Noah, at EP15 / 23 / 38 / 45. Two-shot / embrace / silhouette framing — never a standalone nude.

## 5. Telling Lena and Mara apart (the #1 production task)

Same face. In every shot, distinguish them by — in this order:

1. **The scar (Mara only):** a faint curved scar just below the **left ear** + one on the **right inner wrist**. Normally hidden by hair/styling; shown only in close confirmation cuts.
2. **Wardrobe:** Lena restrained slate / navy / ivory · Mara glossier stolen looks → black → stripped.
3. **Hair:** Lena lower and cleaner · Mara more styled.
4. **Attitude:** Lena still and contained · Mara performed-sweet, or vulgar in private.
5. **Context:** whoever the world is adoring = Mara · bare neck = Lena.

**Never hold both faces in a long static shared frame that asks "which one is real?"** — use OTS, cutaways, position, single coverage.

## 7. Visual priorities

- **Modern chaebol gloss** — marble, glass, gold, tailored suits, gowns. NOT period / fantasy / anime / gray-realism / cosplay.
- **Palette tells the story:** Cross Manor cold marble + gold = the fake's stolen power · Noah's penthouse warm low light = Lena's safe place · glass conservatory sea-light = recovery.
- **Keep it brain-off:** one glance reads who's disbelieved (Lena) and who's adored (Mara). Carry information on screen — TV chyrons, phone feeds, raised crowd phones — not in exposition.
- **Spend the best AIGC budget on the hero beats:** EP01 (the swap + Lena seeing her own face on a TV), EP08 (paywall ballroom), EP24 (Mara's mask snarls on camera), EP37 (the staircase), EP47-48 (wedding crash → confession), EP49-50 (the turn + reclaim).
