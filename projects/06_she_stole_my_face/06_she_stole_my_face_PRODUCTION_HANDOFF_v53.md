## 1. Production Authority

Production may refine or adjust staging, blocking, specified camera shots, pacing, and visual detail when it better serves the scene's core purpose and impact, especially in key payoff, reveal, intimacy, reversal, or cliffhanger scenes. ## 2. Core Pleasure

Primary screen appeal: a stolen face, a world that worships the thief, and the real woman they all call crazy — until the fake breaks herself on camera.

- The pleasure is dramatic irony. The audience sees the full face-swap in EP01-02. We know; the world does not. Never shoot it as a whodunit.
- The fake wins because she is shameless and the world is dumb — not because she is clever. Tone is makjang: broad, brazen, absurd, and infuriating. Do not make it tasteful, cool, or prestige.
- Cruelty is relational and intimate, not spectacle. The intensity axis is how personal and casual the cruelty is — peers filming Lena as content, a friend's wrong funeral flowers, a saint act whispered three feet away. Not scale, not crowds, not set-piece size.
- Money beats: EP01 cold open (two identical faces, scar ECU), EP08 paywall ballroom + Mara's "I forgive her. I do." INTERCUT, EP18 the gala snarl on camera, EP37 staircase self-throw, EP47-48 wedding crash into confession, EP49-50 the reclaim and return.
- Two planted tells the audience holds all series: the scar (EP01 cold open ECU → EP01 end hook mirror → EP03 cracked window gust → pays off EP49 Mara's own hand covering it) and the engraving (EP01 Lena's thumb at graveside → EP03 Mara bored: "It's gold and it's mine" → pays off EP49 Lena says "Lumen meum" out loud and claims it back). Shoot them as deliberate plants.

## 3. The Logic Behind It (not on the page)

- **The world believes the fake from EP09 to EP48.** Only Lena and Noah (and we) know. Interim cracks are about Mara's likability, never the secret: the snarl clip (EP18 → EP30/35 → EP49), one private believer (the woman in pearls, EP26), sponsors and friends drifting (EP36). Forbidden: anyone deducing the swap before EP48.
- **Proof never works — that's the point.** No scar photo, document, or footage convinces anyone before EP48. The truth only lands when Mara destroys herself by confessing ("I should have KILLED you in that chair!!" — EP49). Never solve a scene with evidence.
- **Lena is wronged but not a doormat.** She lands visible un-reversed wins across the middle (EP11 bracelet text, EP18 gala snarl, EP19 clip, EP26 believer's handhold, EP44 bait). Noah sets the table; Lena lands every killing blow herself.
- **Ethan's denial is cowardice, not villainy.** He checks himself twice — EP27 V.O. ("What the hell is wrong with me?") and EP32 V.O. ("Her neck was red") — and chooses comfort both times. EP49: one silent flash of knowing before he turns his back on Mara. No dialogue, no reaching for Lena.
- **Victoria is complicit, not oblivious.** "She suspected and chose the family's comfort" (v42 note). Her final shriek — "Turn those OFF!" — is self-preservation, not shock.
- **"Princess" is Mara's word, never anyone else's.** Mara's private sign-off ("Sleep good, princess" EP03 / "you princesses" EP07) boomerangs at EP10: Mara says it on Q&A live; Lena: *"Mom called me bug. Princess is what SHE calls me."* Do not put this word in any other character's mouth.
- **Tone cues in dialogue:** short parentheses (1-3 words: `(sweet, cruel)`, `(low)`, `(flat)`) are binding emotional direction for that line. Written so non-native crews cannot misread tone. Do not ignore or invert them. AI dubbing is the primary delivery model — every line is designed for tone-independent single meaning. The 13 V.O. instances (Lena 9, Ethan 2, Mara 2) are a separate audio track.

## 5. Telling Lena and Mara Apart (the #1 production task)

**Single casting rule: one actor plays both Lena and Mara.** All doubles scenes use the same performer; distinguish by post, split-screen, OTS, or position. Every shot pair must be identifiable without dialogue.

In every shot, distinguish them — in this order:

1. **The scar (Mara only):** fresh curved silver line below the left ear + line on the right inner wrist. Normally hidden by hair/styling. Shown in close confirmation cuts and three planted beats: EP01 cold open ECU / EP01 end hook mirror / EP03 cracked window gust. Hard Lock: same position, same appearance EP01-50.
2. **The pendant:** Mara wears it EP01-48. Lena is bare-necked EP02-48. The pendant is on Lena's throat in EP49-50 only — and Lena puts it on herself.
3. **Wardrobe:** Lena restrained slate/navy/ivory — Mara glossier stolen looks, then black dress (EP47), then stripped.
4. **Hair:** Lena lower and cleaner — Mara more styled and performed.
5. **Attitude:** Lena still and contained — Mara performed-sweet or vulgar-snarling.
6. **Context:** whoever the world is adoring = Mara; bare neck = Lena.

**Long lace sleeves (EP33 wedding gown):** Mara's wedding dress has long lace sleeves to both wrists. This conceals the right inner-wrist scar line for the wedding sequence. Confirm with wardrobe: the lace must fully cover the wrist in all ceremony shots.

**Never hold both faces in a long static shared frame that asks "which one is real?"** Use OTS, cutaways, position, single coverage. EP01 cold open split-screen is the deliberate exception — it cuts to black before resolving.

## 7. Key Locations (continuity- and geometry-critical)

### EP37 Grand Staircase — Helena Sterling Foundation Gala
**Hard location requirement:** the staircase must have a **curved upper section where the balustrade blocks line-of-sight from the floor below.** Story logic: Mara climbs past the curve, checks down at the cameras, pulls back, then throws herself forward — all in a zone the floor cannot see until she is already in motion. Without the blind spot above the curve, the self-throw is visible from below and the scene's dramatic irony collapses.

- Lena stands just below the curve, off to one side, an arm's length from Mara — visible from the floor.
- Noah is still climbing toward the curve when Mara jumps — he sees it clearly; the phones below do not.
- The confirming shot: [HIGH ANGLE] Mara tumbling, Lena above her. The phones from the floor see only the end-frame.

Confirm geometry before location scout sign-off.

### Conservatory (EP43-50)
Glass walls, sea-facing. Must have: a glass aisle with sea light behind the arch; a bridal suite with a full-length mirror; doors that bang open onto the aisle (EP47 crash entrance). Livestream camera stand at the aisle side — the cameraman Mara brings in EP47 is a separate rig, handheld.

### Cross Manor
Cold marble and gold — the fake's stolen power. The service yard (EP31 choking) must be dark, enclosed between wall and hedge, with a service door that throws light into the yard when opened.

### Noah's Penthouse
Warm low light, glass city wall. Home base. The terrace (EP40 proposal) needs a full city view at night.
