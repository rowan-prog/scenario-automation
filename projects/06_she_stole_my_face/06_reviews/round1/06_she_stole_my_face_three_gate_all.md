## Gate 1 — Commercial Gate

**[PATCH]**

EP01-09 are commercially excellent. The mid-arc (EP12-15, EP25, EP38-47) drifts into HBO-procedural register — press atriums, board votes, formal apologies, prosecutorial language. For a 25-45 NA female paid vertical viewer who is here for *personal* humiliation→reclamation, "informal industry consequence" / "unanimous vote" / "deposition" / "grant category" beats are commercial poison. They are not viscerally satisfying — they are competent corporate drama.

- **EP01-08 free-zone escalation = STRONG.** S#1 graveside necklace → S#3 mirror double → S#4 forced cosmetic + crash → S#5 Mara takes pendant from rain ("You always wore it like it was just a necklace") = top-tier hook. EP02 phone call ("I think you've got the wrong number") + EP03 dragged off the steps + EP04 dashcam bracelet match + EP05 Tessa wire slip + EP06 *Lumen meum* inscription + EP07 bridal room infiltration + EP08 "MARA EILEEN VOSS" on the giant screen with the live record-light. Each EP raises the public-humiliation stakes one rung. Loop is rotating stage/witness/evidence/stakes correctly.
- **EP08 paywall = the strongest in the build.** Six-foot legal name on the wall + frozen toast + Lena's voice over the ballroom speakers + Mara's nape exposed + "WATCH WHAT SHE STOLE. EP 09 NOW." Five-component pawall stack (public name reveal · live record-light · audio override · necklace at her nape · the room going wrong-quiet). EP09 buys this promise cleanly — "Don't touch it. You don't get to touch it anymore." + the necklace clasp not opening + Ethan's "Take it off, Mara." 
- **🚨 EP12 onward bleed into administrative-procedural register.** "The board has you, the prosecutor has you" / "Will you tell a judge what you just told me?" / "I'll stand in the room" / "we are not running a press conference, we are running a deposition" / EP38 "voluntary audit · disclosure waiver" / EP44 "transfer hearing · plea agreement · probation" / EP45 lectern apology / EP47 "vote was unanimous · chair of the company · grant category two — medical reauthorization." This is vertical-no-admin-power's bullseye violation. It is **not** automatically commercial — it is competent-novel commercial, which is exactly the bucket NA vertical audiences pay less for. Per the rule: any character (incl. protagonist) doing hearings/decrees/votes/depositions/grants = re-frame to personal violence + visceral claim.
- **Repeat-reward loop holds through EP09-EP16 then weakens EP17-EP30** where Lena's takedowns become press releases and family council scenes. EP10's antechamber "Take it off, Mara." / "Not from your hand." / Noah fastens the clasp is the last *visceral* reclamation. EP13 Tessa's verdict-stare, EP25's two-corner exit photo-walk, EP38 voluntary audit are commercially correct in intent but *passive*. The audience wants Lena's mouth on Mara's neck, not Lena's PR head's mouth on a press affiliate.
- **EP35 reveal is well-executed.** Mara's mother Eileen on speakerphone is a *personal* takedown — voice, kid in apartment, "Good girl. That's my girl." reveal. This is the model the back half should follow, not the press atrium.
- **EP44 + EP48-50 HEA is well-paced** but the "I love you" budget runs at the limit (EP16 mutual / EP48 private vows mutual / EP49 public vows mutual = 3 pairs / 6 lines — exactly at the ceiling. Verified clean.). Mara airport farewell + Lake house + Pesto + cork board postcards = warm and earned. EP46 mother's letter at the grave reads it aloud (good) but the prose VO is heavy.
- **Setup-before-payoff = OK on the major plot props** (mother's necklace EP1 → EP6 inscription → EP8 paywall → EP9-10 reclamation → EP49 wedding worn at throat; Mara's bracelet EP1 → EP4 dashcam match → EP9 onstage). **But Mara's surgical scar is reveal-prop matched** at EP07 S#4 ("two centimeters under her left ear, the faintest curved scar") and at EP09 S#3 ("the thin curved scar two centimeters under her left ear stands out") — borderline prop-anchored. Per hard_rules.md #11 + memory/feedback_vertical_protagonist_voice_ownership.md, reveal triggers must be dialogue/VO-driven; scar is OK as confirming visual but should not be the *triggering* evidence. Currently it shares trigger weight with the file — acceptable but at the line. Recommend reduce the scar visual lean from "stand out for the first time in front of two hundred people" (which reads as the reveal beat) to a confirmation beat *after* Lena names the file.

## Gate 2 — Native Spoken English Gate

**[PATCH]**

Voice differentiation between Lena (alto, full sentences, holds herself together) / Mara (running someone else's lines, faster, sharper when caught) / Noah (warm low baritone paragraphs) / Ethan (grave baritone, apologizes in paragraphs) / Victoria (cold contralto, old-money diction) is largely held. Zero Korean characters. Almost no 1-3 word ping-pong sequences. The script is unusually clean on theater cadence — but it carries three patterns that need surgery, two of which are *the* patterns hard_rules.md auto-blocks.

1. **EP05 EP-end chyron — tri-colon anaphora** (차원 3·차원 1):
   `**FRIENDS DON'T DO THIS. STRANGERS DON'T DO THIS. SISTERS DON'T DO THIS.**` (line 202)
   → Three identical-cadence fragments, parallel structure, recitation rhythm. Hard auto-block per hard_rules.md "정전 쓰레기 형태 1건 → 즉시 폐기." Patch: `**She wasn't a stranger. She was supposed to be family.**` (or stronger: one line, no triple.)

2. **EP05 in-dialogue tri-colon punchline** (차원 1·3):
   `LENA: ...— I bury you. With pleasure, Tess. With pleasure.` (line 177)
   → Writerly mirror-echo punchline. Lena's voice canon says "thinks before she speaks; once she speaks, she finishes the thought." This is the opposite — a self-conscious tag. Patch: `If you talk to her, if you warn her, if you go to one person — I'll bury you, Tess. And I won't be sorry about it.`

3. **EP10 dialogue tri-colon** (차원 3·4):
   `MARA: She did this to me. She did this to me. We were friends since we were sixteen.` and later `VICTORIA: We will speak. Privately. Soon.` (line 129)
   → Mara's panic repeat is defensible as in-character (charm cracking, falters into sharper register per visual_lock 3-1). Victoria's "We will speak. Privately. Soon." is three short fragments in a row — old-money diction is OK but per her voice canon ("When she breaks (EP15), she breaks into a longer, lower line — not a fragment"). Patch Victoria to: `We'll have that conversation privately. Soon, but not in this room.`

4. **EP08 paywall lower-third** is clean (`**WATCH WHAT SHE STOLE. EP 09 NOW.**`). Most lower-third chyrons across the script are clean — but two more read writerly:
   - EP06: `**SHE WORE MY MOTHER FOR SIX DAYS. SATURDAY I TAKE HER BACK.**` — parallel structure, two-fragment, fine commercial cliffhanger.
   - EP07: `**SHE WON'T LOOK FOR ME. SHE'LL SEE ME.**` — mirror echo (`Won't X. Will Y.` parallel). 차원 3 borderline. Patch: `**TONIGHT THE BRIDAL ROOM TELLS THE TRUTH.**` or `**SHE'S STOPPED LOOKING. THAT'S HOW I FIND HER.**`

5. **EP24 long monologue blocks — 차원 6 violation (uncomplicated sentence structure).** Mara's three-paragraph self-explanation (lines 47-69) stacks 4-5 subordinate clauses per sentence: *"the version of me that had agreed to go this far was someone I could not get back to"* / *"I was told, from the time I was old enough to understand it, that I was Helena Sterling's secretly-rejected daughter"*. This is novelistic prose, not human spoken English. A 25-45 NA actress would struggle to land it as natural confession. Patch direction: break each sentence to 1-clause units, keep meaning, drop embedded modifiers. Eg: `My mother told me from the time I was a kid that I was Helena Sterling's hidden daughter. She showed me documents at twenty-four. She said you had what was supposed to be mine. I believed her. I want you to understand I believed her then.`

6. **EP46 Helena's mother's letter (EP46 lines 41-50)** — Helena's prose is similarly embedded and clause-stacked. Letters can be more written-feeling, but Lena reads it aloud. Trim subordinate clauses, keep meaning, let the audience hear it.

7. **VO heavy in mid-arc** — Lena's VO in EP04, EP08, EP16, EP25, EP38, EP41, EP44, EP46 frequently runs 3-5 sentences with literary cadence: *"I keep waiting for it to hurt. It doesn't. Not yet. What it does is something colder than hurt. It feels like a room going very quiet right before you understand what someone is about to say."* (EP04) — beautiful prose, but 차원 7 violation (metaphor-dodging, "colder than hurt", "a room going very quiet right before"). Per feedback_screen_rhythm_v3, VO target = EP당 1-2문장 이하. Many EPs hit 3+. Patch: trim each VO to 1-2 short sentences, drop the simile/metaphor explainer, let the picture carry.

8. **EP12 procedural register (차원 1·6).** `LENA: Doctor. The board has you, the prosecutor has you, and the news outside will have you by lunch. I am not here to hurt you. I am the only person walking into this room today who can speak for you in front of a judge.` Triple-anaphora ("The board has you, the prosecutor has you") + judge/board/prosecutor stack + administrative language. Patch: `LENA: Three people are coming for you today. I'm the only one who isn't going to make you wish you'd left town last week. Tell me what you did.`

9. **EP38 / EP47 corporate-board cadence.** EP47 line 24 (Lena's four-things-to-say): *"First — I'm not going to change the company. ... Second — ... Third — ... Fourth — ..."* = enumerated parallel structure, 4-point board-meeting cadence. 차원 3 + 차원 1. Patch: rewrite as one short flowing line about her father's company + foundation + Noah, no Roman-numeral list cadence.

10. **EP09 Lena's onstage line** is excellent and worth flagging as the *model* the script should match elsewhere: `My name is Lena Sterling. I'm the woman this engagement was supposed to be for. The woman beside me is Mara Voss.` — direct, plain, native, no tricolon. This is the voice the back half needs.
