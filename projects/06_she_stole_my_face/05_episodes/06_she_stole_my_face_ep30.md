# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP30

## S#1. North Gate · Dusk

Iron gate. NOAH's car back, lights off.

LENA
She's in there with Mara now.

NOAH
We move — she walks Mara out the back.

LENA
Then I'm going to the man who built the title.

NOAH
Bannon.


## S#2. Whitfield's Study · Forty Minutes Later

LENA
Eileen Voss is alive.

WHITFIELD
Known since the third week after her funeral. Six years.

LENA
And you didn't tell me.

WHITFIELD
Mother made me promise.

LENA
You lied to me for six years.

WHITFIELD
Promised to keep you alive. I'd do it again.


## S#3. Small Library · Later

LENA
Mother put Eileen on Halcyon. And Eileen is alive. You knew.

BANNON
Suspected since the funeral. Your mother picked Eileen for the backup line — she had the most to lose if Mara ever found out.

LENA
She picked the wrong woman.

BANNON
Mother wrote a notarized affidavit six months before she died.

LENA
Where is the safe.

BANNON
Behind your grandfather's portrait. Second floor. 1968. 1995.
