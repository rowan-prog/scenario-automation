# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP13

## S#1. Sterling Holdings. Boardroom.

Head chair — Helena's — empty eight years. Lena walks in. Bannon behind. Adele waiting.

Lena lays her coat over the second chair. Not the first.

ADELE
Welcome back. Reinstated by unanimous vote.

LENA
I take the chair tomorrow.

BANNON
Ethan's downstairs. Twenty minutes. Asking for ten.

LENA
Lobby. Standing.


## S#2. Lobby.

Ethan waiting. No ring box.

ETHAN
Thank you for coming down.

LENA
Ten minutes.

ETHAN
I knew. The morning of the announcement.

ETHAN
Clasp didn't catch the way it did for you. Earring half a millimeter wrong. Vowel in her *good morning* a fraction lower. Noticed between seven-fifteen and seven-thirty. Let her come down anyway.

LENA
And you let her walk down the stairs.

ETHAN
I let her walk down.

Ethan pulls out a folded sheet.

ETHAN
Resignation from the joint board. Mother keeps hers — that's your fight.

He extends the page. Lena takes it.

LENA
You picked the right thing to bring.

ETHAN
Wanted to bring the necklace. Understand now why I didn't get to.

LENA
That's progress.


## S#3. Continuous.

ETHAN
Not asking for anything. I know what I lost.

LENA
Don't write. Don't call. Same table — you nod and leave.

ETHAN
Yes.

LENA
Goodnight, Ethan.

ETHAN
Goodnight, Lena.

[END HOOK]
Lena turns. Doors close on Ethan still standing.

Phone buzzes. UNKNOWN:

*Helena's daughter. The trust was for you. Find me before she does.*

Already walking to Noah before the doors open.

Hard Cut.
