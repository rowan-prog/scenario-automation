# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP05

## S#1. Brandt Clinic — Service Entrance · Friday Night

Back alley. Noah in a delivery jacket. Lena in a cap and mask. Stolen keycard.

NOAH
Eight minutes. Cleaners come at ten.

LENA
What am I looking for.

NOAH
Her file.

LENA
And if she's in there.

NOAH
She's at Cross Manor with Ethan.

She slips inside.


## S#2. Records Room · Continuous

Lena swipes. Drawer S unlocks. STERLING, LENA.

Opens the folder under phone flashlight.

LENA
That's not my handwriting.

[INSERT] WITNESS SIGNATURE: TESSA HALE. Six weeks ago.

LENA
(barely)
Tessa.

Lifts the page. Another consent.

[INSERT] PATIENT: VOSS, MARA EILEEN. SCAR MAP — left ear curve, right inner wrist linear.

LENA
Mara Voss.

Door clicks. Footsteps.

Phone-snaps the pages. Stuffs the folder back in V.


## S#3. Hallway · Continuous

Tessa Hale — Lena's friend — in a coat, envelope in hand.

TESSA
(low, to nurse)
The Sterling file. Is it gone?

NURSE
This week.

TESSA
And if Brandt asks?

NURSE
We say the patient asked for deletion.

TESSA
Good. She'll have the rest Monday.

LENA
(whisper)
Tessa.

Heels click past. Stop.

TESSA (O.S.)
Smells like rain. Did someone come in the back?

NURSE (O.S.)
Cleaning crew tracked it in.

Door shuts.

[END HOOK]
Lena texts Noah:

> She paid Tessa. Three guards at the front. Pull the van to the loading bay.

Boots. Closer. Lena flat behind the row of cabinets.

GUARD (O.S.)
Records room. Now.

Door bangs. Beam sweeps the floor. Stops six inches from her shoe.

Across the hall — fire alarm bursts. Sprinklers. Guard turns.

Noah, soaked, badge clipped wrong.

NOAH
(low)
Loading bay. Walk. Don't run.

Out into the rain. Tessa at the curb, keys in hand. Sees them. Doesn't move.

LENA
(passing her)
You don't exist to me.

Hard Cut.
