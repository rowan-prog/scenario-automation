# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP40

## S#1. Eileen's apartment · Night

TV muted. Lower-third: "HALCYON TRUST DISCLOSURE FILE NOW PUBLIC — EILEEN VOSS NAMED."

EILEEN
(dialing)
It's me. One more job tonight. Bring a second man. Same price.


## S#2. Mara's apartment · Same hour

Shoebox on the counter. Mara at the sink. Milo with a bandage roll.

MARA
You don't have to.

MILO
Hold still.

Floorboard creaks. Chain lock not engaged.

MARA
(fast)
Milo. Back stairs. Now.

MILO
Mara—

MARA
GO.

Door opens before Milo clears the kitchen. Two men in dark coats. Second takes Milo by the shoulder.

LEAD MAN
Not here for you. Here for him. Follow us — that changes.

Bandage roll on the floor.


## S#3. Old Brandt clinic · Night

Defunct clinic. Mara through the freight bay.

MARA
Milo.

Freight elevator doors open. Floor — Milo's phone. Mara steps in.

Doors slide shut. Pale haze through the seam.

MARA
(dialing)
Lena. Listen.

INTERCUT — Lena in Noah's car.

MARA (V.O.)
Old Brandt clinic. Freight elevator. Doors won't open. Smoke coming up.

LENA
Nineteen minutes out. Noah's calling it in.

MARA (V.O.)
She's burning this.

LENA
Lie down. Stay on the line.


## S#4. Lena's car · Continuous

Other phone rings. UNKNOWN.

LENA
Eileen.

EILEEN
You choose. Come to the building. Get her out. Or turn around. Mara becomes a tragedy no one ties to a name they prosecute.

LENA
You hurt her — I spend every dollar my mother left me hunting you.

EILEEN
Mara was always going to die in a fire. Question was whose.

(ends the call)

[END HOOK]
LENA
Mara. Eleven minutes. Breathe low. Stay on this line.

Smoke against city light. Freight bay door open three feet.

Hard Cut.
