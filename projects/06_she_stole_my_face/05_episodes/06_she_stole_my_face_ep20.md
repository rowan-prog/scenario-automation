# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP20

## S#1. Halcyon Filing depot. Late night.

Sub-level storage. Noah through the stacks. Lanyard — M. ELLIS.

CLERK
Forty minutes.

NOAH
Row forty-one. 1995. Settlor S.

Label — HELENA L. STERLING — 1995.

[GRAPHIC]
DATE OF EXECUTION: 14 DECEMBER 1995
BENEFICIARY DOB: 17 DECEMBER 1995 (corrected once)

NOAH
That date is wrong.

[INSERT] ECU — correction tail. Different pen.


## S#2. Lena's apartment. Late night.

Sealed letter on the desk. Phone rings.

[INTERCUT] Noah in his car.

NOAH
DOB overwritten. December seventeenth ninety-five sits on a half-erased two thousand. Eileen made Mara look older on the trust.

LENA
The record was altered.

NOAH
I'm coming over.

Picks up the letter.

[GRAPHIC]
*My Lena.*
*If you're reading this — a woman with my face has come for you.*
*The trust is Halcyon. For a girl named Mara. Her father — Julian — a man I loved before your father. Named her mother third.*
*Mother is Eileen Voss. Do not trust her. Under any circumstance.*
*I love you. — Mama.*

Lena folds the page.


## S#3. Continuous.

Opposite curb — the same black car.

LENA
You don't get another night across from my window.

Dials Noah.

LENA
Don't come up. Loop the block. Man across the street has been at this curb three nights.

NOAH
Lena —

LENA
Loop the block.

Picks up her coat.

NOAH (O.S.)
Lena — stay inside.

She doesn't answer. Already opening the door.

[END HOOK]
Lena walks to the elevator. Presses GROUND.

Hand catches the doors. Forces them back.

Woman steps in. Surgical scar under left ear. Mara.

Doors close. Mara hits EMERGENCY STOP.

MARA
Going down?

Hard Cut.
