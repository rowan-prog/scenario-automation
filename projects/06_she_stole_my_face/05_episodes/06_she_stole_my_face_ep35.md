# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP35

## S#1. Living Room · Continuous from EP34

Mara over the phone on the coffee table. Speaker on. Lena two feet behind, own phone to her ear.

LENA
(phone)
Noah. Apartment six, 442 Atwater. Kid in there. Female intruder. Send a unit.

NOAH (V.O.)
Eight minutes out.

LENA
Mara. Eight minutes. We hold her.

MARA
She'll kill him.

LENA
She's bluffing.

MARA
She doesn't bluff.

EILEEN (V.O.)
Sixty seconds. Don't make me explain.

MARA
Don't touch him.

EILEEN (V.O.)
Front door. Now.


## S#2. Moments later

Mara walks to the front door. Lena blocks the path.

LENA
Five minutes. Let Noah's people get there first.

MARA
Move.

LENA
She wants you on a road where I can't see you.

MARA
She touches him because of five minutes — I never forgive any of you. Move.

Lena steps aside.

LENA
Take Noah.

MARA
No. She'll see him.

Mara opens the door.

EILEEN (V.O.)
Good girl. That's my girl.

Mara stops in the doorway. Hand to the scar.

MARA
(very quiet)
Stop calling me that.

She hangs up. Walks out.


## S#3. Front Window · Sixty seconds later

Noah in the back door.

NOAH
She left.

LENA
She left.

NOAH
Unit's six minutes from her apartment.

Lena's phone vibrates. Photo — Lena's face on the lake house gate camera. Second photo — Noah on the ridge.

LENA
She had a man on the road.

[END HOOK]
TEXT FROM UNKNOWN: "Saw you arrive. Saw him on the hill. Don't follow my daughter. She isn't yours. — E."

Mara's headlights disappear. Noah already redialing.

Hard Cut.
