# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP37

## S#1. Storage office · Afternoon

Mara up the back stairs. Shoebox. Red notebook. EILEEN at the window.

EILEEN
You came.

MARA
Sit. At the desk.

Old fracture on her left thumb.

MARA
You wrote in my diary when I was fourteen.

EILEEN
I put the secret there.

MARA
Why.

EILEEN
I gave you a purpose. A name worth wanting. Without me — what would you have been? Surgeon's assistant in a borrowed apartment. Girl with a boy named Milo, going to bed early.

MARA
You took my name out of my mouth. Mara Voss. My father was Julian. You taught me to be embarrassed.

EILEEN
And now you want it back.

MARA
Yes.

EILEEN
(soft)
Too late. Last move is made.


## S#2. The screen

Eileen turns on the TV. Lower-third: "BREAKING — STERLING HOLDINGS' HALCYON TRUST UNDER FEDERAL REVIEW."

Audio — woman's voice, slightly digital:

> "Worked with Ms. Sterling for two years. Money never went where the trust said."

MARA
That isn't me.

EILEEN
Has to sound like you for one news cycle. By the time anyone proves it isn't, she's lost the board.

MARA
You cloned my voice.

EILEEN
Forty seconds from an interview.


## S#3. The walkout

Mara tucks the notebook inside her coat.

EILEEN
She'll be too busy keeping her name out of the dirt to do anything useful.

MARA
You don't know her.

EILEEN
Sit down, Mara.

MARA
No. You raised me wrong.

EILEEN
(soft)
You'll come back. You always have.

MARA
That's how I know this is the last time.

[END HOOK]
Mara pulls the door shut. Eileen lifts the desk phone.

EILEEN
Move.

Cut to Lena's office. Every screen showing the same story.

LENA
Press call. Today. We give them the whole file.

Hard Cut.
