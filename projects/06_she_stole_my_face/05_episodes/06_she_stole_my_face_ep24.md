# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP24

## S#1. Entry Hall · Night

Lena undoes the deadbolt. Mara in the corridor.

MARA
Thank you for opening it.

In. Sit. Hands where I can see them.


## S#2. Living Room · Continuous

Noah at the island.

MARA
I'm not apologizing tonight.

LENA
I didn't ask you to.

MARA
Mother told me since a kid I was Helena's hidden daughter. Documents at twenty-four. Didn't know I'd be made into a face. Didn't know about Brandt until the chair. Threw up the first time I saw myself.

LENA
And the crash.

MARA
Didn't drive that car. But I know who did.

MARA
Want someone else in the room. He's downstairs.

LENA
Who is he.


## S#3. Entry Hall · Five Minutes Later

Doorbell. Julian Voss — mid-sixties, gaunt, dark coat.

MARA
Lena. Julian Voss. My real father.

JULIAN
Ms. Sterling. I'm sorry for what your mother didn't get to tell you.

LENA
You knew my mother.

JULIAN
Thirty-one years.

LENA
Voice on the phone in Mara's apartment. Was that you.

JULIAN
No. Eileen had access to Halcyon money. She's been using my daughter as a face since the surgery healed.

LENA
You can prove it.

JULIAN
Safety deposit box in Lisbon.

LENA
Mother wrote his name in her own hand.

She wrote it in her own hand.
