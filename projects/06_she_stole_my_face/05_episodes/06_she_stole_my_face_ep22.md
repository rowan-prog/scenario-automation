# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP22

## S#1. Rooftop Terrace · Morning

Twelve chairs. Times, Journal, AP. Lena steps up. Necklace. No notes.

LENA
Good morning. I'll be brief. For weeks a woman who isn't me has been representing herself as me. Mara Voss. She stood on that stage at the reception under my name.

A wave through the press row.

LENA
Yesterday she contacted me with a threat. Wants money. Says she has my mother's private papers. Not paying. Not today. Not ever. If she has something to say, she can say it here. On a microphone. Her own name on it.

LENA
Mara — if you're watching — your boss is using you. I'll find him before you do. Questions.


## S#2. Mara's Safe Apartment · Morning

Press conference on the wall screen.

LENA (ON SCREEN)
…your boss is using you.

MARA
She said my name.

She dials.

VOICE (V.O.)
I'm watching.

MARA
She named me on camera.

VOICE (V.O.)
Then we go tonight. Walk in clean. Let her think she chose this.

MARA
And the letter.

VOICE (V.O.)
Letter's hers to open. Be in the room when she does.


## S#3. Press Wing · Continuous

Lena steps off the podium. Andrea falls in beside her.

ANDREA
Times goes wide at noon. AP on the wire. You good.

LENA
I'm good.

ANDREA
Legal wants you at two.

NOAH
You said her name twice.

LENA
I considered four. She'll move tonight.

NOAH
She will.

LENA
Then we go home.

[END HOOK]
Black screen. Voice — alone, no face.

VOICE (V.O.)
Helena. You raised a daughter who fights forward. But she's about to read a letter you didn't finish.

Typewriter strikes. Single bell.
