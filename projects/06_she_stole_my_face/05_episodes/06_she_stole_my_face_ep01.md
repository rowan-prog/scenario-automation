# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP01

## S#1. Glenmoor Cemetery · Tuesday Afternoon

Lena kneels at her mother's headstone. HELENA LOUISE STERLING · 1968-2018. Gold pendant slips out — inscription inside: LUMEN MEUM.

LENA
Mom. I'm getting engaged tonight. I wish you were here.

Phone buzzes.

LENA
Okay. I'm coming.


## S#2. Cross Manor — Ballroom · Tuesday Evening

Ethan Cross at his cufflinks. Victoria watches Lena cross the floor.

ETHAN
You're late.

LENA
I went to see my mom.

VICTORIA
(sharp)
The pendant's showing.

LENA
It's hers. I'm wearing it.

VICTORIA
We agreed on the Cross diamonds.

LENA
You agreed.

ETHAN
Go see Dr. Brandt. For me.

LENA
The skin thing is fine.

ETHAN
For me.

[GRAPHIC] DR. BRANDT · 7:30PM.


## S#3. Brandt's Clinic · Tuesday Evening

Lena in a robe. Tall mirror. Behind it — one-way pane.

A woman steps to the glass. Same height. Same hair. Same face.

Lena reaches. The other reaches back exact same time. Lena's hand stops. The other keeps moving.

LENA
Who is that. Who is that.

Bangs the wall.

LENA
Open the door!

NURSE
(blank)
There's a wall behind the mirror, hon.

LENA
Don't touch me.

Out.


## S#4. Coastal Road · Tuesday Night

Lena driving, hands shaking. Headlights behind.

LENA
It was a mirror. Just a mirror.

Car behind rams her bumper. Spin. Glass.

Black.


## S#5. Roadside · Moments Later

Smoke. Lena upside-down in the belt. Gloved hand reaches in. Clasp snaps.

LENA
(barely)
...mom's...

[END HOOK]
Figure straightens. Lena's face. Mara Voss smiles down. Loops the pendant around her own neck.

MARA
Looks better on me anyway.

Walks away. Siren wails far off.

Hard Cut.
