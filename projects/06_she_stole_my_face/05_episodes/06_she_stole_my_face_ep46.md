# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP46

## S#1. Glenmoor Cemetery · Mid-morning

LENA on the gravel alone. Letter in hand. White peony.

HELENA LOUISE STERLING. Lena kneels.

LENA
Hi, Mom. Brought the last piece.


## S#2. The Last Page

[INSERT] Helena's handwriting:

> Lena. Something I never wrote down. Eleven years I sent money to Eileen Voss and her daughter. Julian — the man I almost married before your father — had a child with her. I knew. Let Eileen keep the girl quiet on a stipend instead of asking her in. Afraid sharing you with her would cost me more than I had to give.
>
> If she ever shows up in your life, look at her once before you decide who she is.
>
> Keep the foundation work if you can. Rather you be alive than dutiful.
>
> I loved you well. I tried.
> — Mom

Lena lowers the page.

LENA
(cracking)
Mom. I think I forgive you. Both of you. Still mad. I'm here.

She stands.


## S#3. Gate · Late Morning

Noah at the passenger door — brown paper bag, two coffees.

LENA
You brought lunch to a graveyard.

NOAH
Soup place by the river.

LENA
Then home.

[INTERCUT] Postcard taped at eye height. Grey-blue water, lighthouse.

[INSERT] Mara's handwriting:

> Reading the ocean. Water here is too cold to swim. — M.

[END HOOK]
Lena leans her forehead against the door. Phone rings. BANNON.

LENA
What.

BANNON (filtered)
Board calling an emergency vote. Three days. Take the chair tonight or I tell them no.

LENA
Chair. Monday.

BANNON (filtered)
One more thing.

Pause too long.

BANNON (filtered)
Eileen's lawyer filed at four. Custody motion. Wants Mara recalled stateside to testify. Federal subpoena clears at midnight.

LENA
She doesn't come back.

BANNON (filtered)
Then we burn the subpoena before it lands.

NOAH
Six hours.

LENA
Then six is what we use.

Hard Cut.
