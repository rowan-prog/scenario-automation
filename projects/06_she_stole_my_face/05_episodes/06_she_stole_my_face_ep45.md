# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP45

## S#1. Sitting Room · Mid-morning

VICTORIA in dove grey. ETHAN in charcoal. LENA opposite. NOAH at her elbow.

VICTORIA
Week of the engagement, a young woman was nearly killed in a car my family arranged. While she was in a hospital bed, I let another woman take her name and seat. Want you to know I know.

(beat)

Helena Sterling Foundation — Cross funds it jointly with Sterling. No review board.

ETHAN
(low)
When you came to my engagement party in a hospital coat, I sent you out. Looked at the woman beside me, told myself I knew her. Didn't. I'm sorry, Lena.


## S#2. Lena's Turn

LENA
I'll judge the paperwork, not the apology.

(beat)

Woman in the Mediterranean used to be called by my name. Has a postcard coming. No comment. Ever.

VICTORIA
You'll have it.

ETHAN
From me too.


## S#3. Front Hall · Ten Minutes Later

VICTORIA
Paperwork tomorrow.

LENA
Thank you.

VICTORIA
Still useful, if you decide.

LENA
You'll know.


## S#4. Apartment Building · Late Afternoon

Halfway up — postcard against the brass mail slot. White town on a cliff above blue water.

JULIAN VOSS in the chair by the window. Sixties. Stands slowly.
