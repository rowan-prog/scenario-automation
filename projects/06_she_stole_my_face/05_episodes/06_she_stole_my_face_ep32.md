# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP32

## S#1. Sitting Room · Afternoon

Victoria in a wingback. Lena across. Noah at the doorway. Lena drops a folder on the table.

LENA
You were the second trustee. Halcyon. My mother named you.

VICTORIA
Yes.

LENA
And the surgery on Mara's face.

VICTORIA
Didn't authorize. Didn't know until six months after.

LENA
But you suspected.

VICTORIA
Long enough.

Halcyon co-trustee record. Victoria's name highlighted.

LENA
Who's the third name.

VICTORIA
Never met her. Your mother kept that one buried.


## S#2. The folded paper

Victoria reaches into her purse. Folded cream paper.

VICTORIA
Helena gave me this the day she went into the hospital. Three names. *If anything ever happens, find the third.*

LENA
Why didn't you.

VICTORIA
Coward. Four years pretending I never received this.

Three names on the paper, Helena's slanted hand:
1. JULIAN VOSS
2. VICTORIA CROSS
3. EILEEN MARGAUX VOSS

LENA
Eileen Voss.

VICTORIA
Woman in the small cemetery. Headstone Mara brings flowers to.

LENA
Then your third trustee is dead.

VICTORIA
That's what I assumed.

LENA
Pull the death certificate. Now.

NOAH
No certificate. Only the headstone.

LENA
We already know she's alive.

VICTORIA
Then why did you come here.

LENA
Because you had her name. And you let me find it last.

[END HOOK]
LENA
Tomorrow my face walks into her daughter at the lake house. You knew this name was alive. Let me go in blind.

VICTORIA
Lena—

LENA
I'm not done with you.

She walks. Front hall — Victoria's housekeeper, frozen with a silver tray. The tray's slipping.

HOUSEKEEPER
Ma'am — she walked in twenty minutes ago. Said you were expecting her.

Lena turns. Sitting room — second wingback. Empty when she walked in.

Cushion warm. Cigarette in the ashtray, still smoking. Lipstick on the filter. Plum.

NOAH
Same plum on Mara's first hospital photo.

LENA
She sat across from me. Listened.

VICTORIA
(very quiet)
She's been in this house before. Hasn't she.

The housekeeper says nothing.

Hard Cut.
