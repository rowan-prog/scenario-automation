# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP15

## S#1. Front steps. Morning.

Press at the base. Victoria through the doors alone.

REPORTER #1
Mrs. Cross — does the family stand by the bride —

VICTORIA
Woman at the reception was not Lena Sterling. She is Mara Voss. I knew the first night something was wrong. I'm saying it out loud now.

VICTORIA
Lena Sterling was the bride. I let another woman take her place.

She walks back up.


## S#2. Sitting room. Late morning.

Lena enters alone.

VICTORIA
Sit.

LENA
Why am I here.

VICTORIA
I didn't fail to see Mara. I chose not to see her.

LENA
Why.

VICTORIA
Two business deals depended on the engagement. And — part of me — wasn't sorry that the daughter I couldn't control got swapped out for someone I could.

LENA
Say the last sentence again.

VICTORIA
Part of me was glad your mother was dead and you were a problem and she was easy.

LENA
(hand over her mouth)
Good. Say the rest.


## S#3. Continuous.

Victoria pulls a drawer. Cream envelope. Wax seal intact.

[KEY CAMERA]
- ECU envelope — *For Lena, in confidence*

VICTORIA
Brought it three months before she died. *If a woman ever claims a seat that isn't hers, give it to my daughter.*

LENA
Tell me the name.

VICTORIA
Julian Voss. Mara's father. Your mother knew him long before your father. Trust was her apology — any child of his looked after *provided that child stayed clear of you.*

LENA
The child has not stayed clear.

VICTORIA
Eileen's been moving Halcyon money for sixteen years.

LENA
She anticipated the wrong woman.

VICTORIA
She anticipated the wrong woman.

[END HOOK]
LENA
Reading this somewhere else. Not this house.

She turns to the door. Pulls it open.

ETHAN — wet from the steps, blocking the threshold.

ETHAN
Don't read it without me.

LENA
Move.

ETHAN
Mara called my line forty minutes ago. *Your* private line. From inside your apartment.

He turns his phone. Voicemail waveform. Lena's own voice, smiling:

MARA (PLAYBACK)
*Hi sweetheart. Stopped by. Borrowed a sweater. The blue one I love.*

LENA
She has my keys.

ETHAN
She has more than that.

Hard Cut.
