# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP48

## S#1. Lake House · Friday Afternoon

LENA pulls up in NOAH's car.

Brown rescue dog at her knees. MRS. HEINEY follows. Red collar — PESTO.

LENA
(crouching)
Worst welcome committee in this county.

LENA
(to Noah)
Moved a dog in without telling me.

NOAH
Surprise was the part you'd like.

LENA
Pesto stays.


## S#2. Upstairs Bedroom · Friday Evening

Wedding in 48 hours. Two velvet ring boxes on the bench.

NOAH
You first, or me.

LENA
Me. If I wait I'll edit it again.


## S#3. The Private Vows

Lena opens the box. Plain platinum band.

LENA
(quiet)
Most of the time you knew me I was trying to prove I exist. You showed up before I knew. I show up before you ask.

(beat)

I love you. Wanted to say it tonight before forty people.

She slides the ring on. Noah opens his box.

NOAH
(low)
Six years investigating people I didn't trust. First time you told me what happened — knew I'd heard truth first time in years. Not a feeling I'll live without.

(beat)

I love you. Glad I get the short version.

He slides the matching band on.

LENA
One kiss. We agreed.

NOAH
One.

[KEY CAMERA]
- ECU lips meeting — slow, open

Lena's hand closes on his forearm.

LENA
(against his mouth)
Two days.

NOAH
Two days.


## S#4. Bedroom · 11:47 PM

Both in bed, fully dressed. Pesto at the foot.

LENA
Don't let Uncle Robert give a toast tomorrow.

NOAH
I'll handle Uncle Robert.

[END HOOK]
Three raps at the front door. Pesto's head up. Noah sitting up.

NOAH
Stay here.
