# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP29

## S#1. Cemetery · Late Afternoon

Eileen Voss's headstone. Mara at the path. Same white stocks.

She kneels.

MARA
(low)
You weren't here yesterday. Stood behind me. Touched my shoulder.

Sets her hand on the stone. Granite warm. Pulls back like it burned her.


## S#2. Down the Slope

NOAH's car. LENA in the passenger seat. Binoculars low.

NOAH
You can go up there.

LENA
She'll stop talking if I do.

LENA
She brought flowers twice.


## S#3. At Eileen's Stone

MARA
You're not in there.

MARA
Stone's warm. Yesterday cold. Grave's empty. You buried yourself so I'd visit and not look for you.

MARA
Fed me a story twenty years. Helena's daughter. Helena's apology. Lena holding what was mine.

MARA
You're alive. Watching me ask a stone for permission.

She turns. Walks past. Doesn't look back.


## S#4. After Mara Leaves

LENA
She knows.

NOAH
She knows.

LENA
Said it for Eileen. Listening from somewhere on this hill.

Black car at the rectory wall.

[KEY CAMERA]
- ECU woman's hand at the wheel. Ring on left fourth finger.

NOAH
She won't let Mara leave with that knowledge.


## S#5. Lower Road · Continuous

Sedan tucks in behind Mara.

LENA
She's following Mara back to where she's keeping her.

NOAH
We follow her there.

[END HOOK]
Iron gate. Hand-painted sign: STERLING ESTATE — NORTH.

LENA
That's my mother's property.

NOAH
She's been hiding on Sterling land.

LENA
Six years on Sterling land.

[GRAPHIC]
> EILEEN VOSS IS STILL ALIVE.
