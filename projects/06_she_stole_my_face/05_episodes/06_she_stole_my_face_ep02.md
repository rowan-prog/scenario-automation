# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP02

## S#1. Harbor General — Room 412 · Thursday Evening

Lena's eyes open. IV in her arm. Bandage on her temple. Hand goes to collarbone. Pendant gone.

[KEY CAMERA]
- ECU bare collarbone, hand patting chest

LENA
(croaks)
My necklace.

NURSE
You had nothing on you.

LENA
My phone.

NURSE
Bag's gone. Whoever hit you didn't stop.

TV catches her eye.

LENA
Turn it up.


## S#2. Same Room · Continuous

TV: LIVE — CROSS MANOR ENGAGEMENT — LAST NIGHT. Ethan at the podium. Beside him — Lena. Smiling. Wearing Lena's pendant.

[KEY CAMERA]
- ECU TV pendant — ECU Lena in bed, face going dead

ETHAN (TV)
Lena and I are honored —

LENA
That's not me.

NURSE
Honey, they said you were home with a migraine —

LENA
That is not me.

Rips the IV out. Blood. Grabs the phone. Dials.

LENA
Mara. Pick up.

[INSERT] Outgoing: MARA. Two rings. Click.

Dials again. Voicemail. Throws the phone. Cracks.

LENA
MARA — pick UP — !


## S#3. Hospital — Hallway · Continuous

Lena, barefoot, gown half-tied. Slams through the stairwell.

GUARD
Ma'am — you can't —

LENA
I'm Lena Sterling. That's not me on TV.

GUARD
(into radio)
Need a doctor on 4. Confused patient.

She shoves past. Runs.

[END HOOK]
Bursts into rain. No shoes. No phone. Across the street — TV in a coffee shop window. Her face. Smiling. Engaged.

She hits the glass with both hands.

LENA
THAT'S NOT ME — !

She slides down. People walk past with phones up. Nobody stops.

Hard Cut.
