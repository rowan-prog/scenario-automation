# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP03

## S#1. Cross Manor — Front Steps · Thursday Evening · Rain

Press line. Lena pushes through. Hospital gown under a stolen raincoat. Bandage soaked.

LENA
(to a guard)
I need to see Ethan Cross.

GUARD
Invitation, ma'am.

LENA
I'm the bride. I'm Lena Sterling.

GUARD
(short laugh)
The bride's already inside.

She tries to push past.

LENA
Ethan! ETHAN!


## S#2. Front Steps · Continuous

Doors open. Ethan steps out. Behind him — Mara. Lena's face. Lena's pendant. Lena's gown.

[KEY CAMERA]
- ECU pendant on Mara's chest

LENA
Ethan.

MARA
(soft, to Ethan)
She's scaring me. How does she know our names?

LENA
Ethan, it's me.

ETHAN
Stop saying that.

VICTORIA (O.S.)
Move her.

Victoria glides into frame.

LENA
Victoria, look at me.

VICTORIA
I am looking.

LENA
Then say you don't know me.

VICTORIA
I don't know you.

VICTORIA
And you're bleeding on my steps.

MARA
(to crowd)
She really thinks she's me. I'm so sorry.

Lena lunges. Two guards catch her.

LENA
I'm LENA STERLING — !

ETHAN
(quiet)
Get her off the property.

Mara glances back. Smiles. For Lena only.


## S#3. Side Driveway · Continuous

Guards push her out. Gate clangs.

LENA
(low)
She knew me.

Black car eases up. Window down. Man — dark coat.

NOAH
Get in.

LENA
What?

NOAH
You're bleeding on the curb in front of forty cameras.

LENA
Who the hell are you.

[END HOOK]
NOAH
Someone who knows you're not crazy.

Lena gets in. Car peels off.

Hard Cut.
