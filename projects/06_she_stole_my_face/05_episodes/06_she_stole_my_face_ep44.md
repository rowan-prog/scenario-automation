# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP44

## S#1. Holding Facility — Cell · Morning

EILEEN in pale institutional blue. Guard at the door. Folded paper.

[KEY CAMERA]
- ECU paper — TRANSFER REQUEST — DENIED

GUARD
Transfer denied. You stay in this wing. Permanent.

EILEEN
Understood.

GUARD
Hearing's set for the 14th.

Cell door swings shut. Bolt slides.


## S#2. International Terminal · Afternoon

MARA beside LENA. Mara in camel coat. NOAH ten paces back.

Newsstand cover — EILEEN at the airfield, cuffs visible. Red headline: **MOTHER ARRESTED — THE WOMAN WHO PAID FOR HER DAUGHTER'S FACE.**

Mara stops. Teenager lifts her phone halfway, sees Mara's eyes, lowers it.

Mara turns away.


## S#3. Before Security · Continuous

MARA
You could have let her finish me. In that elevator. Noah said you had three minutes.

LENA
She doesn't get to pick. I picked you. Get up.

MARA
Not thanking you. You'd hate it. You'd rather I go somewhere your name doesn't reach and sit in a room.

LENA
That's what I'd rather.

MARA
Once I have a kitchen somewhere I send a postcard. One. After that, you know I'm fine.

LENA
I can do a postcard.


## S#4. Security Gate · Continuous

Mara hands her passport across.

MARA
(turning back)
I'll send it when I figure out who I am.

LENA
Take your time.

She turns. Walks through.

[KEY CAMERA]
- Long lens, Mara becoming smaller

[END HOOK]
Phone buzzes — forwarded alert from Bannon:

*JULIAN VOSS LANDED LAGUARDIA — ASKING TO SEE YOU TONIGHT.*

LENA
Where's the car.

NOAH
Out front. What is it.

LENA
Mara's father. He's here.

Hard Cut.
