# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP07

## S#1. Bridal Room C · Saturday Early Evening

Lena, in catering wig and uniform, sets the garment bag on the chaise. Mara at the vanity, back to her.

MARA
Hang the steam bag in the closet.

LENA
(low)
Yes, Miss Sterling.

MARA
Lock the door behind you.

Lena locks it — from the inside. Click.

LENA
(her own voice)
Hi, Mara.


## S#2. Continuous

Same face, same height in the mirror.

MARA
How did you —

LENA
The catering badge. Eight minutes.

MARA
You shouldn't be alive.

LENA
Your car hit me twice. Cameras are very clear.

MARA
What do you want.

LENA
Take off the pendant.

MARA
Walk out of this room. I give you money. A lot.

LENA
What does the inscription say.

MARA
"For my daughter."

LENA
Wrong.


## S#3. Continuous

Mara grabs the hairbrush.

[KEY CAMERA]
- ECU scar 2cm below the ear, curved

LENA
You won't hit me with that.

Lena catches Mara's wrist. Pushes hair back. Scar.

Phone up. Photo.

LENA
Got you.

MARA
(very quiet)
Lena. Don't.

LENA
You stole my face. You stole my mother's necklace. What does the inscription say.

MARA
I don't have to tell you.

LENA
That's the answer.

LENA
Eight thirty-five. Speeches at nine.

MARA
You walk out of that door and we will bury you.

LENA
You already tried.

[END HOOK]
Lena pulls the door open. Two of Victoria's private security.

GUARD
(into earpiece)
South wing. Now.

Lena bolts back through. Slams. Locks.

MARA
Eight minutes was generous.

Guards rattle the handle.

Hard Cut.
