# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP04

## S#1. Noah's Office · Friday Morning

Three monitors. Lena hasn't slept.

LENA
Why are you helping me.

NOAH
I was hired to watch you. Cross Industries. Two months ago.

LENA
You were spying on me.

NOAH
I was watching the people watching you.

LENA
So why aren't you selling me out.

NOAH
Because the woman who walked out of Cross Manor doesn't move like you. You hold your phone left. She holds it right. When you're nervous, your hand goes to your throat. Hers to her hair.

LENA
Prove it.


## S#2. Same · Continuous

NOAH
You weren't hit by accident.

LENA
(quiet)
I know.

Sedan reverses. Figure leans into the wreck. Straightens.

[INSERT] Figure's wrist. Diamond tennis bracelet.

LENA
That's the woman wearing my face.

NOAH
She wore it on Ethan's arm at the announcement. Eight angles.

LENA
She was at the crash. Then walked into Cross Manor in my dress.

NOAH
And kept the bracelet on.
