## S#2. The Video

Mara unfolds the letter. Picks up the phone, sets it upright.

MARA
(to camera)
My name is Mara Voss. This morning my mother sent a letter from custody. I'll read it. Tell you what she's doing in every line.

(lifts the letter)

*I have been thinking about you every night.* Bait. She knows I'll only read on if I believe the woman who tried to kill me has been thinking about me.

(beat)

*Only we can know what we were to each other.* That's where she tells me everyone around me is trying to take her place.

(turning the page)

*There is nowhere else for you. Come home.* Fifteen years she made me believe that. She's wrong. I have somewhere else. Sitting in it.

I'm putting this where she can't take it back.

PUBLISH.
