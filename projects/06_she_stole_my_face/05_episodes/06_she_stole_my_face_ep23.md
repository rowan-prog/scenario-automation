# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP23

## S#1. Kitchen · Evening

Noah lets himself in. Two grocery bags.

NOAH
Dinner. Not arguing.

LENA
Wasn't going to.

LENA
Haven't opened the third page. Handwriting's shaking. She wrote it knowing she wouldn't get to say it.

NOAH
You don't read it alone. When you say. I'll be in the room.


## S#2. Kitchen · Continuous

Her hand onto his forearm. He stops. Right hand up to her jaw.

She closes the distance. He kisses her. Slow. Open mouth.

She pulls back. Forehead against his collarbone.

NOAH
Pan's going to scorch.

LENA
Move the pan.

He reaches blind, slides the pan off. Doesn't let go of her face. She laughs — once. Real.
