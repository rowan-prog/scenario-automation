# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP09

## S#1. Ballroom · Saturday Night · Minutes Later

Screen still glowing. Mara strides to the mic.

MARA
(Lena's voice)
Friends. That woman has been stalking me for weeks.

Lena, wig gone, walks in from side stage.

MARA
Photos are doctored —

LENA
Stop.

MARA
Get her out.

LENA
Nobody touch me.

Climbs onto the stage.


## S#2. Continuous

LENA
You're lying. There's a scar. Dr. Whitfield — please come up.

Gray-haired man climbs the steps.

WHITFIELD
(to Mara)
May I.

MARA
(steps back)
Don't touch me.

LENA
Just LOOK.

Whitfield pushes the hair back.

WHITFIELD
(to the room)
Surgical scar. Recent. Not a childhood scar.

LENA
Open the pendant.

MARA
I don't have to do anything for you.

LENA
You wore it for four days. Say my mother's name.

(silence)

LENA
Say it.

(silence)

LENA
(breaking)
Lumen meum. My light. My mother carved it when she was dying. You wore it four days. Didn't even know how to open it.

ETHAN
Lena.

LENA
Don't say my name right now.


## S#3. Continuous

Detective Ryan. Two uniforms.

RYAN
Your name.

MARA
Lena.

RYAN
DOB.

MARA
(pause too long)
Eleven. Two. Nineteen ninety-six.

LENA
Ninety-five.

RYAN
Come with me.

VICTORIA
(low)
Detective.

RYAN
Not tonight.

[END HOOK]
Mara opens her fist over the stage. Pendant hits the wood — skitters.

MARA
You want it. You pick it up.

Ryan catches her elbow.

RYAN
Both wrists. Now.

Mic catches Victoria broadcast:

VICTORIA
Get her out before she says anything else.

Phones up.

Lena bends. Picks up the pendant.

Hard Cut.
