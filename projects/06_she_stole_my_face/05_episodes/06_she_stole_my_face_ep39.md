# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP39

## S#1. Glenmoor Cemetery · Sunset

Hillside cemetery above the river. Low sun on white marble. Helena's marker. Lena at the foot of the stone. Noah fifteen yards back.

NOAH
She'll come up the south path. No lights.

LENA
Stay where you are.

Mara at the bend. Dark coat. Wrapped white peonies. Shoebox.


## S#2. The handoff

Mara kneels. Lays the peonies. Sets the shoebox on the headstone. Opens the lid. Leather notebook tied with a black cord.

MARA
The one I didn't show you on the phone. Left it on her desk for me to find.

LENA
What is it.

MARA
Two-year plan. Her handwriting in the margins. First half — surgery, wedding, trust position.

LENA
And the second half.

MARA
Section that begins with "if." Assumes in the first two years of the marriage you'd no longer be a problem. She'd planned that. Chosen the method.

Lena unties the cord. Opens.

MARA
After I was no longer useful, she was going to dispose of me too. Same last page.

Lena closes the notebook.


## S#3. The answer

MARA
I don't have an apology the size of what I did.

LENA
We stop her. Bannon in the morning. Use it against her instead.

MARA
Why.

LENA
She pointed you at me like a gun. You decide if you fire.

MARA
Kinder than I prepared for.

LENA
I prepared a harder one. Don't think I need it.

Phone vibrates. MOTHER. She declines.

MARA
She knows I'm here.

LENA
Noah. We're walking out.

NOAH
Copy.

[END HOOK]
They start down the path. Beyond the gate — black sedan pulls from a side road. Follows at distance.

Hard Cut.
