# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP36

## S#1. Noah's office · Pre-dawn

Wall behind Noah's desk a paper grid. Lena in black sweater. Mara picked up safe four hours ago. Eileen gone.

NOAH
Mara's birth records.

LENA
Tell me.

NOAH
Born St. Agnes Memorial. March 14, 2000. Healthy. Eileen Voss mother line. Julian Voss father line.

LENA
And my mother.

NOAH
Three floors up. Same week. Lost a baby. Second trimester. Twenty-year story built on those two floors.

LENA
Mara wasn't Helena's secret. She was Eileen's daughter and a coincidence.

NOAH
Yes.

LENA
She built it from a coincidence and a stipend my mother paid every quarter for thirty years.

NOAH
She was kind. Kind to the wrong person.


## S#2. Eileen's apartment — Closet · Morning

Brick walk-up. Mara back alone. Apartment too tidy. No photographs.

Mara pulls down a shoebox. School notebooks, one red. Stops on an entry from when she was fourteen.

Different hand — adult, slanted:

> "If she finds the photo of Helena pregnant, run with it. Tell her she's been lied to. Make her believe she was the one rejected. Hold it on her until she stops asking questions about her own father. — Plan A. — E."

MARA
(quietly)
Plan A.

Photo of the page. Lifts the box.


## S#3. Apartment doorway · Moments later

Phone buzzes — private number.

UNKNOWN: "I see you went back. Good. Bring the box to the address I sent. Don't bring the man's wife. — Mom."

Mara reads it. Walks down the stairs.

[END HOOK]
On the sidewalk. Mara looks up at the windows. Walks toward the address.

A black town car eases along the curb beside her. Back window down four inches.

EILEEN (O.S.)
Get in. Save your feet.

MARA
Front-passenger or I walk.

EILEEN (O.S.)
Get in the back.

MARA
No.

The door pops. Driver out — broad, bald, glove on.

DRIVER
Back. Now.

MARA
(stepping off the curb)
LENA — !

She doesn't dial. She yells it down the street. Two pedestrians turn. Driver's hand drops.

[INSERT] Across the street — Noah's car at the corner. Lens up. Frame caught.

Town car peels. Mara walks the other way. Hands shaking.

Hard Cut.
