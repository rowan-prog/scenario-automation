# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP9-13 (v2)

## EP9 - S#1  Morning / Interior / Sterling Apartment Building · Lobby
Characters: Lena, Hector, two tenants

△ A marble lobby. Hector stands behind the front desk in his doorman's coat — twenty years on this door.

LENA (soft): Hector. It's me. There's a box of my mother's things in 14B. I take it and I'm gone.

HECTOR (careful): Miss Sterling came by last night. With Mr. Cross. She said you'd come for her mother's things.

LENA (low): They're MY mother's things. I'm Lena Sterling.

△ Hector turns the desk monitor toward Lena.

△ [INSERT] The monitor — a photo of Mara wearing Lena's face, smiling. Under it: RESIDENT: LENA STERLING.

HECTOR (flat): She said you'd say exactly that, too.

△ Two tenants by the elevator raise their phones at Lena.

TENANT (filming, amused): That's her. The one who cut her own face to look like the heiress.

LENA (to the lens, voice cracking): She STOLE my face. A surgeon cut hers to match mine. Those are my mother's things up there —

△ The tenants laugh and film harder.

## EP9 - S#2  Continuous / Exterior / Sterling Apartment Building · Sidewalk
Characters: Lena, Noah · (studio screen: Mara, aide)

△ Two building guards walk Lena out the front door she grew up walking through.

△ A black car waits at the curb, the door already open. Noah sits inside.

NOAH (flat): Get in, Lena.

△ Lena gets in. The door shuts.

△ [INSERT] The new phone in Lena's lap lights up on its own — a morning show, auto-playing.

△ [INTERCUT] A studio green room, off-air. Mara sits in the guest chair, Helena's pendant at her throat, scrolling the fresh lobby clip of Lena.

MARA (bored, contemptuous): Why do they keep letting her near MY building? My face at every desk in that lobby. Anyone who lets her in is done. Fix it.

△ Mara hands the phone to an aide, walks to the set, and drops into the interview chair.

△ A floor manager points at Mara. A red ON AIR light pops on. Mara smiles, then bursts into soft tears.

MARA (on screen, sweet, dabbing one eye): I forgive her. I do. I just pray somebody gets her help. (softer) My mother always said I had her hands. I feel her every time I look down.

△ In the car, Lena freezes, staring at the phone.

LENA (quiet, the tears stopping): Mom hated her own hands. She kept them out of every photo she ever took.

△ Lena drags the clip back and watches Mara's mouth say it again.

LENA (low, steady now): She gets things wrong. On camera. In front of everyone. And I'm the only one alive who knows it's wrong.

[END HOOK]
△ Lena stares at the frozen screen, dry-eyed.

NOAH: You stopped crying.
LENA (flat): She just handed me the first one.

Hard Cut.

## EP10 - S#1  Day / Interior / Noah's Penthouse
Characters: Lena, Noah

△ [HIGH ANGLE] A glass wall with the city behind it. Lena stands in the middle of the room in borrowed clothes three sizes too big.

NOAH: Closet's stocked. Card's on the counter. Driver's downstairs when you want him.

LENA (small): I can't pay any of this back.
NOAH (matter-of-fact): I didn't ask you to. She's spending your money in your apartment right now. I'm not going to let you stand around in borrowed clothes while she does it.

△ Lena sits down on the couch arm.

LENA (low): Then we go to the police. DNA, fingerprints — one test ends it.
NOAH (flat): Not while every camera in the city has you attacking the bride. They bury you as unstable before the test means anything.
LENA (quiet): So the truth doesn't matter.
NOAH (level): Not until people see it for themselves. Cameras first. Then the rest.

## EP10 - S#2  Day / Interior / Noah's Penthouse · Couch · Later
Characters: Lena, Noah · (screen: Mara)

△ [INSERT] Phone screen — Mara's feed. Mara in Lena's closet, Lena's dresses behind her, Helena's jewelry laid out on Lena's bed. Caption: *Healing. Grateful for my family. 🤍*

LENA (flat, to the screen): That's the box. Hector wouldn't let me up for it, and she's got it spread out on my bed for likes.

△ [INSERT] Phone screen — a live Q&A. A fan's question scrolls up: *what did your mom call you?* Mara answers without a pause. Hearts flood the screen.

MARA (on screen, sweet): Princess.

△ Lena watches the phone. Noah sits beside Lena, not touching.

LENA (flat): Mom called me bug. (beat) Princess is what SHE calls me.

△ [INSERT] Phone screen — Mara raises a hand to her face. A diamond bracelet on her right wrist.

LENA (slow): That's my mom's bracelet. She's wearing it on the wrong wrist. Mom broke her left wrist as a kid — it healed thick, the band never closed over it. She only ever wore it on the right.
NOAH: So she's wearing your mother wrong in front of everyone.
LENA (quiet): And nobody knows but me.
NOAH (quiet): Two of us, then.

△ Lena freezes the feed on the bracelet.

LENA (slow): Every post. Every show. She gets one small thing wrong, every time. Every one she gets wrong, I keep. Someday it all comes out at once.
NOAH: Then keep watching.

## EP10 - S#3  Night / Interior / Noah's Penthouse · Kitchen
Characters: Lena, Noah · (screen: Mara)

△ Noah holds Lena's wrist in one palm and changes the dressing on her hand. Lena watches his hands.

LENA (low): You keep getting this close and you don't ask for anything.
NOAH (low): I'm patient.

△ Lena leans in. Noah does not move toward her. Half a foot of air stays between them.

△ Lena pulls her phone up between them and freezes the feed on Mara's face — Lena's own face — mid-laugh.

LENA (barely): She's going to marry him in my mother's pendant. And they'll all cry for her.

[END HOOK]
△ Lena's face crumples and she cries hard. Noah pulls her in and her head drops against his chest.

NOAH (quiet): Cry. I've got you. They don't have to know tonight. I do.

Hard Cut.

## EP11 - S#1  Morning / Interior / Noah's Penthouse
Characters: Lena, Noah · (TV: Tessa, Host)

△ A talk show plays on the wall TV. Lena sits in front of it.

△ [INSERT] The TV — Tessa on a couch, tissue in hand. A chyron under her: TESSA / LENA'S ASSISTANT, 6 YEARS.

TESSA (TV, tearful): I worked for Lena for years. Looking back, the signs were all there. There was a girl who always copied her — same coat, same hair. We used to laugh about it.
HOST (TV): And you think that's the woman who —
TESSA (TV, small): I think she always wanted to be Lena. Now she's telling people she is.

LENA (barely): Tessa held my hair the night my mother died. Now she's turning me into the joke on TV.
NOAH (low): She sold you again.
LENA (cracking): My mom's voice is in my head telling me to stand up straight and just say it. But every time I say I'm Lena, I'm just the stalker who can't let go.
NOAH: Then stop saying it to them.
LENA: To who, then.
NOAH (level): To me. In here you don't have to prove anything to anyone.

△ Lena turns to Noah.

LENA (small): I'm Lena.
NOAH (certain): I know.

△ Lena's new phone buzzes on the table.

△ [INSERT] Phone screen — UNKNOWN NUMBER. Text: *You looked insane on TV this morning, sweetie. People are laughing AT you. Everybody can see you're the crazy one. — your best friend 🤍*

△ Lena stares at the text, then types.

△ [INSERT] Phone screen — Lena's reply: *Mom's bracelet goes on the RIGHT wrist. She broke the left one — the clasp never fit it. You've worn it wrong on every camera in the city.*

△ [INSERT] Phone screen — the typing dots appear, stop, start, stop. Then: *NUMBER BLOCKED.*

△ Hours later. Lena scrolls to Mara's newest post — same smile, same room. The bracelet is gone from Mara's wrist.

LENA (quiet, to the screen): You read it. And you took it off.
NOAH: Why tip her off?
LENA (level): The old clips already show it on the wrong wrist — those I keep. Taking it off now just tells me I'm right. And tells her I'm watching.

[END HOOK]
△ [ECU] Mara's bare wrist on the frozen post — the bracelet gone.

Hard Cut.

## EP12 - S#1  Morning / Exterior / TV Studio · Street Doors
Characters: Lena, First Woman, Second Woman, Doorman

△ The glass doors of the network where Tessa's show tapes. Behind the glass, a lobby screen loops yesterday's clip of Tessa mid-tear.

△ Lena walks straight at the doors — no hood, her own face bare in daylight.

LENA (steady, loud enough to carry): That show yesterday was a lie. I'm Lena Sterling. The woman in that house —

△ Two women Lena's own age, coffees in hand, turn toward Lena and light up — delighted, not afraid.

FIRST WOMAN (delighted): Oh my god. It's her. The copycat from Tessa's show.
SECOND WOMAN (sweet, already filming): Do the line. Please? Say "she stole my face."

△ [PHONE POV] The second woman's vertical screen frames Lena's bare face, the little red dot on.

△ [INSERT] A phone held up beside Lena's face, comparing Lena to a photo captioned LENA STERLING.

LENA (cracking): I'm not copying anyone. She is wearing MY —
FIRST WOMAN (giggling, to the camera): She's so committed. I kind of love her.

△ A doorman steps in and walks Lena off the doors, both hands soft and gentle. The two women wave bye-bye. Nobody is angry. Nobody is scared.

LENA (to no one): ...she did. She stole it.

## EP12 - S#2  Day / Interior / Noah's Penthouse
Characters: Lena, Noah

△ [INSERT] Phone screen — the sidewalk clip: Lena mid-sentence, the giggle in the audio. Caption: *"SHE STOLE MY FACE" GIRL SHOWS UP AT TESSA'S STUDIO 💀*

△ Lena watches the clip once. Noah watches Lena.

LENA (flat): I screamed it at Hector. At a lobby full of phones. At a TV. And this morning two strangers turned it into a punchline for their feed.
NOAH: And?
LENA (cracking): And the more I scream it, the crazier I look.

△ Lena turns the TV off. The room goes quiet.

LENA (low): I'm done begging strangers to see me. I keep handing her my tears and she keeps selling them.
NOAH: So stop handing them over.
LENA (slow): Tessa told me the one thing. She's terrified somebody catches her faking it. She practiced my laugh in the mirror.
NOAH: So stop proving you're Lena. Make her prove she isn't.
LENA (steel coming up): I use what she keeps getting wrong. My mother. The things she can't fake.

[END HOOK]
NOAH: Where?
LENA (looking at him): Somewhere she can't walk away. Somewhere about my mother — the one place she can't fake.

Hard Cut.

## EP13 - S#1  Day / Interior / Helena Sterling Memorial Luncheon
Characters: Lena, Mara, Noah, Ethan, Victoria, Donor, guards, donors, press

△ [HIGH ANGLE] A room of donors and press. Helena's portrait on an easel. Mara stands at the podium as Helena's daughter, the pendant at Mara's throat.

△ Noah walks Lena in at the back — hood up, a borrowed press badge. His hand flat on Lena's spine.

MARA (at the mic, sweet): My mother opened this lunch every year. She stood right up there and led her favorite hymn, the whole room singing along —
LENA (on her feet, loud, steady): That's a lie. My mother never knew a single hymn — she couldn't read music, she just hummed a beat behind everyone. You'd know that if you were her daughter. You're not.

△ The room goes still. Two older donors turn to each other.

DONOR (low): ...that's true. Helena never knew the first verse of anything.

△ Near the front, Ethan stares at Lena. The room turns toward Lena.

△ Mara bursts into tears.

MARA (into the mic, weeping): Oh my god. She's been digging through my dead mother's things. How long have you been studying us?

△ The room turns back toward Mara. The two old donors do not clap. They keep looking at Helena's portrait.

VICTORIA (rising, cold, to the room): She stole family stories and practiced them. Get her out.

△ Two guards close on Lena and grab Lena's arms.

NOAH (stepping in front of Lena, low and firm): Hands off her. Now.

△ The guards stop. The room watches Noah shield Lena.

VICTORIA (sad, to a reporter): That poor man. She's gotten inside his head too.
LENA (voice rising, losing the room): I'm not studying her. Those are MY memories —

△ Ethan stares at the pendant on Mara's throat, then at Mara's face.

ETHAN (low, to Mara only): You told me your mother taught you that hymn. You sang the first verse for me.

△ Mara turns to Ethan, her tears already coming.

MARA (small, broken, to Ethan): I can't even grieve her without that woman crawling through every memory I have. Don't you start too.

△ Ethan looks at Mara a beat longer, then puts his hand on her back and turns her away from the room.

ETHAN (quiet, to Mara): Okay. Okay. I've got you.

## EP13 - S#2  Day / Interior / Noah's Car · Continuous
Characters: Lena, Noah

△ Noah walks Lena out a side door before more security reaches Lena. Lena shakes in the passenger seat.

LENA (shaken): For one second they heard me. I saw it on their faces. Then she took the same words and made me look insane.
NOAH (even): Not all of them. The two old ones by the portrait — they didn't clap when you left. And they never looked at her again.
LENA (flat): That's nothing.
NOAH: It's not nothing. Right now she's cramming your mother's life, sweating the next thing you know.
LENA (steadier): Then next time I make her lose her temper where they can't cover for her.
NOAH: Now you sound like someone who gets it all back.

[END HOOK]
△ [INSERT] An evening news clip. Chyron: *STALKER RECITED PRIVATE STERLING MEMORIES — FAMILY "DEEPLY DISTURBED."*

△ Lena watches the clip from the floor.

LENA (quiet): I told them something only I would know. They called me a stalker for it.

Hard Cut.
