# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP26

## S#1. Kitchen Island · Early Morning

LENA at the kitchen island. Letter open. Three pages on marble. NOAH at the window.

NOAH
Four times.

LENA
Once. Eyes were moving. Nothing went in.

LENA
Mother had an affair twenty-eight years ago. Julian Voss. Paid him off. Paid the woman with him.

NOAH
That's Mara.

Noah tilts the third page against the morning light.

NOAH
Missed something. Under her signature. Pencil. Almost erased.

LENA
Her shorthand. J.V. — 1999.

NOAH
Year before Mara was born.

LENA
Mother had a reason to want him quiet that had nothing to do with a child.

NOAH
Second story under this letter.

[INSERT] Paperclipped photo — Helena, late twenties, hotel lobby. Man's hand at her elbow. Face cut off.

NOAH
Who else has seen this.

LENA
You. Me. It stays there.

NOAH
Your aunt.

LENA
Not yet.

Lena slides the letter into her coat pocket.

LENA
Mara has to think we still don't know.


## S#2. Small Cemetery · Late Morning

Plain headstones. MARA in black coat walks the gravel path.

[GRAPHIC] > EILEEN MARGAUX VOSS

Mara kneels. Sets white stocks at the base.

MARA
(to the stone)
I read the letter. Lena has it. She'll put it together. That's what she does.

MARA
You weren't here yesterday. You stood right behind me. Say something.

Wind moves the stocks. Nothing else.

[END HOOK]
Gloved hand settles on Mara's shoulder. Black leather. Mara doesn't flinch.

MARA
She opened it.

Gloved hand tightens.
