# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP31

## S#1. Sterling Holdings — Lena's office · Morning

Lena at her desk. Noah pushes the door open. Two phones.

NOAH
She filed it. Eight a.m. Paternity petition.

LENA
What.

NOAH
Mara. Claiming Helena Sterling was her mother. Her own lawyer. Not Victoria's firm.

LENA
She's pushing it alone?

NOAH
She thinks she is.

Lena drops the newspaper. Crosses to the window.

LENA
She's going to drown us both. What do I do.

NOAH
You see her face when she says it.


## S#2. The text · Noon

Lena's phone vibrates. Mara. Long message.

TEXT FROM MARA:
"Stop fighting me through the press. If you have something to say, say it to my face. Lake house. Tomorrow. Just us. — M."

NOAH
Don't answer that here. Don't answer it at all.

LENA
I'm answering it.

NOAH
Lena—

LENA
She comes alone or brings somebody. Either way I find out tomorrow.

NOAH
Lake house has no eyes. No cameras. No neighbors.

LENA
Good. Then she won't bring a witness either.

NOAH
I'll be on the ridge.

LENA
I know you will.

ECU her screen — "Lake house. Three p.m. Come alone." Send.


## S#3. Mara's apartment · Evening

Mara reads Lena's reply by lamp light. Behind her — figure in a dark coat. Gloved hand on the armrest. No face.

FIGURE (O.S.)
Don't go.

MARA
I'm going.

FIGURE (O.S.)
She'll set you up.

MARA
Then I'll see how she does it.

MARA
I said I'm going.

Mara grabs her coat. Door closes behind her.

[END HOOK]
Gloved hand sets the water glass down. Picks up a burner. Single button press.

FIGURE (O.S.)
She's coming alone tomorrow. Send someone to the lake road. Want to know what she brings out of that house.

Hard Cut.
