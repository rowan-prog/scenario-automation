# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP12

TEMPORARILY CLOSED. Federal agents in the lobby. Dr. Celia Brandt at her desk.

Skip what you didn't.

Eleven women. Five years.

LENA
*Eleven.*

Same routing number on wires. Never met the woman who paid.

NOAH
Halcyon Trust.

CELIA
You already have it.

NOAH
I want a face.

Celia slides a photograph out.

[KEY CAMERA]
- ECU photo — woman in charcoal coat, pearl earring, sunglasses

That's the coat my mother wore.

Kept it in case I needed to prove I wasn't her.

You'll say all that with me in the room when the time comes.

Yes.

No.
