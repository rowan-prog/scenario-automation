# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP49

## S#1. Front Door · Two Nights Before · 11:47 PM

Three sharp raps. MR. BANNON — sealed envelope.

BANNON
Mara's plea-bargain. Non-contact clause.

LENA
I'll sign it now.


## S#2. Bridal Room · Wedding Morning

OLIVIA fastens the ivory silk gown. White peony above left ear. Pesto on the sheet.

OLIVIA
Hold still. Breathe.

LENA
(laugh)
Generous use of "breathe."

Knock. VICTORIA in the doorway.

VICTORIA
Won't come in if you don't want.

LENA
Come in.

VICTORIA
(velvet pouch)
Your mother's. She wore it under her collar on her wedding day.

Gold pin into Lena's palm. Lena pins it inside the gown.

LENA
Thank you. I mean that.

VICTORIA
I know. Third row. No speeches.


## S#3. Lakeside Aisle · Ceremony

Forty white chairs. NOAH in morning coat. ETHAN fifth row. Victoria third row. UNCLE ROBERT walks her down.

MR. BANNON
Forty years with this family. Helena would have liked today. Say it.


## S#4. The Public Vows

LENA
Forty people watched you decide I was me before they did. Tonight I do the same for you. I love you, Noah.

NOAH
(low)
First photo I took of you was a surveillance frame. Wrong job. Right woman. I love you, Lena.

Ring exchange.

MR. BANNON
You're married. Go on.

Small clap from Victoria. Then the rest.


## S#5. Reception Montage

[MONTAGE]

1. UNCLE ROBERT: *"To Lena and Noah. The next forty years."*
2. CORK BOARD — seven postcards. Olivia adds today's. *Got a job teaching English. — M.*
3. MARA — small table by an open window. Live feed. Alone.


## S#6. Path to the Car

NOAH
Tomorrow we do nothing.

LENA
Tomorrow we do nothing.

[END HOOK]
Phone buzzes. Bannon: *Board moved the vote up. Monday morning.*

LENA
Monday.

NOAH
Get in. We're going home first.

Hard Cut.
