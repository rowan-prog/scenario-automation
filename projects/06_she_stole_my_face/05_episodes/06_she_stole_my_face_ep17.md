# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP17

## S#1. Lena's office. Morning.

Lena at the window. Noah behind her. Cream envelope opened.

NOAH
Ethan.

LENA
Visitor lounge. Velvet box in his hand.

NOAH
He brought a ring.

LENA
Wait here.


## S#2. Visitor lounge.

Ethan on the edge of a couch. Stands when she enters.

ETHAN
You came down.

LENA
Sit, Ethan.

He lifts the box.

ETHAN
Came to ask properly. Press call tomorrow. Re-announce before noon.

LENA
Put it down.

She sits across.

LENA
Not yours. Haven't been since the morning you stood at the Cross Manor doors and didn't recognize my voice. You didn't look for me. You looked at her.

ETHAN
I've been apologizing —

LENA
Keep apologizing. To the next person who stands next to you.

ETHAN
Is there someone.

LENA
Noah.

ETHAN
Noah.

Ethan slides the box back into his jacket.

ETHAN
Thank you for seeing me.

LENA
Take care of yourself.


## S#3. Lena's office. Continuous.

Noah opens his arms.

LENA
Done. Had a ring. Left with it in his pocket.

On the access road. Behind — second car. Black. Plates blanked.

LENA
Third time this week.

NOAH
Same lack of plates.


## S#4. Continuous.

Phone buzzes.

[GRAPHIC]
UNKNOWN: Halcyon doesn't forget. Your mother knew that.

NOAH
Eileen. Or her hand.

LENA
Don't reply.

[END HOOK]
Below — brake lights flare. Window down. Hand out. Phone aimed up.

Lena photographs his car back.

LENA
Get me his face by tonight.

Hard Cut.
