# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP47

## S#1. Father's Study · Morning

William Sterling's chair, untouched eleven years. LENA at the doorway. First time since the funeral.

Pulls the chair out. Sits. Dials Noah.

LENA
It's done. I'm in his chair. Come over. Bring lunch.


## S#2. The Notification

Foundation grant binder open. Phone lights.

[INSERT] Phone screen:

> EILEEN VOSS, 58 — DIED THIS MORNING IN CUSTODY. CARDIAC EVENT.

Lena reads. Face still. Finishes the margin note.


## S#3. The Call

[INTERCUT] MARA on the Mediterranean coast. Bare feet.

MARA
Lena.

LENA
Eileen died this morning. Cardiac. In custody.

MARA
(sitting down)
Thank you. I'm okay. Are you.

LENA
I think I am.

(beat)

I read the last page of my mother's letter. She mentioned Eileen by name. Wasn't kind about herself.

MARA
What did she say.

LENA
Sent money eleven years. Never asked where it went. Should have looked at you once.

MARA
That's more than I would have asked of her.

(beat)

Get married already. Stop calling me with deaths.

LENA
Working on it. Three weeks.

MARA
Send a real photo. I want to see the dress.

LENA
I will.


## S#4. Front Hall · Early Evening

NOAH — two takeout bags, key on a ring she's never seen.

NOAH
News. Good or bad — your call which.

LENA
You picked the soup place again.

NOAH
(holding up the key)
Lake house. Closed at four. Keene name on the deed. Caretaker Friday at noon.

[END HOOK]
Lena takes the key.

LENA
Friday.

Phone buzzes. Bannon: *Sterling board voted unanimous. Chair Monday morning.*

A second buzz. Different name. ETHAN.

[INSERT] *Press desk just pinged me. Eileen's lawyer dropped a sealed file with the Times. Names Helena. Names you. Eight a.m. tomorrow.*

Noah's hand stills on the bag.

NOAH
She filed it before she died.

LENA
She knew she would.

LENA
Call the Times back. Tonight. We open it first.

NOAH
And the wedding.

LENA
Wedding holds.

Hard Cut.
