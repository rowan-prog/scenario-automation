# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP33

## S#1. Mara's bathroom · Morning

Mara at the sink. Cold water. Picks up the phone.

MARA
Mother. Day I was born. Not the version you told me at nine. The real one.

VOICE (V.O.)
Mara. Baby. Where is this coming from.

MARA
The hospital. What floor. Which doctor. Tell me.

VOICE (V.O.)
That was twenty-six years ago.

MARA
Tell me anyway.

VOICE (V.O.)
(pause)
I'll call you back. Five minutes.

Line cuts.

INTERCUT — Lena in her office.

MARA (V.O.)
Don't say my name. Are you alone.

LENA
Yes.

MARA (V.O.)
Called my mother. She hung up to invent the story before saying it to my face.

LENA
You think you weren't born to her.

MARA (V.O.)
Don't think I was born to anyone I've been told I was born to.

LENA
Come to the lake house. Today. Three p.m.

MARA (V.O.)
I said tomorrow.

LENA
Today's better.

MARA (V.O.)
All right.


## S#2. Lena's office · Half an hour later

NOAH
You moved it up. Why.

LENA
By tomorrow Eileen has a sentence ready for Mara. I want Mara to hear mine first.

NOAH
Then drive me.

LENA
I am.
