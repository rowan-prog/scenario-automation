# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP21

## S#1. Kitchen · Morning

Manila envelope. Lena tears the flap. Photograph. Typed note.

[KEY CAMERA]
- ECU photo — Helena pregnant. Beside her, younger woman, ring on left hand.

[GRAPHIC]
> Pay me, or I publish what your mother actually did.
> Twenty-four hours. — M.

LENA
Noah. She's threatening me. Photo of my mother pregnant with a woman I've never seen.

NOAH (V.O.)
Don't answer her. I'm coming.

LENA
Press conference. Today. Her name on a microphone before she puts a price on mine.


## S#2. Living Room · Forty Minutes Later

Phone on speaker. Andrea — PR head.

LENA
Andrea. Rooftop. Ten tomorrow. Twelve seats.

ANDREA (V.O.)
Topic.

LENA
Woman had her face cut into mine. Mara Voss.

ANDREA (V.O.)
Legal —

LENA
Legal can sit in my office at two.

NOAH
Called me to come, started without me.

LENA
You'd have argued.

He holds the photo to the lamp.

NOAH
Same ring as the lobby photograph.

LENA
So my mother knew her.

NOAH
Knew her well enough to be photographed pregnant.


## S#3. Window · Afternoon

Opposite curb — silver sedan, blacked plates.

LENA
She has somebody on the street.

NOAH
That's the point of paying her.

LENA
(into phone)
Frank. Sterling vault. Nine. Silver sedan on my curb — make him feel watched.

LENA
Tomorrow at ten.

NOAH
I'll be on the ridge at nine-thirty.

[END HOOK]
Phone buzzes. Unknown.

[GRAPHIC]
> You don't want a press conference. You want to know who the woman in the photograph is. Pay me and I'll tell you.

LENA
Tomorrow at ten.

She sets the phone down. Second buzz. Different number. Andrea.

ANDREA (V.O.)
Lena. AP just pulled. So did Times. Somebody called every desk in the last twenty minutes. Said you'd cancelled.

LENA
I didn't.

ANDREA (V.O.)
Voice on the call was yours.

Across the room — TV cuts to a chyron: *STERLING HEIRESS CANCELS PRESS CONFERENCE — REASONS UNCLEAR.*

NOAH
She's getting ahead of you tonight.

LENA
Pull every reporter back. Personal calls. Now.

Hard Cut.
