# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP06

## S#1. Noah's Office · Saturday Morning

Morning talk show. Mara — wearing Lena — interviewed live.

HOST (TV)
Tell us about that necklace.

MARA (TV)
(small laugh)
It was my mother's.

HOST (TV)
And the inscription?

MARA (TV)
(a flicker)
Something private.

LENA
She doesn't know what's inside the casing.

[INSERT] Childhood photo. Helena holding baby Lena. Pendant visible.

LENA
"Lumen meum." Mother translated it when I was sick.

NOAH
That's the leverage.


## S#2. Continuous

Noah unfolds a floor plan. Drops two laminated badges.

NOAH
Catering supervisor and assistant. Reception eight. Speeches nine. She's in Bridal Room C from seven.

LENA
And the scar.

NOAH
Left ear, two centimeters down. Curve.

LENA
Photo. Then out.

NOAH
Photo and a question. "What's inside that pendant." She blanks. You have her.

LENA
What if she's better at being me than I am.

NOAH
Then we lose.


## S#3. Mara's Suite · Late Afternoon

Mara at the vanity. Pendant on a velvet tray. Nail along the rim — searching for a seam she can't find.

MARA
What.

EILEEN (PHONE)
Detective dropped his contract. She's with him. Assume she walks into your reception tonight.

MARA
She won't get through the door.

EILEEN (PHONE)
She got through a windshield. Inscription. Did you learn it.

MARA
Tessa pulled the wrong page.

EILEEN (PHONE)
Then don't talk about the necklace tonight at all.

[END HOOK]
Knock. Mara covers the pendant.

MARA
Come in.

Catering assistant. Head down. Dark wig.

ASSISTANT
Touch-up steam for the dress, Miss Sterling.

MARA
Lay it on the chaise.

Assistant lifts her chin a fraction. It is Lena.

Hard Cut.
