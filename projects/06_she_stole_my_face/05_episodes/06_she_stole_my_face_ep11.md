# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP11

## S#1. Police precinct. Observation room. Morning.

Through the glass — Tessa Hale. Crying before anyone speaks.

DET. RYAN
Walked in at six. Asked for you.

LENA
What did she give you.

DET. RYAN
Bookie. Signatures she forged in your name. And Eileen Voss buying out the debt.

NOAH
That's the name.

LENA
I'm going in.

DET. RYAN
Fifteen minutes.


## S#2. Interview room. Continuous.

TESSA
Lena — I'm so —

LENA
Don't you fucking — don't.

LENA
(shaking)
You sold me. Every single thing you handed her.

TESSA
(sobbing)
Debt — three hundred thousand. Bookie had video. Going to send it to my dad —

LENA
Eileen. Skip to Eileen.

TESSA
Came to the Hudson coffee shop. Paid it all. Wanted three things. Your medical history. Travel calendar. Signed referral to Brandt — in your name.

LENA
(breaking)
You held my hair — when my mom — you HELD MY HAIR, Tessa —

LENA
(slamming the table)
Don't say sorry to me.


## S#3. Continuous.

TESSA
One thing I didn't tell him. About Mara. Last spring — drunk on Spring Street. Asked where the money came from. She laughed. Said *Halcyon takes care of me, Tess.* Then *my mother told me. Before she died.*

LENA
*Her* mother. Or *my* mother.

TESSA
She said *my.* But she paused. Like she was picking.

Lena's hand goes to her throat. Empty.

[END HOOK]
LENA
Your lawyer's coming. Shut up until he sits down. Don't ever look for me.

She walks. Noah opens the door.

DET. RYAN (O.S.)
Sterling. Hold.

He turns a tablet to her.

[INSERT] CCTV — Hudson coffee shop, three weeks before the crash. Tessa across the booth. Across from her — woman, dark coat, pearl earring. Mother's coat. Face cut by the angle.

LENA
That coat is my mother's.

DET. RYAN
Footage is two months old.

NOAH
Same coat as Brandt's photo of the woman who paid the wires.

LENA
Somebody is wearing my dead mother to buy people.

Tessa, through the interview-room glass — sees the screen. Stands up. Slams her palm flat. Mouths *I'm sorry.*

Lena doesn't look back.

Hard Cut.
