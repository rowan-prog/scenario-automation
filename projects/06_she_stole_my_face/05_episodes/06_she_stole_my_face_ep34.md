# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP34

## S#1. Lake House — Living Room · 3 p.m.

Wood floor. Stone fireplace, no fire. Mara at the window when Lena walks in.

[KEY CAMERA]
- ECU surgical scar 2cm below Mara's left ear

MARA
You came alone.

LENA
Noah's at the gate. A hundred yards.

LENA
(flat)
You're not my mother's daughter. Never were.

MARA
What.

LENA
My mother lost a baby the night you were born. Three floors up. Same hospital. Same hour. Real. But not you. Julian Voss's daughter. Eileen Voss's daughter. Always were.

MARA
That's the lie my mother told me when I was five.

LENA
Truth your mother hid twenty years. Told you you were owed my life.

MARA
(quiet)
You don't know her.

LENA
Eileen's the third trustee on Halcyon. Moved money for your surgery four years.


## S#2. Five minutes later

Mara's hand to the scar.

MARA
Say her name.

LENA
Eileen.

Mara's hand drops. Eyes wet.

MARA
Came to my apartment three nights ago. Sat on my couch. Said I was almost done. Said you'd talk in circles tomorrow.

LENA
She knows you're here.

Phone vibrates. ECU screen — MOTHER.

LENA
Don't pick up.

MARA
She'll know.

LENA
Let her know.


## S#3. The call

Mara taps green. Speaker.

EILEEN (V.O.)
Mara. Walk out the front door. Now.

MARA
Mother?

EILEEN (V.O.)
Walk out the front door, baby. Right now.

MARA
Where are you.

EILEEN (V.O.)
Closer than you think.

Lena's eyes widen — recognition.

[END HOOK]
EILEEN (V.O.)
Mara. I'm at your apartment. Milo's on the couch. Stays fine until you walk out the door. Two minutes.

Mara's eyes to the front door. Lena dialing Noah.

Hard Cut.
