# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP42

## S#1. Noah's Workspace · Evening

NOAH at his oak table. Boxes labeled VOSS — DEVICES. Eileen had eight stories ready. Till morning to kill them.

NOAH
(headset)
Diane. Packet in your inbox. Reporter on your masthead paid by Eileen seven years. Files by midnight or I file on him by sunrise.

Piece doesn't run.

He marks the first name. Dials.

## S#5. Parking Garage · Night

Noah against his trunk. REYES — fifties, raincoat.

REYES
You said this wasn't a threat.

NOAH
Read it.

Reyes reads.

REYES
Paid me through a numbered company. Six years.

NOAH
A woman's in a hospital bed because you didn't.

REYES
What do you want.

NOAH
Retract the piece. Sign a record. Keep your job.

REYES
All right. Tonight.


## S#6. Noah's Car · Night

Dashboard — LENA on the call.

NOAH
Eleven names between us. Two retractions filed.

LENA (filtered)
You sound wrecked.

NOAH
I am.

LENA
Come over.

[END HOOK]
Phone buzzes. UNKNOWN. Photo: Lena's door, from across the street, twenty minutes ago.

NOAH
She's not the only one with leftovers.

He throws the car into drive.
