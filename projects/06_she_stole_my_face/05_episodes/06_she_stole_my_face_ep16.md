# SHE STOLE MY FACE — EP16

## S#1. Noah's apartment. Night.

Cream envelope on the coffee table. Lena alone on the sofa.

LENA
(to herself)
Open the goddamn thing, Lena.

She stands. Crosses to the balcony.


## S#2. Balcony.

Noah at the rail in shirtsleeves. Waiting.

LENA
I can't open it. I'm scared of who's inside.

NOAH
Then don't open it tonight.

LENA
The night Ethan dragged me off the steps — I tried to feel his loss. Picked the song. The vow line. Felt nothing.

NOAH
Lena —

LENA
Scared even if I get my life back, the me who was going to be happy is already gone.

NOAH
You're not gone. Quit Cross the day I saw Mara's wrist in the crash footage.

LENA
You're not on a clock.

NOAH
Quit because I couldn't keep pretending this was a job.

LENA
Say it.

NOAH
I love you.

LENA
I love you too.


## S#3. Same balcony. Continuous.

She reaches first. He waits one breath. Right hand finds her jaw. She closes the distance.

They kiss. Slow. Open mouth.

LENA
I'm reading the letter tonight. Want you in the apartment. Not in the room.

NOAH
I'll be in the apartment.


## S#4. Mara's window.

Mara at the window. Compact binoculars. Behind her — silhouette. Hand on her shoulder.

[KEY CAMERA]
- ECU the older man's hand, signet ring

MAN (low)
She's not looking.

MARA
For how long.

MAN
Long enough.

MARA
Then we move tonight.

[END HOOK]
Man steps back. Mara stays at the window.

**SHE'S WATCHING. SHE'S NOT ALONE.**
