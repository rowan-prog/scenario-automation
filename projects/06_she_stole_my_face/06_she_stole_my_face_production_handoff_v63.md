## 1. Production Authority

Production may refine or adjust staging, blocking, specified camera shots, pacing, and visual detail when it better serves a scene's core purpose and impact — especially in payoff, reveal, intimacy, reversal, or cliffhanger scenes. The intended story result, relationship beat, emotional result, and continuity must remain intact. **Dialogue must be followed as written; any dialogue change must be reported in advance.**

## 2. Core Pleasure

Primary screen appeal: **the audience watches a woman's whole life worn by the friend who drugged her — and waits for the thief to destroy herself in public.**

- The buy is dramatic irony: the audience knows the truth from EP1. Every scene where the world adores the impostor and spits on the real woman is the engine. Make the world's adoration of the fake BIG and the real woman's humiliation public and filmed.
- Escalation is not scale — it is the kind of theft. Face → fiancé → home → name → dead mother's foundation. Each public scene Mara takes more, in front of more people, more brazenly.
- The discharge is Mara wrecking herself with her own greed/cruelty in front of crowds and cameras — never caught by evidence. Her mother selling her out at the climax is the peak.
- Tone command: **makjang — broad, brazen, cruel, dirty-concrete.** Do NOT make it tasteful, cool, procedural, or a detective story.

## 3. The Logic Behind It (not on the page)

- **Knowledge lock:** the audience knows everything from EP1. No character "discovers" the swap via evidence. The drama is emotional, not investigative.
- **Forbidden** (kills the engine): forensic/DNA/medical-record proof; police or legal procedure driving a beat; a foundation legal/board timeline; detective clue-gathering; Noah recognizing or proving Lena's identity; any new tracked prop.
- **Allowed and wanted:** public humiliation, crowd phones as a spread/shaming device, Mara's greed bursting out on a live mic, her mother's spoken betrayal, the real woman reclaiming her face by standing still while the thief self-destructs.
- **Tone parentheticals** in dialogue `(1-3 words)` are the emotion/delivery instruction for that line. They exist so non-English actors/directors read tone correctly. Do not ignore them.

## 4. Character Handoff

### Lena Sterling — protagonist (the REAL one)

- **On-screen title:** `Lena Sterling` (EP1 first functional cut only)
- **Core expression:** the warm, real woman whose face is stolen — never the avenging villainess.
- **Must read as:** earnest and dignified, with steel — not a cold schemer, not a soft weeping victim.
- **Story lock:** her face is surgically copied onto Mara; she loses everything by EP3 and reclaims her name by standing still while Mara self-destructs. She does NOT win by detective work.
- **Voice:** warm, plain, direct; full human breaths, not staccato; the steel shows in short flat lines at decision points, never in writer-punchlines.
- **Beauty/body:** mid-20s, naturally lovely, soft warmth — this is the face the whole show fights over.
- **Looks:**
  - EP1: own life — soft engagement-night glamour, mother's gold pendant at her throat.
  - EP2-8: stripped — hospital gown under a stolen coat; then a dark wig + catering uniform for the infiltrations (EP8 bridal room). Bare own face, no pendant.
  - EP9-42: Noah's world — clean, simple, well-kept clothes; own bare face, no wig; no pendant (it's on Mara).
  - EP43-50: her own ivory wedding dress (her mother's), then the pendant reclaimed and back at her own throat (EP47→50).
- **Do not:** villainess wardrobe/makeup; smug avenger energy; soft helpless-victim staging; let her "prove" anything with evidence.

### Mara Voss — antagonist (the IMPOSTOR · same face as Lena)

- **On-screen title:** `Mara Voss / Lena's Friend` (EP1 reveal cut only)
- **Core expression:** the thief wearing the stolen face — sweet public mask over naked greed and cruelty.
- **Must read as:** adored in public, vicious in private — a brazen taker, not a tragic double, not a clever mastermind (she is greedy and not smart; the world just hands her things).
- **Story lock:** she has Lena's exact face. The pendant lives at her throat from EP1 to EP47. She destroys herself in public, never gets caught by proof, and her own mother gives her up at the end.
- **Voice:** honeyed and tearful for the room; flat, gloating, dirty-concrete when alone with Lena. The cruelty is plain ("Thanks for the face, you dumb bitch") — never literary.
- **Beauty/body:** identical to Lena (same actress / same-face casting). The difference is carriage and context, not features.
- **Looks:**
  - EP1: nurse mask → reveal; then takes the pendant and Lena's engagement gown.
  - EP2-29: Cross-world luxury — bridal/gala/foundation wardrobe, always the stolen pendant; self-inflicted forearm scratch (EP8) and a split lip framed as Lena's attack.

## 5. Telling Lena and Mara Apart (THE #1 production task — identical face)

Lena and Mara have the **same face** (Mara is surgically matched; cast one actress / identical-face doubles). The audience must always know which is which by **context, never by facial difference**. Priority tells:

1. **The pendant** (primary tell). Mara wears Helena's gold pendant at her throat from EP1 to EP47. Lena's throat is **bare** the entire middle of the show. Pendant = Mara; bare throat = Lena. (Reverses only at EP47 when Lena reclaims it.)
2. **World/wardrobe.** Mara = Cross luxury (gowns, gala, foundation, bridal). Lena = hospital gown → catering disguise + dark wig (infiltrations) → Noah's plain clean world. If she's in luxury with the pendant, it's Mara; if she's in a wig/uniform or plain in the glass penthouse, it's Lena.
3. **Carriage + who's around them.** Mara is adored by crowds, fawned on, sweet-faced in public. Lena is alone, dragged, filmed by jeering phones — or quiet beside Noah.
4. **Wounds.** Lena: temple cut (EP3 crash), throat. Mara: self-inflicted forearm (EP8), glass-cut forearm under lace (EP27/29), hairline bandage (EP33+).

**Do not** frame the two identically in the same cut without at least one tell (pendant / wig / world) visible. In split-screen or mirror cuts (EP1, EP27 service-yard) the pendant + wig + expression carry the read.
